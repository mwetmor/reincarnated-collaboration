REGISTER CARD — Run C-9 — ILLUMINATED v1.4 (Matt rulings R-C9-0, R-C9-8, R-C9-9, R-C9-21, R-C9-29) — do not reinterpret; every line is a constraint
MERGE: every image joins two sources. SUBJECT, COMPOSITION, ICONOGRAPHY and PALETTE come from late-medieval
  and early-Renaissance illuminated manuscripts (Books of Hours, chronicles, romances, c. 1400–1530), as
  shown in the attached reference pages. RENDERING comes from hand-painted watercolor-and-ink character
  illustration of the kind made for 1990s tactics role-playing games: pen-and-ink contour, fine pen
  hatching to model shade on cloth, armour and stone, transparent watercolor washes laid by hand.
RENDERING (the hand must show):
  - washes are UNEVEN: they pool at shape edges, granulate, bloom softly, sometimes stop short of the
    line or overrun it slightly; a second darker wash plus hatching makes the shadow, never an airbrushed
    gradient;
  - large areas are left PALE: cream and bare-paper highlights; no glossy specular highlights, no glow,
    no rim light, no bloom, no lens effects, no digital gradients;
  - the pen line wavers slightly and varies in weight; hatching is drawn by hand, not a texture;
  - it must read as one hand-painted illustration, not a digital painting, a render, or a photo.
PALETTE: the manuscripts' mineral hues at a MODERATE value: lapis blue, vermilion, verdigris, yellow
  ochre, madder, lead white, warm stone greys. Rich colour sits on ACCENTS (a tabard, a banner, a sky
  band, a roof); large surfaces stay calm and pale. Burnished gold only on sacred or preserved objects.
  No neon, no saturated fills across large areas, no large areas of black.
  ACCENTS (Matt M1, R-C9-8): as on a manuscript page, roughly a tenth to a fifth of every image carries
  rich colour in small places (flowers, banners, clothing, a lapis sky band, gold); never on the walkable floor.
VALUE: highlights pale cream; the darkest marks are the ink line and hatching, not filled shadow areas.
LINE (Matt M1, R-C9-9): a crisp DARK sepia-to-near-black pen line, the dark outline of hand-painted
  tactics-RPG character illustration; the darkest marks in the image are this line and its hatching.
LIGHT: soft, even daylight from SCREEN UPPER-LEFT; no dramatic lighting, no cast shadows on figure
  frames, no baked darkness. Night and drama are the engine's job, never the painting's.
PARALLAX LIGHT (Matt R-C9-29): background layers are DARKER, lit by the low SUNSET at screen-left: warm gold rim light
  on sun-facing slopes, deep violet-indigo shadow elsewhere, a purple smoky haze thickening with distance. (The
  LIGHT line below still governs figures and the walkable plate.)
PARALLAX (Matt R-C9-21): background layers show a SUNSET sky (never a night sky) and FIRE burning in the forest,
  at the cathedral and at the tower; saturated warm is allowed ONLY for fire and the sunset sky. NOTHING may repeat:
  no duplicated building, tree clump, rock, cloud or fire (repetition is a tell). NO towns or villages at all. Distant things are SMALL.
SCENES: the walkable ground is the BRIGHTEST and CALMEST region of the image — large pale surfaces,
  little small detail, no pattern on floors; decoration belongs on walls, banners and architecture.
  Stylised manuscript rocks, trees and buildings; never a photographic landscape.
CAMERA: fixed elevated three-quarter ORTHOGRAPHIC game view (the attached geometry guide or the task
  sets it); the manuscripts' flat perspective, profile-only poses and hierarchical scale are NOT copied.
PLATE: figures on flat pure #00ff00, nothing else in the background; matting is done by frozen tools.
  SCALE is a post-process, never a prompt instruction.
FORBIDDEN in prompts: any franchise, studio, game or character name; any living artist's name;
  "pixel art"; "3D render"; "cel shaded"; "anime"; "chibi".
