How to read these measurements

Largest proportional differences among the requested CHECK measurements (absolute change divided by G’s value, comparing distinct metrics): plate uniformity rises from 0.057124348 to 0.125659596 (about 120%; +6.853525 percentage points), and annulus-filtered O3b falls from 4 to 3 (25%; −1 instance). This descriptive ranking is not a quality ranking. Raw O3b falls 7 → 6; the score vectors and inventories are identical.

  • Plate: CHECK measured a tolerance-8 fraction, not exact #00ff00 identity. No minimum fraction is declared.
  • Light: O7 is a luminance-weighted circular-mean azimuth in screen x-right / y-up coordinates, with no declared tolerance. Its very low resultants (~0.01) and high dispersions (~0.99) accompany angles near upper-right; JUDGE instead reports an upper-left key for both masters. Both observations are retained without resolving the disagreement. JUDGE flags brighter, warmer fill than the prescribed deep cool ambient.
  • O3b: advisory, sheet-wide ring-family counts, with no allowed masks. Raw uses hollow_min = 0; annulus-filtered uses 0.5. These counts are not confirmed ornament placements. The red ornament observations come from JUDGE.
  • O8: descriptive profile L2 distance without a threshold. The anchor includes its background, has different source dimensions, and the comparison is not calibrated. Source and 0.5 display measurements are both shown.
  • Silhouette: frozen CHECK resizes alpha to 64 × 64 with Lanczos, then thresholds at 128; the original portrait aspect ratio is not preserved. The page adds only the requested ×4 nearest-neighbour display enlargement. JUDGE axis 4 used mental reduction to 64-px height, a different view.
  • Unavailable: CHECK’s key-facing-edge O2 is unevaluable because the frozen tool lacks an edge/region-mask parameter and no edge annotation or actual ground was supplied. No new metric or image processing was added.
Bible construction requirements used for annotation

Exactly one waist belt; its buckle closes the tabard. Exactly one diagonal harness strap from shoulder to opposite hip, carrying the satchel on the figure’s LEFT hip. Every strap terminates at a named part, and every fastener closes a named gap. The plain dark-walnut staff is held in the RIGHT hand, with small brass ferrules only; rod_head must be absent on the starter. One laced bracer per forearm; a pair of boots buckled at the shin. Pauldrons are advanced-set only. Helmet, cape, shield and quiver are declared absent controls. Starter surfaces have zero motif placements; wear and stitching belong only at fasteners and load points.

Red tabard rows link the inventory part to JUDGE’s ornament observations; present/count transcription itself does not establish decoration. Unknown inventory sidedness is explicitly labeled, rather than treated as an observed wrong side.
