Reading the measurements

Plate uniformity is the non-subject fraction within tolerance 8; no minimum fraction was declared. O7 is circular mean azimuth versus the fixed 135° key, with no declared tolerance; high dispersion and low resultant are retained for interpretation. O3b counts are advisory ring-family detections, not confirmed ornament counts; JUDGE’s surface descriptions are reported separately. O8 is descriptive only: the anchor and candidates differ in source dimensions and alpha support. The anchor is RGB with all pixels included, while candidates use subject alpha. No new threshold has been assigned.

Two varying CHECK quantities highlighted in the summary: O7 azimuth spans 18.581936° (132.027810–150.609745°); O3b raw spans 5 instances (5–10). These have the largest raw numerical spans among the requested base metrics (plate fraction, azimuth, raw count, annulus count, O8 distance); mixed units do not define a quality ranking.

Bible interpretation retained: CONSTRUCTION allows brass satchel corners, while the starter sigil/plain-budget rules require zero motif placements and plain surfaces. Red corner annotations preserve JUDGE’s characterization of conspicuous shapes as deviations; they do not invent a ban on all functional brass corners. The inventories contain only present/count, so side, attachment, closure, lacing and material details are not certified by those counts.
