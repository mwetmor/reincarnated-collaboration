Bible construction context & unresolved evidence

The starter has zero motif placements and no staff head-piece. Every strap terminates at a named part and every fastener closes a named gap. One diagonal shoulder-to-opposite-hip harness carries the olive waxed-canvas satchel on the figure’s LEFT hip; one waist belt closes the tabard. The plain dark walnut rod is held in the RIGHT hand, with small brass ferrules at foot and tip. Forearm bracers are laced, one per forearm; boots are a pair, buckled at the shin. The oversized LEFT pauldron is advanced-set only. The inventories contain no side, material, attachment, or fastener fields, so counts alone cannot verify these details.

Reported ornament concern, all three: the judge flags shaped brass satchel corner plates. The bible explicitly specifies “olive waxed canvas, brass corners” while also requiring plain surfaces. The judge’s shape-specific concern is preserved; brass corners alone are not silently reclassified as a forbidden part.
