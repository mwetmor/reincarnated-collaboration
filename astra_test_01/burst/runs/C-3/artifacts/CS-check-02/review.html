Method notes

Frozen clamp matte applied with a temporary 16-px pure-green border, stripped before restricting alpha to each blockout mask dilated by six 4-connected iterations. DP minimizes edge-to-edge band cost with ±1-px moves. C/A compares against the existing A/B canvas, including its corner. D's two bands independently compare against the pre-D canvas.

D corner owner: D/B, selected by lower full-path mean cost. The other band's corner segment remains drawn for audit, though its blend weights do not own that square.
