# E05F — explicit fractional motion contract

Scoped PASS for the revised source, neutral sampler, sampled raster transfer and recorded continuous-root walks. Godot is the shipping target; Pixi is the test harness. This does not close the animation, art, lighting or production-suite gates.

The previous E05E samplers could not reproduce the original Bézier source between frames. This experiment copies E05Z's source and changes only interpolation to LINEAR. It preserves all 76,650 key times/values, geometry, materials, weights and all 216 active integer palettes. Original source and failed E05E/E07A evidence remain unchanged.

All 35 bones use supported quaternion, full-scale inheritance without constraints. The neutral manifest stores raw local translation/quaternion/scale channels, rest matrices, parent indices, exact integer fallback and source bindings. The sampler normalizes linearly interpolated raw quaternion components; this is the declared source convention, not generic shortest-path slerp.

- All 312 source/physical samples pass: maximum geometry discrepancy 1.450 micrometres and bone-length discrepancy 1.028 micrometres. Contact, penetration, book size and gear clearance meet the unchanged registration.
- Headless JS matches 312 exported fractional palettes within 6.557e-7 matrix units; 216 integer palettes are byte exact. Invalid input, hierarchy, buffer and source-binding controls pass.
- All 864 fractional source/render comparisons pass across six poses, eight headings, three outfits, three lights and native/fresh 3× rasters. Worst MAE 0.0090682; worst fraction over eight channel levels 0.0009634. Every mask is nonempty; all 48 conservative source bounds pass with at least 76.284 logical pixels of crop margin.
- Twenty-pixel gear shift and wrong-pose controls fail as intended. Removing normal corrections does not cross the raster failure threshold here. This insensitive control is retained; it does not establish that normals are unnecessary.
- All 216 integer poses render without GL errors. Nine native videos cover idle/walk/cast in three outfits. All recorded movement commands are accepted, clip endpoints reached, no browser/GL errors.
- Actual fractional sole skinning with the recorded root gives maximum stance drift of 0.354 mm (starter), 0.380 mm (advanced/head), 0.507 mm (advanced/hair), versus the 20 mm limit. Lowest sampled sole is −0.498 mm, within the 1 mm limit. A dense control recreating the old quantized pose produces 32.668 mm drift and fails; continuous sampling gives 0.506 mm and passes.

Fractional normals use normalized interpolation of the union of adjacent sparse corrections; absent endpoint values come from that endpoint's skinned rest normal. Uncorrected vertices use the current palette. This approximation is qualified only by this sampled raster matrix. The diagnostic `normal_union_records` field counts records written during that call and reads zero on cache reuse; it is not a normal residency metric.

Visually inspected labelled skin/source stills and all nine video-derived sequences. The cast, gear and hair retain the working source; no new contour separation was visible in the inspected pairs. The continuous walk advances without the old pose/root stepping error. The partition hides the later part of the walk; a gameplay visibility policy remains open. Current floor-shadow omission and plain chamber art remain visible failures of the broader presentation, not waived by this motion result. Saved videos run at normal speed; extracted JPEG sheets are compressed diagnostic previews, not the pixel evidence.

Capture storage uses full pixel measurements and SHA-256 of both RGBA crops for every comparison, plus selected original PNG pairs and actual negative-control images. Fresh 3× images are rerasterized, not resampled native screenshots. No generation or paid service call occurred. Nine videos are lossy VP9; the deterministic integer audit and raster comparisons are separate evidence.
