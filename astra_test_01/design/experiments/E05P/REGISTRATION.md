# E05P — limited-view persistent-layer motion and gear hypothesis probe

Godot is the shipping target; Pixi is the test harness. Registered before deformation/rendering. This is the architecture §6 limited-view mechanism probe, not the original ASTRA generated-frame campaign or final eight-view pilot. Original ASTRA frame counts and failures remain unchanged. Full eight-view idle/walk/cast/turn acceptance and further action motions remain required after a useful mechanism is demonstrated.

H03/H04: immutable painted regions, explicit joints and time-based motion can preserve torso/bag identity, alternate leading legs and keep planted feet fixed while allowing modular attachments. Use the E03 mage original with E04M2 v3 contour, projection C and an explicitly declared SE candidate direction. It remains an uncalibrated depicted view; do not claim projection transfer from bone math alone. First isolate one view before expanding to a second or eight.

Representation: source-coordinate part polygons, shared immutable source texture, neutral pivots/joints, hierarchical transforms or bounded per-part meshes. Head/torso/bag retain source appearance. Part boundaries inside the figure are not inferred alpha. Hidden regions and exposed joints must be visually inspected; clipping existing pixels does not create missing anatomy. If gaps or paper joints appear, preserve the failure and use a separately recorded generated hidden-region/keypose repair within the cap, or reject the method. Do not stretch a whole character, hide a boot, duplicate a static pose or slow a test until it looks like a walk.

Motion contract: JSON clock and actor root own translation; view owns pose sampling/interpolation. Walk candidate speed 2m/s, cycle 0.8s, alternating 0.4s stance phases, with declared support intervals and smooth swing clearance. Eight equally spaced diagnostic walk samples plus normal 60Hz playback; poses are sampled from tracks, not eight duplicated images. Idle is 1.6s; cast is 1.2s with release at 0.6s and exact return to rest. No VFX integration pass until the release/socket is qualified. Gear initially tests attach/hide semantics and incompatibility rejection; a diagnostic polygon or recolour cannot qualify painted advanced gear.

Freeze before evaluation: planted sole world drift ≤0.02m over declared stance; screen attachment error ≤0.5px at logical gameplay resolution; rigid bag landmark-distance change ≤1% (rotation-aware, not bounding-box height); no repeated leading leg; feet visible during support; cycle first/last derivative continuity; original torso/face registration unchanged at neutral pose. Inspect 50px and 150px body scale, 1× normal-speed walk/cast and source-part boundaries. Control suite includes sliding contact, repeated leading leg, 20% bag swelling, detached staff/gear and malformed compatibility data. Counts/hashes alone cannot pass gait/anatomy. Missing source anatomy, substantial joint gaps or obvious cardboard articulation are visual FAIL even if kinematics pass.

Limits: ≤3 built-in imagegen calls for explicitly needed hidden-region/gear/keypose repairs; zero paid API/service calls; ≤120min active; ≤6 capture batches each ≤5min; ≤150MB. At most two attempts of an unchanged failing repair branch. Source paintings never edited with Python; runtime geometry/masks and diagnostic overlays are distinct from painting. Before each capture, reserve disk for its declared frame count using measured PNG size and a safety margin; guard writes by actual size. Lossless capture encoding is allowed only with decoded-pixel equality recorded. Retain failures and report any limit deviation.

On local pass, test second view and complete turnaround/motion/gear pilot before roster or cross-archetype expansion. On failure, identify whether geometry, hidden-region art, authoring cost or evaluation failed and register a changed method. Continue independent VFX semantic/data and native-reference acquisition preparation; do not count those as passed animation.
