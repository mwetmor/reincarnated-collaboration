What this establishes

The three runtimes can place the same painted source with projection C. Phaser passes all 18 root/socket/projection cases and the planted fault controls. Its basic position, following-camera, scale and translation controls work in Chrome WebGL on this machine.

Fine lines and tiny labels rasterize differently. At this size, the unchanged historical character source does not establish a meaningful artistic winner. The legacy image's depicted camera remains unqualified for C. Floors and object outlines here are diagnostic geometry; the scene has no collision and translation is not walking.

C's position-dependent sprite approximation is still an open painted-art question. E02's geometric proxy flags lateral placements for review; following the actor removes its own relative offset, while other actors and camera lag still need testing.
