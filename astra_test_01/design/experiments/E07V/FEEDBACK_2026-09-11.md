## Recommended differentiation to test

Stronghold silhouette: squat, enclosed, defensive and visibly maintained by its occupants. Keep the weathered defenders and modest sacred objects; cathedral-scale nave/statue composition is not the next test.

**F02 — a reclaimed rock-cut reservoir and passage depot.** Broad low excavated chambers, truncated ancient waterworks, dry channels, asymmetrical salvage platforms, rope lifts and brass route-marking devices. Stronghold silhouette: sunken, layered, scavenged and exploratory. Forbidden relics and maps express pursuit of escape. Avoid the F01 shrine-wall layout, Gothic arches and cathedral nave. Avoid turning it into F04's monumental observatory or F03's industrial foundry. Any “impossible passage” is a deliberate portal with explicit destination data, not an excuse for inconsistent ordinary door geometry.

These are Codex's proposed test briefs, not newly approved faction canon or replacements for the four accepted themes. Retain F04 gameplay clarity and F03 detail/local floor-wall glow under the shared C projection.

## Doorway acceptance added to subsequent environment tests

Before painting, each exit must declare the interior and exterior regions, threshold segment, outward normal, wall thickness, destination region, hinge and door-leaf sweep (where applicable). Derive camera-side visibility and aperture masks from those data. Export labelled interior/exterior blockout views with actual adjacent floor on both sides.

In the painting and runtime, follow the ground continuously through the threshold. Exterior imagery may appear only on its correct side and where a camera ray through the opening reaches that region. A foreground wall opening must not paste an exterior landscape onto the room side. A cutaway reveals the actual next region; an intentional magical portal must be declared separately.

Review both exits in closed/open states and traverse each in both directions. Test leaf collision/swing, jamb occlusion and light leakage against state. Include a deliberately reversed interior/exterior backdrop as a failing control. Require zero semantic side reversals; retain the experiment's frozen geometric tolerances. Do not count an attractive concept as a traversal or art/data pass.

Next visual comparison: same C layout, actor scale and clean material response; distinct F01 waystation versus F02 reclaimed reservoir. Pair each painting with its labelled spatial guide.
