# E03 checkpoint — codex-selected-winner: Pixi.js

All three regimes completed the shared painted checkpoint: 15 scene/control captures each, with the same projection, floor, pillar, mage and silhouette annotations. Final technical validation: **203/203** checks pass. Open [the browser comparison](review.html); it works from disk. This is a completed comparison with scoped verdicts, not a passing full production suite.

**Select Pixi.js 8.20.1 for the next bounded interaction, gear and VFX tests.** The final native renders do not establish an artistic winner. Pixi's direct scene/mask composition worked on the first render batch, its browser capture and UI controls were readily inspected, and the JavaScript event/JSON boundary matches the intended presentation task. Godot also rendered successfully on its first batch, but browser review required a separate native process/capture path. Phaser's initial v3-style GeometryMask call was ignored by v4 WebGL; a source-verified v4 Mask filter fixed it in one repair batch. That is an observed integration cost, not a claim Phaser cannot render the assets. No performance winner is claimed; engine-specific development times were not instrumented finely enough to rank by minutes.

- [Floor alignment](evidence/floor-alignment.json): 36 outer-edge samples; maximum 1.5625 native pixels against the frozen 3px limit. Four manually annotated corners agree within 0.82px, with ±2 source-pixel annotation uncertainty. The material boundary visibly follows its diagonal guide but was not fully certified by the outer-edge instrument. No painted walls/props/pits were observed in the clean floor.
- [Final validation](evidence/validation.json): native 960×640 captures, imports/hashes, actual image transforms/order, reconstructed actor roots, planted visible floor/root/order faults, mask-background pixel probes and browser controls. Maximum transform residual 0.00001523px. Front/behind crossings and prop removal expose clean underlying floor. Final source-scale annotation uncertainty: pillar ≤0.50px, mage ≤0.42px, not an anatomical contact proof.
- Native alpha **FAIL**: generated pillar and mage are RGB, with opaque checkerboards. The originals remain untouched. No fourth call was spent repeating this failure. [E03M](MASK_EXTENSION.md) separately registers explicit runtime silhouette masks. Godot textured polygons, Pixi Graphics masks and Phaser v4 Mask filters now clip the same annotations. Native and enlarged light-background inspection finds no large background patches in the final views. Fine hair, translucent edges and moving silhouettes remain unqualified.
- Initial Phaser mask **FAIL** is preserved as `evidence/phaser-*.png`; the fixed captures are `phaser-v2-*.png`. The first 191-check instrument did not inspect mask leakage and was insufficient for that claim. Its report and pre-fix adapter are retained. Twelve outside-silhouette pixel checks were added after visual inspection exposed the missing coverage; tolerances were not relaxed. The corrected source uses `enableFilters().filters.external.addMask(...)`. The pinned Phaser distribution states that `setMask` is Canvas-only in v4.
- The new mage was requested with C's elevated view, but its actual anatomical projection is still a perceptual question. The floor/body look remains a candidate; source conditioning alone is not calibration. The frozen pose is not walking. Persistent layers, modular gear and reusable VFX have not passed this checkpoint.
