## §2 — THE finding: the authored-capstone experiment (direct dialogue with the capstone thesis)
D3 is the genre's only fully AUTHORED archetype space: **the set IS the build** — designer-written capstones carrying 10,000%-class multipliers, with skills reduced to delivery shells. This is the strongest market evidence FOR the thesis that build-defining capstone mechanics are the core of ARPG fun (every record in this corpus is literally a shipped capstone)… and simultaneously its cautionary pole: an authored-only space collapses buildcraft into tier-list obedience, and Blizzard had to ADD the escape valve — **Legacy of Nightmares/Dreams, the anti-set that pays you for wearing no set** — so free recombination could exist at all. Two of S39's four GR150 builds are LoD builds. The design lesson for RDR is crisp: *authored capstones need an emergent-recombination layer, or the game becomes a menu.* Recommend this pairing (authored capstone × free layer) get a line in the next doctrine pass.
