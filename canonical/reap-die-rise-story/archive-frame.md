## §1 — The frame in one paragraph

The player descends into a failing **archive of the genre's build-history** — every shelf a preserved kit, every floor an era — to **rescue** what is being lost. Matt, verbatim: *"We are enacting the preservation of the Build Horizon in real time."* The motive is RESCUE (OM-1) because under rescue **the fiction is true**: the project literally is a preservation of the genre's build-history (the D7 substrate-grounded-provenance discipline operating at the frame level). The archive's contents war among themselves and see the player as one more adversary; a third force — the devourers — eats the record itself. As the player saves each kit, they *become* its faction's member. The pinnacle kit is authored, at the end, by the only being who has lived every shelf.

## §2 — Motive: RESCUE (OM-1, ruled)

- The descent = preservation in real time. Harvest re-reads as *saving*, not taking — **but complicity is RETAINED, not deleted** (OM-2): the player triages. What you cannot carry, you leave to be eaten. **Triage-guilt** replaces theft-guilt.
- **Anti-alibi guardrail (buried truth, Q4-resident):** the breach that lets the devourers in is connected to the harvest itself — *"you opened the door"* stays buried until the reveal layer. The cult's cover-story is the **inevitability-alibi**: *"they were coming anyway; we saved what we could."*
- Luminous register foregrounded; the §23.3 moral spine re-motivates (not rewrites) inside the Q4 keystone cluster.

## §3 — The antagonist: one, with two faces (OM-4, ruled)

**Forgetting ↦ deadening** — the same enemy in two states. The abstract face: the archive's contents fade, dead-form, go silent. The flesh face: the **devourers**. There is also a diegetic ***someone*** (OM-13, ruled) — a person-shaped presence in the archive; identity + staging resolve in the Q4 cluster (demigod valences live there: *jailer-of-dead-forms* vs. *failed-first-archivist*).

## §4 — The devourers (third force, registered + scoped)

Langolier-class **devourers-of-the-record** — forgetting-with-teeth (silverfish / acid-rot / Níðhöggr register). Four ruled properties:
1. **Outside the faction graph** — hostile to all courts and to the player; no diplomacy, no membership.
2. **They return the rescue's complicity** — harvest → breach → devourers come (the anti-alibi chain, §2).
3. **The frontier is inedible** — the unwritten shelf (OM-8) has nothing for them to eat. **Game thesis: the answer to forgetting is not archiving — it is writing the next page.**
4. **Genre precedent in the record itself:** Grim Dawn's Aetherials-vs-Chthonians three-way (two warring invaders + a force that wants the world unmade).

**MVP scope:** escape-sequence pressure rendering only — *heard before seen*. Full devourer faction-AI = post-MVP gamora work.

## §5 — The faction layer (Q36 + Q38, ruled; probe-verified)

- **Night-at-the-Museum grammar (Matt):** the factions war among themselves and see you as just another adversary. The faction war is the *theater*; the devourers are the *plot*.
- **Axis (Q38, empirically grounded):** **element-courts, k=5** — fire / cold / lightning / physical / chaos-poison, from `elem_raw` (100%/270, 0 NULL). The elrond coalescence probe (`elrond/notes/2026-07-21-faction-coalescence-probe.md`) found elements the ONLY axis with a **native opposition-wheel enmity** reaching Matt's min-degree ≥2 with zero forced edges; mechanical clustering is DEAD (Edition-I continuum — no joints to carve); court membership is therefore **mechanically diverse by construction** (the desired property).
- **Topology:** the 5-wheel gives each court exactly 2 natural enemies; **pentagram diagonals** are the authored edge-family where true three-way melees are wanted (degree 3–4 → triangles). Devourers sit outside the graph.
- **Faction rides the body (§23.3 composition, exact):** you are your latest body → *your latest allegiance*. As you save each kit you become its court's member; aggro inverts per body. **Agency guard:** faction governs who-attacks-whom, NEVER what your buttons do.
- **Fidelity clause (corpus-of-record, content side):** record kits present **element + stats exact-original per class**. `original_element` = surfacing existing `elem_raw` data — a presentation field, NOT a mapping law; **Q21 stands untouched** (engine element axis stays free for EMITTED kits; element-as-light stays the player-side law).
- **Emission:** race + faction **coalesce in the serial content engine** — race = realm-level emitted people (LLM fills narrow blanks, D7-safe); faction = computed court allegiance. Court sizes today (16–77) are a **seed condition, not a verdict** — the engine emits into thin courts.
- **The Larry arc:** the player is the only being who has BEEN every faction → the **pinnacle kit is authored by the one who lived every shelf** (explicit staging, OM-12).

## §6 — Geography: eras for shelves, elements for wings (OM-6 + Q38 rider, ruled)

- **Three descents, named:** **floor** (within a run) → **veil** (between run-strata) → **era** (the archive's deep axis — the genre's actual chronology; fine `eras` data 268/270, normalization only).
- **Depth = era · radial = court · center = contest.** Matt, verbatim: *"the Biomes/floors of the map morph to match each element and meet in the middle."* The war-graph becomes **literal level topology**: each court's wing borders its two wheel-enemies; the **contested middle** is where the diagonals cross — three-way melees get a *place* (NatM main-hall grammar: wings are dioramas at peace with themselves; the hall is where the museum fights).
- **Realm-coherence composition:** one wing = one people, no intra-wing war (ruled 2026-07-06); wars manifest as **INCURSIONS** in the middle.
- **The morph, genre-grounded:** gradient blending at wing seams (PoE Delve register); **hard tileset splice only where an incursion has TAKEN ground** (D3 rift register) — a frost shelf mid-thaw under fire-court occupation is a war-state read environmentally, zero UI.
- **Element-as-light guard:** the world wears the courts' colors; **the player's light stays the player's** — biome = territory, never a statement about the soul passing through it.
- **The frontier is UNWRITTEN** (OM-8): the deepest/newest shelf is blank — F-1's "kit death is not geography" honored; nothing is pre-doomed. **Glimpsed → filled** (OM-9): frontier content arrives by being written, not unlocked. **No era-art at launch** (OM-10): eras read through names/register/curation, not bespoke art sets.
