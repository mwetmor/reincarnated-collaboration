## § 0 — TL;DR

**Decision:** **Expand canonical-four (fire / water / wind / earth) to six substrates: + `lightning` + `holy` + `shadow`.** Phase-1 P1 schedule. VS2a/VS2b ship on canonical-four (no scope creep). Vocab freeze authorized as standing constraint until Phase-1 P1 ships.

Phase-1 P1 is the explicit ship gate. ## § 1 — The gate question, framed cleanly

**Question:** Should Reincarnated's seasonal-mechanical substrate layer expand from canonical-four (fire / water / wind / earth) to a larger substrate set, and if so, to what set and at what ship gate?

The question was surfaced as a recurring structural pressure across multiple recent workstreams:

- **Spirit-swap differentiation ceiling.** Per `project_design_intent` (load-bearing memory; 2026-05-08): "spirit-swap differentiation confirmed load-bearing." At four substrates, the body-swap pool surfaces at most four substrate-distinct archetypal forms per season's class rotation; beyond ~6-8 sessions the player has encountered all substrate-archetypes and the differentiation flattens. The cosmology's "many forms, many lives" framing (per `cosmology-reincarnated.md` § Earth Self) requires substrate breadth to support the meta-layer's accumulation arc — a Court of Forms whose substrate diversity caps at four exhausts its dramatic variety prematurely.
- **Earth-meta-layer luminance alignment.** Per `cosmology-reincarnated.md` § Ascension + `court-of-forms.md`: ascension is rendered as a *light-coded* ritual moment; the Earth Self's hub is the meta-layer of accumulated ascended identity; the Court is "what the Court remembers." The cosmology has been speaking luminance-language (ascension as ascent-to-light; Court-as-radiant-record) without a substrate that grounds that language at the seasonal-mechanical layer. This is precisely the Discipline #13 implicit-pillar drift pattern — the cosmology has an unenforced luminance commitment.
- **ARPG-positioning floor.** Per `gandalf-design-lineage.md` Layer 2 (Diablo founding-team lineage) + Layer 3 (PoE design philosophy): the ARPG genre's substrate convention is 6-8 elements minimum. D1 had fire/lightning/cold/poison/magic (5). D2 had fire/cold/lightning/poison/magic (5) with holy/dark/physical at the class layer. D3 had fire/cold/lightning/poison/arcane/holy (6). D4 has fire/cold/lightning/poison/shadow/physical (6). PoE has fire/cold/lightning/chaos/physical (5 damage types + light vs chaos cosmological axis). Last Epoch has fire/cold/lightning/necrotic/void/physical (6). Grim Dawn has 7 (fire/cold/lightning/aether/chaos/vitality/physical). **Four substrates is below the ARPG floor.** Players coming to Reincarnated from the genre would read four-substrate as either retro-deliberate (which the project is not) or under-developed (which is the actual perception risk).
- **Case-D math-impossibility as a recurring tax pattern.** Per `sprite-scale-math-impossibility-rulings-2026-05-16.md` Case D: god-of-lightning is a boss-tier chierit asset that cannot ship at VS2a because lightning is not a substrate (the slot routing fails); the resolution path (Matt-acquisition + Fire_Lord_Creativkind thunder-shift remap to mini-boss tier; per `per-slug-scale-lookup-path-a-prime-2026-05-16.md`) is recurring engineering tax against the absence of a lightning substrate. The chierit pack ships with lightning + light + shadow assets (lightning-ronin, light-valkyrie, shadow-stalker) that all hit the same impossibility-tax when the engine has no slot for them. **The substrate gap is a tax that compounds as the catalogue grows.**
- **Legolas reverse-VFX-audit TIER 1 finding.** Per the 2026-05-17 legolas reverse-audit `agentic_orchestration/dispatches/2026-05-17-legolas-track-a-reverse-vfx-to-pool-expansion-opportunity-audit.md`: the TIER 1 expansion candidates (high-coverage VFX assets without pool-substrate mapping) concentrate in **lightning / electric / luminance / shadow** territory. Pimen's lightning-spell-effect VFX is GREEN-list. CreativeKind's holy-radiance and shadow-tendril VFX are GREEN-list. The vendor inventory ALREADY supports the substrate expansion at no additional acquisition cost; the constraint is purely the pool's substrate set.

These five pressures converged 2026-05-17 in the substrate-expansion escalation message. Matt's Branch A confirmation resolves the gate.

## § 2 — Options considered

### § 2.1 Three primary options surveyed

| Option | Substrate set | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Stay at canonical-four** | fire / water / wind / earth (4) | No new infrastructure cost; no Phase-1 scope add; preserves existing rubric / matrix / trait-floor work | (1) Spirit-swap differentiation ceiling holds; (2) Cosmology luminance pillar stays unenforced (Discipline #13 drift compounds); (3) ARPG-genre floor breach persists; (4) Case-D tax recurs every time a lightning/luminance asset enters the catalogue; (5) Legolas TIER 1 expansion candidates remain unactionable; (6) **Drift-14 vocab-freeze pressure has no exit path** — `thunder` / `lightning` / `holy` / `shadow` stay perpetually frozen under wind/earth flex with no resolution, accumulating user-frustration over time as Matt or future pool-curation work tries to promote them. |
| **B — Expand to 6 (canonical-four + lightning + holy/shadow paired)** | fire / water / wind / earth / lightning / holy / shadow (6 substrates with holy/shadow as paired-luminance-axis) | (1) Resolves all five pressures named in § 1; (2) Hits ARPG-genre floor; (3) Honors the cosmology's existing luminance commitment; (4) Unblocks Case-D resolution + Legolas TIER 1 candidates; (5) Provides exit path for the vocab freeze; (6) **Paired-luminance treatment matches the Mirror/Body-swap/Passage triadic-opposition pattern already canonical in the game** | Adds Phase-1 P1 scope (resistance matrix 4×4 → 6×6 with luminance valence; trait-floor extension to 2 new classes; gear-affix gating extension; pool D1 re-score under `substrate_native` flag) — but Phase-1 P1 is precisely the gate designed for this kind of structural extension. |
| **C — Expand to 7 (canonical-four + lightning + holy + shadow + poison/acid)** | fire / water / wind / earth / lightning / holy / shadow / poison (7) | Matches D4 / Grim Dawn substrate breadth; gives the catalogue maximum flexibility | (1) Poison/acid is a **damage-flavor** in ARPG canon, not always a substrate (D2 poison is fire-tree adjacency; PoE chaos is poison-rooted; D3 demoted poison to wizard skill flavor in some patches); (2) Adds three new classes to the trait-floor architecture, not two; (3) Scope-creep risk on Phase-1 P1; (4) Poison/acid's VFX vendor coverage is weaker than lightning/luminance per legolas reverse-audit; (5) **Better to ship 6 substrates cleanly and revisit poison/acid as a Phase-1 P2 follow-on if the substrate-set still feels under-broad after Phase-1 P1 lands.** |

### § 2.2 Other expansion paths considered (rationale for declining)

- **Void / chaos as a substrate** — declined. PoE's chaos is load-bearing for its damage-type cosmology, but Reincarnated's existing cosmology (cosmology-reincarnated.md) doesn't have a void/chaos hook; introducing one would require new cosmological framing work. Reconsider only if a future cosmology revision opens a chaos/void cosmological lane.
- **Time / temporal as a substrate** — declined. Last Epoch's time mechanic is core to its identity; for Reincarnated to add it would require a competing cosmological frame against the Wheel-as-fate-mechanism that's already canonical. Cosmologically incoherent.
- **Metal / arcane as a substrate** — declined. Metal is best handled as an *earth-substrate sub-register* (per current pool entries `iron`, `gold`, `silver`, `copper`, `bronze`, `lead` — all earth-allow-list per `drift-14-pool-cull-decisions-2026-05-17.md` § 1.4). Arcane has no clear cosmological hook in Reincarnated's existing frame.
- **Physical as a distinct substrate** — declined as a *substrate*. Physical damage is already present as an attack-type modifier across all classes; making it a distinct substrate would require a Physical-archetype class that has no elemental/cosmological coloring, which conflicts with the spirit-swap-as-cosmological-form premise. Physical remains a *damage-type-modifier* available to all substrates, not a substrate itself.
- **Single-luminance substrate (collapsed holy+shadow into one)** — declined. Considered seriously because it would minimize Phase-1 P1 scope (one new substrate + lightning = 2 new vs 3 new). Rejected for three reasons: (1) **collapses the paired-opposition structure** that mirrors the Mirror/Body-swap/Passage triadic pattern (the cosmology's own design rhetoric is opposition-aware); (2) **loses ARPG-canon archetype fidelity** — D2 Paladin (holy) and D2 Necromancer (shadow/dark) are genre-iconic distinct archetypes, not luminance-mode-variants of one class; merging them at the substrate layer would force class-rotation work to artificially un-merge them; (3) **legolas reverse-audit shows distinct VFX coverage** for holy-radiance vs shadow-tendril — they're not visually one substrate.

## § 3 — Decision: Expand to 6

**Branch A — Expand canonical-four to 6 substrates: fire / water / wind / earth / lightning / holy / shadow.**

### § 3.1 Substrate naming canonical-locked

| Substrate | Canonical name | Considered alternatives | Why this name |
|---|---|---|---|
| fire | `fire` | — | unchanged from canonical-four |
| water | `water` | — | unchanged from canonical-four |
| wind | `wind` | — | unchanged from canonical-four |
| earth | `earth` | — | unchanged from canonical-four |
| **lightning** | **`lightning`** | `electric` / `thunder` | `electric` reads modern-technological (D&D 4e arcane register; Pokemon "electric" Pokemon — not the Reincarnated isekai-fantasy register). `thunder` is auditory (Drift-14 audit § 1.4 culled auditory wind entries for this same reason; auditory ≠ substance). `lightning` is **visual + genre-canon** — D2 Sorceress Lightning skill tree; D3 Wizard Lightning; D4 Sorceress Lightning; PoE Lightning damage type; Last Epoch Lightning; Grim Dawn Lightning. The genre converges on `lightning` as the canonical name across multiple decades. Adopting it inherits genre-fluency. |
| **holy** | **`holy`** | `divine` / `radiance` / `light` | `light` is too literal (player parses as illumination physics, not as substrate-class-identity); `radiance` is over-poeticized (PoE uses `radiance` as a Sentinel-mode flavor, not a damage type); `divine` is close but carries explicit-deity-coding that the Reincarnated cosmology (the impersonal Wheel; no god-figures) does not authorize. `holy` is genre-canon (D2 Paladin Holy auras + Holy Bolt; D3 Crusader Heaven's Fury; D4 Necromancer-vs-Holy class opposition; Last Epoch Sentinel Holy skills) and **carries devotional/sacred coding without locking in a deity**, which matches Reincarnated's cosmological neutrality. |
| **shadow** | **`shadow`** | `dark` / `umbra` / `void` | `dark` is too literal (parses as absence-of-light, not as substrate-identity); `umbra` is academic register (Latin loan; ARPG players don't reach for it — though D2 Necromancer's "Bone Spear" passes through Umbra in lore-tertiary text, the gameplay surface never uses the word); `void` is over-claimed (PoE void / Last Epoch Void = different specific mechanic; would generate cross-game confusion). `shadow` is genre-canon (D2 Assassin Shadow Discipline; D3 Demon Hunter Shadow Power; D4 Necromancer Shadow tree; PoE Shadow class; Solo Leveling Shadow Army — direct precedent for the Court-of-Forms framing per `court-of-forms.md`). **The Solo Leveling precedent is particularly load-bearing** because Reincarnated has already explicitly cited Solo Leveling's Shadow Army as canonical reference for the Court (gandalf-design-lineage.md Layer 5). Adopting `shadow` as a substrate strengthens that precedent's coherence. |

### § 3.2 Light/dark axis treatment — paired-substrate, not collapsed

**`holy` and `shadow` are two distinct substrates that form a *paired-luminance-axis*.** They are NOT collapsed into a single "luminance" substrate. They ARE treated as paired in the following structural ways:

- **Resistance matrix:** holy and shadow occupy *valenced* positions on a luminance axis. Mechanically this means: holy damage is amplified against shadow-substrate targets (and vice-versa); both have reduced effect against same-axis targets (holy weak against holy; shadow weak against shadow). This is the standard ARPG paired-opposition treatment (D3 Crusader vs Cultist; D4 Necromancer-vs-Angel encounters) — see § 5.1 for Phase-1 P1 deferred-design treatment of exact valence numbers.
- **Class-rotation:** holy and shadow are TWO distinct archetypal classes in the spirit-swap pool, not one class with two variants. Each has its own trait-floor, its own skill identity, its own VFX register.
- **Cosmological:** holy is the substrate of the *ascension moment* in the cosmology (the Court-of-Forms accumulates ascended forms; ascension is a holy-coded ritual per `cosmology-reincarnated.md` + `ascension-moment-ritual.md`). Shadow is the substrate of the *Passage* (the death-ritual; per `passage-moment-ritual.md`; the form-not-ascended is left behind in shadow). **This means holy and shadow are already cosmologically active in the game's rituals; the substrate expansion makes them mechanically present as well, closing a Discipline #13 enforcement gap.**

### § 3.3 Why 6 not 8 not 5

- **Not 5 (collapsed luminance):** declined for the three reasons in § 2.2 above.
- **Not 7+ (poison / void / etc.):** declined for scope-creep risk on Phase-1 P1; revisit as P2 candidate after the 6-substrate ship validates.
- **6 is the answer because it hits all five pressures named in § 1 with the minimum new infrastructure**. The 6 substrates land cleanly on existing ARPG-genre conventions (D3 had 6; D4 has 6; Last Epoch has 6); the trait-floor architecture extension is bounded; the resistance matrix extension is bounded (4×4 → 6×6, with paired-luminance valence as one new mechanic, not three); the pool D1 re-score is bounded (156 entries re-scored under one new flag).

## § 4 — Substrate cosmology integration

### § 4.1 How the 6 substrates integrate with the existing cosmology

**The canonical-four (fire / water / wind / earth)** map cleanly to the existing seasonal-vocabulary architecture (per `cosmology-reincarnated.md` § Seasonal Journey + doc 37 § 6 cipher architecture). Each season's per-vocabulary-cipher renders the canonical-four substrates with the season's specific texture (e.g., "Deep Trench" season's water → `tidewater` / `currents`; `fire` → `magma-vent`). No change.

**The two new substrates (lightning + holy + shadow as paired) integrate at three cosmological layers:**

1. **The Court of Forms** (per `court-of-forms.md`). The Court accumulates ascended forms. Pre-expansion, the Court could hold canonical-four forms only (with lightning / luminance forms structurally absent — a quiet ceiling the player would feel over a long meta-arc). Post-expansion, the Court can hold 6-substrate forms; **the meta-arc's substrate-diversity ceiling rises by 50% (4 → 6 first-class form-substrates)**. The Solo Leveling Shadow Army precedent strengthens — shadow-substrate ascended forms now occupy a first-class cosmological position rather than being a flavor-tier curiosity.
2. **The Ascension ritual** (per `ascension-moment-ritual.md`). Ascension was already holy-coded at the presentational layer ("the form rises into the Court's light"; the Spirit Guide voice line "your Court grows" is delivered in a luminous frame). Substrate expansion makes `holy` mechanically present, which means the ascension's holy-coding stops being mere presentational flavor and becomes *substrate-coherent*. A holy-substrate form ascending is a *natively luminous* event; the cosmology's presentational language is now substrate-grounded.
3. **The Passage ritual** (per `passage-moment-ritual.md`). The Passage is the death-ritual; the form-not-ascended is left behind. Pre-expansion, this was cosmologically vague (left behind *in what*?). Post-expansion, the answer is *in shadow* — the un-ascended form recedes into shadow-substrate, consistent with the Spirit Guide's "the form returns to the Wheel; what is left walks no more" language. Shadow is the substrate of the unchosen; holy is the substrate of the ascended. **The luminance axis becomes the cosmological grammar of choice-at-season-end.**

### § 4.2 Spirit-Guide voice integration

Per `spirit-guide-voice.md`: the Spirit Guide's voice register (Beatrice from Re:Zero) commits the Guide to be present, withholding, and ritually precise. Substrate expansion changes nothing about the voice register, but adds vocabulary the Guide may use when referencing lightning / holy / shadow substrates. The Guide's substrate-naming discipline (per `cosmology-reincarnated.md` — never speak canonical-four labels in player-facing prose post-Stage-3 cipher) extends to the two new substrates: lightning / holy / shadow each get their per-season ciphered vocabulary post-Stage-3, same architecture as canonical-four.

### § 4.3 Earth-Self meta-layer integration

Per `cosmology-reincarnated.md` § Earth Self + `project_earth_meta_layer` memory: the Earth Self is the persistent identity that accumulates the Court across many seasons. The substrate-expansion is **architecturally invisible at the Earth Self layer** — the Earth Self does not have a substrate; the Earth Self holds *forms* that have substrates. The expansion simply means the Court that accumulates can hold a broader substrate-diverse roster. **The Earth Self's narrative continuity is unaffected.**

### § 4.4 Trial-boss / Mirror-Trial / Passage integration

Per `cosmology-reincarnated.md` § The Trial: each season's three Trials offer the body-swap-path vs. Mirror-Trial-path choice. Substrate expansion means: (a) Trial bosses can now be lightning / holy / shadow substrate (broadens the dramatic-substrate variety of the act-culminating encounters); (b) Mirror-Trial reflections of the player work identically regardless of substrate (the player's current substrate is reflected); (c) the Passage choice (per `passage-moment-ritual.md`) is unchanged.

**One cosmological refinement to capture:** Trial-boss substrate selection should *avoid same-substrate as the player's current class* per the spirit-swap differentiation pillar — extending this rule from canonical-four to all 6 substrates is trivial. (This is operational, not load-bearing; surface to Phase-1 P1 generation-design.)

## § 5 — Deferred questions (Phase-1 P1 scoping)

The substrate expansion decision is locked. Operational architecture extension work is deferred to Phase-1 P1, scoped as the questions below. Each question is bounded and architecturally tractable; none are open-ended research.

### § 5.1 Resistance matrix shape

**Current state:** Implicit 4×4 with per-element resistance values applied as percentage damage reduction on incoming attacks of matching element. (Per file 28 § B6 + § B10; per `engine-balance-stewardship.md`.)

**Phase-1 P1 question:** Extend to 6×6 with paired-luminance valence handling.

- **Symmetry shape:** four canonical substrates stay symmetric (no paired-opposition; fire is not amplified vs water in current architecture; this is correct and unchanged). Holy and shadow form a **valenced paired axis** — holy damage is amplified vs shadow-substrate targets, shadow damage is amplified vs holy-substrate targets, each is reduced vs same-axis targets. Recommended magnitudes (operational; Matt-approval not needed at this doc): +25% damage on opposed-luminance match; -25% damage on same-luminance match. Standard ARPG-genre paired-opposition default.
- **Lightning's valence:** lightning is **un-paired** within the substrate set. No amplification or reduction against any other substrate; lightning behaves like the canonical-four for resistance purposes. **Rationale:** introducing lightning-water valence (the obvious "lightning conducts through water" hook) would invite a cascade of physics-style elemental interactions (fire melts ice → water; wind fans fire → fire-burst; etc.) that the canonical-four explicitly does not have. Reincarnated has not committed to elemental-physics interactions; introducing them via lightning-water would Discipline-#13-drift the canonical-four's no-physics-interactions implicit pillar. Defer the lightning-water valence question to a future cosmology revision if Matt wants to add elemental-physics later.
- **Math-before-code requirement (Discipline #1):** before code lands, the 6×6 resistance matrix needs an authored numeric specification + a worked DPS-against-each-substrate analysis at L1 / L25 / L50 to confirm no class becomes structurally over- or under-powered relative to existing canonical-four balance. Authorship: gamora seam, design-then-build pattern.

### § 5.2 Trait-floor architecture extension

**Current state:** Per `project_trait_architecture` memory + file 32 § 12: per-class intrinsic trait pool (5-10 traits, floors at L1/12/25/38, converge at L50). Architecture currently spans canonical-four classes plus class-rotation work in progress.

**Phase-1 P1 question:** Extend trait-floor architecture to 2 new substrate-archetypal classes (lightning archetype + holy archetype + shadow archetype = 3 new classes; corrected from "2 new classes" in operational ask because holy and shadow are distinct classes per § 3.2).

- **Architecture extension:** each new class gets the same intrinsic trait pool structure (5-10 traits + 4-floor cadence + L50 convergence). No architectural change required; pure content authorship.
- **Substrate-coherent trait design:** lightning traits should lean *velocity / chain / arc* (genre-canon — D2 Sorceress Lightning chain mechanic; PoE Lightning Strike chain); holy traits should lean *radiance / consecration / cleanse* (genre-canon — D2 Paladin Holy auras; D4 Necromancer-anti Holy); shadow traits should lean *concealment / corruption / drain* (genre-canon — D2 Assassin Shadow Discipline; D3 Demon Hunter Shadow Power; Solo Leveling shadow-army summoning). Authorship: gandalf-canonical-story seam (intrinsic trait design is design-direction territory); gamora implementation.
- **Gear-affix trait rolls:** per `project_trait_architecture`, gear-affix trait rolls are element/mechanic-gated. Phase-1 P1 must extend the gating logic to include lightning / holy / shadow gating. No new architecture; gating-table extension.

### § 5.3 Gear-affix gating

**Current state:** Per file 17 + `project_trait_architecture`: gear affixes roll element-gated traits (e.g., fire-flagged armor rolls fire-coherent affixes only).

**Phase-1 P1 question:** Which affixes gate to lightning / holy / shadow, and how does the gear-roll pool expand?

- **Affix-pool extension:** each new substrate gets its own affix pool sized comparably to canonical-four affix pools. Authorship: design-direction (gandalf surfaces substrate-coherent affix recommendations); gamora implementation.
- **Cross-substrate affix coherence:** some affixes may be substrate-neutral (e.g., "+all damage"); these don't gate. Substrate-flagged affixes must gate cleanly. Coherence-check: no canonical-four affix should silently become substrate-incoherent with the expansion. Surface to jack-ryan engineering-disciplines pass at Phase-1 P1 review (Discipline #13 instance check).
- **Gear-roll-pool size:** approximate 1.5× expansion (since 6/4 = 1.5; affix pool grows proportionally). Operational; Phase-1 P1 scoping confirms.

### § 5.4 Spirit-swap differentiation extension

**Current state:** Per `project_design_intent` (load-bearing memory): spirit-swap differentiation is the load-bearing pillar for body-swap meta-progression.

## § 6.5 — Phase-1 P1 success criterion (post-Legolas-literature-pass amendment)

**Authored 2026-05-17 as amendment** following Legolas Mode A literature pass `agentic_orchestration/research/knowledge/diversity-architecture-literature-pass-2026-05-17.md` Finding 2 (combinatorial thinness > starvation as dominant failure mode in shipped ARPGs).

### § 6.5.1 — The genre reality

The literature pass surfaced a structural finding that shapes how Phase-1 P1 success should be measured. Across mature ARPG genre shops:

- **Grim Dawn's 105 dual-class pairings collapse to ~5-8 dominant build patterns in actual play.**
- **Path of Exile 2 with 36 ascendancy classes still shows Deadeye at 32-34% of top players.**
- **Diablo IV class-fundamentals data shows similar Pareto-collapse to small dominant clusters.**

**Mechanical validity does not guarantee breadth of play distribution.** Even when the architecture produces N mechanically-distinct viable archetypes, players cluster on a small subset. This is the genre operating normally, not a failure of generation.

### § 6.5.2 — The corrected success criterion

For Phase-1 P1 to be judged successful, the criterion is **not** "all 21 archetypes (7 substrate-mechanical-identities × 3 roles) are equally played" — that is genre-impossible and not the architecture's promise.

The criterion is:

> **Of the archetypes produced by the diversity architecture, the dominant cluster of 5-8 actually-played archetypes is *meaningfully different in mechanical shape*, not cosmetic skins of the same shape.**

Three measurable sub-criteria:

1. **Mechanical-shape diversity in the dominant cluster.** Players cluster on 5-8 archetypes; those 5-8 should have demonstrably distinct kit shapes (geometry distribution, role-orientation, cooldown profile, ailment family) — not 5-8 fire-mage variants.

2. **Substrate representation in the dominant cluster.** At least 4-5 of the 7 substrates are represented in the dominant cluster. (If only fire + water + earth + wind appear and lightning/holy/shadow never reach the cluster, the substrate expansion is mechanically valid but functionally vestigial.)

3. **Role representation in the dominant cluster.** At least 3 of 4 role-orientations (damage / support / control / hybrid) are represented in the dominant cluster.

### § 6.5.3 — What this does NOT mean

- **Not "force equal play distribution."** Player cluster behavior is emergent from build optimization, genre familiarity, content pacing, and meta-discovery. The architecture cannot — and should not try to — force equal distribution.
- **Not "reject any archetype that doesn't reach the cluster."** Some archetypes will be played by 5% of players. That is genre-normal. The architecture's job is to *enable* their viability, not their popularity.
- **Not "ship 21 archetypes and stop."** Future P2/P3 expansion (poison/acid; additional roles; substrate-role combinations not in P1) continues to broaden the cluster's outer edge.

### § 6.5.4 — Measurement infrastructure implication

Layer 5 (telemetry feedback) is the natural measurement layer for these sub-criteria. Per-season player-build telemetry can directly measure:

- Dominant-cluster composition (which 5-8 archetypes did most players play?)
- Mechanical-shape distance within the cluster (are they actually different shapes?)
- Substrate representation (which substrates appear in the cluster?)
- Role representation (which roles appear in the cluster?)

**This positions Layer 5 as both the diversity-pressure mechanism AND the success-measurement mechanism.** The same telemetry that pushes converging archetypes apart in next-season generation also reports whether the architecture is delivering meaningful diversity. **Layer 5 is the architecture's observability layer.** Worth holding when scope-cutting tempts cuts to Layer 5.

### § 6.5.5 — Phase-1 P1 retrospective protocol

When Phase-1 P1 ships, the retrospective question is NOT "are all archetypes equally played?" but:

> **"Is the dominant played cluster of 5-8 archetypes mechanically distinct, substrate-diverse (≥4-5 of 7 substrates), and role-diverse (≥3 of 4 roles)?"**

Yes → ship validated; iterate via Layer-5 telemetry pressure for next-season pushing-apart of any convergence detected.
No → diagnose which sub-criterion failed; surface to gandalf for substrate identity declaration refinement, gamora for composition-layer adjustment, or knight-rider for cascade re-sequencing.

This protocol is what jack-ryan Gate 2 review consumes when Phase-1 P1 work returns for validation.
