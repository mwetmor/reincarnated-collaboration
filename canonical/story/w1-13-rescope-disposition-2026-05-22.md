## 3. BDI + T4 architectural alignment preserved

A critical disposition concern: would the rescope shift in W1.13 weaken its alignment with the BDI formalism (`canonical/story/build-defining-resonance-formula-2026-05-21.md`) or the Tier 4 architecture defaults (`canonical/story/tier-4-architecture-defaults-2026-05-22.md`, T4-A this morning)?

**Disposition: NO, alignment strengthens.**

### 3.1 BDI alignment

W1.13 implements per-class-per-season skill tree node population + multi-dim convergence. The BDI formalism predicts rank-3 builds require **interaction-term dominance** over linear terms in the WR landscape. Interaction-term dominance is structurally inexpressible in scalar-modifier-only space — you cannot have a γ_{abc} > β_{ab} > α_a relationship along a single scalar dimension. Multi-dim convergence is the dimensional precondition for BDI rank-3 expression.

The rescope does not change this. If anything, the Scenario B disposition CLARIFIES the BDI alignment — the W1.13 architectural fix's primary value-add is **supporting rank-3 substrate-richness expression**, not "discharge a 42% floor-lock pathology." The BDI hypothesis tests (H1-H5) will run against the post-W1.13 archive to measure whether rank-3 γ-dominance materializes empirically.

### 3.2 T4-A alignment

The Tier 4 architecture defaults (per T4-A this morning) commit:
- 1 signature capstone (rank-3 completer; gear-anchored when `signature_gear_archetype` present)
- 1-3 secondary capstones (rank-2 modulators)
- Hand-authored catalogue ~30-50 keystones
- Each catalogue keystone designed as third leg of a known high-β substrate-pair

W1.13 implements the convergence-time selection of Tier 4 keystones as a **DISCRETE CATEGORICAL choice** per chain. Without W1.13's multi-dim convergence, the categorical selection step has no convergence machinery — Tier 4 keystones would have to be statically pre-selected at generation, which collapses the BDI rank-completer mechanic into a fixed-template architecture (which is exactly what W0.2 removed).

The Scenario B disposition does NOT touch this alignment. T4-A keystone architecture continues to require W1.13's multi-dim convergence to operate.
