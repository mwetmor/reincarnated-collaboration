## 0. TL;DR

The player's first creation moment is a scene: **Earth avatar standing on a hill on Earth, next to an ambiguous spirit form, with the celestial sphere of constellations overhead.** The player chooses their spirit/kit via one of two cognitively distinct paths that converge on the same outcome:

- **Path L (Lasso the sky)** — twirl the celestial sphere over the player's head; spirit-lasso grabs a nebula / constellation / cluster directly. For players who want to jump in.
- **Path I (Drop ingredients)** — drop substrate primitives (potions/ingredients) into the spirit form; the celestial sphere progressively focuses in on the convergent constellation. For players who want to take their time.

Both paths consume the same substrate (atomic-substrate-registry primitives + hypothesis-flow constellations + cosmograph-pivot kit-as-constellation architecture). The ambiguous spirit form **transforms in real-time** as either path progresses — visible compositional feedback solves the blank-canvas onboarding problem viscerally.

**The cosmograph IS the night sky** — not a metaphor, an in-fiction reality. This locks the **spherical shell geometry** for the UE 3D production cosmograph: player stands on Earth (below the sphere); the celestial sphere of constellations is the interior surface overhead; the 3D nebula context (deeper cosmos, distant galactic structures, atmospheric depth) lives beyond the sphere as visual context.

This is a **load-bearing architectural commitment.** It composes prior parked threads (Earth Self meta-layer + spirit guide as future-self + form library accumulation + cosmograph as creation moment) at the most load-bearing scene in the entire player journey.

## 2. The architecture

### 2.1 The scene

**Setting:** a hill on Earth at night. Pastoral or contemplative atmosphere — exact aesthetic deferred to art direction but the emotional register is "moment of quiet possibility before transformation."

**Player avatar:** the Earth Self — the player's persistent identity across reincarnations. Anchored here at the first creation moment (Earth Self meta-layer commitment lands HERE rather than being deferred to post-Phase-0 events).

**Companion presence:** an ambiguous spirit form standing beside the player. Visually: luminescent mist-cloud roughly humanoid in silhouette; translucent; not yet committed to any specific form or color or identity. This is the future-self / ascended-spirit-guide-in-becoming — the canonical 17 spirit guide architecture made visible at its undetermined state.

**Sky:** the celestial sphere of constellations overhead. Not a backdrop — the cosmograph itself. The player's POV is from below the interior of the sphere, looking up. Constellations are visible on the sphere's inside surface as bounded clusters of element-tinted stars per the primitive-as-star + kit-as-constellation architectural lock (cosmograph-pivot § 9). The 3D nebula context (volumetric clouds, distant galactic structures, deep-space atmospheric depth) lives BEYOND the sphere as visual context — visible at sphere edges and through transparency between constellations.

### 2.2 Earth-Self / Spirit-Form scene composition

```
                    [celestial sphere — constellations]
                  ╲ ── kit-as-constellation regions ── ╱
                   ╲ ─ element-tinted bounded clusters ╱
              [3D nebula context beyond sphere]
              [3D nebula context beyond sphere]
                          ▲ player POV ▲
                          │ looking up │
                          │            │
                  [Earth avatar]  [ambiguous spirit form]
                         │              │
                ═══════════════════════════════
                         hill on Earth (ground plane)
```

Player POV is at Earth ground level looking up. Direct manipulation of the celestial sphere via "twirl the night sky" gesture (drag-to-rotate). Spirit form is adjacent in scene space; player can interact with it directly (drop ingredients onto it) or indirectly (lasso a constellation; spirit form transforms toward it).

### 2.3 The two paths

Both paths consume the same substrate. Both converge on the same outcome (a kit selection). They differ in cognitive model + entry mechanic + player archetype served.

**Path L — Lasso the Sky (subtractive-from-view)**

Mental model: "Here is the whole sky. Show me what I grab."

Mechanic:
1. Player twirls the celestial sphere (drag-to-rotate gesture) to orient the desired region overhead
2. Player draws a spirit-lasso on the inner sphere surface — a polygonal selection in screen-projected space at current rotation
3. Substrate-trace lookup resolves which constellation(s) match the lassoed primitive set per cosmograph-pivot § 9.4 / verdict § 4.3 (constellation-overlap composite score: `0.4 × coverage_fraction + 0.3 × density_score + 0.3 × β-weighted overlap`)
4. Matched constellation highlights; spirit form takes on that kit's identity (visual transformation begins)
5. Player can refine: re-lasso, expand/shrink, switch regions; spirit form continuously transforms
6. Player confirms when satisfied → materialization cinematic fires; spirit form solidifies into the selected kit's avatar appearance

Player archetype served: speed-runner / jump-in / visual-gestalt / intuitive. The kit-as-discovery metaphor in its directly-experienced form.

**Path I — Drop Ingredients (additive constraints)**

Mental model: "Tell me what I want. Show me which spirit matches."

Mechanic:
1. Player has access to a curated palette of substrate ingredients (atomic-substrate-registry primitives presented as potions/vials/tokens). Initial palette scope is a refinement question per § 4 below.
2. Player drops an ingredient onto the spirit form (drag-and-drop gesture)
3. The celestial sphere responds: constellations matching that ingredient brighten; non-matching fade
4. Player drops a second ingredient: sphere narrows further — only constellations matching BOTH brighten
5. Iterative narrowing as ingredients accumulate. Spirit form solidifies progressively (translucent mist becomes more defined with each ingredient)
6. Eventually one constellation (or tight cluster) is the convergent match — sphere has "focused in"
7. Player can backtrack: remove an ingredient → sphere re-expands
8. Player confirms when satisfied → materialization cinematic fires (same as Path L)

Player archetype served: build-crafter / take-my-time / analytical / deliberate. The substrate-led composition logic made tangible.

**Path composition (optional advanced UX):** allow players to START with lasso (narrow to a region) then REFINE with ingredients (filter within the region). Maximum expressiveness without breaking either path's standalone integrity. Refinement question per § 4 below.

### 2.4 The ambiguous spirit form (canvas + feedback)

The spirit form is the **visual canvas** for the creation moment. Its state at any moment reflects the player's compositional progress.

**Initial state — ambiguous:**
- Luminescent mist-cloud, roughly humanoid silhouette
- Translucent; soft edges; no committed color or features
- Subtle internal motion (particle drift; soft glow pulses)
- Conveys: "becoming, undefined, possibility"

**Mid-state — partially defined (during composition):**
- Color saturation increases as element-primitives commit
- Silhouette sharpens as weapon-form / archetype primitives commit
- Distinguishing features appear (visible weapon outline; armor hints; element-tinted aura)
- Conveys: "taking shape, not yet final, in progress"

**Final state — fully defined (at confirm):**
- Full avatar appearance of the selected kit
- Element-tinted aura matching kit's primary element
- Weapon visible; armor visible; faction indicators visible
- Conveys: "this is the spirit you become"

The spirit form's transformation is **continuously responsive** to player action. No delay, no "loading" state — every lasso refinement or ingredient drop produces immediate visual feedback in the spirit. This is the load-bearing onboarding-engagement mechanism: the player ALWAYS sees their compositional progress.

### 2.5 The materialization cinematic (confirm-time payoff)

When the player confirms their selection, the creation moment transitions to the materialization cinematic:

- Spirit form fully solidifies into the selected kit's avatar appearance
- Camera may briefly leave the celestial sphere — fly out into the 3D nebula context for cinematic depth
- Selected constellation may visually "descend" from the sphere into the spirit's chest / aura (compositional metaphor: the spirit takes the constellation into themselves)
- Earth avatar reacts — visible recognition / acknowledgment of the spirit's new form
- Transitions into gameplay onboarding (first combat, first tutorial encounter, etc. — TBD per WS2 commission scoping)

The materialization cinematic vocabulary is what the original Veo materialization prompt (per cosmograph-pivot § 7 PARKED — recontextualized as post-confirm cinematic-payoff playbook) feeds. The Veo work is now confirmed-fit-for-purpose at this specific moment.

### 2.6 Spherical shell geometry lock

Implicit in the scene architecture: the cosmograph is rendered as a **spherical shell** in UE 3D — the celestial sphere's interior surface is where constellations live; the 3D nebula context is the exterior + visual depth beyond. This locks the spherical-shell-INSIDE-3D-nebula-context architecture surfaced in the 2026-06-07 design dialogue.

**Geometry properties:**
- Sphere radius: TBD per WS2 — must accommodate ~1000+ constellations at adequate angular separation while remaining "reachable" feeling (player feels they could touch the sky)
- Star distribution on sphere surface: spherical Fibonacci point distribution OR Poisson-disk sampling on the sphere — produces continuous-density-varied visual without uniform-grid mechanical feel
- Element-region blending: element-tinted constellations cluster on sphere surface; element regions blend at boundaries through "twilight" intermediates (e.g., fire-shadow boundary has dim crimson constellations; water-ice boundary has pale cyan)
- LOD architecture: spherical mipmap pattern (Google Earth / Cesium 3D Tiles precedent); Level 0 = visible faction halos + mechanic-family centroids; Level 1 = full constellation centroids; Level 2 = drill into individual stars within constellations
- 3D nebula context: VDB volumetric clouds beyond the sphere; distant galactic structures (filaments, nebulae, distant galaxies); atmospheric depth as compositional dimension

**Why spherical shell wins (recap from design dialogue):**
- It IS the night sky (in-fiction reality, not metaphor)
- Lasso UX preserved (sphere surface projects to 2D polygon at any rotation; no depth ambiguity)
- Zero teaching cost (universal night sky metaphor)
- Substrate-led discipline at rendering layer (finite spherical surface matches finite kit corpus honestly)
- Every region equally visible (rotation-egalitarian)
- Bounded space without pretending infinity
- Composes with Earth-below-looking-up player POV natively

## 4. Refinement questions deferred to subsequent design dialogue

The architectural commitment is locked. Five refinement questions remain for subsequent design dialogue. None block WS2 commission scoping; all affect WS2 implementation specifics.

### 4.1 Q1 — Ingredient inventory at creation moment

**Question:** What ingredients does the player have access to at first creation moment?

**Options:**
- (a) All ~570 Layer 0 primitives → overwhelming; rejected
- (b) Curated palette of ~20-40 high-leverage primitives → recommended starting position
- (c) Progressively unlocked via prior gameplay → requires prior gameplay to exist (chicken-and-egg at first creation moment)
- (d) Predefined "spell" or "archetype" presets that compose primitives behind the scenes → lower expressiveness but higher accessibility

**Gandalf lean:** (b) curated palette. Mix of element primitives + attribute primitives + weapon-form-token primitives + scaling-pattern primitives + a few "wildcard" archetype hint primitives. Aim for compositional variety without overwhelming.

**Empirical-evidence trigger for refinement:** WS2 prototype playtest data; if ~20-40 ingredient palette feels insufficient OR overwhelming, adjust accordingly. Cycle 15+ playtest validation.

**Son's input requested:** if your son has specific ideas about what feels like an "ingredient" vs raw material — what should be in the player's hand for dropping onto the spirit?

### 4.2 Q2 — Convergence criterion for Path I

**Question:** At what point does the sphere "focus in" on a constellation in Path I?

**Options:**
- (a) Continuous narrowing with player-controlled confirm — player drops ingredients; sphere progressively focuses; player clicks "this is my spirit" when satisfied. Allows backtracking (remove ingredients).
- (b) Threshold-snapping — when ingredient composition uniquely identifies one constellation, the sphere snaps focus to it; "you've chosen X"
- (c) Tiered convergence — first ingredient narrows to ~100 constellations; second to ~30; third to ~10; fourth to ~3; fifth to 1. Player sees explicit narrowing milestones.

**Gandalf lean:** (a) continuous narrowing + player-controlled confirm. Maximum flexibility; supports backtracking; honors player agency. Visual cue (a hard-glowing "MATCH FOUND" indicator) when composition uniquely identifies one constellation.

**Empirical-evidence trigger:** WS2 prototype playtest data on convergence pacing + player satisfaction.

### 4.3 Q3 — Path composition (lasso + ingredient hybrid)

**Question:** Can players START with lasso (narrow to a region) then REFINE with ingredients (filter within the region)? Or are paths pure alternatives?

**Options:**
- (a) Pure alternatives — pick one path, no mixing
- (b) Lasso-then-ingredient compose — lasso narrows space; ingredients filter within
- (c) Ingredient-then-lasso compose — ingredients narrow space; lasso refines within
- (d) Full bidirectional compose — both paths interleave freely

**Gandalf lean:** (d) full bidirectional compose. Maximum expressiveness. Players who prefer one path will use it predominantly; advanced players can interleave. Same substrate; same convergence logic.

**Empirical-evidence trigger:** WS2 prototype playtest data on whether compose-mode is used / valued / confused.

### 4.4 Q4 — Earth-avatar pre-existence

**Question:** Is there an earlier creation moment for the Earth avatar before the spirit-selection scene?

**Matt verbatim 2026-06-07:** "Maybe this was an earlier creation moment leading up to this."

- (b) No earlier scene: Earth avatar is default-presented at the spirit-selection scene. Skip Earth customization entirely; focus all onboarding weight on spirit selection.
- (c) Minimal earlier scene: name + maybe one cosmetic choice. ~30 seconds.

**Gandalf lean:** (a) brief earlier scene. Establishes Earth avatar as meaningful identity before spirit selection adds a second identity layer. Composes with form-library accumulation narrative (Earth Self ≠ Spirit; both exist).

**Empirical-evidence trigger:** WS2 prototype playtest data on onboarding pacing.

**Son's input requested:** if your son has thoughts on the Earth avatar scene — what does the player do there? How long is it? What's the emotional tone?
