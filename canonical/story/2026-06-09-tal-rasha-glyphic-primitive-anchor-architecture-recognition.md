## 1. The recognition

### 1.1 What Matt named (verbatim)

> "For the primitive clusters, I feel like maybe an archaic numbering system or language or symbology could fit. This could be a symbol sitting in the night sky - like the symbols on Tal Rasha's tomb (Diablo 2). This would be an obvious 'sign' that a player should 'sign' it into the ipad!"

### 1.2 What gandalf recognized in this

Five load-bearing design insights pack into Matt's framing:

**1. The "sign" overloading is intentional and architecturally rich.**

| "Sign" reading | Semantic role |
|---|---|
| Sign as **noun-celestial** (astrological sign; sign of the zodiac) | The thing in the sky |
| Sign as **verb-input** (sign the glyph; trace the sigil with finger / stylus / mouse) | The player's input gesture |
| Sign as **signature** (signing one's name / commitment) | The act of committing to a build |
| Sign as **the Sign** (proper-noun mystical / portent / omen) | The mythic weight of the moment |

One word carries four cohesive meaning layers at the player-facing UX surface. This is **semantic over-determination of the load-bearing kind** — not coincidence, not pun, but layered meaning where each reading reinforces the others. The player draws the sign into the iPad → they are signing the glyph → they have committed (signed) to a build → they have invoked the sign (omen / portent / celestial figure) of their spirit's journey.

**2. The two-layer cognitive separation gets natural visual register support.**

The 2026-06-08 mobile-Claude dialogue established two-layer + buffer-space architecture (primitive-anchors + kit-clusters with deliberate empty-space buffer). The architectural pattern was sound but the **visual register distinction** between primitive-anchors and kit-anchors was unsolved. If both layers are figurative constellations (kits = constellations + primitives = larger constellations), the visual distinction is "more vs less stars" or "different shape" — readable but not categorical.

Matt's glyph-vs-constellation framing solves the visual-register problem categorically:
- **Constellation layer (kits):** figurative star-figures (lifelike, recognizable, mythically named per cultural-tradition — Leo, Dragon, Eagle, Bear)
- **Glyph layer (primitives):** abstract symbolic forms (geometric, archaic, distinctly non-figurative — Cuneiform wedge, Mayan glyph block, Norse rune, Theban letter, alchemical sigil)

Players read the difference without explanation. **Figurative ≠ symbolic.** The cognitive separation is pre-loaded by the visual register.

**3. Tal Rasha tomb sigil (Diablo 2 Act 2) is the iconic precedent.**

Diablo 2 Act 2 quest: find the true tomb of Tal Rasha among 7 false tombs. The player has the Horadric Scroll showing a glyph; each tomb entrance has a sigil; player matches glyph-to-sigil to identify the true tomb. The Horadric sigils are abstract, geometric, ancient-feeling, distinctly readable, mythically weighted (Horadric mage-order vocabulary).

**Properties that transfer to Reincarnated:**
- Abstract symbolic (not figurative) → categorical visual distinction
- Ancient-feeling / elder-vocabulary tone → mythic weight without horoscope baggage
- Distinctly readable / recognizable → players can match-and-select without ambiguity
- Mage-order / elder-tradition framing → composes with Reincarnated's cosmological frame (Earth Self viewing the sky; spirit guide as future self speaking back through reincarnations)

Other genre precedents in the same visual register: Allagan glyphs (FFXIV Crystal Tower / Eden raid sigils), Sheikah glyphs (Zelda BOTW / TOTK shrine markers), Vault dwellers' wall-graffiti glyphs (Fallout), Magic the Gathering mana symbols (categorical color identity), 7th-Continent symbol-language (board game). All operate in the same "abstract elder-symbolic" register.

**4. Cross-cultural archaic glyph/symbology systems are substrate-rich.**

If Branch A fires, the primitive-glyph layer needs a substrate to draw from. Cross-cultural archaic glyph/symbology systems provide abundance per Discipline #41 (substrate-led):

| Glyph system | Cultural-tradition | Volume / structure |
|---|---|---|
| **Cuneiform** | Sumerian / Akkadian / Babylonian / Assyrian | 600+ signs; pictographic origins → abstract evolution |
| **Egyptian Hieroglyph** | Egyptian | 1,000+ glyphs; pictographic + phonetic + determinative |
| **Mayan glyph blocks** | Maya | ~800 glyphs; logosyllabic; block-composed |
| **Norse Runes (Elder Futhark / Younger Futhark / Anglo-Saxon)** | Germanic / Norse | 24 / 16 / 33 runes per system |
| **Theban alphabet (Honorius alphabet)** | Medieval European magic tradition | 26 letters; magical correspondence to Latin |
| **Enochian** | Renaissance European (John Dee + Edward Kelley) | 21 letters; angelic-language tradition |
| **Solomonic sigils** | Medieval European magic tradition | 72 sigils (Goetia) + planetary sigils |
| **Khipu** | Inca / Andean | 3D knot-based recording system; substrate-rich for non-2D-glyph experimentation |
| **I Ching trigrams / hexagrams** | Chinese | 8 trigrams / 64 hexagrams; binary-composed |
| **Bagua** | Chinese (Taoist) | 8 trigram arrangements + cosmological correspondence |
| **Planetary sigils (Western occult)** | Medieval / Renaissance European | 7 planetary + 12 zodiacal sigils |
| **Alchemical symbols** | Medieval / Renaissance European | ~50 element + process symbols |

Per Discipline #41 (substrate-led; pre-authored taxonomy interrogation), **the primitive-glyph corpus is not pre-imposed by gandalf** — it emerges from a Legolas Mode A follow-on commission scoping cross-cultural archaic glyph/symbology systems. The 20 primitive families in atomic-substrate-registry get glyphic representation derived from cross-cultural substrate rather than gandalf-authored sigils. Same substrate-led discipline as the zodiac pivot.

**5. The Path I (drop-ingredients) creation mechanism gets a glyph-as-ingredient surface.**

Earth-Avatar Creation Moment Architecture § 2.4 establishes dual-creation:
- **Path L (Lasso the sky):** twirl celestial sphere + spirit-lasso grabs nebula/constellation/cluster (jump-in players)
- **Path I (Drop ingredients):** drop substrate primitives into spirit form; sky progressively focuses on convergent constellation (take-time players)

Under Branch A, Path I gets a **literal visual representation of the primitive-ingredients**: each primitive is a glyph the player drags/draws from the sky into the spirit form. The player's input gesture matches the visual register — sign the glyph in the sky → place it in the spirit → spirit form transforms.

Path L gets a parallel literal representation: lasso the figurative constellation → spirit takes the constellation's identity.

The two paths consume **same substrate via different visual surfaces**:
- Path L: figurative-constellation surface (kits as star-figures)
- Path I: symbolic-glyph surface (primitives as ingredient-glyphs)

Players self-select by cognitive style (figurative-recognition vs symbolic-composition) but reach the same convergent outcome.

## 6. Anti-patterns to guard against (post-Branch-A direction)

- ❌ Gandalf authoring glyph sigils from imagination instead of substrate-led corpus
- ❌ Pre-imposing a fixed glyph count per primitive ("each primitive gets exactly 1 glyph") — substrate may vote for compositional glyph-stacks (Khipu-style 3D-symbolic; I-Ching-style binary-composition)
- ❌ Collapsing distinct glyph traditions to broader-geographic labels (Sumerian + Akkadian + Babylonian + Assyrian Cuneiform are DISTINCT; preserve per-tradition tagging)
- ❌ Modern-fictional glyph systems (Allagan FFXIV, Sheikah Zelda) as substrate — these inform DESIGN (visual register reference) but are not substrate per cross-cultural substrate-led discipline
- ❌ Cultural-sensitivity neglect — some archaic glyph systems carry restricted/sacred components (Adinkra has ceremonial subsets; Solomonic sigils have grimoire-tradition gradations; sigil-magic systems have practitioner-knowledge-restricted subsets); same protocol as zodiac commission applies
- ❌ Glyph-system count over completeness — substrate cleanliness > volume; flag higher and exclude restricted content (per zodiac commission § 4.2 protocol)
