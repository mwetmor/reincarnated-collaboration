## § 2 — Why Option γ specifically (over α and β)

### § 2.1 — Why not Option α (strict: all 7 hypothesis tags must fire first)

Strict Option α reads § 6.5 as "tag `hive-rebuild/v1.0-engine-rebuild-complete` ... after all seven workstreams' hypothesis tests pass." It treats all hypothesis tags as the same category of gate.

**This conflates two semantically-distinct gates:**

- **Engine-side substrate completion** (the work the hive is empowered to complete under autonomous-operation) — proven by 5 of 7 workstreams' hypothesis-test tags firing.
- **Player-experience validation** (the work that structurally requires Matt to play) — gated on Matt's wind-down session, which by protocol § 4.9 is the ONLY signal that returns Matt to the loop.

Holding v1.0 strictly until Matt's playtest:
- Blocks the engine-rebuild milestone tag on a non-engine-side gate
- Structurally bottlenecks VS2a continuation (per dispatch § 6.5 explicit ordering) on Matt's wind-down
- Forces the hive to either (a) wait for Matt mid-batch (violating § 4.9 "Matt re-enters only at wind-down — none of these are endpoints. They are milestones. The hive proceeds to the next prioritized work autonomously."), or (b) stop work entirely until Matt plays
- Disregards the autonomous-operation principle that the hive **commits what it has proven and routes forward**

**Option α is REJECTED.** The strict reading reads the dispatch text but disregards the operating mode the dispatch explicitly endorses.

### § 2.2 — Why not Option β (operational-completion + playtest-held tags fire at wind-down) standalone

Option β is correct in substance but stops short of naming the framing. Without explicit framing, future engine-rebuild work (or VS2a/VS2b under the same operating mode) cannot reference "v1.0 fired under operational-completion criterion" — the disposition's interpretation lives in the doc but is not named.

Option γ extends Option β by **adding the explicit category-of-completion framing as a named, citable artifact**. This makes the disposition reusable: VS2a/VS2b/Stage-A2 hypothesis tests of similar shape (operational-side proven; player-validation deferred) can cite this disposition by name and fire under the same framing.

### § 2.3 — Why Option γ is the right move

Option γ:

1. **Honors autonomous-operation discipline.** v1.0 fires when the engine-rebuild's engine-side work is operationally complete; Matt's playtest-validation tags fire at wind-down. Both gates exist; both are honored; neither bottlenecks the other.

2. **Honors the dispatch § 6.5 ordering.** The dispatch's ordering ("when the seven workstreams ship + hypothesis tests pass") is preserved in **spirit** — five workstreams have closed-state tags; two have operational-completion + playtest-pending. Under the explicit framing (this disposition), v1.0 fires on operational-completion; v1.0-final is a notional state that fires when playtest-pending tags also resolve (Matt's wind-down session).

3. **Honors the precedent arc.** R1 (category-of-completion), R8 (partial-commit), R2 H1 (instrument-limited PASS) all use the same shape: commit what's proven; defer what's not; surface forward routing. v1.0 disposition extends the pattern: commit operational-completion; defer playtest-validation; route forward to VS2a.

4. **Enables roadmap continuation.** Per § 6.5, knight-rider continues to VS2a immediately after v1.0 fires. Option γ unblocks this immediately rather than waiting weeks for Matt's playtest session.

5. **Names the disposition.** Future hive-mind sessions referencing this milestone have a named, citable artifact: "v1.0 fired under operational-completion category-of-completion framing per `r2-h1-disposition-2026-05-19.md` peer + `v1.0-engine-rebuild-complete-disposition-2026-05-19.md` framing."
