## § 0 — TL;DR

VS2a moves from geometric environment placeholders (demo v1 known-low-quality drag) to **per-season environment-pack selection** at season-generation time. ONE environment-pack per season; pack carries floor tiles + wall tiles + props/scenery in coherent HD-2D-pixel-art register. ## § 1 — Why this framework exists

### § 1.1 The structural gap

Catalogue research to date scoped TWO visual axes:
- **VFX** (Pimen GREEN-list 11/13 elements + CreativeKind for monsters)
- **Characters** (chierit Elementals + CreativeKind monster sprites)

The **third axis — environment assets (floor tiles + wall tiles + props/scenery)** — was implicit-deferred without being named as a deferred axis. No catalogue dispatch authored a sweep; no canonical doc named environment as a separate sourcing track; no forward-flag captured "environment is out of Step-B-Tier-1 scope; needs separate sweep at named ship-gate." This is the fourth P6 instance (sibling to Drift-11A movement-speed, Drift-11B geometry × element VFX coverage, Drift-14 pool VFX-mapping).

### § 1.2 Why now — the structural incoherence Matt caught

The drax room/hallway topology shipped 2026-05-16 (commit `5463be8`; `canonical/story/arena-room-hallway-system.md`) commits VS2a to Diablo/PoE-style single-camera ARPG framing. Per genre canon — D2 per-act tilesets; D3/D4 per-zone visual identity; PoE per-map tile families; Hades per-region chamber visuals; Octopath/Sea of Stars/Eiyuden HD-2D — environmental visual identity is **load-bearing, not decorative**. Demo v1 shipped geometric placeholders against this convention.

Matt direct catch 2026-05-17 (verbatim): *"In the demo v1, one of the worst parts of it was the geometrically drawn 'random seasonal structures on the ground' and the geometrically drawn walls. The geometrically drawn floor tiles weren't too bad, but they weren't amazing either... would it make sense for legolas to look for anything that might match to our static dimensions of floors/walls? This could REALLY make the difference in the demo."*

VS2a is being shaped to ship multiple ARPG-genre + HD-2D-register commitments — end-game-anchored MS values (sim+demo coherent), Path A-prime ARPG-scale sprites (chierit 2.5×; monsters at genre-scale), pool VFX-mapping closure (Drift-14), room/hallway topology — alongside geometric environment placeholders that contradict every other commitment. Structural incoherence. This framework closes the residual gap.

### § 1.3 Genre design lineage that motivates load-bearing environment art

From design-lineage Layer 2 (Diablo team experience):

- **D1 (1996)** — tile-based pure-square-room dungeon; the iconic Diablo register is *as much the tile art as the monster art*. Lazarus Tristram cathedral hallway is unmistakable. Environment carries atmosphere as much as VFX does.
- **D2 (2000)** — per-act biome-coded tilesets (Rogue Encampment camp → Lut Gholein desert ruins → Kurast jungle outpost → Pandemonium harrowing). Each tileset reads as a different *world*, not just a different palette.
- **D3 (2012)** — per-act tilesets stay coherent within zone but the move to "wider open" dungeon zones (Highlands, Fields of Misery) loosens the room-coherence — environment becomes more wallpaper, less character. This is the D3 register I'd argue *lost* something from D2.
- **D4 (2023)** — restores per-zone visual identity (Fractured Peaks vs Kehjistan vs Hawezar) but environment-art is still the player's primary "where am I?" cue.
- **PoE (2013–)** — per-map tile families; the same monsters in a *Coast* map vs a *Crematorium* map feel like different encounters because the environment frames them differently.
- **Hades (2020)** — per-region chamber-art carries narrative weight; Tartarus stone vs Asphodel obsidian vs Elysium grass is the storytelling layer as much as the dialogue is.
- **Octopath / Sea of Stars / Eiyuden Chronicle (HD-2D)** — environment art is what defines the register; sprites read against tile-detail-density.

For Reincarnated to position as isekai-ARPG with HD-2D-pixel-art register (per `style-register.md` lock), environment art is the load-bearing third leg of the stool. Demo v1's geometric placeholders read as a *prototype*; VS2a needs to read as a *game*. That's the Matt verdict; this framework operationalizes it.

## § 2 — Decision dimensions (the four-axis framework)

Each candidate environment pack is scored on four dimensions. Score is qualitative (good / acceptable / weak) at framework level; gandalf shortlist (Track B § 5) ranks candidates within season-anchor by these axes.

### § 2.1 Thematic fit to season anchor

The first axis. Each season has an anchor (per `canonical/story/cosmology-reincarnated.md`) — Hermit, Full Moon, Capricorn, Death, Waxing Crescent, Sun, Hanged Man, etc. The pack's intrinsic theme should resonate with the anchor's tonal register:

| Season anchor (example) | Compatible environment themes |
|---|---|
| **Hermit** | cave / cathedral interior / monastic chamber / secluded grotto |
| **Full Moon** | moonlit forest / night ruin / silver-hued plateau |
| **Capricorn** | mountain peak / cold crag / high-altitude shrine |
| **Death** | abandoned ruin / crypt / overgrown graveyard / ossuary |
| **Waxing Crescent** | new-growth forest / dawn meadow / first-light shore |
| **Sun** | volcanic peak / sun-baked ruin / golden desert |
| **Hanged Man** | overgrown gallows / suspended platforms / inverted spaces |
| **Tower** | broken citadel / shattered keep / collapsing parapets |

Legolas Track A captures `primary_fit_seasons` per pack as a descriptive-themes-list (e.g., "dark cave / cathedral interior / forest grove"). Gandalf Track B maps themes to anchors at shortlist time.

**Negative cases (thematic anti-fit):** A "candy land sweets" pack lands at zero for every season anchor; a "modern city street" pack lands at zero for any Phase-0 anchor. These get filtered at legolas crawl time (out-of-genre).

### § 2.2 Visual coherence with chierit characters + Pimen VFX

**The single most load-bearing axis after thematic fit.** The pack must render in the locked HD-2D-pixel-art register (Candidate B per `style-register.md`). Specifically:

- **Style sub-register:** hand-drawn pixel-art (HD-2D-shaped), not retro pixel-art (Stardew-class), not vector/clean-line, not anime hand-drawn raster.
- **Pixel density at PIXELS_PER_METER=48:** source-tile sizes 32×32 / 48×48 / 64×64 / 96×96 are ideal — at 48×48 a 1m tile fills exactly the canonical grid; other sizes scale by integer factor. Tiles at non-integer-compatible sizes get filtered.
- **Color palette:** harmonizes with the chierit Elemental palette (warm-mineral / cool-aquatic / aerial-pale / earthen-ochre across the four canonical-slot character lines) and the Pimen GREEN-list VFX palette (saturated-particle-driven). Packs with conflicting palette (e.g., neon-cyberpunk, pastel-cute) fail this axis even if other axes pass.
- **Pixel-density consistency with chierit at 2.5× scale (Path A-prime):** characters render at 100–130 px heights per VS2a-locked sprite scale. Tile pixel-density must look right at that character density — a tile at 16×16 pixel-density beside a 100px character reads as size-mismatch; a tile at 48×48 reads coherent.

**This is the score-don't-filter principle from `style-register.md` applied to environment packs.** The catalogue should retain register-adjacent packs for pivot insurance, but consumption-time filter (gandalf shortlist) hard-gates on register-fit.

### § 2.3 Coverage completeness

A pack ships some subset of:
- floor tiles
- wall tiles
- props / scenery objects
- overlays (lighting, fog, water-edges)
- animated objects (torches, banners — optional)

| Coverage tier | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Full** | floor + wall + props (all three core) | preferred at VS2a |
| **Good** | floor + wall (no props, or sparse props) | acceptable at VS2a; props can be sourced from secondary pack |
| **Floor-only** | only floor tiles | weak at VS2a; ship only if no full-coverage candidate fits the season anchor |
| **Props-only** | only scenery objects | not viable as primary pack; secondary-acquisition only |

For VS2a's single pack, prefer **Full coverage** so drax integration ships floor + wall + at-least-some-props in one acquisition. For VS2b's second pack, the same preference applies; secondary-pack acquisition for prop variety is a VS2c+ cadence concern.

### § 2.4 License + attribution coherence

CC-BY or commercial-royalty-free with attribution is required. Restrictive licenses (non-commercial-only; "personal use") get filtered at legolas crawl. Attribution surfaces via F1 credits overlay (existing chierit pattern; environment-pack-id carries attribution-string forward).

Per Drift-13 lesson (vendor-mixed-register): **inspection register must match consumption granularity.** A vendor whose VFX packs are CC-BY pixel-art but whose environment packs are CC-BY-NC vector is NOT a single-licence vendor; legolas Track A annotates per-pack license, not per-vendor license.

## § 4 — Selection cadence

### § 4.1 Cadence by VS-version

| VS milestone | Pack count | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **VS2a** | **1 pack** | First production-quality environment art; replaces geometric placeholders; ships in VS2a regen season |
| **VS2b** | **+1 additional pack** | Per-season variety begins; two seasons can ship visually distinct environments |
| **VS2c+** | **full per-season cadence** | Each new season gets a fitted environment pack; legolas continues catalogue expansion as needed; gandalf shortlist per new season; Matt picks at per-season cadence |

### § 4.2 Pack-reuse policy

A single pack can serve multiple seasons whose anchors share thematic register (e.g., a "cave / monastic interior" pack serves both Hermit and Death seasons). The framework does NOT require unique-pack-per-season at VS2a/VS2b. Per-season uniqueness is VS2c+ scope.

When a pack serves multiple seasons, **per-season palette-shift or minor compositing is acceptable** to keep visual variety. This is the same operating mode as VFX (Pimen GREEN-list serves multiple elements via palette-shift).

### § 4.3 What is NOT in-scope

Per the 2026-05-17 commission doc § "What environmental theming is NOT trying to be":

- **Not procedurally generated** — pick from acquired packs; no engine-side procedural tile generation in Phase 0.
- **Not fully unique per season** — VS2a/VS2b ship 1–2 packs; full per-season uniqueness is VS2c+ cadence concern.
- **Not animated environments** — Phase 0 ships static environment tiles; animated tiles (waterfalls, flickering torches) are post-Phase-0 polish unless a pack ships them as static-acceptable fallback.
- **Not interactive environment** — destructible walls, breakable props, etc. are out of Phase 0 scope.
