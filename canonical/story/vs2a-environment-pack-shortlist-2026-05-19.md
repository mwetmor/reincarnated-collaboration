## § 2 — Season anchor mapping (season_001003 specific)

### § 2.1 The anchor as canonical

Season_001003's anchor "The Cathedral of Bone — the temple built entirely from the relics of saints" is **not arbitrary text** — it is referenced verbatim in `cosmology-reincarnated.md` § "The Seasonal Journey" as one of two canonical season-anchor exemplars (alongside "The Deep Trench"). This makes season_001003 a **referent season** for the cosmology layer; whatever VS2a ships visually here will be cited downstream as the prototype of how a Reincarnated season presents.

This matters: the environment-pack selection here is not just "the first VS2a pack." It's **the canonical prototype of HD-2D environment art in the Reincarnated register, rendered against a cosmology-referenced anchor.** Get this right and downstream per-season cadence (VS2b + VS2c+) inherits a strong precedent; get it wrong and the cosmology's own examples drift from the demo's own visuals.

### § 2.2 Theme-axis mapping per F3 § 2.1

Cathedral of Bone is multi-anchored:

- **Death** — relics-of-saints, ossuary-aesthetic, bone-as-remnant, the sanctified-dead → Reaper anchor
- **Hermit** — cathedral interior, monastic chamber, secluded sanctum → Reaper or Wizard's Hideout
- **Water** — water-element season; per-season element rename: water→"milk" (white-nurturing-vital); the sanctuary holds water-element power → Naga or Elemental Dungeon (water portion)
- **High Priestess (tarot adjacency)** — sanctified temple holding mystery-of-the-dead → Reaper or Heavenly Palace (off-anchor; too celestial-coded)

The bridging theme is **a holy temple built from death and consecrated by water-as-milk**. This is not a horror dungeon; this is not a celestial palace; this is a Hermit/Death intersection rendered in water-vocabulary. **D2 reference: the Crystalline Passage + Halls of Pain hybrid. D4 reference: Cathedral of Light's lower sanctums. Octopath reference: the Cult of Aelfric chapel interiors. Eastward reference: the Mt. Orbit shrine paths.** Sanctified, hushed, weighty.

### § 2.3 Anti-fits ruled out

- **KR Phoenix** — fire-element; tonally wrong (volcanic-rage vs sanctified-quiet); ruled out
- **KR Snow Castle** — ice/winter-castle; off-element; ruled out for THIS season (excellent for Capricorn season; hold for VS2b+)
- **KR Heavenly Palace** — celestial-divine register; Cathedral of Bone is NOT celestial; ruled out (Sun/Justice season territory; hold for VS2c+)
- **KR Wizard's Hideout** — Hermit-anchor-strong but cavern-aesthetic doesn't read as "temple/cathedral built from relics"; ruled out for primary; secondary-supplement-territory if Matt picks differently
- **Elthen Cultist Dungeons** — occult-ceremony register; thematically adjacent but 32×32 (sub-EXACT_FIT_DEFAULT) + retro register; ruled out vs Kokoro alternatives
- **Foozle Lucifer Dungeon** — CC0 baseline; retro register; ruled out for primary VS2a ship (acquire as drax pipeline-integration placeholder per Track D scope)
- **Val Sama 66 Spring Ruins** — spring-overgrown-temple register; thematically wrong tone (verdant/renewal vs sanctified/death); ruled out

This leaves three Kokoro candidates that genuinely map the Cathedral-of-Bone anchor: **Reaper** (Death-axis lead), **Naga** (water-temple-axis lead), **Elemental Dungeon** (water-portion + strategic-capital lead). The shortlist below addresses each in full F3 four-axis depth.
