## 0. TL;DR

The combat **companion** (Q8's "merc" layer — a single bonded ally occupying the 4th gear slot, available to **every** player archetype) is an **ascended form drawn from the player's Hall of Heroes.** Every companion is a self the player used to be — a life lived to its best end, molted and ascended, now returning to fight beside the form the player wears *now*.

The **convergence item** that binds them is the **record of a shared season between two past selves** — bond-flavor, not stat-flavor. The form-library accumulation finally pays off as **relationships, not just a swap-roster.**

Four corollaries lock with the commitment:
1. **Proxies are NOT Hall forms** (§ 4). The summoner's army is generic constructs; the companion is a singular past self. Scarcity is the emotional engine — do not dilute it.
2. **The companion completes a temporal triad** (§ 6): past self fights beside you (companion), future self advises you (spirit guide), present self *is* you (Earth Self in the seasonal avatar).
3. **Companion identity is lookup-not-generation** (§ 5) — rendered from the same ascended-form records the Hall renders. D7 held.
4. **One bootstrap sub-question is deferred** (§ 7): how does a player's *first* season have a companion when the Hall is empty? Matt design call; my lean inside.

## 1. Vocabulary reconciliation — "form-library" is the Hall of Heroes

The Q8 work and the prior design dialogue used "form-library" as the working term. The locked canonical vocabulary (parent doc § 2) is **Hall of Heroes** — the *possession space*, the court of ascended forms the player HAS accumulated (≤1 ascension/season; sparse for a long time). "Form-library" is retired in favor of **Hall of Heroes** wherever the *possession* of past forms is meant.

This is not a rename of convenience. It changes what the companion *is*: not an entry pulled from a flat library, but **a hero from the player's own court** — one of the lives in the Hall, called down off its pedestal to fight again. The two spaces stay distinct (parent doc § 2): the **cosmograph sky** is possibility (forms you *could* call at creation); the **Hall** is possession (forms you *have* lived). **The companion is drawn from the Hall, never from the sky** — you cannot field a self you have not yet been.

## 2. The commitment — companion = a Hall-of-Heroes ascended form

The Q8 layer ("the D2/D3 mercenary system" — Matt's framing, confirmed 2026-06-13) is a **single bonded ally** in a 4th gear slot, available to all 25 player strategies (not summoner-only; see § 4). This commitment fixes its **identity source:**

> **Every combat companion is an ascended form from the player's Hall of Heroes — a self the player previously lived, molted, and ascended, now traveling forward as an ally.**

Genre precedent (Legolas #9, the load-bearing one): the **Baby/Good-Mimic** pattern — a former adversary-type, bonded, using its nature to *protect* rather than threaten. The companion is the reincarnation premise's version: a former *self*, bonded, using the power of a life you have already lived to guard the life you live now. This is the truest version of the isekai party-bond (Bond-Creatures trope; Seirei Gensouki spirit-contract) — realized not as a met stranger, but as the player's own history.

## 3. The convergence item — a shared-season bond record

The convergence item (Q8-b kicker) is the gear that binds player↔companion. This commitment fixes its *flavor*: it is the **record of a shared season between two of the player's past selves.**

- Not "Defense Talisman +15%." Instead *"Bark-and-Flame Pact"* — the bond between the fire-form the player wears now and the iron-barked forest-warrior they wore three reincarnations ago.
- **Mechanic stays pure role-complement** (Q8 matrix; Session-2 § 6.2 caps — a *behavior*, not a power bump). **Flavor is the two spirits' shared history.** The kicker's trigger (the D4-Reinforcement action-conditional proc) reads, in fiction, as one past self answering another's danger.

Every convergence item is a small story about two of the player's past selves choosing to fight as one. That sentence is the entire reason the companion layer earns its place in the game rather than the balance sheet.

## 4. The two-layer separation — companion (Hall) vs proxies (constructs)

**This is a load-bearing protection, ratified in the 2026-06-13 dialogue.** When Matt asked whether the form-library idea was "only for proxy-type T4's, or… the D2/D3 mercenary system," the answer was *the mercenary system, universal* — and the corollary is that **proxies must NOT be Hall forms.**

| Layer | What it is | Hall-sourced? |
|---|---|---|
| **Proxies** (Q6/Q7; PROXY_* strategies) | The summoner's *army* — disposable/semi-disposable summoned constructs internal to a summoner kit (PROXY_FISSION spawns ~16 transient bodies). Carries proxy-type modifiers, not a full kit. | **NO — generic constructs.** |
| **Companion** (Q8; 4th gear slot) | A *single bonded ally*, a full kit in its own right, available to all 25 player rows (a summoner gets one *on top of* their army). | **YES — a singular Hall form.** |

Why the separation is non-negotiable — **scarcity is the emotional engine:**
1. **Disposability would cheapen the reverence.** PROXY_FISSION death-spamming sixteen "past selves" turns reincarnation into cannon fodder; the premise dies by attrition.
2. **It would dissolve the companion's specialness.** "This one ally is a self you used to be" runs entirely on scarcity. D2's mercenary lands because it is *singular* — you name it, you pay to revive it, you flinch when it drops. Sixteen disposable mercs and you mourn none of them.

So: **companion = one bonded past self (singular, reverent, the merc). Proxies = generic constructs (the summoner's tools, never past selves).** A release valve, if the summoner needs to feel thematically richer: proxies may be *flavored* as transient echoes of forms — summoning a past form's *shape* without its bonded spirit — without being literal Hall entries. Flavor nuance, subordinate to the architecture; the singular companion's weight stays intact.

## 5. D7 — companion identity is lookup-not-generation

The companion is the **same ascended-form record the Hall renders** (parent doc § 5) — name, deeds, final form — rendered as a *combatant* instead of a statue. This is free coherence: the companion IS a Hall entry, displayed in a different posture. Consequences:

- Companion identity is **looked up from engine-emitted ascended-form packets**, never narrated by a runtime LLM at the surface. D7 AI-tell line held.
- The convergence item's bond-flavor (the *"Bark-and-Flame Pact"* register) is **templated-blanks or human-curated** (OP § 3.3 / N1-N4 naming stack), not raw LLM dialogue.
- Spirit-guide voice at the companion-bond moment, if any, uses the templated register — same discipline as the Hall.

## 6. The coherence win — the companion completes a temporal triad

Capturing this because it is a genuine recognition, not a new commitment: with the companion fixed as a *past self*, Reincarnated's three persistent relationships resolve into one clean temporal structure:

| Relationship | Who | Temporal direction | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Earth Self** (in the seasonal avatar) | present self | now | the player — projects, fights, molts |
| **Companion** | a past self (Hall form) | behind | fights beside you — the life you already lived, guarding the life you live now |
| **Spirit guide** | the future-facing advisor (canonical 17) | ahead | advises you — the marginal-value voice that shapes gear and attention |

Past fights beside you; future advises you; present *is* you. The companion was the missing corner — it makes the reincarnation spine a complete temporal triangle rather than a one-way molting chain. This is the kind of structural coherence that makes a premise feel *designed* rather than assembled, and it is a strong independent argument for the Hall-sourced companion over a generic-merc alternative.

## 7. Deferred sub-question — the season-1 bootstrap (Matt design call)

The commitment creates exactly one downstream sequencing question. It does **not** re-open § 2 — it is a consequence of it.

**The question:** a player's *first* season has an **empty Hall** (molting is season-*end*; you have lived no prior life). If the companion must be a Hall form, the first season has **no companion** — and since every new player begins at season 1, this is a permanent structural fact, not a transitional one. The Q8 matrix + convergence gear slot would be dormant in any season-1 experience.

**Two resolutions:**

- **Path Pure — companion is *always* a Hall form; season 1 you fight alone.** Thematically purest: an ordinary Earth Self, first projection, no past lives yet. Season 2 then delivers a massive beat — your first ascended hero (season 1's molt) *returns* to fight beside your new form; the harvest of molting realized as companionship. **Cost:** the merc layer is dead content for every player's first season, forever.
- **Path Two-Tier — a bonded spirit met *within* season 1 (not yet yours), upgrading to your own ascended forms as the Hall fills.** Keeps the merc layer live from season 1 (genre-conventional — D2 hands you a merc in Act 1). Creates the strongest *arc*: you begin beside a stranger-spirit, you end beside your own past lives. **Cost:** dilutes the "every companion is a self you used to be" purity to "most companions, eventually," and introduces a second companion-source concept.

**My lean: Path Two-Tier** — it keeps Q8 live from minute one and the stranger→past-self escalation is, if anything, a *stronger* expression of the reincarnation arc than purity-from-the-start. But Path Pure is the more reverent reading and a legitimate choice, and this is a thematic-sequencing decision that is yours, not mine to assume. **Gating criterion:** a Matt design call (not empirical — this is a fiction-sequencing choice). Until resolved, rocket companion-generation (§ 8) should assume Hall-sourced identity and treat the season-1 source as a parameter, not hard-wire either path.
