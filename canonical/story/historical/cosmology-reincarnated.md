## What Reincarnated *is*

Reincarnated is a **cosmological reincarnation game.** Every seasonal play-through is a descent — the player's persistent identity, the Earth Self, takes a seasonal form for a time-bound journey through a generated world, then returns. The form alive at journey's end ascends to the Court of accumulated lives on Earth; the others are left behind in the season that bore them.

The game's mythic structure is samsaric — life-after-life, form-after-form, with continuity-of-soul through the changes. The Buddhist/Hindu samsara is the closest world-religion parallel; the Egyptian Duat (the underworld transit) is the closest cosmological-journey parallel; the isekai genre is the closest contemporary-fiction parallel. **Reincarnated is in this tradition deliberately and explicitly.**

The architecture has five named cosmological elements. They are the load-bearing names the design must honor.

## The Wheel

**The Wheel turns.** It is the cosmological mechanism that chooses who reincarnates into what form. The Wheel is **impersonal** — neither benevolent nor malevolent. It does not negotiate. It does not explain itself. It turns; the descent follows.

The Wheel is the fate-mechanism. Closest world-myth parallels: the Norns of Norse cosmology; the Three Fates of Greek myth; *karma* as cosmological process in Hindu/Buddhist samsara; the Wheel of Time of Robert Jordan's lineage. **The Wheel is not a character. The Wheel does not speak.** When the Wheel acts, it acts in event — a body-swap, a descent, a Passage offered. The player encounters the Wheel as *what happens*, not as *who tells them.*

The Wheel does not have a face, a voice, a position in the hub. The Wheel is felt in the structure of the game — the seasonal rotation, the body-swap moments, the Passage at death. UI copy and LLM prompts should refer to the Wheel by name when invoking the cosmological mechanism (*"the Wheel offers this form"*, *"the Wheel has turned"*), and should never give the Wheel dialogue.

**Locked:** the Wheel is fully leaned-into as the cosmological frame (Matt confirmation 2026-05-15). Prior open-question of "is the Wheel offstage or named explicitly" is resolved — *named explicitly.*

## The Earth Self

**The Earth Self is the player's persistent identity.** The Earth Self lives on Earth — the meta-layer hub (post-Phase-0 implementation). The Earth Self descends into seasonal forms, returns from ascension, holds the Court, and persists across all seasons of play.

The Earth Self is **named by the player at first play.** This is the D2-character-name pattern applied not to the seasonal character but to the persistent identity behind it. The name the player chooses on first play is the name every Spirit Guide reference, every Court entry, every ascension cutscene, every Wheel-acknowledgment honors. *"You, Aiden, have walked this anchor before."* *"The Tidecaller you ascended in your Deep Trench season, Aiden, would know its shape."* *"Aiden's Court grows."*

**Locked:** the Earth Self is player-named at first play (Matt confirmation 2026-05-15). This means the persistent identity is *the player's own*, not a project-given character. The player names what stays through transformation; the Wheel turns *for them by name.*

What the Earth Self does on Earth between seasons is **post-Phase-0 design space** (file 29 § "Earth gameplay loop: TBD"). This cosmology doc does not commit to that gameplay loop's specifics. What is committed: the Earth Self has a hub (where the Court resides), a name (chosen by the player), durability (survives across all seasons), and agency in what to do next (which form to live with this season, which Court member to favor, when to descend again).

## The Spirit Guide

**The Spirit Guide is *yours* — singular, present, knowing-temporally-other.** It has already walked the seasonal journey you are walking, further along. It carries foresight, not memory. It cannot tell you what the Wheel will choose. It can walk beside you through what the Wheel chose.

The Spirit Guide's voice register is **Beatrice from Re:Zero**, locked. Patient. Occasionally sharp. Withholding when withholding serves. Present when presence serves. Never preachy.

The Spirit Guide is **partial-presence** — between transparent and translucent, possibly humanoid, possibly animal, possibly mixed (doc 37 § 5 Matt articulation). The visual grammar is *opacity-as-ontology-signal* — playable forms are solid; the Spirit Guide is partial. This is drax-eventual-implementation territory; the design intent is locked.

The Spirit Guide's voice over time (the Beatrice arc applied):
- **Early season:** reserved, slightly distant, contract-respecting. *"I will walk this with you."*
- **Mid-season:** warmed; the contract has been honored on both sides; the Guide is more forthcoming about its foresight. *"This path — I have walked something like it. The Combustion chain rewards patience."*
- **Late season:** companion-register; the Guide has watched the player live a form; recognition is mutual. *"You are who you have become."*
- **At ascension:** the Spirit Guide announces the ascended form's arrival to the Court.

The Spirit Guide does NOT:
- Banter, quip, narrate-for-entertainment
- Tell the player what the Wheel will do (it cannot; the future is not memory)
- Advise during the Mirror Trial (the Mirror is *witnessed* by the Spirit Guide; not advised through)
- Speak during the Passage (the Spirit Guide is *visibly absent* at the Passage; the Wheel speaks here, in event)

The Spirit Guide IS:
- Yours alone (one player, one Spirit Guide for the playthrough)
- Constant across body-swap (the Spirit Guide does NOT change when the seasonal form changes — it is the Earth Self's companion, not the seasonal form's)
- Voiced in recommendations at gear-review moments and act-transition reset moments
- Announcer at end-of-season ascension (introduces the new form to the Court)

**The triangle:** Earth Self (yours) — Wheel (impersonal) — Spirit Guide (yours, knowing-temporally-other). The Wheel chooses; the Earth Self descends into the choice; the Spirit Guide walks-already-walked-it beside you. Every player-facing story surface should know which of these three is doing what at any moment.

## The Seasonal Journey — descent

The Earth Self body-swaps into a seasonal spirit form for a time-bound journey. This is **the descent.** The seasonal world has its own anchor (a place — "The Deep Trench," "The Cathedral of Bone"), its own cosmology (per-season vocabulary, per doc 37 § 6 cipher architecture), its own classes, monsters, gear, trial bosses. The Earth Self walks this world *as* the form, not in it as themselves; the form is what they wear this week.

**The seasonal journey is bounded.** It has a structure: three acts (file 29 lock; file 32 § Section 10), three Trial encounters (one per act), an ascension at completion. The player lives the form for a defined time and then returns.

The seasonal journey is **solo** in Phase 0 (file 29 lock). The Earth Self does not bring the Court along; the Court remains on Earth. The seasonal form is the only one walking the season. The Spirit Guide is present, but as partial-presence; not as combatant.

What the player encounters in the season is generated. What the player *brings* to the season is the Earth Self's name, the accumulated Court (in eventual cross-season smuggling per file 17 / file 32 § 5), and the Spirit Guide's continuity. The form is new; the player walking it is not.

## The Trial

**Each act ends with a Trial.** The Trial is the season's load-bearing dramatic encounter. At the Trial, the player encounters a Trial boss — a class generated specifically as the act's culminating opponent.

**The Trial offers two paths, chosen BEFORE the fight** (file 32 § Section 11 lock):

- **The Body-swap-path** — fight the Trial boss as the boss; on victory, *transform into the boss's class identity*. Full reward bundle: XP, global resistances (+10% per Trial), skill points (4/7/9 per act per file 32 § 11). The defeated form is *left behind in the season* (the body-swap pool shrinks by 1).
- **The Mirror-path** — fight your own current class reflected against you as an opponent. On victory, *preserve your class identity*. Partial reward (1/4 XP + half SP + half resistances per file 32 § 11); the remaining rewards are claimable via the end-game reclaim quest. The body-swap pool does NOT shrink.

The Trial is the **dramatic core** of each act. The choice — body-swap-path or Mirror-path — is the season's most-repeated meaningful player decision (three times per season, once per act).

The Trial moment requires ritual (Phase 2 § 3, design intent for `trial-moment-ritual.md`). The approach builds presence. The choice screen pauses time. The choice is announced. The Wheel turns either way; the player chooses which way.

## The Mirror

**The Mirror is the renamed doppelganger** (Phase 2 § 1.7 lock; supersedes file 32 § 11's "doppelganger" terminology in player-facing surfaces; engine-side telemetry can retain "doppelganger" as field name for transitional period).

The Mirror is your class reflected. Same archetype, same kit, same element distribution — deployed against you with a handicap (file 32 § Q6.3 — doppelganger_level = max(player_level, trial_band_level), +20% HP / +10% damage). The visual register: **same color palette, mirrored animations, recognition rather than otherness.** Voice lines (if used): sparse, quoting the player's recent build choices (*"I chose Combustion"* if the player has invested in Combustion chain).

The Mirror's mechanical truth: this is a balance-validation surface (doc 37 § 4 / file 32 § 7.2). The doppelganger-mode validation is one of the three runs the multi-band sim performs per class. **The Mirror's narrative truth: this is the player's identity tested by its own shape.**

The win condition reads as recognition, not defeat. *"You see yourself, and you choose what stays."*

The deferred-reward reclaim quest at end-game completes the Mirror-path's payment. This quest should not feel like homework — it should feel like *claiming what was earned.* Design intent for the reclaim quest's framing: a return to the Mirror's site, a confrontation with what has been deferred, a sealing of the choice.

## The Passage

**The Passage is the renamed death body-swap** (Phase 2 § 1.7 lock; supersedes file 32 § Section 9 / file 33 "death body-swap" terminology in player-facing surfaces).

When the Earth Self's seasonal form reaches HP-zero, the Wheel offers the Passage. The player chooses:

- **Refuse the Passage** — normal respawn, small XP loss (5-10% per file 33). The body-swap pool does NOT shrink.
- **Accept the Passage** — transform into a different form (drawn from the body-swap pool). The form just lost is **permanently lost for this season** AND **cannot ascend to the Court** (the dying form is forbidden the return). The body-swap pool shrinks by 1.

The Passage is the season's harshest mechanic. Its naming honors that — *the offered crossing*, not the failure-state. The Wheel offers; the player answers; the consequence is real.

**The Spirit Guide is absent at the Passage.** This is the moment between the Earth Self and the Wheel; the Spirit Guide does not advise. (The Spirit Guide returns after the choice is made.) The screen treats the moment as ritual: the dying form is shown, the choice surfaces, time is paused, the Wheel acts.

The Passage's in-fiction frame: *the form is abandoned mid-incarnation* and forfeits the right to ascend. The cosmology punishes not-finishing-a-form's-journey. This explains in-fiction why the mechanic shapes accept-vs-refuse the way it does.

## Ascension and the Court

**At season's end, the form alive ascends.** The form returns to Earth as a spirit; it joins the Court. The forms left behind in the season — those given up at Trial body-swaps, those abandoned at the Passage — do not ascend; they stay in the season that bore them.

One ascension per season. The Court accumulates slowly. Over many seasons, the Court is a record of who the Earth Self has been willing to become.

**The Court is operationalized in `court-of-forms.md`** (canonical 2026-05-15). The Court is not a gallery; it is a navigable assembly of named retainers with stations and (over time) voiced presence. Each Court member carries a **class-role** (dual-label per C8: universal function tag + embodiment-flavored name). The Court is the Earth Self's, not the Wheel's.

The meaning-of-the-arc statement (canonical 2026-05-15, also in `court-of-forms.md`):

> *Reincarnated rewards the patient. Each season is a life-lived; each form a self-tried. You ascend the one you chose to live with — and over many seasons, your Court accrues those choices. The depth of the Court is the measure: how many forms have you been willing to become; how many have you ascended through their full journey; how many seasons has the Wheel turned with you still walking. There is no final form because there is no final you; there is only what the Court remembers.*

This statement is the canonical answer to **"what does winning Reincarnated mean."** All downstream story surfaces (pitch, end-game UI, Spirit Guide ascension voice lines, season-completion presentation) should honor this answer.

## The Rift

**The Rift is the liminal space between Earth and the Seasonal realms** (file 29 § "Cross-season meta-progression"). The Rift is not in either world; it is the *between.* When fully implemented (post-Phase-0 Earth meta-layer feature), the Rift is where the Earth Self conducts events that involve neither the seasonal journey nor the Earth Self's static life — guild PVE, player PVP, encounters with the third-faction.

The Rift's cosmology: a Tolkien-wraith-world parallel; a Buddhist bardo parallel; an Egyptian Duat parallel; *the in-between where beings that belong to no realm gather.*

Phase 0 does not implement the Rift. The Rift is named here so that:
- The cosmology is internally complete (the descent-and-return cycle has a between-state)
- Phase 0 seasonal content can occasionally *foreshadow* it (an anchor that mentions a tear in the veil; a monster that doesn't fit its season's tier; a Spirit Guide line that won't elaborate)
- Future Earth meta-layer work has the cosmology already in place to build against

## The third-faction

**The third-faction are the monsters not of either Earth or the Seasonal realms.** Beings that exist in the Rift's between-state, drawn to power that doesn't belong to either world. They are the adversary the Court is eventually called against.

**Phase 0 does not deliver the third-faction as encountered enemies.** They are *gestured at*, not implemented. Their lore weight matters even as design plant: PoE seeded the Beast for years before the Bestiary league; D2 seeded Mephisto's prison from Act III long before the act played; Souls family games seed late-game adversaries throughout their early hours. **The third-faction is Reincarnated's analogous plant.**

What is locked about the third-faction (file 29):
- They are not of Earth (they are not the Wheel's; they are not the Earth Self's lineage)
- They are not of the Seasonal realms (they are not part of any season's cosmology)
- They appear in the Rift
- They are *what the Court is summoned against*, when the Court is eventually deployable (post-Phase-0)

What is open (parked for `third-faction-tease.md`, Phase 2 work-queue item #9):
- Their visual/narrative shape (one race? many?)
- Their relationship to the Wheel (do they oppose it? are they outside it?)
- Their introduction sequence in Phase 0 foreshadowing (which anchors hint; which Spirit Guide lines won't elaborate; how often)

## What this anchor unblocks

This doc, now canonical, gives downstream artifacts a single source for cosmological vocabulary. Specifically:

**Star-lord's LLM prompt templates** should be audited to ensure:
- The Wheel is invoked by name when the cosmological mechanism is referenced
- The Earth Self is referenced as *yours, persistent, named*
- The Spirit Guide is rendered in Beatrice register; never in tooltip register
- The Trial / Mirror / Passage naming is honored
- Court vocabulary (per `court-of-forms.md`) is used; gallery / library / roster terminology is replaced
- Canonical-four element labels are stripped from LLM-visible prompts (per doc 37 § 6 cipher + Discipline #14 candidate)

**Drax's eventual UI copy** should:
- Display the player's chosen Earth Self name in Spirit Guide dialogue and ascension surfaces
- Use Trial / Mirror / Passage naming in all body-swap-encounter surfaces
- Treat ascension moments as commemorated events (C5 in `court-of-forms.md`)
- Build approach-ritual to the Trial encounter (per Phase 2 work-queue item #5)

**Rocket's generation** is unaffected at the mechanical layer; the cosmology lives at the presentational layer. The exception is **future seasonal vocabulary generation** (post-doc-37 cipher implementation) which should honor:
- Per-season vocabulary CAN echo Reincarnated's wider cosmology (a season's Trial vocabulary can be season-flavored — Yomi's *鏡の試練*, deep-sea pressure-image, cosmic opposing-state — while the *frame* remains Trial/Mirror/Passage at the meta-layer)
- Per-season vocabulary MUST NOT echo Earth-realm classical-elements labels (per doc 37 § 6)

**Future canonical design docs** should:
- Reference this doc when introducing concepts that touch the cosmology
- Defer to this doc on cosmological matters when locked items here conflict with prior framings elsewhere
- Add new cosmological elements (if any) by amending this doc, not by introducing parallel framings

- It does **not** override any locked mechanical decision in file 28, file 32, or file 33. Where this doc and those docs use different *names* for the same thing (e.g., "Mirror" vs "doppelganger"), this doc's name is the player-facing canonical; the older docs' names can remain in engine-side fields and telemetry during transition.
- It does **not** specify the mechanical-signature pool (doc 37 § 6.3 high-stakes open), the pair-structure exposure shape (doc 37 § 6.5), or other cipher-architecture implementation specifics. The cosmology layer presupposes the cipher architecture works; the implementation of the cipher remains rocket/star-lord/gamora territory.

## Maintenance protocol

This doc is the cosmological canonical reference. To extend:

1. **Add new cosmological elements** by appending sections (not by editing existing locked sections silently).
2. **Refine existing sections** by adding subsections that supersede prior framings — preserve the canonical-lock-date history.
3. **When tension surfaces** between this doc and downstream docs, this doc wins on cosmology; downstream docs win on mechanics. If the tension is genuinely cosmological-not-mechanical, escalate to Matt for resolution.
4. **When LLM prompt audits** reveal Earth-realm-classical-element leakage (per doc 37 § 6) or cosmology-vocabulary leakage (per this doc), the audit findings file a remediation against THIS doc as the canonical reference for what the prompts should say instead.

The discipline this doc enables is the same discipline doc 37 § 9.1 codified as Discipline #13 candidate (implicit-pillar drift): a load-bearing pillar that is *structurally enforced* by being named, locked, and referenced. Without this doc, every season's LLM call invents its own cosmology by default. With it, the cosmology is enforced by reference.
