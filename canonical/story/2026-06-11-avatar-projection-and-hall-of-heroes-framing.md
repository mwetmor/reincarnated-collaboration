## 1. The recognition

The relationship between the Earth Self and the reincarnated seasonal forms is **projection, not replacement**. The canonical genre anchor is **Warframe's Operator model**: the persistent human in the link, projecting consciousness into powered frames. The frame fights; the Operator *is*. Reincarnated's seasonal journey is the same structure wearing isekai clothing: the Earth Self never stops being the player's identity — the seasonal spirit form is an **avatar projected into**, lived fully for a season, and released.

This resolves a question the 2026-05-11 initial capture left open ("body-swap" was the provisional verb): body-swap implied vacating one body for another. **Projection** is the correct model — the Earth Self remains the continuous thread, and the form is worn, not become. Matt confirmed this framing and the vocabulary below on 2026-06-10.

## 2. Canonical vocabulary (locked)

| Term | Meaning | Genre anchor |

| **Earth Self** | The persistent human in the link; the player's continuous identity; an ordinary modern person | Warframe Operator |

| **Molting** | Seasonal death / season-end release of a form. NOT identity-loss — the Earth Self persists; the form ascends or passes | — |

**The two-space distinction is load-bearing:** the cosmograph sky (possibility — vast, generative, consulted at creation) and the Hall of Heroes (possession — sparse at first, earned, ≤1 ascension/season) are different places with different emotional registers. The sky is wonder and choice; the hall is history and pride. Conflating them in any surface (UI, scene design, copy) is a design error.

## 3. Why this framing is thematically correct

1. **It makes seasonal death survivable as a story.** If the player IS the form, season-end is erasure — the D3-seasonal problem of "my character stops mattering." If the player *projects into* the form, season-end is **molting**: the form's life completes, the most meaningful life ascends to the hall, and the Earth Self — the actual protagonist — carries forward. Loss becomes harvest.
2. **It makes the gacha-accumulation emotionally coherent.** A library of "old characters" is a graveyard. A **Hall of Heroes** is a court — each ascended form a life the player lived to its best end. Warframe proved players form durable attachment to a *roster* when the persistent self stands outside it.
3. **It honors the isekai contract.** The genre's spine (Mushoku Tensei, Slime) is continuity-of-self across bodies. The Earth Self-as-Operator gives Reincarnated that spine structurally, not just in flavor text.
4. **It future-proofs the Rift.** Post-Phase-0 Rift events (PVP/PVE with library spirits) need a "who is fighting?" answer. Projection answers it: the Earth Self fields heroes from the hall — the Pokemon-trainer / MOBA-roster shapes Matt sketched 2026-05-11 all compose with projection; none compose with body-swap-as-replacement.

## 4. Build consequences (flagged to Radagast / David-H — PC seam)

Two architectural consequences for the UE manifestation work. Both are framing constraints NOW so spike-wave work does not harden the wrong assumptions:

### 4.1 The manifestation is a RECURRING transition, not a one-time birth cinematic
Every session begins on Earth (Matt 2026-05-11: "they always begin on Earth"). The jump-in — Earth Self → seasonal form — therefore happens **every play session**, and jump-out happens at every session end or season boundary. Build implications:
- The transition must be **cheap, repeatable, and interruptible** — a 10-second ritual that stays beautiful on the 200th viewing (Warframe transference: ~2 s, never skipped because never resented). A 90-second one-shot cinematic is the wrong target.
- The *creation* moment (first projection of a season — the full cosmograph scene) is the **ceremonial maximal case** of the same transition, not a different system. Stage the creation scene so its core transform (S2→S4 staged-register chain) is reusable as the routine jump-in at reduced ceremony.

### 4.2 The persistent realm is a RETURNABLE HUB that starts sparse
The Earth-side space (knoll / eventual hall) is not a menu — it is a **place the player returns to** between and during seasons. Build implications:
- It must read as intentional when nearly empty (season 1: an ordinary person, a night sky, no heroes yet) and scale gracefully as the hall fills (≤1 form/season — sparse for a long time).
- Sparse-by-design is an asset, not a gap: the empty hall is the promise. Genre anchor: Warframe's Orbiter, Hades' House — hubs that work at zero unlocks.
- UE-side: the knoll scene IS the embryonic hub. Do not architect it as a disposable creation-flow level.
