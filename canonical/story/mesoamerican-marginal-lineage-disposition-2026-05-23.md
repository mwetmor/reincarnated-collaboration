## 0. TL;DR

The Phase E-1 frame-revision clustering produces **no coherent mesoamerican-isolate cluster**. Per the dispatch's Open Question 4 framing, cluster-distribution check was performed to determine whether mesoamerican has a coherent home (substrate-coherent-but-marginal-pool-size framing) or scatters (no-coherent-home framing). The empirical answer is **scattered**: 83 rows distribute across 16 clusters as follows:

The largest mesoamerican footprint is Cluster 112 at 19 of 115 rows = 16.5%. By comparison, "coherent home" thresholds elsewhere in the substrate look like: south_am Cluster 87 at 94.4% purity, arctic Cluster 24 at 88.2% purity. 16.5% is **not** a substrate-led coherent home — it's a "largest passenger absorption" finding.

Per the dispatch's Open Question 4: **mesoamerican framing is "no coherent cluster home," not "substrate-coherent but marginal pool size."** The pool is above the stratified-subsample floor (83 > 20), but the substrate fragments rather than density-connecting; 33 subsample rows distributed across 16 clusters yields a mean per-cluster footprint of 2.06 rows, well below any clustering threshold.

Within Cluster 112 the 19 mesoamerican-tagged rows are **predominantly modern Mexican arms-industry hardware + cross-tagged non-Mexican items + one Pre-Columbian artifact** (Obsidian Blades with Gold Handle). The lineage-tag is again capturing Mexican-geographic-origin (Mendoza/Cabañas/Mexican Mauser/Zaragoza/Soloflex/Mexican-deployed-bombs) rather than Mesoamerican-cultural-tradition.

The 7 macuahuitl variants in Cluster 69 (a 502-row cross_cultural mixed-munitions pool) are genuinely Pre-Columbian Mesoamerican items but appear as passengers (1.6% within-cluster). The 2 Hummingbird Bloodletters in Cluster 109 are Aztec ceremonial bloodletting implements (genuine cultural items) but again passengers.

Mesoamerican is the **most-counterintuitive** of the 5 indigenous-rare-lineage cases: the lineage pool is the second-largest (83 rows; only south_am is bigger at 197), the substrate contains the most genuine Pre-Columbian items (~12-15 of 83 = ~17% cultural-tradition coverage; highest absolute count and percentage of the 5 cases), AND there is still no coherent cultural-tradition cluster because the items scatter across 5+ clusters at 1-7 rows each. The substrate has enough culturally-coherent items to *almost* cluster (a hypothetical macuahuitl + Tlaximaltepoztli + Macehead + obsidian-bladed-weapons pool would be ~15 items, above mcs=10), but the axis-discovery + subsample-stratification produced clustering that spread them rather than concentrating them.

The disposition is:

1. **No coherent mesoamerican cluster home exists** (per Open Question 4 resolution). 16.5% top-cluster within-cluster percentage is passenger absorption, not substrate-led isolate.
2. **Architectural commitments to a "Aztec / Maya / Olmec / Mesoamerican cultural-tradition" Spirit Form, faction, or Phase E-3 category are deferred** pending empirical triggers in § 4.
3. **Substrate-tagging-discipline cleanup (sub-carry 9.11-E) is the corrective path for the Mexican-geographic-vs-Mesoamerican-cultural conflation**, parallel to south_am and arctic but with the additional opportunity that the substrate has more genuine cultural items to surface.
4. **Mesoamerican has the highest reclamation-potential among the marginal lineages.** With ~12-15 cultural items already present in the 89.8K-row substrate (highest absolute count of the 5 cases), targeted re-tagging + modest substrate expansion (~80-90 additional Pre-Columbian items targeting macuahuitl variants, atlatl-spear-throwers, Aztec / Maya / Olmec ceremonial weapons, obsidian-bladed weapons, Toltec/Mixtec/Zapotec material) could plausibly cross the clustering threshold. This is sub-carry 9.11-E + 9.10-E joint-fire territory.

The discipline: **a coherent cluster requires both a cultural-content tail AND axis-coherence at the chosen k.** Mesoamerican has the cultural-content tail (12-15 items) but the axes 1-3 at k=3 do not concentrate them. Sub-carry 9.11-E re-tagging + Phase E-1.5 sensitivity sweep + possible substrate expansion are the layered paths.

## 2. What this disposition is NOT

This disposition is **not**:

1. **Not a rejection of any Phase E-2 cluster label.** No Phase E-2 cluster was labelled "Mesoamerican" — the cluster-labels artifact correctly captured that there is no coherent mesoamerican cluster.
2. **Not a substrate-cleaning prescription.** Sub-carries 9.11-D and 9.11-E are elrond's queue.
3. **Not a statement about the design value of Mesoamerican weapon-making traditions.** Aztec macuahuitl + Maya wooden longsword + Mixtec atlatl + Olmec stone-axes + Toltec obsidian weapons + Zapotec ceremonial blades are extraordinarily rich design seeds; the Aztec military traditions (Eagle Knights, Jaguar Knights, *cuachicqueh* "shorn ones" elite warriors) are mythically resonant Fate-genre faction material. The recognition is about the **current 89.8K-row substrate as currently tagged**, not about the eventual game.
4. **Not a clustering-methodology criticism.** Lowering mcs would not surface a coherent Pre-Columbian Mesoamerican cluster because the items are spread across axis-positions adjacent to modern Mexican hardware; the substrate-tagging issue must be resolved upstream for clustering to be informative.
