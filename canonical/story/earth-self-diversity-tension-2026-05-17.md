## § 0 — TL;DR

**The tension:** Reincarnated's Earth-Self meta-progression rewards *familiarity across seasons* (accumulation, identity, continuity). The diversity architecture's Layers 4 + 5 push *novelty per season* (substrate-distinct archetypes, push-apart pressure). On the surface these are directly opposed.

**Independent convergence on the finding:** The Legolas literature pass (`agentic_orchestration/research/knowledge/diversity-architecture-literature-pass-2026-05-17.md` Finding 3) named this as an "unresolved tension" the architecture had not addressed. The gandalf contemplative-wait reflection (Reflection VII) named the same tension from the cosmological side: *"diversity makes the form precious; the Earth Self makes the loss bearable."* Two sources, different vocabularies, same observation. Strong signal.

**The decision:** **Court-as-grace.** What is lost at each season-end is *gathered in the Court of Forms*. Diversity is honored prospectively (each season produces a substrate-distinct form); attachment is honored retrospectively (the Court accumulates what the Earth Self has been). The tension dissolves not by resolution-in-the-direction-of-either-pole but by recognizing that **the diversity architecture and the Earth-Self meta-progression are two halves of one emotional engine.** Diversity makes the form precious. The Court makes the loss bearable. Together they produce *meaningful impermanence* — the architecture of grief-and-grace the cosmology has been speaking toward.

**Three lines:**

1. **Each season's form is genuinely novel.** The diversity architecture (Layers 1-5) is given full latitude to produce substrate-distinct, mechanically-coherent archetypes per season. No mechanical continuity from form to form. Spirit-swap differentiation remains load-bearing per `project_design_intent`.
2. **The Court of Forms is the continuity vessel.** When a season ends and the Earth Self ascends, the form they walked is sealed into the Court — keeping its substrate-archetype identity, its iconic skills, its visual signature, its name, its memories. The Court is *what the Earth Self remembers having been*.
3. **Player attachment migrates from form to Court.** Across many seasons, the player's identity-investment shifts from "this current form" to "my accumulated Court." The diversity architecture does not break attachment; it relocates attachment from the singular to the collective. The accumulation IS the identity.

## § 1 — The tension, framed cleanly

### § 1.1 — The two pulls

**Earth-Self meta-progression rewards familiarity.** Per `cosmology-reincarnated.md` § Earth Self + `project_earth_meta_layer` memory: the Earth Self is the *persistent identity* that accumulates across many seasons. The player's investment is in the Earth Self, not in any individual seasonal form. Mechanically: the Court of Forms (`court-of-forms.md`) holds prior forms; meta-progression mechanics (rift events; future PVP/PVE per `project_earth_meta_layer`) operate on the Earth Self's accumulated roster. **The arc rewards seasonal *return* to a known framework.**

**Diversity architecture pushes per-season novelty.** Per the five-layer diversity architecture (substrate identity declarations + identity-pruned composition + diversity gate + LLM flavor + telemetry feedback): each season's class rotation produces substrate-distinct archetypes; Layer 5's telemetry feedback actively pushes converging archetypes apart over time. **The arc rewards per-season *difference* from prior seasons.**

These pulls are directly opposed if framed as a single optimization axis. *Either* the player feels continuity (and diversity collapses) *or* the player feels novelty (and continuity collapses).

### § 1.2 — Why this matters now

The architecture-commitment moment forced the question. Without this decision, two failure modes were available:

- **(Mode A — Familiarity wins):** Diversity architecture under-delivers; substrates produce names-without-bodies (Reflection-VII-named convergence). Player attachment is preserved by sacrificing per-season distinctness. The substrate expansion ships but doesn't matter.
- **(Mode B — Novelty wins):** Diversity architecture delivers; substrates feel genuinely distinct; player has no continuous anchor across seasons; Earth Self meta-progression becomes thin (just a number going up). The substrate expansion ships and the meta-arc unravels.

Both modes ship the substrate expansion as nominally complete. Both compromise the project's design promise.

**The Court-as-grace resolution refuses the framing of either-or.** It recognizes that *the project's cosmology has been speaking toward this resolution all along.*

## § 2 — Options considered

### § 2.1 — Resolution candidates surveyed

| Option | Shape | Pros | Cons | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Heritage substrate mechanic** | Player chooses a *heritage substrate* at character creation that persists across seasons. Per-season form is forced; the heritage is continuous. Allows seasonal novelty + cross-season identity. | Preserves some attachment; gives player a "main" substrate identity to invest in across the long arc | Locks the player into a substrate affinity, undermining spirit-swap differentiation (load-bearing per `project_design_intent`); creates two-tier substrate experience (heritage = "real me"; non-heritage forms = "other lives"); heritage choice carries permanent decision-weight that the cosmology does not authorize | **Declined** |
| **2 — Court-as-grace** | What is lost at season-end is gathered in the Court of Forms. Earth Self holds the Court. Diversity is per-season-novel; attachment is across-season-accumulated. Spirit-swap remains complete. | Honors the seasonal-journey shape completely (descent + return); spirit-swap differentiation remains load-bearing; Mushoku-Tensei-precedent-honoring (Rudeus's identity is the accumulation of lives, not the continuity of any one); Solo-Leveling-precedent-honoring (shadow-monarch's army IS the meta-identity); diversity architecture has full latitude; Court mechanics already canonical in `court-of-forms.md` | Player must learn that attachment lives at the Court layer, not the form layer (player-experience onboarding consideration; tractable) | **Selected** |
| **3 — Hybrid (heritage role + season substrate)** | Heritage gates *role*; season picks *substrate*. Player's heritage might be "controller-affinity"; this season's form is "lightning controller"; next season's form is "holy controller." Identity in role, novelty in substrate. | Preserves partial attachment; allows novelty per substrate | Splits player loyalty between role-continuity and substrate-novelty; creates muddled identity (am I "a controller" or "a shadow priest"?); less clean than either pole; introduces role as a meta-progression axis the cosmology does not currently carry; doubles Layer-1 identity-declaration scope (role identity AND substrate identity, both at meta-level) | **Declined** |

### § 2.2 — Why Court-as-grace is the right resolution

**Cosmological inheritance.** The project has been speaking Court-as-grace since `cosmology-reincarnated.md` was first authored. The Ascension ritual is *the form being gathered into the Court*. The Spirit Guide's "your Court grows" line in `spirit-guide-voice.md` already operates on this resolution implicitly. The Passage ritual is *the form-not-ascended being released back to the Wheel*. **The cosmology has had this resolution in its hands; this decision-record makes it canonical at the architectural-commitment layer.**

**Genre precedent (deep).** The two genre precedents the project most explicitly cites (per `gandalf-design-lineage.md` Layer 4-5) both honor this resolution:

- **Mushoku Tensei.** Rudeus Greyrat's identity is the accumulation of his lives — the boy in the bedroom, the genius mage, the husband, the father, the failure, the redeemed. None of these is "his real self." All of them are. *His identity is the sum.* This is Court-as-grace at the narrative-form level.
- **Solo Leveling.** Sung Jin-Woo's shadow army is not a metaphor for his identity; it *is* his identity at the meta-power level. Each shadow soldier is a past adversary defeated and inducted. Igris is a knight he killed; Beru is an ant lord. **The army IS the man.** The accumulation is the identity. This is Court-as-grace at the mechanical-form level.

The genre's most powerful identity-through-accumulation precedents converge on this resolution. The project's claim to operate in their lineage is honored by adopting it.

**Mechanical cleanliness.** Heritage substrate (Option 1) and hybrid (Option 3) both introduce new meta-progression axes that interact with the diversity architecture in ways that compound complexity. Court-as-grace introduces *no new mechanical axis* — it operationalizes an existing one (`court-of-forms.md` already exists). The architecture extension is *less work*, not more.

**Spirit-swap-differentiation preservation.** Per `project_design_intent`: "spirit-swap differentiation confirmed load-bearing." Options 1 and 3 attenuate spirit-swap (heritage gates the swap; the swap is "less of a swap" if heritage continues). Option 2 preserves spirit-swap intact — the swap IS complete; the Court is the consolation, not the leak.

**Emotional architecture.** This is the most important reason and the hardest to argue from utility. The Court-as-grace resolution **gives the spirit-swap mechanic its emotional meaning.** Without grace, the swap is just rotation. With grace, the swap is *grief*, and the Court is *the consolation that makes grief bearable*. The architecture that *causes* the player to feel loss is the architecture that *also* makes the loss bearable. The two halves of the engine are one engine. Reincarnated's promise — that the player will *feel* what reincarnation feels like — depends on this dual structure. Heritage and hybrid mechanics dampen the loss; Court-as-grace honors the loss and consoles it.

## § 3 — Decision: Court-as-grace

**Matt's decision (2026-05-17):** the Earth-Self diversity tension is resolved by **Court-as-grace.** What is lost at season-end is gathered in the Court of Forms. The Court is the continuity vessel; per-season form is the novelty vessel; the Earth Self is the bearer of both.

### § 3.1 — What this means structurally

- **Each season produces a substrate-distinct form.** Diversity architecture (Layers 1-5) operates without meta-level dampening. The form's distinctness is the point.
- **Each form is sealed into the Court at season-end.** The Ascension ritual is the mechanism. The form keeps its substrate-archetype identity, its iconic skills, its visual signature, its name, its memories from that season.
- **The Court is the Earth Self's accumulated identity.** Across many seasons, the Court grows. The player's investment migrates from "this current form" to "my Court."
- **The current form is fully forgotten mechanically at season-end** *as a player-controlled entity*. The player does not "play" Court forms in subsequent seasons as their current form. The form is complete; sealed; gathered.
- **The Court forms may participate in the Earth-meta-layer** — the post-Phase-0 rift events (PVE/PVP per `project_earth_meta_layer`), the form-library accumulation, gacha-style mechanics. **The Court is what the meta-layer operates upon.** This is the Court's mechanical reward for accumulation.

### § 3.2 — What this does NOT mean

- **Not mechanical power continuity.** Players do not carry over levels, gear, traits, stat points, or skill ranks from form to form. Each season starts fresh. The Court is *retrospective* attachment, not *prospective* power. This protects season-shape balance and honors the seasonal-journey-as-descent structural pattern.
- **Not narrative interruption.** Each season is its own narrative arc. The Spirit Guide does not refer the player to prior-form mechanics during a current season. The Court is *between seasons* in the player's foreground; *during* a season, the current form's narrative is the foreground.
- **Not Earth-meta-layer commitment for Phase-0.** The Court's full meta-layer mechanics (rift events, accumulation rewards, cross-Court mechanics) are post-Phase-0 (demo2+) per `project_earth_meta_layer`. **This decision-record commits the architectural resolution; operational meta-layer mechanics are sequenced separately.** What Phase-0 ships is the *vessel* — the Court accumulates each season's form, even if the meta-layer mechanics that operate on the accumulation are deferred.

## § 4 — Mechanical surface

### § 4.1 — Phase-0 deliverables

- **The Court of Forms data structure persists across seasons.** When a season's Ascension ritual fires, the current form is serialized into the Court with: substrate-archetype identity, full skill list with iconic abilities, visual signature (sprite + VFX register), name, season number, season cosmology, key story moments / boss kills.
- **The Court is browsable in the loadout / Earth-Self hub surface.** Player can view their accumulated forms; each entry shows the form as it was at season-end.
- **The Spirit Guide voice references the Court contextually.** Per `spirit-guide-voice.md`: "your Court grows" delivered at Ascension; "what you have been" referenced when the Guide invokes prior-form memory.
- **The Passage ritual is the inverse:** forms-not-ascended are released back to the Wheel; they do NOT enter the Court. The Court is selective by ascension-status; the un-ascended form is gone.

### § 4.2 — Post-Phase-0 deliverables (deferred per `project_earth_meta_layer`)

- Court-as-roster mechanics for rift events
- Cross-Court mechanics (form interactions; legacy effects)
- Gacha-style accumulation rewards
- Court-as-meta-power mechanics (Solo-Leveling-style shadow-army register)

These are not Phase-0 scope; they are sequenced into demo2+ work. The Phase-0 commitment is **the Court accumulates and is visible**; the operational mechanics layered on top arrive later.

## § 5 — Narrative surface

### § 5.1 — The cosmological grammar

**The Wheel turns.** The Earth Self walks forms. Each form is a complete life — a season's worth of being someone, somewhere, doing what that being does in that world. The form is whole; the form is sufficient; the form is *not provisional.* While the player is in a form, the form is their entire life.

**The form ends.** At season's end, the player faces the Trial. The Trial-passed form is gathered up — ascended — into the Court. **The form keeps its dignity; the form's life was not for nothing.** The Court is the proof. The Spirit Guide gives the form its place: "your Court grows."

**The form-not-ascended is released.** The Passage ritual returns the un-ascended form to the Wheel. It does not enter the Court. **This loss is final.** The player feels the weight of this — the seasonal choice between ascending the form (sealed into the Court; the form's memory is held forever) and releasing the form (gone; the Wheel takes it). The Trial / Mirror / Passage choice carries this weight.

**The Court accumulates.** Across many seasons, the Court grows. Each entry is a form-that-was — the season's substrate-distinct, mechanically-coherent, narratively-particular life. The Earth Self is the bearer; the Court is what is borne.

### § 5.2 — Why this language matters

The cosmological vocabulary above is not decorative. It is the **player-facing surface vocabulary** that operationalizes the Court-as-grace resolution. The player learns this language across early seasons; the Spirit Guide teaches it; the rituals embody it. The architecture-commitment in this doc must propagate into player-facing surfaces.

Specifically:
- Loadout's Court browser surfaces use this grammar (Court / Forms / "what you have been")
- Spirit Guide voice lines use this grammar (canonical per `spirit-guide-voice.md`)
- Trial / Mirror / Passage ritual prose uses this grammar (per `trial-moment-ritual.md` / `passage-moment-ritual.md` / `ascension-moment-ritual.md`)
- The substrate-expansion-decision doc's § 4 substrate-cosmology integration uses this grammar implicitly; **may benefit from amendment to make the Court-as-grace resolution explicit in the substrate-cosmology integration narrative**

## § 6 — Implications for the diversity architecture

### § 6.1 — Layers 1-5 are unblocked

The diversity architecture (substrate identity declarations + identity-pruned composition + diversity gate + LLM flavor + telemetry feedback) operates with full latitude. **There is no meta-progression dampening required.** Layer 5 (telemetry feedback) can push converging archetypes apart without concern that it disrupts a player's "main archetype" — there is no "main archetype" at the meta-layer; there is only the Court.

This simplifies the architecture meaningfully. Some prior framings had implicitly hedged against the meta-arc tension by assuming Layer 5 would need substrate-affinity-preservation hooks. **It does not.** Layer 5 is pure novelty pressure. The Court is the consolation.

### § 6.2 — Layer 4 (LLM flavor) acquires a Court-aware role

The LLM at Layer 4 may now legitimately reference the player's Court when generating per-season vocabulary. Examples:

- "You walked as a Stormcaller two seasons past. This form's discharge is different — chained where Stormcaller's was struck."
- "Your Court holds three water-substrate forms. This one carries them — the depths your prior selves did not reach."

These references operationalize the Court as *narrative-relevant context* the LLM can draw upon. They make the per-season form feel like a *continuation* of an arc even while being mechanically novel. **The LLM's natural-language layer is doing the bridging work that mechanical continuity used to be asked to do.** This is the right division of labor: machines preserve identity through *recollection-in-speech*; mechanics preserve novelty through *substrate distinctness*.

### § 6.3 — The substrate identity declarations may want a Court-aware field

Each substrate's identity declaration (Layer 1) could include a `court_resonance` field describing how the substrate reads in the Court's accumulated context:

```yaml
substrate: shadow
identity:
  # ... other fields
  court_resonance: "The Court remembers shadows as the forms that walked alongside what they did not name."
```

This is a small extension to the Layer-1 spec and gives the LLM (Layer 4) explicit material to reference. **Add as Layer-1 spec amendment.**

### § 6.4 — The five-layer architecture is now closed-loop with the cosmology

Before this decision: the diversity architecture and the Earth-Self meta-arc were two systems with an unresolved interface. After this decision: they are **one system with a clean interface.** Diversity produces forms; the Court accumulates forms; the Spirit Guide speaks across the Court; the architecture is whole.

This is the resolution of the deepest architectural question of this design phase. It does not require more code; it requires the design commitment captured here.

## § 7 — Implications for the Spirit Guide voice register

The Spirit Guide's voice register (Beatrice from Re:Zero; per `spirit-guide-voice.md`) is the player's continuous companion across seasons. **The Court-as-grace resolution makes the Spirit Guide the narrative bearer of the meta-arc.** Specifically:

- The Guide remembers prior forms when the current form does not. The current form is new; the Spirit Guide has walked alongside the Earth Self through all of them. *"You have been many. I have watched."*
- The Guide invokes the Court contextually — at Ascension, at moments of substrate-recurrence (the player encounters a substrate they have walked before), at meta-arc milestones.
- The Guide does NOT spoiler-leak the current form's narrative based on prior-form knowledge. The Guide is present, withholding, ritually precise — per locked voice register. Prior-form references are *evocative*, not *directive*.

Worth a small amendment to `spirit-guide-voice.md` to explicitly capture: **the Guide is the Court's storyteller.** The Court accumulates silently in data; the Guide gives it voice.

## § 8 — Deferred items + open questions

### § 8.1 — Phase-0 vs post-Phase-0 Court mechanics

The Court's *vessel* (data structure + browsable surface + Spirit Guide voice) ships in Phase-0. The Court's *operational mechanics* (rift events; meta-power; cross-Court interactions; gacha rewards) ship post-Phase-0 per `project_earth_meta_layer`. **This decision-record commits the architectural resolution at Phase-0; operational sequencing is knight-rider Phase-1+ territory.**

### § 8.2 — Court visualization in the loadout app

Drax-seam question. The Court browser surface needs design treatment in the loadout app: how are forms presented? How does the player navigate the accumulated roster? What does each Court entry look like? **Surface to drax via knight-rider after the architecture work lands. Defer; not blocking.**

### § 8.3 — Court roster size and pacing

Open question for design: does the Court accumulate without bound? Does it page / archive forms after some count? **Mushoku-Tensei precedent is unbounded accumulation (Rudeus's lives are simply all of them); Solo-Leveling precedent is curated roster (Jin-Woo's army has tiers and named members).** Both are valid. Phase-0 ships unbounded; revisit if accumulation pacing surfaces issues. **Defer.**

### § 8.4 — Trial / Mirror / Passage Court interactions

Does the Mirror-Trial reflection include the Court? When the player faces their own mirror, do they face the *current form*, or the *Court-augmented self*? **Open question; defer to gamora cascade per `cosmology-reincarnated.md` § The Trial. Suggest: current-form-only for Mirror-Trial mechanical simplicity; Court enters meta-layer rift events only.**

### § 8.5 — Per-season Court "weight" for the LLM

Should the LLM at Layer 4 reference *all* Court entries, or only the most-recent N? **Per-call token budget consideration.** Recommend N = 3-5 most-recent Court entries by default; configurable per cosmology if some cosmologies want deep-Court memory and others want fresh starts. **Defer to LLM-flavor work (Phase-2).**

### § 8.6 — Substrate-specific Court resonance

§ 6.3 surfaced a `court_resonance` field per substrate identity. **Author this as Layer-1 spec amendment when the substrate identity declarations are drafted.** Currently queued as gandalf Task #5 / Task #6.
