## 0. TL;DR

The Phase E-1 frame-revision clustering produces **one substrate-led arctic_circumpolar-labelled cluster**: Cluster 24 (db_cluster_id 24; HDBSCAN id 23) at 88.2% purity / N=34 (30 arctic_circumpolar rows + 4 middle_eastern). On raw clustering metrics this is a healthy rare-lineage isolate. Phase E-2 labelled it "Arctic/Northern Contemporary Heavy Weapon Systems" with cluster_type `modern_military_hardware_pool` and override notes already capturing the lineage-tagging issue.

The rep audit confirms the cluster is contemporary military hardware whose lineage-tag captures **arctic-geographic-deployment** (cold-weather-rated equipment; northern-hemisphere arms manufacturers; cold-war-northern-front systems), not **circumpolar cultural tradition**:

- 2S1 Gvozdika — Russian 122mm Amphibious Self-Propelled Howitzer
- RBS-70 / RBS 70 NG — Swedish Man-Portable Air Defense Missile Systems (MANPADS)
- Mistral 3 — French Man-Portable Air Defense Missile System
- ENVOY — Canadian Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV)
- CV 9040 / CV 9030 — Swedish Infantry Fighting Vehicles
- GAZ-2975 Tigr — Russian 4×4 combat support vehicle
- Tor M2DT — Russian Amphibious Short-Range Air Defense Missile System
- Orkan — Russian Autonomous Surface Vehicle
- AW50 / AW50F — British 12.7mm Anti-Material Rifles
- JRTC VISMOD: GAZ-2975 Tigr — US training-environment Russian-vehicle visual modification

True culturally-circumpolar items (Sámi spear, Inuit harpoon, Yupik kayak-throwing-board, Aleut bone weapons, Chukchi composite bow, Saami knife) appear as **zero items** in the audited reps. The 56-row lineage pool is functionally a "weapons used in northern climes" pool, not a "weapons crafted by circumpolar indigenous peoples" pool.

The remaining 26 of 56 arctic-tagged rows scatter across 7 other clusters (109 @ 12 rows, 49 @ 4, 88 @ 4, 120 @ 3, 50 / 102 / 112 @ 1 each), none of which are culturally-circumpolar.

The disposition is:

1. **Cluster 24 canonical label stands.** Phase E-2 labelled it "Arctic/Northern Contemporary Heavy Weapon Systems" with override-reason and framing_audit_notes already capturing this tagging issue. The label is substrate-descriptively correct for what the substrate actually contains; renaming would manufacture coherence in the opposite direction.
2. **No "Sámi / Inuit / Yupik / Aleut / Chukchi circumpolar cultural-tradition" cluster exists.** The cultural-circumpolar representation in the substrate is ~0 items at audited resolution — five orders of magnitude below what clustering needs.
3. **Architectural commitments to a "Circumpolar Indigenous cultural-tradition" Spirit Form, faction, or Phase E-3 category are deferred** pending the empirical-evidence criteria in § 4. Cluster 24 is a valid substrate-led cluster; it describes northern-climate modern military hardware, not Sámi/Inuit/Yupik warrior-culture, and no design surface should inherit "the substrate supports a circumpolar indigenous faction" from this evidence.
4. **Substrate-tagging-discipline cleanup (sub-carry 9.11-E)** is the corrective path for the geographic-vs-cultural conflation. Elrond's queue.

The discipline: **arctic-geographic-deployment is not arctic-cultural-tradition.** The substrate's lineage-tag vocabulary collapses these two concepts under one label, producing a cluster whose Phase E-2 label is technically accurate AND substrate-descriptively-correct AND design-load-bearing-misleading for any downstream surface that interprets "arctic_circumpolar cluster" as "circumpolar cultural-tradition warrior class."

True culturally-circumpolar items audited: **zero in the top-12 reps, zero in the additional confirmed Cluster 24 sample**. ### 1.4 Why this is substrate-tagging-discipline failure, not clustering failure

The clustering algorithm found a tight density-connected region for arctic-tagged rows (88.2% purity is high; the cluster has clear axis-coherence). The substrate-led discipline produced a real cluster. Phase E-2's labelling correctly named it as modern military hardware (overriding the broken provisional "lance/rifle" label per Finding 7 from the labeler bug).

The failure is at the upstream tagging discipline: the `arctic_circumpolar` lineage tag was being applied to "cold-weather-deployed" / "northern-arms-manufactured" / "operational in arctic theater" rather than to "weapon-making tradition of circumpolar indigenous peoples." This is the cleanest example among the 4 marginal lineages of substrate-tagging cross-talk because:

- The cluster IS coherent (high purity, clear identity)
- The cluster's identity IS substrate-honest (contemporary northern military hardware)
- The lineage-tag IS misleading about what the cluster describes (no Sámi/Inuit/Yupik content)

Phase E-2's override notes already captured this: *"Substrate-honest arctic_circumpolar coherent weapons cluster does NOT exist at this k."* This disposition extends that observation into an architectural-commitment-deferred recognition record.

## 5. The geographic-vs-cultural tagging discipline (arctic perspective)

Arctic_circumpolar is the load-bearing positive-control case for the meta-pattern documented in `canonical/story/marginal-lineage-tagging-pattern-2026-05-23.md`. Where south_am illustrates "94% purity but 2 cultural items," arctic illustrates "88% purity and 0 cultural items" — the geographic-tag-as-cultural-lineage collapse without any cultural-tradition contamination to muddy the signal.

This makes arctic the cleanest case to cite when explaining the tagging-discipline gap to a fresh reader: a cluster reading 88.2% pure on a lineage tagged "arctic_circumpolar" turns out, on rep audit, to be Russian self-propelled howitzers and Swedish MANPADS. The cluster is real; the lineage's *cultural meaning* is not what the tag implies.
