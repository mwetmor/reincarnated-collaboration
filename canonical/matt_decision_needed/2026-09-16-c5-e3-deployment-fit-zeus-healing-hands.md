## Forks that remain — one recommendation each; rule with a letter
- **F3 Holy glyph.** The painted seal is a hexagram; the source has *no* seal at all. **Recommend (a): drop the seal from Healing Hands** (the burst is ring + petals at the ground point — per source; the seal primitive stays in the alphabet for a future aura). *Alt (b):* repaint with a non-denominational sigil (2 images) and keep it as register. *Alt (c):* keep the hexagram.
- **F4 FF-08 (anti-metronome jitter).** Every source cadence is a clean metronome; FF-08 exists only in our specs, mis-labelled as observation. **Recommend (a): re-declare FF-08 as a HOUSE rule scoped to the *painted* layer only** — residue erosion, ember timing, lick flicker — and let mechanics cadences (orb emission, pool ticks, hops) follow the source metronomes. *Alt (b):* drop FF-08 entirely. *Alt (c):* keep it universal as a deliberate deviation.
- **F5 Healing Hands radius + post-1.4 look.** *Frames (pre-1.4): a filled golden dome 3.4–3.6 BH wide, peak 0.15 s, gone 0.40 s — the original 220 px radius matched; T4x's 160 reverts.* Nobody publishes the gameplay radius; all footage predates the 2026-03-26 VFX change. **Recommend (a): author 1.5 BH radius (≈ 200 px) as a register choice and mark it AUTHORED, not sourced**; you source post-1.4 Paladin footage when convenient. *Alt (b):* wait for the footage before building.
- **F6 Concoction residue — RULED (b) by Matt 2026-09-16: the full composite** — lobbed vial burst (gem) + the caustic-ground / Alchemist's Mark dark puddle spreading from the burst point ("giant black puddles" — the build as played). Numbers from the class-E frame measurements. R-C5-80. **Correction after the frames (R-C5-84):** the dark ground in PConc footage is *Plague Bearer* (a player-anchored miasma), not Alchemist's Mark (which grants ailments on flask use and paints nothing) — and the gem alone leaves nothing on the ground. The composite is therefore a **declared house borrow**, authored 2.5 BH / 4 s at the burst point; the ruling stands as a design choice.
- **F7 Aim assist on touch (new, from the v34 target proof).** Forward casts on touch currently ignore targets entirely (every cast resolves as a "cursor" cast to range); with the dummies now real targets, a bolt that passes 0.3 BH in front of a body sails past it. **Recommend (a): 30° cone snap** — a forward cast locks to the nearest target inside a 30° cone within range and flies at it; nothing in the cone → forward at range as now (the Hades / Diablo IV controller convention; the exporter's `resolve_target` already implements the cone). *Alt (b):* no assist, but widen the bolt's sweep radius to ~0.25 BH. *Alt (c):* both.
