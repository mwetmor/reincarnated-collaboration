## §1 — The reconciliation Matt asked for (verdict first)

**Matt's lean SURVIVES the digest — and the digest sharpens it.** The thread contains no argument for dispossession. Its longevity engine — *friction* — lives entirely in **itemization depth** (316 affixes vs 112; niches per tier; junk affixes as sandbox; emergent re-evaluation à la Fortitude-after-Warlock), never in **loss**. Three specific reconciliation points:

1. **Digest §1.5 "Bypassing the gear treadmill" is a PRO-travel argument, verbatim genre canon.** D2 items are not class-bound or character-bound; you farm BiS *for a character you haven't created yet*, and that freedom is named as a core joy engine. RDR's structural analogue is exact: **gear not bound to forms** — you bank a drop for a form you haven't yet risen into. Stuff-doesn't-travel would delete this engine from a game whose whole premise is serial re-embodiment. RDR is *better* positioned than D2 to exploit §1.5, not worse.
2. **But digest §3 + §1.4 defend the FRESH ECONOMY.** The casual counterpoint's on-ramp is *"casually join a new ladder"* — a periodically reset economy is what keeps drops meaningful and re-entry even-footed. Forever-accumulation is the D3-non-season / PoE-Standard graveyard: when everything persists into the active economy forever, drop satisfaction (§1.4's whole taxonomy) dies of inflation.
3. **The genre's own resolution is MIGRATION, not deletion.** D2 ladder→non-ladder; PoE league→Standard. **Nothing is ever confiscated; the ACTIVE economy resets while yield banks to a persistent-but-parked realm.** Both positions in the apparent conflict are right — they're answers to *different boundaries*. Which forces the structural upgrade:

## §2 — The structural upgrade: TWO boundaries, not one

The five surfaces were originally posed against a single "reset" boundary. The digest reconciliation shows the contract must be ruled **per boundary**:

| Boundary | What it is in RDR | ARPG-native answer (genre anchor) |
|---|---|---|
| **B-RUN** — death within a season (the Reap-Die-Rise loop itself) | you die; you rise in a new form; the season continues | **Items survive death. Death taxes PROGRESS, not PROPERTY** (D2 corpse-run recovers gear; PoE deducts XP, never items). No major ARPG deletes items on death outside explicit hardcore modes. |
| **B-SEASON** — the seasonal journey ends | the run of acts concludes; a new season begins | **League/ladder model: active economy fresh-starts; prior yield MIGRATES to the persistent layer** (non-ladder / Standard). RDR's persistent layer already exists in canon: the Earth-Self meta-layer / form library / ARCHIVE. |

**Conductor lean:** B-RUN should be ruled *now-ish* and cheaply — it's genre-universal, story-light, and it's what A5's +3-becoming band actually needs. B-SEASON is where Matt's "not enough story" is correct — it needs the §4 gaps.

## §4 — Story-gaps that gate SURE rulings (why "not enough story" is right)

Named so the story session — whenever it fires — has its agenda pre-cut:

1. **What IS a season's end, diegetically?** A return to Earth? A harvest? Does the realm close behind you? (S5 redeal + S2 migration both hang on this.)
2. **What is the caravan ACROSS seasons?** Within-season companion (T3-F5 ruled it into acts) vs cross-season vessel (S2-a needs it to make the crossing). One line of lore decides.
3. **ARCHIVE: record or vault?** Does it *know* things (codex) or *hold* things (stash)? S1 vs S2 hinge.
4. **What does a new form arrive with?** Naked rise vs inherited kit — the first five minutes of every season.
5. **Does the Earth-Self layer have an economy?** If banked yield is *spendable* between seasons (gacha/shop frame), S2-a's vault gains a function beyond museum.
