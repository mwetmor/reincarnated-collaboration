# Tone and colour measurements, 2026-09-26 (revision 2)

**Method:** CIE L\*a\*b\* (D65), per pixel. Figures are separated from the ground by flood-filling pale, low-chroma background from the frame edges; text and logos are masked. "% ink" is the share of figure pixels darker than L\* 35 (line, hatching and dark costume together). The darkest 2 % column describes the line: hue h ≈ 20–60 is brown or sepia; a low C is neutral black.

**Revision 2:** the FFT oracle is now **five *Final Fantasy Tactics: The Ivalice Chronicles* Steam frames**, supplied by Matt. The earlier composite page is **FFT *Advance*-era art** (its bangaa, viera, moogle and nu mou are FFTA races), so it is demoted to secondary. Revision 1's conclusions are corrected below.

## Reading (corrects revision 1)

1. **FFT figures carry rich colour.** The green cloak has 52 % of pixels above C\* 40, Agrias 29 % and the black mage 22 %, while Ramza and Delita are earthy (3 %). Rich hue is a property of the figures; "dull" is **not** measured from these frames. *(An earlier draft of this revision read the cloud backdrop as FFT's ground. That was wrong and is withdrawn.)* **There is no valid FFT daytime scene reference yet**, so no claim is made here about how FFT scenery compares with our cliffside. The painted-book scene pages (Belles Heures choir mean C\* 10; *Très Riches Heures* July 27) remain the scene references.
2. **FFT's darks are real ink, not lifted.** Its lines reach L\* 1–6, from near-black to dark brown. Revision 1's "no black, line ≈ L\* 8" held for the FFTA page and the painted books, **not for FFT itself**. The painted books keep their lifted darks (L\* 25–33); that is one of the two things the merge has to reconcile.
3. **FFT shades with pen hatching over an uneven wash**, not with smooth gradients: 12–40 % of figure pixels are ink. The hatching and blotchy wash **are** the imperfection Matt is after. **This contradicts the brief's "no hatching on sprites" rule.** The three-way imperfection test (clean / carried by the video model / designed boil) must include hatching as a variable, judged at sprite size in motion.
4. **FFT scenery is very muted**: mean C\* 6 in the night frame, with visible paper grain and dry-brush streaks. That is one night frame, so it is direction, not a daytime value.

## Working target band (revision 2)

- **Scene plate:** no FFT-derived band. Use the painted-book scene pages (Belles Heures choir, *Très Riches Heures* months) as the reference, plus the brief's rule that the walkable floor is the brightest and calmest region. Revisit if an FFT in-game or scenic daytime frame is added.
- **Figure:** L\* 95th ≈ 83–92 (pale highs present); dark ink allowed (L\* 5th ≈ 4–20); saturation set by costume (FFT figures range from 3 % to 52 % of pixels above C\* 40).
- **Line:** dark brown to near-black. Hatching is permitted pending the imperfection test.
- **Merge decision still open:** FFT's near-black ink against the painted book's lifted, warm line. Mint both and let the anchor A/B decide.

**Caveats:** all FFT frames are store or trailer captures (scaled and compressed). Fine texture is softened, so high-frequency texture is **not** comparable across sources; the tone and colour figures are direction, not calibration. The night frame measures a night scene.
