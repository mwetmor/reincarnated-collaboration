# PART I — THE CANON DOCTRINE (Staged Epistemic Expansion)

## I.0 The claim class
**We do not certify fun. We certify conformance to a measured envelope of genre-canonical anchors.** This is a reference-class claim — "this candidate behaves like content millions of players already paid to keep playing" — and reference-class claims are falsifiable in simulation, which fun-claims are not. The fitness metrics (KPM, AOE%, spatial σ, and the Part III battery) are **canon-conformance metrics, not fun metrics**. Public language follows: *"certified to behave within genre-canonical envelopes"* — never *"certified fun."*

## I.1 Stage 1 — Map the canon (current stage)
- The **31+ canonical anchors** (plus gated BC cells) are the empirical reference distribution — canon is the *training set*, not the constraint.
- **Anchors are certified first, in OUR sim**: envelopes are drawn from anchors' *measured-in-our-sim* behavior, never from genre lore about how they played in their home games (canon's numbers were validated inside other games' density, camera, and TTK — the envelope inherits context, so it must be re-measured in ours).
- All emission (identity variations, operators, packets) bands against the anchor envelope. The seasonal identity tranches ride this stage indefinitely.

## I.2 Stage 2 — Single-axis extrapolation (the standing R&D lane)
- **Method:** hold a canonical anchor fixed; push exactly one axis past the envelope edge. One variable per probe — **attributable novelty**: every success or failure teaches which dimension carried it, so later emission is aimable.
- **Deliverable per axis: the response curve** — where fun-proxies degrade smoothly (gradients) vs. collapse (cliffs). **Cliffs become emission masks**; peaks become candidates. (Published template: Isaksen et al.'s survival-analysis mapping of a game's parameter space.)
- **Evidence ladder** (this claim class cannot be sim-certified, so the gate order is fixed):
  1. sim-fair (fairness bands still apply out-of-envelope)
  2. battery reading — conformant OR anomalous, both are information
  3. **human play gate** (Hale + Judge)
  4. **arcade anomaly-slice preview** (the already-designed rotating experimental mode; membrane makes it safe — no power economy to contaminate)
  5. play-data promotes or kills
  6. **survivors JOIN the anchor set** — the measured canon grows; future bands include them.
- **Cadence:** one extrapolation survivor ≈ one season's mechanical ingredient. Stage 2 is not a future phase; it is the R&D lane that feeds the seasonal war chest.
- Genre-historical note for confidence: much of today's canon *was built by this exact move* (zero-aim combat, single-mechanic-to-absurd-density builds were single-axis extrapolations that survived). We are mechanizing how the genre already evolved.

## I.3 Stage N — Multi-axis and new-axis search (future)
- Proceeds **only over regions whose constituent axes have known response curves** from Stage 2.
- Throughout all stages, the **BC-cell hypothesis program remains the licensed out-of-band channel** — deliberate envelope violations, human-gated, canon-expanding. Canon-tuning is conservative by construction; the cell program is where the conservatism is deliberately broken, on purpose, with instrumentation.

# PART II — THE LAYER CONTRACT (single source of truth for the factored mechanism)

## II.0 History (so nobody loses the plot)
64,000 pts → 68,040 → (204,120 planned with the timing axis) → **factored to ~1,000 BC points × distributed machinery.** Axes were relocated to their **variance scope**, not deleted. The BC space's job is to be the space we *search*; everything else parameterizes points rather than defining them.
**Vocabulary rule:** describe the system as *"~1,000 mechanical archetypes × kernels × chain structures × operator space"* — never as a raw point count in either direction.

## II.1 The layers

| Layer | What varies here | Selected / authored by | Consumed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BC combination space** (~1,000 pts) | mechanical KINDS: trajectory frames (incl. rotational), timing class, resource model, targeting, trigger hooks, proxy/summon, persistent rings | BC-cell hypothesis program; QD-archive cell-targeting | kit generator; the clustering ledgers |
| **Per-skill kernels** | damage geometry, magnitude curves — per SKILL | generator sampling per skill slot | sim + engine (compiled from packet) |
| **Chain role structure** | control density, sequencing, combo roles (opener/spender/mobility/ult) — RELATIONAL | generator + chain templates | sim, utility-AI pilots |
| **Attribute catalog** | scalar stat blocks — MAGNITUDES, selected pre-combination | catalog curation; sim convergence tunes | kit assembly |
| **Operator space** | archetype-agnostic gear transforms (value/structural/transform) | operator search (§6, loot doc) + fairness validation | loot system; band power |
| **Band ladder** | the stats→art integer (2 meshes × 5 treatments per slot) | power_to_band curve (loot-owned) | ensemble renderer; enemy telegraphing |
| **Identity layer** | elements (amphibian: rule-package + identity), era/culture, race, order, faction; theme briefs → ensembles | LLM emission under style bible; seasonal tranches | art pipeline; naming; factions |
| **Element-application block** | primary + ONE secondary element, hybrid structure(s), realized-share rate band — a BINDER over kernel/chain/emission slots, never new machinery (see element-application addendum) | emission draw under per-cell affinity masks; global prevalence dial (seasonal lever) | sim (realized-share certification); VFX + naming legibility; runtime element assignment |
| **Packet families** | kit / ensemble / minigame / beat packets — the serial JSON contract | content engine emission | **sim AND engine compile from the SAME packet** (fidelity law) |
| **Certification layer** | envelopes, fairness bands, battery, blacklist, exploiter, proficiency tiers, cascade | anchors define envelopes (Part I) | release gate — nothing ships uncertified |
| **Surfaces + membrane** | main game (serial, powered) vs. arcade (compositional, cosmetic) | power_mode per instance | players. Vocabulary & cosmetics cross the membrane; power never does |

**What is a kit:** a kit = **BC point + kernel assignments + chain structure + attribute block**, realized as a packet; its *identity* comes from the identity layer; its *power expression* at runtime comes from operators + bands.

## II.2 The placement test (every new axis passes this gate)
An axis lands in **BC space iff** it is (a) kit-defining, (b) a discrete mechanical **kind** (not a magnitude), (c) sim-feelable, (d) search-worthy. Otherwise: magnitude → attribute catalog · per-skill variation → kernel · relational/sequencing → chain structure · identity/fiction → identity layer · cross-kit modifier → operator space. New BC axes ship as **substrate addendum docs** in the rotational addendum's format (axis table, degenerate identities, sim requirements, blacklist candidates, "do not regress" clause).

## II.3 The uniqueness-accounting rule
**Mechanical uniqueness is computed from sim behavioral fingerprints** (spatial signature, uptime shape, kill cadence, ability entropy — the gauntlet's own metrics), **never from coordinate distance** — because uniqueness lives across layers (two kits at one BC point can be deeply different via kernels and chains). The identity ledger measures name/theme/ensemble distance (Judge/embeddings). The two-ledger clustering and the QD archive both run on these fingerprints.

# PART III — PRIOR-ART RECOMMENDATIONS (absorbed from the standalone doc)

**Framing rule:** favor *structural synthesis* — recombine existing machinery — over further decomposition. Each item names what it wires together.

## III.1 ADOPT — ranked by leverage-per-effort

**A. The fun-metric battery** (Browne's Ludi/Yavalath). Score candidates on a *battery* of aesthetic proxies, all computable from the existing event log: lead-change count (comeback), outcome-uncertainty curve (drama), decisiveness (kill density vs. stall), ability-usage entropy (whole-kit expression), time-to-first-meaningful-event. Per Part I: these are **calibrated against the anchors** ("how much drama does the Hammerdin exhibit?"), never trusted as universal. New report columns + clustering axes; cheapest high-information upgrade.

**B. The evaluation cascade** (multi-fidelity, from the Bayesian-optimization balancing lineage). Staged certification: schema/mask (free) → smoke sim (few fights, coarse, seconds) → full gauntlet for survivors → (later) surrogate model prioritizing full runs. On the M2, the difference between overnight and week-long batches.

**C. The reflection loop** (Eureka). Feed evaluator statistics back into the generator's next prompt. **The battle report generator is already the payload** — wire failed/clamped candidates' reports (power-delta, broken kit regions, violated metric) into re-emission prompts. One wiring job; the engine's memory of why things fail.

**D. Formalize the two-ledger clustering as a QD archive** (MAP-Elites). Maintain an archive of elites over behavioral-descriptor axes (fingerprints per II.3); **steer emission toward empty/sparse cells.** Generation becomes coverage-directed. The archive doubles as the **season planner** (sparse cells nominate next BC-cell hypotheses) and a devlog artifact ("watch the space illuminate").

**E. The adversarial exploiter agent** (EA SEED generator/solver co-evolution). An agent whose fitness is *maximizing band violation* over gear × kit × tactics — active degenerate discovery. Reuses the doppelganger harness with a new objective; pre-registered blacklists are its seed corpus. Certification stage: ship only what the exploiter fails to break within budget.

**F. Proficiency-tiered certification** (AlphaZero variants + RaidEnv). Balance is only defined relative to pilot skill. Make pilot skill a sim parameter; bands must hold across tiers (or publish per-tier). One knob, three consumers: certification tiers, minigame bot difficulties, endgame band-differential tuning.

**G. Usage-fitness live loop** (Galactic Arms Race, 2009). Post-launch telemetry (pick/reap/retention per kit and template) as a second fitness channel feeding seasonal emission; with the measured-faction matchup matrix, this is the closed-loop anti-faction/counter-meta season generator.

**H. Thin in-engine validation pass** (the nunu.ai pattern). A scripted Godot-side bot replaying **golden fights** to catch sim↔implementation drift. Mitigation for Gotcha 1; gates the first in-engine milestone, not sim work.

## III.2 GOTCHAS

1. **Sim-to-game fidelity gap** — certified-in-sim ≠ fair-in-engine. Mitigations: sim and engine compile from the SAME packet (fidelity law, enforced in review); golden-fight cross-checks (Adopt H); engine-only mechanics carry fidelity notes.
2. **Evaluator myopia** — balance = what pilots can express (no kiting AI → kiting kits undervalued). Mitigations: proficiency tiers; tactic-library backlog; a flagged "AI-unpilotable / human-strong" class routed to human review.
3. **Fun ≠ balance — RESOLVED BY DOCTRINE (Part I).** We tune to *past success in genre canon*, a measurable reference class, not to fun. Stage 2's evidence ladder is the only path by which "probably fun" claims are made, and they are human-gated. Language discipline stands: *fair/conformant* in all public claims, never *fun*.
4. **Homogenization pressure** — tight bands sand off spice. Keep diversity objectives (QD coverage) alongside fairness; per-rarity band widths stay honest (legendaries wider on purpose).
5. **Compute blowup** — gear × kit × tier × density is combinatorial; without the cascade, certification kills cadence.
6. **LLMs must not touch numbers** — LLM-proposed balance changes regress to plausible-sounding nerfs (the RuleSmith-era temptation). Our split stands: LLMs own identity; the sim owns numeric truth.
7. **Climate & legal** — GDC 2026: 52% developer negativity (from 30%); 7,300+ Steam AI disclosures; US Copyright Office requires human creative contribution. Mitigations: **provenance field on every packet** (who authored/curated/approved — serves copyright, disclosure, devlog receipts in one schema field); lead public claims with the certification story.
8. **The priority window** — RuleSmith (2/2026) and the AIIDE doctoral pipeline close the method window from research; platform tooling from the other side. The durable moat is the shipped game whose certification is a product property. Ship; timestamp the lineage in the devlog.

## III.3 STRUCTURAL SYNTHESES

| Synthesis | Combines | New capability | New parts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Report → Reflection → Re-emission | battle reports + emission prompts | self-improving generator (Eureka) | one wiring job |
| QD archive as season planner | two-ledger clustering + seasonal doctrine | sparse cells nominate next BC hypotheses | archive persistence + targeting prompt |
| Exploiter = red-team Judge | doppelganger harness + blacklist seeds | active degenerate discovery as a cert stage | new objective fn |
| Proficiency tiers, one knob | skill-parameterized pilots | cert tiers + bot difficulty + endgame tuning | tier definitions |
| Provenance field | packet schema | copyright + disclosure + devlog receipts | one field |
| Telemetry → matchup matrix → anti-faction seasons | telemetry + faction clustering + emission | closed-loop counter-meta live-ops | live-ops ETL |

## III.4 SEQUENCING
- **Now:** A battery · B cascade (stages 1–3) · C reflection wiring · provenance field.
- **Next:** D QD archive · E exploiter · F proficiency tiers.
- **Post-launch:** G usage loop · anti-faction generator · H at first in-engine milestone.
