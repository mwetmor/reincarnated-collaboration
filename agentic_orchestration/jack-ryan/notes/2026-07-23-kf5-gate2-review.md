## The item-6 adjudication — the 120-center magnitude-shape (KFL-16b), decision-shaped for Matt

**The situation, precisely.** For every compiled kit, the compiler injects the band MAX as the per-hit magnitude (`magnitude = base_max`, `kit_compiler.py:280`). The resolver then multiplies by a single symmetric variance draw `U[0.80, 1.20]` (unit-mean, `damage_resolver.py:943`). So realized per-hit damage centers on `base_max`. But the fidelity gauge's `expected` centers on `base_mean = (base_min+base_max)/2` (Pin A, Matt-ratified). The ratio is `base_max/base_mean` — for bonestorm `309/257 = 1.202`, i.e. **realized damage runs ~120% of the documented build-point expectation, every hit, structurally.** The gauge is not wrong; it is faithfully reporting that the *compiled damage shape* is hotter than the *documented build point*. The run's fidelity story (KF-7: Matt reads ≈100% as "faithful") would show bonestorm floaters centered ~120% — reading as a +20% fidelity miss that is actually a magnitude-shape choice, not a source-anchoring drift.

**Why this is Matt's call and not mine:** changing the injected magnitude is a *combat-behavior* change (kill times shift ~-17% under option (a) — mobs die faster because per-hit damage drops from base_max-centered to base_mean-centered). That crosses the commitment boundary; my Gate-2 authority covers process/principle, not the damage shape the game ships with.

**Options + tradeoffs:**

- **(a) Compiler emits `magnitude = base_mean`.** Center → 100%; the fidelity story reads true (floaters ≈100%). Smallest, most surgical touch (one line, `kit_compiler.py:280`: `base_max` → `(base_min+base_max)/2`). Combat outcomes CHANGE: per-hit damage drops ~17% for bonestorm, kill times lengthen ~+20%; every downstream balance number computed against the current base_max-centered damage shifts. The realized damage then matches the *statistical mean* of the source band — which is exactly what Pin A already defines "expected" to be, so gauge and reality re-converge at 100% by construction. **Faithfulness: highest. Blast radius: every compiled kit's combat output moves once.**
- **(b) Compiler emits min/max; resolver rolls `U[base_min, base_max]` per hit.** Source-game-faithful damage SHAPE (real ARPGs roll a per-hit band, not a point). Larger resolver touch (new band-roll path). **Double-variance risk — the concrete hazard:** the resolver already applies `magnitude *= U[0.80,1.20]` AFTER modifiers (`:943`). A band-roll of `U[base_min,base_max]` STACKS on top of that ±20% draw, so total per-hit spread becomes the CONVOLUTION of two uniforms — wider tails, and the existing `[90,150]` regression band would need re-derivation. It also raises "which variance is the source-faithful one?" — the source band-roll IS the game's damage variance, so the existing `U[0.80,1.20]` (a *synthetic* balance-variance, `_DMGVAR_LO/HI`) arguably double-counts variance and should be reconsidered/removed for compiled kits if (b) is taken. Center lands at `base_mean` (uniform mean) → gauge reads ~100%, same as (a), but with source-faithful per-hit scatter. **Faithfulness: highest on shape. Complexity + interaction risk: highest.**
- **(c) Keep `base_max` + document the +20%.** Zero code change; the gauge stays honest (it already reports 120%). But the KF-7 fidelity story is compromised — Matt reads 120% and must mentally subtract a known +20% offset on every floater, which defeats "≈100% == faithful." **Faithfulness: lowest (reader must carry a correction). Cost: zero.**

**My recommendation: (a) — compiler emits `base_mean` as the injected magnitude.** Rationale: (1) it makes realized damage equal the *statistical mean of the source band*, which is precisely what Pin A already ratified `expected` to be — so gauge and reality re-converge at 100% by definition, and the KF-7 fidelity story reads true with no reader-side correction; (2) it is a one-line, well-understood change with a bounded blast radius (all compiled-kit per-hit damage shifts down ~17% once, re-measurable in a single balance pass); (3) it avoids (b)'s double-variance interaction entirely — the existing `U[0.80,1.20]` synthetic-variance draw is left intact and correctly provides the ±20% per-hit spread the gauge band already expects. Option (b) is the *more source-faithful shape* long-term (real per-hit band-roll) but should be deferred until someone resolves the synthetic-vs-source variance double-count deliberately — it is not the right first move under a run whose goal is a readable fidelity gauge. Option (c) I reject: it ships a known +20% reader-correction into the exact number KF-7 exists to make legible.

**One caveat I flag for whichever option Matt picks:** the resolver's per-hit variance docstring says "±15%" (`damage_resolver.py:942`) but the live constants are `_DMGVAR_LO=0.80 / _DMGVAR_HI=1.20` = ±20%. gamora already noted this in math §1.0; it does not affect the math (the argument holds at either label), but the stale docstring should be corrected opportunistically so nobody re-derives the band against ±15%.

## NOTE — the assert-class that would have caught the dead wire (item 3, requested)

My KF-4 Gate-2 and the KF-4 smoke were structurally blind to the `flat_damage` dead wire: every compiled-kit hit realized `amount == 0.0`, and the asserts tested composition-EXISTENCE + FINITENESS — but `0.0` is finite and the composition existed, so both passed a dead pipeline. **The assert-class that catches it: a realized-damage POSITIVITY assert on the projection path — `amount > 0` on at least one dealt hit of a compiled fighter that carries a non-GAP damage base.** The KF-4 smoke ran the fighter through `run_spatial_fight` and checked "finite damage, HP resolves, no crash" — but finite-and-zero passed. A single `assert any(hit.amount > 0 for hit in dealt_hits)` (which the KF-5 smoke now has, post-fix) would have red-flagged the dead wire at KF-4. **Discipline refinement candidate:** any smoke that compiles a fighter with a non-GAP damage base MUST assert realized-damage positivity, not merely finiteness — "finite" is satisfied by a dead wire; "positive" is not. This is the empirical-inspection (Disc #11) lesson: existence + finiteness are necessary but not sufficient; the wire must be proven LIVE (carries nonzero signal), not merely present. I will fold this into the disciplines as an INFO refinement (documentation-only, within my authority per ADR-002) — noted here, not blocking.
