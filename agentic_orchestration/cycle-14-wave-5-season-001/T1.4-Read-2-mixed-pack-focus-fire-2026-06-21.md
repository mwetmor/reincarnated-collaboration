## 3. The load-bearing mechanism nuance — the boss-focus HIT override (where the dump DAMAGE lands)

There are TWO distinct targets on a boss shell, and they diverge on `boss_with_adds`:

- **(a) Nearest target** — governs the rage build-vs-spend SKILL CHOICE (`_select_skill_for_entity` computes
  `nearest_target = min(targets, key=distance)`; the anchor predicate keys off it). The §1/§2 measure (0.511 /
  0.745) is this view.
- **(b) HIT target** — governs where single-target damage LANDS. On a `boss_killed` shell (`boss_with_adds`)
  the engine's `_get_player_primary_target` (`spatial_engine.py:855`) returns the **boss-focus entity
  REGARDLESS of distance** ("boss-focus mode: the player prioritizes that entity regardless of distance"). STR's
  rage skills are ALL `single_target` geometry (verified first-hand: every chain skill `geometry='single_target'`,
  range 3.0). So on `boss_with_adds` the dump's DAMAGE lands on the boss even when an ADD is the nearest target
  — the boss-focus override directs the hit onto the anchor.

**Consequence for the honest read:** on `boss_with_adds`, the 0.511 nearest-target fraction UNDERSTATES the
fraction of dump DAMAGE that reaches the boss. The boss-focus override means the dump's single-target damage
lands on the boss ~100% of the time (whenever the boss is alive), even on the ~49% of casts where the
skill-choice saw an add as nearest. So:
- **By the skill-CHOICE measure (nearest-is-anchor):** boss_with_adds 0.511 (below criterion).
- **By the DAMAGE-LANDED measure (boss-focus HIT):** boss_with_adds focus-fire is effectively ~1.0 (the dump
  damage lands on the boss via boss-focus, regardless of the nearest-target skill-choice) — which is EXACTLY
  why STR's banked `boss_with_adds` survive-and-kill is 1.000 (Read-1: STR raw-throughput-kills the boss in
  ~15s; the boss-focus keeps the single-target dumps on the boss).
- **`elite_pack` has NO boss-focus** (it is `all_mobs_killed`, no `boss_index`/`mini_boss_index`), so its hit
  lands on nearest → the 0.745 nearest-target measure IS the damage allocation on elite_pack (and it passes).

So the criterion (≥60% anchor-targeted spend on mixed shells) reads:
- **elite_pack: PASS by both measures** (0.745 nearest = hit).
- **boss_with_adds: BELOW by the skill-choice measure (0.511); effectively focus-fired by the damage measure
  (boss-focus override directs the dump onto the boss).**

## 6. Instrument note (Discipline #11 — surfaced, not papered over)

The first Read-2 pass recorded ZERO spender casts because the harness keyed "spender" off
`_is_spender_skill`, which for rage requires `skill_energy_cost ≥ 0.5·max_energy = 50`; but the rage T4
capstone (the actual dump) costs 40 (< 50), so `_is_spender_skill` never labels the dump a spender. I caught
this first-hand (94 rage casts, 74 at cost-40 T4 = the dump, but `_is_spender_skill` returned builder for all)
and corrected the instrument to key off the selector's ACTUAL spend BRANCH (`energy ≥ 0.5·max_energy AND
anchor_present` → `costliest_spender()`), which is the operationally correct "did the dump fire" signal. The
0.511/0.745 numbers are from the corrected instrument. (Separately, this surfaces a latent
`costliest_spender` / `_is_spender_skill` cost-threshold inconsistency in the rage branch — the costliest
ready skill costs 40 but the spender-label threshold is 50 — which is a pre-existing affordability-gate detail,
NOT introduced by the rescale and NOT in scope here; flagged for the post-workstream constant sweep.)
