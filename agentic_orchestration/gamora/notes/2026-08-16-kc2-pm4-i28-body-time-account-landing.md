## 2 — WHERE THE OCCUPANCY DEFICIT LIVES

*(The decomposition the conductor asked for. § 2.2 is **POST-HOC** and is labelled as such
everywhere it appears — in the artifact, on every row, and here.)*

### 2.1 The body-count leg is **neutralised by construction**, and that is a property, not a finding

`N_dec(w)` is **the same artifact, the same limb, the same number** on both sides of every grade. It
*cannot* carry any part of the deficit in this account, because it divides both sides identically.
This is a property of how the comparator was built — it is **not** a claim that the sim's roster is
referent-true. (I-22 established roster fidelity separately; that finding is not re-quoted here.)

So `L = Q · N_dec / T` has three legs, and this commission closes exactly one of them.

### 2.2 ⚑ **POST-HOC** — the saturation bound

`Q_sim = f · W̄`, where `f = n_bodies_entered / N_dec` (*did the body ever reach the ring?*) and
`W̄ = ` mean per-body total ring time among bodies that entered (*how long did it stay, given it
reached?*). Both are read from the I-27 pin. Setting `f := 1` and holding the **measured** `W̄`
fixed gives the largest `Q_sim` the body-count leg alone could ever buy.

| | value |
|---|---|
| rows reaching `Q_ref_A` with `f` saturated | **0 of 10** |
| max ratio→A at `f = 1` | **0.4761** (salt1, w153) |
| median ratio→A at `f = 1` | **0.2650** |
| median ratio→A, rows with `f ≥ 0.85` (n = 5) | **0.2231** |
| median ratio→A, rows with `f < 0.85` (n = 5) | **0.0731** |

⚑ **Read the two split rows together.** On the five rows where the sim already delivers
85–104 % of the decoded bodies into the ring, it still sits at **0.22** of the referent's *lower*
bound. Fixing arrival entirely — every decoded body at the ring, on every wave — buys at most
**0.48**, and typically **0.27**.

> **The deficit lives in PER-BODY RESIDENCE.** Body count is not free — it costs a factor of ~1.5–3×
> on the low-`f` rows — but it is **not sufficient**, on any wave, by any margin. A remaining factor
> of **2.1×–11.5×** on the lower bound (and **5×–78×** on the upper) sits in how long a body stays.

This converges with I-27's mechanism finding (`R-PM4-72 part 2`): the sim's residence terminates by
**displacement**, and `R-PM4-74 part 3` ruled displacement a real exit channel on both sides. What
I-28 adds is the **magnitude**: the exit channel is not a rounding term on the residual — it is,
arithmetically, the whole of what is left after body count is given away for free.
