## §0 — STEP 0: THE TWO OWED FIXES, BEFORE THE BATTERY

Both routed by charter §8.16 from Gate-2's `m4to9` finding. Separate commits, per the routing.

### 0.1 — WARN-A: the report stopped declaring absent a channel it carries — `2594bbb`

`kitcal_g5_harness.py` shipped `NAMED_ABSENT[2]` reading *"crit labelling in the trace (HALT-3) —
the resolver crits but no call site logs it"* while **every** report emitted since M-6 (`1e5b136`)
carries a populated per-tier `crit` block and every damage record carries non-null `crit`.

The reach is wider than the report. `NAMED_ABSENT` is also passed into `G5TraceSink(named_absent=…)`,
so the contradiction lands in **every trace header** as well — which is what makes it a downstream
reader's problem rather than a cosmetic one.

**Why it is worse than a stale comment:** `NAMED_ABSENT` exists so nobody mistakes an absence for
the fixture's behaviour. A stale entry inverts that function into a **false absence claim**, and
the reader who trusts the list stops looking for a channel that is right there.

**Replaced, not deleted.** M-6's own instrument found the real residual absence and it belongs in
the slot: `combatant_projection_from_monster_dict` hard-codes `crit_chance = 0.0` with no override
door, so the **received-side** crit column is a STRUCTURAL zero, not a measured one. *"Did the boss
crit the killing blow"* answers *"no, by construction of the projection."* G-B grades a death, so
that is not an idle fact. Pinned by T-M6-9b.

**Guard added (T-M6-11)** and it asserts on the **emitted artifact**, not on the constant — the
artifact is what travels.

### 0.2 — WARN-B: the owed ADR-004 entry for `hp_provenance` — `d38e00d`

### 1.3 — THE FINDING THE WALK IMMEDIATELY PRODUCED

**The M-2 nova ships `effects: []`.** Its entire payload — including the lock — lives under the
`_gd_nova` key, which an effect-only walk never opens. **The A-FRZ-1 effect walk therefore had a
blind spot over precisely the mechanism P-5 exists for.** Nothing was hiding there, and that is not
the point: a guard whose coverage stops at the one skill the gate is about is not the guard the
ratification described.

Closed by `_walk_gd_nova_block`, and its predicate is deliberately **the shatter operands only**:
`NovaParams.freeze_min_s / freeze_max_s` are GD's own `offensiveFreezeMin/Max` (1.3–1.8 s), and a
substring match on `"freeze"` here would refuse the very mechanic M-2 was built to model. T-BAT-8b
asserts both directions — the legitimate lock passes, a planted shatter operand refuses.

Reported, not worked around (Discipline #11).
