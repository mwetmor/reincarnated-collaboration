## §2 — PART 1: THE REALIZED-COUNT OPERATOR (R-WR1-11)

### 2.1 — The geometry, and the erratum in one line

A spoke covers a target at `(r, φ)` iff `|wrap(φ − θ₀ − jS)| ≤ α(r)`, with

```
α(r) = π                 for r ≤ blast   (the origin is inside every spoke's corridor — all 16 cover)
     = arcsin(blast / r)  for r >  blast
```

Then `E[n] = W/S = α·N/π` **exactly** (linearity of expectation; overlap is irrelevant), the support
is the two adjacent integers `⌊W/S⌋` and `⌊W/S⌋+1`, and `P(max) = frac(W/S)`.

**`n_max = ⌊W/S⌋+1` is the falsified operator's own value.** That identity *is* the erratum: EXT §1.3
shipped the maximum as the expectation.

### 2.2 — The three required properties, each unit-tested

| requirement | result | test |
|---|---|---|
| mean converges to `W/S` | deterministic 7,200-offset sweep to 2e-3 **and** a 20,000-draw seeded MC inside 4·SE, at all six spec radii | `test_R_WR1_11a_MEAN_…` |
| max = `⌊W/S⌋+1` | holds at every radius; the realized count never leaves `{min, max}` over a 3,600-offset sweep, and **attains both** | `test_R_WR1_11a_MAX_…` |
| min = 0 beyond 7.689 m | zero is **impossible** at 2.0/5.0/7.0/7.6 m and **attained** at 7.7/8.5/9.0/12.0 — aimed at the worst azimuth (half a spoke spacing), which is the definition of the boundary rather than a lucky sample | same |
| deterministic under seeded RNG | offset sequence is a pure function of the fight seed; different seeds differ (non-vacuity); `n_realized` draws nothing itself | `test_R_WR1_11a_DETERMINISM_…` |

Exact boundary: `blast / sin(S/2) = 1.5 / sin(11.25°) = ` **7.6890 m**. EXT's quoted 7.64 is the arc
approximation.

**RNG discipline.** ONE draw per **cast**, on the nova's dedicated sub-stream (R-M2-3). Not per tick,
not at the telegraph, not in the policy. The offset is a property of the *ring* — so a target that
moves **laterally** during the windup can change its count without changing its range, which is
correct spoke-ring physics and a degree of freedom the fractional model did not have.

**The closed form is checked against an INDEPENDENT segment-distance implementation** (the oracle),
per azimuth, with `==`. Two implementations is deliberate: the oracle exists to falsify the closed
form, so sharing code between them would dissolve the check.
