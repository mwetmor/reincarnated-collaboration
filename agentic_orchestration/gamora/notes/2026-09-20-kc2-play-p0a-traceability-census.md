## 4 · (c) — hazards a human-piloted build surfaces

**(c-1) ⚑ Dodging is not model-true, and the confound is worse than "unverified" — it is *verified in the wrong direction*.**
§ 2.7(i) asked whether arrival hit-tests at arrival or resolves at fire time. **It resolves at fire time.** A human who strafes out of a projectile's path takes the damage anyway — the hit was decided when the monster swung, against where he stood then. Three consequences, in increasing order of nastiness:
1. If the runtime is built model-true, **strafing does nothing**, and the most basic ARPG instinct silently fails. Matt will read that as a bug in the build.
2. If the builder "fixes" it by hit-testing at arrival, **T-A is no longer like-with-like** and every downstream difference becomes unattributable — the exact failure SKIRT § 4.2 and the T-A tier exist to prevent.
3. ⚑ The sim **already measured what the fix would cost** and declined to spend it: arrival separation differs from cast separation by a **median −0.26 m on an 8.8 m median**, i.e. bodies are usually *closing*. That is small *for the sim's pilot*, who drives through packs. **It is not a bound on a human who strafes deliberately** — the instrument measured a policy, not a capability, and quoting the −0.26 m as "dodging barely matters" would be reading a pilot's habits as a law of the world.
**Recommendation:** build it model-true, expose arrival-time hit-testing as a **declared, off-by-default divergence** (SKIRT § 4's conductor-law carve-out), run T-A with it off, and fire **S1** if the run wants the answer. Say so in the HUD or the README, because an unexplained non-dodge reads as broken.

**(c-2) ⚑ The pack tells the builder not to implement monster-side mitigation, and the oracle implements it.**
`monsters.mitigation_note` (provenance `PRV-BATON-V1`) says `NONE-MODELLED … BY CONSTRUCTION` and warns that adding a term will **double-count against eHP that already carries the armour/resist chain**. The PM4-lineage sim runs `player_offense.applied_damage` — `after_armor(raw, armor, absorption) × (1 − res_physical) × crit_mult` — per body, from a pinned Lap-L board (`player_offense.py:321-336, 499-521`). **I am flagging the contradiction, not adjudicating it**: either the note is stale (describes v1) or the sim double-counts, and I did not determine which. A builder following the pack gets **materially higher player DPS against armoured bodies** than the oracle.
**Cheapest refuting test, named:** take one tier-16 record at one wave, compute `player_damage_per_tick` against its `monsters.blocks` eHP with and without `applied_damage`, and compare the implied time-to-kill against the sealed cell's own per-body kill times (already in the mech cell). One record, no sim run.

**(c-3) ⚑ Pools: the conductor law and the Matt ruling give opposite answers, and both are live.**
Conductor law says *the runtime's registered choice := the sim's choice*. The sim's choice is `POOL_DOT_MAGNITUDE_ABSENT = 0.0` — **pools are inert**. D-LIFT-1/2 rule the opposite and rule it concretely: **⅙ of max health pool per tick, defences ignored, six ticks kill, ~1 s ticks**. Against a 20,005 HP pool that is **~3,334 hp/tick and death in ≈ 6 s** — for a human who wanders into a spawn disc, which is a thing humans do and the sim's pilot policy never did. Applying both is incoherent; applying the conductor law alone contradicts a Matt ruling; applying the ruling alone breaks T-A comparability.
**Recommendation:** treat D-LIFT-1/2 as a **declared divergence from the oracle**, on for play and **off for T-A**, and fire **S2** if the run wants the sim side closed. And note the geometry: the sim's pools are the **six spawn discs at radius 8.0 m**, which is where every monster arrives — a lethal pool there is a very different game from an inert one.

**(c-4) ⚑ Three of the sim's live defaults are known-bad, and a human will feel two of them.**
`WarCryLimb.I8_LEGACY` — War Cry's duration is the **retired invented literal 5.0**, which priced **19.43 % of all incoming damage** (`counterplay.py:391-418`). `PotionLimb.I4_EXCURSION_MAX` — θ = 0.49, **measured-falsified on 5 of its own 9 predicted actuations** (`counterplay.py:340-351`). `LifeMonitorLimb.POLL_AT_SLOT` — the circuit breakers poll a post-lift HP value and **measurably miss floor ticks** (Turtle 51 vs 41; Menhir 13 vs 10; in waves 151/153 the breaker was off entirely).
