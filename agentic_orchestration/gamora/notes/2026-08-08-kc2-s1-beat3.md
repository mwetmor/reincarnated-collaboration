## § 3 — The sign argument: why this is not "the kill term is missing"

`engagement_kill_time ≥ 0` — it is a duration. So

```
cycle_true(any completion) = 0.5 + traversal + kill + 0.5  >=  0.5 + traversal + 0 + 0.5 = cycle_sim
```

**this build's cycle is a lower bound on every completion of the model.** On any wave where the
lower bound already exceeds `measured + 1.0`, supplying the absent term can only make it worse.

**89 of 92 waves are falsified independently of the absence.** The 3 survivors are **80** (82.13 s
measured), **90** (26.43 s) and **92** (78.45 s): galadriel's two re-verified extreme outliers and
the Crucible's first nemesis wave (Benn'Jahr, solo, **2** expected bodies). Those are precisely the
waves where the fixture spent a long time on *few, high-HP* bodies — **the absent kill term is
exactly the term those three need and exactly the term the other 89 do not.**

The residual has structure. The structure names the mechanism.

## § 4 — The mechanism, located and measured

### 4.1 The sim is a body-count model; the fixture is not

### 4.2 The two-class inversion is the fingerprint

| class | n | measured | sim | E[bodies] |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ×10 | 9 | **28.57 s** | 34.04 s | **14.28** |

### 4.3 The mechanism: the opposition does not move

`simulate_wave` hands `disc.resolve_tick` each actor's **spawn coordinate** for every tick of the
wave's life. The board is static and the **player tours it**. The encounter's own choreography is
the reverse — § 2.2 (*"how a monster's approach between spawn and engagement is choreographed"*),
§ 10.6 (p05 `alertArea = 100.0`), § 10.9 (*"spawn + approach + kill"*).

Measured cost — player path length against the farthest spawn:

| wave | bodies | tour | max spawn dist | ratio |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 12 | 87.8 m | 35.3 m | 2.49× |
| 8 | 11 | 114.3 m | 38.0 m | 3.01× |
| 47 | 16 | 159.3 m | 39.1 m | 4.07× |
| 87 | 40 | 191.3 m | 39.1 m | 4.90× |
| **90** | **2** | 34.9 m | 38.1 m | **0.92×** |
| 91 | 41 | 199.2 m | 38.9 m | 5.12× |

The ratio is monotone in body count and is ~1× only on the two-body wave. Under a
"monsters close on the player" model traversal is ~1× the farthest distance **regardless of body
count** — which is exactly the body-count dependence that has to disappear.

**NOT BUILT, and the reason is charter § 4.2.** The missing half needs a monster approach rate in
m/s. HALT-2 established the engine's m/s reference is **NAMED-ABSENT** (`characterRunSpeed` is a

declared free parameter — introduced against a failing pinned gate, in the same lap that reports
