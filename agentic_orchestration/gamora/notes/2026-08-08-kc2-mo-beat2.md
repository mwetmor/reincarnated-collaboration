## § 1 — Why a pin is not an oracle, and what that cost

`fixture.py` already carried `MO_ENERGY_CEILING`, `MO_ENERGY_RESERVATION`, `MO_S2_IN_COMBAT_ENERGY`,
`MO_PLAYER_HP_MAX` and `MO_CYCLE_FLOOR_S` before this beat. Five `assert constant == constant` lines
would have produced a green suite in ten minutes and measured nothing.

An oracle is a **two-sided arrangement**: a target on one side, a value the model computes **without
reference to the target** on the other, and a declared comparison class between them. Three rules
follow, and each is enforced **by AST scan rather than by convention** (Discipline #11):

1. **The mechanism may not import the oracle table.** `micro_oracles` imports `energy` /
   `wave_engine` / `run` / `fixture`; none of them may import `micro_oracles`. The scan catches all
   three import spellings — including `from . import micro_oracles`, which a line-regex on
   `from .micro_oracles` would miss — and carries a **firing negative control**, because a scanner
   that has never been shown to fire is not evidence.
2. **A derivation may not read its own target.** MO-2's pin says *derived from DB, not hard-coded*,
   so `energy.py` is parsed: `MO_ENERGY_RESERVATION` must not appear as a code identifier and 982
   must not appear as a numeric literal anywhere in it. `observe_reservation` states **no numbers at
   all** (asserted: its numeric-constant set is empty).
3. **Comparison class is DATA.** `EXACT_INTEGER` vs `ONE_SIDED_FLOOR` rides on the record and the
   evaluator dispatches on it, so MO-5 cannot be quietly two-sided later without editing a declared
   field — a visible act.

**No `pytest.approx` on MO-1..MO-4.** Every term is an integer, exactly representable in binary64.
A tolerance on an exact-integer pin buys only the right to be quietly wrong by less than the
tolerance. (AC-3.1 and AC-5.1 already assert 1594/982 with `approx`; those stand — the § 12 pins now
have their exact form beside them.)

### MO-5 — cycle floor, ~7.0 s, **ONE-SIDED**

**Derivation path — geometry, never a constant:**

```
traversal = (emitter_radius 30.0 m − disc_radius 3.0 m) / v_ref 4.0 m/s = 6.75 s
total     = spawn_resolution 0.5 + 6.75 + engagement_kill 0.0 + advance_latency 0.5 = 7.75 s  >= 7.0
```

`engagement_kill_time_s` is held at **zero deliberately**: § 10.9's floor is what a wave that dies
*instantly* still costs, so crediting kill time would flatter the comparison. A negative control
asserts the floor is not being *summed in* — halving the distance drops the total to 1.75 s, below
the floor, which it could not do if the constant were added.

⚑ **One-sided for a charter reason, not a style one.** The dominant term divides by `v_ref`, a
**DECLARED-FREE-PARAMETER** (HALT-2 — player run speed in m/s is NAMED-ABSENT from the corpus). A
two-sided band around 7.0 could only be closed by *choosing* `v` to land on it — fitting a free
parameter to an oracle, the exact move charter § 4.2 forbids. The **+10.71 %** margin is reported,
never a verdict, and the one-sidedness is asserted as a **declared field** rather than left implicit
in an operator, because at today's geometry a hand could "tighten" MO-5 to a band and it would still
pass.

**Tests:** `test_MO_5_the_floor_EMERGES_FROM_GEOMETRY_and_clears_7_0_s` ·
`test_MO_5_the_floor_is_NOT_added_as_a_constant` ·
