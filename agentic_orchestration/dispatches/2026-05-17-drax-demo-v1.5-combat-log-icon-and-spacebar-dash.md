## Why this matters

Two small UX deltas:
1. Combat log currently defaults OPEN and lives as a permanent floating overlay. Matt wants it to behave like the Character / Inventory panels — closed by default, opened by an icon click (or existing `H` key). Reduces visual noise at start; player opens log when they want it.
2. Dash binding moves from `Shift` to `Space`. Space is more natural for "dodge/dash" in ARPG canon (D4 uses Space for evade; PoE uses CTRL/Space variants; Last Epoch uses Space). Shift was a stopgap.

### OBSERVATION — Space alias in input.ts

`getAbilitySlotPressed()` previously mapped `Space` as an alias for `Digit1` (ability slot 0). This was not noted in the dispatch. Binding Space to dash without removing this alias would have caused dual-fire: one Space press triggers both a skill cast AND a dodge. Alias removed. `Digit1` still maps to slot 0. No player-visible change beyond dash working correctly.

The dispatch noted "Earlier grep found no Space bindings" — the grep may have searched main.ts only, missing input.ts. Caught and resolved.

### Line 1936 note

The dispatch referenced "line 1936 standalone click region" to potentially remove. In the actual file, line 1936 is inside the `TouchIcons` constructor (mobile path) — `() => combatLog.toggle()` is the third argument to `TouchIcons`, not a standalone desktop click zone. No removal performed; mobile toggle remains intact.
