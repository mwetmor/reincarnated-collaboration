### The governing design law (brief § 3, adopted from the run's own practice)

**DECODE BEFORE DECLARING.** Where the referent engine's own transition rule can be decoded, decode
it and fold it. Where it cannot, the mechanism is **DECLARED, with its bound stated, its alternative
left runnable, and its cost reported** (the `D-PURSUIT-TIME` pattern). **The forbidden third path is
a plausible-looking invented rule with no provenance.**

> **If you judge a decode lap is needed, that is a SEQUENCING item — route it to knight-rider and I
> fork legolas.** It is never a licence for this wave to improvise. Named decode candidates already
> on the board: `AlertBeforePursue` (animation `0x21`, `UNREACHED-AA-3`) for re-engagement latency;
> any monster-side re-target cadence; any pack-coupling term (`distressCall`).

- [ ] **Component (1) — post-displacement re-engagement · NEW** (brief § 3.1)
  - [ ] The condition **is noticed**: a body out of its own attack range with a live acquired target
        is in a **LOCOMOTIVE state, not a waiting state**. Expression (state transition / per-tick
        range predicate / re-issued path) is **your call**.
  - [ ] **BOTH displacement channels covered** — (i) player motion carrying the engage ring away,
        and (ii) **crowd-shove from the non-penetration solver**. A rule that handles only (i) is a

## 4 — Acceptance criteria: A-1 … A-10 (brief § 4.2)

**The wave is complete when each of the following is DEMONSTRATED FROM EMITTED ARTIFACTS, not
asserted in prose. Every one is a *does-the-behaviour-exist* question. NONE HAS A MAGNITUDE.**

- [ ] **A-1 — A displaced body's disposition is total and enumerated.** Every ring interval
      terminating by displacement resolves into a named outcome: re-entry · death en route · a named
      terminal cause (leash / despawn / wave end). **No unassigned residue — an unclassifiable body
      HALTs the instrument.**
      *Evidence:* exit-cause partition closing exhaustively, in the operational partition form
      adopted at `R-PM4-75 part 3` — **assembled from the artifact's OWN CONSTRUCTION, never from a
      remembered list of classes; every top-level key explicitly assigned; an unassigned key HALTs,
      never defaults.** (Three-in-three instances — `D-I27-1`, `D-E7-1`, `D-I28-1` — is the class
      telling us memory does not scale.)
- [ ] **A-2 — Re-entry events exist and are individually observable.** The residence ledger
      distinguishes first entry from re-entry and carries per-body re-entry latency.
      *Evidence:* ledger schema + non-empty re-entry population on the record cell.
- [ ] **A-3 — No body burns its attack cycle out of attack range.** An actor with a live acquired
      target beyond its own attack reach is in a locomotive state.
      *Evidence:* a guard/assert on the state predicate, **armed and demonstrably reachable** — a
      guard that cannot fire is `D-I27-2`'s green-by-absence class, **which ran green for four laps.**
- [ ] **A-4 — The player path is a trajectory, not a constant, on the record cell.**
      *Evidence:* `tracks.player_path` non-degenerate; policy limb declared in the emission receipt;
      camp limbs still runnable.
- [ ] **A-5 — The pack tracks a moving player.** Monster target positions update on the declared
      cadence; the engage ring travels with the player rather than persisting at a stale location.
      *Evidence:* a positional-lag census, **published AS A CENSUS — no target value.**
- [ ] **A-6 — Every constant this wave introduces is DECODED-with-citation or DECLARED-with-bound,
      and NONE IS FITTED.** Declared constants name their alternative and leave it runnable.
      *Evidence:* `Cited(...)`-form provenance, and a **Law-3 witness set showing no constant moved
      toward an outcome.**
- [ ] **A-7 — Determinism preserved.** Same seed ⇒ byte-identical emission, **×2, layer declared**
      (`FG-10`).
- [ ] **A-8 — The instrument PM5 needs exists and emits.** Per-body ring residence intervals;
      exit-cause partition including re-entry; re-entry latency; per-wave movers-only occupancy.
      *Evidence:* artifacts on disk, digests published, **re-hashed from bytes** (`DO-NOT 8`).
- [ ] **A-9 — The new frozen checkpoint is cut, digested, and sibling-safe** (D5).
      *Evidence:* new file, own digest, **`E-s09-cp150` byte-unchanged and verified so.**
- [ ] **A-10 — A pre-registered falsifier existed and was graded as written.** Whatever the wave
      predicted about its own behaviour, it says so *before* running and reports the result unedited.
      *Evidence:* **prereg committed ALONE, BEFORE code.**
