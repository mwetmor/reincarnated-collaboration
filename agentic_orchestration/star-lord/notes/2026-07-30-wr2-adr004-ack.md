## 3. Concerns flagged to the conductor — flags, not fixes

**CONCERN-1 (substantive; drax-facing) — the `engine-default-ungraded` census is a FIXTURE
property, and my seam is the reason it will flip.** Cell BAT §2 records
`provenance_census = {"engine-default-ungraded": 150}` on all six legs (900/900 fights,
`values_observed_ms = [5.75]`), because the fixture's class dicts declare no
`movement_speed`. **Exported content does declare it.** `export/schemas.py` `ExportClass`
carries `movement_speed: float = 8.0` (Stage B 2026-05-16, per the movement-speed-baseline
canon; drax derives px/s at `PIXELS_PER_METER = 48`), `cycle14_wave5_emitter` stamps `8.0`,
and `cycle13_normal_season_export` stamps `7.5`. So **any kit sourced from an export bundle
grades `"kit"`, not `"engine-default-ungraded"`, and `v` is 8.0 (or 7.5), not 5.75.**
The escape law then reads `T = 12.0 / (0.90 × 8.0) = 1.667 s` — a **2.22×** change off the
0.750 s M value, not the entry's **3.09×**, and a tell delta of ~0.92 s, not 1.57 s.
The entries already say "do not hard-code 2.3188 s"; this flag names *the specific,
already-shipped source* that makes the flip certain rather than hypothetical, and it is
worth stating in the drax-facing thread before a renderer budgets for 1.57 s.
**Not a defect in the emission** — the provenance field working exactly as §2 designed it.

**CONCERN-2 (low, forward-looking) — the `movement_speed_ms` name now has a THIRD site.**
Cell BAT §5 disambiguates two (`header.entities[].movement_speed_ms`, conditional,
all entities; `fights[].movement_speed_ms`, unconditional, player-only). My seam holds a
third name for the same quantity: the bundle's `movement_speed` (m/s, no unit suffix,
per-class). Harmless today — nothing of mine joins on it. But if a future dispatch does put
`movement_speed_ms` on the telemetry row (§4 explicitly says that would be a NEW proposal,
not implied here), the join key must be disambiguated at the schema-design step, not after
the column exists. Flagging so the constraint is on record ahead of that proposal.
