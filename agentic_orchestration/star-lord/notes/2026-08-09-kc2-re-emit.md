## 3 · ② THE H-1 PIN — the repaired invariant is NOT the one the halt pin predicted

The halt pin's docstring said the twelve clipped legs "become uniform and the count goes to zero".
**They do not, and they should not** — gamora's L-83(e) correction, adopted verbatim in substance.
A clipped step *is* a real speed change and stays non-uniform under a per-leg implied-speed metric.
What the repair removes is the **interior integer tick**:

```
non-uniform legs : 12 on 12 bodies · tick spans [1] · all span exactly 1 tick: True
    8 × ('clip',)            -> ('engage', 'halt')
    4 × ('contact', 'clip')  -> ('engage', 'halt')
```

Re-measured here, not copied. The pin now asserts **`tick_span == 1` for every non-uniform leg** —
a leg spanning one tick has no interior tick for `run_tick` interpolation to misplace a body on —
plus the class set, so a silent change of shape is still visible.

### ⚑ 3.1 · The 13th body, and why the gate is unchanged

gamora measures **13** clipped steps; my per-leg metric finds **12**. That is not a disagreement,
it is a property of the metric. `w162_a012`'s entire path is one clipped leg, so a metric that
compares each leg's implied speed to *that body's own maximum* compares it **to itself**: ratio 1,
never flagged, however clipped the leg is.

**Only the source can tell that a step was short when there is no unclipped step beside it.** I
left the gate alone and wrote the fact onto it as a comment: **12 was a lower bound and is now
known to be one.** Repairing the blind spot would mean re-deriving `travel` on the consumer side,
which is § 11.3's forbidden re-derivation of a sim-owned quantity — the cure would be the disease.

## 4 · ③ R-L82-2 · M-7 — DECLARE THE SHAPE

`run.py` rolls `sx = ex + U(−8,+8)` and `sy = ey + U(−8,+8)` **independently** — a **BOX**. The
wire said only `"SIM-ROLLED"`, an owner with no geometry, and **the stub silently supplied the
missing half of the declaration with its own assumption**: a radius. Measured: **272/344 inside the
disc, 342/344 inside the box.** A Godot loader drawing an 8 m circle places **72 of 344 bodies
wrong**.

The criterion is unchanged — *every spawn lies inside the declared placement geometry*. What
changed is that the wire now declares the geometry and the stub tests **the declared one**:

```
"<OWNER> <SHAPE> -- <prose>"     OWNER ∈ {SIM-ROLLED, SYNTHETIC-ROLLED}
                                 SHAPE ∈ {BOX, DISC}
```

* ⚑ **The magnitude is NOT in the prose.** `placement_extents_m` is typed and was always correct;
  duplicating it into a string is the § 11.5 [R-38] defect this schema keeps retiring. Only the
  **shape word** rides prose.
* ⚑ **The fixture declares its own DISC** rather than inheriting the sim's BOX — it really does
  roll polar `(r = U(0,7), θ)`. A fixture that claimed the sim's geometry would put a false shape
  on its wire **and** make the shape test vacuous on the only baton that runs with no engine.
* ⚑ **Found by measurement, not by design:** my first BOX declaration contains the sentence *"NOT A
  DISC"*, so a substring scan matched **two** shapes and the parse came back ambiguous. The grammar
  is positional for that reason, and the reason is written where it can be re-read.

## 7 · ⑥ THE `path_coverage` SENTENCE — velocity, not direction

gamora's Discipline-#12 flag, taken. The old sentence — *"a knot is recorded at every **direction
change**"* — was **true and incomplete**, and a consumer who read it correctly still drew bodies up
to 0.154 m off on ticks where damage was dealt. His suggested wording rode his MIGRATION; these are
my words, and they say more than his suggestion because the boundary rule (§ 6) lands in the same
field:

> …a knot is recorded at every change of the walked path's **VELOCITY** — its direction **or its
> per-step travel** (the arrival step is CLIPPED at the engage ring and at an exact patrol-node
> arrival, which is a speed change on an unchanged bearing) … VERTEX-COMPLETE therefore means every
> **VELOCITY vertex**, which is STRICTLY MORE than every direction vertex … **A consumer MUST NOT
> assume uniform speed across a leg boundary; within a leg the speed IS uniform, and that is what
> makes the interpolation exact.**

⚑ **The wire sentence is bound to the artifact it ships with**, by test: the declaration describes
clipped-step vertices, so the supply must actually contain the 13 `clip` knots — *"or the sentence
is true of some other artifact"*.
