## 1 · What landed

`export/kc2_run_adapter.py` (new) + `tests/test_kc2_run_adapter.py` (new, 13 tests).

`build_baton()` consumes a *run-record*; `simulation/kc2/run.py` produces `KC2Run` objects; nothing
joined the two, so **`build_baton` was unreachable for a real run** — L-75(f). This module is that
join, for `E-s09-cp150`.

**The shape is the point.** The adapter wires every field with a sim-resident or DB-cited source
and **refuses to invent the rest**. A required field with no source raises `AdapterForkHalt`, which
names each fork, its options, and *the provenance consequence of each*. There is no default. A
default here would be indistinguishable, on the wire, from a measurement — `Actor.level: int`
cannot say *"I guessed"*, and the halt is the only construction in which the absence stays visible.

`resolve_forks({})` raises with the whole inventory; `resolve_forks(<partial>)` raises with the
remainder; a ruled-but-unavailable option raises with the missing supplier named. All three are
tested.
