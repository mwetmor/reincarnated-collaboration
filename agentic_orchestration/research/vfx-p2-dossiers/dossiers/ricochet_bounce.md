- skill_or_mtx_name: Spectral Shield Throw
- primary_url: [Mechanics showcase — chain at 2:54; split/return at 4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=68WmB7GJDKI)
- secondary_urls: [Official GGG skill reveal](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2089922), [mechanics reference](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Spectral_Shield_Throw)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: Controlled tests isolate the shield’s launch, target-to-target chain, impact shards, and modified return path. The separated demonstrations make this especially useful for studying the constituent legs of ricochet_return.
- readability_notes: The bright shield silhouette and straight inter-target paths remain readable at the standard isometric camera. Secondary shrapnel becomes noisy in dense encounters, but the primary projectile is distinct.

## Candidate 2: Blessed Shield — Shattering Throw (Diablo III)
- source_game: Diablo III
- skill_or_mtx_name: Blessed Shield — Shattering Throw rune
- primary_url: [Focused Shattering Throw video showcase](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tUuuudsmHTM)
- secondary_urls: [Official Diablo III skill page](https://eu.diablo3.blizzard.com/en-us/class/crusader/active/blessed-shield), [all-rune video index](https://www.blizzplanet.com/blog/comments/diablo-iii-reaper-of-souls-crusader-skills-videos/2)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: The initial shield produces a decisive first impact, then splits into three smaller shields that bounce among nearby enemies. Its repeated hard redirects are an exceptionally literal visualization of ricochet geometry.
- readability_notes: Bright holy coloration and discrete hit flashes make each redirection visible against Diablo III’s dark terrain. Rapid repeated casts can merge the fragments into a dense web, so isolated casts are the best reference frames.

- source_game: Grim Dawn
- skill_or_mtx_name: Aegis of Menhir with Avenging Shield

- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y

- readability_notes: The luminous shield and compact trails read well over Grim Dawn’s muted environments. Other devotion and aura effects occasionally obscure later bounces, but the skill showcase and lower-density encounters expose the path clearly.

## Candidate 4: Shield Throw — three-ricochet mechanic demo (Last Epoch)
- source_game: Last Epoch
- skill_or_mtx_name: Shield Throw
- primary_url: [Gameplay showcase with isolated three-ricochet demo in the final five seconds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FuYT1KrQorI)
- secondary_urls: [Forum guide explaining every ricochet and return leg](https://forum.lastepoch.com/t/0-8-5-updated-kryces-manifest-armour-shield-throw-simply-unethical-damage/46581), [skill mechanics page](https://lastepoch.tunklab.com/ability?a=ShieldThrow)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: This is the closest mechanical match: the shield travels from caster to target, ricochets between eligible targets, then returns to the caster. The final training-dummy demonstration deliberately exposes the individual path segments.
- readability_notes: The isolated demo gives excellent path readability because the caster, minion, and target form a sparse line. In full combat, Manifest Armour’s triggered spin effects can mask individual contacts, but the returning shield remains trackable.
