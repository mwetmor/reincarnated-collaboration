# VFX Reference Dossier — cone

## Candidate 1: Seismic Slam (Diablo III)
- source_game: Diablo III
- skill_or_mtx_name: Seismic Slam
- primary_url: [GameStar isolated skill video](https://www.gamestar.de/videos/diablo-3-skill-video-seismic-slam%2C65244.html)
- secondary_urls: [Official Blizzard skill page](https://eu.diablo3.blizzard.com/en-gb/class/barbarian/active/seismic-slam); [Blizzard patch notes confirming the cone geometry](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/14138344/patch-2-0-5-now-live)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: A forceful weapon windup resolves into a widening ground shockwave projected directly forward—the clearest “Seismic Slam” interpretation of a cone zone. Enemy launch and knockback provide an unmistakable impact phase.
- readability_notes: The expanding ground disturbance remains legible at the native isometric camera. Enemy reactions reinforce its reach even where the earthy VFX blends with terrain.

## Candidate 2: Ground Slam (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Ground Slam — updated base effect
- primary_url: [Official Path of Exile showcase video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NI8590SoqPA)
- secondary_urls: [Official Grinding Gear Games announcement](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/875033)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: The weapon strike originates at the caster and drives a broad fan of rock ridges outward across the ground. Its expanding lateral edge communicates cone width and forward travel without relying on targeting decals.
- readability_notes: The bright, raised rock front contrasts well against neutral terrain. The caster-to-edge progression remains readable through repeated attacks and different area sizes.

## Candidate 3: Celestial Tectonic Slam Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Celestial Tectonic Slam Effect
- primary_url: [Official MTX announcement with showcase videos](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2879022)
- secondary_urls: [Direct official embedded video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d1gz5ibobaM); [Official shop page](https://www.pathofexile.com/shop/item/CelestialTectonicSlamEffect)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: A grounded slam releases branching celestial fissures through a forward wedge, offering a segmented rather than continuous treatment of fan-spread motion. The fissure branches provide useful reference for filling a cone without rendering it as one opaque sheet.
- readability_notes: Bright celestial cracks make direction and reach highly visible at gameplay scale. The lingering luminous aftermath is attractive but comparatively dense and could obscure targets if copied at full intensity.

## Candidate 4: Blazing Salvo (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Blazing Salvo
- primary_url: [Official skill announcement and showcase](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2934377)
- secondary_urls: [Direct official embedded video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KUcy3O7-f0A)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: The cast launches multiple fiery bodies that spread toward a widening field of impact zones, making it a strong alternate interpretation of fan_spread plus zone delivery. It is less ground-bound than the slam references but offers especially clear launch, travel, and staggered-impact staging.
- readability_notes: Bright projectile arcs reveal direction before the ground detonations occur, while separated impact circles preserve gaps between targets. Dense repeated casts can become visually noisy.
