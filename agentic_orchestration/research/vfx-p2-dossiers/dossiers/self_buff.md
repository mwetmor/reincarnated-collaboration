## Candidate 1: Magmatic Molten Shell Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Magmatic Molten Shell Effect
- primary_url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EPzz5w6bKPI
- secondary_urls: https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3303018
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=N; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: A stationary, character-bound shell establishes the buff without implying a travel path. Its flowing-lava body and fiery expiration burst provide unusually complete active and termination phases.
- readability_notes: The bright orange shell strongly modifies the player silhouette at isometric scale. The expiration burst is conspicuous, though potentially noisy amid fire-heavy combat.

## Candidate 2: Archon (Diablo III)
- source_game: Diablo III
- skill_or_mtx_name: Archon
- primary_url: https://www.gamestar.de/videos/diablo-3-skill-video-archon%2C65241.html
- secondary_urls: https://eu.diablo3.blizzard.com/de-de/class/wizard/active/archon, https://www.diablowiki.net/Skill_Animations_Videos
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=N
- why_it_fits: Archon uses a centered transformation with no path signature, closely matching the Werewolf-style branch of self buffs. The footage covers the transition into a persistent empowered form and subsequent action while transformed.
- readability_notes: Replacing the entire silhouette makes the state exceptionally legible at gameplay distance. It communicates a major mode change more strongly than a lightweight statistical buff.

## Candidate 3: Illusionist Aura Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Illusionist Aura Effect
- primary_url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KDer1UFJ9W8
- secondary_urls: https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2527111
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=N; active=Y; impact=N
- why_it_fits: A turquoise-and-gold geometric symbol remains locked beneath the character while crystals and pyramids emanate locally. It is an especially clean reference for the archetype’s aura delivery geometry and absence of directional motion.
- readability_notes: The crisp floor symbol communicates ownership and radius without obscuring the character. Small emitted shapes may lose definition against bright or effect-dense terrain.

## Candidate 4: Dark Immortal Call Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Dark Immortal Call Effect
- primary_url: https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2138936/page/1
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=N; active=Y; impact=N
- why_it_fits: Feathers and black mist appear directly around the character with no projectile or path component. It is a useful reference for a short defensive self buff that needs a forceful activation cue without implying an outgoing attack.
- readability_notes: The dark mass contrasts well against bright combat effects and clearly marks the player’s state. It risks disappearing over dark floors, so value separation would need careful tuning.
