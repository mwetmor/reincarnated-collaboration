# VFX Reference Dossier — ring

## Candidate 1: Stormcult Shock Nova Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Stormcult Shock Nova Effect
- primary_url: [Official announcement with embedded showcase](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3177421)
- secondary_urls: [Direct showcase video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cea4AeqNNLE), [official MTX shop page](https://www.pathofexile.com/shop/item/StormcultShockNovaEffect)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: A dedicated player-centered nova reference: electrical energy gathers at the caster, resolves into a sharply defined ring, then expands through the surrounding zone. The preview repeats the cast against enemies, exposing the complete effect cycle.
- readability_notes: Bright lightning and a strong circular contour remain legible against dark terrain. The secondary broad pulse adds spectacle, though rapid recasting could create overlap.

## Candidate 2: Condemn (Diablo III)
- source_game: Diablo III
- skill_or_mtx_name: Condemn
- primary_url: [Condemn Crusader gameplay guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9gyow_LYzzE)
- secondary_urls: [Official Diablo III skill page](https://eu.diablo3.blizzard.com/en-gb/class/crusader/active/condemn), [rune-effect video collection](https://diablo.blizzplanet.com/blog/comments/diablo-iii-reaper-of-souls-crusader-skills-videos/4)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: Condemn provides unusually useful temporal separation: a three-second charge around the Crusader, an active inward pull with the Vacuum rune, and a centered radial explosion. It is a strong reference for making a ring burst feel anticipated and consequential.
- readability_notes: The charge establishes the character as the center before the pale-gold explosion fills the radius. The final bloom communicates force clearly, although its exact outer boundary is softer than Shock Nova’s.

## Candidate 3: Poison Nova (Diablo II: Resurrected)
- source_game: Diablo II: Resurrected
- skill_or_mtx_name: Poison Nova
- primary_url: [Poison Nova Necromancer build showcase](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2lJi7VTOANQ)
- secondary_urls: (optional)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: The Necromancer’s cast releases a complete 360-degree band of poison projectiles that visibly travels away from the caster. Its expanding circumference is an especially clean example of `burst_around_self` expressed through moving particles rather than a filled disc.
- readability_notes: Saturated green projectiles contrast strongly with Diablo II’s muted environments, while the open center preserves character visibility. Poison coloration on affected enemies reinforces impact after the ring passes.

## Candidate 4: Ring of Steel (Grim Dawn)
- source_game: Grim Dawn
- skill_or_mtx_name: Ring of Steel
- primary_url: [Ring of Steel Blademaster gameplay — Normal difficulty](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIwfKG231v4)
- secondary_urls: [Current Crate forum guide and video index](https://forums.crateentertainment.com/t/hc-ssf-ring-of-steel-blademaster-leveling-and-endgame-guide-1-2-1-6/149114), [Ultimate-difficulty footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1nq7RRpdhzs), [official Grim Dawn Wiki skill page](https://grimdawn.fandom.com/wiki/Ring_of_Steel_%28Skill%29)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=N; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: A corona of phantasmal blades appears around the Nightblade and strikes every adjacent direction simultaneously. It is a useful compact, weapon-driven alternative to elemental novas, with nearly instantaneous activation.
- readability_notes: The silver blade ring gives the burst a recognizable radial silhouette, especially in the lower-density Normal footage. Its brief lifetime and Grim Dawn’s proc-heavy combat can obscure the exact radius in endgame encounters.
