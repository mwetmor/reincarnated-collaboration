## Candidate 1: Celestial Flameblast Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Celestial Flameblast Effect
- primary_url: [Official video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FcsRlt_wjxY)
- secondary_urls: [Official MTX shop page](https://www.pathofexile.com/shop/item/CelestialFlameblastEffect)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: A remotely placed circular field expands through a clearly staged channel before resolving as a concentrated explosion. The growing perimeter communicates both the targeted location and eventual blast radius.
- readability_notes: The luminous boundary remains legible against dark terrain at the standard isometric camera. Interior detail intensifies without obscuring the circle’s edge.

## Candidate 2: Astral Storm Call Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Astral Storm Call Effect
- primary_url: [Official video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lhQiZae-djE)
- secondary_urls: [Official announcement with embedded video](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3261105), [Official MTX shop page](https://www.pathofexile.com/shop/item/AstralStormCallEffect)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: Each cast places a circular ground marker that persists as an anticipation telegraph before a vertical lightning strike detonates at its center. This is an especially clean reference for delayed ground placement followed by punctual impact.
- readability_notes: High-contrast purple markers clearly separate valid impact locations from the environment. Multiple placements remain individually readable, demonstrating how this geometry can scale under repeated casting.

## Candidate 3: Purple Firestorm Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Purple Firestorm Effect
- primary_url: [Official video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mp4SY6SQASA)
- secondary_urls: [Official announcement with embedded video](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3256281), [Official MTX shop page](https://www.pathofexile.com/shop/item/PurpleFirestormEffect)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: The cast establishes a remote circular zone, followed by descending projectiles and repeated localized impacts throughout that footprint. It provides strong coverage of a persistent circle whose active phase consists of continuing impacts rather than a single detonation.
- readability_notes: Bright descending streaks make individual impacts easy to anticipate from the gameplay camera. The overall circle is communicated cumulatively by impact distribution, so its outer boundary is softer than Flameblast’s or Storm Call’s.
