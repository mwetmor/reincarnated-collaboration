- skill_or_mtx_name: Demonic Split Arrow Effect

- secondary_urls: https://www.pathofexile.com/shop/item/DemonicSplitArrow
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y

- skill_or_mtx_name: Multishot — Arsenal
- primary_url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z24OI7oTYKo
- secondary_urls: https://eu.diablo3.blizzard.com/en-us/class/demon-hunter/active/multishot
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: This is the canonical screen-wide fan attack: a short bow windup releases a wedge-shaped volley, with Arsenal adding three secondary rockets. Sustained gameplay demonstrates how the fan reads during movement, repeated firing, and dense enemy impacts.
- readability_notes: The broad wedge communicates facing and coverage immediately at the standard isometric camera. Individual arrows become less separable during high-density combat, but the aggregate cone remains extremely legible.
