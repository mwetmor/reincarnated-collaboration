## Candidate 1: Ancestral Warchief (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Ancestral Warchief
- primary_url: [https://youtu.be/53exbcqunns](https://youtu.be/53exbcqunns)
- secondary_urls: [Official skill reveal](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/1657983)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: A stationary ancestor manifests at the placed totem, remains anchored, and independently performs repeated ground slams. The emergence, raised-arm anticipation, and circular shockwave cleanly separate summon, delegate-active, and impact phases without introducing a movement path.
- readability_notes: The large spectral silhouette and expanding ground shockwave read clearly at Path of Exile’s gameplay camera. Its compact circular attack footprint remains understandable amid surrounding combat.

- readability_notes: The trap bodies are small, but their bright pulsing heads and long lightning bolts make the delegate’s location and targeting direction immediately legible. Multiple traps preserve a recognizable formation while increasing electrical clutter.
