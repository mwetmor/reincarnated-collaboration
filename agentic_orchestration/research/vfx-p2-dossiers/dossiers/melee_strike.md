- skill_or_mtx_name: Prismatic Double Strike Effect
- primary_url: [Official showcase video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZ1bj_ysJFA)

- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: Two rapid, target-anchored weapon strokes produce compact prismatic arcs and discrete contact flashes. It is an especially clean point_strike reference because no projectile travel or persistent ground effect obscures the melee delivery.

- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
