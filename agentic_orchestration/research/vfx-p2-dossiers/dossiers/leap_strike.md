# VFX Reference Dossier — leap_strike

## Candidate 1: Demonic Leap Slam Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Demonic Leap Slam Effect
- primary_url: [Official Path of Exile video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TVLZ1wX443g)
- secondary_urls: [Official MTX shop page](https://www.pathofexile.com/shop/item/CultistLeapSlamEffect), [PoE Wiki identification page](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Demonic_Leap_Slam_Effect)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: A direct cosmetic treatment of Leap Slam, preserving the crouch-to-arc-to-ground-strike sequence while adding a red-and-black travel trail and forceful landing burst.
- readability_notes: The dedicated showcase uses Path of Exile’s normal isometric camera and repeats the complete move. Its restrained palette keeps the character trajectory and compact impact radius separable from the environment.

## Candidate 4: Fury Leap / Leaping Shaman (Last Epoch)
- source_game: Last Epoch
- skill_or_mtx_name: Fury Leap
- primary_url: [Leaping Shaman build-guide video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SkYPspTspaM)

- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: Fury Leap is a direct target-location leap followed by radial landing damage. The video’s Fury Leap section begins around 4:15 and repeated gameplay casts provide both long and short arcs plus impact-triggered secondary effects.
- readability_notes: The base movement silhouette remains legible at Last Epoch’s isometric camera, although proc-heavy landings become busier than the Path of Exile or Grim Dawn references. Repetition makes the core jump and impact distinguishable from the secondary effects.

- Searched Path of Exile’s official channel, MTX shop, and announcement archive for Leap Slam effects; verified dedicated Demonic, Sin, and Celestial showcase videos. Celestial was excluded from the strongest set because its intentional disappearance/teleport illusion obscures the physical leap arc.

- Searched Grim Dawn movement-rune guides and videos; found the Crate-hosted visual guide with three looping Leap GIFs. Dedicated official standalone video coverage was lacking.
- Searched Last Epoch official media/support pages and YouTube for Fury Leap; official documentation was available, but the clearest temporal footage found was the Leaping Shaman build guide.
- Searched Lost Ark for Dragon Advent; verified the skill description and general Scrapper guide footage, but found no clean dedicated official showcase.
- Checked Diablo IV’s official gameplay trailer; Leap appears around 1:20, but the brief edited shot offers weaker windup coverage than the selected references.
