# VFX Reference Dossier — blink

## Candidate 1: Harbinger Flame Dash (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Harbinger Flame Dash Effect
- primary_url: [Official Path of Exile video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-n8g6QtQixc)
- secondary_urls: [Official MTX shop page](https://www.pathofexile.com/shop/item/HarbingerFlameDashEffect)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=N; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: The character crosses a straight segment almost instantaneously while a luminous blue trail preserves the travel vector. Distinct departure and arrival bursts make this an especially clean reference for a line-directed blink.
- readability_notes: The saturated cyan trail and bright endpoint flares remain legible against subdued isometric environments. The persistent ground residue helps observers reconstruct the move after its brief active phase.

## Candidate 2: Distortion (Lost Ark)
- source_game: Lost Ark
- skill_or_mtx_name: Distortion
- primary_url: [Reaper skill-effect showcase; Distortion begins at 05:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEFT27d0IuA)
- secondary_urls: [Official Lost Ark Reaper Academy](https://www.playlostark.com/en-us/news/articles/reaper-academy); [Official Reaper preview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SujhoA09mHw)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: A compact darkness-gathering tell leads into a ten-meter straight-line displacement, with damage along the path and an additional landing hit. The controlled showcase repeats the base effect and variants, making all three phases easy to compare.
- readability_notes: The elongated shadow streak communicates direction and distance clearly at the gameplay camera. Its dark palette can lose contrast on dim terrain, but the endpoint flare and moving silhouette preserve legibility.

## Candidate 3: Shadow Strike (Grim Dawn)
- source_game: Grim Dawn
- skill_or_mtx_name: Shadow Strike
- primary_url: [Dedicated Shadow Strike skill overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uODooeXJGdE)
- secondary_urls: [Official Grim Dawn Wiki skill page](https://grimdawn.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_Strike_%28Skill%29); [Recent high-resolution build footage; gameplay begins at 08:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9K2CxJKRI_w)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=N; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: This is the target-locked Shadow Step variant of the archetype: the attacker seemingly vanishes, traverses a direct line, and reappears over the victim with a concentrated melee impact. The dedicated overview supplies repeatable inspection, while the newer footage demonstrates it under real combat density.
- readability_notes: The arrival strike and target response are strong, but the transit trace is thin and extremely fast. Cool-colored builds improve the trace, whereas dense proc effects can obscure it.

## Candidate 4: Automaton Lightning Warp (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Automaton Lightning Warp Effect
- primary_url: [Official Path of Exile video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hLUuMGm4sHM)
- secondary_urls: [Official MTX shop page](https://www.pathofexile.com/shop/item/AutomatonLightningWarpEffect); [Effect and skill description](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Automaton_Lightning_Warp_Effect)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: This provides the clearest staged-blink timing reference: cast and destination markers establish anticipation, the character disappears during transport, and arrival produces a strong circular discharge. It encodes the straight-line displacement through paired endpoints rather than a continuous trail.
- readability_notes: Red mechanical markers separate cleanly from most terrain and make both endpoints unmistakable. It is excellent for timing and destination readability, but weaker than Flame Dash as a literal visualization of the intervening line.

## Candidate 5: Demonic Flicker Strike (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Demonic Flicker Strike Effect
- primary_url: [Official Path of Exile video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=haybUUR1AAk)
- secondary_urls: [Official MTX shop page](https://www.pathofexile.com/shop/item/CultistFlickerStrikeEffect)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=N; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: The bloody-mist disappearance and reappearance provide a strong target-locked blink reference, particularly for rapid Shadow Step-style chaining. It demonstrates how a very short transit can remain readable through paired departure and impact motifs.
- readability_notes: The red-black mist has good contrast on pale terrain and clearly punctuates each hit. During repeated multi-target chains, individual travel vectors become difficult to follow, so the slower isolated casts are the most useful reference frames.
