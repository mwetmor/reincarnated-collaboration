## Candidate 1: Magic Missile (Diablo III)
- source_game: Diablo III
- skill_or_mtx_name: Magic Missile
- primary_url: [Gameswelt — Wizard Magic Missile Gameplay](https://www.gameswelt.de/diablo-iii/video/wizard-magic-missile-gameplay-131984)
- secondary_urls: [Official Blizzard skill page](https://eu.diablo3.blizzard.com/en-us/class/wizard/active/magic-missile); [Diablo Wiki visual breakdown](https://www.diablowiki.net/Magic_Missile)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: A compact purple projectile travels rapidly along a straight line with a restrained wake and resolves in a small target flash rather than reading as a beam. The dedicated gameplay clip covers the complete cast-flight-hit cycle.
- readability_notes: The bright projectile head contrasts clearly against Diablo III’s dark terrain while the narrow trail preserves source-to-target direction. The footage is pre-release, so it is strongest as a geometry and phase-separation reference rather than a final-polish target.
