# VFX Reference Dossier — circle

## Candidate 1: Stormcult Shock Nova Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Stormcult Shock Nova Effect
- primary_url: https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3177421
- secondary_urls: https://www.pathofexile.com/shop/item/StormcultShockNovaEffect
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: The official showcase isolates a caster-centered lightning burst with concentric energy rings and a strongly marked circular footprint. The gathering electricity, radial expansion, and terminal nova provide a clean windup-to-impact progression.
- readability_notes: Bright blue arcs and the luminous ground ring remain legible against muted terrain at gameplay distance. The inner and outer rings communicate radius exceptionally well, although the electrical filaments could become busy in dense combat.

## Candidate 2: Revamped Shock Nova (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Shock Nova
- primary_url: https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/1281570
- secondary_urls: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Skill%3AShockNova
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: This official teaser demonstrates the base move directly: a ring erupts around the caster, followed by a slightly larger nova area. It is especially valuable for studying how two successive radial layers can communicate a self-centered zone without changing the move’s origin.
- readability_notes: The expanding circumference is immediately understandable from an isometric camera. The distinction between the damaging ring and larger filled area introduces some semantic complexity but also offers a useful layered-circle reference.

## Candidate 3: Celestial Warcry Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Celestial Warcry Effect
- primary_url: https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2921459
- secondary_urls: https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Celestial_Warcry_Effect; https://www.pathofexile.com/shop/item/CelestialWarcryEffect
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: The official showcase applies a cosmic radial treatment to Warcry skills, making it a direct reference for the archetype’s War Cry exemplar. The character pose supplies anticipation, followed by an outward circular pulse whose contact communicates the affected zone.
- readability_notes: The expanding wave reads clearly around the stationary caster, while its celestial color contrast separates it from the ground. Its brief duration makes the boundary less persistent than Shock Nova’s, so the initial pulse must carry most of the gameplay communication.

## Candidate 4: Ring of Steel (Grim Dawn)
- source_game: Grim Dawn
- skill_or_mtx_name: Ring of Steel
- primary_url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nYh9Wij7NUA
- secondary_urls: https://grimdawn.fandom.com/wiki/Ring_of_Steel_%28Skill%29
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: The Nightblade active-skill showcase includes Ring of Steel, in which phantasmal blades erupt simultaneously around the character and strike adjacent enemies. The evenly distributed blades form a literal circumference and give the burst a physical, directional texture.
- readability_notes: The radial projectile distribution makes both origin and radius easy to parse at the normal camera. The older footage and subdued steel palette provide less contrast than modern elemental effects, but enemy hit reactions reinforce the impact.

## Candidate 5: Stygian Plague Bearer Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Stygian Plague Bearer Effect
- primary_url: https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3180474
- secondary_urls: https://www.pathofexile.com/shop/item/StygianPlagueBearerEffect
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=N; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: Activating Plague Bearer releases stored poison as a circular damage zone centered on and moving with the character. It is a strong reference for the longer-lived interpretation of the archetype: an initial release followed by a sustained active circle rather than a single instantaneous nova.
- readability_notes: Dark-blue perimeter flames establish a persistent boundary that remains visible while the character moves. The filled interior can obscure floor detail, making it useful for studying the tradeoff between zone persistence and battlefield clarity.
