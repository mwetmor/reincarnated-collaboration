# VFX Reference Dossier — orbit

## Candidate 1: Blade Vortex (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Blade Vortex
- primary_url: [Official skill-demonstration video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rCro9h8reZw)
- secondary_urls: [Official 2.1.0 patch notes](https://webcdn.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/1489915/filter-account-type/staff)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: Repeated casts accumulate ethereal blades into a persistent, player-centered orbit. The demonstration clearly exposes formation, sustained rotation, movement with the caster, and contact damage.
- readability_notes: The discrete blades and uncluttered circular silhouette remain legible at the normal isometric camera. The older effect is visually restrained, making its motion especially easy to study.

## Candidate 2: Shurikens — Blade Shield (Last Epoch)
- source_game: Last Epoch
- skill_or_mtx_name: Shurikens with Blade Shield
- primary_url: [Skill breakdown video—Blade Shield section at 7:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uUinnDksyzk&t=441s)
- secondary_urls: [Official Rogue skill-tree documentation](https://support.lastepoch.com/hc/en-us/articles/46362944419611-Rogue-Skill-Tree)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: Blade Shield converts the normal forward throw into shurikens that spin around the character for a fixed duration. The guide isolates the basic orbit and the larger Chakram variation, providing clean references for both sparse and heavy orbital payloads.
- readability_notes: Individual shuriken silhouettes remain identifiable against the ground, while their even spacing clearly communicates the circular path. Enemy contact is readable without a large impact burst obscuring the orbit.

## Candidate 3: Blessed Hammer — Dominion Rune (Diablo III)
- source_game: Diablo III: Reaper of Souls
- skill_or_mtx_name: Blessed Hammer — Dominion
- primary_url: [Dominion Rune gameplay showcase](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EXOpfzFUXC0)
- secondary_urls: [Official Diablo III skill page](https://eu.diablo3.blizzard.com/en-us/class/crusader/active/blessed-hammer)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: Dominion changes Blessed Hammer into a mobile orbit that remains centered on the Crusader as the character moves. It is an unusually literal match for `orbit_fixed`, with summon, sustained circulation, and enemy intersections visible in one short sequence.
- readability_notes: The bright holy hammer reads clearly against Diablo III’s darker environments and maintains a recognizable path during movement. The footage is from an early beta, so it is strongest as a motion reference rather than a final-polish reference.

## Candidate 4: Sawblade Blade Vortex (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Sawblade Blade Vortex
- primary_url: [Official MTX showcase video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3PVnPYuKPI)
- secondary_urls: [Official Sawblade Bundle announcement](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2804952), [official shop page](https://www.pathofexile.com/shop/item/SawbladeBundle)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: This preserves Blade Vortex’s player-centered fixed orbit but replaces wispy blades with large, materially solid saws. It is a strong reference for communicating orbital geometry through unmistakable payload silhouettes and rotational trails.
- readability_notes: The large circular teeth make rotation direction and occupied radius exceptionally clear at gameplay distance. It is visually heavier than the base effect but retains useful negative space around the character.
