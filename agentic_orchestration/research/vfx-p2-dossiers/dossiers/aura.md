## Candidate 1: Stygian Plague Bearer Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Stygian Plague Bearer Effect
- primary_url: [official announcement with embedded video](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3180474)
- secondary_urls: [direct showcase video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EVcP9Tiqu30), [official shop page](https://www.pathofexile.com/shop/item/StygianPlagueBearerEffect)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=N; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: The instant toggle produces a persistent, actor-centered field with no path signature. Dark-blue flames communicate continuous aura activity, while enemy damage supplies clear application feedback rather than a projectile impact.
- readability_notes: Strong color contrast and a stable player-centered footprint remain readable at the isometric camera. The boundary is somewhat soft, so peripheral flame density must carry the radius.

## Candidate 2: Conviction Aura (Diablo II: Resurrected)
- source_game: Diablo II: Resurrected
- skill_or_mtx_name: Conviction
- primary_url: [D2R Skills & Abilities — Conviction Aura](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aDa8ZWS8ano)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=N; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: This is an exact exemplar: a continuously active, character-centered debuff aura with periodic visual pulses and recipient-side status feedback. It demonstrates how an aura can communicate influence without filling the entire radius with opaque effects.
- readability_notes: The repeating ground motif and affected-enemy markers survive the distant gameplay camera well. Exact range is inferred between pulses rather than continuously outlined.
