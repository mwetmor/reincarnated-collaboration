- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: The caster erects a persistent, laterally oriented strip at a chosen location; enemies crossing it receive damage, chill, and a freeze response. The clip clearly separates placement, sustained lane activity, and enemy contact.
- readability_notes: Bright end pillars and the dense white-blue center make the wall’s endpoints and crossing boundary highly legible from the gameplay camera.

## Candidate 2: Stygian Flame Wall Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Stygian Flame Wall Effect
- primary_url: [https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2975769](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2975769)
- secondary_urls: [official embedded YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/embed/0_e_leOf6Qk); [skill and MTX mechanics](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Stygian_Flame_Wall_Effect)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: This is a direct placed-lane implementation: a cast establishes a fixed transverse wall, the flames persist, and contact or projectiles passing through create the effect’s interaction phase. It is especially useful for studying how animated height can reinforce a narrow ground footprint.
- readability_notes: Saturated blue-white flames contrast strongly against PoE’s brown and gray environments. The bright base and darker upper wisps preserve the lane silhouette without becoming a completely opaque screen.

## Candidate 3: Blade Sentinel (Diablo II: Resurrected)
- source_game: Diablo II: Resurrected
- skill_or_mtx_name: Blade Sentinel
- primary_url: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pEO9CrTHPrk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pEO9CrTHPrk)
- secondary_urls: [focused Blade skills explainer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=twNiNMBGDKU); [official Blizzard Blade Sentinel patch notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23788293/diablo-ii-resurrected-patch-2-4-ladder-now-live)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: The Assassin places a spinning blade onto a fixed path between cast origin and target point; it repeatedly patrols that lane and damages enemies intersecting it. This is the strongest reference for motion inside the lane rather than a visually static wall.
- readability_notes: Repeated back-and-forth travel teaches the lane direction clearly, but the individual blade is small and relatively low-contrast. A modern interpretation would benefit from a faint persistent path marker or brighter edge trail.

## Candidate 4: Firewall (Diablo IV)
- source_game: Diablo IV
- skill_or_mtx_name: Firewall
- primary_url: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mYVb1KXsNuw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mYVb1KXsNuw)
- secondary_urls: [official Blizzard Firewall patch reference](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24123440/diablo-iv-1-5-0-patch-notes)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: Firewall places elongated burning strips at targeted positions, with a clear ignition event, sustained ground phase, and damage response as enemies occupy the lane. Repeated casts also demonstrate how the geometry behaves when several lanes overlap.
- readability_notes: The hot central strip reads well against Diablo IV’s subdued environments. Heavy stacking eventually obscures individual boundaries, making the early single-wall casts more useful than the densely layered combat shots.
