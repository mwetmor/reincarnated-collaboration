# VFX Reference Dossier — line

## Candidate 1: Twilight Explosive Arrow Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Twilight Explosive Arrow Effect
- primary_url: [official showcase](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3256281)
- secondary_urls: [direct showcase video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1PQNwLENUmU)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: A shining arrow follows a fixed straight trajectory, embeds in the target, and culminates in a delayed celestial explosion. The delay makes launch, travel, contact, and detonation especially easy to study separately.
- readability_notes: The luminous gold projectile and trail remain legible against subdued terrain. Multiple embedded arrows can become busy, but the directional line and terminal burst stay clear.

- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Vaal Orb Ice Shot Effect
- primary_url: [official showcase](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3098201)

- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: The bow release sends a compact red projectile directly along the aim ray, followed by a conspicuous impact cone. It is a strong reference for differentiating a narrow line-delivery body from a wider contact response.
- readability_notes: The saturated red trail reads well over Path of Exile’s muted environments. The impact fan is broad without obscuring the projectile’s original direction.

## Candidate 3: Bone Spear (Diablo III)
- source_game: Diablo III
- skill_or_mtx_name: Bone Spear
- primary_url: [Bone Spear gameplay and build showcase](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=peMAi0k5j-g)
- secondary_urls: [official Diablo III skill page](https://eu.diablo3.blizzard.com/en-us/class/necromancer/active/bone-spear)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: This is an exact exemplar: a narrow bone projectile travels in a straight line and pierces every enemy on that ray. The video provides repeated casts in both its opening gameplay and the Greater Rift demonstration at 07:40.
- readability_notes: The pale spear contrasts strongly with Diablo III’s dark floors and makes the piercing line easy to follow. Simulacrum duplication and dense combat occasionally add clutter, so isolated casts are the best frames to study.

## Candidate 4: Scientist and Project Essence Drain Effects (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Scientist Essence Drain Effect / Project Essence Drain Effect
- primary_url: [official dual-effect showcase](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2854542)
- secondary_urls: [Scientist video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SjYiiXS-GPE), [Project video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8LIQpG_OtFY)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: Both variants expose the same compact cast-to-target grammar: a brief hand cast, a bright straight-moving bolt, and a distinct target-contact state. They are useful references for a magical line projectile without the visual mass of an arrow or spear.
- readability_notes: Electric green and glowing orange produce excellent contrast at the normal isometric camera. The projectile trail communicates direction clearly, though the impact is less forceful than Explosive Arrow’s detonation.
