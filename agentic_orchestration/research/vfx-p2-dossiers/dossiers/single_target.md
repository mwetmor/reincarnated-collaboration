# VFX Reference Dossier — single_target

## Candidate 1: Ice Spear Rework (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Ice Spear (3.5 reworked effect)
- primary_url: [official GGG showcase video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cpvThDY-pnM)
- secondary_urls: [official announcement](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2241894), [skill reference](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Ice_Spear)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: GGG explicitly designed Ice Spear for eliminating single enemies. Its compact shards travel along a clean straight trajectory, visibly arm after a short distance, and accelerate toward the target.
- readability_notes: The bright shard cores and narrow trails remain distinct against dark terrain. Sequential firing can become busy, but individual projectile paths remain legible.

## Candidate 2: Project Essence Drain Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Project Essence Drain Effect
- primary_url: [official GGG showcase video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8LIQpG_OtFY)
- secondary_urls: [official announcement](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2854542), [effect reference](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Project_Essence_Drain_Effect)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: Essence Drain is an especially clean one-cast, one-projectile reference: a compact emissive payload travels directly from caster to target and resolves with a localized contact effect. The Project treatment clearly separates projectile body, trail, and impact residue.
- readability_notes: Its orange core and electric-green accents maintain strong contrast at gameplay distance. The restrained trail preserves the targeting line without resembling a beam.

## Candidate 3: Direblade Fireball Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Direblade Fireball Effect
- primary_url: [official GGG showcase video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2dum83y6rz0)
- secondary_urls: [official announcement](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3206147/filter-account-type/staff), [effect reference](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Direblade_Fireball_Effect), [base-skill reference](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Fireball)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: A discrete blue-purple payload follows a direct projectile path and terminates in a sharply timed collision burst. Fireball’s small splash is a secondary feature; its primary read remains caster-to-target travel followed by impact.
- readability_notes: The saturated core and persistent flame trail make direction and speed easy to parse. The terminal burst is conspicuous without obscuring the projectile’s arrival point.

## Candidate 4: Void Emperor Ice Spear Effect (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Void Emperor Ice Spear Effect
- primary_url: [official GGG showcase video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nCqeCyanL78)
- secondary_urls: [official announcement](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3283240), [effect reference](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Void_Emperor_Ice_Spear_Effect)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: This preserves Ice Spear’s narrow, accelerating straight-line motion while replacing the pale ice treatment with compact red void shards. It is useful as a darker thematic alternative to Candidate 1 on identical delivery geometry.
- readability_notes: The red cores contrast well with PoE’s muted environments, while thin trails communicate direction without widening the perceived hit area. Rapid volleys are less clean than isolated casts.
