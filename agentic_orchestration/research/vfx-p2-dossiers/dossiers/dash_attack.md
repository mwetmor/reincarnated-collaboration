# VFX Reference Dossier — dash_attack

## Candidate 1: Demonic Shield Charge (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Demonic Shield Charge Effect
- primary_url: [Official Path of Exile showcase video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obUQAgSCbxs)
- secondary_urls: [Official MTX shop page](https://www.pathofexile.com/shop/item/CultistShieldChargeEffect); [Shield Charge mechanics reference](https://www.poewiki.net/wiki/Shield_Charge)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: An exact straight-line attack-movement reference: the character braces behind the shield, accelerates along a narrow path, and terminates in a frontal collision burst. The demonic treatment adds a persistent directional trail without obscuring the underlying charge geometry.
- readability_notes: The dark red trail and concentrated arrival burst remain legible at the normal isometric camera. The effect clearly separates travel direction from the larger impact footprint.

- skill_or_mtx_name: Shield Charge
- primary_url: [Focused Shield Charge demonstration and explanation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-jrsw_04QWQ)
- secondary_urls: [Shield Charge animation-phase reference](https://www.poe2wiki.net/wiki/Shield_Charge)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: The move exposes the archetype’s phases unusually clearly: shield-raising windup, steerable straight-line rush, and forceful collision/winddown. It is especially useful for studying how body pose and shield silhouette can carry the motion before heavier VFX are added.
- readability_notes: The sustained defensive pose and forward dust wake make direction readable even amid combat. The slower, extended active phase is useful for frame-by-frame study.
