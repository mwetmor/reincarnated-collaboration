## Candidate 5: Splitting Steel (Path of Exile)
- source_game: Path of Exile
- skill_or_mtx_name: Splitting Steel
- primary_url: [https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3109096](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3109096)
- secondary_urls: [https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2940439](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2940439)
- media_type: video
- temporal_coverage: windup=Y; active=Y; impact=Y
- why_it_fits: The second embedded clip in the official article shows a single metallic projectile dividing at its target point into multiple outgoing shards, followed by compact impact bursts. Mechanically and spatially, it is one of the closest references to a literal fork_split.
- readability_notes: The sharp launch sound, narrow projectile trail, and clustered impacts reinforce the phase boundaries. Metallic shards are less chromatically distinct than the elemental candidates and become harder to track during dense combat.
