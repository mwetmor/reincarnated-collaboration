## Summary

Six confirmed cross-franchise mechanical groups each carry a distinct player-feel signature that transcends their mechanical definition. WHIRLWIND is a momentum-and-spectacle archetype — players praise effortless forward motion and pan-screen destruction; the dominant pain point is resource fragility when the spin breaks. TOTEM-SENTRY players feel like turret managers or absentee landlords — they set placements and move away while structures fight for them. TRAP-MINE splits sharply between ambush-preloading satisfaction and frustration with detonation delay; the archetype's most celebrated word is "melt" applied to boss encounters. CHANNELED-BEAM is defined by a bargain: commit to immobility, receive ramp-powered overwhelming output; community language circles around "holding" and "rooting" and the voltage-building charge. AURA is the proximity-kill archetype — walking itself is the weapon; celebrated phrases all involve motion-as-offense. MINION-PET produces the commander/general fantasy — player as director of a proxy force, hands-off once summoned, with re-summoning friction as the recurring complaint.

## Group 1: WHIRLWIND (15 kits)
*d2-ww-barb, d2-bvc, d2-ww-sin, d3-ww-wastes, d4-blood-wave, di-whirlwind-barb, gd-eor-warlord, hades1-aspect-guan-yu, le-warpath-vk, poe1-cyclone, poe2-twister, tq2-whirlwind-rogue, ud-cwc-spin-caster, ud-whirlwind-str, vs-fuwalafuwaloo*

### 1. Play-feel vocabulary

The dominant community vocabulary is consistent across franchises:
- **"Spin to Win" / "Spin2Win"** — most-repeated phrase across D2, D3, D4, GD, LE communities (maxroll.gg D4, mobalytics.gg D4, D3 guide, GD grimtools community). Signals effortless victory through perpetual rotation.
- **"Human blender"** — used in D4 guides (maxroll.gg D4 WW guide) to describe the AoE sweep sensation.
- **"Unstoppable hurricane of steel"** — Icy Veins D4 phrase; mythic-register naming candidate.
- **"Chill and comfy"** / **"chill gameplay"** — repeated in D2 (maxroll.gg D2R guide) and D4 guides; signals low-stress uptime play.
- **"Blast around like crazy"** — D3 Wastes guide (maxroll.gg D3); captures the speed-plus-chaos feel.
- **"Lighting quick"** — D3 guide speed descriptor.
- **"Momentum"** — implicit in GD Eye of Reckoning community references ("Spin 2 win-style combat... familiar to players of the Diablo Whirlwind Barbarian," LevelSkip/Grimtools community).
- **"Zone control"** — Hades Guan Yu community (steam discussions); the spinning radius as territory.
- **"Mowing"** — VS Fuwalafuwaloo community; lawn-mowing motion through enemy clusters.

### 2. Usage pattern

Moment-to-moment play: maintain continuous spin, steer into enemy density, manage resource (Fury/Wrath/Spirit) to avoid spin-break. D4: move in circles through packs, deploy shout cooldowns before tougher enemies, use gap-closer to recharge. D3: spin through monsters while generating Dust Devils that trail behind; the visual result is screen-filling tornado patterns. D2: position into tight triangles cutting pack edges to optimize hit frames (guide: "cutting around the outer edge of the density"). GD Eye of Reckoning: commit spin, manage Energy regeneration, hold terrain. PoE Cyclone (pre-3.13): click-route routing — cursor distance controlled animation length; post-conversion: hold-button channeling with cursor-follow. Hades Guan Yu: charge the spinning throw, release into groups; upgrade path targets Massive Spin and Quick Spin.

### 3. Celebrated moments

- Dust Devil tornado visual cascade in D3 (screen fills with fast-moving tornado trails).
- Grouping enemies every 2 seconds via D4 Gohr's Devastating Grips — satisfying cadence (maxroll.gg D4).
- "Wiping out entire rooms just by holding a button" — Hades Guan Yu community (steam discussion).
- Full-speed clear in GD/PoE where resource holds and the spin never breaks.
- PoE Cyclone: "Love the skill, nothing else to say" despite frustrations — the core feel is beloved.

### 4. Pain points

- **Resource fragility**: "Fury collapse can break the loop" (D4); "can feel a bit clunky" (D2R); if the spin stops mid-pack, the power fantasy collapses.
- **Potion lockout**: "CANNOT use Potions while spinning" in D2 — mechanical restriction creates vulnerability windows (maxroll.gg D2R guide).
- **Single-target gap**: all WW variants struggle against single stationary bosses; the sweeping AoE is overkill on 1v1.
- **Repetitive loop**: D2R guide explicitly flags "Repetitive Gameplay" as a con.
- **PoE Cyclone routing lock**: community feedback thread (poewiki/forum thread/251424/p135): "you can't cancel the cycloning to leap slam or run away," "it will continue the full path to where the cursor was," "Cyclone needs to stop when button is released." Players developed workaround: keep cursor as close to character as possible to shorten animation lock.
- **Hades Guan Yu charge vulnerability**: "I have lost count of how much damage I took while charging it up" — the brief immobile charge window is a repeated complaint.

### 5. Cross-franchise feel drift

| Franchise | Dominant feel shift |
|---|---|
| D2 WW | Precise mechanical optimization (breakpoints, IAS, weapon-swap); "triangles" positioning; corpse dependency. Skill-expressive, somewhat clunky. |
| D3 WW Wastes | Speed-maxed spectacle with Dust Devil visual trail; "blast around like crazy"; near-permanent Wrath of the Berserker. Smoothed into momentum pure-play. |
| D4 WW | Resource-management rhythm; shout rotation as secondary loop; Fury collapse is the core tension. Most mechanically demanding of the Diablo family. |
| PoE Cyclone | Historical controversy over click-routing animation lock ("pseudo-channeling even before Channeling was introduced"). Post-3.13 conversion to true channel normalized feel. Most player-control-sensitive variant. |
| GD Eye of Reckoning | Most positional-combat feel; tank-forward (Physique investment); least "speedfarm" of the group. |
| Hades Guan Yu | Shortest engagement loop: charge-release-zone; healing-on-hit creates a feedback loop not present elsewhere. Room-clearing satisfying only when "maxed out." |
| VS Fuwalafuwaloo | Autonomous orbit (circle of slashes cycles around character passively); hybrid of active WW and passive aura; unlike all others it does not require player movement initiation. |

## Group 2: TOTEM-SENTRY (24 kits)
*chr-mechanist-turret-drone, d3-m6-sentries, d3-mundunugu-sb, d4-death-trap, d4-mighty-throw, d4-touch-of-death, gd-skeleton-ritualist, le-skeleton-necro, le-squirrel-bm, le-storm-totem-shaman, le-wraithlord-necro, poe1-ea-ballista, poe1-fire-trap, poe1-forbidden-rite, poe1-glacial-cascade-mines, poe1-pizza-sticks, poe1-seismic-trap, poe1-siege-ballista, poe1-warchief, poe2-infernal-legion, poe2-minion-infernalist, poe2-warbringer-totems, tq-battlemage-warfare-earth, tq2-forge-turrets*

### 1. Play-feel vocabulary

- **"Set and forget"** — most-cited totem phrase across PoE and GD communities (poe-vault.com guide, rpgstash.com PoE2 totem guide); describes placement then disengagement.
- **"Turret manager"** — poe-vault.com Freezing Pulse Totem guide: "totem builds employ a unique playstyle where you are not the one dealing damage... rely on relatively compact structures that attack monsters for you."
- **"Lazy totem build"** / **"one-button build"** — rpgstash.com PoE2 totem overview; "100% beginner-friendly."
- **"Place and run"** — practical execution phrase; "run in circles until everything dies" (poe-vault.com totem guide).
- **"Autonomous"** — community vocabulary for totems' independent fire behaviour.
- **"Squishy"** — the recurring counterweight: "Never stops feeling squishy" (poe-vault.com Freezing Pulse Totem guide); the player is mobile but unarmored.
- **"Delegate"** / **"proxy damage"** — implicit in D3 M6 Sentries and PoE Siege Ballista; player as damage-delegator.
- **"Outpost"** / **"fortification"** — poe2-warbringer-totems community (u4n.com Shockwave Totem guide); totem as placed defensive structure.
- **"Deploy and kite"** — movement strategy described across multiple totem guides.

### 2. Usage pattern

Placement is instantaneous; the skill rotation is minimal. Primary loop: enter zone, place totems at optimal positions (often center-of-pack), move away and circle. Totems fire independently. Player repositions to pull remaining enemies into totem range or places additional totems at choke points. Boss fights involve gem-swapping to single-target gems (noted pain point in poe-vault.com). PoE2 Warbringer totems add rage mechanic layer — player must maintain aggression to feed rage into totem network.

### 3. Celebrated moments

- "Great damage on no budget" — poe-vault.com Freezing Pulse Totem; early-game power without investment is deeply satisfying.
- D3 Mundunugu Sentries: placing all six sentries in an optimized formation then watching a boss dissolve.
- LE Storm Totem Shaman: storm visuals with permanent uptime; passive spectacle while player dodges.
- PoE Siege Ballista: ballista forest visual with multiple placed units firing simultaneously.
- "Very fun to play" and "beginner-friendly" signals: the totem archetype is reliably praised for accessibility.

### 4. Pain points

- **Squishiness**: totems absorb attacks but the player is the exposed unit; multiple guides flag persistent fragility.
- **Gem-swap friction**: bossing requires switching gems (poe-vault.com); disrupts flow.
- **Placement timing**: if totems are placed poorly or in a choke that clears before they fire, the cycle resets wastefully.
- **Totem limit**: hitting the totem cap and managing which to replace is a micro-management layer not present in direct-cast builds.
- **"Not hardcore viable"** — poe-vault.com; survivability concern for permanent death modes.

### 5. Cross-franchise feel drift

| Franchise | Dominant feel shift |
|---|---|
| PoE Totems/Ballistas | Richest positioning meta; gem-swap bossing adds friction; wide variety (ballista, shockwave, warchief) shifts from pure-passive to rage-enabled active role. |
| PoE2 Warbringer Totems | Rage-mechanic layer: player must remain aggressive to fuel totem network; hybrid of active/passive. |
| D3 M6 Sentries | Fixed number (6) with placement management; visual saturation when all sentries fire; boss-phase resets all placements. |
| LE Totems/Skeletons | Overlaps with Minion-Pet border; Storm Totem is clearly stationary; skeleton units show behavior drift toward autonomous movement (group boundary case). |
| GD Skeleton Ritualist | Ritualist-flavor: totems tied to necromantic identity; less "engineer" more "summoner." |
| Chronicon Turret Drone | Most "engineer" feel of the group; technology framing rather than arcane. |

## Group 3: TRAP-MINE (23 kits)
*chr-crown-proc-engine, chr-fire-berserker, chr-firestorm-warlock, chr-fulmination-templar, d2-frenzy-barb, d2-horker, d3-frenzy-h90, d3-manald-heal, d3-pestilence-lance, d4-payback-sb, hades1-zeus-chain, hot-landsknecht-grenades, hot-shieldmaiden-block, le-healing-hands-paladin, le-smite-paladin, poe1-aegis-max-block, poe1-autobomber, poe1-bladefall-bladeblast, poe1-cwdt-loop, poe1-detonate-dead, poe1-lightning-conduit, poe1-mjolner, poe1-soulrend*

*Note: This group as constituted in the CSV is mechanically broad — it includes PoE true trap-throws (seismic trap), CWDT self-trigger loops (autobomber, heartbound), block-proc triggers (aegis max block, shieldmaiden-block), and reactive counter-triggers (Hades zeus-chain, D2 frenzy). The feel-layer analysis addresses two sub-signatures within the group: (A) throw-and-trigger traps, and (B) self-trigger / proc-engine loops.*

### 1. Play-feel vocabulary

**Sub-signature A — Throw-and-trigger (trap/mine placement):**
- **"Toss and move"** — core rhythm phrase in poe-vault.com Ice Trap guide: "Throw a few traps and move on."
- **"Preload"** — established community verb: throw traps before boss becomes vulnerable; "throw all Seismic Trap charges onto the boss before triggering the fight" (poecurrency.com seismic trap guide).
- **"Stack"** — accumulation language: "You can have over 20 traps out at a time" (poe-vault.com Ice Trap).
- **"Melt"** — the celebrated payoff word: "Melts bosses" (seismic trap guide); trap stacking into single burst describes best-in-class moments.
- **"Architect"** / **"engineer"** — implicit in build vocabulary; deliberate layering of trap types.
- **"Divisive"** — community self-description: "Some people love it and some hate it" (poe-vault.com Ice Trap guide). The gap between placement and detonation splits players.
- **"Like playing Brands"** — PoE community comparison (poe-vault.com); indirect-damage-feel vs direct-cast.

**Sub-signature B — Self-trigger / proc-engine loops:**
- **"Infinite loop"** — CWDT autobomber: "Infinite Cast when Damage Taken loop" (odealo.com Heartbound Loop guide); perpetual self-sustaining cycle.
- **"Engine"** / **"machine"** — community vocabulary for autobomber: "self-sustaining system," "engineered elegance" (odealo.com).
- **"Loop fires"** — celebration language when the breakpoint calculates correctly.
- **"Rube Goldberg"** — implicit in community descriptions of CWDT cascades; not explicitly cited but the "painfully complex process" language maps to this concept.
- **"Effortless execution"** after complex setup (odealo.com): "you don't really have to do anything except pop your Flask."
- **"Breakpoint"** — precision-math vocabulary; "0.231s breakpoint" (odealo.com); community mini-games around tick-matching.

### 2. Usage pattern

**Sub-A:** Enter zone, throw trap cluster into pack center (broad AoE, no precision required for maps), move on. For bosses: position before phase start, throw full charges, trigger fight, watch explosion cascade. Repositioning mid-boss to throw additional stacks. Flask management during movement.

**Sub-B (CWDT loop):** One-time complex setup via Path of Building math. Gameplay loop: weapon-swap twice to initiate, pop flask, loop self-sustains indefinitely. Player's in-combat role is nearly zero — "you don't really have to do anything." The complexity cost is all in gearing/setup phase, not execution.

### 3. Celebrated moments

- Full trap stack detonating on boss = "melt" moment (seismic trap community, poecurrency.com).
- Loop validation in hideout before entering content: "you can stay in your hideout to try out if it will actually work" (odealo.com autobomber) — the test-fire moment is celebrated as a proof-of-craft.
- Ice Trap's "big AoE thrown into the center of packs" — mapping efficiency feel (poe-vault.com).
- D4 Payback Sorceress counter-trigger: the retaliatory damage spike when absorbing an enemy hit.
- Hades Zeus chain lightning branching across rooms — the cascade visual is the celebrated moment.

### 4. Pain points

- **Detonation delay / travel time**: trap must land, arm, then trigger on contact — gap between input and output creates frustration vs instant-fire skills.
- **Boss phase resets**: traps laid pre-fight are cleared when boss enters immune phase; requires re-throwing.
- **Setup complexity (Sub-B)**: "Very expensive and hard to set up" (odealo.com); "requires external tools (Path of Building)" to verify breakpoints.
- **Flask dependency (Sub-B)**: "reduced Flask Charges gained Maps" can break the infinite loop (odealo.com).
- **Emotional split**: the trap archetype's biggest community pain point is that some players find the detonation-delay feel fundamentally unrewarding vs direct damage confirmation.

### 5. Cross-franchise feel drift

| Franchise | Dominant feel shift |
|---|---|
| PoE Traps (Seismic, Ice, Fire) | Placement-and-move playstyle; rich preload-boss meta; explicit "toss and move on" rhythm; most trap-native feel. |
| PoE Mines (Glacial Cascade) | Slightly more involved: mine must be detonated by separate button; adds a detonation layer vs trap auto-trigger. |
| PoE CWDT/Autobomber | Proc-engine archetype; machine feel post-setup; zero active play once loop fires. |
| D2 Frenzy Barb / Horker | Counter-trigger framing absent; these are aggressive-stance builds where "trap" label refers to coordinate system, not placed objects. Feel is closer to reactive-stance than ambush. |
| HoT Grenades | Explicit "throw object" feel closest to trap fantasy but on shorter loop. |
| Hades Zeus chain | Reactive/triggered: chain fires on hit of primary attack; cascade visual is celebrated; setup is invisible. |

## Group 4: CHANNELED-BEAM (9 kits)
*d3-arachyr-firebats, di-ray-of-frost-wizard, gd-aar-spellbinder, gd-drain-essence-spellbinder, gd-flames-of-ignaffar-purifier, le-ghostflame-warlock, poe1-divine-ire, poe1-incinerate, ud-flamethrower-channel*

### 1. Play-feel vocabulary

- **"Hold still"** / **"stand still"** — most recurring community constraint descriptor; guides universally flag immobility as the cost of channeling (Icy Veins D4 Incinerate, zleague.gg Last Epoch channeling guide).
- **"Ramp"** / **"charge up"** — the build-to-peak language: D4 Incinerate "must Channel for two seconds for maximum damage and Overheating" (maxroll.gg D4 Incinerate guide); PoE Divine Ire charges before releasing nova.
- **"Flamethrower"** — player-coined fantasy phrase for fire beam experience (maxroll.gg D4 Incinerate: "becoming a living flamethrower").
- **"Melt"** — beam payoff word; "defeats everything it touches" (maxroll.gg D4 Incinerate).
- **"Locked in"** / **"rooted"** — constraint vocabulary from community; Icy Veins D4 Incinerate: "requires you to stand still for extended periods of time that can make surviving harder."
- **"Commit"** — the decision point; channeling demands a position commitment before enemies are fully visible.
- **"Pierce"** — GD AAR community (wiki); beam geometry; hitting through lined-up enemies.
- **"Death ray"** / **"aether beam"** — GD community names (grimdawn.fandom.com); mythic-register naming candidates.
- **"Sustained pressure"** — LE Ghostflame community (zleague.gg): continuous damage output without cooldown gaps.
- **"Intermittent channeling"** — LE Ghostflame mechanics (zleague.gg); recommended to pulse rather than hold to manage positioning.

### 2. Usage pattern

All beam skills share a core constraint: the player roots to a spot and aims the beam at enemy clusters. Moment-to-moment:
1. **Setup phase**: group enemies or position to maximize beam path length.
2. **Channel phase**: hold skill button, steer beam toward target.
3. **Reposition**: break channel, dodge/kite, re-establish position for next channel.

D4 Incinerate adds ramp: partial channel yields partial output; full 2-second hold reaches maximum ("Overheat"). PoE Divine Ire is burst-channel: charge to maximum, release as single large nova rather than sustained stream. GD AAR is sustained stream with no ramp limit. LE Ghostflame: pulsed channeling recommended for survival — intermittent hold rather than sustained lock.

### 3. Celebrated moments

- Beam threading through packed enemy rows, hitting every enemy in line (all guides; "pierces through all enemies," Icy Veins D4).
- Full ramp payoff in D4 Incinerate — the Overheat damage spike after 2 seconds of sustained fire.
- PoE Divine Ire charge release — the visual and mechanical payoff of a fully-charged beam discharging as area explosion.
- GD AAR: converting between chaos, aether, and vitality damage allows thematic customization; community celebrates the build flexibility.
- LE Ghostflame: "dynamic gameplay and strong synergy with other skills" (zleague.gg); Wandering Spirits coexistence gives visual texture.

### 4. Pain points

- **Immobility = vulnerability**: the root cost is the universal complaint. "Stand still for extended periods that can make surviving harder" (Icy Veins D4 Incinerate; maxroll.gg D4 Incinerate).
- **Enemies closing distance**: beam works best at midrange; melee enemies converge during channel.
- **Ramp waste**: interrupting D4 Incinerate before 2s loses ramp investment; creates anxiety around channel commitment.
- **Kiting tension**: "Kite enemies when they become Unstoppable" (maxroll.gg D4 Incinerate) — must break channel for the hardest content moments.
- **Single-target limitation in some variants**: GD Drain Essence community notes poor AoE when enemies are not clustered.
- **LE Ghostflame community concern**: "comparative lackluster performance in clearing content" vs other Warlock builds (zleague.gg).

### 5. Cross-franchise feel drift

| Franchise | Dominant feel shift |
|---|---|
| D3 Arachyr Firebats | Spider/bat visual wrapping; aesthetic uniqueness but same hold-and-aim feel. Resource management (mana) as secondary loop. |
| D4 Incinerate | Ramp mechanic adds commitment tension not present in other beam builds; Overheat state creates explicit peak moment. |
| DI Ray of Frost Wizard | Slowdown/freeze utility layered on beam; control-plus-damage dual identity. |
| GD AAR Spellbinder | Reflect/mirror mechanics; Spellbinder class adds layered passive persistence; most mechanically complex variant. |
| GD Flames of Ignaffar | Ground-burn trail secondary effect; beam leaves lasting zone rather than point-in-time damage. |
| LE Ghostflame | Hybrid fire+necrotic; pulsing recommended over sustained hold; most survivability-aware of the group. |
| PoE Divine Ire | Charge-to-release vs sustained hold; unique burst-beam rather than stream; closest to "charge up and unload" rather than "flamethrower." |
| PoE Incinerate | Ramp without release peak — scales indefinitely while held; most extreme version of the channel-reward curve. |
| Undecember Flamethrower | Close to D4 Incinerate feel; fire stream with visual burn effect. |

## Group 5: AURA (8 kits)
*d2-auradin, d4-auradin-paladin, hot-cleric-radiant, le-fire-aura-spellblade, poe1-aurabot, poe1-deaths-oath, poe1-righteous-fire, vs-soul-eater*

### 1. Play-feel vocabulary

- **"Walking nuke"** — community shorthand for Death's Oath and RF builds; the player IS the damage zone (pathofexile.com forum thread 2466591; poe-vault.com Death's Oath guide).
- **"Keep walking and everything will explode"** — verbatim title from PoE Death's Oath build thread (pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/2466591); defines the fantasy precisely.
- **"Melt"** / **"steamroll"** — PoE RF and Death's Oath community vocabulary for the smooth clear feel.
- **"Living aura"** / **"death field"** — implied vocabulary in Death's Oath community; proximity-damage identity.
- **"Ambient damage"** — implied in poe-vault.com Death's Oath guide: "chaos damage over time to enemies within a radius"; damage needs no action.
- **"Minimal engagement"** — poe-vault.com Death's Oath: "minimal active play-style."
- **"Walking buff dispenser"** — PoE Aurabot community self-identifier; party support role where the player's value is their presence, not their actions.
- **"Chaos version of Righteous Fire"** — community comparison for Death's Oath (poe-vault.com); normative reference point for the solo aura-kill archetype.
- **"Pretty explosions"** — poe-vault.com Death's Oath: "pretty explosions to smoothly clear through maps"; aesthetic appeal flagged explicitly.
- **"Passive killing"** — Vampire Survivors Soul Eater: persistent soul-drain aura needs no activation; absorbed passively.
- **"Auradin"** — D2 community name for the Paladin who weaponizes auras; naming candidate with legacy resonance.

### 2. Usage pattern

The aura archetype has almost no active combat rotation. The player walks through content and the damage field resolves enemies. For PoE RF/Death's Oath: primary action is movement steering and flask maintenance; secondary is corpse-explosion node procs (Profane Bloom for Death's Oath). For PoE Aurabot: no personal damage at all — player exists to maintain a stack of party-buff auras; in solo content the archetype effectively does not function. For LE Fire Aura Spellblade: melee attacks trigger aura pulses — hybrid activation model. For D2/D4 Auradin: aura pulses from standing proximity; no cast required.

### 3. Celebrated moments

- Walking into a dense pack and watching them dissolve without pressing an attack button — cited universally across RF, Death's Oath, Auradin communities.
- PoE Death's Oath: "Profane Bloom" node causes corpse explosions; secondary spectacle layer on top of ambient damage.
- LE Fire Aura Spellblade: aura synergy moment when melee hit triggers burst pulse.
- D2 Auradin: the moment a paladin with stacked auras enters an area and everything dies without any cast.
- VS Soul Eater: the late-game moment when the soul drain aura is large enough to kill across the full screen.

### 4. Pain points

- **Single-target weakness**: ambient DoT auras struggle on isolated high-HP bosses requiring burst output (poe-vault.com Death's Oath: "Limited end-game boss damage").
- **Gear barrier**: "not recommended for new players" (PoE RF forum thread); the aura damage output requires significant investment to function.
- **Solo viability gap for PoE Aurabot**: designed for party support; solo play strips the build of its identity.
- **Chaos resistance gating (Death's Oath)**: chaos damage dealt to self requires chaos resistance investment; significant barrier (pathofexile.com forum thread 2466591).
- **Endgame scaling**: "heavy investment needed for good single target" (Death's Oath community, forum thread).
- **"Minimal active play"**: a pain point for players who want direct feedback; the archetype can feel like you're a spectator.

### 5. Cross-franchise feel drift

| Franchise | Dominant feel shift |
|---|---|
| PoE Righteous Fire | Fire aura burns self and enemies; survivability built from life regen exceeding self-damage; the "nuclear reactor" self-burning identity adds risk that other auras lack. |
| PoE Death's Oath | Chaos variant; "walking explosion" via corpse-explosion ascendancy; more visual spectacle per kill. Less risk-to-self than RF. |
| PoE Aurabot | Zero personal damage; pure party-buffing presence; the most extreme form of the archetype. Only meaningful in multiplayer. |
| D2 Auradin | Static aura zones; paladin stands and enemies approach the aura radius; less movement-as-weapon, more territory-control. |
| D4 Auradin Paladin | Modernized version; aura stacking with active synergies; more hybrid feel than pure passive. |
| LE Fire Aura Spellblade | Melee-triggered aura pulse; hybrid active/passive — closest to a "trigger" model within the group. |
| VS Soul Eater | Fully passive persistent aura; no player input at all; most extreme form of "proximity kills." |
| HoT Cleric Radiant | Holy-zone aura; aesthetic register (divine light) differs from necromantic/fire variants. |

## Group 6: MINION-PET (7 kits)
*chr-demon-legion-warlock, chr-pet-warden, di-minion-necro, tl2-bot-engineer, tl2-shadowling-outlander, tli-moto-bots, tq-petmaster-summoner*

### 1. Play-feel vocabulary

- **"Commander"** / **"general"** — consistent across D4, LE, PoE minion communities; player role framing (maxroll.gg D4 Minion Necro, icy-veins.com LE Necro).
- **"Send in the boys!"** — D4 Minion Necro guide (maxroll.gg); captures the directive-to-force model.
- **"Casual gameplay"** / **"hands-off"** — D4 guide lists "Casual Gameplay" as primary pro; icy-veins.com LE describes "hands-off playstyle" and "Lazy Necromancer" (icy-veins.com LE Necromancer Minion Army guide).
- **"Army of the dead"** / **"undead army"** — community vocabulary for the necromancer variant (maxroll.gg D4: "commanding an undead army").
- **"Meatshield"** — Chronicon community (steam discussions): "wolf caster... large summon army as meatshield."
- **"Get out of jail free card"** — D4 guide (maxroll.gg) for Blood Mist skill; the player's personal escape — minions handle offense while player manages crisis.
- **"Proxy"** — implicit in coordinate system; minions AS the player's damage proxy; player stays back.
- **"Fire and forget"** — summoning loop in LE (icy-veins.com): auto-summon rings create fire-and-forget cycle.
- **"Re-summon friction"** / **"resummon your mages"** — pain language (maxroll.gg D4): "simply resummon your mages as the timer expires."
- **"Satisfying experience"** — icy-veins.com LE: aspirational but conditional on gear.
- **"Swarm"** — D4 guide vocabulary: "a relentless swarm of Skeletons, Mages, and Golems."

### 2. Usage pattern

Player summoning loop: summon full army, position behind or alongside minions, direct via command skill (D4), maintain buffs. Minions handle all primary damage; player's active role is positioning (staying out of danger), re-summoning expired units, and occasionally pressing command skill for damage multiplier. LE Necro: auto-summon via unique ring automates the primary loop; player focuses on positioning and survival. Chronicon: army-size scaling drives damage — larger army = stronger bone spirit; player incentivized to maximize summon count, not direct-cast frequency.

### 3. Celebrated moments

- D4: watching a full Skeleton/Mage/Golem army dissolve a boss while the Necromancer stands at a safe distance.
- LE: poison stacking cascade building to "devastating amount of stacks" — visible escalation.
- Chronicon: Wispmother set firing super bone spirits from every mage simultaneously — visual density peak.
- "Incredible gameplay experience" with casual content — D4 guide (maxroll.gg): minion army handles "hordes, pits, or casual bossing" without pressure.
- "Insanely tanky" minions maintaining frontline indefinitely — the durability satisfaction.

### 4. Pain points

- **Re-summoning friction**: D4 Mage timer expiry requires regular recasting; LE pre-auto-summon build required active summons described as "quite annoying" (icy-veins.com LE guide).
- **Minion AI dependency**: player must monitor minion count in UI ("top left corner") to detect losses (icy-veins.com LE).
- **Player fragility**: "Can be squishy" — player lacks personal defenses; minions tank but player is soft (maxroll.gg D4; icy-veins.com LE).
- **Army reset on death**: "Minions do not typically die unless you do" — player death = army rebuild from scratch (maxroll.gg D4).
- **Gear lock**: "final version is very gear specific" — passive play fantasy collapses at low investment (maxroll.gg D4).
- **Minion targeting**: AI pathing failures are a recurring implicit complaint in all minion communities (not always explicitly stated but present in guide workaround language).

### 5. Cross-franchise feel drift

| Franchise | Dominant feel shift |
|---|---|
| D4 Minion Necro | Richest command layer; Command skill adds active multiplier; Golem as taunt/utility; most directive of the group. |
| LE Necro (Minion Army) | Auto-summon automation via ring; most passive execution of all variants; feel approaches "walking with companions." |
| DI Minion Necro | Mobile ARPG constraints; more active dodge requirement for player while minions fight. |
| Chronicon Demon Legion / Pet Warden | Army-size-as-damage-scalar unusual mechanic; more incentive to accumulate minion count than manage individual units. |
| TL2 Bot Engineer | Technology framing; bots are machines, not undead; "engineer deploying units" identity closer to totem-manager than necromancer. Overlaps with TOTEM-SENTRY border. |
| TL2 Shadowling Outlander | Shadow/assassin flavor on minions; temporary-summon loop is faster-cycle than necromancer; more fragile army. |
| TQ Pet Master Summoner | Classical summoner; diverse pet roster rather than homogeneous army; "bestmaster" identity rather than death-commander. |
