## BOUNDARY JUDGMENT CALLS

1. **Artifact scope:** The corpus commission uses "kit surface" language for MCD. The classless architecture means artifacts are the active-skill layer — equivalent to skill slots in classed ARPGs. All ~44 artifacts included as kit rows. Elrond may choose to scope-limit on curation; the full surface is presented for that decision.

2. **Twisting Vine Bow:** Listed as unique on category page but individual wiki page not fetched (would have been the base variant if it exists). Included as kit row with minimal data; Elrond may want a follow-up fetch to confirm intrinsic.

3. **Seasonal/event items:** Included (Haunted Bow, Sugar Rush, Webbed Bow, Shrieking Crossbow, Gloopy Bow). These are now Tower-obtainable and are legitimate kit entries. Noted provenance in mech_note.

4. **Wind Bow (base/common rarity):** Has the gale-arrow pull mechanic but is not unique-rarity. NOT included as a kit row per unique-weapon-grain ruling. Burst Gale Bow and Echo of the Valley are the unique variants of that weapon family and ARE included.

5. **Fishing Rod (artifact):** Name implies pull but confirmed mechanic is stun, not positional displacement. pull_pending_vocab = false.

6. **Gravity enchant (non-intrinsic stacks):** Not catalogued as separate kit rows per kit-boundary ruling (Hades-boon precedent). The 6 pull-primary kits above all carry Gravity I as an *intrinsic* property of that specific weapon. The player-applied Gravity enchant on other weapons = assembly, not kit.
