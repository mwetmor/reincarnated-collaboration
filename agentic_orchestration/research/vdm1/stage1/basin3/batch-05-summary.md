"Void" refers to a runeword. Possible origins for this spec entry: (a) community nickname conflating the Void runeword + Abyss build; (b) a minor patch-variant skill not documented in main guides; (c) spec fabrication error. Recommend steward investigate before elrond processes negative_canon verdict.

Maxroll's "Summoner Warlock" uses Goatman+Defiler as primaries, not Tainted. The Tainted summon appears primarily in the Blood Boil Warlock build. The spec folk name may conflate two distinct build architectures.

Honest-U filed; steward may want to investigate whether this token was spec-entered intentionally or is a carry-forward error from ww-barb's long era history.
