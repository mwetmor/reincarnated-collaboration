## Contradictions (2)

1. **hades1-aspect-guan-yu / mechanics** — corpus claims "lifesteal special" as a core skill. CONTRADICTED: multiple sources confirm the lifesteal is on the **Spin Attack (charged attack/Attack verb)**, not the Special. The Special is a separate explosive throw ("Crackling Skewer" per one source). Verbatim anchor: "Frost Fair Blade Alternate Move Set; Heavy Attack, explosive Special, Life-stealing Spin Attack." The corpus mislabels the lifesteal verb as Special when it is the Attack.

2. **hades1-beowulf-cast / mechanics** — corpus claims "bloodstone lodges in enemy on hit; retrieve by bashing the enemy" and names "Igneus Eden" as a core skill. CONTRADICTED on two counts: (a) bloodstones do NOT lodge in enemies with Beowulf — they load into the shield and fire off at the end of the Dragon Rush (Bull Rush) as a radial AoE burst; (b) "Igneus Eden" is the Aspect of Lucifer on the Adamant Rail (gun), not any Shield of Chaos mechanic. Verbatim anchor: "bloodstones never (afaik) lodge into enemies, and they fire off alongside your bull rush."
