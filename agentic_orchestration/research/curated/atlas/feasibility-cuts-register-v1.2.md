## 1a. The `pull` function level (register definition — spec §10.1)

**Definition.** `pull` = **inward displacement force — knockback's inverse.** Ontology (taxonomy line `3f20f738`): the distinction is **volition vs displacement** — taunt moves the *mind*, pull moves the *body*. So `pull ≠ taunt`, and `pull ≠ knockback` (opposite force direction).

**Boundary rule (the collapse this lattice refuses).** The level is defined by force **DIRECTION**, not density **OUTCOME**. A mechanic that throws enemies OUTWARD to a convergence point and thereby achieves pull-equivalent density (D3 zBarb's Ancient Spear **Rage Flip** — throws targets *behind* the Barbarian) is **knockback, not pull** — outward direction governs, regardless of the clustering it produces. Conversely, an in-engine "counts as Knockback" tag on an inward-drawing skill (D3 Wizard **Black Hole**, for Strongarm-Bracers proc purposes) does **not** make it knockback here — the register level is force-direction-defined, and Black Hole draws inward. Black Hole is precisely the genre precedent for the collapse our lattice refuses.

**Two anchor flavors — ANNOTATION, not levels** (no evidence pressure for a grain split):
- **pull-to-self** — enemies drawn to the caster's location (wind-helix vortex bows; DI Cyclone Strike; LA Vortex Gravity).
- **pull-to-point** — enemies drawn to a placed anchor (gravity-impact weapons; DMO vacuum; D3 Black Hole; LA Gravity Force along an axis).

Both flavors carry the same function level. They are recorded as a `pull-anchor:` annotation on any kit that keys `function=pull`, never as a second function value.

**Ecology-sensitive annotation (MANDATORY — spec §10.1.2).** Pull is **balance-hot**: S-tier only where a density-scaling damage economy × cheap fire meet. The genre's *containment grammar* is the strongest datum on why it is not omnipresent — cite verbatim from the legolas probe + pull-intrinsic tranche:
- **PoE's DELIBERATE ABSENCE of player-owned live-enemy pull** (GGG has actively avoided it by design; Void Sphere is corpse-vacuum on-death only, 10 s CD, 1 charge) — **the strongest single datum**: a top-tier genre studio treats intrinsic live-enemy pull as a thing to withhold.
- **zBarb 40 % CC-resistance trigger** on all pulled targets (Ground Stomp Wrenching Smash, 24 y radial-nova) — the genre's repeat-pull tax; elite/Rift-Guardian/large-monster immunity; Waller blocks.
- **D3 Black Hole 12 s cooldown floor** + "underpowered vs cooldown, solo utility" community read — the cooldown-floor containment on a placed inward pull.
- Further grammar cited in the tranche: elite immunity, class confinement (Destroyer's gravity identity), boss-centric raid formats that blunt modest pull radii.

The level is admitted as **design ground, flagged balance-hot** — the register enumerates it; the balance loop prices it.

**Evidence bar — INTRINSIC ONLY (spec §10.1.3).** A pull cell lights from **skill / rune / talent / class-level** evidence only. **Gear-assembled pull is rider-proc / assembly lineage** (four-probe test) — documented, never kit-grained, never level evidence: Ranslor's Folly bracers (DMO auto-pull proc), Empire's Grasp-class knockback→pull reversals (item modifier), D3 Raiment/In-geom teleport-pull sets, MCD Gravity-enchant economy (classless-gear architecture — ALL MCD pull is gear-assembled), DI Cyclone Strike essence variants (Legendary-chest overlays), Hades Poseidon "Rip Current" (boon-assembled), Ess of Johan / Strongarm Bracers (amulet/bracer procs). The 8-row exclusions table in the tranche is the assembly-lineage record.

**Hybrid bar interaction (deferred rows).** The tranche proposed `treatment=hybrid` on two rows (`la-destroyer-vortex-gravity`, `di-cyclone-strike-monk-base`). Under the four-probe deletion test (`hybrid-assignment-criteria-2026-07-15.md` §4) **both resolve to damage-primary + pull rider** (P2 degraded-but-operable + P3 configurable / CONFIGURABLE-OUT). The active corpus therefore stays **hybrid-EMPTY** — the T5 frontier intact, honestly.
