### WARN-E2c-1 · Row 5's dispatch hard-codes the player-stream lane, but the summon lane reaches it too (#86, A-3) → before E3

## § 4 · (c) Are WARN-1 and WARN-2 discharged?

### BLOCK-1 (scoped to E3) · The v2 fingerprint is blind to a `functools.wraps` substitute (#75 cl. 6)

`fingerprint()` does `base = getattr(fn, "__wrapped__", fn)` and then fingerprints `base`. A substitute built with `@functools.wraps(sealed)`:
- carries `__wrapped__ = sealed`;
- copies `__module__` and `__qualname__`;
- **is a plain `function` of the same type name.**

So its fingerprint **is the sealed function's.**

**Demonstrated at this Gate.** A stdlib probe loads the witness v2 file as a library, then installs on the **sealed** `threat.mitigate` a `wraps` delegate that halves `applied`:

> fingerprint equal to sealed: **True** · behaviour equal: **False**

`functools.wraps` is the **idiomatic way to write a delegating substitute.** It is the single most likely shape for a dispatch-then-delegate binder row, or for any future wrapper. At the GD profile Limb A cannot see such a leak either. **This is the defect class WARN-1 was written to close, reopened by the remedy's own unwrap step** (a remedy does not inherit its predecessor's instrument; #75 cl. 6).

**Fix:**
- Unwrap **only** when `fn` is not a plain function or method (for example `functools._lru_cache_wrapper`, which has no `__code__`).
- Otherwise fingerprint the **outer** object, and record `"has___wrapped__": bool` as part of the fingerprint. Sealed `kc2` functions carry no `__wrapped__`, apart from the lru_cache loaders.
- Where `__wrapped__` exists, also fingerprint the inner object under a sub-key.

**Add nc8h, predicted first:** a `functools.wraps` delegate on `threat.mitigate` that changes nothing → census differs exactly `['threat.mitigate']` (`has___wrapped__` True vs False), path GREEN, witness fingerprint hit on the HEAD pid only.

**E3 may not land before this is fixed and nc8h is RED.**
