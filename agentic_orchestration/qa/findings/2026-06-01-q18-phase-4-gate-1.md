## § 4.1 Methodology-lock fidelity to elrond PG-0 § 5 (CRITICAL) — PASS

### Cluster method (§ 3.2)

Dispatch § 3.2 specifies: substrate-type clusters first; HDBSCAN for keyword-embedding clusters ONLY where candidate count ≥ 8; low-yield primaries (wind especially) may not have enough candidates for keyword clustering. This matches PG-0 § 5 paragraph 1 verbatim. The wind-specific callout (per gandalf PG-1 § 2 override surface 1) is present. PASS.

### Frequency weighting (§ 3.3)

Dispatch § 3.3 specifies: weight per candidate = `sum(recognizability_score) across all citations` — NOT row count; Phase 1 + Phase 3 rows COMBINE on `(track, primary, candidate)` for citation summing. Matches PG-0 § 5 paragraph 2 verbatim, including the note that catches "one row with 5 citations vs five rows with 1 citation each." PASS.

### Contamination matrix construction (§ 3.4)

Dispatch § 3.4 specifies: symmetric pair count; Primary-A↔Primary-B cell = count of candidates with both A and B in their `cross_primary_contamination` set; diagonal cells = 0 by construction. Matches PG-0 § 5 paragraph 3 verbatim. PASS.

### Cardinality floor recommendation rule (§ 3.5)

Dispatch § 3.5 specifies: floor = count of candidates with citation-weighted score ≥ T; T calibrated against existing pool entries that survived Matt's d1_status filter; T locked at this dispatch's authoring time; NOT re-calibrated during analysis. Matches PG-0 § 5 paragraph 4 verbatim. The substrate-led calibration anchor (existing pool at `~/Games/reincarnated-engine/data/seasonal_elements/pool.json`) is named. PASS.

**INFO 1 — T numeric value not pre-stated in dispatch (minor)**

Dispatch § 3.5 says "T locked at this dispatch's authoring time" but does not provide the numeric value of T in the dispatch text. PG-0 § 5 paragraph 4 specifies T is "calibrated against existing pool entries" — calibration is elrond's seam-authority analytical work. The dispatch correctly delegates T-computation to elrond (who owns the calibration against the existing pool) and locks its direction (substrate-calibrated; not re-calibrated during analysis). Discipline #18 practical rule 3 requires "acceptance criteria defined upfront" — the acceptance criterion here is "citation-weighted score ≥ T (substrate-calibrated)"; the actual T-value is part of elrond's analysis output per PG-0 § 5 design, which names T as "calibrated" rather than pre-fixed. This is not a methodology-lock violation because T is not an arbitrary parameter — it is substrate-derived by the analyst performing the calibration. The dispatch correctly constrains the calibration procedure without pre-imposing T, which would actually violate substrate-led discipline (#41) by asserting a threshold before substrate evidence is measured. Noting for the record. No action.

### Acceptance criteria upfront (§ 3.6)

Dispatch § 3.6 specifies: variance threshold on bootstrap-stability of cluster assignments; minimum agreement across 3 tracks for "high-confidence" candidate classification (at least 2-of-3 tracks + citation-weighted score ≥ T); explicit per-primary confidence-degradation naming for sparse-yield primaries; borderline candidate audit (lux, celestial, others). Matches PG-0 § 5 paragraph 5 + Discipline #18 practical rule 3 scope. All four criteria are concretely checkable pre-output. PASS.

**Methodology-lock fidelity overall: PASS (5 of 5).**
