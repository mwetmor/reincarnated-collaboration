The fold is **sound on all three questions raised, and the gate is threaded correctly.** Dispatch 3 (telegraph combat-model) is correctly the *single binding upstream gate* for the cert wave; dispatches 4 (star-lord export) and 5 (drax render) correctly inherit it *transitively* via their existing `Depends on` chain (4→3, 5→4) with no independent cert-gate of their own; dispatches 1 (auto-amp delete) and 2 (dodge bake) are correctly carved out as **cert-INDEPENDENT**, and — the part that makes the carve-out trustworthy — each carve-out is *justified in-line by what the dispatch touches*, not merely asserted. The RESOLVE-vs-W-F gate-phase question is correctly surfaced-not-decided and routed to gandalf/Matt, with a defensible conservative recommendation stated but not self-authorized.

## Rationale

**(a) Gate threading — SOUND (no over- or under-propagation).** Disp-3 carries the binding cross-wave gate; its WAVE DEPENDENCY block names the three Matt constraints exactly (spatial-minted §7.4; does-NOT-own-1D-deletion = W-F; gate-phase to surface). Disp-4/5 say "transitively cert-gated" and explicitly locate the gate *upstream at disp-3* ("no drax-side action — the gate lives upstream") — correct: they neither re-assert an independent gate (which would be redundant/conflicting) nor drop it (which would let them fire ungated). Disp-1/2 are NOT gated, and the justification is load-bearing per Discipline #5 (triage): disp-1 is sim-internal balance honesty touching no telegraph geometry; disp-2 mints a composition skill + a sim no-op and mints NO telegraph geometry. A cert-INDEPENDENT carve-out is only correct if the dispatch produces no spatial-minted geometry — both satisfy that. Gate is propagated to exactly the dispatches whose *output is telegraph geometry* (3, transitively 4/5) and withheld from those whose output is not (1, 2). That is the correct cut.

**(b) §7.4 provenance — CORRECT placement, CORRECT strength (not too strong).** The claim "reuse of `spatial_gauntlet/arena.py` is CONDITIONED on certified-provenance — a reusable-but-uncertified surface is not a valid source" is the temporal-axis application of the cert-wave type-wall (`CommitGradeVerdict` vs `SearchGradeEstimate`). It is placed at the math-note task-0 (the SOURCE), which is exactly where §7.1's no-drift invariant can be defeated *before the export schema ever sees the geometry* — drift-by-construction at the source. It is not too strong: a reusable-but-uncertified `arena.py` is precisely the `SearchGradeEstimate`-class hazard the cert wave exists to wall; permitting reuse on *reusability alone* would re-open the recombination-drift trap the cert wave closes. Conditioning reuse on certified-provenance is the minimum discipline consistent with §7.4, not an over-reach.

**(c) Surfacing-not-deciding — CORRECT call, and the conservative read is defensible.** The telegraph is combat-RESOLUTION geometry (danger-zone shape + wind-up + where damage lands), which is a **RESOLVE-cert property (W-C, already passed)** per the cert-wave doc §5 (RESOLVE = W-C exit; MEASURE = W-D/W-F exit). It is NOT a behavioral-identity-MEASURE property. So the conservative read — "RESOLVE-cert certifies the geometry *resolves correctly*, so W-C-clear (post the K4≥K2/M1 disposition) is the natural gate; W-F merely removes the *alternative* (1D) source" — is grounded in the cert-wave doc's own two-cert split and is defensible. This is a genuine design call (it sets the actual fire-moment), not a process call, so routing it to gandalf/Matt with a stated recommendation + HALT is the right disposition per Discipline #5 (don't self-authorize a downstream-gating design decision) and the recognition→validate→commit discipline. I do NOT read this as a hard W-F gate: nothing in §7.4 requires the *alternative source to be deleted* before the certified source can be extended — it requires only that the geometry be minted by the certified source. W-F's deletion removes the *temptation/recombination-hazard* of the 1D path, but the §7.4 invariant is satisfied the moment the source is the RESOLVE-certified spatial engine. **Caveat I'd want gamora to carry into the math-note** (not a fold — a Gate-2 watch-item): the K4≥K2/M1 movement-credit gap is a *surfaced non-pass within W-C* — confirm with gandalf/Matt that the gate-phase answer accounts for whether that specific gap touches the geometry surface the telegraph extends (if the movement-credit gap is orthogonal to danger-zone geometry, it does not block; if it touches the spatial resolution the telegraph rides, it may). That is a disposition input for the gandalf/Matt call, already implied by "W-C-clear including the K4≥K2 disposition," and I flag it only so the disposition is explicit about geometry-surface relevance, not just W-C-clear in the aggregate.
