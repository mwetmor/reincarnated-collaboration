**Reviewer:** jack-ryan
**Mode:** DESIGN-MODE (Gate-1, collaborative — gating a DISPATCH before gamora builds W1; NOT a Gate-2 of built code)

**Developer:** gamora (simulation seam) — pre-dispatch
**Principles applied:** REVIEW_PROCESS #1 (math-before-code), #3 (cross-seam impact), #5 (severity/scope honesty); Disciplines #1, #3 (seed hygiene), #11 (empirical inspection), #12 (semantic shift); ADR-004 (cross-seam MIGRATION)

## Verdict

**ENDORSE-WITH-CONCERNS.** The W1 scope is correctly bounded, genuinely independent of rocket's G1/G2, and the extension-not-fork line holds in the dispatch. The G-SOLO guard is testable as written. The seed-hygiene and empirical-question framing are correct. ONE concern is load-bearing and gamora MUST carry it into the build: the dispatch's "TWO hard-wired sites" enumeration is **factually undercounted at the line-citation level** — there are more player-hard-wired writes than the two cited, and a THIRD conceptual site (mob→target for ATTACK, not navigation) exists that W1 must consciously LEAVE to W2 rather than accidentally trip over. The undercount does not break the scope, but if gamora builds to the literal "two sites at :954 and :1662" the build will be incomplete and the byte-identical guard could mask it. The remaining concerns are advisory.

## What I found

**Focus 1 — W1 scope correctly bounded; genuinely independent of rocket G1/G2 (CONFIRMED).** W1 generalizes the hard-wired mob sites to an allegiance filter and gives proxies a position; it does NOT read the `proxies` dict that G1/G2 will emit. I confirmed the seam separation in source: the W1 work touches `_navigate_entity` (`:894-1002`) and the world-set construction (`:1668`) — neither reads emitted proxy stats. The realized-damage path (which DOES consume `damage_multiplier`/`base_hp` from gen) is explicitly fenced to W2 (dispatch §"Out of scope"). The concurrency knight-rider planned is real: W1 can build against hand-stubbed/positional proxies (exactly as the T2.3 spike did) without any gen output. **No finding against the independence claim** — it holds.

**Focus 2 — the hard-wired sites are undercounted at the citation level (THE load-bearing concern).** In my T2.2 finding I verified the player-target nav site and the binary world-set as "the two places the mob concept is hard-wired." That was correct at the CONCEPTUAL level (two KINDS of hard-wiring: navigation-target and entity-set). The W1 dispatch, however, cites them as single line locations — `_navigate_entity:954` and world set `:1662` — and that line-level framing is wrong in three ways gamora must know before building:

  - **(a) The world-set is at `:1668`, not `:1662`.** Line 1662 is `max_flanking_count = 0`; `all_entities = [self.player] + self.mobs` is at `:1668`. Minor, but a builder grepping `:1662` finds the wrong line. (The T2.2 finding and the spec both carried `:1662` — it has drifted by 6 lines, or was always approximate.)

  - **(b) `_navigate_entity` hard-codes the mob's target to `player` at FIVE points, not one.** Beyond `:954` (`melee_aggressive`/`charge_then_melee`), the same `target_x, target_y = player.x, player.y` write appears at `:972` (`cast_at_range` approach), `:986` (`hit_and_run` close), and `:989` (the else-fallback) — plus `:947` (`stationary_caster` heading faces `player`) and the `ranged_kite`/`hit_and_run` reposition math (`:960-964`, `:980-984`) which all compute relative to `player.x/y`. **Generalizing only `:954` leaves four other behaviors still walking every mob toward the player regardless of allegiance.** The correct generalization is parametric: `_navigate_entity` must take a `target` (the entity's nearest-enemy-by-allegiance) instead of the literal `player`, so ALL behavior branches re-path against the allegiance-filtered target. This is exactly the "allegiance as a clean filter, not a per-site patch" discipline the spec §1.1 / §7.3 calls for — but the dispatch's single-line citation invites a per-site patch that would be both a fork-smell AND incomplete.

  - **(c) There is a THIRD conceptual site the dispatch does not name: the mob→target for ATTACK.** `_select_skill_for_entity(mob, [self.player], elapsed)` (`:1943`) and `_compute_aoe_hits(mob, [self.player], skill, geo)` (`:1952`) hard-wire the mob's ATTACK target to `[self.player]` literally — independent of navigation. This is the spatial mirror of the nav site: even after W1 makes a mob *walk toward* an ally-proxy, the mob will still *swing at the player* because attack-target resolution feeds `[self.player]`. **This site is correctly W2's (it is the mob→proxy realized-damage/aggro cross, explicitly fenced out of W1).** The finding is NOT that W1 must do it — it is that the dispatch's "two sites" framing does not flag its existence, so gamora must consciously LEAVE `:1943`/`:1952` untouched in W1 and declare that the W1 navigation generalization is intentionally decoupled from the W2 attack-target generalization. Otherwise a builder who "finishes generalizing the mob's player assumption" could pull the attack site into W1 and silently start crossing the realized-damage cut W2 owns.

**Focus 3 — G-SOLO byte-identical guard is testable as written (CONFIRMED).** The acceptance "solo byte-identical at `proxy_bin=solo`, empty-decl-gated" is a real, checkable criterion, not a hand-wave. The mechanism: a solo kit has `proxy_decls=[]`, `_build_player_proxies()` returns `[]` (`self._proxies` empty, `:1537-1539`), so no proxy enters any positional set and the allegiance filter degenerates to the current player-vs-mobs world. gamora proves it by running a solo fixture before and after the W1 diff and diffing the `SpatialFightResult` outputs (the T2.3 spike already demonstrated the harness can do this). **One advisory sharpening:** the guard is only as strong as the fixture's COVERAGE. A solo run that exercises only `melee_aggressive` mobs would pass byte-identical even if `cast_at_range`/`ranged_kite` navigation were broken by an incomplete generalization (Focus 2b). gamora should confirm the byte-identical fixture covers mobs of MULTIPLE behaviors (so all five nav branches execute on the solo path), or the guard can pass while masking the 2b undercount. This is the seam where Focus 2 and Focus 3 interact: a thorough generalization + a multi-behavior byte-identical fixture together close the gap; either alone leaves a hole.

**Focus 4 — extension-not-fork line held in the dispatch (CONFIRMED).** The dispatch's G-EXTENSION-NOT-FORK guard states it verbatim: allegiance is a filter reusing `_navigate_entity`, no autonomous-AI fork, no new navigation engine. This is consistent with the spec §7.3 and the T2.3 spike's empirical finding that the cross wired cleanly as an extension (no new navigator needed). The Focus-2b correction REINFORCES this line rather than violating it: the right fix (parametric `target`) IS the clean-filter extension; the wrong fix (patch `:954` only, or branch per-allegiance inside each behavior) is the fork-smell. **No finding** — the dispatch holds the line; gamora must implement to it.

**Focus 5 — W2/W3 fence is correct (CONFIRMED).** The dispatch fences OUT realized damage/targetability/death (W2), calibration vs boss_with_adds/mini_boss (W3), and the encounter-model shape (gandalf's W3 ruling) explicitly and correctly. The G-CONSTRAINT note (do not entrench the `:2066`/`:2123` non-empty-population gate in a way W2 must unwind) is the right forward-carry. **One advisory:** Focus-2c (the `:1943`/`:1952` attack-target site) should be named in this fence too — it is W2's, and naming it as out-of-scope makes the W1/W2 boundary crisp and prevents the accidental-overreach I flagged.

**Focus 6 — discipline check (CONFIRMED, both).** Seed hygiene (#3): the dispatch mandates 50M+ with prior bases used through 49M. I confirm disjointness — the T2.3 spike used BASE_SEED 46,000,017 (its note records "prior bases this run: 41M-45M + 700k-40M; 46M+ disjoint"), so the 49M ceiling is consistent and 50M+ is cleanly disjoint from all prior bases including the spike's. The dispatch correctly requires gamora RECORD the base used. Empirical question (#11): the dispatch frames the navigation question ("does `_navigate_entity`'s hard-coded `player` target generalize cleanly to nearest-enemy re-pathing... verify the navigator re-paths an ally-proxy to nearest enemy without special-casing") as something gamora must EMPIRICALLY answer with evidence, NOT assume — correct. This is precisely the half the T2.3 spike left UNTESTED (spike §5.0: "the harder half of the fork question... is UNTESTED here and remains the Wave-1 empirical question"). The dispatch correctly inherits it as W1's genuine open question. #12 (semantic shift): the dispatch asks gamora to declare if allegiance shifts outcome semantics — correct carry of the spec §8 declarations.

## What gamora MUST carry into the build (load-bearing)

1. **Generalize `_navigate_entity` parametrically, not per-site.** The mob's target is hard-wired to `player` at five points inside `_navigate_entity` (`:947, :954, :972, :986, :989` plus the `:960-964`/`:980-984` reposition math). The correct W1 generalization passes the entity's allegiance-filtered nearest-enemy target INTO `_navigate_entity` so ALL behavior branches re-path against it — not a patch of `:954` alone. Patching one site leaves four behaviors walking mobs at the player regardless of allegiance, AND a per-branch allegiance conditional would be the fork-smell the guard forbids. (Discipline #11, #12; spec §1.1/§7.3.)

2. **Leave the ATTACK-target site (`:1943`/`:1952`) untouched in W1 and declare it.** `_select_skill_for_entity(mob, [self.player], ...)` and `_compute_aoe_hits(mob, [self.player], ...)` hard-wire the mob's attack target independently of navigation. This is W2's mob→proxy realized-damage/aggro cross. W1 generalizes WHERE a mob walks, NOT WHO it swings at. gamora must state in the report that the navigation generalization is intentionally decoupled from the attack-target generalization (W2), so the W1/W2 boundary stays crisp. (Spec §2 mob→proxy cross; dispatch fence + G-CONSTRAINT.)

3. **The byte-identical fixture must exercise multiple mob behaviors.** A solo byte-identical proof that runs only one nav behavior can pass while masking an incomplete generalization (concern 1). The G-SOLO guard's teeth depend on the fixture covering all five nav branches. (Discipline #11; REVIEW_PROCESS #2 smoke-gate.)

## What is advisory (not blocking the build)

- **Line-citation drift.** The world-set is at `:1668` not `:1662` (line 1662 is `max_flanking_count = 0`). The `:1662` citation appears in the spec, the T2.2 finding, AND this dispatch — it has drifted ~6 lines or was always approximate. gamora should grep for `all_entities = [self.player] + self.mobs` rather than trust the line number. Worth a one-line correction in the W2 dispatch so the drift does not propagate. Not blocking — gamora will find the right line by content.
- **Update the dispatch's "TWO hard-wired sites" wording.** Strictly there are two KINDS (navigation-target, world-set) but more line-level locations, plus a third KIND (attack-target) that is W2's. knight-rider may optionally tighten the dispatch §Scope bullet to "generalize the navigation-target hard-wiring (multiple branches in `_navigate_entity`) and the world entity-set; the mob ATTACK-target site (`:1943`/`:1952`) is W2's, leave untouched." This is the cleanest framing, but gamora can carry it via concerns 1-2 above without a dispatch edit — knight-rider's call whether to amend the text or rely on this finding accompanying the dispatch.

## What I am NOT flagging

- No BLOCK is available or appropriate in DESIGN-MODE; this gates a dispatch, not a commit. The undercount concern is load-bearing but the FIX is "build it correctly + cover the fixture," which gamora carries — it does not require reworking the dispatch's scope, only sharpening what gamora knows going in.
- The independence-from-gen claim, the extension-not-fork line, the G-SOLO testability, the W2/W3 fence, seed hygiene, and the empirical-question framing are all sound. No concern.
- The G-CONSTRAINT carry-forward (do not entrench the `:2066`/`:2123` non-empty-population gate) is correctly noted as primarily-W2 and is the right forward-discipline.
