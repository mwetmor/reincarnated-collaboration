## § 3 · Is the A-12 exclusion ENFORCED, or only unobserved?

**Engine: enforced on three layers, two of them structural.**
1. **The loader refuses** any config but `"JOIN"`. I checked at this Gate: `ORACLE`, `PLAY`, `join` and `None` were all `RulebookPackRefused`; `JOIN` loads 31. This layer is *cooperative*: it binds a caller that declares its config.
2. **§ 4.7 Limb B (structural).** The pack's 7 files and the loader are tracked files added after the seal (`git diff 969fbd8d..HEAD` names all 8). Any ORACLE § 4.7 child that loaded or opened them would fail Limb B's "no loaded/opened engine file changed since the seal". That holds independent of anyone's honesty.
3. **The J-S8 emitter's own `foreign_reads` (structural).** The pack lives in the main checkout, outside the sealed worktree. An ORACLE-half emission cell that opened it would HALT `JOIN1-HALT reads`.

**INFO-S1-1.** Neither the § 4.7 B′ forbidden-directory list nor the witness's `FORBIDDEN_DIRS` names the pack directory or the loader. So B′ and per-pid witness records would report such a read **only indirectly**, through Limb B (in § 4.7 runs) or `foreign_reads` (in emitter cells). Add both paths to `forbidden_dirs()` and `FORBIDDEN_DIRS` (they are asserted equal by test). Add one control, predicted first: an ORACLE § 4.7 child opens `manifest.json`, giving B′ RED naming the pack. Then the exclusion is named where it is enforced. **Not blocking:** layers 2 and 3 already make a breach fail.

**Port: enforced statically today. The runtime layer is owed at D2/D3.**
- A grep of `kc2_runtime/`, `kc2_play/src/`, `project.godot` and `main.tscn` for `kc2-rulebook-pack`, `kc2rb_`, `join2_rulebook` and `join/rulebook` finds **0** hits.
- D2's P1 audit carries the same grep.
- **When the port mirror switches its operand source to the pack (drax's re-pin)**, its pack loader must carry the same JOIN-only refusal as the engine loader, with a port NC equivalent to NC-S1-d / NC-E5-2. That is part of the D2-Gate delta.
