### 1.3 J0-W1 (WARN): the legacy-path caveat. No LB call falls; the INCIDENTAL side does not support the README's reading

**The LB calls stand.** The rule asks whether a fold's presence, against `simulate_wave`'s default path, moves a classifying metric beyond threshold. I read the signature defaults:
