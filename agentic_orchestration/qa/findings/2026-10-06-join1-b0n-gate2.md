## Rationale and findings

### WARN-1 — KC-1's predicate over-reaches: 4 of its 6 moves are bucketings, not conversions (Discipline #12, #80, #86)
The decision as written in the math note is: a physical-**converted** record compiles as `physical`, and courts that are buckets are excluded. The shipped predicate (`court == 'physical' and original != 'physical'`) uses the court **as the evidence of conversion.** For GD `pierce` and `bleed` the physical court is a bucket, just as `aether → lightning` is. The rulebook JOIN-1 defaults to resists them **separately** (Physical 16 vs Pierce 80 / Bleeding 85 in the sealed `threat.RESIST_PCT`). A consumer that maps `dominant_element` to a family would move those kits' damage 64–69 points of resist. `physical?` is a hedge that gets silently resolved. **Only the referent is a conversion** (`eor_fire_to_physical_conversion_pct` = 100). The suite tests the bucket case only for non-physical courts (`(chaos, chaos-poison)`, `(aether, lightning)`). No test exists that could catch this, which is #80.
**Does not affect B0-N:** C-01's one converted record is right.
**Action:** see below, KC-1b. **Gate:** before any consumer maps `dominant_element` onto a resistance family (J2's family table at the latest), and before any of those five records is compiled by a harness of record. No pilot moves either way, so the KP-236 Matt condition stays untriggered.
