### 1.2 WARN-E3-1 and INFO-E3-1: discharged

- **The witness** records `wrapped_fp`, the full WARN-1 fingerprint of the innermost wrapped callable. That callable is found through the closures, matched by the census symbol's `__name__`. A substitute with any other name resolves to `unresolved`, which is a hit, so the rule fails safe.
- **`classify()`** compares `wrapped_fp` against the sealed census for do-not-substitute rows, and against the bind log's install-time fingerprint for substituted rows. The closure name is no longer consulted.
- **R-5 in the binder** now decides "sealed" by four tests: no `__wrapped__`, a sealed `co_filename`, `__globals__` is the home module dict, and the code equals the code compiled from disk. That closes INFO-E3-1.

### 1.3 The `47c976f7` classifier fix and the offline recheck: accepted

- **The defect is demonstrable.** The witness adds two record-level keys, `defined_on_class` and `wrapped_fp`, that no sealed-census fingerprint carries. Every class member and every emitter wrapper therefore mismatched by construction (380 / 250 hits).
- **`_norm` drops exactly those keys** from the top-level equality. `wrapped_fp` is still consumed by `classify()`, and `defined_on_class` is still checked on its own (False is a hit).

### 1.4 The two misses

#### NC-J2-2: an honest diagnosis; a finding with J3 consequence

- **What the prediction got wrong.** "Pair ROWSET-equal on all 7" assumed that the order operand moved the order **everywhere**.
- **What actually moved.** In v0, `intake_order_override` (`a3eb0e54`) moves only the arithmetic inside the substituted `physical_applied`. `IntakeFold.order` is on the do-not-substitute list, so it stays sealed. The sealed order then feeds the attribution string and the emitter's G2 stage recomputation.
- **Result.** The damage is identical. The **record of the order contradicts the order that was applied** on 26,406 packets.
- **Two sources of truth for one rule.** A JOIN emission run under this operand carries **internally inconsistent G2 telemetry**. G2 is the only instrument that sees the mitigation order (charter § 4.3).

**Ruling:**
- (a) The miss **stands as a miss.**
- (b) `intake_order_override` is labelled **CONTROL-ONLY, split-source** in `operands.py` and in the A-2 JSON. It may not be offered as a lever or a profile setting until the order has **one home**.
- (c) **J3 owes the route.** `IntakeFold.__init__` and `IntakeFold.physical` are emitter-wrapped (do-not-substitute), so the binder cannot simply set the order there. J3 needs a composition-level binding of `IntakeFold.order` whose fingerprint the classifier accepts, or an amended do-not-substitute rule, with a Gate.
- (d) **The control did its job.** It showed that the rulebook's order and the sealed order can diverge.

#### NC-J2-5b: an honest diagnosis; a design limit of the referent's cadence gate

- **The error was the prediction's model, not the build.** E1-b R-6 assumed that a kit faster than the world moves the player's hit-tick set. The sealed gate is one hit per world tick at most, so `kit_AS > world_AS` saturates silently.
- **NC-J2-5c settles the split's existence.**
  - It was registered with its prediction at `99043eed`, **after** 5b's miss and **before** its own first run, at a different operating point (kit 98 → 0.5 hits per tick).
  - It went RED where predicted: G7 bit-equal on 250/250 rows, leech per world tick lower in 25/25 cells, G3 differing in 25/25.
  - It is **admissible as a post-miss control**, labelled as such. It does **not** re-grade 5b.

**Ruling:**
- (a) The miss **stands.**
- (b) **The world/kit split (F-J2-3) is SHOWN only for `kit_AS ≤ world_AS`.**
- (c) **J3 owes the L-01 decision for kits faster than the world clock**: a multi-hit gate, a world clock that follows the fastest kit, or a declared ceiling printed on the fidelity table. The D2 Barbarian (IAS) and the Sorceress (FCR) are both candidates to exceed it. This goes on the J3→J4 owner-eye sheet as a known cost.
