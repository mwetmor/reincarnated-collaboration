### 2.2 The scoping question for MY Lane-3 bars — precise determination (THE CRUX)
**The bars themselves are UNAFFECTED.** They are genre-anchored, fit-direction, derived one layer down from the spec bands — never off the kits or the room. - **Artifact (mob→kit WR *competency-band* readings):** the **F2 0/40 and F3 0/40 "WR saturates at 1.0"** conclusions. These are *two-sided competency bands* ([0.85,0.95] / [0.60,0.80]); WR=1.0 sitting ABOVE them is precisely the dead-channel artifact. gamora proved a lived channel drops F2/F3 WR to 0.00 at dm=1.0 — so the tunable middle is real and reachable. **Your §7 caster-PASS reframe — CONFIRMING your read, with one precision:** casters PASS F1 (2/2) and F4 (2/2). I confirm this **survives** — and here is the exact reason it survives that Matt should hear: both caster passes are gated on a **KPM component (kit→mob, dead-channel-independent): F1 caster KPM 39.5 ∈ [30,60]; F4 caster KPM 71.8 ≥ 60**. **Net §7 survival for Matt's ruling:** the bars survive whole; all KPM dispositions survive; the caster-PASS reframe survives as a competency verdict; the ONE thing that must be re-measured is the **F2/F3 WR-band saturation** (was a dead-channel artifact, not a tunable "too easy") and, secondarily, the **floor-side WR/exit margins** on the passing cells (F1 WR, F4 exit).
