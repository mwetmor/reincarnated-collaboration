## What I found

The dispatch correctly identifies the decisive risk (an AoE-proxy mechanic masquerading as a coverage mechanic re-testing the dead lever) and places a hard HALT before the build. The PASS criterion reuses the locked 3a/3b/3c vocabulary and the HONEST_FAIL clause is load-bearing. The control arm (mechanic-OFF reproducing the d003f8f baseline, same seed) is valid. However: (1) the dispatch's PASS-criterion prong (b) cites a constant — `MODIFIER_FLOOR_NEAR` — that **does not exist in `balance_loop.py`**; the real constants are `MODIFIER_SEARCH_FLOOR=0.01` (`:318`) and `MODIFIER_LOW_THRESHOLD=0.30` (`:92`), and the observed floored value was `0.0719` (above the search floor, not at it) — the criterion is not yet numerically pinned and gamora must pin it in the math-note. (2) The dispatch frames the coverage mechanic as needing invention ("does the swarm encounter itself need a spike-local enrichment?") but the swarm tier **already routes through a multi-mob spatial arena** (`SCENARIO_OPEN_ARENA`, 8-mob engagement, `_run_spatial_slot` `balance_loop.py:2742`/`run_spatial_fight`) with entity positions, aggro/leash radii, and an explicit "coverage gap" concept already in `spatial_engine.py:81-82`. This is the encounter-side substrate the mechanic should live in, and the dispatch should point gamora there so she does not reinvent it as a kit-side abstraction (the AoE-proxy trap). (3) The Principle-6 "not applicable" claim is *defensible but over-flat*: the swarm path sits on top of a star-lord-owned spatial telemetry contract (`SpatialFightResult` / `FightEvent`, schema 2.12/2.14, `spatial_telemetry.py`); the round-trip is genuinely not-applicable ONLY because the convergence path falls back to `NullSpatialTelemetryWriter` (no DB write, `:2774`) AND the spike adds no field to those dataclasses — the dispatch should state the claim with that condition attached, not unconditionally.

## Rationale

- **Amendment 1 is Principle 1 / Discipline #1.2 (math-note code-citation).** A PASS criterion that cites a non-existent constant cannot be numerically pinned and is a force-pass surface — gamora cannot honestly evaluate "modifier off the floor" against a name that resolves to nothing. The criterion math-note must pin prong (b) against a real, cited constant.
- **Amendment 2 is the decisive anti-AoE-proxy guard (the check this Gate-1 exists for).** The dispatch's guard ("acts on the ENCOUNTER, not on the KIT's clear-rate") is correctly worded but ABSTRACT — and an abstract guard is a guard gamora can convince herself she cleared. The operational distinction is concrete and already in the codebase: the spatial arena gives mobs *positions*, so "coverage cost a pure-ST kit can't buy out" means **uncovered-mob pressure derived from spatial state (mob position/spread vs. the kit's reach-per-tick), not from the kit's AoE-share count.** The AoE-share lever (Rule A, 4 area skills) was already proven insufficient because it acts on kit composition; a position-derived coverage cost acts on the encounter geometry, which ST throughput cannot increase by adding skills. This is a genuinely distinct mechanism — but only if it reads spatial state. The dispatch must (a) point gamora at the existing spatial arena as the home of the mechanic, and (b) require the math-note to state the falsification test for the guard: "if this mechanic's pressure can be driven to zero by a kit adding AoE skills WITHOUT moving/repositioning, it is the dead lever — name why it cannot."
- **Amendment 3 is Principle 1 (criterion-gaming guard).** The reviewer's brief flags a degenerate pass: a coverage cost so brutal it craters swarm AND upper tiers equally, then claims "the modifier moved off the floor." This IS reachable — if the mechanic drops swarm WR far below band, the global modifier rises off 0.0719, but the upper tiers may rise only because the modifier rose, not because the kit is healthy; and swarm itself would now be sub-floor (below `TIER_FLOORS["swarm"]=0.65`). The locked criterion *already* guards this via the conjunctive 3a/3b prongs (swarm must come INTO band `[0.65,0.80]`, not merely off the ceiling) — but the dispatch's prose (line 30) says "swarm WR comes into band (off the 1.0 ceiling, within TIER_FLOORS band)" which is correct, while the Acceptance line (7) and Context (15) say only "off the 1.0 ceiling," which is the gameable phrasing. The math-note must restate prong (a) as **swarm WR ∈ [TIER_FLOORS["swarm"]=0.65, TIER_CEILINGS["swarm"]=0.80]** (both bounds, `balance_loop.py:533/540`), so a crater-everything outcome is an HONEST_FAIL, not a pass.
- **Amendment 4 is Principle 6 (cross-seam round-trip).** The round-trip is correctly not-applicable, but for a specific reason the dispatch omits: the convergence path uses `NullSpatialTelemetryWriter`. The dispatch must add the conditional ("not-applicable BECAUSE the convergence swarm slot writes through the Null telemetry writer and the spike adds no field to `SpatialFightResult`/`FightEvent`") and the explicit HALT trigger: *if the spike needs to emit a new spatial-telemetry field to measure coverage, that touches star-lord's schema 2.12/2.14 contract — HALT and flag a MIGRATION.md before proceeding.* gamora's own Prereq-B math-note (§4) already models this verify-don't-assume boundary discipline; the dispatch should hold the spike to the same bar.

No production-wiring ambiguity. CLEAR.
