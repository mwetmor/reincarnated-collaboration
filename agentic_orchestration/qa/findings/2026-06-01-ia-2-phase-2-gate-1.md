## INFO items

### INFO-1 — MODERN per-cell anchor-count derivation is WS2.P1 by-reference; gandalf receives no per-cell floor

**Observation:** The dispatch's § 2.1 MODERN rows carry WS2.P1 anchor-count targets (5-7 or 6-8 per cell) but the dispatch does not provide gandalf with the per-cell WS2.P1 audit table inline. Gandalf is directed to read WS2.P1 (`2026-06-01-modern-caster-substrate-coverage-audit.md`) via § 1 references, but the per-cell floor for MODERN is embedded in that audit's § 5.2 and § 0 table — not surface-quoted in the dispatch body. For 7 MODERN cells this creates a mild ambiguity risk: gandalf could author MODERN anchor counts against the dispatch's § 2.1 table (5-7 per cell, standardized) rather than the WS2.P1 cell-specific recommendation (lightning 8-12; fire 7-10; others 5-8).

**Impact:** low; the dispatch's standard 5-7 range is within WS2.P1's per-cell range for most cells. MODERN × lightning is the one cell where WS2.P1 recommends 8-12 — the dispatch's 6-8 for lightning is conservative but not outside audit intent. Gandalf design-judgment per LOCK C handles this autonomously.

**Recommendation:** no remediation required. Note for gandalf at authoring time: re-read WS2.P1 § 0 per-primary gap quantification table before committing MODERN anchor counts — especially MODERN × lightning (8-12 per WS2.P1) and MODERN × fire (7-10).

**Cite:** Discipline #41 (substrate-led; cell-specific audit data should anchor per-cell scope)

### INFO-2 — ANCIENT per-cell anchor targets in dispatch are slightly higher than audit § 6.3 per-cell recommendation

**Observation:** The audit § 6.3 per-cell split recommends ANCIENT.earth = 1 gandalf anchor + 1 legolas, ANCIENT.holy = 1 + 1, ANCIENT.lightning = 2 + 1, ANCIENT.wind = 2 + 1. The dispatch's § 2.1 ANCIENT column carries 3-4 for earth, 3-4 for holy, 3-4 for lightning, 4-5 for wind. These are higher than the audit's per-cell minimums. The audit's § 6.3 table is labeled "recommended per-cell Y3 split" and the audit § 6.2 aggregate range for ANCIENT is 15-24 gandalf anchors.

The dispatch figures are within the audit's aggregate ANCIENT range (15-24) and reflect KR interpreting the audit's per-primary narrative text (§ 2.4: "recommend 2-3 anchor weapons") rather than the § 6.3 tighter split recommendation. No conflict with LOCK C ~140 cap — total dispatch estimate of 67-88 gandalf anchors is within the audit's 67-88 aggregate and within the ~140 cap with legolas ~22 added (≤110 total).

**Impact:** none architecturally; within cap; within audit aggregate range. Gandalf design-judgment per LOCK C handles per-cell distribution autonomously. The dispatch correctly notes "final cell-by-cell distribution at your design judgment" (§ 2.1 closing note).

**Recommendation:** note for record only. Cite: Discipline #41.
