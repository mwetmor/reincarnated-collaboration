**ENDORSE-WITH-CONCERNS.** The spec is engine-grounded, the two load-bearing design claims are sound and (critically) **empirically backed by gamora's measured guard sweep**, and it respects all nine encounter-model constraints. I verified every cited engine line first-hand against `spatial_gauntlet/spatial_engine.py` and `t4_sim_cycling.py` — nothing is invented; the threats build on real mechanisms. ## A. Engine-grounding claims — ALL VERIFIED FIRST-HAND (with two minor citation-precision notes)

The spec's core claim is that the threats build on EXISTING substrate, filling only the skill-less-mob gap. **Confirmed.** Read first-hand, not taken on framing:

- **Mobs CAN cast damaging spatial skills with geometry + cooldown cadence — VERIFIED.** `spatial_engine.py:1933-2001` (spec cites `:1933-2000`): the mob-action phase selects a ready skill, and for `geo not in ("self","none")` resolves `hits = _compute_aoe_hits(mob, [player], skill, geo)`, then `raw_dmg = damage_multiplier × 300 × MOB_DAMAGE_SCALE; dmg = raw × (1 − armor_factor); player.hp -= dmg`. The formula matches the spec **verbatim**. Cadence = `cooldown_seconds` (`:1991-1993`). `self`/`none` heals the mob (`:1942-1944`). Positional: damage applies only if `_compute_aoe_hits` returns the player as hit. **Sound.**
- **Telegraph-mint is metadata-only with NO avoidance branch — VERIFIED, in engine comments.** `_mint_telegraph_spec` at `:744-812`; minted at `:1975-1988` (spec cites `:1968-1988`). The decisive confirmation is the engine's own comment at `:1519-1520`: *"the telegraph is ADDITIVE metadata, NOT a damage-model change; the sim damage ALWAYS applies, NO dodge/avoidance term"* and `:1969-1970`: *"adds NO avoidance branch."* The damage application (`:1951-1952`) is unconditional once `hits` is non-empty — there is no dodge roll. **Sound and load-bearing for claim D.**
- **Geometry palette + movement behaviors — VERIFIED.** `_compute_aoe_hits` (`:716-741`) dispatches `circle`/`cone`/`line`/`point` to real positional kernels (`point` = single nearest); `self`/`none`/`mixed` = no external hit. Movement behaviors `melee_aggressive`/`stationary_caster`/`charge_then_melee`/`ranged_kite`/`cast_at_range`/`hit_and_run` all present at `:906-984` (spec cites `:906-976`). The `stationary_caster` branch (`:945-948`) does not move; `ranged_kite` (`:956-967`) backs away to `preferred_range_m`. **Sound — and this is exactly what makes claim D's positional avoidance real (see D).**
- **The skill-less synthetic-mob gap — VERIFIED.** `t4_sim_cycling.py:1082`: `_synthetic_mob_dict_for_spatial` builds the mob dict with `"skills": []` and `"preferred_behavior": "melee_aggressive"`. A skill-less mob never enters the cast branch → zero damage by construction. **The gap is real; this is the one thing the design fills. Sound.**

**Minor citation-precision notes (INFO, not findings):**
1. The spec's path `spatial_engine.py` is actually `spatial_gauntlet/spatial_engine.py`. gamora's diagnostic already flagged this same imprecision (its §1). Harmless — the file is unambiguous and the line numbers resolve against it — but worth a one-token correction so the build doesn't chase a non-existent path.
2. §1 names `burst/ring/nova/wave/chain/arc` as "rarer shapes [that] exist." First-hand, `_compute_aoe_hits` (`:740-741`) falls through to `return []` for any geometry not in `{circle, cone, line, point, self, none, mixed}` — so those extra shapes do **not** currently resolve to hits. The spec correctly calls only the four load-bearing, so the design is unaffected; but the "exist as rarer shapes" aside is loosely worded (they exist as *names*, not as wired hit-kernels). If the build ever reaches for them it would be net-new engine, contradicting the recompose-first framing. Recommend tightening to "the four are the only wired hit-geometries."

## B. The two load-bearing DESIGN claims — BOTH SOUND, both empirically backed

**B1 — Boss-threat = HEAVY-per-hit + SLOW-cadence discriminates by HP/armor (makes the spread); preserves the guard. SOUND.**

The reasoning is correct and, more importantly, **gamora already measured the spread holding**. Her guard sweep (diagnostic §4.5, my prior Gate-2 §3) held HP and armor fixed and proved two viable paths at `MOB_DAMAGE_SCALE=4.0`: a slow kit (dm≤0.8) dies, a fast kit (dm≥1.6) survives-by-killing, and the bruiser survives-by-enduring at the same knob-set. That IS the homogenization guard (constraint #6) holding — kill-fast OR endure, no mandatory armor floor. The heavy-discrete-hit logic ("one slam is a large fraction of a paper kit's HP, an absorbable dent to a bruiser") is the correct mechanism for producing the ~0.6–0.8 glass / ~0.95+ bruiser spread, because the death channel `dmg = dm × 300 × scale × (1−armor)` is **linear in (1−armor)** and a paper kit's lower HP crosses zero in fewer hits. The arithmetic supports the claim.

**CONCERN B1 (the one I most want on the record — a precision gap, not a refutation):** the empirical guard sweep that proves the spread was run on a **FAST chip-stream**, not the heavy-slow profile the spec rules. The harness fixture (`scripts/...SPIKE_THROWAWAY_.py:65,78-82`) is `circle, dm=1.0, cooldown_seconds=2.0` — a **2-second cadence**, the *opposite* of "few big hits." So the measured result is "spread holds at scale=4.0 on a fast cadence," and the spec's claim "heavy-slow is what *makes* the spread; a fast chip-stream would homogenize" is **a reasoning step beyond what was measured.** The two are not in conflict — at a fixed total-DPS, concentrating damage into fewer-larger hits widens the HP-mediated outcome variance (a near-dead paper kit that would have survived a small chip can be finished by one big hit; a bruiser still absorbs it), so heavy-slow should *sharpen* the discrimination relative to the 2s sweep, not weaken it — but it is **not yet demonstrated** that heavy-slow preserves the guard rather than tipping toward a one-shot (the PoE-rip failure the spec itself names as the boundary to avoid). **Recommendation:** the spec should hand gamora an explicit acceptance test — *the heavy-slow profile must re-pass the guard sweep (fast kit survives-by-killing, slow kit of identical HP/armor dies, bruiser endures) AT the chosen per-hit×cadence, not inherit the 2s-cadence guard result.* The design is sound; the *transfer* of the guard from fast-cadence to slow-cadence is gamora's empirical burden and should be named as such, not assumed. §6 already routes "guard sweep as acceptance test" to gamora — this just sharpens it to "re-run at the ruled cadence."

**B2 — Swarm candidate (a) per-hit VARIANCE tests defense via HP, not clear-shape; respects the guard; doubles as cliff-softener. SOUND, with one buildability caveat.**

The design logic is correct and traces cleanly to the measured substrate. gamora's diagnostic §4.7 explicitly names the source: the glass death-band is sharp *because* "mob hits are FLAT (no per-hit variance)," and "a softer grade would come from per-hit variance on the mob." So variance-as-cliff-softener is gamora's own measured recommendation, not a new invention. And the "HP is the mediator" claim is right: variance makes the outcome depend on whether a high roll lands before the kit clears — a high-HP kit absorbs the spike, a paper kit doesn't — which is **defense (HP), independent of clear-shape**, exactly the axis trash should test. This respects the guard (a fast-AOE *tanky* kit still shrugs it; a fast-AOE *paper* kit can get unlucky-bursted — that is HP discriminating, not a forced floor).

**Does it reintroduce coverage-crank under a new name? NO — verified the distinction is real.** Coverage-pressure (`:815-845`, `_coverage_footprint_uncovered`) bleeds only for mobs *outside* the kit's AOE reach — so a fast-AOE kit covers+clears before bleed accrues and survives 1.000 regardless of crank (gamora §4.7; my Gate-2 carried this). Per-hit variance is a fundamentally different channel: it rides the **existing mob-cast hit** (`:1951`), which lands on the player when the player is in the footprint — it does not care whether the kit's *own* AOE covers the swarm. A fast-AOE paper kit that takes even a few mob hits before it clears can eat a high roll. So variance reaches the fast-AOE-paper case that coverage **cannot reach by construction**. The spec's claim that variance "makes DEFENSE, not clear-speed, the mediator" is the correct read of the mechanism, and it is genuinely **not** the coverage-crank failure renamed. Sound.

**CONCERN B2 (buildability caveat — for rocket/gamora, not a design defect):** the spec's framing that variance is "content poured into existing pipes" is *mostly* true but slightly overstated for this one field. First-hand: the engine HAS a `±15% per-hit variance` mechanism, but it lives on the **player-attacker** resolver path (`_apply_skill_damage` / `damage_resolver.resolve_skill`, `:1344`, threaded via `_resolver_rng_dmgvar` at `:1877`). The **mob→player death channel** (`:1946-1952`) is a *separate, simpler* branch that computes flat `raw_dmg` and does **not** call the resolver or any variance roll. So adding per-hit variance to the mob path is a **small new wiring addition to the mob-cast branch** (reuse the existing variance machinery, point it at the mob damage line) — not literally zero-new-code. This is well-precedented and trivial, and the spec does acknowledge "mob hits are currently flat... give mob damage variance," so the intent is honest — but the §1/§6 "nothing here is a new engine system; content poured into existing pipes" framing should carry a one-line honest exception: *the mob-path variance field is a small wiring addition (the variance primitive exists on the player path; it is not yet wired to the mob death channel).* This is a Discipline #11 precision point, not a blocker.

## D. The recompose-first avoidance ruling (§5) — SOUND. Positional avoidance is genuinely reusable; no hidden new model required.

Verified first-hand that "competent kit reads the slam" does **not** secretly require a dodge model that doesn't exist. The mechanism is purely positional and already complete:

- A `stationary_caster` (`:945-948`) sets heading but **does not move** → it stays in the footprint when the slam fires → `_compute_aoe_hits` returns it as hit → it eats the damage. A `ranged_kite` (`:956-967`) actively backs away to `preferred_range_m` → it can be *outside* the footprint when the slam fires → `_compute_aoe_hits` returns no hit → no damage. The avoidance is an **emergent consequence of position vs footprint at fire-instant**, computed by the existing `_compute_aoe_hits`, with **no dodge roll, no reaction stat, no new branch.** The engine comment at `:1519-1520` ("NO dodge/avoidance term") confirms there is no hidden avoidance term to collide with — avoidance is *only* positional today, which is exactly what the spec rules.
- So the §5 claim "avoidance reads positionally through existing movement behaviors and `_compute_aoe_hits`" is **literally true at the code level**, not aspirational. The defensive axis-set (HP / armor / clear-speed / kill-speed / positioning, NOT twitch-dodge) is the correct and *only currently buildable* axis-set. Sound.
- Naming explicit telegraph-reaction as a future fork (not this wave) is correct and disciplined — it would be net-new engine (a reaction stat reading `telegraph_buffer` and branching damage application), and the spec correctly keeps it out. This is good Discipline #12/#13 hygiene (the design names the deferred lever rather than letting it drift in).

**One honest nuance (INFO, not a concern):** positional avoidance being purely emergent means the boss-threat's "readability" is **entirely a function of the kit's movement behavior assignment**, not of telegraph wind-up. A `stationary_caster` glass kit cannot "read and dodge" the slam no matter how long the telegraph — it eats every slam by construction. That is *genre-true and design-intended* (the spec says exactly this: "the greedy kill-racer takes real risk"), but it means the boss-threat tests **movement-behavior choice** as much as HP/armor/kill-speed. The spec's player-consequence framing (§7) is consistent with this, so no change needed — just noting that "positioning" in the restored axis-set is, mechanically, "which movement behavior the kit carries," a discrete kit attribute, not a continuous skill expression. The design is honest about this; I flag it only so the build and any downstream population-design knows positioning is a *kit-shape* lever, not a *play-skill* lever, on this wave.

## Rationale

The spec clears Review Principles #1/#3/#4 and Disciplines #11/#12/#13. Every engine claim was re-derived first-hand from source, not taken on the spec's framing (Discipline #11). The two load-bearing design claims are not only logically sound but trace to gamora's *measured* substrate (the guard sweep for B1's spread; the §4.7 flat-hit caveat for B2's variance) — the design is built on evidence, not vibes. The recompose-first avoidance ruling is verified true at the code level. ENDORSE-WITH-CONCERNS rather than clean ENDORSE because of three build-side caveats that do not change the design intent but must be carried into the seam work: (B1) the guard must be re-proven at the ruled heavy-slow cadence, not inherited from the measured 2s-cadence sweep; (B2) the mob-path variance field is a small wiring addition, not literally zero-new-code, and the "poured into existing pipes" framing should carry that one honest exception; (C9) the spec under-restates the full-population validation burden to gamora.
