## The two rulings I was asked to make (called out explicitly)

### Q4 RULING — Gate-B successor inheritance → **Finding F-1 STANDS UNREPLACED at this N.** A per-law Gate-B successor is NOT powered; do NOT introduce it in Leg B.

This is a QA / power-calc judgment and it is my call. The ruling:

**F-1 ("kit death is not geography") remains the Gate-B position for Edition-V, carried verbatim and NON-DOWNGRADABLE (RIDER-1 §2, inherited).** The CLARIFY-1 powered per-law corpse minimum is **NOT cleared** at v2.0, for three independent reasons:

1. **The per-law N is structurally too small.** CLARIFY-1 requires a *per-law* locality test (one of the 3 distinct red laws at a time), not an aggregate intrinsic-red test. DB truth: `intrinsic-red` = 12 total, but only **8 are record-class** (the deriving set; the other 4 — `d4-incinerate`, `tq-calculated-strike`, `tq-flame-surge`, `vs-gatti-amari` — are annex and project supplementary, they do not derive and are the weaker evidence for a locality claim about the basis's own geometry). Eight record-class corpses split across three red laws is ≈2–3 corpses per law. A permutation locality test on k=2–3 points has no meaningful power — the null and the observed are indistinguishable at any conventional α, and a single point's assignment flips the verdict. This is the *same* small-N hazard I flagged for AURA/MINION-PET silhouette in Run A (A3), one order worse.

2. **The red-law assignment is itself an unfrozen, post-hoc-constructible label.** The 3-red-law split is doc-only (commit 1bf4c2c9); it is not a DB column. Assigning each of the 8–12 corpses to a red law is a judgment; if made after the analyst has seen the E5 embeddings it is retro-fittable — the exact A1-class defect. To even *attempt* a powered per-law test, the corpse→red-law table would have to be frozen as an artifact BEFORE derivation, and even then (reason 1) it would be underpowered.

3. **CLARIFY-1 places the burden on the prereg, and the prereg has not discharged it.** CLARIFY-1 (gate-B diagnosis §4 R3, binding): *"the v2 prereg must state the minimum per-law corpse count that makes a per-law locality test powered, with the power calculation behind it, AND the census-growth trigger."* The prereg §4 Gate-B row does not state a minimum or a power calc — it *defers both to me*. That deferral is itself the CLARIFY-1 non-compliance. The honest disposition is not "jack-ryan back-fills the power calc at Gate-1"; it is "the census has not grown enough to power the successor, so F-1 stands, and the census-growth trigger is re-stated for a *future* edition." (For orientation: even the aggregate intrinsic-red set is only 12; Run A already showed at N=5 that these corpses are genuinely *dispersed*, p_dispersed 0.036 — nothing in the v2.0 growth suggests per-law concentration would emerge, and a per-law slice cannot test it at N≈2–3.)

**Amendment A-LB4 makes this the prereg's stated position.** F-1 stands; the Gate-B successor is deferred with its census-growth trigger re-pinned; the freeze criterion for Edition-V is A/C/D + F-1, exactly as Edition-I. This is the RIDER-1-honoring outcome: F-1 is not quietly converted, it is affirmed, and no underpowered gate is invented post-hoc to replace it.

### Q5 RULING — §6 scaling-curve → **registering-as-ASSUMPTION (not gating) is CORRECT; it BELONGS in the Edition changelog as a stated precondition.**

The §1.6 root-cause (exponential-monster-HP over a fixed push-interval structurally forces every stat to multiply damage → the court×register two-axis structure re-collapses into pure damage-multiplication regardless of derivation quality) is **not testable in the catalogue.** The catalogue is a static census of skill-kit *geometry*; the scaling curve is an endgame *progression/economy* property that lives in the battle-sim + itemization layer, not in any column the MCA consumes. A gate can only test what the analyzed data can falsify. Gating on an untestable precondition would either (a) be vacuous (nothing in the data can fail it) or (b) force the analyst to import an external judgment mid-run, which is precisely the retro-fittability the prereg exists to prevent. So **registering it as a named prereg ASSUMPTION is the correct disposition** — it is the honest form: "the E5 axes presuppose a non-collapsing scaling curve; this run does not and cannot verify that; downstream viability is conditional on it."

**It belongs in the Edition-V changelog as a stated precondition (A-LB7).** This is the RIDER-1 discipline applied to a *positive* structural claim: if E5 ships a court×register two-axis basis, and later the endgame curve is found parabolic-over-fixed-interval, the Edition must not be able to claim the two-axis structure was ever *sufficient*. Recording it now as a disclosed precondition ("the two-axis design is necessary but not sufficient; the scaling curve can silently defeat it") makes the flattening risk predicted-and-disclosed rather than discovered-later — the same posture that made F-1 and P-E4-6 honest. And routing it to a progression/keystone-design conversation (not this run) is correct scope discipline: it is adjacent to Edition derivation, owned by progression design, and does not gate the derivation. My eye on it: yes, put the one-sentence precondition in the changelog, cross-referenced to the Gear-fold §1.6 digest; do not let it become a gate, and do not let it silently drop.
