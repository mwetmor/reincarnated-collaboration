## The load-bearing adjudication — is (a)+(c)=GATE a legitimate reading of oracle §6.2 cond.4?

**Reading the acceptance authority verbatim.** Oracle §6.2 cond.4 (lines 262-263) states the spatial engine "measures kit identity correctly" when:

> "The complete axis surface is computed from spatial telemetry — every axis per the Bucket-B coverage ruling (§6.3) is assigned from the spatial run (replacing the placeholder `bc_cell`) and **wired, not default-valued** (the Bucket-A check, on the spatial seam); the result is minted as a `CommitGradeVerdict`."

**The oracle's cond.4 text requires exactly two things: (a) wired-not-default (Bucket-A) + (c) CommitGradeVerdict mint.** It does NOT require the discriminating bins (b) to land, and it does NOT contain the M1 ablation (d). The ablation lives in oracle §5.2 as a **separately-tracked K4 ⚠B *mechanism-half* obligation** that "MEASURE/W-D inherits" (§5.2 line 237) — it is a W-D-scope item, but it is NOT written into the cond.4 exit-gate text. The dispatch confirms this division: cond.4 acceptance (dispatch line 8) = "wired, not default-valued ... minted as a CommitGradeVerdict"; the M1 ablation is a separate scope bullet (dispatch line 58) carrying its own relief-valve ("STOP and surface ... do not half-build").

**Therefore: gamora's "(a)+(c) = the GATE" reading IS a legitimate interpretation of the oracle.** The oracle's cond.4 is genuinely a wiring + mint gate, not a discrimination gate. gamora's math-note §8 pre-registered a STRICTER four-part criterion than the oracle requires — §8 folded (b) measured-bins and (d) the ablation INTO cond.4 ("MEASURE cond. 4 PASSES iff ALL FOUR hold"). When the stricter self-imposed criterion did not hold, gamora fell back to the oracle's actual (looser) text. **Falling back from a self-imposed stricter bar to the acceptance authority's actual bar is not a goalpost-move — it is the correct precedence (the oracle is the acceptance authority, not the developer's own math note).** This is the engine > game > phase / acceptance-authority-governs ordering working as designed.

**Where the move is imperfect (the WARN basis):** Discipline #2 (pre-registration) exists precisely so a developer cannot quietly relax a bar after seeing the result. gamora's §8 said "iff ALL FOUR"; the result relaxed to two; and the §10 narrative reframes (b)/(d) as findings — but the math note does NOT carry an explicit "§8 was stricter than the oracle; I am reverting to the oracle's actual cond.4 text" reconciliation line. The reader must reconstruct that the oracle is looser than §8 by reading both docs side-by-side (which I did). More sharply: `wd-six-axis-measure-2026-06-13.json` emits `measure_cond4_pass: true` as a sibling key to `prereg_pass: false` and `ablation_pass: false` — the harness encodes the (a)+(c) framing as machine-truth without a co-located note that the prereg/ablation falses are *expected-and-explained against the oracle*, not silent failures. A future agent recombining this JSON (the exact 14-agent recombination risk the type-wall exists to guard) could read `prereg_pass: false` as a regression. **The reframe is substantively right; its paper-trail discipline is thin.**

**Adjudication verdict: LEGITIMATE interpretation, not a pre-registration violation — BUT the §8→oracle relaxation must be made explicit, and the JSON's `measure_cond4_pass: true / prereg_pass: false` co-occurrence annotated.** This is a documentation-completeness fix, not a re-run.

This is the relief-valve outcome the oracle/dispatch designed for. **No concern on (d)'s disposition; only that `ablation_pass: false` in the JSON reads as a fail without the co-located "discharged-negative" annotation.** gandalf owns the oracle §5.2 amendment recording the negative result (correctly surfaced in completion record (g)).
