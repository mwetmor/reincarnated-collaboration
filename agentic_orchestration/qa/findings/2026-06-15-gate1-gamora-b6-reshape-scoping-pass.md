## What I found

**Finding 1 — the load-bearing Leg-3 discriminator is the soft criterion this pass must harden, and the dispatch under-scaffolds it (A1, BLOCKING-for-math-note).**
B's whole lesson was that the criterion definition is the decisive act. The dispatch correctly applies that to signature elements 1 and 2 (both code-cited, both reuse locked constants — the same Discipline-#12-clean defang as B's §3b). But element 3 — "what caster measurement counts as casters-collapse-too vs casters-have-a-shape-the-envelope-lacks" — is handed to gamora as an open question (Scope line, Open-questions line) with NO scaffolding, while it is simultaneously named "the decisive discriminator" and "the load-bearing definition." That asymmetry is the steer surface: an under-specified discriminator on the leg whose convenient answer ("envelope-only") is the one that keeps the problem small. The math-note must pre-register the Leg-3 discriminator with the SAME rigor as elements 1-2: a measurable, code-cited, pre-registered threshold stating, in `balance_loop.py` terms, what caster-kit per-tier-shape result reads as architectural vs envelope-specific — BEFORE any caster run. Gate-1 (the HALT) ratifies THAT definition, not a post-hoc read.

**Finding 2 — the existing probe has NO caster-kit path; "reuse / extend the existing probe" understates the Leg-3 build (A2, BLOCKING-for-math-note).**
The reused harness (`scripts/g7_hold_sim_b6_prereq_B_2026_06_15.py`) builds BOTH arms as PHYSICAL kits: `_PHYSICAL_ARCHETYPES` (`:69`) fed through `_build_structural_template(... "damage" ...)` → `ClassGenerator.generate(...)` (`:149-152`), plus the envelope composer. There is NO caster-kit construction in it. The reference gauntlet contains a `"caster"` entry (`:241`) — but that is an OPPONENT monster archetype, NOT the player kit under test. Caster PLAYER kits compose through a structurally different path (`archetype_composer.py` — the `*_caster` / `*_mage` archetypes, `mana`/`power_tier=50`, `:541`, `:845`), not the physical structural-template path. So Leg 3 is not a "reuse"; it is an EXTENSION that must (a) load or compose a caster-kit population and (b) run it through the identical single-global-modifier `balance_class()` probe so the comparison is apples-to-apples. The dispatch must say WHERE the Leg-3 caster kits come from — an existing committed caster population (which artifact / tag) or a fresh composition — because that choice is load-bearing: if the caster kits are freshly composed, their provenance becomes a confound the discriminator must control for. This is not a fatal gap; it is an unstated step on the decisive leg, and unstated steps on decisive legs are where diagnostics go soft.

**Finding 3 — Principle-6 assessment is correct for Legs 1-2 and the envelope arm, but its caster half is unverified (A2 corollary).**
"NO cross-seam contract change / round-trip not applicable" is CORRECT for a pure read-side characterization that runs the existing probe against existing envelope kits — that boundary is the one B already verified clean at `b85d038` (the shared Skill dict, no new field). I confirm the physical-side reasoning. BUT the assessment extends the same "consumes existing kits cleanly" claim to caster kits WITHOUT establishing that a caster-kit population exists at the sim boundary with the sim-required fields (`damage_scaling_type`, `scaling_attribute`, `canonical_element`, etc.) present and non-None. The precondition-assert (Scope line) names "envelope + caster kits" but the caster half rests on Finding 2 being resolved first. Principle 6's verdict (no contract change, no MIGRATION) is still correct — the probe is read-side either way — but the precondition-assert must explicitly enumerate the caster-kit field check the same way B enumerated the envelope-kit field check, not inherit it by analogy. Verify-don't-assume (Discipline #11) applies symmetrically to both arms of Leg 3.

**Finding 4 — fences are correct and complete; one clarifying addition (A3, non-blocking).**
NO balance_loop.py modification, NO reshape build, NO b6 touch, NO generation-side change, NO decisions-log/canonical write, NO push — all correct, and the "you MEASURE with the single-global-modifier design, you do not change it; whether the loop needs per-tier modifiers is precisely the question this pass FEEDS" framing is exactly right (the diagnostic is not licensed to answer its own question by editing the loop). The one addition: the fences are silent on what gamora MAY add. Finding 2 means gamora WILL need a caster-kit loader/harness extension — that is in-scope (harness-side, the same seam B's harness lived in), but the dispatch's "reuse the existing probe" language could be read as fencing it out. State explicitly: harness EXTENSION to load/run caster kits is in-scope; what stays fenced is `balance_loop.py` itself, the composers (`weapon_envelope_composer.py`, `archetype_composer.py`), and b6.
