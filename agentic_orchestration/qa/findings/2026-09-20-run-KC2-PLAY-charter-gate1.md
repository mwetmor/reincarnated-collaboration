### BLOCK-A — R2D-2's two `ppm` presets are computed at `h_fig = 2.0 m`, while the spec, the acceptance criterion itself, and the conductor's own OQ-2 ruling all fix `h_fig = 1.9 m`. No correct build can pass it.

`2d-retarget-spec.md:60-66` declares **`h_fig := 1.9 m`** normatively ("*a substrate-decided value, not a taste call*"), prints the preset table, and then works the derivation in the open so nobody re-derives it wrong:

> `ppm = (fraction · 1080) / (h_fig · cos α)`. Worked once … `86.616 / (1.9 × 0.602463) = 86.616 / 1.144680 = **71.885**`

**`86.616 / 1.144680 = 75.668`, not 71.885.** Verified (Python, α = 52.9535411256029°, cos α = 0.602462):

| preset | fraction · 1080 | `ppm` at `h_fig` **1.9** | `ppm` at `h_fig` **2.0** | spec / R2D-2 claims |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `ZOOM-GD` | 86.616 | **75.668** | **71.885** | **71.885** |
| `ZOOM-HOUSE` | 183.600 | **160.394** | **152.374** | **152.374** |

Solving the other way closes it: `86.616 / (71.885 × cos α) = **2.00000**`, and `183.6 / (152.374 × cos α) = **2.00001**`. **Both printed constants are the `h_fig = 2.0` values.** The intermediate `1.144680` is correct for 1.9 and the products are not — the signature of a value changed after the table was computed.

This is not a rounding quibble, and its magnitude is the spec's own named hazard turned on itself: `2.0 / 1.9 = **5.26 %**`, against the spec's warning that choosing 2.0 *"buys a permanent, invisible **5.3 %** figure-to-arena error … permanently baked into every T-C 'does it read at the right scale' judgment, and invisible."*

**Why it is a BLOCK at Gate-1 rather than a finding for W2.** Charter § 1 **S-4** freezes `R2D-1…n` as substrate, and **R2D-2** (`spec:386`) reads: *"`ppm` derived, not typed … with `h_fig = 1.9 m`; both F4 presets reproduce **71.885** and **152.374**."* As written, a builder who implements the formula at the ruled `h_fig` **fails the criterion**, and a builder who hits the numbers has silently used the value the spec argues against — with a 5.26 % error in the one constant that sets the whole screen scale, invisible by construction, and downstream of it every T-C scale verdict and every geometry-true probe threshold in § 4.4. The run would meet this at W2 or W3 and have to amend a frozen criterion mid-flight, which is a charter amendment and a HALT to Matt.

**Minimal discharge (conductor, no Matt needed unless he wants `h_fig` re-ruled):** a corrigendum to the P0-b spec (forward-supersession, in its own § 6-style block) that either (i) **keeps `h_fig = 1.9` and re-derives the presets to `71.885 → 75.668` and `152.374 → 160.394`**, or (ii) **re-rules `h_fig := 2.0`** and answers its own 5.3 % argument out loud.
