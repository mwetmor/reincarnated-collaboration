### 2026-05-16: Cipher-width sub-lock resolution — Outcome 2 (single classical-element-anchored grouping) + Foundation L2 + per-season vocabulary coupling β + D1 reconsideration scope conditional on Flag A

**Decision:** The four form-bias catalogue-track sub-locks deferred per Entry 3 of the 2026-05-16 form-bias batch (committed `5d51b5a`) are resolved as follows, based on elrond's emergent-grouping analysis at full substrate width:

#### Sub-lock 1 — Cipher-width: **Outcome 2 (refined-Option-A)**

Per Entry 2 of the form-bias batch's three-outcome framework, the empirical-data-determined outcome is **Outcome 2: 1-2 robust groupings survive**. The cipher collapses to a single fixed grouping; cross-season variety lives in vocabulary + anchor, not in grouping.

**Substrate width:** 4-6 tags (the genre-canonical primary-element width per Legolas Pass 4 ARPG-community discourse evidence). The grouping is the classical-element-anchored set: fire / water / earth / lightning, with optional inclusion of ice / wind / holy / dark / melee-slash as substrate-anchor candidates.

**Critical empirical finding:** the 17 novel substrate tags from Step B (death-necrotic, blood-sanguine, blood-wound, cosmic-stellar, void-spatial, void-adjacent-arcane, crystal-gem-arcane, technology-vfx, midas-transmutation, implosion-gravitational, time-temporal, warp-teleportation, chromatic-arcane, shadow-kinetic, melee-slash-kinetic, summoning-object, poison-biological) do NOT form their own coherent emergent groupings. They dissolve into a heterogeneous kinetic-mega-cluster (35-41 packs across multi-element + impact-kinetic + multi-novel-substrate) or remain as singletons. Per-substrate-supply was thin (n=1-2 per novel substrate); the substrate-evidence does NOT support multi-grouping cipher-width-expansion at current crawl breadth.

**Outcome 1 admissible under a "generous per-anchor reading"** (each classical-element substrate-anchor read as a candidate per-season-selectable slot, yielding 5 candidates → Outcome 1 nominally). The data does NOT strongly support this reading — only one cosmologically-coherent grouping emerges. If a future seasonal-rotation-as-structural-pillar goal motivates re-opening Outcome 1, a Tier-2 catalogue crawl (specialist novel-substrate vendors raising per-substrate n above 3) would need to land first.

#### Sub-lock 2 — Foundation layer placement (Flag B): **L2 (substrate decoupled to Reincarnated cosmology)**

Per Entry 2 + Entry 3 of the form-bias batch's Flag-B framework: Foundation either grows to N substrate tags (Foundation-coupled-to-substrate; L1 ownership) OR decouples (substrate becomes L2 Reincarnated-cosmology concept; Foundation stays at 4-rotating-plus-1-physical as L1 generic).

**Decision:** **L2-decoupled.** Foundation stays at 4-rotating-plus-1-physical as L1 generic (no Foundation validator change required). Novel substrates (death-necrotic, cosmic-stellar, void, crystal, etc.) live at L2 as per-season vocabulary slot fills against the L1 abstract pair structure, not as new L1 mechanical types.

**Rationale:** Per elrond's analysis, the empirical substrate width that survives viability gating (4-6 classical-element-anchored tags) matches the existing Foundation 4-rotating-plus-1-physical structure. L1-coupled-to-substrate (growing Foundation to N) would over-commit the engine to substrate categories that don't carry the empirical clustering signal needed to support them as first-class mechanical types. L2-decoupled is the lower-regret choice; per-season-vocabulary can name a death-necrotic season without Foundation knowing about death-necrotic as a mechanical concept; resistance translation lives at the substrate-L2-layer per the three-layer model (Entry 2).

#### Sub-lock 3 — Per-season vocabulary coupling policy: **β (in-prompt constraint)**

Per Entry 3 of the form-bias batch's α/β/γ framework, the operationally-sensible policy given Sub-lock 1 (Outcome 2; 4-6-substrate width) + Sub-lock 2 (L2-decoupled) is:

**Decision:** **β (in-prompt constraint).** Engine constrains LLM prompts so per-season vocabulary maps cleanly to the 4-6-substrate slot structure at generation time.

**Rationale:** Substrate width is narrow + stable enough that prompt-construction can reliably steer LLM-generated vocabulary into a clean mapping. α (validation-and-regenerate) is over-engineered for a 4-6-slot substrate — regeneration loops would be costly with little marginal benefit. γ (runtime fallback) is under-engineered — if the substrate is narrow + stable, accepting mismatch with post-hoc resolution is a Discipline #14 violation (runtime ad-hoc handling for a structurally-known constraint). β puts the engineering cost at one-time prompt design + occasional prompt iteration as new substrates surface; ongoing operational cost is zero.

**Caveat:** if a future Outcome-1-permissive reading lands (substrate width grows past 6), β's prompt-engineering cost grows. At that point reconsider α as a fallback for high-novelty seasons. The reconsideration trigger is "substrate width determined to be >6"; this entry doesn't pre-commit β beyond the Sub-lock-1-locked width.
