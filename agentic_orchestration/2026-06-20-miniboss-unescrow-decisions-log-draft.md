### 2026-06-20 (closes the boss-gate escrow): the `mini_boss` caster-wipe is ruled a stale-calibration DEFECT (not identity), the two stale constants are fixed recompose-first, the inversion is GONE, and the `mini_boss` column is BANKED

**Decision**: The `mini_boss` boss-shell column — HELD in escrow at the boss-gate ruling (`d5b7ac2`) — is closed. The two-half diagnosis (engine + design) ran to its pre-registered close criterion: a first-hand explanation of why int/wis survive `boss_with_adds` but not `mini_boss`, plus a design ruling on what `mini_boss` is FOR. Both halves agree the caster-wipe is a **stale-calibration DEFECT**, not honest texture. Matt **ratified the `mini_boss` identity as "a smaller boss"** (not a burst-window enrage check) and authorized a recompose-first fix. gamora fixed the two stale constants, re-measured, and the inversion is gone; jack-ryan Gate-2 PASS-WITH-INFO. **The `mini_boss` column is now BANKED.**

The ruling has four load-bearing parts:

- **ENGINE-half diagnosis (gamora, tag `gamora/v-miniboss-caster-diagnosis-1`; per-cell deepening same tag): the caster `mini_boss` 0.000 was 100% TIMEOUTS, 0% early deaths.** First-hand from the 2026-06-19 clean-boss harness: int|mini_boss 100.0% timeout / 0.0% death (960 fights), wis|mini_boss 100.0% timeout / 0.0% death (2400 fights); **0 of 240 caster `mini_boss` cells had any early death; total caster `a_dead` across both boss shells = 0.** Casters were alive and winning the fight — the clock killed them mid-kill (caster single-target TTK 125–175s). Two stacked stale constants: (1) `mini_boss` `soft_timeout_s = 150s` guillotine (`spatial_engine.py:1684-1696`), while `boss_with_adds` uses `None` → the 240s enrage cap; (2) `mini_boss` HP rolled ABOVE the full boss (a config inversion from a W-α7+ Phase-3c "T4 achievability" reach), so the "easier" shell was the longer grind. STR cleared (~15s raw-throughput kill) under both. 80 of 240 wis|mini_boss cells removed ≥90% of the boss and still timed out — "a hair short, the clock kills them," by-cell.
- **DESIGN-half ruling (gandalf, `2026-06-20-miniboss-design-half-ruling-what-is-miniboss-for.md`; Matt-ratified): `mini_boss` is "a smaller boss," and both constants are DEFECTS.** No canonical doc ever ratified a burst-window / DPS-race identity; the class-fantasy outcome was inverted (burst casters wiped, grind STR cleared); the "mini" naming-contract was violated (the easier-named shell was the harder grind). Both stale constants are the **same dead-absolute-constant / stale-calibration class as the four instrument-validity workstream targets** — the 150s soft_timeout was calibrated to a ~3s-TTK / KPM~60 regime and mis-applied to slow caster kills; the HP-above-boss roll is a config inversion. Neither is honest texture. **Matt ratified the "smaller boss" identity** (the one decision reserved to him) and authorized the recompose-first fix.
- **THE FIX (gamora, recompose-first; tag `gamora/v-miniboss-smaller-boss-fix-2`, engine fix `c2a8392` + re-measure `82ae9b9` + test-correction `fb3e702`, collab `da40542`): two constant re-scales, no new mechanic.** (a) `simulation/spatial_gauntlet/arena.py` — `SCENARIO_MINI_BOSS.soft_timeout_s: 150.0 → None`, aligning `mini_boss` to `boss_with_adds`' 240s hard cap (the guillotine logic is left in place but goes inert via its existing `is not None` guard). (b) `simulation/t4_sim_cycling.py` — new `_floor_miniboss_hp()` clamps the `mini_boss` kill-target HP DOWN to the boss midpoint (~231k, read live from `ENDGAME_MOB_PROFILES["boss"]`) so the smaller boss is actually ≤ the boss. Applied at the simulation consumption site; rocket's generation HP-factor range was NOT edited (flagged for the post-workstream absolute-magnitude-constant sweep instead). Math-note `simulation/math/miniboss-smaller-boss-fix-2026-06-20.md` (Discipline #1, falsifiable against the per-cell termination table).
- **BANKED — the re-measured `mini_boss` column; the inversion is GONE.** Re-measure on disjoint seed base 711000 (960 cells, 20 fights/cell): caster `mini_boss` survive-and-kill rose from 0.000 toward boss level and timeouts collapsed (100% → 32–44%): **int 0.000→0.681, wis 0.000→0.563, dex 0.667, str stays 1.000**; `a_dead` remains 0 everywhere (residual timeouts are honest near-kill 240s-window shortfalls, not guillotine artifacts). The banked `boss_with_adds` column is UNMOVED (int 0.999→1.000, wis 0.951→0.956 — within seed variance; mechanically guarded: `threat_tier="boss"`, soft_timeout already None). Caster `mini_boss` landed GRADED (not full-clear), which the math-note pre-registered as criterion-passing. No over-correction: `mini_boss` ≤ `boss_with_adds` for every archetype (the smaller-boss contract holds), STR still clears, `mini_boss` is not trivially cleared by all. The close criterion — STR no longer the only archetype clearing `mini_boss` — is met first-hand.
