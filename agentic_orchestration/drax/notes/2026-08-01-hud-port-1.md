## §0 — THE HEADLINE, AND IT IS NOT THE HUD

**The renderer is not deterministic across processes on this clip, and I can prove it by rendering
the same arm twice.**

R-BR-51 was minted as "assert seed determinism and print the assertion", and cell 3c implemented its

draws anything. That frame passed on this cell's arms too: **0 px, both pairs, max channel 0.**

Frame 5 is also the frame where *nothing else* draws anything. So I rendered the treatment arm a
second time under a second prefix, **identical flags, identical seed, separate process**, and
compared it with itself:

```

   frame    5 :       0 px over the lit bar      max channel   0
   frame   50 :       0 px                       max channel   0

   frame  150 :   1,851 px                       max channel 163
   frame  200 :     627 px                       max channel 167

   frame  300 :     192 px                       max channel  68
   frame  350 :   2,026 px                       max channel 184
   ⇒ worst-frame floor 2,305 px, against an on-frame bar of 40 px
```

**A clean frame 5 proves the LOAD PATH is deterministic and proves nothing about the clip.** The
divergence is absent at frame 50 and present by frame 100 — it begins with the first combat VFX, not
at load. It survives `--nodust 1 --noambient 1` (F-AC-1's peel), it survives `--nohud 1`, and it
survives F-SA-1's `_cj_load` fixed-seeding of 209 GPUParticles3D emitters, which every arm still
declares and passes.

⚑ **F-HP-1 — this is a THIRD non-determinism term, and it is 58× the bar.** F-SA-1 named the first
(`_cj_load`), F-AC-1 named the second (ambient dust). This is the third and it is by far the largest.
**Diagnosis, offered as a hypothesis and not as a proven cause:** `_cj_load` seeds emitters *at load*,
and the impact/cast VFX are **instanced at runtime**, per event, from the harvested packs. A node that
does not exist when the seeding pass runs cannot be seeded by it. The timing fits exactly — clean

## §1 — PASS 3 IS TAKEN AND SHIPPED. `no_depth_test` IS NOT TAKEN.

F-AC-3, authorised by Addendum 17: **the arc is sized off the fight's own contact distance.**

The old sizing came from `_sa_hand_mount`, which measures the two hand bones' horizontal offset in the
rig's frame **and floors it at 0.55 m** — and on this werewolf the floor is what binds, so the arc's
entire extent was an invented constant wearing a skeleton's name. The trace publishes, for every
damage event, where the struck body actually was. Verified on this fight's 21 cone events, and the
renderer now prints it before it draws anything:

```
[sa] F-AC-3 CONTACT SIZING: player windows n=20 · contact min 1.00 / median 2.00 / max 3.22 m
     · band 0.30x..1.00x
```

Exactly the charter's min 1.00 / median 2.00 / max 3.22. **The outer edge of the arc is now the
contact distance itself** — the swing reaches as far as the blow reached, no further — and the inner
edge is 0.30× of it, so the band scales with the strike. The hand mount is still read, but only for
the arc's **height**, which is a real bone measurement with no floor on it.

⚑ **A second, smaller change rides with it and must be stated separately: PASS 3 changed WHICH BODY
the window reads.** The window's bearing used to come from the **nearest live entity** — a scene
heuristic, not a fight fact. The trace names the bodies the swing actually **struck**. On tick 217
those two answers differ and the heuristic picks the wrong one: nearest is the mob at **1.00 m**,
while the same swing landed the fight's second crit on the boss at **3.22 m**. Aiming and sizing off
the nearest body pointed a 1.27 m arc into a torso. The window now takes bearing and extent from the
**farthest** body the swing struck — a cone that reached 3.22 m reached 1.00 m on the way, and an ±86°
sweep covers both; the converse is not true. The nearest-live-entity walk survives only as a fallback
for windows with no resolvable struck target, and the render prints a line if it is ever taken. **It
was never taken on this trace.**

Consequences, both intended: tick 217's arc draws out to **3.22 m instead of 1.27 m**, which moves its
bright geometry off the torso that was occluding it (F-AC-2); and a 1.00 m contact now draws a
**smaller** arc than before, which is correct — a swing at something standing on top of you is a short
swing.

**`SA_ARC_NO_DEPTH` is back to `false`.** Addendum 17's order was explicit: *"if it clears 90 %,
`no_depth_test` is NOT taken at all."* The arc ships **depth-tested, like every other body effect.**
The flag stays wired and stays commented, because the Addendum-16 authorisation still stands if a
clean instrument later shows PASS 3 missing. **I am not claiming it cleared 90 % — I am declining to
take a second change on a lap whose instrument cannot say whether the first one worked.** Shipping
depth-test-ON is the conservative reading of the ruling, not a claim of victory.

## §3 — LEECH ON THE HP BAR

`event: leech` fires **29 times** — 23 realised, 6 wasted at full HP, **362.8 healed of 616.1
capacity.** The player falls to 65 % HP (496.8 of 759) and climbs back to 90 % (686.1) **entirely on
leech**, and until this cell the number simply went back up.

Genre convention is unanimous and none of it is a floating number: D3 ticks a globe, PoE animates a
leech fill *inside* the life bar, Last Epoch shimmers the bar. **Leech reads on the resource bar.** The
realised amount is drawn as its own **rising fill** — a paler, warmer red than the settled HP fill, so
it reads as *arriving* life rather than as a second health pool — inset in the HP bar's own trough,
ahead of the settled line, receding as the settled line catches up.

⚑ **When `healed == 0` and `capacity > 0` it draws NOTHING, and the refusal is at the source.**
`_ps_leech_note` returns before appending, so a wasted leech never reaches the bar's event list at all
— it is not hidden, it does not exist. Six of the 29 heal exactly zero against a real capacity, and a
bar that swelled on those would depict a heal that did not happen.

⚑ **R-BR-25 said "no presentation until the adapter stops discarding", and its own condition is now
met by measurement.** When it was written, every `healed` on the battery read 0.0. On this trace 23 of
29 read nonzero. The ruling is satisfied, not overridden.

**Measured presence** (full-fight arm, HUD census — see the cell-5 note for the same file):

```
[hudcensus] §3 LEECH BAR: 23 realised / 6 wasted | 22 of 23 realised events reached the bar
            (95.65 %) | 536 frames carried a nonzero leech fill
            | wasted events drew: 0 by construction (`_ps_leech_note` refuses healed <= 0)
```

⚑ **The 22-of-23 is a counting artifact of my own instrument and the presence is 100 %.** The census
keys realised events by tick, and **tick 217 carries two realised leeches** (18.39 and 11.28 healed) —
the same tick as the fight's second crit. Twenty-three events land on **twenty-two distinct ticks**, so
a dict-by-tick can never report more than 22. **Every realised leech reached the bar; none was
missed.** I am reporting the raw line and the correction rather than silently fixing the denominator,
because a denominator that quietly changes between laps is how a gate stops meaning anything.

**Absence: 0 of 6.** The six wasted leeches — ticks 0, 74, 100, 106, 112, 121 — drew nothing, and they
drew nothing *structurally*, not by a visibility flag.

## §4 — THE SHELL SHEEN, RE-MEASURED AT WATCH SCALE. IT READS. THE TINT IS COLDER STILL.

Matt ratified the frost shell and made **sheen a requirement**. Cell 2c measured it on the `arena`
**instrument** pose at 1280×720 (17–23 px/m). A requirement is about the picture he will actually
watch — `player_lock` at 1600×900, **36.32 px/m** — so I re-ran cell 2c's own statistic there, on a
pair one word apart (`--nomark 1` peels the ward and rime shells and nothing else), both arms carrying
`--nodust 1 --noambient 1`.

```
POOLED over 83,473 shell pixels — normalised tint (0.4172, 0.8836, 1.0000)
                    sampled floor-body tint (0.7145, 0.9118, 1.0000)   [ARSENAL-2]
       cell 2c pooled tint at the INSTRUMENT pose (0.5760, 0.9051, 1.0000)
percentiles 5 / 25 / 50 / 75 / 95 / 99  ->  -25.9 / 14.6 / 24.9 / 46.5 / 91.8 / 122.9
p99 / median = 4.93     [cell 2c at the instrument pose: 13.98]
```

**The sheen reads.** A flat wash tends to 1; 4.93 is a specular tail. It is a *smaller* multiple than
13.98 — and the reason is legible in the same table rather than being a mystery: **the median added
luma more than doubled (11.2 → 24.9) and the negative shoulder collapsed (−81.4 → −25.9).** At 36 px/m
the whole shell is brighter and more of it is lit, so the highlight is a smaller multiple of a much
larger body. That is the shell getting *more* present at watch scale, not less.

⚑ **Instrument honesty:** this pair is subject to §0's non-determinism term. The floor is ~2,000 px
against an 83,473-px sample — about **2.4 %** — and it enters as speckle scattered over the frame
rather than concentrated on the shell. The pooled tint and the percentile ratio are means over tens of
thousands of pixels and are not dominated by it, but they are not clean either, and I would not quote
either figure to more than two decimals.

**Green and blue land on the sample. Red does not, and it runs colder the closer you get:**

| | red | green | blue |
|---|---|---|---|
| ARSENAL-2 sampled ice | 0.7145 | 0.9118 | 1.0000 |
| render @ instrument pose (cell 2c) | 0.5760 | 0.9051 | 1.0000 |
| render @ **watch scale** (this cell) | **0.4172** | 0.8836 | 1.0000 |

### Recommendation — and it is a recommendation, not a decision

**I recommend correcting the red channel toward the sample.** Three reasons, in order of weight:

1. **The shell is bluer than the ice it is made of, and it gets bluer the closer the camera stands.**
   A property that moves with camera distance is not a colour choice; it is the fresnel rim and the
   body underneath winning more of the pixel as the shell resolves. Nobody chose 0.4172.
2. **The crypt is already a cold room.** Against a cold ambient, an ice shell that reads *bluer than
   the arsenal ice the telegraphs are made of* stops reading as ice on a body and starts reading as a
   magic tint over a body. The telegraphs, the impacts and the shell should be the same substance.
3. **The correction cannot cost the sheen.** The sheen statistic is a luma-distribution property; a
   red lift moves the tint and leaves p99/median where it is. This is the rare fix with no trade.

**Against it, honestly:** Matt looked at the colder image and ratified it. Colder ice is a legitimate
and arguably better *look* — it separates the shell from the warm torchlight more cleanly than the
sampled palette would. **Taste is Matt's and I am not taking it.** If he wants it corrected the change
is one channel on the shell's tint and I would target red ≈ 0.60–0.71 at watch scale, measured on this
same instrument rather than dialled by eye.
