## §4 — Scope 2: AI-state tags, and the source that had to be refused

Declared an **INSTRUMENT OVERLAY** (owner-eye legibility, same class as the frame banners), not final game UI. Enemy combatants only; **the player gets no tag**.

**⚑ THE BRIEF LISTS A SOURCE THAT CANNOT BE USED, AND THE TRACE IS WHAT SAYS SO.** Census over all 33 WR3 traces — **92,525 `decision` records, ONE key-set, no exceptions**: `{event, intent, record_type, t_s, target_id, tick}`. **No subject field.** `target_id` is the entity the decision is *about*, and the whole intent set (`advance`/`reposition`/`hold`/`evade:tg`/`evade:commit`/`evade:pressure`) is the **player's** movement policy. `intent: evade:commit, target_id: <boss>` means *the player is evading the boss*. Painting "EVADE" over the boss would render the exact inverse of the fight, in the most confident typeface on the frame. **Refused.** The relation is real and is drawn — as `player target`, its own node, in the **player's** cyan.

| element | source | rule |
|---|---|---|
| state word + colour bar | per-entity `commit_state` | idle/windup/strike/recovery; unknown value carried **verbatim**, never folded into idle |
| yellow bar above | `telegraph`, matched on `attacker_id` (a *stated* subject) | **additive — never masks the state word** |
| `player target`, cyan | `decision.target_id` | attributed to the player; separate node, separate colour |
| `state absent`, magenta | **missing `commit_state` key** | a missing key and a stated `idle` are different statements |

**⚑ A BUG CAUGHT BEFORE SHIPPING.** The first cut wrote `TELL` *over* the state word. The boss's tell windows here are `[7,30] [68,72] [113,136]` — and the second **is a commit** (windup 68–71, strike 72). That word would have covered a wind-up and its strike, hiding the commit lock — the entire WR3 finding — on the one commit carrying a telegraph. Two true statements competing for one slot; the fix is a second slot, not a priority rule. The half-rendered clip was **discarded at 1,805 frames**. Verified on the delivered frames: tell bar present at ticks 68 and 72, **gone at 74** when the window closes, state word never masked.
