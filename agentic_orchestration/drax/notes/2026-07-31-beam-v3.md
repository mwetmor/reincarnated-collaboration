## §0 — The cell in six sentences

spot's projective mask coordinate as `(dot(d, lamp_right), dot(d, lamp_up))`, and Godot's own

lit floor at the shipped `(1,1)` and 0.103 m at `(1,-1)`**, where 0.103 m is the measuring slab's
own lean smear. **⚑ THE SUN WAS NEVER IMPLICATED AND THE BUILD ALWAYS SAID SO:** beam-axis

after it — what was mirrored is the aperture pattern, which no axis comparison can see, and the
mirror line measures **−43.5°, which IS the lamp's own u axis on the floor**. **⚑ AND THE GATE

POOL's axes, and a bar set projected onto a foreign axis has a LARGER bounding extent than an

## §6 — SHADOW A/B (standing fork ①): the evidence, not a proposal

Matt: *"why am I not seeing shadows on characters — do I need to rule?"* `UNIFIED_KEY_ENERGY`
1.00 (shipped) vs 3.50, same trace (G-5 baton seed 74000909), same camera, same beat, same VFX
arm, 330 frames each. **`SKY_ENERGY_REF` and the fog are untouched. The shipped default is
UNCHANGED — nothing in this cell moves it.**

**⚑ ISOLATING *CHARACTER* SHADOWS IS SOLVED BY MOTION, NOT BY SEGMENTATION.** `--keycast 0`
stops the one shadow author casting, so `D_t = luma(NOCAST_t) − luma(SHIPPED_t)` is exactly the
darkening the key's shadows write — nothing estimated, no human drawing a region. But D contains
the pillars and walls, which is most of what a whole-frame ρ measures. The lighting is
WORLD-STATIC (Scope 20 #2) and the camera is player-locked, so geometry shadows are constant
across the take while a BODY's shadow sweeps: the per-pixel **median** of D estimates the static
field and `C_t = D_t − median(D)` is the moving casters. Median, not mean, because a body pausing
would drag a mean into its own shadow and then subtract it.

| statistic (55 frames sampled, stride 6) | **KEY 1.00 (shipped)** | **KEY 3.50** | ratio |

| character-shadowed fraction of frame | 19.40 % | **28.88 %** | **1.49×** |

The residual is the p99 of |C| on pixels no character shadow ever touched: at 0.86–0.93 against

### 7.1 ⚑ F-BV-1 — THE PROBE HAS BEEN RENDERING THE PRE-SHADOW-UNIFY KEY SINCE SHADOW-UNIFY

## §11 — At Matt's eye

1. **YOUR DISJOINT IS ONE SIGN AND I CAN NAME IT: the mask's V axis was inverted.** Godot's spot
   projector reads the mask upside-down relative to the way the beam shader was reconstructing

   and the shadow ray have been on it to six decimal places the whole time, before and after.
2. **⚑ AND THE GATE THAT WAS SUPPOSED TO CATCH IT WAS PAYING IT A BONUS.** The pin gate measures
   how far the beam SPREADS along the pool's axes. Bars pointing the wrong way spread FURTHER.
   So BEAM-PIN2's "100.00 % of the slats' length" — a number I gave you as a win — was partly the
   defect flattering itself, and fixing the direction makes that number go DOWN to 77 %. The
   statistic that can't be fooled is overlap, and on the slats it went from 0.13 to 0.38.

   inside your frame. Watch the tilt clip: the old shaft closes to a point in the middle of the
   picture. The new one is 54 % as wide as its own base as it goes off the top edge, and its apex
   sits 21.5 m above the highest point your camera can see.
