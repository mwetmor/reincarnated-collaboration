## 2. What I could NOT source, and what I did instead

### 2.1 `max_energy` does not exist anywhere
Not in `header`, not in `entities[]`, not in the `g5` block, not in `door_values`. Observed energy on this fight runs 4 → 90 and touches 90 exactly **once**, so 90 is a **lower bound** on the cap, not the cap.
**Shipped:** the bar normalises against the highest value observed *so far*, and the label reads **`EN 57 (>=90 seen)`**. A HUD printing `57 / 90` would be asserting a cap the trace never published. → **F-HB-1**

### 2.2 No cooldown base duration is published
`skill_cooldowns[i]` is seconds REMAINING; `header.skills[]` carries `name`/`geometry`/`range_m`/`element` and no duration.
**Shipped:** the sweep denominator is a **running maximum over the past**, never over the whole trace — a HUD that normalises against a number it has not seen yet is showing the observer the future. On this fight it converges on the first cast and never moves: **0.455556 s** (skill 0) and **2.400 s** (skill 1). Printed in the footer and in the dump. → **F-HB-2**

### 2.3 `commit_state` / `commit_skill_idx` are DARK on this trace
`commit_state == "idle"` on **361 of 361** ticks; `commit_skill_idx == −1` on **361 of 361**. Scope 33 asked for the active-cast highlight off `commit_skill_idx`; that field has no signal here.
**Shipped:** both paths are wired. The one that fires is a 0.22 s flash keyed to a `damage` event that named that `skill_idx` — the only live evidence of a cast the trace contains — and the footer says so in those words. Nothing was faked to make the skill bar look alive. → **F-HB-3**

### 2.4 There is no join key between a telegraph and its damage
`telegraph.attack_id` is populated **9 of 9**. `damage.attack_id` is populated **4 of 57** and `null` on every one of the 7 hits the player took. So "did that nova HIT" is **not answerable** from this schema; only "was he standing in the announced footprint at `fire_tick`" is.
**Shipped:** the verdict tokens are **`IN` / `CLR`**, never `HIT`, and the footer states the reason. → **F-HB-4** (see §3.2 for the measured consequence)

### 2.5 Element glyphs cover the wrong elements
The Synty pack ships **4** element icons (Air / Earth / Fire / Ice) — not 6/6 as R-PC-11/12 records. This fight's element set is `{chaos, cold, physical}`: **1 of 3** has a plausible glyph (Ice≈cold), and `chaos` has none.
**Shipped:** elements render as the trace's own **words**. Mapping `chaos` onto a glyph that does not mean chaos would be a render lie for a decoration. → **F-HB-5**

### 2.6 No monospace face is redistributable
There is no OFL mono in the repo; Menlo / SF Mono are Apple-licensed and vendoring one would be a licence defect shipped to look tidy.
**Shipped:** Godot `SystemFont` with a declared preference list; the **resolved face is logged** (`Menlo` here) so "the strip is not monospace" can never be a silent condition.

### 2.7 R-BR-9's fallback leg is built and UNEXERCISED — structurally
The nearest-in-heading-cone fallback (±60°, nearest alive, **empty panel if the cone is empty** — it deliberately does not fall through to "nearest anywhere", because "what he is facing" and "what is closest" are different claims) is implemented and never runs on this cohort. **Checked across seeds 74000900–74000909: the player's first `damage` event is at `t_s = 0.0` on all ten.** The werewolf opens with `rip_and_tear` on tick 0, so the sticky leg always wins before the fallback can be reached. Dead by construction, not by omission. → **F-HB-6**
