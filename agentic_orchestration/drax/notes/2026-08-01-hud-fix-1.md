## 3. M-2 — circle → box

### 3.1 The diagnosis: it was the radius, and nothing was clipping it

The retired `_draw()` ran a fan to `r = size.length()`, commented "over-reach so the
wedge covers corners". It does cover them. `size.length()` for a 92.8 px box is
**131.24 px** from the centre against a **46.4 px** half-edge, so the fan reached
**84.84 px past the nearest box edge**, on a `Control` with `clip_contents` false and
no parent clip. Inside the box the picture was already a correct box-shaped wedge;
outside it, the fan's **polygonal arc** was painted over the arena floor at 0.72 alpha.

That arc is the circle. Visible on the shipped watch's own plate
`tmp/restage/plates/R2_0300.png`: skill 1 at 0.8 s of a 2.400 s base, shade running
from the box centre out past x = 940 against a box whose right edge is 899.3.

The `--sweepcircle` control arm reproduces it and the measurement matches the
arithmetic to within a pixel: **furthest orphan sweep pixel 84 px outside a box
edge** (predicted 84.84).

### 3.2 The fix: cast the fan to the box, do not clip it

`clip_contents = true` would have worked by *hiding* a wrong polygon — the vertices
would still be off the box, and anyone later measuring the geometry would measure a
circle. So the fan is cast to the boundary instead: `r(θ)` is a ray/rect slab
intersection, and the **four corners are inserted as vertices** whenever the sweep
crosses them. Between two corners the box boundary is a straight line, so a straight
polygon edge lies exactly on it — no corner-cutting inset, no overhang. Exactness is
then a property of the geometry, provable without a picture, and the picture confirms
it afterwards.

Drawn as **explicit triangles**: a wedge of more than half a box is concave about its
own centre, and `draw_colored_polygon` on a concave fan is triangulator-dependent. The
old code had the same concavity and got away with it; "got away with it" is not a gate.

`wedge_points()` is exposed rather than inlined so the headless gate asserts on the
same call `_draw()` makes. A gate that re-implements the geometry it gates proves that
two authors agreed.

## 4. M-4 — the phantom health bars: what they are

**Matt asked what this is before he asked for it to go. Here is the answer.**

### 4.1 The cause, in one sentence

The overhead HP bar is a **pre-HUD instrument that Scope 33 superseded and Scope 33a's
retirement list missed** — because the retirement list was a list of `Label`s, and this
thing is a pair of `MeshInstance3D` quads parented to a body in world space. It fell
through the seam between "UI" and "scene".

### 4.2 Hypothesis dispositions — all three, said plainly

**(a) "spawned per actor and never freed on `alive→false`" — CONFIRMED, in substance.**
Not *freed*: never **hidden**. `_set_corpse` hides the identity stalk (`body.get_node("Stalk").visible = false`)
and says nothing about the bar. And nothing else could have: `_apply_frame_at_pos`
only writes `hp_fg` for entities **present in the tick frame**, and the renderer's own
comment says a dead entity is dropped from the frame entirely. So the last write a
corpse received was from its final living tick, and the widget then stood in world
space forever. Measured on the control arm over 1,264 frames:

| entity | dead from frame | widget-frames after death | on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| `slitha_shaman_c01_2` | 0 (dead at tick 0) | 1,264 | 424 |
| `slitha_melee_b01_1` | 698 | 566 | 566 |
| `boss&quest_slith_wightmirecave01_0` | 1,113 | 151 | 151 |
| **total** | | **1,981** | **1,141** |

**66.3 % of the watch's frames (838 / 1,264) carry at least one on-screen phantom.**
One escort is `alive: false` at tick 0, so its black plate stands over an empty patch
of floor for the entire fight — which is why Matt could see one "the entire fight".

**(b) "the player's own overhead widget draws on top of the HUD player bar, spawned
lazily on first damage" — REFUTED, on both limbs.** The player's widget is built at
roster build with every other actor's, not lazily. And **0 overlaps** between *any*
overhead widget and the HUD player-bar rect, on any of the 1,264 frames of the
*pre-fix control arm*. The mechanism (b) proposes does not occur.

What does occur, and is what Matt described: the escort dies **next to the player**, at
frame 698, and its phantom is on screen for every one of the remaining 566 frames. Over
those frames the phantom's screen centre sits a **median 142 px** from the player's own
overhead widget, **minimum 62 px**, and is **within 200 px on 58.7 %** of them; on
frames **706–708** the two rects literally overlap. So "appears at one point in the
fight and then lasts the rest of the way through it" is exact — it is the escort's
death, and it never leaves. The occluded bar is the player's **overhead** bar, not the
HUD bar; the HUD bar was never touched.

**(c) "a bar whose fill has gone to zero/NaN, rendering its own backing plate" —
CONFIRMED, as the explanation of the COLOUR.** `bg` is `Color(0.06, 0.06, 0.08, 0.85)`;
`fg` is the green fill whose quad width is `max(bar_w · hp/max_hp, 0.0001)`. At death
`hp = 0`, so the fill collapses to 0.0001 m and what remains is the backing plate
alone. That is why every phantom is a **black** bar and not a health bar. (a) explains
the persistence; (c) explains the appearance. They are not competing.

**The boss's bar is not a leak at all.** It is the live widget working as designed and
being enormous: `bar_w = max(radius · 2.6, 1.4)` with the boss's radius 1.5 gives a
3.9 m plate, measured on screen at **mean 199.7 × 15.4 px, max 257.5 px wide**, drawn
with `no_depth_test = true` over every body and wall for 1,057 frames. "Occluding
things the entire fight" is a correct description of correct behaviour.

### 4.3 The fix, in two parts

1. **The leak.** `_set_corpse` hides the bar; `_restore` un-hides it, and asks the
   retirement question again rather than assuming `true`.
2. **The retirement.** Under `--playstats 1` the whole overhead layer is dark, because
   Scope 33 already puts the player's HP in the bottom-left bar and the current
   target's HP in the target panel. A second copy drawn `no_depth_test` over the fight
   is not redundancy, it is occlusion. Under `--playstats 0` — measurement and
   diagnostic arms — it stays, because there it is the only health readout there is.

## 7. ⚑ MY OWN INSTRUMENTS THAT FAILED — INCLUDING ONE THAT WOULD HAVE FAILED LIKE A SUCCESS

Three, and the first is the dangerous one.

### 7.1 The player-id guess — this one would have PASSED while measuring nothing

To answer Matt's first symptom literally ("a strange health bar … occludes the player
character's health bar") I needed the player's own widget, so the census analyser had:

```python
if r["eid"].startswith("player") or r["eid"] == "player":
```

**The player's entity id on this fixture is `gd-werewolf-kitcal-1`.** That predicate
matches nobody. The loop would have found no player rows, skipped every frame, and
printed *"dead-actor widget over the player's own overhead widget: none"* — a clean,
confident, PASS-shaped sentence produced entirely by a name guess. It would have
**refuted a true symptom**, and it would have looked like evidence.

Caught before it ran, by dumping the trace's actual entity ids instead of assuming
their shape. Replaced with a test on the data: `energy` is player-only on this schema
(the mobs carry an explicit `null`), so **presence of the key is the identification**.
The reasoning is now in the function's own docstring, because the next person to
write a player check here will reach for the name first too.

### 7.2 The footer ink census read the arena floor as font — twice

First cut, tolerance 42: **68,539 "footer pixels" on a build that draws no footer.**
The removed tier's rect now shows the arena floor, and bluish-grey stone under fog is
within 42 of a bluish-grey font. That failed like a *failure*, so one reading caught
it — but only because the floor happened to be cool. On a warm floor the identical
instrument returns 0 for entirely the wrong reason and the gate PASSES while measuring
nothing at all. Same defect class as 7.1; it just happened to land on the loud side.

Tightening to 12 was **not enough**: frame `R3_0604`, a frost flash, still put 12,610
scene pixels inside the font's colour box. Colour alone cannot separate a glyph from a
lit floor. What can is the **outline**: every Label on this HUD carries `outline_size`
4–6 in near-black, so a glyph pixel always has a very dark pixel within two. With that
requirement the same rect reads 118 px on the old build and **0 on every one of the
1,264 new frames**, including `R3_0604`.

That is why G-M1 carries **arm 1b** — the sheet's own height off the hairline rule.
Two independent readings, one pixel-based and one structural, because a single
colour-matched census had already lied twice.

### 7.3 The M-2 footprint measure asked a per-box question of a shared region

First cut measured each skill box inside its own ±120 px window. **The two boxes are
12.99 px apart.** Each window swallowed the other box's sweep; both boxes reported the
same bounding rectangle and a **+105.5 px overhang that no pixel actually had** — on
the *post-fix* build, whose geometry gate was already reporting 0.0000 px error. Had I
trusted it, I would have gone looking for a defect in a fix that was correct.

The repair is not a smaller window. It is to stop asking a per-box question of a shared
region: every sweep pixel in the whole HUD band is classified as belonging to the box
it falls in **or as an ORPHAN**, and orphans *are* the overhang by definition. No
window, no neighbour contamination, one number.

The same instrument also nearly counted particles as sweep. A raw pixel diff of the
A/B pair reports 55–239 differing pixels per frame that are **not** the sweep (VFX,
which is trace-timed but re-seeded), in a window where the boxes occupy 17,222 of
240,000 px — so a raw-diff instrument would have reported hundreds of "overhang" pixels
per frame on a build with zero. It is pre-empted by requiring the pixel to satisfy the
sweep's own alpha blend, `A = 0.28·B + 0.72·(5,8,13)`, and the residual is printed
separately every frame rather than folded in.

**A residual caveat, stated rather than hidden:** on the `--sweepcircle` control arm the
per-box bounding boxes are still cross-contaminated — box 1's overhang lands *inside*
box 0's rect and is attributed to box 0, which is why that arm reports 6,598 px of
"box 0 sweep" on a frame where box 0 is READY. The **orphan count** is the
uncontaminated number on that arm, and it is the one quoted.
