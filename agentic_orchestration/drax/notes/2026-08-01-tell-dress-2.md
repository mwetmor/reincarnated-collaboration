## §0 — WHAT THIS CELL WAS FOR

Cell 2b authored three shaders, the playback wiring and two instruments, and then the host volume
reached zero bytes with every gate unmeasured. Its G-C first pass had already failed on a defect it
caught against itself: **4,675 of 4,996 "telegraph pixels outside the arena" were the render's own

because the render omitted `--nohud 1`. Cell 2's own law requires `--nohud 1` on a measurement frame.

This cell re-rendered the gate arms with that one word added and called the four gates. It also cut
the owner-eye clip.

**The banner was real and it is gone.** In the treatment differential over the same eight gate
frames, the top-60-row count falls from **4,675** to **~60 px per frame of isolated speckle**.

**And the banner was not the only contaminant.** Two more turned up, both of them instrument-side,
both of them found by controls rather than by inspection, and neither of them anything the shaders

## §3 — G-2a. **NOT CALLED, and the reason is a third contaminant the gate diagnosed itself**

Cell 3 collapsed the 31 / 43 gap violations to 1 / 1 with `--nomark 1` and refused to call 6/6,
because that pair's own baseline was **237 lit px at frame 5, where nothing is drawn**. F-CJ-1 pinned
the cause: `--tgoff` peels the whole tell layer including `_tell_dress`'s write of a body's resting
emissive, while `--tgfam` peels only non-matching decals, so the two arms were never one word apart.

**I built the pair that does not need `--tgoff` at all** — `--tgfam nova` (treatment) against
`--tgfam none` (control), both through `--tgbody 0 --nomark 1 --nohud 1`. Both arms run `_tell_dress`
identically, so the body-emissive disagreement F-CJ-1 named cannot arise, and **cell 2's `--tgoff`
control arm is not touched**, which was the conductor-call part of F-CJ-1.

**It got worse, and the instrument said why before I did.** Baseline at frame 5: **2,431 lit px**, up
from 237, and `g2_gate.py` printed its own prescription verbatim:

> `[baseline] ⚑ the two arms disagree where nothing is drawn — peel the non-deterministic authors (--dust 1 --nonum 1 --noambient 1)`

Same contaminant as §2, ten times larger, because §2's differential only counted it *outside* the
arena quad and G-2a's lattice samples the floor where the dust actually is. The 141 / 64 gap counts
that pair produced are **uninterpretable** and are not quoted as a result.

## §8 — FINDINGS TO ROUTE

- **F-TD2-1 — the ambient dust layer is seeded PER PROCESS, and every differential instrument in this
  run is standing on it.** Measured floor: ~107 px/frame outside the arena quad, ~2,500 px/frame over
  the whole frame, on frames where nothing is drawn. It cost cell 3 a G-2a call (the "237 px body
  emissive" residue is at most partly body emissive — the dust term alone is an order of magnitude
  bigger on a whole-frame count) and it cost this cell a render pass. `g2_gate.py` has printed the
  prescription (`--nodust 1 --nonum 1 --noambient 1`) on every run and no cell had read it.
  **Every differential arm in this run should carry the peel set as standard.** Routed as a
  discipline candidate, not patched unilaterally.
- **F-TD2-2 — the telegraph's emissive lights wall geometry outside the arena footprint (713 px on
  the gate frames, 789 px in the control).** R-BR-41 clips the DECAL; it does not cull the LIGHT.
  Whether that is in scope for the clip law is a conductor call; the fix, if wanted, is a light cull.
- **F-TD2-3 — `ShieldAuraBlue` is achromatic** (0.463, 0.464, 0.464). Carried forward from cell 2b
  because it is the second name/material disagreement in this pack and the register is decided by
  sampling.
- **F-TD2-4 — the nova declares ONE gapless radius and the frame has ANOTHER.** `gapless inside
  2.1528 m` is emitted, printed and consumed by the gate; the radius at which sixteen 0.42 m corridors
  at 22.5° are actually *separable at 23 px/m* is larger — the gap is 0.165 m (≈ 4 px) at r = 3.00 m,
  and every G-2a gap violation on the clean instrument lies between 2.55 m and 3.15 m. Two candidate
  responses, both of which are conductor calls and neither of which I made in-cell because both move
  a pre-registered number: (a) the render declares a SEPARABLE radius alongside the gapless one and
  the gate excludes below it, or (b) the star's inner band tapers its corridors so the gap opens
  sooner. **(b) changes what the player is told about where the hazard is and must not be done to
  make a gate green.**
- **F-CJ-1 IS DISCHARGED, and this cell can say so from the tree rather than from a plan.**
  `_body_lang` no longer returns early on `not _tg_enabled` (cell 2b's banked commit), so `--tgoff`
  and `--tgfam` now differ only in which decals are drawn. The 237 px cell 3 measured is gone; what
  replaced it as the floor was F-TD2-1, which is a different animal and needed a different peel.
