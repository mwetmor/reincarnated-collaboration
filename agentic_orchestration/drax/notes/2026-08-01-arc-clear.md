## §1 — G-14b AND G-14d. THE DIAGNOSIS, AND WHY THE HONEST FIX AND THE LEGIBLE FIX ARE THE SAME FIX

The conductor ruled geometry first: *"an arc that the torso occludes is an arc drawn too close to
the torso."* Measuring the build made that literal in a way worth stating.

⚑ **The bright end of the arc was the occluded end BY CONSTRUCTION.** The ribbon's leading edge is
aimed **along the strike bearing**, and the strike bearing points **at the target**. On all five of
cell 3b's misses the target is the body standing between the camera and the player. The arc was
therefore drawing its brightest, outermost geometry exactly where the occluder is — not by an
accident of camera pose, but because that is where a swing points.

⚑ **And the arc was smaller than the body it was mounted on.** `_sa_hand_mount` measures the hand
bones' horizontal offset in the rig's own frame and floors it at 0.55 m; on this werewolf the floor
is what binds. At the cell-3b band (0.45–1.95×) the outer edge sat at **1.07 m** on a body of
`entity_radius_m` 0.5 m and 2.07 m height — an arc barely wider than the torso, inside a
silhouette that a 1.5 m-radius boss can cover entirely.

### The three geometry changes, and what is deliberately NOT among them

| | cell 3b | ARC-CLEAR | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| sweep half-angle | 1.15 rad (~66°) | **1.50 rad (~86°)** | lit geometry reaches AROUND to the flanks, off the bearing the occluder stands on |
| radial band | 0.45 – 1.95 × reach | **0.60 – 2.30 × reach** | the outer edge clears the silhouette laterally instead of ending inside it |
| edge heights | 0.94 / 1.08 × hand height | **1.02 / 1.44 ×** | the arc RISES as it extends — a claw rake — so its outer edge climbs above the occluder at this camera's −41° pitch |
| alpha, tail falloff | — | **UNCHANGED** | the conductor's diagnosis is that this is not a magnitude problem; moving gain here would leave the re-measure unable to say whether geometry or brightness moved the number |

The leech pulse takes the same two changes for the same reason: `_tell_body_shell` at **1.06×** the
body is *inside* the body's own screen silhouette by construction, so anything that covers the body
covers the whole pulse and leaves no rim to see. Widened to **1.34×** and lifted by 0.14 × body
height. That is also the better picture of a heal, which swells *around* a body rather than skinning it.

## §3 — FINDINGS TO ROUTE

- **F-AC-1 — the ambient dust layer is per-process non-deterministic and is NOT covered by the
  `_cj_load` fix.** F-SA-1 seeded 209 GPUParticles3D emitters; the speckle survives it and is
  peeled only by `--nodust 1 --noambient 1`. **Any pixel differential in this run whose arms did
  not carry both flags is standing on a ~500 px floor at this camera.** That is a concrete item
  for the R-BR-51 blast-radius enumeration owed at exit review, and it is a *second* term beside
  the one Addendum 16 already listed.
- **F-AC-2 — an arc's brightest geometry points where its occluder stands, by construction.** Any
  body-level effect aimed along a strike bearing has this property. It is worth carrying into the
  3D seam generally, not just for this arc.
- **F-AC-3 — the melee arc is sized off a SKELETON OFFSET floored at 0.55 m, not off any fight
  quantity.** The trace publishes the contact distance for every damage event. Routed to BR-3.
- **G-14d's single remaining miss is a CLIP BOUNDARY** (tick 360, the last captured frame), not a
  render defect. Named so a later reader does not chase it.
