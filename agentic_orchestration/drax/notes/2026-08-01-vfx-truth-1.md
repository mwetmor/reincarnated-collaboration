## 5. Two render defects the gates found that the eye had passed

**Fog was inverting the danger grade.** The first build let the room's depth fog attenuate the
footprint; G-4 came back **decreasing** (47.40 → 40.69 → 30.75 against emitted scales 0.50 → 1.00 →
1.40) and the wave's far cuts fell below the threshold entirely (5.25 m of trapezoid measured as
**0.13 m**). The rim is the nova's **hottest** band and also its **farthest**, so fog was destroying
the one piece of information the shading exists to carry. `fog_disabled` on the decal shader — a
telegraph is a marker layer, not a physical surface, which is why `_tg_mat` has been unshaded all
along, and **a danger grade that fades with distance is a danger grade the player cannot read.**

**Sixteen overlapping strips made the hub the brightest part of the star.** Inside the gapless
radius all sixteen lane quads cover the same floor, so sixteen alphas composited and **inverted the
emitted ×0.50 hub band** — measured as a flat G-4 profile (102.75 / 101.45 / 101.42). The correct
construction is also the exact one: the union of *n* strips of half-width *w* through a common
origin **contains the full disc of radius `w / sin(π/n)`** and equals the strips outside it. So one
disc out to 2.1528 m plus sixteen strips from 2.1528 m is not an approximation of the union — **it
is the union, drawn once.** The hub now reads at ×0.50 and the 2.5 m step is visible on the frame.

The footprint also ships **non-emissive** (alpha carries the band), because an emissive thin lane
crossed the environment's glow threshold and glow bleeds outward — 321 lit gap samples in the
footprint-only phase, i.e. G-2a's binding clause failing by post-process. Same decision, same
measurement, as `_tg_mat`'s non-emissive interiors.
