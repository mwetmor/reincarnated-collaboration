## §4 — MY OWN FAILURES, in the order they happened

### 7.1 ⚑ THE FIRST CENSUS READ THE WRONG RECORD AND REPORTED AN EMPTY ROSTER

Census run 1 read only the `header` record and printed `opposition_roster: []` — while LAP-1's own log
line quotes a roster tier and a boss label. Two things that cannot both be true. `g5_header` is a

### 7.2 ⚑⚑ I WROTE A LEFT/RIGHT INVERSION AND MY OWN PROBE CAUGHT IT BEFORE ANY RENDER

The octant is the angle of velocity in the body's frame. I derived it from `heading_to_yaw` — local
+Z is forward; at `h = 0` local +X lands on sim SOUTH; a body facing east has south **on its right** —
and wrote `right = dx·sin h − dy·cos h`.

**That everyday intuition is wrong here**, because the sim→world map (`world.X = sim.x`,
`world.Z = sim.y`) does not preserve the handedness it assumes. In a right-handed frame the right hand
is `forward × up`; for a **+Z-forward** Synty body that is `(0,0,1) × (0,1,0) = (−1,0,0)` —
**local +X is the character's LEFT.**

The probe found it independently, and found it *loudly*: under the old sign **all seven off-axis Synty
labels inverted** (every `_L_` clip measured `R`) while **F measured +4.4° and B measured −178.5°,
both exact**. An all-or-nothing X flip across seven independently authored assets is a convention
error, not seven mislabelled files. Two channels, one answer; the sign is negated in both
`wr2_playback.gd` and the probe.

⚑ **Had this shipped, the fix would have replaced a body running backwards with a body strafing
confidently in the WRONG DIRECTION — which reads as deliberate, and is therefore worse than the bug it
replaced.** This is the single best argument for the probe existing.

*(Related, and reported so the record is clean: the probe's FIRST direction reading blamed the
werewolf — one body of nine inverted. That was the instrument, not the asset:
`get_bone_global_pose` is SKELETON space and a negative-X node transform above the skeleton mirrors it
while the RENDERED motion stays correct. Re-measured in **rig-local** space — the frame the viewer
actually sees — the werewolf agrees 17/17 and its speed magnitudes are unchanged. I nearly wrote up a
mirrored-asset finding that did not exist.)*

### 7.3 ⚑ I SHIPPED A COLD WASH, MEASURED IT, AND IT WAS DOING ESSENTIALLY NOTHING

The albedo wash at 0.30/0.34 A/B'd at **+0.85 B−R on a −63 warm baseline — 1.4 % of the thing it was
supposed to oppose.** I had already written the comment block explaining why an albedo multiply was
the right device. **A wash that moves body colour by 1.4 % is not a cold family; it is a rounding
error with a paragraph attached.** Replaced with a uniform mask-free cold self-glow (ADDED after
lighting rather than multiplied into albedo) → **+13.49, 21.28 %**, a 15.9× improvement. The
superseded reasoning is kept in the source with its measurement, not deleted.

### 7.4 ⚑ A GODOT CRASH I CAUSED, DIAGNOSED BY ELIMINATION

Signal 11 inside `AnimationPlayer::play_section`, on 6 of 9 bodies, **intermittently** — the same body
crashing in one process and completing in another with identical inputs. That signature is a dangling
cache, not a bad asset. Cause: the parent's `_build_anim` **ends by playing** `loco/idle`, so the
player holds a resolved track cache into a library this subclass then adds animations to. LAP-1 added
three clips and tripped it rarely enough never to be seen; **I added seventeen.** Fixed with
`_anim.stop()` before the library is mutated. **This is a latent bug LAP-1 shipped and I made
load-bearing** — worth saying, because the fix protects LAP-1's three clips too.

## §8 — HONEST DEBTS

1. **⚑ THE GAIT FALLBACK IS LARGE AND IT IS THE BIGGEST REMAINING DEFECT.** This tree ships **no
   sprint strafe** (`ls .../Locomotion/Sprint/` returns `F`, `Up25_F`, `Down25_F` and nothing else).
   An off-axis body in the sprint band therefore plays the RUN strafe of the same octant. On the
   player that is **371 of 423 moving frames — 87.7 %**, because his median speed (5.09 m/s) sits well
   above his sprint band edge (3.49 m/s). **The octant is right; the gait is one rung slow.** He no
   longer moonwalks, but for most of the fight he backpedals at a run cadence while travelling at a
   sprint. Retiring it needs sprint strafes this tree does not own.
2. **The attack animation is ONE generic swing on every body.** `strike()` plays
   `A_MOD_GBL_Idle_Fidget_Swipe_Neut` — a goblin *idle-fidget swipe*, the only attack-shaped clip in
   the tree (RIVAL-CAST §2.6). Matt's sentence says "their movements **and attacks**": the movements
   are per-body and measured; **the attacks are animated and correctly timed but not per-body, and not
   a real melee.** Scope 25's strike-connect ruling is the cell that owns this.
3. **Four of seven cast bodies never appear on the fixture seed** — BigOrk, Troll, PigButcher,
   MutantGuy live in `trash`/`champion`/`mixed_pack`. They are verified by the parade and by the
   probes, **not by a fight**. A `mixed_pack` or `trash` render would close it cheaply.
4. **Sprint clip direction is confirmed only loosely** (concentration R ≈ 0.32 on all nine bodies).
   Magnitude and octant are right; the reading is not tight, and the number is on the record.
5. **The casting is a presentation judgement and is veto-open.** Medusa-for-a-slitha and
   ForestWitch-for-an-Evocator are my reads of the roster's own labels; nothing in the engine says
   what a *slitha* looks like. Same for the whole scale ladder (§2). Drax holds rig-quality say per
   R-BR-5's pattern — **this is the sentence that makes it overridable.**
6. **The cold wash is kept at a strength I measured as near-useless** (+0.85 B−R, 1.4 %). It is free
   and acts on a different term from the glow, but it is **not** what carries the cold family and the
   source says so.

   one-author law is untouched and the bodies inherit it; **I have not put a number on the new
