**⇒ `SwordChargeUp`, `SwordTrail` and `SwordTrailShadow` are NAMED UNUSABLE.**

**⚑ Before reporting a negative I proved the instrument was alive** — this cell was about to name an

Minimum, not mean — every one of the 60 effects got the full swing. And the treatment plainly bites

×1.87, `MissileIce` ×1.67, `NovaLightningWave` ×1.62. The rig moves; the ribbons do not care.

**Why they do not care, read from the source rather than guessed.** `SwordTrail`'s and
`SwordTrailShadow`'s ribbon emitters are Shuriken **LOCAL** simulation space with rate 0 and a single
burst — one particle riding the host, whose trail follows the host by construction. No host motion
can make that streak, in Godot **or** in Unity. Their visible streak comes from a *sibling*
(`SwordParticleTrail`, WORLD space, 25/s billboards), and that sibling does respond — chg ×1.28 both
— it is simply faint. `SwordChargeUp` is **WORLD** space and fell anyway.

**One honest caveat on the instrument, not the result.** The lit metric thresholds luminance on a
black stage, so it is structurally near-blind to a dark effect — `SwordTrailShadow`'s
`colorOverLifetime` ends at (0.12, 0.05, 0.23). That is why the moving gate also reports `chg`,
pixels differing from the in-run control frame by any visible amount. On `chg` the trail class scores
0.67 / 1.28 / 1.28 against a non-trail band centred on ~1.1–1.2. **Both metrics agree.** The verdict
does not rest on the metric that could be accused of bias.

## 4. Defects caught in my own output — three, and the third is the worst

**1 — the prefab root's TRANSLATION is scene residue, and we were baking it in.**
`AuraElementalFrost` failed the pixel gate at **0 lit pixels**. Not a dim effect: its prefab-asset
root sits at **(7.853, 0, −21.177)**, so it drew 22.6 m off, entirely outside frame. Four of the 60
prefabs carry a non-zero root position, and **two of them shipped in ARSENAL-HARVEST displaced**:
`NovaLife` by 0.29 m and `NovaLightningWave` by 1.28 m. Both PASSED that cell's pixel gate at 9,285
and 24,045 lit pixels, because *"did pixels light"* is not *"did pixels light in the right place"*.
`WallWater` (3.40 m) would have joined them.

**Settled by probe, not by reasoning about Unity semantics.** Exactly one target has a
`PrefabInstance` in the pack's own 60 demo scenes — `ElementalAuraFrost` in `PolyAura4.unity` — and
that instance's `m_Modifications` record both properties:

```
m_LocalRotation  w .7071068  x −.7071068  y −0  z −0   == the prefab-asset root quat, bit for bit
m_LocalPosition  (−4.286, 0, −1.432)   vs prefab-asset root (7.853, 0, −21.177)
```

**Rotation preserved. Position replaced.** Fixed in `chain_transform` by dropping the translation of
the root node only — and verified by regenerating with the fix off and diffing: **exactly 4 scenes
differ**, the four with a non-zero root position, and nothing else. This is the same "basis yes,
origin no" asymmetry the mesh extractor already taught us one layer down (ARSENAL-HARVEST §3
defect 3), recurring at the prefab layer.

**2 — ARSENAL-HARVEST's orientation evidence was read off the wrong document class, and the right
answer survived anyway.** That note wrote: *"`PolyCombat02.unity` instantiates `GroundSlamRed` at
`m_LocalRotation (0,0,0,1)` — identity. The prefab's own root rotation IS the authored orientation."*
Probed: **`GroundSlamRed`'s guid occurs in `PolyCombat02.unity` exactly once, inside a `!u!114`
MonoBehaviour as `chosenEffect:` — a spawner's prefab reference.** There is no `!u!1001` PrefabInstance
for it in any demo scene; it has **no scene transform at all**. The identity rotation cited belonged
to some other GameObject in that file. The **conclusion** is correct — the `PolyAura4` read above
proves root rotation is preserved, independently and on a real PrefabInstance — but the citation
supporting it was not evidence. Right answer, wrong evidence, and only R-BR-34's "cite the probe"
turned it up.

**3 — my own verification probe returned a clean, confident, entirely false result.** To prove the
root-translation fix touched only what it should, I regenerated with the fix disabled and diffed.
It reported **"0 effects differ"** — which I already knew was impossible, since I had watched
`AuraElementalFrost` go from 22.59 m to 0.000 m. The patch it applied had produced
`drop_translation=False  # TEMP probe), m)` — a comment swallowing the rest of a call expression, a
syntax error — so `pa_emit.py` crashed, wrote nothing, and the files on disk were still the fixed
ones. `capture_output=True` ate the traceback and there was no return-code check. **A null instrument,
in my own hands, in the same cell in which I built two tripwires against exactly this.** The corrected
probe asserts `returncode == 0` and asserts the expected output line before it is permitted to report
a diff; it then found the 4. Its first assertion attempt also fired wrongly (counted 3 substitutions
where 2 were intended — the third was `unity_trs`'s own signature default), which is a tripwire doing
its job noisily rather than a second defect.

**And one near-miss that is not a defect but is a fact about the gate.** `SwordChargeUp` measured
**139** lit pixels in ARSENAL-HARVEST and **16** in both ARSENAL-2 static runs, at the same origin,
on a byte-identical transcription (proven: it is not among the 4 changed scenes, and `git diff` on
`pa_emit.py` shows only the root-translation edit). I cannot explain the 9× swing from here and I am
not going to invent a cause. What it means is unambiguous: **the static pixel gate is not reproducible
run-to-run at low counts**, so the 139 never supported a conclusion about that effect. The moving gate,
by contrast, reproduced **bit-for-bit** across two full runs — which is why the G-11 verdict rests on it.

## 5. G-12 — licence, and what we refused rather than faked

Method unchanged and unchanged deliberately: `pa_parse.py` is a **deterministic parser**. It reads
Unity's YAML, transcribes the numbers it finds, and emits Godot scene text by a fixed field mapping.
**No pack content was fed to a model, in this cell or the last.** EULA §2.2.1.1(g) honoured by
construction.

**Assets NOT committed.** `Assets/PolygonArsenal/` stays gitignored — commercial third-party IP,
same rule as the Synty trees. The tracked authority is `scripts/pa_harvest/run_harvest.sh`, which
rebuilds the whole 60-effect tree from Matt's imported copy in one command.

**Provenance is now committed in a shareable form.** The full record
(`Assets/PolygonArsenal/harvest_manifest.json`) carries transcribed parameter values and stays
untracked. `scripts/pa_harvest/pa_provenance.py` emits the *index* —
`tmp/arsenal2/provenance_digest.json` — with per effect: source prefab path, emitter and light
counts, which modules were **unmapped** (refused rather than faked), which were **partial**
(approximated and named), and the material/shader/texture names. **No parameter values cross into
it.** So the tracked repo can answer "where did this come from and what did we lose" without
republishing anything we do not own.

**Refused, not faked — the census.** Across 217 emitters: `SubModule` unmapped on **21 of 60**
effects (sub-emitters are not instanced; `BarrageNovaIce`/`BarrageRainIce` each lose 4). Partial
