### 1.2 ⚑ TWO INTEGERS WERE ADMISSIBLE AND THE TIEBREAK WAS STRUCTURAL, NOT NUMERIC

16 centres the mean better. **17 is ODD, and odd is load-bearing here.**

Class is `g % 2` on `g = rev * rate + slot`. At an **EVEN** rate, `g` changes by an even number
between revolutions, so **the parity at a given slot never changes** — and because a cut's bearing is
`a0 + TAU · birth`, the integer revolution contributes a whole turn and **drops out**, so the slot's
BEARING repeats too. An even rate therefore paints **the same class at the same bearing every
0.36 s** in the stationary cadence: a texture that repeats at precisely the timescale A2e named as
the one the eye reads — introduced into **segment A, the reference half of the comparison**, to buy
0.16 of a cut per revolution. **17 declines the trade.**

The undulating cadence is parity-independent (`g` runs continuously across epochs of varying length),
so nothing about the shipped article turns on this — only the reference does.

**The 16 fork is one character and its numbers are in the comment.** ⚑ The alternation declaration
now **derives the words "odd"/"even" from the constant** and states the consequence for whichever
parity ships, so the claim cannot outlive the value that made it true.

## 2 · ITEM 2 — ⚑ THE SCENE DOES NOT REPRODUCE · commit `bf39491`

**There is no clip. Nothing was promoted. Nothing is claimed.**

### 2.1 What happened

The render reached FG-10 and halted: two renders of the identical span disagreed. A2e had fixed
exactly this once already — unpinned `GPUParticles3D` seeds — so the first question was whether the
rate raise had broken something. **It had not.**

| experiment | result |
|---|---|
| d-close, rate **17**, **5 passes** | **3 distinct states**; disagreements confined to frames 16..25 |
| d-close, rate **11** (`a1ece3a` restored, my change reverted) — **4 passes** | **3 distinct states**, frames 16..23 |
| b-ring (a **different tick window**), rate 17, **3 passes** | **3 distinct states**, frames 18..25 |

**The defect is PRE-EXISTING, reproduces at the exact rate Matt ratified, and reproduces on a shot
this cell never touched.** The rate raise is exonerated by measurement, not by argument.

### 2.2 ⚑ WHICH MEANS A2e's PROBE PASSED ON A COIN FLIP

`N` was **two**. Two passes can only falsify a defect that makes a random *pair* disagree more often
than not; against two or three near-equiprobable states, a 2-pass gate is close to a coin toss, and at
A2e it came up heads. **The A2e clip on Matt's desk cannot be regenerated from its manifest**, and its
landing's § 0 claim to the contrary is **withdrawn here**.

This is NOTE-58 one level up. A2e's law was *assert determinism at the ARTIFACT, not only at the layer
you built*. The sequel: **assert it enough times to see a distribution.** One comparison of two
samples is not a determinism test, it is an anecdote.

### 2.3 What is characterised — measured, not inferred

* **Bounded band.** Frames before ~16 and after ~26 **always** agree, on both shots. Only a middle
  band ever disagrees.
* **Sub-frame in magnitude, and the obvious hypothesis is REFUTED.** If a run were simply offset by
  one frame, variant X at frame N would match variant M at N±1. It does not: mean |Δ| is **1.03** at
  offset 0 against **3.5–5.3** at ±1, where one frame of scene change is ~3.6. This is not a
  whole-scene time shift.
* **Largest single differences are a body's pose and silhouette** — an armoured dweller, head and
  shoulder plates in visibly different positions between variants. The cut arcs also differ, at lower
  magnitude (mean |Δ| 6.6 on hot pixels vs 3.1 elsewhere), consistent with glow responding to a
  changed scene rather than to a changed cut layout.
* **The cut layout is untouched by any of it** — a pure function of the tick, proven five ways at the
  layout level and re-proven at rate 17, including two fresh builds agreeing at the same tick.

### 2.4 What is NOT established, and why I stopped

**The mechanism.** Every layer driven by the engine's own **process clock** rather than seeked from
the tick is a candidate, and I am naming them rather than choosing:

* **`AnimationPlayer` phase.** `kc2_body_anim.gd` calls `player.play(clip, BLEND_S)` on state change
  and `set_active()` on visibility. **Nothing anywhere seeks a body's locomotion clip from the tick.**
  Its phase is a function of how many process steps that player has been active for.
* **`GPUParticles3D` simulation phase.** `FX_SEED` pinned **which** random numbers each system draws.
  It never pinned **when** they are drawn relative to the captured frame. Same seed, different phase,
  different pixels.

⚑ **Isolating it changes what every body looks like in every future frame.** That is a scope
amendment and a presentation change Matt has not seen, and a cell chartered to raise one constant is
the wrong place to make it. **Routed up, not patched down.**

### 2.5 The instrument fix, which IS this cell's to make

A gate I am required to run turned out under-powered — the same shape as A2e's blind seat instrument,
and mine to repair. **FG-10 now renders N passes (default 4), counts DISTINCT STATES, and prints the
exact frame indices that ever disagree.**

```
distinct states across 3 passes: 3    frames that ever disagree: 16,17,18,19,20,21
!!! FG-10 HALT — 3 renders of the same span produced 3 DISTINCT results.
```

Raising N **does not fix the scene** — it stops the gate lying about it. *"Not identical"* is not a
diagnosis, so the band ships with the verdict and the next reader does not rediscover it.

## NOTES (continuing from NOTE-66)

**NOTE-67 — A DETERMINISM GATE WITH N=2 IS AN ANECDOTE, NOT A TEST.** Two samples can only falsify a
defect that makes a random pair disagree more often than not. Against a process with two or three
near-equiprobable states a 2-pass gate is a coin flip, and A2e's came up heads — shipping a clip whose
own manifest claims byte-reproducibility it does not have. **Sample a determinism claim enough times
to see a DISTRIBUTION, report the number of DISTINCT STATES, and print WHICH frames disagree** — "not
identical" is a verdict, not a diagnosis.

**NOTE-68 — PINNING A SEED IS NOT PINNING A CLOCK.** A2e set `use_fixed_seed` on all five particle
systems and both passes then agreed, which looked like a fix and was half of one. A fixed seed
determines **which** random numbers a system draws; it says nothing about **when** they are drawn
relative to the captured frame. Anything advanced by the engine's process step — particle simulation,
`AnimationPlayer` phase — is a function of process history, and process history is not reproducible
across runs. **The tick must be the only clock, which means every layer has to be SEEKED from it, not
merely seeded.**

**NOTE-69 — WHEN A GATE FIRES ON YOUR CELL, FALSIFY YOUR OWN CHANGE FIRST AND IN THE OTHER
DIRECTION.** The cheap move is to argue that a constant raise cannot have caused a body-pose
difference — true, and the same shape of reasoning A2d used to conclude the cut layer was
deterministic. The honest move is to put the old constant back and re-run: four passes at the
ratified rate returned three states, which converts "cannot have" into "did not."

**NOTE-70 — AN EVEN-VALUED RATE COLLAPSES A PARITY THAT A SHIPPED PROSE CLAIM DEPENDED ON.** Class is
`g % 2` on `g = rev·rate + slot`, so an even rate freezes class-at-slot across revolutions — and since
the bearing is `a0 + TAU·birth`, the whole-turn term drops out and the BEARING freezes with it.
Raising a rate is never only a magnitude change: **check what the value's arithmetic PROPERTIES were
quietly carrying**, and make any prose that leans on them compute the word instead of typing it.
