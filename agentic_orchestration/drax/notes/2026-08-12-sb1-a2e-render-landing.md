## 0 · THE THING TO READ FIRST

⚑ **EVERY STILL THIS PROJECT HAS EVER SHIPPED WAS ONE DRAW FROM A DISTRIBUTION.**

`GPUParticles3D` picks a **random seed at construction** unless told otherwise, and this scene has
five of them — the smoke haze, three spark emitters and the ember garnish. Nothing in A2b-r, A2c or
A2d ever rendered the same span twice, so nobody found out. A2e's FG-10 probe leg renders 45 frames
**twice** and digest-compares them, and it **HALTED THE FIRST RENDER ATTEMPT**: the two passes
disagreed in **every single frame**.

```
pass 1  ab66642dc7093510993d096c1c19e539107921c8bb588343b5366dc393d9b509
pass 2  298221a0eec5945f794863dc9811909a8acc0b127fe93d2b5bb0c688d2601faf
```

A2d asserted the cut layer's determinism **five ways** and all five held — banned-token scan, no
shader clock, whole-pool state across a tick round trip, two fresh builds, non-periodicity. **Not
one of the five looked at a particle system.** The claim was true and the artifact was not.

Fixed: `use_fixed_seed` + a declared seed on all five, from one `FX_SEED` block. After the fix both
passes return `d8835455ce228e6f5245ece98cc51e36e1aa32447242702c3e91df68cb119ba8`. A smoke row now
fails if any particle system is ever left unpinned again.

**What this does and does not invalidate:** the geometry, pose, cut layout and measured blocks of the
earlier stills were always deterministic and remain valid. What was NOT reproducible in those frames
is the **particle content** — which spark was where, which smoke puff was where. Those frames cannot
be regenerated byte-identically from their manifests. Frames from this cell forward can.

### 1.1 Two defects, both found by measuring

1. **THE FIRST DRAFT OF THE ASSERT FOUND ZERO CONSUMERS AND SAID SO IN A PASS-SHAPED SENTENCE.**
   Rooted at `fx`, it found the smoke haze and nothing else — the bursts hang off `body_holder` and

2. **EVENLY-SPACED STOPS MISSED THE RAMP BY 0.087 — twenty-two 8-bit colour steps.** `a = life^0.55`

   and paints the head the wrong colour. Stops are laid evenly on the **decayed axis** instead, where
   the function actually moves, and one more is pinned **exactly on the knee** because the knee is a

| stops | spacing | worst deviation |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | uniform | **0.08694** |
| 12 | decayed | 0.02955 |

not tuned away: no finite piecewise-linear gradient reproduces an unbounded slope. The
shortest-lived consumer is the 0.20 s ember, whose **first 30 fps frame lands at life 0.167** —

## 2 · ITEM 1 — THE UNDULATION (R-CPB-14) · commit `8159aff`

> *"The sequence should begin again, linearly (horizontally) at rather random intervals so that the
> circle of laser-cuts/claws/sparks seems to undulate/morph naturally."*

**WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG, AS A PROPERTY.** A2d's cadence was **statistically stationary**: eleven
births every revolution forever, ~5 cuts alive at every instant, uniformly scattered, always. The
jitter made the pattern unpredictable in **detail** and perfectly predictable in **texture** — and
texture is what the eye reads at 0.36 s/rev. **Matt did not ask for more randomness; the intervals
were already random.** He asked for the randomness to have **structure at a longer timescale**.

So the births are organised into **EPOCHS**: a sequence of 5..13 cuts runs at the ruled 11/rev rate,
**ENDS**, and **BEGINS AGAIN** after a seeded gap of 0.10..0.40 rev.

### 2.1 ⚑ THE RE-ANCHOR IS A TIME SHIFT, NOT AN ANGLE OFFSET, AND THAT IS FORCED

*"Begin again, linearly (horizontally)"* is an angular re-anchor, and the obvious implementation —
add a seeded angle to the epoch's cuts — **would draw burn marks where the hammer never was.** A2d
established that these cuts are world-fixed **because** they are marks left where the steel touched,
so a cut's bearing is `a0 + TAU · birth` and is **not a free parameter**. The only honest way to move
where a sequence begins on the circle is to move **when** it begins.

The gap is therefore the re-anchor, in the one currency that keeps every cut honest, and the
**realized** anchor is *reported* rather than authored — 199.7°, 79.8°, 114.0°, 184.1°, 38.1°,
wherever the hammer happens to be when the next sequence starts.

### 2.2 The schedule is re-summed from epoch zero on every call

It is a cumulative sum and could have been carried between frames — which would make the pattern
depend on **which ticks were visited**, the exact residue NOTE-55 caught in the pool's dark slots.
Re-summing costs one cheap loop and buys *same tick, same frame* outright: there is no state to be
stale.

### 2.3 Two of my own numbers were wrong, and the rows found both

1. **THE BAR ON NON-STATIONARITY WAS A NUMBER I INVENTED.** "×16 on the variance" came out of my head,
   not out of anything Matt said, and it **missed at ×14.1** — at which point the temptation is to
   lower it until it passes. Re-derived from the **visible** claim instead: he asked for the ring to
   thicken and thin, and for a count *"visibly"* is a **ratio**. Bar is now thick/thin ≥ 2
   undulating **and** < 1.5 stationary.
2. **`CUT_EPOCH_GAP_HI_REVS` WAS 0.52 ON A DERIVATION THAT MISSED A WHOLE BIN.** The silence between
   sequences is **not** the gap: the last birth sits half a bin before the sequence ends and the
   first birth of the next half a bin after it starts, so `silence = gap + (1 + jitter)/rate`. At
   0.52 that is 0.661 rev and left the ring **fully dark for two frames** — I had computed a quarter
   of one. The row measured the **rendered window** and disagreed. At 0.40 the worst blackout the
   seed can produce is 0.033 s, **exactly one frame**. A ring that switches off reads as a bug, not
   as a breath.

## 3 · ITEM 2 — SCALE 1.95, AND A BLIND SEAT INSTRUMENT · commit `9affc6c`

### 3.1 ⚑ THE CONTACT INSTRUMENT WAS BLIND ON THE RIGHT HAND, AT BOTH SCALES

The two-sided seat bar is {burial below noticing} **AND** {hands still in contact}. The contact half
was `r − env[station][azimuth]`, a max-radius-per-bin grid over 20 × 24 bins. **Bins with no weapon
vertex hold −1 and are skipped** — so a hand whose vertices all land in empty bins never updates its
clearance and keeps its `1e9` sentinel. At `GRIP_FRAC 0.20` the **RIGHT HAND — the one the hammer
hangs off — scored exactly that**, at 1.65 *and* 1.95:

```
seat 0.10 -> buried 2  deepest 0.00427  gapR +1000000000.00000  gapL -0.01527   LAW true
```

And `min` over the two hands meant *"at least one hand touches"*, which for a **baseball grip** is
the wrong quantifier: R-CPB-6 ruled **both** fists onto the haft.

So A2d's *"exactly one of seven seats satisfies both"* was **a one-hand measurement**, and at 1.95 it
made the bar look **unsatisfiable** — every seat from 0.00 to 0.16 failed contact. It is not
unsatisfiable. The instrument was.

Contact is now the **exact distance from each skinned hand vertex to the nearest weapon triangle**
(Ericson region test), signed negative where the parity test says the vertex is inside, and the law
binds on the **worst** hand. No bins, no holes. The retired envelope number is kept and reported and
**disagrees exactly as NOTE-47 requires**: `+0.0600` where the exact one measures `−0.0112`.

## NOTES (continuing from NOTE-57)

**NOTE-58 — A DETERMINISM CLAIM ASSERTED ONLY ON THE LAYER YOU SUSPECT IS A CLAIM ABOUT YOUR
SUSPICIONS.** A2d proved the cut layer deterministic five ways and every one held. None looked at a
`GPUParticles3D`, which Godot seeds randomly by default, so two renders of the same span disagreed in
every frame. **Assert determinism at the ARTIFACT — the pixels — and not only at the layer you
built.** The only reason it was ever caught is that this was the first cell to render one span twice.

**NOTE-59 — AN UNWRITTEN SENTINEL IS A MEASUREMENT THAT NEVER HAPPENED, AND `min` HIDES IT.** The
contact clearance initialised to `1e9` and was only updated inside a binned envelope with holes; the
right hand's vertices fell in empty bins at every grip station ≥ 0.20, so its clearance stayed `1e9`
— and `min(left, right)` silently handed the whole predicate to the left hand. **Print the sentinel.
Better: make an unwritten sentinel FAIL.**

**NOTE-60 — A BAR YOU INVENT IS A BAR YOU WILL LOWER.** "×16 on the variance" came out of my head,
missed at ×14.1, and left exactly two options: lower it, or re-derive it. **Derive the bar from the
VISIBLE claim** — Matt asked the ring to thicken and thin, so the bar is a thick/thin **ratio** with
a stated perceptual reading, not a moment of a distribution chosen after seeing the data.

**NOTE-61 — WHEN A CONSTANT IS DERIVED FROM ANOTHER, WRITE THE DERIVATION *AND THEN MEASURE IT*.**
`CUT_EPOCH_GAP_HI` was set against the persistence "because 0.52 − 0.45 is small". The real silence
is `gap + (1 + jitter)/rate` — a whole bin wider — and the ring went dark for two frames instead of a
quarter of one. The arithmetic was in a comment; the row measured the **rendered window** and won.

**NOTE-62 — REACH IS AFFINE IN WEAPON SCALE, NOT PROPORTIONAL TO IT.** The A2d blocker priced 1.95 by
multiplying: `1.9593 × 1.95/1.65 = 2.3156`. The man does not scale; only the haft outboard of the
fists does. Measured: 2.2003. **A ratio applied to a quantity with a fixed offset is a wrong number
that looks like arithmetic** — and it reached a ledger row.

**NOTE-63 — A PROBE SCALE TYPED BESIDE THE SHIPPED SCALE EVENTUALLY *BECOMES* THE SHIPPED SCALE.**
The weapon-truth row hard-coded 1.95 as its second scale; the moment 1.95 shipped it compared a body
with itself and printed `x1.0000` as a PASS. **Derive the probe from the thing it is probing.**

**NOTE-64 — IN A COMPARISON, THE TOGGLE MUST BE ONE BOOLEAN IN ONE BINARY.** Two builds asked to
agree is not a comparison; it is two renders and a hope. Everything else — camera, window, seed,
baton — is then *verifiable as identical* off the sidecars rather than promised in prose.

**NOTE-65 — macOS SHIPS bash 3.2, WHERE `${ARR[-1]}` EXPANDS TO THE EMPTY STRING.** A negative array
index in the render script would have applied the dip to **no shot at all** and produced a seamless
concatenation that looked deliberate. Caught before the first render; the shell has a comment saying
why the index is computed the long way.

**NOTE-66 — A GRADIENT IS PIECEWISE-LINEAR AND A GAMMA RAMP IS NOT.** Sampling a curve onto a
`Gradient` with evenly-spaced stops chords hardest exactly where the curve is steepest. Sample on the
axis the curve is linear in, pin a stop on every corner, and **report the residual with the reason
it cannot be zero** instead of adding stops until an invented bar passes.
