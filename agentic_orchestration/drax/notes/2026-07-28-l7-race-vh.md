## §0 — TASK 0: the corner-torch stage dressing (R-LR-2, not judged)

### §0.1 What it is

Matt, verbatim: the two lit corners *"probably need a fire VFX or a torch … to indicate why those
corners are lit."* PC-LIGHT left that debt NAMED in its own source comment (finding **L-2**: *"four
lights nothing in the room makes — CEILING-1's own pathology in miniature"*). Task-0 discharges it.

Authored ONCE by the stage, **frozen**, inherited verbatim by V-H / V-PRO / V-MUR. It motivates the
warm corners; it does not compete with any arm's brief.

- **Module, measured not preferred** (`scripts/vh_probe_props.gd`): `SM_Prop_Torch_08.fbx`, 2.0434 m
  tall, base at y=0, footprint 0.203 × 0.235. Its head lands 0.26 m under the existing sconce at
  y=2.30 — under a third of the flame's own vertical extent at a −50° camera. Runners-up rejected on
  their numbers: Torch_06/07 (1.23 m — head a metre below the light), Brazier_01/02 (3.67–3.79 m,
  up to 4.68 m wide — furniture), Brazier_04 (1.24 m; bowl at waist height, so the gap to the
  sconce reads).
- **Cost, as a number:** 4 × (34 flame + 10 ember) = **176 particles**, +8 draw calls, against
  L7-V's 673-particle ambient pass. TCP-51 is what makes that cheap — area is the entire cost, and
  these are ~0.30 m sprites.
- **Refactor carried:** `SCONCE_H` (2.30) / `SCONCE_OFF` (7.90) promoted from locals in
  `_build_lighting` to file consts, and both the light loop and the new torch loop read the same
  `_sconce_positions()`. The prop arrives under the light; the light is never dragged onto the prop.

### §0.2 ★ The verification — the "refactor, not retune" claim is PROVED, not accepted

The dead instance's source comment asserts *"VALUES UNCHANGED from B-cold (godot `8caa733`). This is
a refactor, not a retune."* That is a claim about pixels, so it was tested against pixels.

**Test A — byte-comparison against the pre-dressing builder.** Same rig, same camera, same settle,
one render with the working-tree builder at `include_torches = false`, one with `git HEAD`'s builder
(`8caa733`), which has no torch code at all:

| frame | builder | sha256 |
|---|---|---|
| `t0_head.png` | HEAD `8caa733` | `12beadd132d4ea05c4267c9c6deb5efbd0a9f0701ccc93cf98103b177e96754a` |
| `t0_dressed_off.png` | working tree, torches off | `12beadd132d4ea05c4267c9c6deb5efbd0a9f0701ccc93cf98103b177e96754a` |

**BYTE-IDENTICAL.** The +242/−8 diff contributes exactly zero pixels when its one flag is off.
Node count agrees too (813 = 813). That is a stronger statement than any luma tolerance.

**Test B — LSTAT-2 against the B-cold datum of record.** Mask frozen to
`tmp/pclight/frames/before00.png` (72,175 px), the PC-LIGHT convention, unmodified:

| frame | **LSTAT-2 contrast** | p50 | u12 % | masked mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B-cold datum** (`tmp/pclight/stats_final_B.json`) | **6.833235** | 26.887 | 7.149 | 32.7386 |
| `t0_head` (HEAD builder, this cell's rig) | **6.833235** | 26.887 | 7.149 | 32.7386 |

| `t0_dressed_on` — **the shipped stage** | 6.847105 | 26.887 | **7.156** | 32.8474 |

Two things worth separating:

1. **The datum reproduces through a NEW rig.** `t0_head` was taken with `scripts/vh_race_rig.gd`
   (this cell's file, a different scene from `tmp/pclight/light_rig.tscn`) and lands on
   **6.833235** to six decimals — the same number PC-LIGHT shipped. The camera echo is identical
   too: `eye = (15.983573, 27.045511, 14.904923)`, fov 24. The race rig is therefore on the frozen
   photometric path, not merely near it.
2. **The dressing's ENTIRE photometric footprint is +0.20 % contrast** (6.8332 → 6.8471), from the
   additive flame sprites themselves. **p50 does not move at all** (26.887 → 26.887) and u12 moves
   +0.007 pt. Not one lighting property was touched — no light added, moved, recoloured or
   re-energised. B-cold is intact.

**Eye check:** `tmp/l7race/VH/frames/t0_dressed_on.png` (+ 3× crops `_t0_zoom_L/R.png`). The torch
