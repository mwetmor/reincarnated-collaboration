## §1 — GATE G-1: the cone's base pins to the pool

**Tolerance, named before the measurement: ±5 % of the pool's diameter (±0.63 m).**

### 1.1 The construction identity, printed on every build

```

              vs POOL diameter 12.605745 m | residual 0.000000 m (0.0000 %)

```

It is an identity and not a fit: the volume and the pool are the same cone, through the same mask,
from the same `SpotLight3D` transform. It is printed anyway, because a residual nobody prints is a

## §6 — ⚑ Four things I got wrong, and how each was caught

### 6.1 THE SCREEN-FADE OVERRIDE DISABLED THE THING IT OVERRODE, AND A BIT-IDENTICAL CENSUS CAUGHT IT

`sky_screen_fade_span()` tested `override > -1.5 and override <= 0.0` for "explicitly retired" — a
range that **swallows the −1.0 that means NOT SET**. So the screen factor was retired on every build
and the first census after it landed came back essentially **bit-for-bit identical to the census
before it**. That unchanged table is what gave it away: a shader change that measures as *exactly
nothing* is either a no-op or a bug, and it is worth stopping to find out which. Sentinel narrowed
to `[−0.5, 0.0]`.

### 6.2 THE `beamfix_legacy` ARM NEVER REACHED THE ROOMS, AND 600 FRAMES OF NOTHING WERE RENDERED

`wr2_playback.gd` set `beamfix_legacy` on the WR1Level; `wr1_level.gd` did not declare it or forward
it. GDScript raised `Invalid assignment of property 'beamfix_legacy'` **at runtime**, the level never
built, and the before/after watch rendered **600 frames of the bare-arena fallback** — a flat grey
field with the banner on it and no room at all. Caught by looking at a frame instead of at the
"frames: 300" line. `sky2_legacy` and `beamreal_legacy` each needed the identical forwarding line;
the third arm needed it too, and the file's own comment already explained why.

### 6.3 ⚑ AN INHERITED INSTRUMENT DEFECT: BEAM-FIX'S "BEFORE" WAS NOT WHAT BEAM-REAL SHIPPED

`sky_shaft_gain()` and `sky_dust_gain()` had no BEAM-REAL branch — the legacy arm fell through to
whatever `SKY_SHAFT_ENERGY` / `SKY_DUST_ENERGY` currently were. Those constants were **equal to
BEAM-REAL's values when the arm was written** and BEAM-FIX then moved them (0.26 → 0.22, 0.10 →
0.08). So BEAM-FIX's before/after watch showed BEAM-REAL's *geometry* at BEAM-FIX's *gain* — a beam
that never shipped, in the one deliverable whose entire job is to show a beam that did. Found while
adding the third arm. **Fixed:** `BR_SHAFT_ENERGY = 0.26`, `BR_DUST_ENERGY = 0.10`, and every arm now
reads its own frozen constant. **The lesson generalises:** a legacy arm that reads a live constant
will always eventually stop being that legacy.

### 6.4 `git add -A scripts/` SWEPT IN ~140 FILES OUTSIDE THE DECLARED SURFACES

The first commit attempt used `git add -A scripts/`, which staged every untracked file under
`scripts/` — ~140 `.uid` files, a `__pycache__/`, and a pile of unrelated probe scripts from other
cells — alongside the five surfaces this cell declared. **The guard caught it because the guard is a
list**: `git status --porcelain` printed 140 `A ` lines where five `M ` lines were expected. Undone
with `git reset --soft` (working tree untouched) and re-committed by naming the five files
explicitly. The commit of record touches exactly `sky_shaft.gdshader`, `sky_dust.gdshader`,
`kit_replica_level.gd`, `wr1_level.gd`, `wr2_playback.gd` and nothing else.

### 6.5 I NEARLY GRADED G-1 AT THE THRESHOLD THAT PASSED

The first `conebase.py` swept the level and reported PASS at 0.20 and FAIL at 0.02–0.10. The
tempting move is to name 0.20 as "the" level and call the gate green. Instead the sweep was kept in
