### 5.2 Locomotion is blended on a MEASURED ladder, and the ladder is per-body

`scripts/lap1_stride_probe.gd` walks each clip's **planted foot** through its own cycle on the
retargeted, scaled skeleton and reports the implied authored ground speed. Per-body because a stride is
a LENGTH and the two bodies are scaled 0.9883 and 1.5077 — the same walk clip is a different speed on
each. Band edges are the **midpoints between neighbouring implied speeds**, so a body moving at exactly a
clip's own authored speed gets that clip at `speed_scale` 1.0 and slides zero.

| | walk | run | sprint | bands |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| werewolf ×0.9883 | **1.311** | **3.268** | **3.708** m/s | 2.29 / 3.49 |
| golem ×1.5077 | **2.000** | **4.986** | **5.657** m/s | 3.49 / 5.32 |

Declared weakness: contact detection is a height comparison, not a contact solver, so the samples either
side of a foot swap are a stride pretending to be a displacement. They are **excluded by construction**
(only same-foot sample pairs are scored) and the count is printed. **Sprint is the noisiest rung**
(werewolf p25 2.364 / p75 5.322) because the clip has a long airborne phase and the planted window is
short; the median is used and the spread is reported so the number can be distrusted on its evidence.

`Run_F` and `Sprint_F` were unretargeted in this tree; `scripts/lap1_apply_retarget.py` applies the
**byte-identical** block RIVAL-CAST and KT-2 used. Two clips, and only two — an unretargeted clip nothing
plays costs nothing, and a retargeted clip nothing plays is a lie about what the cell needed.

### 5.3 ⚑ FOOT-SLIDE, NAMED WHERE IT IS WORST — and it is not the cadence

R-BR-2 asked for the debt to be named. **The cadence model is not it.** Ground speed = stride × cadence,
so `speed_scale = v / v_authored` reproduces ground speed exactly, and the render's own log says the
clamp essentially never bit: worst overflow **0.118** at v = 0.57 m/s (the LOW side, `CADENCE_MIN`), and
the boss's clamp **never engaged at all**.

**The debt is DIRECTIONAL, and it is enormous** (`measure/foot_slide.json`):

| | player | boss |
|---|---:|---:|
| \|heading − velocity\|, median | **124.87°** | 10.15° |
| ticks over 30° | **94.1 %** | 1.7 % |
| **ticks over 90° — moving BACKWARDS** | **93.2 %** | 0.4 % |
| lateral slide, median / p90 / max | **4.178 / 4.344 / 6.679 m/s** | 0.695 / 0.736 / 1.588 |
| speed, median / max | 5.092 / 6.745 m/s | 3.943 / 4.025 |

**The player KITES.** Verified rather than inferred: his `heading_rad` points at the boss within 30° on
**97.9 % of ticks** (median 10.37° off the bearing to the boss, `measure/heading_semantics.json`), so
the heading convention is "facing the target" and the consumer's mapping is right — he really is running
away while facing it. **Only forward clips are bound, so he moonwalks for 93 % of his moving ticks at a
median 4.18 m/s of pure lateral slide.** That is the single worst thing in the watch and it has a named,
cheap fix: the goblin Sidekick set ships eight strafes and two backstrafes per gait, **all on disk, all
unretargeted**; binding them and picking by the heading-velocity angle retires the debt. Not done here —
it is a clip-set expansion, not a Lap-1 clause.

   obviously wrong thing in the frame. **Caught by looking at the collapse keyframe**, not by any number
   this cell computed. Fixed with a generated radial falloff built in code (no asset dependency a
   licence-gitignored pack could take away).
3. **Every burst was drawn at the WORLD ORIGIN.** I set `root.global_position` **before** `add_child`,
   which is a no-op outside the tree. Godot said so — `Condition "!is_inside_tree()" is true` — **19
   times**, and the frames did not, because an impact that lands off-screen just looks like no impact.
   **Third defect in this run caught by scanning a log rather than by looking at a picture** (TELL-DRESS
   §6.4, ROOM-DRESS §4.1, now this). Every deliverable was re-rendered at the fixed revision; the shipped
   log carries **0** `SCRIPT ERROR` / `Parse Error` / `SHADER ERROR` / `is_inside_tree`.
4. **My second readability instrument was also wrong, and a picture caught it too.** The ship-vs-ghost
   difference set is body ∪ own-shadow, because hiding a body removes its shadow as well. Superseded by
   the ρ split; both refuted files are kept in `measure/` with `supersedes` fields naming what they are.
5. **My first wait-loop watched a directory that already had 400 files in it**, so I reported a render
   complete that was still in flight and read a partial frame set. Recorded because it is the same class
   as a null measurement wearing a PASS.
6. **`--headless --import` silently rewrote `project.godot`**, stripping
   `[rendering] mesh_lod/lod_change/threshold_pixels=1.0` (the editor prunes a value equal to its own
