== caveats ==
iOS texture memory: ~45 MB first download and the 6 big parallax/tile textures import as lossy WebP q0.9;
  unchanged from web6, which Matt ran on device. The seven dummy sprites add 209 KB to the pck — negligible.
Android 4096 px: parallax/tiles/tile_0_0.png is 4096x4096, at the GLES3 max-texture-size floor. Devices that
  report exactly 4096 are fine; anything lower would drop that tile. Unchanged from web6 — no new large texture.
Single-threaded build (no SharedArrayBuffer), so no COOP/COEP headers are needed on Vercel.
