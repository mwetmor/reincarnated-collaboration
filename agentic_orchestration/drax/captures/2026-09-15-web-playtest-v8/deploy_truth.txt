== caveats ==
Unchanged from web7: iOS texture memory ~45 MB first download, the six big parallax/tile
  textures import as lossy WebP q0.9, and parallax/tiles/tile_0_0.png is 4096x4096 (at the
  GLES3 max-texture-size floor; devices reporting exactly 4096 are fine).
The necromancer prop adds 20156 B of source PNG — it is 70x130, well under the 2048 px
  lossy threshold, so it imports lossless. No new large texture in this build.
Single-threaded build (no SharedArrayBuffer), so no COOP/COEP headers are needed on Vercel.
