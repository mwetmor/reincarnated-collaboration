#### A. The clear-shell timing-floor — a METRIC-DOMAIN artifact, resolved by a domain GUARD (T1.1 collapsed into T1.3)

- **The ruling (gandalf, `gandalf/notes/2026-06-21-T1.1-magnitude-halt-ruling-metric-domain-not-magnitude.md`):** `observed_kpm = kills/(duration_s/60)` is **undefined for a sub-second clear**. A greedy caster capstone one-shots a 3–4-mob blue/elite pack in 4 ticks (0.4s, TICK_SIZE=0.1) → `4/(0.4/60) = 600`. The 600 is `1/tick-floor`, not a throughput. gamora proved first-hand (recompose-first, pre-registered prediction then falsified) that **no constant HP re-scale can move it**: the caster cohort is internally bimodal in burst, so any `H` large enough to de-saturate the high-burst tail drives the caster median below the build-spend floor (INVERSION) and craters elite_pack into a mass zero-clear. **T1.1's scoped fix (a constant) is correctly abandoned — none exists, and per the genre call none is wanted** (a geared caster vaporizing blue trash is THE ARPG caster power-fantasy; inflating trash HP into sponges is the D4-launch defect both GGG and Blizzard spent years walking back). **T1.1 collapses into T1.3.**
- **The fix (gamora, T1.3-A; math-note `simulation/math/clear-shell-domain-guard-and-reband-2026-06-21.md`; tag `gamora/v-clear-shell-domain-guard-1`, engine `02467b3`, collab `ccb6b80`):** a metric domain-of-validity GUARD at the band layer. `T_min` derived first-hand from the tick math (`TICK/T_min ≤ 0.107 → 0.9346s → ceil 10 ticks = 1.0s`); new constant `CLEAR_SHELL_DOMAIN_TMIN_S = 1.0`. Guard installed at `_route_tier_1` (`t4_sim_cycling.py:664`), `band_override is not None` branch ONLY. A sub-`T_min` clear is **out-of-domain for the KPM band**: gated on clear-COMPLETION (`term=b_dead`, no timeout) — PASS/FAIL on "did the pack die, fast" — and **EXCLUDED from the band FIT** so the band is never fit over 600s. In-domain cells (duration ≥ `T_min`) get band + ceiling as before.
- **Clear re-band (in-domain cells only):** magic_pack `(18.61, 100.00)`, elite_pack `(8.26, 28.13)`.
- **Semantic shift declared (Discipline #12):** clear-shell PASS for sub-`T_min` cells now means "**cleared the pack within tick-floor time**" (completion), NOT "KPM in band." Historical sub-second KPM numbers are not comparable to the guarded instrument.
