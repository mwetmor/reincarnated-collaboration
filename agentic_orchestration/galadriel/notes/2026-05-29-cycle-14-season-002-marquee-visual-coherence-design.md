## 0. TL;DR — the picture

Season 002 stages **four factions** with materially distinct visual handles: a **convergence-of-three-elements ranged trio** (Stormcallers — european medieval); a **nine-body mass-engine of crushers** (Ironsoil Vanguard — european medieval); a **broad-fellowship thirteen of wind-water-holy line-holders** (Gale-Blessed Wardens — fantasy_generic medieval); and an **eight-fighter shadow-threaded chain compact** (Duskchain Ranging Compact — fantasy_generic medieval). The first two are doctrine-coded (european-medieval — Triangle Strategy / Octopath / FF Tactics genre stock); the last two are pluralistic-coded (fantasy_generic medieval — Eastward / CrossCode / Sea of Stars territory).

**The marquee succeeds when each faction's group portrait reads in under two seconds as fundamentally different from the other three.** Stormcallers = sky-electric convergence; Ironsoil = ground-mass-weight; Gale-Blessed = wide-front fellowship-light; Duskchain = shadow-margin patience. Per-kit portraits inherit faction palette + element-tempo signature while preserving individual identity (faction-mates must read as kin, not clones).

**Strongest visual-coherence asset:** Ironsoil Vanguard (4.83 / 5 — strongest mono-doctrine clarity); **highest dramatic-staging payoff:** Stormcallers (tri-element convergence is the most cinema-ready of the four); **highest risk:** Gale-Blessed Wardens (13 members + 4 elements + fantasy_generic placeholder = real risk of generic-fantasy-stock-art if prompt construction doesn't anchor wind-primary aggressively).

## 1. Group composition designs — 4 factions

Rubric per faction: **scene framing** (camera + staging) — **member composition** (full-roster vs representative) — **dominant visual element** (the visual handle) — **lighting + atmosphere** (HD-2D treatment) — **cultural lineage signaling** (european vs fantasy_generic medieval) — **faction-distinction** (how it reads against the other three).

### 1.1 Faction 1 — Stormcallers of the Pale Reach (3 members; european medieval; ranged / large-AOE; lightning 33% + shadow 33% + fire 33%)

**Scene framing.** Mid-action, mid-distance stage. Three ranged practitioners arrayed in a loose triangle on a stark, weather-bleached cliff or moorland edge (the "Pale Reach"). Each is mid-cast — arms raised, sleeves and cloaks lifted by released elemental pressure. The viewer's eye reads the triangle as a *converging arc*: lightning crackles down-frame-left, shadow gathers center-rear, fire blooms down-frame-right. The eye-line of each Stormcaller is OUT-OF-FRAME (each looses toward a different unseen target), reinforcing the faction's *wide-arc* doctrine — they do not concentrate fire; they saturate.

**Member composition.** **All three members in-frame.** Three is small enough that omission would feel arbitrary; full-roster reads as canonical. Triangle composition naturally hierarchizes (hero-front-center + two-flank-rear) but per faction-narrative *no member is doctrinally subordinate* — so use loose-triangle, NOT pyramidal hierarchy. Each member roughly equal visual mass.

**Dominant visual element.** **Multi-element-convergence** as the handle (the faction's whole identity is *three elements at equal weight in convergence*). The risk here is choice-paralysis — substrate doesn't pre-commit which element wears the colors. Resolution: **palette tri-band** — frame-left lightning-violet/white, center shadow-indigo/black, frame-right fire-amber/red. The PALE REACH ground/sky stays cool-bleached-grey across the full frame, so the elemental colors sit as the saturation contrast. This is the Pale Reach's *visual logic*: muted ground, saturated convergence.

**Lighting + atmosphere.** Twilight overcast — light source is *the elemental release itself*, not the sun. Strong rim-lighting on each Stormcaller's silhouette from their own element. Long cast shadows from the rim-light interplay (lightning casts cold shadow; fire warm; shadow itself eats light). Ambient is pale-grey-blue (the "Pale" of Pale Reach). HD-2D treatment: hand-drawn pixel sprites at ARPG 100-130 px figure-content; environment in higher-resolution hand-drawn pixel illustration register; element VFX as 6-8-frame stylized animation per § 4 style-register composition.

**Cultural lineage signaling.** European medieval — *armor* not robes; tabards and surcoats with faction heraldry; sallet/bascinet-class helms or coifs for two of three (visible faces for narrative readability); leather + steel + chain construction. Color register pulls from Holy Roman Empire / Burgundian / late-medieval mercenary visual lineage — earth-tone wools, dyed linens, brushed-steel plate. Heraldic device suggestion: a triple-arc sigil (three radiating arcs from a central point — visual signature of the faction's convergence doctrine).

**Faction-distinction.** Reads against the other three as: *the only ranged-only faction* (Ironsoil close; Gale-Blessed close; Duskchain ranged-chain but low-tempo). The convergence-of-three-elements is **uniquely the Stormcallers'** in the season — no other faction carries three elements at equal weight. Visual signature for instant recognition: **the triple-element rim-lighting palette** + **the pale-bleached environment** + **mid-cast staging with sleeves/cloaks lifted by release pressure**.

### 1.2 Faction 2 — Ironsoil Vanguard (9 members; european medieval; close / large-AOE; physical 56% + earth 44%)

**Scene framing.** Mid-engagement, close-distance stage. The Vanguard is captured **mid-charge** or **mid-impact** — bodies in motion forward, weapons in mid-swing or mid-plant, earth/dust kicked up from the wide-arc swings. The camera sits low-angle (looking up at the line) to emphasize *mass* — the faction's whole identity is *bodies-as-terrain*. Background is broken ground, churned soil, perhaps the edge of a ruined wall or earthwork (the "Ironsoil" — soil so iron-rich it darkens to near-black where boots and weapons strike).

**Member composition.** **5-7 representative members in-frame** (NOT all nine). Nine bodies in a tight close-quarters group portrait risks visual-soup; representative subset preserves *mass impression* without sacrificing per-figure legibility. Recommended selection logic for legolas prompt construction: **3 STR melee (the doctrinal core — Breakers/Crushers/Rampart) front-line + 2-3 WIS variable-tempo melee (Wardens/Sages) mid-distance reading the terrain + 1 ranged-WIS (Dustwarden) rear-distance providing the ranged spine.** This staging matches the faction's substrate: STR-dominant front-line, WIS-driven terrain-reading mid, single ranged spine.

**Dominant visual element.** **Earth + iron + ground-mass.** Physical (56%) reads as *raw kinetic* — weapons-and-bodies — while earth (44%) reads as *terrain-as-weapon* — kicked dust, cracked stone, ground tremor visible as motion lines and particulate. Palette: dark earth tones (umber, burnt sienna, ironsoil near-black), brushed steel highlights on weapons + plate, ochre dust haze. Element VFX: pulverized stone, crumbling earth, weapon-impact dust plumes. The faction's identity is **weight** — every visual choice serves *heaviness*.

**Lighting + atmosphere.** Overcast late-afternoon — diffuse warm-grey ambient with the dust-haze catching warm light from a low sun source behind the camera. The dust haze is *load-bearing* visually: it softens the background, isolates the front-line figures in sharper focus, and reads as the faction's *ambient signature*. HD-2D treatment per § 4 — figures at ARPG-genre 100-130 px; environment at higher-resolution hand-drawn pixel illustration; dust + stone particulate VFX as 6-8-frame animation, deliberate-feel per family-pace asset budget.

**Cultural lineage signaling.** European medieval — heavy. Full plate or three-quarter plate for the STR front-line; mail-and-padded-jack for the WIS mid-distance; padded-gambeson + light reinforcement for the WIS ranged spine. Weapons: warhammers, mauls, two-handed swords, halberds, polearms — the *crushing-and-cleaving* weapon family, NOT the precision-piercing family. Heraldic device suggestion: a single broken-iron-anvil sigil OR a furrowed-field sigil — terrain-coded. Visual lineage anchors: late-medieval Burgundian/Swiss/German Landsknecht infantry; the Ironsoil reads as a *vanguard infantry block*, not a knightly order.

**Faction-distinction.** Reads against the other three as: *the only mass-engine* (Stormcallers ranged-thin-triangle; Gale-Blessed broad-fellowship; Duskchain dispersed). The Ironsoil is **the densest cluster in the season** — 9 members compressed into close-quarters geometry. Visual signature: **dust-haze ambient** + **low-angle mass-reading camera** + **physical-and-earth palette absent any bright elemental highlights** (the only faction without a glowing-element signature; the absence reads as *grounded*).

### 1.3 Faction 3 — Gale-Blessed Wardens (13 members; fantasy_generic medieval; close / large-AOE; wind 31% + holy 23% + water 23% + lightning/physical/shadow 8% each)

**Scene framing.** Long-distance / panoramic stage. The Wardens are arrayed across a **wide front** — visually staged as a *line-holding fellowship*, NOT a cluster. The camera pulls back to mid-long-shot to capture the *breadth*: this faction's whole identity is **wide-front coverage**, so the marquee staging must honor breadth over depth. Setting: an open ridgeline, moorland, or hilltop pass — somewhere wind reads as visible (grass bent, banners snapping, cloud-rank moving). The Wardens are **mid-formation** — some mid-stride, some mid-cast, some holding station — capturing the *sustained-tempo* doctrine the faction's flat-tempo majority carries.

**Member composition.** **7-9 representative members in-frame** (NOT all 13). Thirteen in single frame collapses to visual-noise; representative subset preserves *fellowship breadth* without sacrificing legibility. Recommended selection: **3-4 wind-primary close-melee (Windreave / Gale Warden / Dextrous Warden) at frame-center holding the front-line spine** + **2 holy + 2 water as flanking elements** + **1 shadow OR lightning at far flank as the "loose-fit member" the fellowship's pluralistic identity admits**. Stage the wind-primary cluster as the visual anchor (per substrate's 31% wind-dominant element distribution).

**Dominant visual element.** **Wind** as the primary handle. Wind is the hardest element to render statically — resolve via **visible-wind environmental cues** (grass bent in long sweeping curves; cloak/banner/hair lifted in the direction of the gust; airborne particulate — leaves, dust, light spray — caught mid-flight; banner-snap angles all unified). Holy-secondary reads as **rim-light + warm glow** on the holy-aligned wardens (cream/gold rim against the cool wind-blue ambient). Water-secondary reads as **light spray or moisture haze** at the wind-edge of the formation. Lightning/shadow secondary fighters get *subtle* element-color rim-lighting only — they are loose-fit members, not the doctrinal core.

**Lighting + atmosphere.** Midday-clear-with-broken-cloud — broken-cloud is load-bearing because it creates the *dramatic shaft-of-light effect* across the line-holding formation (some wardens in direct sun, others in cloud-shadow — the geometry of the broken light reinforces the *broad-front* visual logic). Ambient is cool-blue-grey (wind-coded); shafts of warm gold-cream light pick out the holy-wardens; the water-wardens carry the cool-blue-spray accent. HD-2D treatment per § 4.

**Cultural lineage signaling.** **Fantasy_generic medieval** — this is the pluralistic-coded faction; resist the temptation to lock to a single real-world historical anchor. Mixed-armor profile: some in plate, some in lamellar, some in robes-with-armored-bracers, some in scaled cloaks. The *fellowship* identity reads as *gathered from many roads* — different-traditions-united-by-shared-doctrine. Color register pulls from fantasy-medieval pluralism: warm earth-tones on robes and leathers, cool steel on armor pieces, accented by elemental palette (wind-blue, holy-cream-gold, water-cyan). Heraldic device: **none unified** — different members carry different personal sigils, signaling the loose-fellowship pluralism. (This is itself a visual signature — the absence of unified heraldry is the visual claim.)

**Faction-distinction.** Reads against the other three as: *the only broad-fellowship faction* (Stormcallers tight-triangle; Ironsoil dense-block; Duskchain dispersed-margin). The Gale-Blessed are **the breadth of the season**. Visual signature: **wide-frame panoramic staging** + **wind-coded environmental motion** + **broken-cloud lighting** + **pluralistic-armor-profile** + **absence-of-unified-heraldry**.

**Risk callout.** This faction carries the highest visual-coherence risk in the season — 13 members + 4 active elements + fantasy_generic placeholder lineage. Without aggressive wind-primary anchoring, the group portrait drifts toward *generic-fantasy-medieval-adventurer-party-stock-art*. **Mitigation:** legolas's prompt construction must front-load wind as the visual primary; broken-cloud lighting; pluralistic-armor; environmental wind cues. Without these, the Gale-Blessed read as the "default fantasy faction" — which is exactly what the marquee must avoid.

### 1.4 Faction 4 — Duskchain Ranging Compact (8 members; fantasy_generic medieval; ranged / chain; shadow 38% + physical 25% + lightning 12% + earth 12% + wind 12%)

**Scene framing.** Mid-distance staged in a **margin-territory** environment — frontier woodland edge, half-light forest verge, ruined-wall threshold, or twilight-mist hollow. The Duskchain are **NOT arrayed in formation** — they are **dispersed across the frame at varying depths**, some near, some receding into shadow. The camera sits at *mid-eye-level* (looking across, not up or down) — peer-perspective with the faction; they are *watchers* and the camera is one of them. Bodies are **mostly still or coiled** — pre-strike, mid-load, the *patient* posture the faction's low-tempo doctrine carries. One or two figures may be mid-release (a single arrow loosed, a single shadow-thread visible) — the *chain* is mid-cascade; the moment is *the load, not the discharge*.

**Member composition.** **5-7 representative members in-frame** (NOT all 8). The Duskchain's dispersed-depth staging means front-figures will be sharper / rear-figures partially-occluded-by-shadow — the *visual count* should read as "maybe 5, maybe 8, hard to tell" which is itself the faction's signature (cascading shadow margins; you don't see who's there). Recommended selection: **2-3 ranged-shadow-primary fighters at varying depths (Duskchain Hexer / Striker of Hollow Margins / Striker of Pale Margin)** + **1-2 ranged-physical (Striker of Gaunt Reach, Striker of Pale Margin str)** + **1 lightning (Stormbreak Ranger of Pale Verge) for color contrast** + **1 wind / earth (Galewatch Striker / Dune Watcher) at deep-frame margin**. Stage the shadow-primary fighters at frame-foreground; the off-element fighters at deeper frame for visual variance.

**Dominant visual element.** **Shadow** as the primary handle — but *shadow as ambient*, not shadow as VFX-burst. The faction's *shadow-threading* doctrine reads visually as **shadow that LEAKS BETWEEN figures** — thin tendrils, drifting darkness, ground-pooling shade — connecting one figure to the next in visible *threads*. Where Ironsoil's earth is *heavy*, Duskchain's shadow is *delicate*. Palette: deep cool blacks, indigo-blues, twilight-violet, sparing slate-grey highlights. Element VFX: shadow-thread tendrils (NOT smoke or fog — *threads*, narrow and intentional, suggesting *connectivity* and *load*). Lightning + earth + wind secondary fighters carry their element only as *thin accent* — a single arrow tip glowing lightning-violet; a single earth-toned arrow-vane; a single wind-snapped cloak edge.

**Lighting + atmosphere.** Twilight or pre-dawn — low ambient overall; horizon line visible with a single warm-pale color band (twilight purple-grey-pink) providing the only color contrast against the cool-shadow palette. The figures are mostly silhouette + rim-lit; faces partially or fully occluded by hoods, masks, or shadow-cast. Mist or low-cloud at ground level reads as the faction's *ambient signature* — the margin is *where vision fails*. HD-2D treatment per § 4 — extra care on the shadow-thread VFX (must read as intentional thin tendrils, not amorphous fog).

**Cultural lineage signaling.** Fantasy_generic medieval — lean into *frontier ranging* visual lineage: hooded cloaks, leather lamellar or studded leather, soft boots, layered scarves and wraps (for the cold/damp margin climate), short bows and crossbows and long daggers (the *ranging* weapon family). No heavy plate; no heraldry; no faction insignia (the faction operates in *grey margins*; visible faction-identification would compromise the doctrine). Color register pulls from frontier-ranger / scout / pathfinder visual lineage — muted earth-and-shadow tones; no bright accents except the elemental thin-accent treatments noted above. Visual lineage anchors: the Witcher (Geralt-coded) for the ranging-frontier mood; Bloodborne hunters for the twilight palette; Mistborn for the shadow-thread visual logic.

**Faction-distinction.** Reads against the other three as: *the only dispersed-formation faction* (Stormcallers tight-triangle; Ironsoil dense-block; Gale-Blessed broad-line). The Duskchain are **the depth of the season** — staged at varying frame depths rather than left-to-right line. Visual signature: **dispersed-depth staging** + **shadow-thread tendrils visible between figures** + **twilight palette with single warm horizon band** + **hooded silhouettes with occluded faces** + **ambient mist at ground level**.

6. **HD-2D-genre-fit** — does the faction's substrate compose to genre-canonical HD-2D imagery (Octopath / Triangle Strategy / Sea of Stars / Eastward / CrossCode)?
