## 3. What this does and does not settle for R-CPB-17b

**Does:** puts all three subjects on one method, so the one-pager can quote a single
scale. Audits and confirms the harness band.

**Does not — and this is the load-bearing caveat:** subject-height fraction
**conflates camera framing with body size**. The two frames photograph *different
bodies*. The 1.37× gap does **not** by itself prove the cameras differ, and parity
would **not** have proved they match. Matt's ruling turns on "zoom/angle looks the
same"; the eye that answers it is his, and the reading is gandalf's. I supply
register only.

I could not recover this frame's camera operands from the footage: the clip contains
a **camera move** (off-modal frames differ across the whole subject band) and the
subject is **static throughout** (frame-160 diff vs a 42-frame median has p50 = 0.0),
so neither differencing nor motion can segment it or solve the camera.
