## 1 · Method (unchanged from P3, restated so the scores are comparable)

`R` = readability at **our** gameplay camera (fixed 2.5D isometric, Godot Forward+/Metal), scored
against drax § 7.5's measured coverage band **0.03 %–67 %**; occlusion of the caster is a defect
(EoR failure #2). `P` = parameterizability (survives Tier-1 recolour / rescale / motif-swap;
identity in geometry/motion/silhouette scores 5, identity in one un-swappable texture scores ≤ 3).
`S` = style-register fit against the locked A-register — **never against source-game polish**.
Each score carries a one-sentence evidence cite. L-19 causality class stated per selection.
**C-1** (non-PoE preferred) applied **at quality ties only**, and named wherever applied.

**Evidence tier**, stamped per candidate: `FRAMES-INSPECTED` / `THUMBNAIL-ONLY` /
`DOSSIER-TEXT-ONLY`. New in this delta: `FRAMES-INSPECTED-BY-EXTRACTION` (I downloaded the master
and cut frames myself) and `TITLE-CARD` (a maxres thumbnail exists but contains **no gameplay** —
promotional art, splash screen or clickbait plate). `TITLE-CARD` is not a slur; it is the honest
statement that the highest-resolution published still teaches nothing about the effect. It is the
same measurement that downgraded `3BnHvNZ_4YM` at L-30.
