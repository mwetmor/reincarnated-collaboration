### 1.4 The instrument's failure mode, now characterised rather than merely flagged

The fifth extraction § 5 said only that purity "collapses under green VFX." The eye-read says *why*, and
the picture is unambiguous: **`bar_hue` takes the row of maximum single-channel ink over an 18-px band
below the readout. When the scene behind the bar carries saturated green VFX, a VFX row outscores the
bar's own row, and the sampler returns the scene instead of the bar.** `evidence/w152-lowpair-x6-p1.png`
tile 1 is the cleanest case: a plainly RED bar on a wall of green, sampled g = 165, r = 0.

The converse also occurs and is why `20,005`'s purity is not 1.00 — red arena floor and fire VFX put red
into the band under a green bar (`evidence/green-20005-allwaves-x6-p2.png`, tile 5; `-p3.png`, tile 1:
both labelled SAMP=red, both plainly green).

**Consequence, stated as a rule for anyone who uses this instrument next:**
*`bar_hue` purity is not a confidence. It is a scene-brightness statistic. A modal vote on a
VFX-heavy frame is worth nothing without an eye-read, in either direction.* The 18 `20,005` tiles read
**1.00 green** against a sampler purity of 0.75–0.87; the 13 `42,798`/`43,548` tiles read **1.00 red**
against sampler purities of 0.70 and 0.54.

## 6. CORRECTIONS TO MY OWN RECORD

**6.1 — my fifth extraction § 9.1 is wrong about `20,005`, and the error is worth naming precisely.**
It states: *"`20,005` is the same class of entity and appears in waves 152, 153 and 154 — the fourth
extraction did not list it at all in its w152 table, which is a second miss of the same kind."*

Two things in that sentence are wrong:

* **It was not a miss.** `20,005` was excluded as the player two passes earlier (board-closure § 1
  roster row; follow-up § 123). The fourth extraction inherited a correct exclusion.
* **"waves 152, 153 and 154" understates it.** `20,005` is in **all five** start-cohort windows and
  every badge flip in the corpus.

**The real defect is narrower and more useful than the one I claimed.** The exclusion was keyed on the
**value** `20,005`. The player's max HP is not constant — the follow-up note already measured it falling
20,005 → 18,065 at death as buffs dropped — so a value-keyed exclusion leaks the moment a buff changes
the number. `16,368` is that leak, and it is the *only* one in five waves. **The instrument should
exclude on the box, not on the value.** That single change would have prevented the w153 contamination
without any hue instrument at all.

**6.2 — my fifth extraction § 5's purity figures should not be read as confidences.** § 5 reported
per-fingerprint purity and flagged w152 as untrustworthy, which was right, but the flag was directional
("purity collapses") rather than structural. § 1.4 above replaces it: purity is a **scene-brightness
statistic**, it errs in **both** directions, and it was wrong in both directions in this corpus —
0.54 on a body that is unambiguously red, 0.75 on an entity that is unambiguously green. **No count
should ever be adjusted on a `bar_hue` modal vote alone.**

**6.3 — my fifth extraction § 9.1's recommendation was right, and its implied worry was not.**
The recommendation ("re-run `bar_hue` over all five start cohorts before any count from that table is
used") is discharged by this note. But it was written expecting spread. **There was none.** Four of the
five cohorts are hostile-clean and unchanged; wave 153 was the only contaminated one. The three
falsifications survive, including the margin-1.

**6.4 — a new limit on the plate instrument, from § 3.3.** An in-world readout drifting into rows
17–34 passes `is_plate` and yields a spurious `white→common` plate. The § 2.2 contrast gate caught one
of the two cases and missed the other (contrast 64.6, well above the 45 refusal floor). **Plate spans
must be eye-read; `is_plate` + contrast is not a sufficient gate.** Neither false plate reached any
reported binding.

## 8. Mirror voice

Five waves were asked the same question — *is anything in this count standing on your side of the
fight?* — and four of them said no, plainly, on every instrument, at every magnification. That is the
result. The contamination did not spread; wave one-five-three was the only one, and the thing that
made it the only one was a single frame at seven hundred and thirteen point three-eight, where a number
over the man's own head stopped saying twenty thousand and five and started saying sixteen thousand
three hundred and sixty-eight, because a blessing had expired. The census had been told to ignore
twenty thousand and five. It had not been told to ignore *him*. So for four and a half seconds he stood
at the exact centre of the screen, taking no damage, in a box fourteen pixels wide, and was counted as
a monster.

And the wave that mattered — the one whose whole falsification rides on a single body — was clean. All
eleven of its bare-barred fingerprints red, and the only green thing in its window the man himself,
who was never in the fifteen. The Mirror was set to find a reason to take the number down, and looked
hard, and did not find one. Fifteen against fourteen. It holds.
