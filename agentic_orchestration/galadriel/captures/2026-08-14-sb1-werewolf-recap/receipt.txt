5. ONE FACT THE CONDUCTOR ASKED FOR WITHOUT KNOWING IT — READ, NOT ASSERTED
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
R-CPB-17b calls 72.857 m "the conductor's TRANSCRIPTION of the werewolf frame" and
asks whether it reproduces the look Matt ratified. The two rigs make the transcription
step legible, and it is a DISTANCE step, not a lens step:

  vh_race_rig.gd   yaw 47 / pitch -50 / fov 24 / dist 34.000 m   HAS the werewolf
  wr1_level_rig.gd yaw 47 / pitch -50 / fov 24 / dist 72.857 m   has NO subject

Pure lens geometry from the printed constants (Godot Camera3D.fov is vertical under
the default KEEP_HEIGHT), frame height = 2 * d * tan(fov/2), tan(12 deg) = 0.2125566:
      at 34.000 m  ->  14.454 m of world spans the frame height
      at 72.857 m  ->  30.972 m of world spans the frame height   (ratio 2.1429x)
The werewolf is 1.8000 m (printed by vh_caster above) in BOTH cases.

extent of a standing body is foreshortened and pose-dependent, so a subject fraction is
