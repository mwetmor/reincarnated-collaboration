A1. CROWN OCCLUSION BY THE CYAN HUD SELF-MARKER BAR.  DECLARED, BOUNDED, NOT

    (1027) / y=425 (1062). On 1027 the head's fur onset is y=416 at x 806-807 and
    y=418 at x 796-797, i.e. the apex sits at the bar's right shoulder. Linear
    interpolation across the eight occluded columns puts a hidden apex no higher
    than y=415. THE CROWN COULD THEREFORE BE 1-2 px HIGHER THAN REPORTED, which
    would move the number of record from 9.889 % to at most 10.111 %. Reported
    both ways; the measured value is the number of record.

A2. THE LOWER-RIGHT DARK MASS ON 1027 — leg, or cast shadow?  RESOLVED TO BODY,
    on chromaticity, with the discriminator stated. The one-sun vector is az 137
    deg / el 66 deg (run log), so a ground shadow would be a darkened sample of
    the floor's own hue. Measured: the mass at x808-816 y496-504 reads r/b
    fraction 0.440/0.267 — fur chromaticity, b well below floor's 0.31-0.34. The
    adjacent mass at x814-820 y504-510 reads 0.350/0.316 — floor. The classifier
    therefore places the boundary at y=505 without my choosing it there.

A3. FRAME 1027's y=422-423 GAP in the fur map.  DECLARED AS A THRESHOLD ARTEFACT,
    not an absence of body: those two rows are the shaded top of the skull, whose
    r-fraction dips below 0.415. The luminance-deviation instrument shows the head
    continuous through them. This is exactly why the chroma map is a BRACKET and
    the manual read is the number.

A4. RIGHT EDGE OF 1027 — 817 or 819?  REPORTED CONSERVATIVELY AT 817. Rows 474-477
    carry fur-classified pixels out to x=819, but that band reads L 157 with
    r-g = 74 — the signature of the red telegraph arc, not of fur (which reads
    L 89-136 everywhere else in this frame). With a L <= 150 guard the rightmost
    unambiguous fur is x=818 at y=476 and x=817 at y=428. 817 is taken. Note this
    affects w_px only; h_frac is untouched.

A5. LEFT-EDGE FALSE POSITIVES AT x 756-759.  EXCLUDED, with the test shown. Those
