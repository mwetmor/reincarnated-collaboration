  kc2_cpb_clip.gd

8. THE SUBJECT BAND — ONE PROJECTION AND ONE MEASUREMENT, KEPT APART
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROJECTION (NOT A MEASUREMENT). The charter's arithmetic: take WW-3a's banked
band of 20.896118 % at k=1.00 and divide by k, i.e. assume apparent height goes
exactly as 1/distance.

    k=0.90 -> 23.218 %      k=0.80 -> 26.120 %      k=0.70 -> 29.851 %

MEASUREMENT (harness geometry, `optics_measured.subject.screen_height_fraction`,
read by projecting the subject through the camera that renders):

    k=1.00    20.896118 %   [WW-3a banked, for the comparison]
    k=0.90    23.300161 %   (projection said 23.218 %, +0.082 pt)
    k=0.80    26.329268 %   (projection said 26.120 %, +0.209 pt)
    k=0.70    30.263668 %   (projection said 29.851 %, +0.413 pt)

THE TWO DO NOT AGREE, AND THAT IS REPORTED RATHER THAN RECONCILED. The 1/k
projection is a thin-subject idealisation; the subject has depth and the camera
is a perspective camera, so its nearer parts gain more than 1/k as the camera
closes. The gap grows monotonically as k shrinks, which is what that explanation
predicts — but this cell did not test that explanation and does not assert it.
Both numbers are stated; neither is a claim.

HONEST BOUNDARY ON BOTH NUMBERS (NOTE-62, inherited from the WW-3a receipt):
these are HARNESS GEOMETRY — the subject's extent projected through the Camera3D
— NOT a pixel measurement of the delivered PNGs. No CV was run on these stills.

    itself is recorded by the engine as 34.8311882019043 where float64 gives

    this run acts on the difference. Declared so nobody later "corrects" a
