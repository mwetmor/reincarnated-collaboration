4. RESOLUTION — REQUESTED vs ACTUAL, AND THE ONE-LOOK DIAGNOSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQUESTED   1600 x 900   (--width 1600 --height 900; BR2W's own resolution)
ACTUAL      1920 x 1080  read from each delivered PNG's IHDR, per rung, in § 6
VERDICT     DIVERGE at all four rungs. Every filename in evidence/ states the
            ACTUAL dims. The divergence is declared here rather than papered over.

THE DIAGNOSIS (one look, then stopped — the ladder is the deliverable):

Godot's Movie Maker (`--write-movie`) locks its encode size to the PROJECT's
viewport size. project.godot carries

    window/size/viewport_width=1920
    window/size/viewport_height=1080

and BOTH per-run overrides were MEASURED not to reach the encoder:

  (a) the scene's own `get_viewport().size = Vector2i(_shot_w, _shot_h)`
      (kc2_cpb_clip.gd:532) — the WW-3a still and the v1 ladder stills are the
      evidence: 1600x900 requested, 1920x1080 bytes.
  (b) Godot's own `--resolution 1600x900` — probe render performed by this cell
      on this host at 4.6.3.stable, metal driver: output IHDR still 1920x1080.

Two independent overrides ignored. So --width / --height DO reach (i) the
Camera3D aspect, `float(_shot_w) / float(_shot_h)`, and (ii) the anchor audit's
px restatement, `delta_frac * vp` — but they never reach the encoder. That is
exactly the shape of WW-3c finding A0 / NOTE-97: a mislabeled resolution poisons
every px-denominated number downstream while leaving every FRACTION-denominated
number correct.

NOT FIXED HERE, AND WHY. Both remedies exceed a still's scope:
  - editing project.godot's viewport size is a GLOBAL change in a shared tree
    that every other harness in this repo renders under; and
  - replacing the still path's capture mechanism would break byte-comparability
    with the banked WW-3a record.
Rendered at the path's actual dims, NAMED TRUTHFULLY FROM THE BYTES, diagnosis
ROUTED UP for disposition by the conductor.

WHAT THE DIVERGENCE DOES AND DOES NOT COST. 1600x900 and 1920x1080 are both
exactly 16:9, and fov_v is VERTICAL under KEEP_HEIGHT, so the FRAMING is
identical and every height FRACTION is invariant. This is a pixel-count fact, not
a composition fact — Matt's eye judgement is unaffected. What it DOES cost:
  - the scene's PL-AUDIT console line prints its px delta "at 1600 x 900" while
    the bytes are 1920 x 1080, so those px are low by 1.2x in x and 1.2x in y;
  - the sidecar's `player_lock_anchor_audit.viewport` key likewise reads
    [1600, 900] on a 1920x1080 render, and its `delta_px` inherits that.
This receipt therefore reports the anchor delta as a FRACTION first (which is
resolution-independent and correct as printed) and restates px on BOTH frames.
The anchor row for these bytes is 0.550925123426649 * 1080 = 595.00, not the
495.83 that a 900-height reading would give.

 1. SINGLE PRECISION, NOT DOUBLE. Godot's Vector3 is float32. Every scaled
    offset and stand-off above is what the ENGINE holds, not the exact float64
    product. Worked example at k=0.70: float64 k*x = 10.3083434104919 where the
    engine holds 10.3083429336548; float64 |k*o| = 24.3818319822206 where the
    engine holds 24.3818302154541 — ~5e-7 m on a component, ~2e-6 m on the
    stand-off. Declared so nobody later 'corrects' a receipt number against a
    calculator and reports a drift that is a float32.
 2. THE ANCHOR DELTAS ARE AT FLOAT32 NOISE, NOT AT ZERO. Near 0.5 a float32 ULP
    is 5.96e-8; the deltas above are 2-4 ULP. 'Invariant by construction' is a
    geometric claim and the measurement is consistent with it to the precision
    the arithmetic has. It is not a claim of exact bitwise invariance.

    no side-by-side against BR2W was produced or scored by this cell. § 8 is a
