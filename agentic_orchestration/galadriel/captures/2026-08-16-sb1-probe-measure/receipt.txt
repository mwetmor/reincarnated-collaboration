2. METHOD
================================================================================

2.1 CHROMA REFERENCE
    magenta-ness   m = min(R, B) - G        on 8-bit sRGB, no colour conversion,
                                            no normalisation, no gain
    The probe's albedo is pure (1,0,1) and unshaded, so m saturates at 255 over
    the box body while scene content sits at m < 16. Three thresholds are
    carried end to end rather than one being chosen in advance:

        tight     m >= 200    near-pure box only
        nominal   m >= 128    the 50%-coverage antialias convention
        generous  m >=  40    any appreciable magenta above the noise shelf

    Threshold floor READ off the frame, not guessed (GL-17). The m histogram in
    both stills is bimodal with an empty shelf: k1000 has 108 px in m∈[16,40)
    and k0665 has 145; below 16 the frame's ordinary content begins. At m>=40
    each frame yields EXACTLY ONE connected component and zero stray islands;
    at m>=20 it yields 3 (k1000) and 6 (k0665), including a singleton 145 px
    away at x=1127. The floor is therefore placed at 40.

    Core sanity: at m>=200 the median pixel is (255, 0, 255) in both stills;
    R range 219–255 / G 0–46 / B 219–255 (k1000), R 216–255 / G 0–47 /
    B 215–255 (k0665).

2.2 PRIMARY CHROMA = GENEROUS (m >= 40).  Declared and justified, not assumed.
    Scene smoke is drawn over the lower half of the box in both stills. It
    attenuates the box's chroma without destroying the silhouette boundary. The
    consequence is visible on the contour plates: the tight contour PEELS OFF
    the straight wall below the smoke line and traces a spurious inward V that
    has nothing to do with box geometry; the nominal contour does the same, less
    severely. The generous contour tracks the straight wall through the smoke.
    All three are reported; generous is the read.

    Two independent corroborations that this is the right call, not a convenient
    one: (a) at the TOP of the box, where there is no smoke, all three contours
    coincide to the pixel; (b) under generous chroma the two walls agree in
    height within 1 px in both stills, which two nearly-equidistant vertical
    edges of one box must — under nominal they disagree by 6–7 px, under tight
    by 8 px in k1000.

2.3 EDGE IDENTIFICATION
    The box is world-axis-aligned and viewed corner-on under a pitched camera,
    so the silhouette is a hexagon: top apex, two near-vertical side walls, two
    slanted top-face edges above them, two slanted ground-face edges below, and
    a bottom (nearest-corner) ground apex. The PRIMARY instruments are the two
    side walls — the leftmost and rightmost near-vertical boundaries.

    For every row y the boundary is walked: x_left(y) = min magenta x,
    x_right(y) = max magenta x. The wall is the PLATEAU in that profile.

2.4 CORNER LOCATION — read, not fitted
    Local boundary slope dx/dy over a +/-4 row window. A true vertical box edge
    is flat to hundredths of a px/row; the top-face and ground-face silhouette
    edges run at ~1.1–1.4 px/row. Three values per endpoint:

        TIGHT     innermost row unambiguously on the flat wall
                  (first/last row with |slope| <= 0.15 px/row)
        READ      the half-slope crossing — the row at which |slope| first
                  passes half the adjacent slant magnitude.  PRIMARY.
        GENEROUS  outermost row still plausibly on the wall
                  (last/first row before |slope| reaches 0.9x the slant)

    Under a symmetric window the half-slope crossing lands on the corner row for
    a sharp corner. Each automated READ was reproduced by hand off the printed
    slope table before it was accepted (k1000 left: slant 1.25, half 0.625;
    |slope| = 0.62 at y=528 and 0.75 at y=527, so READ = 527 — matching the
    instrument exactly). Corners were then LOOKED AT on ruler crops at 9x–18x
    with per-pixel contour overlays before any number was believed.

    NOTE on the two senses of tight/generous, kept separate throughout:
    (a) chroma threshold — the m cut; (b) endpoint bracket — the row bracket at
    a fixed chroma. The brief's "TIGHT reading + GENEROUS bracket" is sense (b),
    delivered in § 3. Sense (a) is the method-sensitivity sweep in § 5.

2.5 OCCLUDER HANDLING
    An excursion of the wall off its plateau by > 2 px that returns within 12
    rows, and that lies strictly between the first and last plateau contact, is
    logged as a NOTCH, excluded from the slant statistics, and never repaired.
    Interior holes (scene objects drawn over the box's middle, not its edge) are
    counted and declared but cannot affect a row's min/max x.

2.6 EXCLUSION BY CHROMA
    The fighter and all FX are excluded by construction: only magenta pixels are
    ever considered. Verified: no non-magenta object touches either right wall
    in either still, and the objects that DO touch the left wall are the notches
    of A2.

    recorded here so the correction is auditable rather than invisible. The
    corrected read was then hand-verified against the printed slope table.

C1  NADIR LEAN, 4 of 4 walls correct in sign. Every left wall's x INCREASES
    descending and every right wall's x DECREASES descending, by 1–3 px. That is
