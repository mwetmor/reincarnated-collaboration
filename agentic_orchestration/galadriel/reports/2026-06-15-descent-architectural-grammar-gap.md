## 0. METHOD CAVEAT — read this first (it governs every number below)

**This is a PRESENCE / COHERENCE review, NOT a pixel-similarity review.** Three reasons, all load-bearing:

1. **Different camera framings.** The reference frames are hand-art marketing shots at a low, near-horizontal hero angle that *foregrounds* one pier→arch bay and looks *along* an arcade. Our iter3 frames are a steep diagonal spine shot + near-top-down per-zone grabs that look *down into* enclosed rooms. The reference camera is composed to show the building's storeys side-on; ours is composed for combat readability from above. **You cannot score pixel-similarity or composition-match across these framings** — and you should not. The question gandalf asked is not "do these look alike" but "is the architecture our build PRESENT and COHERENT as a built structure, by the same grammar the reference obeys." That is what I score.

2. **Edge-density / histogram instruments are the WRONG tool here (Discipline #4).** I ran an objective Sobel-band probe (`pipeline/arch-grammar-band-probe.{mjs,json}`) as *backing*, and it confirms the caveat rather than scoring the dimension: our iter3 upper-band edge densities are often **ABOVE** the reference (zone4 0.294 vs ref-mean ~0.18) — because our dense floor-and-perimeter dressing clutter generates edges. Edge density measures **busyness**, not **built-coherence**. The probe's one discriminating signal is the **arcade-rhythm periodic-peak count** (a regular colonnade/arcade/balustrade produces periodic autocorrelation side-lobes; scattered verticals do not) — reference frames score **0–4 periodic peaks (5 of 8 ≥1; cathedral/timber/graveyard frames 2–4)**; our iter3 scores **0–1 (5 of 6 frames = 0)**. That single number backs the load-path and arcade gaps; everything else is a defensible manual inventory read against the rule, which is the correct gate.

3. **The score is a manual architectural-inventory read, paired with the objective backing where it discriminates.** Each dimension below: a 0–5 PRESENCE/COHERENCE score for the reference (the target) and for our iter3 (current), a quantified gap (% of reference presence achieved), and a one-line evidence cite naming the specific frame + location. Reproducible-by-inspection: another galadriel-instance, given these frames + this rule, lands the same dimension scores within ±1.

### 2.1 Per-dimension evidence detail

**(a) Coherent load-path read — iter3 1.5/5, gap −70%.**
What the picture shows: every chamber (zone0/zone1/zone2/zone4 top-downs) rings the annulus with **vertical elements standing free on the floor against a flat wall plane** — columns, column-drums, vine-wound pillars, occasional arch-fragments. They read as *scattered decorative verticals placed near the wall*, not as a *load path*. There is no point in any frame where you read "pier rises → arch springs from it → wall infills between → this carries that." The 1.5 (not 0) credits that the perimeter columns at least sit against the wall mass (some read as quasi-engaged), and the wall ring itself is a coherent bounded enclosure. But the §4 load-path test ("if this were stone and gravity were on, would it stand, and is it doing a job?") **fails** for the verticals: they carry nothing. Backing: the arcade-rhythm probe finds no periodic organization (0–1 peaks) — objective corroboration that the verticals are scattered, not structured.

**(b) Arcade / colonnade presence — iter3 0.5/5, gap −89%.**
What the picture shows: there is **no arcade** in any iter3 frame — no run of arches each springing column-to-column in a regular rhythm forming a see-through bounding screen. zone1_arcane has the most arch-shapes (top edge), but they are discrete shapes sitting on the wall-top, not a continuous column-to-column run (see §3 falsifier F2). The 0.5 credits zone1's arch-cluster as a faint gesture toward "arches near the top of a wall." The reference's defining inner-bounding move — Matt's "separate 'wall' set… built as if it was actually made as a coherent structure" — is **absent**. This is the single largest gap and the highest-value second-wave target (§4).

**(c) Storey / gallery presence — iter3 0.5/5, gap −89%.**
What the picture shows: every chamber is a **single-storey box**. There is no raised walkway, no balustraded gallery edge, no upper inhabited level with set-pieces (statuary / votive arrays / banners) above the fighting floor. The prior re-score's §4 mentions "gold lanterns on the upper gallery" for zone4 — on re-inspection for *this* dimension, that reads as lanterns high on the wall, not a true raised **storey** you could stand on; there is no balustrade edge and no walkable upper level. The 0.5 credits the wall-height grandeur (the four-sided full-height walls from Change A give vertical scale) — but height is not a storey. Per gandalf §5, "grandeur is not tall walls; it is that the building has floors." We have tall walls and zero floors. Gap −89%.

**(d) Arch-as-window motivating the god-rays — iter3 1.0/5, gap −80%.**
What the picture shows: god-rays / lit shafts ARE present (register-2 lighting gate PASSES 6/6 in the re-score), so light-drama exists. But the light does NOT enter **through arched openings cut into wall planes**. In the establish frames the shafts read as ambient/overhead and through the camera-faded near wall; in the per-zone top-downs the brazier/pyre glow is floor-level point-source. No frame shows a window-arch (masonry continuing above + beside an arched opening) with a light shaft passing through it — the reference's god-ray motivator (ref_3 window row; ref_6/#65 window). The 1.0 credits that lit shafts exist at all. The gap is that they are **un-motivated by architecture** — light falls into the box rather than entering through built openings. Closing (d) is coupled to (b)/(c): window-arches live in wall planes and gallery-storey clerestories.

## 4. RANKED RESIDUAL — what to fix FIRST (the second-wave target, most-off-reference first)

Ranked by **gap magnitude × buildability-leverage** (a fix that closes multiple dimensions at once ranks higher). This is the target for Drax's second wave, in priority order:

1. **Build the ARCADE (dimension (b), −89%) — highest value, closes (a)+(b)+(e:F2) together.**
   A regular arch-on-column run as an inner bounding screen inside the outer wall ring (gandalf §3b) is the single move that does the most: it *converts* orphan uprights (F1) into arcade members carrying the run, it *establishes* the coherent load path (a) by definition (every arch lands on a column at both feet), and it *absorbs* the zone1 silhouette arches (F2) into a real column-to-column rhythm. The objective backing predicts the win: a real arcade will register periodic peaks (2–4, like the reference) where iter3 reads 0. **Build this first** — it is the largest gap AND the highest-leverage fix.

2. **Build at least one STOREY (dimension (c), −89%) — the true source of grandeur.**
   A lower-arcade → upper-gallery relationship (gandalf §5): the arcade from (1) carries a raised gallery with a balustrade edge, reached by the stairs we already have, with set-pieces (statuary / votives / banners) on it so it reads inhabited. Kept in `nonpassable_dressing`, outside the playable footprint (combatants never go up). This is gandalf's "grandeur is floors, not tall walls" — we currently have tall walls and zero floors, so this is the biggest *qualitative* leap available. Ranks just under the arcade because it *depends* on the arcade (the storey sits on it) and is the larger build.

3. **Convert wall-top silhouette arches → WINDOW-IN-WALL openings (dimension (d), −80%; falsifier F2).**
   Drop the zone1 (and any other) wall-top arches INTO the wall plane as openings with masonry above + beside, positioned where the establishing camera's light shafts pass through them — so the god-rays (which already PASS register-2) become **architecturally motivated** (light enters THROUGH built openings). This closes (d) and clears F2 in the same move, and it is cheaper than (1)/(2) because the arch assets are already placed — they need repositioning into the wall, not new construction.

4. **Resolve the remaining orphan uprights / floating arches (dimension (e): F1 residual, F3, F4).**
   Whatever F1 columns the arcade (1) doesn't absorb → make **engaged columns** (into the wall plane) or **piers** (paired with a window-arch head). F3/F4 free-floor verticals and floating arch-feet → demote to **rubble** (on their side, broken) or land both feet on real support. This is the cleanup pass that takes falsifier-count to zero (the §7 acceptance criterion "zero upright orphan columns; zero floating arches").

**Coherent-load-path (dimension (a), −70%) is NOT a separate build item — it is the EMERGENT RESULT of doing 1–4.** When the arcade carries the storey, the window-arches sit in the walls, and the orphans are converted or demoted, the load path reads by construction. That is the §4 test passing. So the ranked work is: **arcade → storey → window-in-wall → orphan-cleanup**, and (a) falls out.
