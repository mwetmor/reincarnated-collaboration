## ADMISSIONS

Over-quota discoveries noted per T3-V3:

- **SHAPESHIFT (Age III):** Appendix B records 1 SHAPESHIFT member for Age III. The Shaman enemy class in GD (Bloodsworn Summoners channel Chthonian entity transforms) is thematically adjacent but strictly a summoner-variant, not a body-morph shapeshift. GD's player-side Shaman mastery uses totems, not shapeshifting. Finding: Age III's single Shapeshift record-entry is likely the player kit record; no clear mob-template analog found. The Ravager boss (Celestial — endgame optional) has three different forms triggered by player choice, which is a shapeshift-phase mechanic, but Ravager is post-game content, not Act 1–3 accessible. Logged for conductor/Elrond review.
- **Ravager boss (optional Celestial, Barrowholm):** Three-form shapeshifter boss — Ravager of Flesh (bleed), Ravager of Soul (aether+wraith summon), Ravager of Mind (elemental+totem summon). Exceptionally strong SHAPESHIFT pattern AND TOTEM-SENTRY secondary in Mind form. However: post-story optional boss, not representative mob template. Logged for next-lap consideration.
- **Chthonian Bloodkeeper (Acts 5/6):** Grimtools confirms this is a Chthonian type with strong DoT pattern and appears in Void areas, but Act 5/6 are DLC expansions (Ashes of Malmouth / Forgotten Gods) not base game Age III content. Not harvested per Age III = base GD scope.
- **AURA (Age III):** Absent per Appendix B. GD hero monsters have Support archetype (aura-emitter for nearby allies) per the official monster guide, which is structurally an AURA mechanic. However, Appendix B records 0 AURA members for Age III — the family-claim system may not have captured GD's hero Support archetype. Factual observation: the hero "Supporter" type (creates aura for nearby allies) exists in GD but is not ratified into AURA family. Not fabricated — logged as a potential future docket candidate.
- **MULTI-PROJECTILE-VOLLEY (Age III):** Absent per Appendix B. GD Gunman enemies (Cronley's Gang) use ranged projectile attacks that structurally match MPV, but Appendix B records 0 MPV members for Age III. Row #7 notes Gunmen; their ranged fire is single-shot not fan-volley per confirmed Gunman page ("use only one-handed ranged weapons"). Finding supports the hole: no native fan-volley mob template confirmed.
