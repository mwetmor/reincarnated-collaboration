## ADMISSIONS

Over-quota discoveries noted here per T3-V3 (never extra rows, logged for next lap):

- **DASH-STRIKER (Age I):** The record counts 1 Dash-Striker entry for Age I (Appendix B). The Assassin's Dragon Talon kick-dash is the player side; on the monster side the closest analog is Fanatic-enchanted monsters with +100% move-speed that close distance in a single burst (no true dash-skill, just speed modifier). Factual finding: no dedicated mob template using a dash-teleport mechanic exists in D2 — Age I Dash-Striker family presence may be the player kit record-entry only, not a mob pattern. Flag for Elrond/conductor review.
- **SHAPESHIFT (Age I):** Appendix B records 4 Shapeshift members for Age I (Druid class ships with Shapeshift tree). D2 has no monster-side shapeshifter at the mob-template level — Andariel has a poison metamorphosis flavor but no explicit shapeshift mechanic. The Possessed champion modifier (monster that "possesses" a new body) is thematically adjacent but mechanically distinct. Finding: Age I Shapeshift is player-class origin; no mob template analog found.
- **Andariel boss encounter (Act 1, Catacombs Level 4):** poison DoT + charge attack in small chamber; strong DOT-AILMENT + DASH-STRIKER pattern but is a single unique boss not a mob template — logged here, not a row.
- **Duriel boss encounter (Act 2, Tal Rasha's Chamber):** extreme-close-range boss with Holy Freeze aura radiating constant slow + charge attack in tiny room; AURA + DASH-STRIKER pattern; single-boss encounter, not a repeatable mob template — logged here.
