## ADMISSIONS

Over-quota discoveries (one-liner log):

- **PoE2 Zarokh (Trial of Sekhemas, Act 2 ascendancy):** plants stationary time-mines then sweeps a lightning beam counter-clockwise — strong dual TRAP-MINE + CHANNELED-BEAM encounter; excluded as ascendancy-system content rather than act-spine content.
- **PoE2 Rathbreaker (Act 2):** Hyena demon swarms + cliff-archer combo — the strongest pure MELEE-STRIKE + MULTI-PROJECTILE-VOLLEY split encounter in the PoE2 act spine; row 4 captures the family; Rathbreaker adds the two-tier simultaneous-pressure refinement.
- **PoE2 Krutog, Lord of Kin (Act 4):** direct WHIRLWIND boss specimen (beetle-body spinning charge); row 10 covers the family but Krutog is the cleanest single-entity WHIRLWIND boss in PoE2.
- **LE Crystal Lotus (Crystal Mines, Ancient Era):** SHAPESHIFT specimen — crystallized elemental beast that transforms form; Age IV SHAPESHIFT is covered by row 11 (Geonor); Lotus adds a non-boss-tier shapeshift patrol for future laps.
- **LE Spymaster Zerrick (Imperial Era):** DOT poison pool + invulnerability-phase hide pattern — strong DOT-AILMENT stack-and-retreat boss; row 21 covers the family adequately.
- **LE Osprix swarm (Divine Era Scorched Grove):** fire-damage swarm with sustained defense against spawning waves — MELEE-STRIKE swarm specimen for LE; row 4 (PoE2 Raiders) covers the family; Osprix adds a fire-typed melee swarm variant.
- **PoE2 Xyclucian, the Chimera (Act 3 boss):** tri-elemental SHAPESHIFT boss (three-head alternating form); row 11 (Geonor) is the anchor SHAPESHIFT row; Xyclucian would add a second specimen confirming SHAPESHIFT as an Age IV strength.
