[COLOR=orange][SIZE=115][b]V1.3.0.0[/b][/SIZE][/COLOR]

[aname=Major][b][Major New Features][/b][/aname]
[LIST]
[*]Grim Dawn has received back-end changes to accommodate the release of the Fangs of Asterkarn Expansion.
[*]The game's UI has received a complete visual overhaul. A new Health Orb design HUD is now the default, while the classic Health Bar design can be enabled in the game options, under Interface.
[*]The Stash has been overhauled with new dedicated Component and Crafting Materials shared pages and +5% additional space per tab. In addition, stash pages can now be customized and labeled.
[*]New functionality has been added to the player inventory to instantly deposit all Components and Crafting Materials in their shared tabs from anywhere in the world. To support this, objects that ask for crafting materials (ex. Treasure Troves), can now also access the stash instead of just the inventory.
[*]New accessibility control schemes have been added under Keybindings: Keyboard Movement and Keyboard Only, enabling WASD-based controls with and without the mouse. You can set keybinds for WASD movement independently of the default controls and switch between them at any time. WASD controls mimic the controller and do not enable run and gun gameplay.
[*]Detailed tooltips now display stat roll ranges (ex. 12 Fire damage [8-14] indicates that the damage could have rolled as low as 8 or as high as 14).
[*]A new Video Option has been added: HDR Lighting, as an alternate visual style for the game.
[*]Monster Infrequents now always drop at the highest possible level (ex. if an MI drops, and possible variations in your level range are level 84 and 94, it will always drop the level 94 version).
[/LIST]

[aname=Shattered][b][Shattered Realm][/b][/aname]
[LIST]
[*]Clearing Shards by hunting down Nemesis spawns has become a too dominant strategy, reducing the viability of AoE builds in favor of single target burst, which is already favored in the boss arenas. To address this, Shard Progress from gathering a Nemesis soul has been reduced by 25%. Progress gained from Hero souls has been increased by 33%, and by 50% from Common and Champion souls. Chance of Champion monsters dropping souls has been increased by 18%.
[*]The Shattered Realm timer has been rebalanced to be more challenging and now becomes shorter at higher shards. Timer penalty from deaths is now lower, but bonus from kill streaks is now also lower and kill streaks now require a minimum of 25 kills, up from 15.
[*]Shattered Realm shrines no longer take crafting materials and activate immediately upon interaction.
[/LIST]

[*]Unlocking the next Crucible difficulty now also triggers at Wave 110 and Wave 160, rather than requiring clearing the Crucible Waves 1-100.
[*]Failing the Crucible on the same tier as a checkpoint no longer awards Tribute.
[/LIST]

[*]Fixed an issue where UI scaling could cause monster and pet health bars to break.
[*]Skill modifiers are now always loaded in a fixed order, rather than equip order. This is relevant particularly for how skill FX and pet swap modifiers function so the visual overrides always happen in a predictable order.

[aname=Modding][b][Modding][/b][/aname]
[LIST]
[*]A new template has been added: Skill_WPAttack_Radius. This skill type generates a radius eruption around the target when the WPS triggers, similar to how Shattering Smash for Oathkeeper generates a wave.
[*]New attributes have been added: % Total Resist and % Crowd Control Resist. These increase all the respective resists, similar to how % Total damage increments all damage types.
[*]Channeled skills (ex. Albrecht's Aether Ray) now apply Defensive Attributes in their data to the player while the skill is channeling. Defensive attributes have been added to their respective templates.
[*]A new tDyn field has been added: forceHighestLevel. Setting this to True will cause the loot table to always drop the highest level valid item it can within the level range.
[*]Skill modifiers assigned to SkillSecondary effects now display the SkillSecondary's name and icon instead of the base skill.
[/LIST]

[aname=Game][b][Game][/b][/aname]
[LIST]
[*]Menu UI windows now scale with UI scaling. UI now automatically scales down to fit the screen at lower resolutions, enabling controller UI compatibility at any resolution.
[*]Increased Health per point of Spirit to 12, up from 8.
[*]Treasure Troves now correctly have a guaranteed chance to drop a crafting material with a 30-50% (based on difficulty) chance to drop an additional material and have significant chance to drop blueprints. Previously, the chance to drop a second material was 100% across all difficulties and the chance to drop a blueprint was halved.
[*]Aether Crystal enemies now have an additional chance to drop a second Aether Crystal/Shard/Cluster.
[*]Increased all Boss Health scaling with level, by ~32% at level 100 (~15% for superbosses). Some bosses with lower health pools received larger increases. These changes do not apply to the Crucible.
[*Many older boss encounters have received tuning passes to bring their damage up to modern standards, as was commonly done in past updates.
[*]Reduced Monster Armor by 17% on Champion+ enemies and reduced Monster % Armor Absorption by 20% on all enemies. This reduces monster resistance to Physical damage, particularly for small fast attacks (ex. dual wielding or Eye of Reckoning).
[*]Reduced Monster % Life Leech Resist on Ultimate difficulty. This makes % Attack damage Converted to Health roughly 16% more effective against an average boss.
[*]In-game search has been updated to work with unicode (ie. non-English) characters.
[*]Reduced infamy gained from killing Beast heroes and bosses and increased kills required to spawn their Nemesis.
[*]Increased infamy gained from killing Cronley's Gang heroes and bosses and reduced kills required to spawn their Nemesis.
[*]Enemies that do not grant experience (ex. summons or Kubacabra's clones) no longer count towards achievement progress.
[*]Valdaran and Archmage Aleksander now share their animosity towards you and will spawn in each other's faction areas.
[/LIST]
