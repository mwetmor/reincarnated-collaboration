Special thanks to @RSnoomak who helped me figuring some forum post-creating stuff and @Dammitt for quick bug fixes and gtools update, some functions are now much more convenient to use

**DISCLAMER**
None of us had access to closed playtest, therefore listed builds are purely product of theorycraft and can be either top20 material or not work at all (although we really hope most of them will work just fine XD)
