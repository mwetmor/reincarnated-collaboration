<span><details><summary>[size=150][color=#FFBE7D]Tips and info[/color][/size]</summary>

- spend leftover Attributes points in Cunning (Physique / Spirit just to meet requirements) but left 10 points in the tank just in case there's suddenly a high requirement to meet

- I have Blitz, Vire's Might and Medal movement skill macro'ed to 1 button that spams them
  same with Ascension, Judgment and War Cry
  for chill netflix gameplay

- Aether Crystal farming: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5uMD_sWN6Wo

- after killing Cronley you got enough reputation too buy DW relic in Devil's Crossing
  go to forgotten gods to get shoulders and weapons

- I sided with Kymon's, later in Ultimate it's super easy to switch to Order and max its rep if needed
  by doing bounties in Warden's Lab because you get rep for both a bounty and aetherial heroes and monsters
  - remember to stack up Kymon's armor augments before doing so though

- Treasure Troves are good source of blueprints

- Steward Halberd - alternative for 2h (for Gutsmasher for example) - but subpar most probably

- when at 75 level, farm Totems till you get all 75 components blueprints - it's easier that way [hypothesis]

- consider of Scorched Ends prefix Grim Internals notification

- bloom farming vid https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GOk4SfWMRws

- Death's Vigil farming vid https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IC2MnRwlmgY

- blueprints (i.e. for Warborn set) probably the easiest to farm by doing SR75-76 Normal

- check if your devotions are bound especially to items auras after you mess with your equipment

- read blueprints as you get them

- important - when you run out of energy, you lose Beronath conversion aura and need to recast it

- in Normal to quest for Barrowholm and Ugdenbog but don't go further (to Malmouth).
  
- I moved to Elite at level 50. To much grind to reach 75 to be able to equip Beronath at the start of Elite

- on the very rare times you don't have Energy and pot's on cooldown - stand in some DoT for the bar to quickly feel up by proccing Scales

- can buy troll bone crushers at Benevald's

- don't forget to farm Mistborn Talisman in Smuggler's Pass (I did)

- it's always worth it to spam Rifts so that you can teleport to them after dying
  - or next to some side road to come to it later and be able to teleport back

- you can plan how to use [u]augments[/u] & [u]components[/u] in GrimTools before doing in on your character

-  in Ugdenbog always kill all trees and plant, click stomps https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GOk4SfWMRws

- can wear some items with [u]Energy Regeneration[/u] for example with **[Of Insight](https://www.grimtools.com/db/suffixes/6371)** suffix

- Clear all [u]totems[/u] along your way to get a lot of greens and [u]Iron Bits[/u] from selling greens, it's going to be useful later.

- Keep [u]multiple[/u] item pieces for every [u]slot[/u] with various resistances so that you have a lot of [u]options[/u] while making adjustments.

- Early on it might be  worth it to buy some [u]Scraps[/u] from vendors.

- **[Quests that reward the player with Eldritch Essences.](https://grimdawn.fandom.com/wiki/Eldritch_Essence)**

- **[Horrus](https://www.grimtools.com/map/markers/smiths/11320)** from [u]Gates of Necropolis[/u] can [u]transmute[/u] materials into each other, see **[:hammer_and_pick:  Blacksmiths’ Crafting Materials Exchange](https://forums.crateentertainment.com/t/blacksmiths-crafting-materials-exchange-table/108823)**.

- Always kill [u]Ch'thon Cultists[/u] along the way for a good chance of [u]Blood of Ch'thon[/u].

- Read [u]blueprints[/u] that drop from a [u]totem[/u] before farming the next one. I'm not sure of this but I read once that the game checks which [u]blueprints[/u] the player has already read.

- I followed the [u]Ugdenbloom[/u] route below but not the whole one - I restarted after clearing [u]Undergrowth[/u] https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GOk4SfWMRws:

  ![ugdenbloom|587x500](upload://g8UbP32nO4v593TxsSbJ3zOE4Pj.jpeg)

- Skip [u]Malmouth[/u] in [u]Normal and Elite[/u]

- skip [u]Forgotten Gods[/u] in [u]Elite[/u].

- side with Solael in Forgotten Gods

- I filled up the whole [u]Death's Vigil[/u] Honored bar to [u]Revered[/u] in an hour. The best (and only) bounties I recommend are the ones in [u]Warden's Lab[/u] and maybe also the crafting ones https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IC2MnRwlmgY

- Side with [u]Anasteria[/u] but do NOT give her 3 rare crafting mats on any difficulty - it's a [u]scam[/u]. You'll max out [u]Outcast's Secret's[/u] reputation easily anyway.

- Don't farm [u]bounties[/u] on lower difficulties if you can progress smoothly anyway. The higher the difficulty, the more [u]reputation[/u] you get (?)

-  pushing SR Normal, stop every 5 levels for the loot

- You don't need to grind SR[1-25] on [u]Ultimate[/u] to get 3 [u]Skill Points[/u] for doing the following quests:
  - **[ Entering the Shattered Realm](https://grimdawn.fandom.com/wiki/Entering_the_Shattered_Realm)**
  - **[ Exploring the Shattered Realm](https://grimdawn.fandom.com/wiki/Exploring_the_Shattered_Realm)**
  - **[ Conquering the Shattered Realm](https://grimdawn.fandom.com/wiki/Conquering_the_Shattered_Realm)**

  You can reach high [u]SR[/u] on [u]Normal[/u] and do just SR35 on [u]Ultimate[/u] instead.
