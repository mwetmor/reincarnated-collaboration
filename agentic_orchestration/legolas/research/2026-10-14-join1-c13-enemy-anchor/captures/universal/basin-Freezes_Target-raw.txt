{{Movement nav}}
{{Intro}}
*Freezes target can freeze normal or minion monsters, and chill all other units, when applied with a successful weapon attack, as long as they are not [[Immune to Cold]] and they do not have 0 [[chill effectiveness]].
*Bonuses from multiple sources are summed, although when [[two weapons]] are equipped only that of the primary or attacking weapon is added (that of the other weapon is ignored).
*Applied by any melee or ranged attack with the exception of [[Blade Sentinel]], [[Blade Shield]] and the additional arrows of [[Multiple Shot]] (only applied by the original two arrows).

*Minimum 0% chance and maximum 100% chance to freeze or chill target.
*Freeze or chill length in frames is a random odd number from {27, 29 ... 2*('''% Chance''') + 25}, viz, 1-9 seconds. [https://www.mannm.org/d2library/faqtoids/hft_eng.html] [http://d2wissen.d2chars.de/items/110/eig_angriffseffekte-x.html#erstarren]
*Length of freezing or chilling is affected by target [[Cold Resist]] and difficulty level penalties (1/2 in Nightmare and 1/4 in Hell).
*Applied independently of [[freeze length]] and [[Cold Damage|cold length]]: [[Freezing Arrow]] would freeze eligible targets within its 3 1/3 yard [[radius]] for ~2 seconds, Freezes target would freeze any eligible target hit by the arrow  for 1-9 seconds.
