*-% Damage does not apply to physical cast damage, including the following:
**[[Bone Fetish]] death explosion [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/118104-monster-bonuses-death-damage-and-corpse-explosion/&do=findComment&comment=1464450]

*DR -50% is reduced to DR -10% (1/5) against [[Immune to Physical]] monsters: this is applied to [[undead]] before [[Sanctuary (skill)|Sanctuary]].
**In D2R it works at full effectiveness (DR -50%) against [[Physical Immunity Sundered]] monsters.
