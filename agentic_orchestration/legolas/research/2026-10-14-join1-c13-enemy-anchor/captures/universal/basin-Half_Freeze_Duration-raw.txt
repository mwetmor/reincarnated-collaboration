{{Intro}}
*Half Freeze Duration halves [[Cold Damage|cold length]] or [[freeze length]] applied to an equipping unit.
*HFD and [[Cold Resist]] multiply: CR 75% reduces length to 1/4 and HFD reduces it to 1/2, so when combined, length is reduced to 1/8.
*Length is halved once only, regardless of the number of items equipped supplying HFD.
*Does not apply to [[Holy Freeze]], since it does not slow with cold length.
