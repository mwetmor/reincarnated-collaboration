{{Attribute nav}}
{{Intro}}
{{Mb|=Replenish Life}} is the life gained per frame in 1/256ths of a point:


Life gained per second = Replenish Life * 25/256 (≈ Replenish Life/10) [https://www.mannm.org/d2library/faqtoids/leben_eng.html#liferepl]


*Unlike [[damage regeneration]], it still applies when damage, [[Poison Damage]] and Open Wounds are being applied.
*Direct counter to Open Wounds: that applied by a level 30 player's [[melee]] attack on another player would result in <sub>109/256</sub> life drain per frame, which Replenish Life +109 would counter entirely; a ranged attack would result in <sub>54/256</sub> life drain per frame, which Replenish Life +54 would counter entirely.
