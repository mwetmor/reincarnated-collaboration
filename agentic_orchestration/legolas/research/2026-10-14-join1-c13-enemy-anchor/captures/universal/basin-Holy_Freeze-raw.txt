[[File:Holy Freeze.gif|center|frame|Overlay displayed at feet when active.]]


*Radial cold damage is applied to hostile targets within radius once every two seconds. If a target is within the radius of multiple auras, then the radial cold damage of all of them is applied.
*Weapon cold damage is only applied by the supplier, and it is added to both the arrows and explosions of [[Freezing Arrow]].
*+% [[Cold Skill Damage]] is applied to skill damage before it's added to weapon damage, then it's applied a second time to [[melee]] attacks.
*No cold length is applied: HF is a separate source of attack rate and movement speed reduction which adds to that of cold length, [[Slows Target]], and [[Decrepify]], although no unit can be reduced below 15% attack rate or 25% velocity. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/93022-about-slows-target-mechanics/&do=findComment&comment=1143042]
** In D2R it also adds to that applied by [[Impale]]. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/127638-d2r-impale-slowing-technical-details/]
*[[Cold Resist]] or [[Cannot be Frozen]] won't reduce or negate the slowing effect, so [[Immune to Cold]] targets may still be slowed. However, the value applied is the maximum (least negative) of HF or the target's [[chill effectiveness]]. Consequently HF will never apply more than -50 attack rate and -50% movement speed at level >11, and 0 chill effectiveness results in no slowing or radial cold damage being applied. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/120504-chill-effectiveness/]
*Can slow [[Aura Enchanted]] [[Unique (monster)|Unique]] monster, even if it has higher level HF. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/120504-chill-effectiveness/]
*Does not slow or apply radial cold damage to [[Evil]]s. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/120504-chill-effectiveness/&do=findComment&comment=1492459]
