{{Intro}}
{{Mb|=Slower Stamina Drain}} reduces the rate at which a player character's stamina drains while running in the wilderness by a percentage.
*The rate at which stamina is drained while running in the wilderness is as follows:


Stamina drain per second = 25 * max( (40 * (1 + [{{0|}}[[Body armor|Armor speed]] /10] ) * (100 - Slower Stamina Drain) /100 , 1) /256 [https://www.mannm.org/d2library/faqtoids/stamina_eng.html#stamdrain]


*Stamina is '''not''' drained while running in towns, or while a Stamina [[Shrine]] or [[Stamina Potion]] is active.
*Armor speed penalties are as follows:

*All other body armors have no speed penalty (0). Unlike [[movement speed]], stamina drain is '''not''' affected by [[shield]] speed penalties. [https://www.mannm.org/d2library/faqtoids/stamina_eng.html#stamdrain]
*Maximum stamina drain when wearing heavy (10) body armor is 7.8125 stamina per second.
*Enough stamina comes from gaining levels and investing points in Vitality to make Slower Stamina Drain increasingly irrelevant as progress is made, but it can be helpful at lower levels: 100% Slower Stamina Drain can be achieved as early as level 11, reducing stamina drain to the minimum <sub>25/256</sub> stamina per second (so it takes 256 frames or 10.24 (256/25) seconds of running just to drain a single point of stamina).
