[[File:Stun.gif|right|frame|Stunned]]
*When this overlay displays above a unit, stun length is applied.
*Can be active at the same time as a [[curse]], [[Inner Sight]] and [[Slow Missiles]].
*Normal and minion monsters are completely incapacitated until length expires.
*Only 10% chance of applying to [[Champion]], [[Unique (monster)|Unique]] and [[Super Unique]] monsters, who will still be completely incapacitated until length expires.
*Players are not incapacitated but they are always put into [[hit recovery]] when hit before length expires, even when no damage is applied.
*Length is not subject to any penalties, but there is normally a 10 second ceiling. {{Warn|=Skill description does not always apply this ceiling to stun length.}}
*Only the most recent of multiple sources of stun length is applied: if a 2 second stun length is applied 2 seconds after a 10 second stun length, only 4 second stun length is applied in total.
*Does not apply to [[boss]]es, or the following static units:
