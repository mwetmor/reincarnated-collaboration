{{Unique bonus nav}}
*80% chance of being chosen when a random Champion or Unique pack spawns. Unique can also spawn from Evil Urns in Act 5 and Monster Shrines throughout the game.
*Spawn with 3-6 minions.
*One bonus is randomly selected in Normal, two are randomly selected in Nightmare and three are randomly selected in Hell. All permitted bonuses have an equal chance of selection, and are applied in displayed order. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/112167-which-elements-are-most-resisted-in-hell/&do=findComment&comment=1409307] [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/112167-which-elements-are-most-resisted-in-hell/&do=findComment&comment=1409496] 
*Poison Enchanted cannot be randomly selected, but it is a bonus for [[Achmel the Cursed|{{Ub|=Achmel the Cursed}}]] and [[Lilith|{{Ub|=Lilith}}]].
*Unique and minion level is base monster level +3: this level is used for chance-to-hit, [[Aura Enchanted|aura]] and [[Cursed|curse]] level calculation, selecting relevant [[MonLvl]] values to calculate base [[MonLvl#Attack rating|attack rating]] and [[MonLvl#Damage|damage]] of all types, and the clvl/mlvl modifier applied to [[experience]] when clvl < mlvl; base level continues to be used to calculate base [[MonLvl#Defense rating|defense]], [[MonLvl#Life|life]] and [[MonLvl#Experience|experience]]. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/118104-monster-bonuses-death-damage-and-corpse-explosion/&do=findComment&comment=1495104] [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/118104-monster-bonuses-death-damage-and-corpse-explosion/&do=findComment&comment=1520493]

*[[Ignore Target's Defense]] only applies to minions.
*-% [[Target Defense]] is 100% effective against both Unique and its minions.
*[[Freeze length]] chills instead; only minions can be frozen.
*[[Stun length]] only has 10% chance of being applied, but suffers no other penalty.
*Effects [[altering AI]] cannot be applied directly, but can affect minions.
*Life drained by [[Open Wounds]] is halved.
