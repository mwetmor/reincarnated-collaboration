Every additional point of Dexterity also results in +5 [[Attack Rating]] for player character, [[mercenary]] and [[pet]]s, while every four points results in +1 [[Defense]]. In [[Expansion]] games, Dexterity also affects player character's chance of [[block]]ing when equipping a [[shield]], [[auric shield]] or [[shrunken head]].


Player character Dexterity varies depending on chosen class at start of game:


{{classes table}}
|-
|25||20||20||20||25||20||25||20
|}


Player character can increase Dexterity by allocating attribute points, while player character, mercenary and [[pet]]s can also gain bonuses from equipped items, and charms in inventory in Expansion games. Typically, a character only needs enough Dexterity to meet item requirements, or achieve desired chance of blocking in Expansion games, although more experienced players with better equipped characters applying physical attack damage may allocate more points if it increases that damage, usually at the expense of Vitality.
