{{Intro}}
Player character equipping a [[shield]], an [[auric shield]] or a [[shrunken head]] normally has a Chance to Block a successful enemy attack, which applies after a successful hit check but before an [[Amazon]] [[dodge]]s, [[avoid]]s or [[evade]]s. This prevents damage and other [[:Category:Modifiers|modifiers]] being applied, although it does not prevent [[missile]] piercing due to [[Pierce]] and [[Piercing Attack]].
*The following can be blocked:
**[[Melee]] attacks
**[[Arrows]] fired by [[bow]]s
**[[Bolts]] fired by [[crossbow]]s
**[[Javelin]]s
**[[Throwing axe]]s
**[[Throwing knife|Throwing knives]]
**Missiles applying physical damage
**[[Bone Fetish]] death explosion [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/118104-monster-bonuses-death-damage-and-corpse-explosion/&do=findComment&comment=1464450]
**[[Suicide Minion]] death explosion [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/120127-nihlathaks-skeletons/&do=findComment&comment=1487888]
**[[Fire Enchanted]] explosion [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/118104-monster-bonuses-death-damage-and-corpse-explosion/&do=findComment&comment=1464380]
*The following '''cannot''' be blocked:
**Level >49 [[Fire Arrow]]
**Level >49 [[Cold Arrow]]
**[[Lightning Bolt]]
**[[Molten Boulder]] explosion
**[[Tornado]] periodic [[radius|radial]] damage
**[[Armageddon]] radial impact damage
**[[Corpse Explosion]]
**[[Paladin (Diablo II)|Paladin]] [[Smite]]
**[[Charge|Charging]] [[Claw Viper]]s and [[Reanimated Horde]]
**[[Nihlathak (boss)|{{Ub|=Nihlathak}}]] corpse explosion [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/118104-monster-bonuses-death-damage-and-corpse-explosion/&do=findComment&comment=1464624]
|}

*Ceiling of 75% Chance to Block applies when stationary (whether idle, attacking or casting), walking or using [[Whirlwind]]. Chance to Block is reduced to 1/3 while running, ''after'' this ceiling is applied (so maximum 25% Chance to Block while running). [https://www.mannm.org/d2library/faqtoids/block_eng.html#ibc]
*Chance to Block displayed on the shield itself is the sum of its base value, a class [[Block#Base Chance to Block|bonus]] and Increased Chance of Blocking on the shield itself (including Holy Shield when active). Although 75% ceiling is applied to its ''displayed'' Chance to Block, any excess and Increased Chance of Blocking from other items is still taken into account when calculating ''actual'' Chance to Block displayed when cursor is over [[Defense]] field in character screen.
*Minimum Dexterity required for maximum 75% Chance to Block is 15 + {150*clvl / (Shield + Bonus) }.

==Mercenary==
No [[mercenary]] has a Chance to Block: [[Iron Wolf]] can equip a [[shield]] but its Chance to Block does not apply and thus he cannot block.
<br/>
<br/>
==Pet==
[[Pet]]s have no Chance to Block, with the exception of a [[Raise Skeleton|Necroskeleton]] with shield.
*Necroskeleton only has 3% Chance to Block with shield, otherwise it cannot block. Arrows will still be lodged in shield arm even without shield, so this is '''not''' an indication one is present. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/110242-monsters-chance-to-block/&do=findComment&comment=1517009]
*[[Iron Golem]] summoned from a [[shield]] does not apply its Chance to Block and thus it cannot block.
<br/>

==Monster==
[[Monster]] cannot block unless positive values are entered in ToBlock fields of MonStats.txt '''and''' either of the following conditions are met:
*Block animation is defined in AnimData.d2 '''and''' enabled in MonStats2.txt.
*NoShldBlock field is flagged in MonStats.txt. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/110242-monsters-chance-to-block/&do=findComment&comment=1421610]
Monster block chance is capped at 75%. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/112384-what-is-the-effect-of-defense/#findComment-1577320]
<br/>
Following only have Chance to Block with a shield. Those with no value for Normal only spawn in [[Act 5]] in Nightmare and Hell, those with no value for Normal and Nightmare only spawn in [[Pandemonium Event]].
