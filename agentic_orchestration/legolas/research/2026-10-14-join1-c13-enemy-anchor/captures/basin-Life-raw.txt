{{Intro}}
[[File:Life.png|thumb|600px|Current and maximum life are displayed both on the character screen and by the contents of the globe in the bottom left of the screen, which empties as life depletes (and turns green when the player character is [[Poison Damage|poisoned]]). Holding the cursor over the globe displays numerical values, which since [[1.13c|1.13]] will remain even when the cursor has been removed after clicking once on the bottom of the globe (clicking a second time will undo this).]]
|}


{{Mb|=Life}} determines how much damage can be sustained before dying. Life displayed is rounded down to the nearest point, but the smallest unit is actually 1/256 point. Life varies depending on chosen class at start of game:


{{Classes table}}
|-
|50||50||55||55||45||55||40||55
|}


Life gained as a character gains levels also varies depending on class:


{{Classes table}}
|-
| +2||+2||+2||+1.5||+1.5||+2||+1||+2
|}


Life can be increased by allocating attribute points to Vitality. Life gained from each additional point also varies depending on class:


{{Classes table}}
|-
| +3||+3||+4||+2||+2||+3||+2||+3
|}


Unlike mana, life does not naturally regenerate. Player character returns to maximum life upon gaining a level (as does a [[mercenary]] upon gaining a level). Returning to town and clicking on a [[Townsperson#Heal|healer]] will instantly heal to maximum life for a player character and any mercenary or [[pet]]s, while each drink from a [[Shrine|well]] in the wilderness will heal 50% of maximum life for all. Activating a Health or Refilling [[Shrine]] in the wilderness will only heal the player character to maximum life.


Player character (and mercenary in [[Expansion]] games) can drink a [[Full Rejuvenation Potion]] to heal to maximum life (while each [[Rejuvenation Potion]] will heal 35% of maximum life). Healing [[potion]]s can also be drunk to heal life over time; any excess which isn't required to heal to maximum life may not be redundant, since further life can be lost while healing. The amount healed from drinking any healing potion depends on class:

Healing from potions also has a chance to double based on total Vitality. Other methods of recovering life are described on separate pages.

==Monster==

Base life = <nowiki>[</nowiki>[[MonLvl#Life|MonLvl]] * MonStats/100] [https://d2mods.info/forum/kb/viewarticle?a=354]
