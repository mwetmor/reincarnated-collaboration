{{Intro}}
{{Mb|=Vitality}} affects a player character's stamina and life, and the percentage chance of doubling life restored by any healing [[potion]]. Player character Vitality varies depending on chosen class at start of game:



{{classes table}}
|-
|20||20||25||25||15||25||10||25
|}


Player character can increase Vitality by allocating attribute points and can also gain bonuses from equipped items, and charms in inventory in [[Expansion]] games. Stamina and life gained from each additional point also vary depending on character class:


{{Number table}} width=75%
|-align=center
!width=12%|Attribute
!width=11%|Amazon
!width=11%|Assassin
!width=11%|Barbarian
!width=11%|Druid
!width=11%|Necromancer
!width=11%|Paladin
!width=11%|Sorceress
!width=11%|Warlock
|-
!align=left|Stamina
| +1||+1.25||+1||+1||+1||+1||+1||+1
|-
!align=left|Life
| +3||+3||+4||+2||+2||+3||+2||+3
|}


Typically, a character allocates every remaining point to Vitality once Strength and Dexterity meet any [[requirements]] for equipped items (and achieve desired chance of [[block]]ing when equipping a [[shield]], [[auric shield]] or [[shrunken head]] in Expansion games), although more experienced players with better equipped characters applying physical attack damage may allocate less points, preferring to allocate them to Strength or Dexterity to increase that damage.


Chance of doubling life restored by any healing potion is as follows:
