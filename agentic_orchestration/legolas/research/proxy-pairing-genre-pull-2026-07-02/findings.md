## Q1 — Minion Pairing Complementarity Precedent

### Validated Pairing Patterns

| Pattern | Exemplar Game + Build | Minion A | Minion B | Mechanism | Transferable Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tank-Anchor + DPS-Swarm | D2 Summonmancer | Clay Golem (taunt via slow-on-hit, high resists, single unit) | Skeleton Warriors x20 (front-line sustained DPS) | Golem holds aggro and slows; skeletons attack uncontested behind it. Its function is purely multiplicative on Raptors. Frenzy Totem stacks on top. | One companion exists solely to buff; its combat output is irrelevant to build identity. Wolves boost bears and raptors (melee) but NOT scorpions or birds — cross-species mixing loses the aura. |

**Key design rules extracted:**

**Rule Q1-A — Single Mastery Multiplier.** D2's Skeleton Mastery applies to all minion types. When a player skill investment scales across both proxy types, pairings feel more cohesive. A passive that benefits both halves of a DUAL_PROXY pair substantially strengthens that pairing's viability.

**Rule Q1-B — Enabler/Empowered Split.** PoE Carrion Golem + Zombies is a validated pattern where one type exists primarily to make the other functional. This is a valid DUAL_PROXY structure (Resource Conduit enabling Charged Threshold Proxy), but for CONVERGENCE fusions the lopsided origin reads as "one type won." Fusions should draw from pairings where both types contribute distinctly.

**Rule Q1-C — Spatial Complementarity.** Grim Dawn Blight Fiend + Briarthorn validates the clusterer + AoE-exploiter pattern directly. Transferable to Terrain Anchor (buff/debuff zone) + Volatile Emitter (stationary AoE pulse that benefits from enemies standing in zone).

**Rule Q1-D — Cross-Species Aura Penalty.** Last Epoch community confirmed: Wolves' Howl buffs melee companions (bears, raptors) but not scorpions or birds. "Mixed-species zoo" does not maximize any aura. DUAL_PROXY pools should curate pairs that share a buff-amplification layer, or that are both buffed by the same player skills.

## Q2 — Fusion/Merge Precedent

### Merger/Fusion Systems

| System | Game / Media | Mechanism | Legible | Problematic | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Star-up (1→2→3 star) | TFT / Auto Chess | Three identical units auto-merge into one larger, stronger version with visual transformation (size increase, new particles). | Player selects 3 copies deliberately. The unit's identity is preserved — recognizable as "that champion, better." | At merge the new unit is visually transformed but still one character type, not a hybrid. Low soup risk. | Visual transformation at merge must be large enough to register instantly. Merged entity should be a superior version of something familiar, not a new thing entirely. |
| Demon Fusion (Skill Inheritance) | SMT / Persona 5 Royal | Two+ demons combine in the Velvet Room. Player-controlled skill inheritance: choose which parent skills pass to offspring. Inheritance type restricts certain skill categories per demon archetype. Only signature skills cannot be inherited. | P5R: player explicitly picks inherited skills from a menu showing BOTH parents' available skills. Arcana (identity) shown throughout. Zero ambiguity about what was inherited. | Older SMT/Nocturne: randomized inheritance. Players had no visibility into fusion outcome until after commitment. Investment with unknown output = opacity. | Player legibility during fusion is the crux. Showing inheritance OPTIONS before commitment is the gold standard. Applied to PROXY_CONVERGENCE: the fused entity's stat card should show two labeled columns of inherited properties from each parent, not a blended description. |
| Monster Synthesis | Dragon Quest Monsters: The Dark Prince (2023) | Two parents create an offspring that inherits: 3 Talents (skill trees) from parents, 25% of each parent's stat growth, partial XP from parent levels. Parents consumed. | Three inherited Talents shown in synthesis preview. Player sees the result before committing. | Players who rush miss optimization; optimal chains require deliberately sacrificing leveled monsters. | Preview-before-commit is essential. If PROXY_CONVERGENCE requires a 2-proxy kit build-up, the player must see the fused entity's behavior at kit-creation time, not at first deployment. |
| Iron Golem | D2 Necromancer | Necromancer sacrifices a metal item permanently to create a Golem that inherits the item's magical properties (runeword auras, weapon damage bonuses, armor defense). One Golem at a time. Item destroyed on creation. | "Made from" framing is excellent legibility — the Golem IS the item. Late-game Insight runeword on a pole gives the Golem a Meditation aura — the Golem is visibly functioning as the aura source. | One-time permanent loss creates risk. If the Golem dies (Hardcore), item is already gone. Some magical properties (Mana Steal, MF) don't transfer, creating confusion about valid investments. | "Made from" origin framing is very strong for legibility — the resulting entity's identity flows from the sacrificed input. One-time committed investment with visible mechanics is acceptable if the loss contract is clearly communicated upfront. |
| Animate Guardian | PoE Necromancer | Player equips a summoned humanoid with in-game items (helmet, armor, weapons etc.). AG uses items' stats + granted skills in combat. On AG death: all equipped items permanently destroyed (except body armor with special crafted mod). | When alive, maximum legibility — it's wearing the gear and using its skills. Players can see "my AG has Dying Breath staff — it provides auras to my army." | Permanent-destruction-on-death: players systematically under-invested in AG gear to avoid loss. AG wore low-value items and functioned poorly. The mechanism creating richest potential (gear-fusion) was avoided precisely because of its downside. GGG partially addressed with body armor death-preservation mod, but the reputation for loss-risk persists. | Investment-grade fusions must have a legible loss-contract AND a loss-prevention option. Loss risk suppresses investment toward underperformance. |
| Necronomicon Pair (contrast case) | Dota 2 | Item summons Warrior (melee, mana burn, death nuke) + Archer (ranged, movement/attack speed aura, Purge at L3) simultaneously. They are NOT fused — designed pair. | Clear functional distinction: Warrior = frontline threat, Archer = support+vision. Level 3: True Sight on Warrior (counters invisibility) + Purge on Archer (removes buffs). Designed interaction. | This is the contrast case. The pair works BECAUSE the units are different. It is the DUAL_PROXY model, not the CONVERGENCE model. The single Necronomicon item creates a psychological fiction of "one entity" but combat output is two distinct units. | Designed-pair logic (DUAL_PROXY) may sometimes outperform merger logic (CONVERGENCE) because you get both coverage types simultaneously rather than a compromise. The VALUE of a merge is specifically when both types CANNOT be separately preserved — otherwise the pair alone is sufficient. |
| Predator / Gluttony — Rimuru | That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime | Rimuru absorbs any target (organic, inorganic, skill, magic). Analysis component studies and produces copies of absorbed skills. Mimicry allows shape-shifting. Great Sage narrates each absorption with full skill-name legibility. | Full legibility through narration. Player/viewer always knows what was absorbed and what was gained. "I now have Tempest Serpent's Poison Breath." Skill NAMES are preserved. | Rimuru's power is deliberately OP fantasy — designed to be unbalanceable. Direct implementation would produce an endgame player impossible to design around. | Narrative model shows the IDEAL of what players want from fusion: inheritance + accumulation + full legibility ("I have BOTH things I ate"). Game design challenge is providing this fantasy within constraints. PROXY_CONVERGENCE hits this note by showing clearly what each component contributed. |
| Summon Hierarchy | Overlord (anime) | Ainz commands Floor Guardians (Albedo, Shalltear, Demiurge, etc.) — summoned NPCs with fixed roles and personalities. They operate autonomously within their floors. | Nazarick hierarchy provides role legibility through TITLE and FLOOR. Each Guardian has a known function. The hierarchy is comprehensible as an org chart. | Not a fusion system — a hierarchy system. Ainz does not merge units; he commands them. Closest analogue to the D2 army composition, not to Persona fusion. | Named/titled hierarchy structures are highly legible. If PROXY_CONVERGENCE produces a named fused entity (not just "your proxy"), legibility increases substantially. Names that contain both parent words ("Fighting Anchor") telegraph the pairing. |

**Three-question legibility test for fused entities:**
1. What did it inherit from Component A?
2. What did it inherit from Component B?
3. What was lost in the merge?

**Soup fusion** = player cannot answer these questions. The fused entity behaves in surprising ways because inheritance is opaque or randomized.

**The SMT P5R fusion menu is the gold standard:** show both parent skill lists, show which ARE inheritable, let player choose. Zero ambiguity. The resulting entity has a known identity.

## Q3 — Complementarity Heuristics

### Heuristic Validation

| Heuristic | Status | Where It Appears | Mechanism | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Role Coverage (tank + DPS) | Strongly validated | D2 Summonmancer (Clay Golem + Skeletons); D3 WD (Gargantuan + Fetishes); D4 Necro (Bone Golem taunt + Skeleton Mages); Grim Dawn (Briarthorn + Blight Fiend) | Tank absorbs incoming aggro; DPS operates in protected space. Works because minion AI cannot dodge, so aggro diversion is the primary mitigation mechanism. | Most universal heuristic. Appears in every validated ARPG pairing studied. Tank can be stationary or mobile. |
| Cadence Contrast (continuous + burst) | Validated | D2 Summonmancer (Skeletons continuous + Revives burst vs bosses); D3 WD (Fetish swarm continuous + Gargantuan Wrathful Protector burst); Last Epoch (Scorpion DoT continuous + Raptor burst melee) | Continuous DPS clears packs; burst DPS handles boss phases. Players feel the pulse of the fight as burst fires. | Requires both cadences remain effective. If burst unit dies to boss or DoT unit irrelevant vs fast-moving enemies, the comp loses identity. |
| Spatial Contrast (static emplacement + mobile chaser) | Validated with caveat | Grim Dawn (Familiar stationary-adjacent aura + Briarthorn mobile taunt); D2 (Clay Golem holds position + Skeletons pursue) | Static units create a persistent effect or safe zone; mobile units extend reach or chase fleeing enemies. | Caveat: AI pathing failures (D2 skeletons stuck in Maggot Lair, Arcane Sanctuary narrow corridors) mean this breaks under pathfinding failures. The complement only works if mobile unit can reach the target. |
| Trigger Interplay (condition generator + consumer) | Validated — strongest signal for Convergence | PoE Carrion Golem + Zombies (Zombie count enables Golem damage multiplier; Zombies apply Maim debuff Golems benefit from); D4 Bone Golem (generates corpses through damage-tanking → additional skeleton spawns); Grim Dawn Blight Fiend + Briarthorn (cluster created → AoE lands on cluster); PoE Spectre aura-providers → army consumers | Condition-generator sets up amplified state; condition-consumer deals with enemies in that state. | Most interesting design space for CONVERGENCE because the trigger link is what makes two things feel like ONE system. The PoE Carrion Golem/Zombie relationship is essentially inseparable in build function — cannot play one without the other meaningfully. |
| Economy Pairing (resource generator + spender) | Validated in specific contexts | D4 Bone Golem (generates corpses → feeds additional skeletons); D3 WD (mana-spender spells generate Fetishes — player is generator for own swarm); PoE Desecrate + Bone Offering (corpse-consuming skill) | One entity produces a resource; another consumes it. Creates mechanical interdependency. | In most ARPG builds the generator is the PLAYER, not another minion. D4 Bone Golem (generates corpses from tanking) is the cleanest ARPG minion-to-minion example. Highly applicable to Resource Conduit + any proxy type in the 14-type taxonomy. |
| Buff Stacking / Aura-Provider + Beneficiary | Validated | PoE Spectres (Power Zealot provides power charges for whole army); Last Epoch Wolf (Howl buffs all melee companions); Grim Dawn Familiar (Storm Spirit aura buffs elemental damage for all pets); PoE Animate Guardian (Dying Breath staff provides auras to nearby minions) | Dedicated buff-provider raises the ceiling of the whole army. The provider's individual DPS is irrelevant. | Functional archetype of the Warcry/Buff Spirit (type 10) and Resource Conduit (type 8) in the 14-type taxonomy. DUAL_PROXY pairings that include these should pair with types that benefit from the aura, not types already self-sufficient. |
| Size Contrast (swarm + anchor) | Validated (psychological/visual) | D2 (1 Golem + 20 skeletons); D3 WD (1 Gargantuan + up to 21 Fetishes); Last Epoch Necro (1-2 Golems + 33 total minions) | Players visually process "one large stable unit + many small mobile units" as a complete army. Cognitive model is feudal: lord + troops. | Pure visual heuristic, but it compounds with role coverage to produce the most archetypal minion compositions. Worth encoding in CONVERGENCE design notes for visual differentiation of the fused entity. |

**Cross-heuristic finding:** The strongest genre pairings validate multiple heuristics simultaneously. D2 Golem + Skeletons = role coverage + spatial contrast + size contrast. PoE Carrion Golem + Zombies = role coverage + trigger interplay + economy pairing. D3 Gargantuan + Fetishes = role coverage + cadence contrast + economy pairing. Single-axis pairings are perceived as "just a second minion." PROXY_CONVERGENCE pairings that cover two or more axes will read as genuine systems.

## Q4 — Anti-Patterns

### Documented Failure Modes

| Anti-Pattern | Exemplar | What Failed | Why | Prescription |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Screen Illegibility / Pet Density | PoE "86 Minions — Visual Clutter is Insane" (official forum thread); D3 multiplayer pet density | At 86 minions + aura effects + Blight encounter particles: "nearly impossible to see anything" — enemies hidden behind minion bodies; severe frame rate drops. D3 forums: requests for ally pet transparency toggle showing that pet opacity was causing enemy-read failures. | No individual minion is visually distinct at high counts. Aura effect particles compound multiplicatively. Player's target information (enemy position, incoming attacks) obscured by their own army. | Cap total proxy count. CONVERGENCE fused entity replaces 2 slots with 1. DUAL_PROXY = max 2 simultaneous types. Per-proxy type: distinct silhouette + color key required. Fused entity visual effects must NOT equal both parents' effects stacked. |
| Minion Soup — Role Unreadability | PoE high-minion builds; "Minions Are the Problem" forum thread | Too many minion types with overlapping visual profiles. Players cannot tell which minion is doing what or why the build succeeded/failed. Critical feedback loop (which minion killed what) is broken. GGG eventually simplified minion generation for PoE2. | Visual profiles insufficiently distinct. When zombies, spectres, skeletons, golems, animate guardian, and skitterbots are all on screen simultaneously, they occupy similar size/color/behavior niches. | Each proxy type in the 14-type taxonomy needs a distinct visual silhouette and behavior legible at a glance. Fused entities (CONVERGENCE) inherit ONE visual identity, not both parents' visual effects layered. |
| Strictly Dominant Pool Choice | PoE aurabot Necromancer + Spectre builds (2.x–3.x); Last Epoch endgame minion build | One configuration was always optimal: Necromancer Aurabot + Power Zealot Spectre was mandatory in group play. Player choice in support composition became a puzzle with one correct answer. GGG nerfed aura-stacking repeatedly (3.15, 3.22). Last Epoch community finding: competitive play required single minion + dread/infernal shade buff — only one support configuration reached endgame. | When build difficulty and reward are both high, players converge on the optimal solution. A pool of 3 options with one clearly superior DPS choice has dead entries. | DUAL_PROXY compatibility pools of 3 must be differentiated by playstyle/encounter type, not by damage numbers. Option A = better clear speed, Option B = better boss kill, Option C = better survivability. All 3 should feel like real decisions. If one option is 40% higher DPS, the others will never be chosen. |
| Aurabot Degeneracy (role hollowing) | PoE Necromancer Aurabot 3.14–3.15 | Dedicated support player projected auras + Spectre-sourced charge generation across multiple screens, boosting group DPS by 800%+. Support player had zero personal DPS, existed only to make others functional. GGG stated goal: reduce gap between solo and party play, nerfed aura projection range to one screen. | When buff-provider's output scales to extreme multiples of group DPS, the build creates a hard dependency: group cannot function at endgame without the bot. The bot has no solo function. Both are locked into each other. | DUAL_PROXY pairings including Warcry/Buff Spirit (type 10) or Resource Conduit (type 8) should cap the buff-provider's amplification magnitude. The DPS proxy must function at ~70% of ceiling without the buff proxy. Enhance, not mandate. |
| Mixed Static/Mobile AI Pathing Failure | D2 Necromancer in Maggot Lair, Arcane Sanctuary | Skeleton Warriors (mobile AI) became permanently stuck in narrow corridors. 20 skeletons clustered and blocked in tight tunnels. Players had to run far away to trigger teleport behavior. Patch 2.3 added explicit teleport-to-Necromancer when too far as a workaround. | Minion AI designed for open areas fails in geometry that creates bottlenecks. A mixed static/mobile composition is worse than static-only in tight geometry — mobile units prevent access while adding no DPS. | Any DUAL_PROXY pair with a mobile type (Passive Fighter, Autonomous Caster, Bodyguard) alongside a stationary type (Totem/Turret, Volatile Emitter, Terrain Anchor): validate that the stationary type's zone is not blocked by mobile unit pathfinding. Design mobile type to fall back to a defensive perimeter around the stationary unit, not pursue independently through tight geometry. |
| Investment Suppression via Loss Risk | PoE Animate Guardian (perma-death of equipped items) | Players systematically under-invested in AG gear. AG wore low-value items and was therefore weak. The mechanism creating richest potential (gear-fusion) was avoided precisely because of the downside. GGG eventually added body-armor death-preservation mod as partial mitigation. | Loss risk + permanent irreversibility = rational underinvestment. Expected value: (upside of investing rare item) × (probability AG never dies) — for content above trivial difficulty the probability term approaches 0. Investment never positive EV. | PROXY_CONVERGENCE fused entity death behavior: reset to inactive (not deleted) preferred. If deletion occurs, both component slots must be returned. Do not produce a system where rational play is to under-invest in the fused entity's kit. |
| Respawn Friction in Zoo Builds | Last Epoch Necromancer 33-minion composition (diehard forum post) | When full zoo dies, player must spend extended time recasting all unit types: "You have to spend a long time recasting them until you have them all back online." Player is nearly helpless during this window. | Resurrection mechanics designed for single-type builds (one recast button) do not scale to multi-type compositions. Each type requires a separate skill activation. 33 minions across 6 skill types = 6+ seconds of recasting minimum. | DUAL_PROXY: if both proxy types die simultaneously, a single "resummon all" action should restore both. Do NOT require the player to separately re-summon each of 2 proxy types after a wipe. |

## Synthesis — Rules for Gandalf's Matrix

1. **Multi-axis pairing is the mark of a real system.** Every strong genre pairing covers at least two complementarity axes simultaneously (role coverage + trigger interplay; spatial contrast + cadence contrast; etc.). Single-axis pairings are perceived as "just a second minion." Design all CONVERGENCE pairs and DUAL_PROXY pools to satisfy two or more axes.

2. **Trigger interplay is the strongest signal for CONVERGENCE eligibility.** When Proxy A creates a condition that Proxy B consumes — or A's count directly enables B's power ceiling (PoE Carrion Golem + Zombies) — the two are functionally inseparable. That inseparability is what makes a fused entity feel earned. Pairs where the two types could operate fully independently make weaker CONVERGENCE candidates.

3. **Fused entity legibility requires visible inheritance from both parents.** The SMT P5R fusion menu (player-selected inherited skills, labeled by parent) is the gold standard. The PoE Animate Guardian (wearing visible gear) is the gameplay equivalent. Soup = inheritance is invisible. PROXY_CONVERGENCE fused entity stat card must show two labeled columns of inherited properties, not a blended description.

4. **Preview-before-commit is mandatory for investment-grade fusions.** Dragon Quest Monsters Synthesis shows the outcome before consuming parents. If PROXY_CONVERGENCE requires building a 2-proxy kit to trigger the fusion, the player must see the fused entity's behavior at kit-creation time, not at first deployment.

5. **Loss risk suppresses investment toward underperformance — every time.** PoE Animate Guardian is the definitive proof. Design the fused entity's death behavior as: reset to inactive (not deleted). If deletion occurs, return both component slots. Do not produce a system where rational play is to under-invest in the fused entity.

6. **DUAL_PROXY pools of 3 must be differentiated by playstyle, not by power level.** If one option is strictly dominant in DPS, it will be chosen exclusively. Differentiate by: (a) clear speed vs boss kill, (b) mobile vs stationary encounter fit, (c) survivability vs damage ceiling. All three must read as real decisions for different players.

7. **Buff-providers (Warcry/Buff Spirit, Resource Conduit) pair with almost anything, but cap their ceiling.** Every ARPG studied includes a pure-buff minion that elevates the army. The failure mode is scaling to mandatory: GGG had to nerf PoE aurabot repeatedly because the buff magnitude made the DPS proxy dysfunctional without it. The DPS proxy should be functional at ~70% of ceiling without the buff proxy present.

8. **Do not pair two mobile-chaser types as a DUAL_PROXY option without validated pathfinding.** D2's experience in narrow geometry proves two mobile chasers compound pathing failures. At least one proxy in every DUAL_PROXY pair should be anchored (stationary or area-locked) so they cannot block each other.

9. **Avoid DUAL_PROXY pairs that collapse to a single function.** If two proxy types both end up just hitting the player's target with different attack patterns, the pair reads as "two damage dealers." One proxy must handle a non-DPS function (buff, resource, zone control, aggro) for the pair to feel like genuine coverage.

10. **Fused entity naming should compound both parent identity words.** "Fighting Anchor" (Passive Fighter + Terrain Anchor) works because both parent words survive. Names that reference neither parent produce an identity rupture — the fused entity feels disconnected from the investment that created it. This mirrors both SMT (Arcana identity preserved through fusion) and D2 Iron Golem (named after the item type that created it). PROXY_CONVERGENCE entity names should be derived compound identifiers from both source types.
