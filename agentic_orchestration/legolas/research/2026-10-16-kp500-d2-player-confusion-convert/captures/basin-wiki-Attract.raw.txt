*Only applies to one normal or minion monster (with [[Altering AI|a few exceptions]]) within radius, but [[Champion]], [[Unique (monster)|Unique]], [[Super Unique]] and [[boss]] monsters can attack the attracted if it is their closest target. Attracted will also attack anything (normally the closest target again).


[[File:Attract.png|center|frame|Although Attract is best cast on a selected target, this is not necessary: an eligible target just needs to be within radius of cursor (monsters do not have to be within radius to attack attracted).]]


*Gain [[experience]] from monsters killed by attracted, and those killed while attracted.
*Target may continue to be attacked after expiry until attacker's next AI check.
*Friendly [[aura]]s and buffs supplied by players and allies do not apply to attracted, although hostile auras and debuffs still apply.
*Friendly [[Aura Enchanted]] Unique monster auras do not apply to attracted but buffs which have not expired still do, as do hostile monster auras and debuffs.
*Cannot be overwritten, even by itself. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/113968-chance-to-cast-and-curse-tests/&do=findComment&comment=1425193]
