After trading blows with the Paladin and facing the fire of righteousness burning in his eyes, an enemy will sometimes be struck with a divine epiphany and momentarily repent his past undertakings. So complete is the transformation, that the converted will turn to slay its former comrades.}}

*Chance to convert applied on attack.
*Only converts normal and minion monsters (with [[Altering AI|a few exceptions]]).


[[File:Conversion.gif|center|frame|Overlay above convert.]]


*When clvl < mlvl, clvl is used to calculate convert [[MonLvl#Defense rating|defense]], [[MonLvl#Attack rating|attack rating]] and [[MonLvl#Damage|damage]], but [[MonLvl#Life|life]] is still calculated using mlvl. When clvl >= mlvl, mlvl continues to be used to calculate all level-adjusted stats. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/121381-onderduiker-clarification-please-on-conversion/&do=findComment&comment=1505822]
*While converted, monsters are affected by friendly buffs and [[aura]]s and hostile debuffs and auras. Any buff, debuff, [[Poison Damage]] or [[Open Wounds]] that has not expired before conversion will remain afterwards: a monster can be cursed with [[Amplify Damage]] before conversion to maximise damage returned by [[Thorns]]. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/109036-how-effective-is-thorns/&do=findComment&comment=1380476]
*Converted monsters capable of spawning, healing or resurrecting other converts will not do so; [[Pain Worm]]s from infested converts are not converts, and behave as normal. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/121381-onderduiker-clarification-please-on-conversion/&do=findComment&comment=1505822]
*Gain [[experience]] from monsters killed by converts, but not from those killed while converted.
*Converts will not follow [[Paladin (Diablo II)|Paladin]], or be transported when using portals or [[Teleport]].
*Auras may still apply for up to two seconds after Conversion expires.
*Upon expiry, mlvl is used to calculate all level-adjusted stats again; {{Warn|=however, when clvl < mlvl then life is 1/256.}} [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/118210-handling-pis-with-conversion/] [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/121381-onderduiker-clarification-please-on-conversion/&do=findComment&comment=1505822] [https://planetdiablo.eu/forum/threads/der-bekehrungs-bug.1663002/]
