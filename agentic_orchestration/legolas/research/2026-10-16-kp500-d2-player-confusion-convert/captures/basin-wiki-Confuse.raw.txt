[[File:Confuse.gif|center|frame|Overlay above [[curse]]d target.]]


*Can only be cast on normal and minion monsters (with [[Altering AI|a few exceptions]]), but [[Champion]], [[Unique (monster)|Unique]], [[Super Unique]] and [[boss]] monsters can attack the confused.
*Target will continue executing its last action prior to its next AI check, then become confused.
*Confused can attack anything, including other confused monsters: nearest target is normally attacked.
*Gain [[experience]] from monsters killed by confused monsters, but not from those killed while confused.
*Target may remain confused after expiry until its next AI check.
*Friendly [[aura]]s and buffs supplied by players and allies do not apply to confused monsters, although hostile auras and debuffs still apply.
*Friendly [[Aura Enchanted]] Unique monster auras and buffs which have not expired still apply to confused monsters, but now so do hostile monster auras and buffs: confused monsters can be cursed, which may overwrite Confuse.
*Cannot be overwritten by the following:
**[[Dim Vision]]
**[[Terror]]
**[[Attract]]
**[[Taunt]]
**[[Grim Ward]]
**[[Hit Blinds Target]]
*Also cannot be overwritten by itself: once a monster is confused, Confuse must expire before it can be confused again. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/113968-chance-to-cast-and-curse-tests/&do=findComment&comment=1425193]
