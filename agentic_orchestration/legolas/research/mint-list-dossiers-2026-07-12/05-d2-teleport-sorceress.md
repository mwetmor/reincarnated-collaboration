## Build identity (2–4 sentences)

The Teleport Sorceress is the D2 archetype where the MOVEMENT SKILL IS THE LOOP: Teleport to pack → cast spell → Teleport to next pack before enemies can react. The mechanics of Teleport (instant repositioning anywhere on screen, costs mana, has a brief animation that is shortened by faster-cast-rate / FCR breakpoints) enable an engagement rhythm unique in ARPG history: the player is never walking, never running — they are always teleporting. This kit identity is entirely defined by two investments: maximum FCR (to minimize Teleport animation delay) and enough damage to kill before the next Teleport. The Enigma runeword (1.10 patch) extended Teleport to all other D2 classes — but the Sorceress's native access, zero-opportunity-cost (no Enigma required), and perfect FCR scaling make her the canonical representative of this identity.

## Raw descriptors (not engine keys)

**geo:** Large-AoE coverage via area spells (Blizzard: large falling-ice zone; Frozen Orb: spinning ring projectile covering large area; Lightning: cone/bolt spread). Player is always far from the damage zone center — throws AoE from a distance.

**ctrl:** Damage-pure; Blizzard and Frozen Orb have minor slowing/chilling effect but this is secondary to the damage. Teleport itself is the primary defensive tool (reposition rather than mitigate).

**mob:** Teleport IS the mobility verb — the highest-mobility movement in D2. The build DEFINES its mobility through Teleport; all farm routes are designed around Teleport's instant repositioning. No walk cycle; no run; just teleport.

**def:** Glass cannon / evasion-by-teleport. The Sorceress has low life pool. Defense strategy is entirely Teleport-based: move before hits land. Items like Shako (life/mana), Vipermagi (MF + resist), and Mara's Kaleidoscope (resists) provide moderate sustain but no armor.

**econ:** Mana-intensive; Teleport costs mana (reduced by FCR items + mana pool investment); spells cost mana; the build requires significant mana pool + regeneration via Insight (Meditation aura on mercenary) or Mana Potions. Spirit/Infinity on mercenary is common for late-game mana sustain.

**elem:** Cold primary (Blizzard = dominant form; Frozen Orb = second canonical form); Lightning alternative (Nova/Lightning builds — same Teleport identity, different damage element); Fire (Fire Sorc = less Teleport-reliant, but still uses it).
