# Evidence — poe1-cwdt-loop (CWDT Self-Hit Loop)
**Crawl date:** 2026-07-22
**Sources:** WebSearch poewiki.net CWDT (2026-07-22); Ward Loop search (2026-07-22)

## Dossier claims targeted
- Core skills: Cast when Damage Taken + Forbidden Rite/VD; geo: large-AOE; element: chaos
- Economy: proc; self-hit loop

## Claim 1 — CWDT mechanic
Source: WebSearch poewiki.net CWDT (2026-07-22)
"triggers supported spells when you take (528–3272) total damage from hits." Threshold scales by gem level. Cooldown: 0.25 seconds. "All damage counters reset to 0 upon entering new area." Counter resets while skill is on cooldown.

## Claim 2 — Self-hit source
Forbidden Rite: "40% of maximum Life and 25% of maximum Energy Shield as Chaos Damage" per cast. The self-hit satisfies CWDT's damage threshold, triggering the loop.

## Claim 3 — Volatile Dead variant
Alternative loop driver: Volatile Dead fires orbs that home toward player (self-target), dealing AoE chaos. Self-hits from VD also satisfy CWDT.

## Claim 4 — Ward Loop variant
With high Ward (Olroth's Resolve or similar ward gear), self-damage is absorbed by ward before reaching life. Ward regenerates quickly. This allows the loop to fire indefinitely without dying — "self-sustaining spell loop." Loop spells deal actual damage to enemies while player takes absorbed self-hits.
