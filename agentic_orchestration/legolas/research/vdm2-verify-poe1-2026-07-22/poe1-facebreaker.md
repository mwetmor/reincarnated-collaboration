## Claim 1 — Facebreaker gloves mechanic
Source: WebSearch poewiki.net (2026-07-22)
Facebreaker unique Strapped Mitts: "(600–1000)% more Physical Damage with Unarmed Melee Attacks." This is the defining item — it converts unarmed attacks from weak baseline to absurdly high multiplier.

## Claim 2 — Unarmed requirement
The kit requires no weapon in main hand and no off-hand weapon. All damage comes from the Facebreaker glove modifier + passive tree scaling.

## Claim 3 — Infernal Blow
Explodes killed enemies, dealing fire damage (8% of max life of nearby enemies within radius). Provides AoE clear with physical → fire conversion. Dossier geo=small-AOE confirmed.

## Claim 4 — Ancestral Warchief totem
Grants "8–18% more Melee Damage while Totem is Active." Used as a damage amp buff while the player attacks unarmed. Standard pairing.
