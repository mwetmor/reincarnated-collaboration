Source: WebSearch poewiki.net Inpulsa (2026-07-22)

## Claim 3 — Chain/cascade mechanic
Herald of Ice explosions shatter nearby frozen enemies, which in turn trigger more Herald of Ice explosions. Inpulsa lightning explosions shock frozen enemies which then shatter faster. Combined: cascading screen-clear from player movement + Shield Charge speed. Dossier "chain" geo and "proc" economy confirmed.

## Claim 4 — Movement as trigger
"no attack button in the loop at all; movement is the trigger." Confirmed: player runs (Shield Charge as movement skill), packs are shock-frozen by one strong hit, then Inpulsa + Herald of Ice chain propagates automatically.
