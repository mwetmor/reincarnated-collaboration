# Evidence — poe1-ward-loop (Ward Loop)
**Crawl date:** 2026-07-22
**Sources:** WebSearch pathofexile.com CWDT Ward Loop (2026-07-22); WebSearch poecurrency.com Ward Loop explained (2026-07-22)

## Dossier claims targeted
- Core skills: CWDT + Summon Skeletons + Forbidden Rite; geo: multi-spawn; element: chaos

## Claim 1 — Ward mechanic
Source: WebSearch (2026-07-22)
"Ward is a yellow bar above your Life bar, which will disappear when you take damage and reduce the damage you take by the amount of Ward you had. Ward will regenerate all at once after a short delay." High ward = take zero life damage from hits below the ward total.

## Claim 2 — Loop mechanic
With Olroth's Resolve or high ward gear: self-damage from CWDT-triggered Forbidden Rite (or other self-hit spells) is completely absorbed by ward. Ward regens quickly. This allows CWDT loop to fire continuously without depleting life.

## Claim 3 — CWDT specifics
Source: WebSearch (2026-07-22)
CWDT threshold: "528–3272 damage from hits." Cooldown: 0.25 sec. The self-hit from Forbidden Rite or summoned skeleton deaths feeds this threshold.

## Claim 4 — Forbidden Rite self-damage
"40% of maximum Life and 25% of maximum Energy Shield as Chaos Damage" per cast. When ward fully absorbs this, the player takes no effective damage but the CWDT counter accumulates.

## Claim 5 — Summon Skeletons in the loop
Skeletons die rapidly (from Forbidden Rite or enemy hits), their deaths release bone explosions. Also used to continuously feed the CWDT counter or as the damaging payload in the loop.
