## Claim 1 — Blood Magic keystone
Blood Magic keystone removes mana from character — all skill costs are paid with life instead. This enables stacking more attributes on gear instead of mana. The character's life pool becomes the resource for all abilities.

## Claim 2 — Pain Attunement
Source: WebSearch poewiki.net Passive_Skill:Pain~attunement (2026-07-22)
Pain Attunement keystone: "30% more Spell Damage when on Low Life." Low life condition achieved by reserving >50% of life via Arrogance Support linking to auras.

## Claim 3 — Arrogance Support + life reservation
Arrogance Support allows auras to reserve life instead of mana. With Blood Magic (no mana) + Arrogance (life reserves for auras), player can maintain permanent low life and gain Pain Attunement's damage bonus. This is the defining mechanic of the "Low Life" or "Blood Magic" spell damage archetype.
