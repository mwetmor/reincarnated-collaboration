# Evidence — poe1-edc (Essence Drain + Contagion)
**Crawl date:** 2026-07-22
**Sources:** poedb.tw/us/Essence_Drain (direct fetch 2026-07-22); poedb.tw/us/Contagion (direct fetch 2026-07-22)

## Dossier claims targeted
- Core skills: Essence Drain + Contagion; geo: chain; element: chaos
- Economy: mana spend

## Claim 1 — Essence Drain
Source: https://poedb.tw/us/Essence_Drain (2026-07-22)
"Chaos damage spell." Projectile applies DoT debuff on hit. "42–2170.2 Base Chaos Damage per second." ADE: 160%. Mana: 8–23. Cast time: 0.70 sec. Duration: 3.8 seconds.

## Claim 2 — Contagion
Source: https://poedb.tw/us/Contagion (2026-07-22)
"Spell, AoE, Duration, Chaos." "If an enemy dies while affected by Contagion, the debuff spreads to other enemies." Base radius: 1.7 metres. Mana: 5–20. Cast time: 0.60 sec. Duration: 5 sec.

## Claim 3 — Spread mechanic (load-bearing)
When enemy with BOTH ED and Contagion dies, Contagion spreads BOTH debuffs to nearby enemies. This is the chain-clear mechanic. Confirmed: "the resulting Contagion proc will spread both effects." Dossier geo=chain confirmed.

## Claim 4 — Life leech
"You are healed for a portion of the debuff damage" — ED provides life recovery from DoT ticking.

## Claim 5 — Economy
Two-button setup: Contagion (setup), ED (deliver). Single cast each; spread does the rest.

## Claim 6 — Ailment note
Contagion and ED are chaos DoT effects, NOT poison. "wither" in dossier ailments likely refers to Withering Step or Wither totem commonly paired in the build, not innate to the skills.

## Anomaly noted
Dossier ailments: "poison + wither." Neither ED nor Contagion apply poison or wither innately. These are typically added via support (Wither Support) or separate skill. Flag for W5.
