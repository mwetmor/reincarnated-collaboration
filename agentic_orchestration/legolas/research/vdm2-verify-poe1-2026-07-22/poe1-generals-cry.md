# Evidence — poe1-generals-cry (General's Cry)
**Crawl date:** 2026-07-22
**Sources:** poedb.tw/us/Generals_Cry (direct fetch 2026-07-22)

## Dossier claims targeted
- Core skills: General's Cry + host attack; geo: small-AOE; element: physical

## Claim 1 — Skill type
Source: https://poedb.tw/us/Generals_Cry (2026-07-22)
Warcry. "Performs a warcry, taunting nearby enemies to attack the user, and summoning Mirage Warriors from nearby corpses."

## Claim 2 — Mirage warrior count
Maximum 5 summoned (6 with quality). Each "uses a linked attack skill once before dissipating."

## Claim 3 — Corpse requirement
"Summons warriors from 2 corpses per 5 Power, minimum 1 corpse." Power = exerted-enemies count.

## Claim 4 — Cooldown / mana
Cooldown: 3.00 seconds (fixed as of v3.24.0). Mana: 15–19.

## Claim 5 — Damage penalty on linked skill
"Supported skills deal 51–60% reduced damage" (L1–L20). Mirage warriors hit at reduced effectiveness.

## Claim 6 — "host attack" note
Dossier "host attack" means the attack skill linked to General's Cry, which the mirage warriors perform. Any melee attack can fill this role (Earthquake, Earthshatter, etc.).
