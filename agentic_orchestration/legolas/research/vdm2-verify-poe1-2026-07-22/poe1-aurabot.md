# Evidence — poe1-aurabot (Aurabot)
**Crawl date:** 2026-07-22
**Sources:** WebSearch poewiki.net auras poe1; poedb.tw/us/ (general aura knowledge)

## Dossier claims targeted
- Core skills: aura suite + Smite (token); geo: large-AOE; element: n/a
- Economy: mana (full reserve); "near-zero personal damage whose output is other players' power"

## Claim 1 — Aura reservation model
Standard auras reserve 35–50% mana each. Full reservation of mana pool means the character has no mana left to actively cast skills. Common auras: Hatred, Wrath, Grace, Determination, Haste, Precision, Purity of Elements, etc.

## Claim 2 — Smite (token damage)
Smite is a melee spell that also grants an aura buff on hit. Used as the single "attack" the aurabot uses to stay legal as a combat participant — token personal damage.

## Claim 3 — Large-AOE (aura radius)
Aura radius scales with AoE modifiers. Typically extended via cluster jewels or Generosity Support to very large radii to cover entire party. Dossier large-AOE confirmed as the aura projection radius.

## Claim 4 — Economy: full-reserve model
"Reserves the entire resource pool to project auras onto ALLIES — a build with near-zero personal damage." Confirmed: this is the canonical aurabot archetype. Character invests all attribute and skill points into aura effect, aura reservation reduction, and aura radius. Personal damage is incidental.
