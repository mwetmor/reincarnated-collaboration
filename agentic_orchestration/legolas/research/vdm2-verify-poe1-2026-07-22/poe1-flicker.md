## Claim 1 — Skill type
Source: https://poedb.tw/us/Flicker_Strike (2026-07-22)

"15% chance to gain a Frenzy Charge on Hit" (scaling with quality). Cooldown of 2 seconds "can be bypassed by expending a Frenzy Charge." With enough frenzy charges via stack sources, cooldown is effectively zero — rapid successive teleport-strikes.

## Claim 5 — Frenzy (separate skill)
"Frenzy" as listed in dossier core_skills is a bow/claw attack skill that reliably generates frenzy charges on hit. Used as the charge generation engine before switching to Flicker Strike for the main loop.
