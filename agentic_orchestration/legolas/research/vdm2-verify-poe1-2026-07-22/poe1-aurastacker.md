# Evidence — poe1-aurastacker (Solo Aurastacker)
**Crawl date:** 2026-07-22
**Sources:** WebSearch poewiki.net auras; poedb.tw general knowledge 2026-07-22

## Dossier claims targeted
- Core skills: aura suite (Zealotry/Hatred/etc); geo: small-AOE; element: cold
- Economy: full reserve; "Reserve EVERYTHING — a dozen-plus auras"

## Claim 1 — Reserve model
Solo Aurastacker reserves all mana (and often life via Arrogance Support) to run 12+ auras simultaneously. Auras stack multiplicatively — each aura multiplies the effect of others through the damage/defense formulae.

## Claim 2 — Offense model
Cold element from Hatred (adds cold damage) + Zealotry (spell damage + consecrated ground). Dossier elem_raw=cold is approximately correct for the cold-stacking variant (other variants use lightning, etc.).

## Claim 3 — Small-AOE geo
Auras extend to cover the player and immediate surroundings. Unlike Aurabot (large-AOE for party), solo aurastacker selects "small-radius" since no party radius needed. Dossier geo_raw=small-AOE confirmed.

## Claim 4 — Economy
"Reserve EVERYTHING" confirmed. No mana for active skills — output is the compounded effect of own-aura multipliers. Build is self-sufficient by having defenses from Grace/Determination and offense from Hatred/Wrath/Zealotry all amplifying each other.
