# Evidence — poe1-archmage (Archmage Mana Stacker)
**Crawl date:** 2026-07-22
**Sources:** poedb.tw/us/Archmage_Support (direct fetch 2026-07-22)

## Dossier claims targeted
- Core skills: Archmage Support + Ball Lightning/Manabond
- Economy: mana scaling — "unreserved MANA — the resource pool IS the weapon"
- Element: lightning

## Claim 1 — What Archmage Support does
Source: https://poedb.tw/us/Archmage_Support (2026-07-22)
"lightning spell damage scales off unreserved mana." Adds flat lightning damage per 10 unreserved mana. The resource pool is literal weapon damage.

## Claim 2 — Delivery type
The supported spell (Ball Lightning / Manabond) is the active delivery. Archmage is the modifier. Kit is "mana stacker" — unreserved mana pool stacked as high as possible via Mind over Matter interactions and large mana pool investment.

## Claim 3 — Element
Lightning — confirmed by Archmage Support adding lightning damage to the supported skill.

## Claim 4 — Economy model
Reserve model: the kit's damage output scales off how much mana is NOT reserved. Via Mind over Matter, mana also acts as defense. Dossier "resource pool IS the weapon and (via MoM) the shield" is mechanically accurate.

## Anomaly noted
Dossier core_skills lists "Ball Lightning/Manabond" — these are separate skills. Manabond is a flat lightning damage spell that hits all enemies equal to unreserved mana and scales very directly; Ball Lightning is the traditional slow-orb carrier. Both are valid Archmage vehicles but represent two distinct sub-variants of this kit. Flag as architecture note — not a contradiction.
