## Publisher A: Blink — "Stylized RPG Weapons" Series

**Register:** Stylized fantasy-medieval-European / heroic
**Style:** Low-to-medium poly, hand-painted style, evolving weapon variants (Basic / Medium / Epic per type)
**Pipeline:** Built-in + URP + HDRP (all three across the series)

Blink offers the most systematically organized weapon series on the Asset Store, with per-archetype packs that can be purchased individually or as bundles. The series is the closest Asset Store analog to a substrate-friendly procurement baseline.

## Publisher B: Synty Studios — POLYGON Series

**Register:** Low-poly stylized (POLYGON aesthetic) — the definitive "indie ARPG polygon" aesthetic
**Style:** Very low poly, flat shading, vibrant palette, modular pieces
**Pipeline:** Built-in + URP (HDRP not listed for most packs; check before purchase)
**Pricing model:** One-time purchase OR SyntyPass subscription ($30/month, 130+ packs)

Synty's POLYGON series covers multiple aesthetic registers through themed packs. For Reincarnated, the most relevant packs are Fantasy Kingdom, Samurai, Dungeon Realms, and Knights.

### Key Synty weapon packs

| Pack | One-time price | Weapon count | Archetype coverage | Register |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| POLYGON Fantasy Kingdom | $349.99 | 183 items (111 complete + 72 modular pieces) — arrows, bows, quivers, axes, hammers, maces, sceptres, shields, spears, staffs, swords, daggers, elephant guns | Full 15-archetype breadth (swords/daggers/spears/maces/bows/staves/blunderbuss/etc.) | Medieval-heroic-European |
| POLYGON Samurai Pack | $29.99 | 245 total assets; weapons include tonfa, tanto, shuriken, sai, odachi, naginata, kunai, jumonji yari, bo staff | Twin daggers (#2), battle spear (#3), throwing knives (#8), wand/staff analog | Ancient-East-Asian |
| POLYGON Dungeon Realms | $199.99 | 81 weapon assets (axes ×13, knives ×5, mace ×7, shields ×8, spears ×8, staff ×6, swords ×27, hammer ×6) + 69 character attachments (shoulder armor ×7, helmets, masks, hoods) | Greatsword/twin daggers/mace/spear/staff — covers ~8 of 15 archetypes | Dark-dungeon-European |
| POLYGON Knights Pack | ~$99.99 (est.) | Medieval knights-focused; swords, lances, maces, shields | Greatsword/mace/spear family | Medieval-heroic-European |

**Subscription economics:** At $30/month, all four packs (combined $680+) are accessible within 2 months of subscription. For a full-coverage procurement, the SyntyPass model is dramatically cheaper than one-time purchase of all packs.

**URP flag:** Confirmed compatible on Fantasy Kingdom and Samurai. Verify Dungeon Realms and Knights before purchase — "URP conversion tutorial available" suggests not natively URP out-of-box.

**Archetype coverage note:** POLYGON Fantasy Kingdom alone covers 12–13 of 15 archetypes by weapon category breadth. The three underserved archetypes (censer #13, holy symbol #14, war-trumpet #15) are not explicitly in any Synty pack found.

**Aesthetic register:**
- Fantasy Kingdom: `tech_level`: medieval; `tone`: heroic; `cultural_lineage`: European
- Samurai: `tech_level`: ancient; `tone`: heroic; `cultural_lineage`: East-Asian
- Dungeon Realms: `tech_level`: medieval; `tone`: grim; `cultural_lineage`: European

## Publisher C: Magic Pig Games / Infinity PBR — PBR Fantasy RPG Series

**Register:** Realistic-fantasy PBR
**Style:** High-poly, physically based rendering, detailed textures; closer to AAA realistic than stylized
**Pipeline:** Built-in + URP + HDRP (both packs confirmed tri-pipeline)

| Pack | Price | Content (keywords) | Reviews | Archetype coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weapons & Armor PBR Pack #1 | $60 | Axe, knife, sword, spear, dagger, poleaxe, staff, helmet, armor | 28 reviews, 447 favorites | Greatsword/twin daggers/spear/staff family + armor |
| Weapons & Armor PBR Pack #2 | $60 | Axe, sword, dagger, spear, staff, knife; 2 GB file | 8 reviews | Extended coverage of same archetypes |

**Style note:** These are PBR realistic-style assets. They do NOT match Reincarnated's locked style register (HD-2D Octopath-coded painterly + cel-shaded). They are relevant for multi-genre profiles (grimdark / realistic fantasy) but require consideration for Reincarnated Profile A style coherence.

**URP flag:** Both packs confirmed tri-pipeline.

**Aesthetic register:**
- `tech_level`: medieval
- `tone`: grim (realistic-texture leans toward grim, not heroic)
- `cultural_lineage`: European

**Summary of gaps:**

- **Critical gaps (no Asset Store coverage found):** Censer/thurible (#13), Holy symbol/icon (#14), War-trumpet/horn (#15). These three archetypes have no dedicated Asset Store weapon pack. They are Meshy-generation candidates.
- **Thin coverage (one fragmented option):** Crossbow (#6), Blunderbuss (#7), Throwing knives (#8), Tome (#12). One or two poor options exist; dedicated quality packs are absent.
- **Orb (#10) gap:** Orbs are in prop/environment packs, not weapon packs. Procurement requires sourcing from prop categories (Mega Fantasy Props, RPG Crystal Kit) or Meshy generation.
- **East-Asian register gaps:** Mace/warhammer analogs, crossbow, blunderbuss, wand, censer, holy symbol, horn — all absent in East-Asian register. Coverage is primarily swords/spears/daggers/throwing.
- **Grimdark/dark-fantasy register:** Synty Dungeon Realms covers sword/dagger/spear/mace family. Wands, staves, tome, censer, horn, holy symbol, blunderbuss are absent in dark register.
