[[File:Mana Stolen.gif|right|frame|Overlay when mana is returned.]]
{{Mb|=Mana After Each Kill}} adds mana after each kill made by the equipping player character, although it does not apply when the kill is made by player character’s [[mercenary]], [[pet]]s (including sentry [[traps]] and [[Hydra]]) or [[Conversion|converts]], or monsters killed by other monsters due to [[Attract]] or [[Confuse]].
*Does not apply to kills made passively by the following:
**[[Blade Shield]]
**[[Holy Fire]] radial damage
**[[Holy Freeze]] radial damage
**[[Holy Shock]] radial damage
**[[Sanctuary (skill)|Sanctuary]] radial damage
*Does not apply to kills made by returned damage from the following:
**[[Attacker Takes Damage]]
**[[Attacker Takes Lightning Damage]]
**[[Iron Golem]] thorns
**[[Iron Maiden]]
**[[Thorns]]
**[[Shiver Armor]]
**[[Spirit of Barbs]]
|}
