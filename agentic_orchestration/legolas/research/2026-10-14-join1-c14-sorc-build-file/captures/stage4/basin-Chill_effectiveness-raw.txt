{{Unit nav}}
{{Intro}}
*Unit chill effectiveness determines the [[attack rate]] and [[movement speed]] modifiers applied when chilled by applying [[Cold Damage|cold length]], or [[freeze length]] or [[Freezes Target]] to units other than normal and minion monsters.
*Also determines the maximum attack rate and movement speed modifiers applied by [[Holy Freeze]]: level 12 Holy Freeze may apply -50 attack rate and movement speed to targets with -50 chill effectiveness, but it only applies -25 to targets with -25 chill effectiveness. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/120504-chill-effectiveness/]
*[[Bone Wall]] and [[Bone Prison]] have 0 chill effectiveness, while all other [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] and [[pet]]s have -50 chill effectiveness (like players).
|}
