*[[Cast rate]]
*Can only apply to [[Sorceress]].
*Skill level set when cast.
*Can apply freeze length to any hostile target that executes a successful [[melee]] attack which damages the Sorceress. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/114320-sorc-with-a-spear/&do=findComment&comment=1424978]
*Can be active at the same time as both [[Cyclone Armor]] and [[Blade Shield]] but not [[Bone Armor]], [[Shiver Armor]], [[Chilling Armor]] or [[Psychic Ward]]{{RotW-inline}} (last one cast is active).
