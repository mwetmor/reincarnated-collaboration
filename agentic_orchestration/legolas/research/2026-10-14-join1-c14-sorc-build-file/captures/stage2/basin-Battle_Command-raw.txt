*Increases skill levels of caster and all allied players, [[mercenary|mercenaries]] and [[pet]]s within area of effect.


[[File:Battle Command.png|center|thumb|600px|Area of effect has ~12 2/3 yard [[radius]]. Although gaps appear between [[missile]]s as they move further away, these are not normally large enough for targets to be missed.]]


[[File:Battle Command.gif|center|frame|Overlay displayed at feet when active.]]


* [[Cast rate]]
* Also applies to itself, so casting a second time will result in 10 second longer duration.
* Since it is applied to pets as well, in order to maximize the efficiency of pets using skills it's the best to cast Battle Command before summoning a pet (to increase the summoning skill level), then summon a pet, and then cast Battle Command again (to increase the pet's skills levels).
* Last Battle Command cast is applied, so higher level can be overwritten by lower level cast later (although this will only affect its duration).
