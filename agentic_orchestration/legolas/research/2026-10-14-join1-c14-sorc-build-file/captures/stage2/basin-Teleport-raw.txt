{{U|=A Sorceress trained in this arcane skill has the ability to traverse the Ether, instantly rematerializing in another location. Without the aid of waypoints or portals, she may teleport anywhere within her immediate vicinity. Though not suitable for larger distances, a Sorceress can make good use of this spell for evading capture, or to reach otherwise inaccessible areas.}}


==Detail==
*[[Cast rate]]
*{{Warn|=While level >23 costs 1 [[mana]], level >24 can be cast with '''no''' mana.}}
*Transports caster and dependent [[mercenary]] and [[pet]]s to targeted point. Target does not have to be in line of sight, but there must be space for caster to stand.
*When teleporting to the top edge of the screen using the right mouse button, distance teleported can be increased by opening the Character (C key by default) or [[mercenary|Hireling]] (O) screen on the left side, or the Inventory (I) or Skill (S) screen on the right side. [https://planetdiablo.eu/forum/threads/arbeitshread-frogger.1734534/post-26605511]
*[[Conversion|Converts]] and [[Reanimate|Returned]] do not teleport with caster.
*Extremely useful for repositioning caster and dependants, escaping or travelling quickly.
*While teleporting dependants are stacked on top of caster, and are targeted by [[missile]] attacks instead of the caster. However, caster may still be targeted by [[melee]] attacks and persistent or piercing missiles with [[Last Collide]] enabled, and any [[Radius|radial]] effects still apply.
