*Freeze length can freeze normal or minion monsters, and chill all other units, when applied, as long as they are not [[Immune to Cold]] and they do not have 0 [[chill effectiveness]].
*Length of freezing or chilling is affected by target [[Cold Resist]] and difficulty level penalties (1/2 in Nightmare and 1/4 in Hell).
*Applied independently of [[Cold Damage|cold length]] and [[Freezes Target|Freezes target]]: 2 second freeze length and 10 second cold length will both be applied, resulting in 2 seconds freezing and 10 seconds chilling of eligible targets.
*Item cold length is converted to freeze length and added to that of [[Ice Arrow]], and [[Freezing Arrow]] explosions.
