{{Unique item nav}}
* [[Annihilus]] is dropped only by [[Diablo (World Event)|Über Diablo]] during the [[World Event]].
* [[Hellfire Torch]] is dropped only by the last Greater Evil boss killed during the [[Pandemonium Event]].
* Lord of Destruction [[Sunder]] charms are dropped only by [[Champion]], [[Unique (monster)|Unique]], [[Superunique]], and [[Boss]] monsters in [[Terror Zone]]s in Resurrected in Lord of Destruction mode only. They do not drop in Reign of the Warlock, and were replaced by Latent and Renewed Sunder charms.
* Latent Sunder Charms can drop from [[Herald]]s, or from any monster, Terrorized and non-Terrorized, using Magic Find, on Hell difficulty only.[https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24261478/diablo-ii-resurrected-ladder-season-14-has-concluded#Notes][https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24296140/diablo-ii-resurrected-ladder-season-15-now-live#Notes] They are like Lord of Destruction Sunder charms.
* Renewed Sunder charms can be obtained by [[Horadric_Cube#Sunder_Charm|upgrading respective Latent Sunder charms in the Horadric Cube]]. The resistance penalty is reduced to the lowest in the range, and they get additional random affixes.
* Player ''can'' have multiple copies of Lord of Destruction or Latent (but not Renewed) Sunder charms in the inventory. However, multiple Sunders ''of the same element'' do not reduce enemy resistance any further, while reducing the player's (see [[Sunder]] for technical details).
: Only one copy of any Renewed Sunder charm is allowed in the inventory at a time, regardless of the element.
* 2.6 [[Rune Word]]s [[Bulwark]], [[Cure]], [[Ground]], [[Hearth]] and [[Temper]] were designed specifically to offset the negative resistance from Sunder charms[https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23899624/diablo-ii-resurrected-ladder-season-three-has-concluded#Rune] (except from [[Black Cleft]]).
