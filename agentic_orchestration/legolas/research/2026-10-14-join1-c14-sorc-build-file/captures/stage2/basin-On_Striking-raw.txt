{{Skill nav}}
{{Intro}}
{{Mb|=Chance to Cast on Striking}} gives a percentage chance to use a skill whenever the equipping player character, [[mercenary]] or [[pet]] uses a [[Item type#Weapon|weapon]] attack which does not miss and is not blocked, or to which [[Dodge]], [[Avoid]], [[Evade]] or [[Weapon Block]] are not applied.
*[[Venom (skill)|Venom]] and [[Enchant]] are cast on the user only.
*[[Poison Nova]] and [[Static Field]] are cast from the user's location.
*The following skills are cast at the target from the user's location:
**[[Firestorm]]
**[[Twister]]
**[[Tornado]]
**[[Bone Spirit]]
**[[Charged Bolt]]
**[[Frozen Orb]]
**[[Diablo|{{Ub|=Diablo}}]] [[Firestorm (Diablo)|Firestorm]]
*All other skills below are cast on or from the target's location.
|}
