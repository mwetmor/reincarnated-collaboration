{{U|=A devastating spell of limited range, Static Field instantly removes a good portion of the health of nearby enemies. Thusly injured, enemies are easy prey to the follow-up attacks of the Sorceress or her party members. Subsequent castings do less damage, but in combination with other spells or a decisive melee attack, a Sorceress can rely on this powerful ability to quickly dispatch even the strongest of foes.}}


==Detail==

*Reduces current [[life]] of targets within radius by 25% when cast, unless target already has <33% life in Nightmare or <50% life in Hell. [[Lightning Damage]] animation won't display on targets to which Static Field can no longer be applied.
*Damage is a percentage of final life, not base life.
*+% [[Lightning Skill Damage]] from [[Lightning Mastery]] and items does not increase damage.
*Target [[Lightning Resist]] reduces damage: LR 50% halves damage, and thus current life is reduced by just 12.5%. Although [[Lower Resist]] and [[Conviction]] can reduce LR >0% and prevent it from decreasing damage, if they result in LR <0% this won't ''increase'' damage. 
*-% [[Enemy Lightning Resistance]] is applied ''after'' Lower Resist and Conviction and if it results in LR <0% this ''can'' increase damage: applying -100% ELR to LR 0% will result in LR -100% and double damage, reducing current life by '''50%'''.
*However, if Lower Resist and Conviction already result in LR <0% then damage increase due to -% ELR will be reduced: at the extreme, if Lower Resist and Conviction already result in LR -100% then there will be '''no''' damage increase due to -% ELR and damage will remain just 25% of current life. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/103919-static-field-lod/]
