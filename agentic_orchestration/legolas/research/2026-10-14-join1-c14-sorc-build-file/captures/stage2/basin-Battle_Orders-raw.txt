*Applies to both current and maximum stamina, life and mana of caster and all allied players, [[mercenary|mercenaries]] and [[pet]]s within area of effect.
**Only applies to base stamina, including that from levelling up, points invested in [[Vitality]] and + Maximum Stamina items.
**Only applies to base [[life]] including that from levelling up, points invested in [[Vitality]] and + Life items.
**Only applies to base [[mana]] including that from levelling up, points invested in [[Energy]] and + Mana items.


[[File:Battle Orders.png|center|thumb|600px|Area of effect has ~12 2/3 yard [[radius]]. Although gaps appear between [[missile]]s as they move further away, these are not normally large enough for targets to be missed.]]


[[File:Battle Orders.gif|center|frame|Overlay displayed at feet when active.]]


*[[Cast rate]]
*Last Battle Orders cast is applied, so higher level can be overwritten by lower level cast later.
*{{Warn|=When active on a mercenary when he or she gains a level, it is reapplied: current life is reduced to maximum life '''before''' Battle Orders was cast when current life with it is greater.}} When current life upon levelling up is less than or equal to maximum life before Battle Orders was cast, it applies to both current and maximum life as usual.
