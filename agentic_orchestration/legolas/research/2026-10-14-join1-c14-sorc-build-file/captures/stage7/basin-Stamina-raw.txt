{{Intro}}
[[File:Stamina.png|thumb|600px|Current and maximum stamina are displayed both on the character screen and the interface bar (when the cursor is held over the stamina meter). Stamina meter turns red when current stamina is less than 25% maximum stamina, and blue when a Stamina [[Shrine]] or [[Stamina Potion]] is active.]]
|}


Stamina normally drains whenever a player character runs in the wilderness; when it runs out, a character can no longer run, and must walk. Stamina displayed is rounded down to the nearest point, but the smallest unit is actually 1/256 point. Stamina varies depending on chosen class at start of game:


{{Classes table}}
|-
|84||95||92||84||79||89||74||86
|}


Stamina gained as a character gains levels also varies depending on class:

{{Classes table}}
|-
| +1||+1.25||+1||+1||+1||+1||+1||+1
|}


Stamina can be increased by allocating attribute points to Vitality. Stamina gained from each additional point also varies depending on class:


{{Classes table}}
|-   
| +1||+1.25||+1||+1||+1||+1||+1||+1
|}


*Stamina can also be increased by bonuses applied in the order listed below. However, character typically gains sufficient stamina from gaining levels and allocating attribute points to Vitality to make these bonuses unnecessary. [[Faster Run/Walk]] and other [[movement speed]] modifiers also reduce the need for higher stamina, since taking less time to travel the same distance means less stamina is drained.
|}

==Increase Maximum Stamina %==
Increase Maximum Stamina % only applies to the following:
*Base stamina
*Stamina gained from gaining levels
*Stamina gained from [[attribute]] points allocated to [[Vitality]]
*+ Maximum Stamina bonuses
It does '''not''' apply to the following:
*Stamina gained from + [[All Attributes]] bonuses
*Stamina gained from + Vitality bonuses
*+ [[Stamina#Maximum Stamina (Based on Character Level)|Maximum Stamina (Based on Character Level)]] bonus
Consequently maximum stamina may not be increased by the expected percentage.

==Maximum Stamina (Based on Character Level)==
{{Mb|=Maximum Stamina (Based on Character Level)}} bonus is not as desirable as + Maximum Stamina bonuses since it is added '''after''' Increase Maximum Stamina %. {{Warn|=Furthermore, displayed value is divided by 256 before being applied, so even at level 99 only <sub>99/256</sub> Maximum Stamina is actually added, '''not''' 99.}}
