*Fire Balls have the same velocity, range and [[size]] as Fire Bolts (although the latter appear smaller visually) but normally apply more damage, potentially to multiple targets within radius of impact. However, with full synergy bonuses and skill level >46 (>47 without any hard points in Meteor), Fire Bolt can apply slightly more damage to a single target while costing much less [[mana]] than Fire Ball.
