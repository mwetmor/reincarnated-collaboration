## Lore/narrative framing

Valkyrie is Lost Ark's female Holy Knight class — a gender-lock lift class that grants female-character access to the Holy Knight identity previously locked to Paladin. Weapon: one-handed sword (both engravings) + shield component (Liberator emphasizes the shield; Shining Knight emphasizes the sword). Tagline from PlayLostArk release page: "The light I shine upon the battlefield shall never fade."

## Build identity (2–4 sentences)

The Shining Knight Valkyrie is the DPS arm of the Valkyrie class — a Light Meter fill-and-burst kit built around a two-stage Z→X commit. She casts 15 skills to fill her Light Meter (each skill contributes 6.7% per Maxroll), triggers Z (Shining Knight identity) to apply party Synergy and grant a "next 3 Holy Blade skills +35% damage" buff, then chains the three buffed Holy Blade skills (Requiem Ash, Requiem Rain, Cataclysm) and finishes with X (Final Splendor) as a single high-damage finisher. Shining Knight then unlocks for the next rotation. The kit is "fully non-positional" — Back Attack affixes are removed from most skills, so the Valkyrie DPS is a stand-and-face-boss identity rather than a positional dodge-around-behind identity.

## Core skill loop

**Two-stage commit (Z → X):**

1. **Fill stage.** Cast 15 skills to fill Light Meter (6.7% per cast, cap at 15). Use low-cooldown Justice skills and Synergy/Counter skills to keep the fill cooldown-rolling. Rotate through Whisper of Judgment, Judgment Stigmata (Justice fillers), Sword of Revelation, Lunging Stab, Approach of Revelation (Starlight/mobility).
2. **Burst stage.** Press Z (Shining Knight identity):
   - Applies party Synergy to nearby enemies
   - Buffs your next 3 Holy Blade skills for +35% extra damage
   - Locks Shining Knight until Final Splendor is used
3. **Chain the three Holy Blade skills:** Requiem Ash, Requiem Rain, Cataclysm.
4. **Press X (Final Splendor):** "a single hit that deals very high damage" — unlocks Shining Knight for next cycle.

**Rotation priority:** "You want to hit your low-cd skills and synergy/buffs first to keep the cooldown rolling, then fill the gaps with filler damage skills" [Maxroll]. Rule: "You should never use any of the main Holy Blade skills without the Shining Knight buff" [Maxroll].

**Perfect Zone mechanic:** Cataclysm converts to "Perfect Combo" with a "Perfect Zone" timing sub-mechanic requiring precise input [Maxroll].

**Commitment:** WIND-UP HIGH. The 15-skill fill IS the commit; the Z→3 skills→X chain must be executed in order. Meteor Strike is explicit long-animation (Spacebar cancel available).

## Delivery verbs and geometry

- **Holy Blade skills** (Requiem Ash, Requiem Rain, Cataclysm) — mid-melee-range sword strikes with light-effect AoE. Cataclysm's "Perfect Zone" implies a spatial trigger.
- **Justice skills** (Whisper of Judgment, Judgment Stigmata) — Filler damage; sword-based, close-mid range.
- **Starlight skills** — Sacred sword summon in left hand (dual-blade or infused strikes) [PlayLostArk framing: "summon a sacred sword in her left hand, wielding it like a dual blade, or infusing her one-handed sword with divine energy for powerful strikes"].
- **Final Splendor (X)** — single-hit high-damage finisher.

Geometry: predominantly melee-mid AoE around the Valkyrie (self-origin large-radius) or frontal cone. Precise footprint per skill not fully extracted from Maxroll's public sections.

## Mobility-while-casting posture

Rated "Good mobility" pro on Shining Knight variant [Maxroll class overview]. Mobility tools:
- **Lunging Stab** — mobility dash + damage
- **Approach of Revelation** — mobility skill
- **Sword of Revelation** — Counter + Move Speed self-buff ("keep up your Move Speed self-buff for Raid Captain efficiency" [Maxroll])
- **Spacebar cancel on Meteor Strike** — animation cancel for long casts

Fully non-positional per Maxroll: "most of your skills with Back Attack affix have it removed, making you fully non-positional." Casts are wind-up-rooted for burst chain; movement is via dedicated mobility skills interspersed.

## Defense verbs

- **Sword of Revelation** — primary Counter skill
- **Foresight Slash** — secondary Counter (also main Synergy skill)
- **Meteor Strike** — long-animation attack with Spacebar cancel option (defensive out)
- **No dedicated shield/block layer** in Shining Knight (contrast with Liberator's shield-focused kit)
- Standard class HP/armor scaling

## Economy

**Primary resource:** Light Meter (Piety Meter in official PlayLostArk terminology).
- Fill rate: 6.7% per skill cast [Maxroll]
- Cap: 15 casts to full
- Trigger: Z (Shining Knight) when full
- The Z→X commit consumes the meter and locks/unlocks around Final Splendor

**Secondary:** Move Speed self-buff maintenance via Sword of Revelation / Lunging Stab.

**Destiny activation counts:** "Destiny activation counts as a skill cast towards identity" [Maxroll] — an economy detail relevant to fill optimization.

## Element / damage mode

**Element:** Holy (Light) — thematic; not a D2-style elemental damage type per se. HIT-based damage. Some skills have Destruction/Stagger implications (relevant for boss-phase content).

## Raw descriptors

**geo:** Self-origin large-radius AoE for Holy Blade skills; frontal/at-target for Justice fillers; Final Splendor single-hit large-area.
**ctrl:** Party Synergy (damage-received debuff) via Shining Knight Z. Destruction/Stagger present on select skills. Counter skills (Sword of Revelation, Foresight Slash) for boss phase.
**mob:** Good — Lunging Stab, Approach of Revelation, Sword of Revelation, Meteor Strike animation cancel. Fully non-positional (BA removed).
**def:** Counter-and-mobility based. No shield/block layer in this build. Standard warrior HP scaling.
**econ:** Light Meter / Piety Meter (6.7% per skill × 15 casts = full) → Z→X commit cycle. Move Speed self-buff maintenance loop.
