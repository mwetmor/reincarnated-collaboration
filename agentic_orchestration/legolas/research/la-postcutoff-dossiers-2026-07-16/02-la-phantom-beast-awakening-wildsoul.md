## Build identity (2–4 sentences)

The Phantom Beast Awakening (PBA) Wildsoul is the timed-super-mode arm of the Wildsoul class. The player fills the Phantom Beast Meter through combat, then presses Z to enter Phantom Beast Awakening state — a 30-second identity form that grants +20% attack speed and +20% move speed and enables an aggressive burst window where each landed skill generates Phantom Beast Spirit stacks (up to 20). Pressing Z again during the window converts those stacks into cooldown reduction — "5% of the remaining cooldowns of your skills (excluding awakening and T-Skill) for each stack consumed" — creating a self-cycling loop where each successful Awakening window shortens the wait for the next one. Maxroll characterizes the resulting cadence as "practically infinite" Awakening access.

## Shapeshift-relevance (GX-02 keystone evidence)

**VERDICT: MEDIUM POSITIVE — temporal-window shapeshift flavor.** Distinct from Ferality's persistent-form shapeshift geometry:

- **Form-entry cost.** Phantom Beast Meter must be filled (main center gauge). Fill-rate is passive baseline + skill-cast accelerated: "use these skills to fill the gauge faster" [Maxroll]. No mana cost at the moment of Z; the cost is the fill-time.
- **Form-locked skill set.** Inside PBA state, the primary damage skills are Ursine Windup, Boulder Bear, Fox Flame, Swish Bear, and Fox Leap [Maxroll]. Notably: **Bear-form and Fox-form skills are BOTH usable inside PBA state** — the temporal window collapses Ferality's form partition. This is the mechanical inverse of the Ferality lock.
- **Form duration and buffs.** 30 seconds (per PlayLostArk spotlight). +20% attack speed, +20% move speed [Maxroll cross-corroborates].
- **Phantom Beast Spirit stack economy.** Up to 20 stacks accumulate through skill landing inside PBA state. Consuming stacks (Z again) provides "5% Cooldown Reduction per stack" and "2% Mana recovery per stack" per Maxroll. Manual cancellation of PBA state is also available (defensive escape).
- **Companion-summon flavor.** From mmorpg.com dev framing: "summon a beast or becoming one." Maxroll's Ferality guide cross-references PBA as "summoning your fox and bear as companions to attack in your stead." PBA carries the summon-adjacent flavor, though its Z-form is a player transformation.

**Implication for RDR GX-02:** if the RDR shapeshift keystone chooses a "temporal super-mode" geometry (cooldown-triggered timed transformation with recharge economy), PBA Wildsoul is the atlas anchor. This is the mode where SHAPESHIFT compounds with COOLDOWN-REDUCTION-AS-SPEND, forming a self-cycling burst-window mechanic. The genre-precedent stacks with D3 Hulking Phantom / Wizard Archon (timed super-form) and D4 Werewolf/Werebear form-swap without persistent identity partition.

## Core skill loop

**Outside PBA state:**
- **Vulpine Velocity** applies synergy (setup skill)
- **Rolling Wheel** functions as filler damage
- Continue filling Phantom Beast Meter through skill usage

**Inside PBA state (post-Z):**
- Rotate: **Ursine Windup, Boulder Bear, Fox Flame, Swish Bear, Fox Leap** as main damage skills
- "There's not a fixed order to use these skills in. You should adapt to boss patterns, distance, and other factors" [Maxroll].
- After landing skills, "activate identity again to reduce cooldowns" (Z consumes accumulated Phantom Beast Spirit stacks)

**Loop shape:** WIND-UP → BURST → RECYCLE. The wind-up is the meter-fill outside form; the burst is the 30s inside form; the recycle is the Z-consume of stacks to compress the next wind-up.

**Commitment:** WIND-UP dominant. Meter-fill wind-up gates burst window. Individual skills inside PBA are instant-cast or near-instant.

## Mobility-while-casting posture

+20% Move Speed inside PBA form (baseline is class-standard). Fox Leap is explicit repositioning (movement-as-skill). Maxroll flags a commitment window: "casting Fox Leap before Phantom Beast Awakening skill will result in missing your 3rd cycle" — the dash has interaction cost with the burst window. Class-level Maxroll rating for PBA: "Good mobility" pro listed [Maxroll class overview] — corroborates the +20% MS advantage and mobility-oriented playstyle.

## Defense verbs

- **Claw:** Counter skill. "Claw is your Counter skill, it also deals Destruction" [Maxroll].
- **PBA manual cancel:** "You can manually cancel your Phantom Beast Awakening state at any time" — used for defensive positioning during boss attacks [Maxroll]. This is a defense verb baked into the identity system.
- **No shield / block / dodge layer.**

## Economy

**Primary resource:** Phantom Beast Meter (main center gauge). Fill-rate accelerated by skill usage; passive baseline in combat.

**Secondary economy:** Phantom Beast Spirit stacks (up to 20) — accumulated by landing skills inside PBA state. Consumed by Z-press to yield cooldown reduction (5%/stack) + mana recovery (2%/stack).

**Meta-loop:** each successful PBA cycle compresses the next fill wait via the CDR consumption — the class is designed to converge on high uptime.

## Element / damage mode

Damage is HIT-based on the transformation skills. No dominant elemental label. Destruction stagger from Claw. No confirmed DoT vector.

## Raw descriptors

**geo:** Bear-heavy melee self-origin; Fox mid-range at-target; PBA state as a 30s temporal amplifier around either.
**ctrl:** Destruction stagger via Claw; PBA state itself has no CC rider. Adaptive rotation permits reactive control coverage.
**mob:** Good — +20% MS in PBA, Fox Leap dash, PBA manual cancel = defensive positioning tool.
**def:** Claw counter + PBA cancel escape. No shield/block/dodge. Relies on burst-window mobility for survival.
**econ:** Phantom Beast Meter (main) + Phantom Beast Spirit stacks (secondary CDR economy). Self-cycling — CDR recycle compresses cooldowns per stack consumed.
