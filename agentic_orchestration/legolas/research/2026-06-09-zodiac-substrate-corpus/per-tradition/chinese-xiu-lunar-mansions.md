## Tradition overview

The Twenty-Eight Mansions (二十八宿, Èrshíbā Xiù) are the Chinese astronomical system of 28 lunar lodges — roughly equivalent to the Western zodiac but tracking the Moon's sidereal month rather than the Sun's annual path. Each mansion is defined by a determinative star and spans a sector of the ecliptic/equatorial band; the Moon passes through one mansion per night over 27–28 days. The system originated approximately in the 5th–4th century BCE and was codified during the Zhou and Han dynasties; it remains in use in traditional Chinese astrology and calendrical calculation. The 28 mansions are grouped into four quadrant palaces, each guarded by one of the Four Symbols (四象) — the Azure Dragon (East/Spring), Black Tortoise (North/Winter), White Tiger (West/Autumn), and Vermilion Bird (South/Summer).

## Element cycle pattern

The 28 mansions follow a repeating 7-element cycle within each quadrant: **Wood → Gold → Soil → Yang → Yin → Fire → Water**, then repeating for the next quadrant. This cycle is applied independent of the quadrant's own element (e.g., Black Tortoise / North / Water quadrant still begins its cycle with Wood for mansion 8). This means each quadrant has exactly one mansion per element type, giving the 28-mansion system a systematic internal symmetry.

## Substrate/game design notes

- **Element cycle (Wood/Gold/Soil/Yang/Yin/Fire/Water)** is a 7-item cycle not mapping to standard 5-element wuxing — Yang and Yin here serve as independent elemental slots. This is the traditional 7-element pattern for the 28 Xiu specifically.
- **Four guardians as character archetypes:** Azure Dragon (spring/east/growth), Black Tortoise (winter/north/endurance), White Tiger (autumn/west/power), Vermilion Bird (summer/south/fire) are prime substrate material for form-library theming.
- **Animal associations** (28 unique animals, one per mansion): jiaolong, dragon, raccoon dog, rabbit, fox, tiger, leopard / xiezhi, bovini, bat, muroidea, swallow, pig, yayu / wolf, dog, pheasant, rooster, corvus, monkey, ape / bian, goat, water deer, horse, deer, snake, earthworm. These 28 animals are distinct from the 12 Chinese zodiac animals and from Western zodiac creatures.
- **High game-design salience:** Xīn (Heart/Antares) + Shēn (Orion) opposition proverb; Guǐ (Ghost/Praesepe) mist; Dǒu (Dipper/fate-dispensing) — all carry strong narrative potential.
- **Hydra dominance in southern quadrant:** Mansions 24 (Willow), 25 (Star), 26 (Extended Net), and 27 (Wings) all draw stars from Hydra — the longest constellation, whose immense body serves as the Vermilion Bird's body-scaffolding.
