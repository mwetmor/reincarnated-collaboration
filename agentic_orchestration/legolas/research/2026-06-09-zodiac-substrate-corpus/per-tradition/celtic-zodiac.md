## CRITICAL PROVENANCE NOTE

**The Celtic tree zodiac is a modern construction.** It was synthesized by Robert Graves in *The White Goddess* (1948) from disparate Celtic, classical, and other sources. It is **not attested** in:
- Ancient Celtic astronomical records
- Druidic manuscripts or inscriptions  
- Pre-Christian Celtic religious or calendrical texts
- The Ogham inscriptions (4th–7th century CE), which were used for names and simple inscriptions, not for a zodiacal calendar system

Contemporary Neo-pagan, Wiccan, and Druidic practitioners have adopted this calendar sincerely and it has genuine modern religious significance. The corpus documents it as a living modern tradition with explicit provenance transparency. Every YAML entry contains `authenticity_note: "Modern synthetic tradition; not attested in ancient Celtic astronomical records"`.

This provenance is flagged not to dismiss the tradition but to prevent downstream design confusion — if game narrative anchors to this system as "ancient Celtic sky wisdom," that framing would be historically inaccurate. It is better described as "Celtic Revival / mythopoeic tradition."

## Substrate highlights

- **13-month structure**: Unlike the 12-sign Western/Chinese/Vedic zodiacs, the tree zodiac has 13 months (the 13th month = Elder, ᚏ, Nov 25–Dec 23). The 13th month frames the year-boundary. If the game ever features a "13th sign" or liminal spirit category, Celtic tree zodiac provides tradition precedent.
- **Oak / Holly duality**: The Oak King (summer/growth) and Holly King (winter/contraction) battle at the solstices in neo-pagan cosmology. This is a natural design hook for opposing-sign mechanics or seasonal event framing.
- **Ash (Nion) ↔ Norse Yggdrasil**: The Ash tree's Celtic and Norse roles converge — Ash governs the liminal sea-coastal forests (Celtic) while being the World Tree Yggdrasil in Norse. This cross-tradition alignment is noted in the corpus entry.
- **Ivy as the sole non-tree**: Ivy (Gort, ᚋ) is the only non-arboreal sign in the calendar — a botanical anomaly that makes it distinctively liminal.

## Design note for Gandalf

The Celtic tree zodiac's primaries are seasonal-ecological archetypes (the tree's role in the British ecology), not astronomical (no star coordinates), not mythologically dense (Graves synthesized the mythology). The design vocabulary is **organic / cyclical / botanical**. This distinguishes Celtic tree signs from stellar-tradition entries. If used in game, framing as "nature spirits" rather than "star spirits" is more thematically accurate.
