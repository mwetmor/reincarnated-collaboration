## Tradition overview

Vedic Jyotish (Hindu astrology) uses 12 Rashi (राशि — zodiac signs) within a **sidereal zodiac** fixed to the actual positions of stars (Nirayana system), as distinct from the Western tropical zodiac which is fixed to the vernal equinox and moves with the precession of equinoxes. Due to this ~23-degree ayanamsa offset (Lahiri ayanamsa, 2026), a person's Jyotish sun-sign typically differs from their Western sun-sign — a Western Aries (March 21 – April 13) is Jyotish Meena (Pisces). Each Rashi is governed by a ruling planet (graha), belongs to one of four elements (Agni/fire, Jala/water, Vayu/air, Prithvi/earth), and one of three modalities (Chara/cardinal, Sthira/fixed, Dwiswabhava/mutable). The Rashi also maps onto Kalapurusha (the cosmic person whose body is the zodiac), assigning each sign a body region. Jyotish additionally uses 27 Nakshatras (lunar mansions) which subdivide the zodiac further — each Rashi spans approximately 2.25 Nakshatras, giving each sign a rich inner texture of planetary sub-rulerships.

## Key distinction from Western zodiac: Sidereal vs Tropical Zodiac

**This is the most important technical distinction in the Vedic corpus.**

| | Vedic Jyotish | Western Tropical |
|---|---|---|
| Zodiac type | Sidereal (Nirayana — fixed to stars) | Tropical (fixed to vernal equinox) |
| Aries begins | ~April 14 (Sun enters actual Aries constellation) | March 21 (vernal equinox, regardless of star position) |
| Drift mechanism | Does not drift (fixed to stars) | Drifts ~1 degree per 72 years (precession) |
| Ayanamsa offset (2026) | ~23°15' (Lahiri ayanamsa) | 0° (definition anchor) |
| Example discrepancy | Western "Aries" born March 28 = Jyotish "Meena" (Pisces) | Western "Aries" born March 28 = Western Aries |

The practical consequence: a person's "rising sign," "moon sign," and "sun sign" will differ by approximately one Rashi between Western and Vedic systems for most people born in the 20th–21st centuries. Both systems have their own internal coherence; neither is "wrong" — they are different frameworks.

**Additional Vedic-specific features not present in Western zodiac:**
- **Nakshatras**: 27 lunar mansions that subdivide the zodiac further; each Rashi spans ~2.25 Nakshatras. Each Nakshatra has its own ruling deity, ruling planet, and mythological story.
- **Kalapurusha**: The zodiac as the body of the Cosmic Person — each Rashi governs a specific body region (Mesha = head; Meena = feet).
- **Makara distinction**: Vedic Makara is a distinctly Hindu mythological creature (not the Western goat-fish) — the vehicle of Varuna and Ganga, a crocodile-elephant-fish hybrid. Corpus entries document this explicitly.
- **Graha (planetary) system**: 7 visible planets (Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn) + 2 shadow planets (Rahu and Ketu — lunar nodes). No Uranus, Neptune, Pluto in classical Jyotish.
