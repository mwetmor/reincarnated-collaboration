## Usage notes for Reincarnated substrate

The Arabic Manazil corpus has richer image coverage than the Egyptian decan corpus because:
1. Al-Sufi's manuscript shows constellations that directly contain the Manazil star groups.
2. Multiple manuscript copies survive in major institutions and are digitized.
3. Wikipedia's Book of Fixed Stars article has ~12 distinct images from different manuscripts.

**Per-Manzil image assignments in corpus:**
- Manazil 4 (Ad-Dabaran): Taurus image (Al-Sufi, Bodleian) — Aldebaran directly visible
- Manazil 5 (Al-Haq'a): Orion image (Al-Sufi, Bodleian) — Orion head visible
- Manazil 6-7 (Gemini Manazil): Gemini image (Al-Sufi) — Alhena, Castor, Pollux visible
- Manazil 8 (An-Nathrah): Cancer image (Al-Sufi) — Beehive cluster region
- Manazil 28 (Al-Hut): Bodleian folio (1009-10 CE) — Andromeda/Pisces region
- All other Manazil: 12th-century Iraq manuscript (general Book of Fixed Stars image)
