## Key Norse art collections (public domain)

1. **Eddic manuscripts (13th century)**: Several Prose Edda manuscript pages are on Wikimedia Commons. The AM 738 4to (Árni Magnússon collection, Copenhagen) has illustrated pages. Search: "Prose Edda manuscript" on Wikimedia.

2. **19th century Norse mythology illustrations**: The "golden age" of Norse illustration (1879–1920) produced many now-public-domain works:
   - Friedrich Wilhelm Heine (1835–1912) — Yggdrasil, cosmological scenes
   - Lorenz Frølich (1820–1908) — Norse deity portraits  
   - Johannes Gehrts (1855–1921) — dramatic mythological scenes
   - All PD-old; search by artist name on Wikimedia Commons

3. **John Bauer illustrations** (1882–1918): Swedish artist; fairy tale + Norse aesthetic; many PD-old on Wikimedia. Visually closest to a "Nordic fantasy" game aesthetic.

4. **Alan Lee**: Still living; copyright. Do not use — listed for reference only.
