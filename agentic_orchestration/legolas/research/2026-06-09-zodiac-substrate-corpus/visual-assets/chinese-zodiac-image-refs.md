## M.A. N.Isis Alexandre "Zodiaque" series — series notes

The primary images for 9 of 12 animals come from a consistent illustrated series created in 2004 by M.A. N.Isis Alexandre. All are licensed CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GFDL. The series naming convention follows the French animal names:

- Rat (Rat/Souris), Ox (Buffle/Boeuf — note "Zodiauqe" typo in filename), Tiger (Tigre), Rabbit (Lièvre), Dragon, Snake (Serpent), Horse (Cheval), Goat (Chèvre), Rooster (Coq)

**Missing from this series:** Monkey, Dog, Pig. No "Zodiaque-Singe.jpg," "Zodiaque-Chien.jpg," or "Zodiaque-Cochon.jpg" were found in the respective Wikimedia Commons categories during this crawl. SVG alternatives were used.

**Ox filename note:** The ox/buffalo illustration filename contains a typo: `Zodiauqe-Buffle.jpg` (transposed letters in "Zodiac") — this is the correct filename on Wikimedia Commons and resolves correctly.
