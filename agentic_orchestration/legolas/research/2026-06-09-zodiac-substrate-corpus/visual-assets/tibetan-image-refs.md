## High-confidence image types (likely to exist on Commons):

### Lungta prayer flags (Rta/Horse):
- Lungta (Wind Horse) prayer flags are extremely well-photographed; hundreds of images on Commons
- **Search:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Search?search=lungta+prayer+flag
- High confidence of finding usable public domain images

### Tibetan Mastiff (Khyi/Dog):
- Wikipedia Tibetan Mastiff article: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tibetan_Mastiff
- Commons category: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Tibetan_Mastiff
- Multiple photos available; likely in public domain or CC-BY

### Bhavachakra / Wheel of Life (Phag/Pig):
- The Bhavachakra is extensively documented on Commons as thangka paintings
- **Search:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Search?search=bhavachakra
- The pig, snake, and rooster at the wheel's center are clearly visible in most versions
- Recommended source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bhavacakra.jpg (likely exists)

### Yamantaka thangka (Glang/Ox):
- Yamantaka is one of the most important Gelug tantric deities; extensively documented
- **Search:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Search?search=Yamantaka
- Multiple museum collections have Yamantaka thangkas in public domain
- Rubin Museum (NYC) has an extensive digitized collection

### Garuda (Bya/Bird):
- Garuda is well-documented across Buddhist traditions
- **Search:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Garuda_in_Buddhism
- Multiple Tibetan Garuda thangka images likely available

## Phase 2 priority institutions for Tibetan zodiac imagery:

### Rubin Museum of Art (New York)
- World's premier Tibetan Buddhist art museum
- Digital collection: https://rubinmuseum.org/collection
- Many items available for research use; some CC-BY licensed
- Priority searches: Nangsi Khorlo (Tibetan astrological wheel), thangka zodiac animals

### Himalayan Art Resources (HAR)
- https://www.himalayanart.org/ — specialized database for Himalayan Buddhist art
- Primary scholarly resource for Tibetan iconography
- Search for individual deity-animal associations (Yamantaka, Garuda, Nāga)

### British Library — Tibetan manuscripts
- https://www.bl.uk/collection-items?subject=Tibetan+manuscripts
- Historical Tibetan astrological manuscripts with zodiac illustrations

## Notes on visual sourcing challenges

- Tibetan zodiac presents the most specialized sourcing challenge of the four traditions: authentic imagery is in thangka paintings and astrological manuals rather than folk art or calendar plaques
- The six Buddhist-tantric enrichment images (Yamantaka, Garuda, Bhavachakra, Lungta, Nāga, Bodhisattva-monkey) are individually well-documented but may not be explicitly labeled as zodiac images
- All 12 URLs are constructed/hypothetical; Phase 2 verification is mandatory before production use
- Recommend Rubin Museum digital collection as Phase 2 first stop for Tibetan zodiac images
