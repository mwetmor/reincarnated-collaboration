## Verified-working image alternatives (Phase 2 research targets)

### High-confidence alternative image sources:
1. **British Museum collection** — extensive Japanese zodiac netsuke and ema collection
   - Search: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection (search "Japanese zodiac")
   - License: typically CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0 for research use
2. **Tokyo National Museum** — official high-resolution ema and zodiac art
   - https://webarchives.tnm.jp/ (requires registration)
3. **Smithsonian Freer/Sackler** — Japanese art with zodiac motifs
   - https://collections.si.edu/search/ (search "Japanese zodiac")
4. **Wikimedia Commons actual category** — search for "ema" + animal name
   - https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Ema
