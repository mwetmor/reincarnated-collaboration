## Image strategy notes

### Yazata iconographic challenge
Most Yazatas do not have a single canonical visual representation in the way Greek gods or Hindu deities do — Zoroastrianism historically avoided anthropomorphic deity images (especially post-Sassanid). Visual assets therefore represent:
1. **The Yazata's material domain** (fire for Atar/Azar; water for Apas/Aban; cattle for Vohu Manah/Bahman)
2. **Associated festivals or cultural practices** (Nowruz fire-jumping, Tiregan water-play, Mihragan harvest, Yalda Night)
3. **Symbolic artifacts** (Faravahar for Fravashi/Farvardin; Faravahar Gold for Ahura Mazda/Dey)
4. **Archaeological remains** (Persepolis Ahura Mazda relief; Mithra relief; Anahita relief)

### Mithra / Mithraism distinction
The tauroctony image (Roman Mithras slaying a bull) is clearly labeled as "Roman derivative iconography" — the corpus entries explicitly note this is NOT Avestan Zoroastrian Mithra (who does not slay bulls). If a game asset uses the bull-slaying motif for the Mehr/Mithra entry, attribution should reflect the Roman Mithraism tradition, not core Zoroastrian tradition.

### Faravahar (Farvardin + Dey overlap)
Two entries (persian-001 Farvardin and persian-010 Dey) both use Faravahar imagery because:
- Farvardin: the Fravashi is represented by the Faravahar symbol
- Dey: Ahura Mazda is the creator of Fravashis and the Faravahar is the supreme Zoroastrian symbol generally

Phase 2 should seek to differentiate: use a more Nowruz-specific image for Farvardin and reserve Faravahar for Dey (Ahura Mazda's own month).

### Phase 2 image improvement targets
- **Tishtrya / Tir (persian-004)**: A purpose-drawn illustration of the white horse vs black horse cosmic battle would be ideal but does not exist as a free image. Current Sirius NASA image is a good scientific substitute; consider an Avestan manuscript illustration if one exists in Wikimedia.
- **Mithra (persian-007)**: Seek an Avestan/Persian Mithra image (not Roman Mithraism tauroctony) for the primary — the Khuzestan relief secondary is a better culturally specific primary.
- **Azar vs Ordibehesht (fire temple overlap)**: Both currently reference fire temple imagery. Phase 2 should find distinct images — Azar could use Chaharshanbe Suri fire-jumping as primary (festival of fire); Ordibehesht retains the temple interior.
- **Khordad (persian-003)**: Qanat image is indirect. A more direct image of Persian water ritual (Haurvatat Jashn celebration or haoma preparation) would better serve the Wholeness/water domain.
- **Batch URL verification**: All approximate image URLs need HTTP 200 confirmation. Recommend a Python requests batch-check in Phase 2.
