## Game design visual note

The tree zodiac's strongest visual identity comes from the trees themselves (botanical illustration style) + Ogham inscriptions carved into wood. A wood-carved inscription aesthetic would suit this tradition better than starfield/astronomical imagery. Phase 2 could source historical botanical illustrations from John Curtis's *British Entomology* series (Public Domain) or similar.
