2. **Matariki ceremony images** — Te Papa Tongarewa publishes Matariki-specific imagery; their public media release images may be available under institutional-use terms. Phase 2: contact Te Papa media.

3. **Hawaiian zenith demonstration** — a clear image showing a star directly overhead (zenith concept) would strengthen the Hokule'a entry. No such image was found in this crawl.

4. **Dark-cloud constellation map** — an annotated photograph overlaying the Yacana / dark-cloud figure outlines on the actual Milky Way photograph would be ideal for andean-001 through 006. Academic publications (Urton 1981) include hand-drawn diagrams; a Wikipedia Commons public-domain version would need sourcing.
