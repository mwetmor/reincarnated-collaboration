## MUL.APIN cuneiform text
The cuneiform transliterations used in the corpus (e.g., 𒀯𒀉𒆪 for MUL.IKU) are standard Assyriology transliterations. Cuneiform Unicode block: U+12000–U+1237F. Fonts: Noto Sans Cuneiform (Google Fonts, OFL license) renders these correctly.
