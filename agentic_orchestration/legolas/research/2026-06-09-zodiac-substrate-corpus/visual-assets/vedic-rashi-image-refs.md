## Image strategy notes

### IAU SVG consistency
All 12 Rashi use IAU constellation SVGs as the astronomical sky-sector reference — consistent with the Western zodiac corpus (corpus-western-zodiac.yaml). This is intentional: the sidereal Vedic system uses the SAME stars as the Western system (just without the tropical equinox drift). Showing the same constellation SVG but with a different cultural entry makes the relationship explicit.

### Makara as the key visual distinction
Makara (vedic-rashi-010) deliberately uses the Rajasthani Makara illustration as its **primary** image (not the IAU Capricornus SVG) — because the Makara creature is the most culturally distinctive visual element in the entire Vedic corpus. The Rajasthani miniature tradition produces the most accessible and aesthetically rich depictions of the Makara creature. The IAU Capricornus SVG is retained as a **secondary** image with explicit labeling that the creature is DIFFERENT from the Western goat-fish. This visual pairing makes the corpus distinction concrete.

### Nakshatra-level imagery (Phase 2 — not yet in corpus)
The 27 Nakshatras each have distinctive iconography, deity associations, and symbolic animals that would substantially enrich the visual vocabulary. Notable high-value targets:
- Ashwini (healing horse-twins of the dawn) — Mesha
- Rohini (red star / the beloved of the Moon) — Vrishabha
- Magha (royal throne, ancestral pitrus) — Simha
- Chitra (the jewel / Vishwakarman's bright star) — Kanya
- Jyeshtha (the eldest / Indra's vajra) — Vrishchika
- Mula (the root / galactic center / Nirriti) — Dhanus
- Revati (Pushan the shepherd / end of cycle) — Meena

## Cross-tradition visual coherence note

Both the Persian/Zoroastrian and Vedic Rashi corpora use Wikipedia Commons IAU SVGs as the astronomical sky-sector anchor — creating visual coherence with the Western zodiac corpus. This is the intended design: all three traditions share the same star field; the cultures differ in how they read, name, and mythologize it. Game assets can layer cultural overlays onto the same underlying star-map geometry.
