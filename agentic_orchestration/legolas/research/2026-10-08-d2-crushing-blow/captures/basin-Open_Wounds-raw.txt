{{Intro}}
{{Mb|=Chance of Open Wounds}} can cause target to bleed uncontrollably when applied with a successful weapon attack, draining life and stopping [[damage regeneration]] for its 8 second (200 frame) duration. {{Warn|=Bleeding occurs even when life drain cannot be applied to target.}}
*Percentage chance from multiple sources is summed, although when [[two weapons]] are equipped only that of the attacking weapon is added (that of the other weapon is ignored). >100% is redundant.
*Applied by any [[melee]] or ranged attack with the exception of [[Blade Sentinel]], [[Blade Shield]] and the additional arrows of [[Multiple Shot]] (only applied by the original two arrows).
*Always applied to another player character, but only applied to other units (monster, [[mercenary]] or [[pet]]) if their life value changes, either due to damage taken or regenerated. If no damage is taken then Open Wounds will not be applied if regeneration has already been stopped by [[Prevent Monster Heal]].
*Life drain is dependent on the level of the player, mercenary or [[Iron Golem]] applying it. Life drain per frame in 1/256ths of a point is currently calculated as follows:

*Special units are monsters other than normal and minion monsters ([[Champion]], [[Unique (monster)|Unique]], [[Super Unique]], [[boss]] (excluding normal and minion [[Putrid Defiler]]s) and [[Evil]]). Life drain is unmodified against all other targets.

*Life drain cannot be countered by any form of damage reduction.
*Duration cannot be reduced by [[Cleansing]] or [[Fade]]. [https://d2mods.info/forum/viewtopic.php?f=10&t=44308] [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/120090-open-wounds/&do=findComment&comment=1487517]
*When Open Wounds is applied again before the previous duration expires, duration simply gets reset regardless of the level of the second application: if it is applied at level 80 while it is still being applied at level 60, level 60 damage is still applied for the reset duration (and vice versa).
*[[Poison Damage]] and length can be applied separately and simultaneously. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/120090-open-wounds/&do=findComment&comment=1487545]
*Replenish Life and + Life After Each (Demon) Kill from items, and healing from [[potion]]s, [[Prayer]] (including its passive bonuses to Cleansing and [[Meditation]]) and [[Holy Bolt]] still apply while active.
*Replenish Life from items is a direct counter: Open Wounds applied by a level 30 player's melee attack on another player would result in <sub>109/256</sub> life drain per frame, which Replenish Life +109 would counter entirely; a ranged attack would result in <sub>54/256</sub> life drain per frame, which Replenish Life +54 would counter entirely.
