{{Resistance nav}}
{{Intro}}
{{Mb|=Damage Reduced by %}} or Damage Resist modifies any physical damage by a percentage, after Damage Reduced has been applied; DR -100% is the floor, while DR > 99% makes a unit [[Immune to Physical]].
* Player and [[mercenary]] normally have a ceiling of DR 50% (although any excess can offset penalties that might otherwise reduce it below this ceiling).
* This floor and ceiling do not apply to Crushing Blow damage: while DR > 0% will decrease this damage (and DR > 99% results in immunity to it), DR < 0% will not ''increase'' it.
* [[Sanctuary (skill)|Sanctuary]] normally ignores DR > 0% when applying the supplier's physical damage to [[undead]] [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/113633-monster-resistances-and-immunities-guide/&do=findComment&comment=1418033], but this does '''not''' apply to Crushing Blow damage: Immune to Physical undead will remain immune to it. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/110076-sanctuary-crushing-blow/&do=findComment&comment=1382731] [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/113633-monster-resistances-and-immunities-guide/&do=findComment&comment=1418132]
* It does not apply to [[Sacrifice]] damage to self. [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/114184-martyrs-and-damage-taken-goes-to-mana/&do=findComment&comment=1425323]
* Renamed to '''Physical Damage Received Reduced/Increased''' in D2R.
|}

*[[Ghostly|{{Mb|=Ghostly}}]] [[champion]]s are '''set''' to Damage Resist 80%, even when [[Immune to Physical]]; {{Warn|=however, the Immune to Physical label remains.}} [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/112167-which-elements-are-most-resisted-in-hell/&do=findComment&comment=1408483] [https://www.theamazonbasin.com/forums/index.php?/forums/topic/118104-monster-bonuses-death-damage-and-corpse-explosion/&do=findComment&comment=1495241]

*DR +50% is applied by the [[Stone Skin]] [[Unique (monster)|Unique]] bonus. If this results in DR > 99%, it is only applied if a monster has less than two immunities beforehand (in which case it either becomes Immune to Physical, or increasingly so).
