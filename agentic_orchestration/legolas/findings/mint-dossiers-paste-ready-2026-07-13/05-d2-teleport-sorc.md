## Provenance + Genre Lineage

Teleport is a native Diablo 2 Sorceress skill available from the game's launch (Lord of Destruction, June 2001). As a SORCERESS-ONLY skill, it defined early D2 mobility: Sorceresses could teleport to any cursor-visible location instantly, making them the fastest-traversal class. However, the "Teleport Sorceress" as a BUILD IDENTITY — where Teleport is the PRIMARY defining loop rather than a utility option — emerged with the Enigma runeword in patch 1.10 (October 2003).

The Enigma runeword (Jah-Ith-Ber, inserted in 3-socket body armor) grants Teleport to any class at level 1, regardless of the character's own skill tree. This made Teleport a universal repositioning tool accessible to Barbarians, Necromancers, Paladins, Amazon, and Druid — but the Sorceress, already having native Teleport, could build the most extreme expression: pairing native Teleport skill (maxed for low mana cost) with Enigma's granted Teleport as a backup, and building an entire kit around the repositioning loop as the primary gameplay identity. The "Teleport Sorceress" BUILD IDENTITY = a Sorceress who plays Teleport not just for mobility but as the central game action, pairing it with instant-cast spells (Meteor, Blizzard, Lightning) to eliminate threats immediately post-teleport.

Genre lineage:
- **Ancestor:** Early D2 Necromancer "Teleport + Bone Spirit" (utility teleport with cast after landing; not a pure movement-loop build)
- **Contemporaneous:** D2 "Hammerdin" (Paladin, Enigma-enabled post-1.10) developed a parallel Teleport-loop identity as the most mobile Paladin; the Sorceress native Teleport remains the purest expression
- **Descendants:** PoE1 Blink Arrow / Lightning Warp / Frostblink builds (instant repositioning as movement-verb identity); LE Bladedancer Shift; D3 Demon Hunter Vault Natalya builds

The Enigma era (1.10, 2003) is the stabilization point because this is when the build became systematically accessible and community-documented — the "Teleport Sorceress" as a corpus entry is the Enigma-era expression, not just the pre-1.10 utility Teleport use.

## Mechanical Identity

The Teleport Sorceress defines the ARPG movement-verb build archetype: the primary game action is repositioning, not attacking. The loop is: Teleport to safe position → instant-cast damage skill (Meteor, Blizzard, Nova) → Teleport again to avoid retaliation. Kill speed comes from placing damage while permanently out of reach of enemy retaliation. The Sorceress's native Teleport skill has mana cost reduced by points invested; a maxed Teleport costs 17 mana per use (vs. ~80 base), enabling spam cadence.

This is the genre's founding "movement IS the identity" kit — the direct ancestor of all "dash-to-play" ARPGs. The B5 roster mapping captures this accurately: B5 is the movement-verb INT caster; d2-teleport-sorc is the founding genus entry.

The "Enigma era" framing matters for the naming law: pre-Enigma Teleport Sorceress was a utility choice; post-Enigma it became a CROSS-CLASS design statement and the basis for the PoE movement-verb design philosophy (Lightning Warp, Blink Arrow, Flame Dash, etc.).

## Raw Descriptors

**geo:** Self-origin teleport: player relocates instantly to cursor location. Post-teleport spells vary by damage build: Blizzard = at-cursor AoE; Meteor = at-cursor delayed AoE; Nova = self-origin radial. The movement IS the delivery mechanic; the damage is the secondary action.

**ctrl:** Teleport has no CC component; it is purely repositioning. Spells may have chill (Blizzard), stun (Nova at close range), or knockback — but these are secondary to the movement identity.

**mob:** Maximum mobility; the build's entire identity is mobility. Sorceress can traverse the entire D2 game map faster than any other class using Teleport spam. The "glass cannon" stereotype of D2 Sorceress comes from trading defense for this mobility.

**def:** Glass cannon — minimal investment in survivability; relies entirely on Teleport to avoid incoming damage. Pre-emptive repositioning IS the defense model. No block, minimal armor, no melee engagement.

**econ:** Mana economy; Teleport costs 17 mana per cast (maxed skill); damage spells cost 20–60 mana; total mana pool and mana-per-second recovery determine sustainable teleport cadence. High-end builds use Infinity runeword (for conviction aura) + mana-leech gear to sustain continuous Teleport loop.

**elem:** Sorceress-specific: Lightning (Lightning Sorceress + Teleport) or Cold (Blizzard Sorceress + Teleport) are the two canonical element arms. The "Teleport" identity is element-agnostic — Teleport is the movement verb; the damage element is a secondary specification.
