### Damage model note for KF-2 join-key derivation

Fire Wall outputs FIRE DAMAGE PER SECOND (per-second sustained tick damage) — NOT a single-hit. The stat is labeled "damage per second" in all sources. The actual tick rate (frames per damage application) is not publicly documented with verbatim anchors; modding community reverse-engineering identifies a per-frame burn sub-missile, but no citable authoritative source states e.g. "hits once per 4 frames." **This creates a formula-rule situation for KF-2:** the sim will need to model FW as a sustained DoT with a tick rate, but the per-tick value must be derived from the per-second figure (÷ tick_rate). The tick rate itself is a gap — see gaps section.

**JOINT-KEY NOTE FOR GAMORA:** Fire Wall is a sustained tick-damage skill. The `source_value` for kit_numeric should capture damage-per-second (the game's documented unit), not a single-hit value. The normalization_rule must express this as a DoT with duration 3.6 sec. Expected total damage per cast = (dmg/sec) × duration = 1,296–1,320 × 3.6 = 4,665.6–4,752 fire damage per stationary pass-through, pre-synergy, pre-Fire Mastery.

### Cast Speed / FCR Breakpoints

**Source:** maxroll.gg/d2/resources/sorceress-overview (accessed 2026-07-23)
Verbatim FCR breakpoints for Sorceress (normal spell cast):
- 0% FCR = 13 frames per cast
- 9% FCR = 12 frames
- 20% FCR = 11 frames
- 37% FCR = 10 frames
- **63% FCR = 9 frames** (cited as "at least 63% FCR" target by Maxroll FW guide)
- **105% FCR = 8 frames** (cited as "63% or 105% FCR" breakpoints by Maxroll FW guide)
- 200% FCR = 7 frames

D2 has no "attack speed" stat as a separate value — cast speed is expressed in frames per cast, not as a multiplier. At 105% FCR: 8 frames per cast = 25/8 = 3.125 casts/sec.
