## Selection method (per gandalf axes, shortlist-prep §"Selection axes")

1. **PRIMARY — BC cell:** one seat per cell (the only true mechanical differentiator; within-cell kits share geometry — skin not shape).
2. **SECONDARY — element (mixed preferred):** element measured at skill level (`canonical_element`; the envelope `dominant_element` is F1-None until the star-lord bridge lands). Every STR cell is physical-base (8/12 skills) with a mixed tail carrying one secondary at 4/12; every DEX cell is fire-base with a mixed tail. **Finalists drawn from the mixed tail**, rotated so the roster-wide secondary spread covers the whole wheel: fire×3 · lightning×5 · water×4 · wind×4 · shadow×4 · earth×4 · holy×4. **One mono anchor per seat** retained as the baseline read.
3. **TERTIARY — name/flavor:** the star-lord pass now produces it for these 35.

## Caveats riding into flavor

- **Geometry is cell-locked** — all five finalists in a seat share one spatial geometry; flavor differentiation rides element + tempo/amplitude texture, not shape. Do not write flavor that promises a mechanic the kit doesn't carry (Discipline #11 attribution).
- **Element read is skill-level** — the envelope `dominant_element` pip only renders once the star-lord F1 bridge lands (same star-lord dispatch, Beat A). Flavor may reference the secondary element; the pip is the visual confirm.
- **GLASS CANNON fragility is F1-unverified** (glyph findings §5 check-3): `damage_multiplier` is uniform ~1.01 across glyphs and `stat_distribution` is F1-None, so "glass" texture isn't in the data yet — flavor the *ranged spike*, not fragility, until the bridge + a stat pass back it.
- **Seat 7 is the one physical-ranged chassis** — reads as a martial skirmisher, not a mage; flavor accordingly (no caster-fantasy; the whole batch is martial resource-feel per the addendum).
