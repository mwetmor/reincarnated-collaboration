  3. Stage 0 5-tuple collapses to substrate 3-tuple. Substrate carries (range, tempo, attribute) but NOT amplitude or proxy_density. 5 pairs of Stage 0 cells collapse to the same substrate 3-tuple; routing depends on Stage 4 mechanical-tagging or Stage 1.5 named-bearer joining.   4. All 30 mythological-register rows are NULL-typed by proxy fingerprint v1.1. Excalibur / Mjölnir / Gungnir / Gandiva / Caladbolg / etc. won't be reachable by naive (range, tempo, attribute) sampling — they're proper-noun named items the fingerprinter doesn't recognize. Sketch F Tier-1 mythological-named-bearer protection requires either Stage 1.5 named-bearer joining OR Stage 4 mechanical-tagging.
  5. Sketch F 4-zero anchors confirmed: Hattori Hanzō (1 fantasy-coinage row), Lu Bu (0), Moctezuma (0), Gilgamesh (0). Substrate-honest gap; routes to Stage 3 design call decision: substrate-search via Sidecar B / Stage 3.5 engine-authored gap-fills / cell descope.

1. Methodology

This aggregation reads the weapon_knowledge_entries table joining the v1.1 proxy fingerprint columns (proxy_range_class, proxy_tempo_class, proxy_attribute_class, proxy_geometry_class, proxy_fingerprint_confidence) with the existing canonical tagging columns (register_canonical, historical_period_canonical, cultural_lineage_canonical) and the Stage 1.5 extracted fields (extracted_named_bearer) to surface the joint distribution of axes against the Stage 0 Sketch A cell-targeting intent.

Sketch A 5-tuple vs substrate 3-tuple: Sketch A defines cells over (range × tempo × amplitude × attribute × proxy_density) = 324 cells. Substrate proxy-fingerprint v1.1 only types (range, tempo, attribute); amplitude and proxy_density columns do not exist on weapon_knowledge_entries. Geometry exists as proxy_geometry_class but is orthogonal to Sketch A. This cross-tab therefore evaluates each Stage 0 cell against the substrate-available 3-tuple, marking 5 Stage 0 cell-pairs that collapse to the same substrate 3-tuple as routing-ambiguous:
  • melee|low|STR — Heavy Barbarian (cell 1, proxy=none) + Ancestor-Warrior (cell 5, proxy=light)
  • ranged|high|DEX — Archer (cell 7, proxy=none) + Falconer/Pet-Archer (cell 10, proxy=light)
  • ranged|medium|INT — Standard Wizard (cell 12, proxy=none) + Arcane-Familiar Mage (cell 16, proxy=light)
  • mid|low|INT — Pyromantic Caster (cell 14, proxy=none) + Necromancer Summoner (cell 17, proxy=heavy)
  • mid|medium|WIS — Channeling Cleric (cell 19, proxy=none) + Witch Doctor Petmaster (cell 25, proxy=heavy)
For each collapsed pair, the substrate count is shared; Stage 3 must decide how to allocate the pool across the two forms.

6. Stage 3 design-call routing summary

What this cross-tab tells Stage 3:
  1. Composition policy under 5-tuple → 3-tuple collapse. Decide: do we keep all 25 Stage 0 cell-archetypes and use Stage 1.5 named-bearer joining + Stage 4 mechanical-tagging to disambiguate? Or do we collapse the routing-ambiguous pairs into a single substrate-side cell and let Phase 5 cohesion-coalescence handle form-routing at generation time?
  2. 9 CRITICAL cells need substrate-or-not decisions. Pyromantic Caster (cell 14), Red Mage/Spellsword (cell 15), Necromancer Summoner (cell 17), Channeling Cleric (cell 19), Storm Caller/Druid (cell 22), Monk-archetype (cell 23), Druid Beastmaster (cell 24), Witch Doctor Petmaster (cell 25), Artillery Mage (cell 13). All are INT/WIS-attribute. Three routing options per cell: (a) Sidecar B substrate enrichment, (b) Stage 3.5 engine-authored gap-fill, (c) descope from v1.
  3. 1 THIN cell (Ritual Mage/Oracle, cell 21). 51 typed rows; 49 high-conf. Borderline. Recommended: Sidecar B enrichment OR accept low floor.
  4. 2 UNDER-FLOOR-HIGHCONF (Light Fighter, Twin-Blade Fencer). Light Fighter has 476 typed but only 3 high-conf — fantasy-coinage saturation. Twin-Blade Fencer has 215 typed but only 5 Mode-A clean.
  5. Mythological-register pool (Excalibur et al.) is invisible to current fingerprint. If Sketch F Tier-1 mythological named-bearer protection is load-bearing for v1, Stage 1.5 named-bearer join is the path (and is already wired); Stage 4 mechanical-tagging can fill the gap for non-named mythological weapons.
  6. Sketch D tradition gaps for v1_scope sourcing. Mesoamerican (-3.89% gap), Egyptian (-3.99%), Sumerian/Mesopotamian (-3.00%), Vedic/Hindu (-3.98%) — Sidecar B substrate-enrichment crawl targets all confirmed at substantial gap. Slavic-via-tag shows 0.17% (gap -2.83%), but most Slavic substrate is encoded under european lineage.
