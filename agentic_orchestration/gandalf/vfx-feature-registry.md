## § 1 — Rules for induction

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **I-1 ENTRY** | A candidate family enters when a **blind pass surfaces a residual** — a phenomenon not describable by any existing family — with evidence frames named. Entry status: **PROVISIONAL**. Conductor drafts the row; no family enters from theory alone. |
| **I-2 PROMOTION** | PROVISIONAL → **ESTABLISHED** when observed in **≥2 skills of different archetypes**, OR when **Matt's eye names it** (the owner's eye is an instrument of record). ⚑ **RATIFIER AMENDMENT (jack-ryan, 2026-08-25): the OR is disjunctive and the Status cell MUST NAME WHICH CLAUSE promoted the row.** Founding rows applied the two clauses inconsistently — FF-01..FF-05 were ESTABLISHED on Matt's eye alone while FF-07, on the same route, was held PROVISIONAL pending a second archetype. One evidence route, two statuses, is not a rule; it is a habit. Naming the clause makes the inconsistency visible at the row instead of at a ratification pass. |
| **I-3 PHRASING LAW** | Families are phrased as **element-agnostic, archetype-agnostic PRINCIPLES** (Tier-1 law: the heat ramp is fire's *instance*; the principle is the intensity gradient). **Litmus: the definition must read sensibly for a healing aura, a laser beam, and a ground slam.** Content-specific phrasings are rejected at entry. |
| **I-4 MERGE/SPLIT** | Families that always co-occur or describe one phenomenon at two scales MERGE; a family doing double duty SPLITS. Conductor proposes; ratifier rules. |
| **I-5 RETIREMENT** | A single-exemplar family that never recurs may be demoted to its exemplar's per-skill notes. Retirement is recorded, not deleted — lineage stays. |
| **I-6 PROTOCOL FEEDBACK** | A family may motivate a Layer-1 protocol amendment, but the amendment must be an **ATTENTION DIMENSION** (*"characterize any changes to the ground plane during and after the effect"*), never **EXPECTED CONTENT** (*"look for scar decals"*). Same litmus as I-3. |
| **I-7 ROUTE ≠ FAMILY** *(added by ratifier, jack-ryan 2026-08-25)* | A family's **status** is a claim about the PHENOMENON. A **detection route** is a separate claim about an INSTRUMENT, and it carries its own separately-recorded validity. **An ESTABLISHED family may have a disqualified route, and a route disqualified by its own control MAY NOT CARRY A BAR** — it may only inspect. Every route cell that a control has failed, capped, or confounded must say so **in the cell**, not only in a § 3 caveat: a consumer who reads § 2's table and never § 3 is the normal consumer, and a caveat that does not travel with the number is not a caveat. *(Same shape as the run's recurring finding — an instrument returning cleanly after it stopped answering the question; cf. CLAUDE.md's `git diff HEAD` amendment chain.)* |

  ### ⚑ RULED — jack-ryan, 2026-08-25: **UNDER-FIRE CONFIRMED. AMENDED, BUT NOT AS PROPOSED.** The candidate repairs a ROW; the defect is in the PREDICATE.

  **Adopted (now law, row FF-08):** **`CV < 0.25` TRIPS ALONE.** The spectral-tone term is **demoted from trip-condition to corroborating diagnostic** — recorded beside the CV, never gating it. *Inspect, never auto-pass* is unchanged.

  **Rejected: `CV == 0.000 exactly` as a standalone clause.** Three reasons, all from the tabled data rather than from taste:

  1. **It leaves a second escape standing.** `OURS_teleport` reads CV 0.021 with tone 473× — under BOTH bars. galadriel inspected it anyway, *"on the CV alone."* The proposed clause rescues `blink` and still not `teleport`.
  2. **It is a knife-edge predicate on a rounded display.** A metronome with one jittered interval reads 0.004 and escapes again. A law whose reach is one float-equality wide will be re-amended by the next row that misses it by a rounding digit.
  3. **The conjunct never once ADDED a trip; it only ever subtracted.** Measured record of the tone term across the whole matrix: 2 trips, 2 escapes, and **both escapes overridden by the instrument's own operator in the same table.** A condition whose entire observed effect is to suppress calls its owner then makes by hand is not a gate — it is friction with a spreadsheet.

  **The separation the amendment rests on, stated as the arithmetic:** twenty-six reference legs across twenty-four archetypes and five classes span **CV 0.449 – 1.149**; **not one falls below 0.449.** A sole-trip bar at 0.25 therefore has **zero false-positive exposure on the entire measured reference corpus, with 0.199 of margin** beneath the lowest reference leg, and the empty band 0.103 – 0.448 contains no row of any kind. That is a *measured* bar. `CV == 0.000` is a bar fitted to one row.

  **Carried caveats (binding on consumers):** (a) CV is computed on inter-event intervals of specular mass — it inherits event-detection validity, and 30 fps caps observable intermittency at 14.985 Hz. (b) **Low interval counts:** `OURS_ground_slam` trips on six events. A CV over few intervals is noisy, and that noise inflates rather than deflates, so a near-zero CV over few events is *strong* evidence — but a row below the minimum interval count records **INDETERMINATE, never PASS.** ⚑ **The minimum is galadriel's to set: it is an instrument threshold inside her seam, and this ruling does not invent one.** *(Shape borrowed from the same session's KR revision: derive only where the test is real, else fail loudly to UNKNOWN.)* (c) The finding remains an **AUTHORING** finding, not a capability one — `OURS_leap_strike` (0.668) and `OURS_dash_attack` (0.955) sit inside the reference band, same seam, same engine, same day as the four that do not.

Its CV outputs are content-free and remain legal at MEASURE and SPEC per the matrix's own quarantine stamp.
