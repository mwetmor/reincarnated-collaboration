## 3. Coverage framing + vocabulary law

**Lattice-coverage replaces kit-count.** Coverage is stated over the 972 cells with per-cell status: **occupied-by-us / genre-attested / genre-negative / whitespace**. The roster is the set of occupants. **Founding roster** = the 48 (29 K + 6 H available + 13 bench). **Expansion roster** = new kits designed toward genre-texture coverage.

**RETIRED vocabulary (Matt, 2026-07-12):** "core" (for the founding 35+13) · "anchor" / "pilot" (for expansion kits) · kit-rank grades · **any numeric roster target**. Corollary law (views README): name-lineage and cell-lineage are separate columns; divergence is declared design or a reconcile flag, never silent.
