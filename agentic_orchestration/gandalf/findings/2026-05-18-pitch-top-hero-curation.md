## § 1 — Hero of the Engine

### Canary of the Drowned Seam

**Why this hero:**
This name should stop a marketing director mid-sentence. It does the *exact* thing the pitch is about, in three words: the LLM took a fire substrate (engine grammar) and authored it through the lens of a coal-mining underworld anchor (LLM thematic dressing) into a class that fuses *mining-mortality folklore* (the canary as harbinger), *industrial disaster* (the drowned seam as flooded coal-vein), and *fantasy elemental magic* (fire-mage as kindler) — all in a single coherent identity. No human authored "Canary of the Drowned Seam" as a class concept and then handed it to the LLM. The LLM constructed the *whole compound metaphor* from the season's substrate grammar + the anchor's narrative register. That is the engine's argument made flesh.

- **Season context:** "The Dwarves' Empty Halls" (S13, theme=coal); cosmological vocabulary slot-fills include *Seam Pressure / Damp Creep / Forge Remembrance / Withdrawal Soot* — the LLM authored an entire register of industrial-archeology language around the substrate set
- **Substrate:** fire (`mechanical_signature: [ignite, escalate, area_persist, burn_apply]`; `iconic_verbs: ignites, burns, scorches, engulfs, flares, consumes, kindles`; `iconic_register: martial`; `cosmological_commitment: "the substrate of escalation — what begins small and becomes total"`)
- **Pair-rationale prose to display on the pitch page beside this hero:** the season's `pair_thermal_rationale` — *"Seam Pressure builds in pockets until the coal-vein ruptures outward in cascading release, while Damp Creep is the slow wet air that seeps through abandoned corridors and settles into lungs and joints without ever announcing itself."*

## § 6 — Honest caveats

1. **The HD-2D pixel-art register is a known stress-test for image-gen models.** GPT Image 2 and MJ v8 can hit Octopath-class register but quality is variable. The pre-flight A/B (§ 5 reserve) is *load-bearing* for the pipeline — if the first sample of "Canary of the Drowned Seam" comes back as generic-anime-character or grimdark-painterly, the prompt template needs revision before the full pass fires.

2. **Image-gen models drift between calls.** Even with a locked prompt prefix, character consistency *between* portraits is not guaranteed. For iteration-1 this is acceptable (the gallery effect of 26 distinct portraits is fine — players' minds reconstruct identity per-image). Future iterations may benefit from reference-image injection (Flux + IP-Adapter; or Midjourney's `--cref`) for character-arc continuity across seasons.

3. **Hybrid_mage names retained in supporting rosters.** Several seasons' supporting rosters above include hybrid_mage classes (e.g., "Brine Cartographer", "Cartographer of Sacred Winds") because the names are too good to lose. Pitch page can footnote: *"Hybrid mage architecture retired in canonical-6 transition (2026-05-18); these heroes remain in seasons generated under the prior architecture."*

4. **Cosmological_vocabulary prose is the second deliverable.** The image portraits are valuable, but the *pair-rationale prose from each season's cosmological_vocabulary.json* is the higher-value pitch artifact per gandalf's framing memo § 2.1. The `/pitch` page should display this prose with literary typography breathing room — see analytics-IA § 3.3 arc-2 special case.

5. **This curation is gandalf-judgment; not algorithmic.** Star-lord, drax, or any reviewer can argue for swaps. The rubric is transparent (§ 0 table); contested selections can be re-scored on the spot.

## § 7 — Downstream signal

Star-lord pipeline (`generate_hero_images.py`) consumes this curation file as input:

1. Parse the 6 bespoke prompts in §§ 1–2 verbatim
2. Run pre-flight A/B per § 5 (Hero of the Engine across GPT Image 2 + one alternative)
3. Surface samples to gandalf for register lock (or auto-proceed if GPT-2 default lands cleanly)
4. Generate all 26 portraits
5. Store under `reincarnated-loadout/public/pitch/heroes/<season_id>/<class_slug>.png`
6. Emit `heroes-manifest.json` mapping season → class → image-path + prompt-used + provider + timestamp + cost

Drax `/pitch` page (`Pitch.tsx`) consumes:

1. This curation file (for class names, season assignments, supporting-roster orderings)
2. Per-season cosmological_vocabulary.json (for pair-rationale prose display)
3. heroes-manifest.json from star-lord (for portrait URLs)
4. substrate-identity-declarations (for accent palette + iconic-verb chips)
