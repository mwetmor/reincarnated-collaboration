## 1. Form & silhouette
- **Ref:** ground-plane phenomena. The ring is a continuous ragged annulus with radial flame tendrils licking outward (A f124–130); the devils are vertical tapering columns of stacked toruses (B f808). Silhouettes are noisy, organic, never geometric.
- **Render:** geometric and discrete. A perfect ellipse of 16 identical round puffs (f22–30, C f28 zoom); one clean crescent ribbon (f60–90); a dotted ellipse (f178). The ribbon is the only vertical element and is a thin sail, not a column — it has no volume, no taper, no stacked-band construction.
- Net: the render trades the reference's ragged continuous sheets and volumetric columns for tidy discrete primitives; at a glance it reads "magic ping" where the ref reads "physical blast + weather".

## 2. Intensity & colour field
- **Ref:** warm-dominant. Flash (157,130,112); flame arc region (112,75,78 with floor mix, A f124); crest cools to dark steel-blue (44–65, 56–58, 75–78) — all values sit *below* ~160, embedded in the scene's exposure. The blue is desaturated and dark, never neon.
- **Render:** cool-dominant and brighter than its scene. Puffs near-white (154,147,147) on a floor of (84,35,36); crescent is saturated emissive teal (25,198,158) — chroma far beyond anything the reference effect ever shows. Measured check: in A f132 the only pixels matching the render's teal class (G+B−2R>60, bright) are UI — ally overhead bars, e.g. (68,194,161) at (1286,416); the effect's own blue maxes out around (50–70, 77–94, 85–125).
- Net: **hue identity is inverted** — reference is fire-into-dust (warm→neutral); render is white/teal throughout.

## 3. Discrete elements (count, lifetimes, phases)
- **Ref:** ring = one continuous emitter (dozens of tendrils/embers, unresolvable as units), lifetime ~14–20f (0.23–0.33s); haze cloud ~1–2s (A f140–200); devils 3–5 concurrent, each ≥2s and up to ~3.5s (B f740–950); recasts every ~3–6s keep phases overlapping so the screen never returns to zero.
- **Render:** 16 puffs (10f each, all born f22 and dying together ~f32); 1 crescent (~106f); 1 glow decal (~100f); ~25 dots (~50f each). Phases are strictly sequential — ring ends before crescent peaks, crescent ends before dots peak — and the scene returns fully to zero at f210.

## 4. Medium & atmosphere
- **Ref:** heavy participation. The burst converts into a drifting blue-grey haze that persists and slides laterally after the ring dies (A f140–170); devils are literally made of atmospheric dust and shed it; ambient torch smoke blends with effect smoke (B f900–950 shows blue smoke + red ribbons co-mingling).
- **Render:** no atmosphere. No haze survives any phase; puffs fade without leaving residue; the floor is as crisp at f80 as at f0. The only "smoke" is the puff sprites themselves.

## 5. Environment (persistent change, ground state, cast light)
- **Ref:** measured floor patch (1300,600): (121,79,43)@f110 → (129,87,52)@f122 (warm flash lift) → (139,89,96)@f132 — blue channel +53 as the dust crest passes over it; wall patch (250,120) lifts (25,23,19)→(32,34,32) at the flash. Ember residue ring visible ~f140. The effect lights and tints its surroundings.
- **Render:** floor near caster (850,650): (88,35,46)@f16 → (106,66,74)@f26 — but this is puff-sprite overlap, not light: during the crescent phase (f80) it reads (88,36,47), identical to idle. The only floor illumination is the moving white glow decal under the crescent — a faked, local light. No scorch, ember, or residue at any point; f240 vs f287 diff YAVG=1.0 (scene fully restored).

## 6. Camera behaviour
- **Ref:** locked follow-cam. Static-background diff across the slam (f114→130 pairs, crop (1550,80)+300x200): YAVG 3.0–3.2, *below* the ambient baseline (5.0–5.3 at f60/f400 — torch flicker) — no measurable impact shake or zoom at this framing.
- **Render:** slow follow drift with slight reframe over the clip (f0 vs f240 diff shows edge ghosting on the right-side architecture), fully static at the end (f240 vs f287 YAVG=1.0). No impulse on cast in either — parity on shake, difference only in idle drift.

## 7. Timing & rhythm
- **Ref envelope:** anticipation 4–7f crouch → 1–2f flash → 14f ring expansion (fast attack, medium decay) → seconds of haze/devil sustain → recast on a ~3–6s combat cadence (bursts near A f120, ~f300, ~f405–430, ~f780, ~f1270; B f208 and the f740–860 devil-dense stretch). The rhythm is: sharp percussive hit, long reverberant tail, overlapping repeats.
- **Render envelope:** 1-frame ring pop at f22 with **no anticipation before it** (the sword swing follows at f42–54), then a long steady mid (orbit, constant brightness f48–140), then a graceful 60f outro. The envelope is attack-less and sustain-heavy — closer to a buff aura's rhythm than an impact's. Cadence untestable (single cast), but nothing in the clip implies stacking.

## 8. Attachment & motion (what moves, victim response)
- **Ref:** everything *detaches*. The ring is world-anchored at the cast point and travels; devils spawn in the world and wander among targets independent of the caster; only the crimson whirlwind ribbon is caster-attached (B f780–860). Victim response is untestable (training dummies are static by design), but damage numbers pop as the wavefront reaches each dummy (A f126–128: 76.7B/280B/91.8B appear at ring-arrival radii).
- **Render:** everything is caster-attached. The crescent orbits the caster and translates with him as he walks (f60–90, f120–150); the dot ring stays centered on him (f152–200); the glow decal follows the crescent. Nothing is left in the world when the caster moves. Skeletons show zero reaction (no flash, stagger, or displacement) — as inert as the dummies, so victim-response parity is accidental but equal.

## 9. Layering
- **Ref burst:** ≥4 distinguishable layers with distinct hue/opacity/lifetime: (1) bright core flash (shortest, warmest), (2) flame-tendril sheet (orange, additive, mid-life), (3) dust crest (steel-blue, alpha-blended, longer), (4) ember sparks (points, longest fliers), plus (5) lingering haze. The devils add a scene-lit dust layer + ember glints; the crimson ribbon is hue-keyed so attack (red) parses separately from hazard (neutral dust) — layer identity is carried by hue *and* blend mode.
- **Render:** 3 thin layers — white puff sprites, teal additive ribbon, white dots/glow — differentiated mostly by shape and time, weakly by hue (white/white/teal), and all reading as additive-emissive. No scene-lit (lit-particle) layer exists at all, which is precisely the layer that gives the reference devils their physicality.

## 10. Contrast relationship (effect vs its own scene)
- **Ref:** effect sits *inside* the scene's exposure: fire matches the torchlit palette (figure-ground carried by motion and value, not hue), while the dust crest reads by being the only desaturated cool mass in a warm scene — a deliberate, temporary complementary accent that then dissipates. Scene SATAVG ≈7.
- **Render:** the scene is dark, heavily saturated red-plum (SATAVG ≈23, YAVG ≈57), and the effect is near-white/teal — simultaneously the brightest and the most hue-opposed thing on screen. Its figure-ground pop is *stronger* than the reference's, which over-announces a small effect: puffs at (154,147,147) on (84,35,36) floor is a bigger luminance step than anything in the ref burst. Ironically the recreation reads louder while being physically smaller and shorter-lived.

## 11. RANKED LIST — most visually consequential differences (largest first)
1. **The ring does not travel.** Reference: radius ~0→~6 caster-heights in 0.23s (A f120→f132), damage popping at wavefront arrival; render: 16 puffs pop in at a fixed 2.6-character radius in one frame (f21→f22) and fade in place. The single defining kinetic event of the ability is absent.
2. **Hue identity inverted: fire-into-dust became white/teal.** Ref effect is warm flash + orange tendrils cooling to dark steel-blue dust (max ~(65,56,78)); render is near-white puffs + emissive teal (25,198,158). The render's hero color does not exist in the reference effect at all — its only match there is ally-nameplate UI teal.
3. **Dust devils replaced by one caster-locked ribbon.** Ref: 3–5 detached, wandering, multi-second, scene-lit tornado columns built of stacked rotating dust rings (B f808, f740–950); render: a single flat emissive teal crescent orbiting the caster at 0.27s/rev (f48–142). Count, attachment, verticality, materiality, and threat-reading all differ.
4. **No impact anatomy — and the order is inverted.** Ref: crouch → leap-slam → flash → fire pool → ring (A f114–124); render: ring fires at f22, the (mild) sword swing happens afterward at f42–54. Cause precedes effect in the ref; effect precedes cause in the render.
5. **No aftermath.** Ref leaves drifting blue-grey haze, embers, and devils for seconds after every burst (A f140–400); render restores a pristine scene by f210 (measured diff YAVG 1.0). The ability's "the air remembers it" quality is missing.
6. **Effect scale halved relative to the character.** Ring reach ≈5.5–6 character heights (ref) vs ≈2.6 (render); devils ≈1.5–2 character heights tall vs a ribbon about 1–1.5 with near-zero mass.
7. **No scene lighting response.** Ref burst lifts walls (+~9 luma) and blue-shifts the floor (+53 blue) as the crest passes; render contributes no measurable light outside its own sprites except a small faked glow decal.
8. **Discretization/regularity tell.** 16 identical, evenly spaced puffs and a tidy dot ellipse read as procedural placement; the ref's emitters never resolve into countable identical units.
9. **Missing lit-particle layer.** Every render layer is additive-emissive; the ref's most physical element (dust) is alpha-blended and scene-lit — tan near fire (81,56,48), ghost-grey in shadow (A f200) — a rendering-mode difference visible at any zoom.
10. **Louder-than-reference contrast.** The render's white-on-dark-magenta figure-ground step exceeds the ref's, so a smaller, shorter effect paradoxically over-dominates its frame (dimension-10 measurements).

## 12. FREEZE-FRAME DESIGN-INTENT PASS
**A f114→f122 (2f steps):** crouch compresses the pose, wings sweep back, then the body rises and drops with the flash landing exactly on the down-frame (f120–122). Inference: the designer is buying *weight* — the fire ring must feel like a consequence of mass hitting ground, so ignition is slaved to the landing frame. A fire pool persists at the feet for several frames after the ring leaves: the impact point is kept "hot" so the eye can trace the ring back to its cause.
**A f124 / f128 / f130 (Acrest zoom):** each frame the tendrils extend ahead of the smooth annulus — flame *fingers* lead, sheet follows. Inference: tendrils exist to keep the wavefront ground-attached and organic; a clean expanding torus would read as a magic nova, and this is meant to read as fire racing across a floor. By f128 the trailing zone is already steel-blue while the front is still orange: color encodes *age of combustion* per-point, not per-effect — the designer is using hue as a spatial clock so a single texture communicates "burning here, burnt there" simultaneously. It also solves a combat-legibility problem: the high-salience warm hue lives only at the damage frontier; spent interior de-emphasizes so it doesn't mask the fight.
**B f204–208 (1f steps around the second take's slam):** an orange arc slash appears one frame before the radial flash. Inference: the cast is weapon-driven (a strike, not a spell) — the recreation's choice of a sword swing is actually *right* in kind, but its ring is not slaved to the swing's contact frame, which is the entire trick.
**B f808 freeze (devil zoom):** the column is unmistakably 4–6 stacked donut bands, each band's silhouette ragged, widths increasing with height, with a warm rim-lit side and an ember glint mid-column. Stepping f780→f838 every 4: band edges shift phase every frame but the column translates only a few pixels — high spin frequency, low travel speed. Inference: a cheap volumetric tornado — stacked rotating ring sprites — whose *materiality* comes from being scene-lit dust, and whose ember glints tie it back to the fire that spawned it. It is designed as lingering area denial: tall enough to occlude, slow enough to dodge.
**B f780–860:** the caster's crimson ribbon swirl and the neutral devils co-exist; stepping shows they never share hue or height band. Inference: deliberate channel separation — red = the player's attack, dust = the spawned hazard. The recreation collapses both roles into one teal ribbon.
**C f21→f22→f26 (1f steps):** empty → complete ring at full radius and brightness → slight loosening. Inference: the author treated the ring as an *AoE telegraph decal* (appear-hold-fade), not as a propagating shockwave; there is no birth choreography at all.
**C f60–90 (2f steps):** the crescent's bright head leads and its tail drags; the white ground glow swings under it with a slight lag; small dots shed off the head. Inference: the author is building a "wind spirit orbiting the caster" — the glow decal is a hand-faked light source, and the shed dots are a deliberate breakup pass. The craft instinct is real; it is aimed at the wrong construct (an orbiting pet-like swirl rather than a wandering world-anchored devil).
**C f146–162 (4f steps):** the crescent rises, shortens, and its tip literally decants into the dot ring, which inherits the orbit. Inference: a designed *outro* — dissolve the hero element into particles, orbit them down, fade. The render has a more considered death than birth; the reference is the exact opposite (violent birth, entropic death).
**Step-only reveals (invisible at speed):** (a) the ref ring's tendril-first growth pattern; (b) the per-point orange→blue aging gradient; (c) the render's single-frame ring pop; (d) the crescent-tip→dot-ring handoff; (e) devil band-phase rotation vs slow translation; (f) that the render's swing happens after its ring.

## RESIDUAL OBSERVATIONS (outside the 12 dimensions)
1. **The render's teal has a traceable false source.** A full-frame chroma scan of A f132 finds saturated teal *only* in UI (ally overhead bars, party frames, e.g. (68,194,161) at (1286,416)); the effect's own blue never exceeds dim desaturated values. If the recreation's palette was keyed off screenshots, the hero teal was plausibly sampled from interface elements or ally cosmetics rather than the ability.
2. **Damage numbers in the ref timestamp the wavefront.** Number spawn radii grow with the ring (A f126–132): an incidental but reliable ground-truth that ref damage is applied on wavefront arrival, not on cast — useful spec detail the render (no gameplay hookup visible) will eventually need.
3. **Pre-roll contamination in the render:** a faint teal glint is present near the caster's feet as early as f8–14, before any cast — likely a looping emitter or un-reset particle system, visible only on stills.
4. **The reference's devils change color by neighborhood** (tan near burning dummies at B f808 vs ghost-grey in shadow at A f200) — a free environmental-storytelling behavior that comes entirely from choosing lit particles; no render element can do this because all are emissive.
5. **Both clips accidentally share a "no victim response" property** (static dummies vs unreacting skeletons), which means the recreation currently has no evidence either way about hit-reaction design — a blind spot, not a verified parity.
6. **The render's camera performs a slow settle** (edge ghosting f0→f240, perfectly static f240→f287), suggesting a scripted dolly ease-out rather than gameplay camera — worth normalizing before future A/B captures so motion diffs aren't polluted.
