**2. ### RANKED LIST — most visually consequential differences, largest first

1. **Colour family & intensity hierarchy inverted and flattened**: fiery orange/red with white-hot cores and per-swing intensity spikes (castcrop_021/031/036) vs flat pale teal/white with no hot core and no spikes (curcrop_022/038). The current effect does not read as the same element, and does not read as *powerful*.
2. **No target response**: reference enemies flinch, bleed, die, and pile into corpses (ref_late rows 5–9, ref_crop_032); current skeletons are statues with sparkle overlays (f_001 vs f_090 identical poses). This removes the entire consequence read of the ability.
3. **No persistent on-hit/DoT layer**: reference applies seconds-long pulsing wound-orbs + body flames per enemy (castcrop_016/031, ref_crop_002); current hit sparkles last well under a second and leave nothing. The ability's defining "wound that stays" signature is missing.
4. **No environment persistence**: accumulating blood pools/decals and corpses (castcrop_036, ref_crop_032) vs a pristine floor at every checked event frame and in the aftermath (curcrop_090).
5. **Rhythm**: percussive repeated swings at ~0.5–0.7 s cadence with per-swing envelopes vs one flat 2.3 s continuous hum (dimension 7 evidence). Even a continuous-channel design needs internal pulsation to match the reference's energy.
6. **Sweep-form geometry**: broad ragged flame-textured swath/disc vs thin clean uniform crescent (castcrop_014 vs curcrop_022). Width, edge break-up, and per-swing shape variation are what make the reference arc feel like a weapon.
7. **Discrete-element physicality**: ballistic blood spray + embers with size spread vs uniform drifting soft dots (castcrop_021 vs curcrop_050).
8. **Atmosphere coupling**: effect-generated smoke plumes feeding scene fog (ref_crop_002) vs a small local mist veil (curcrop_038).
9. **Camera**: both essentially locked; current adds one small smooth translation at t≈1.85–2.4 s (measured f_037–f_048) not present in the reference — negligible, but it is the only camera difference.

- The current render's **cast ring and decay ring** (f_008, f_062) are structural additions with no counterpart in the reference — good bookending instincts, but their perfectly even spacing and identical puff sizes make them read procedural/placeholder.
