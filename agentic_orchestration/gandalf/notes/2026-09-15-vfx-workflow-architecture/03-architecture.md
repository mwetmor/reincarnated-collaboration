## 2. The workflow — three contracts

### 2.0 Declared units (⊳G1 BLOCK-2a/2d — one clock, one pixel)

- **Clock:** every band and gate is in **frames of 1/60 s** (the Hades data model; validation at 60 Hz). `strike_fast_v1` = peak in 0–1 frames (0–17 ms) · half in 2–7 frames (**33–117 ms**) · residue 0.3–1.0 s. E1's "half-peak ~4 ticks" = 67 ms. **Consequence for § 2.2b:** at 24 fps a strike decay is **0.8–2.8 video frames** — video cannot carry strike decay at all; it could only ever have carried ≥ 300 ms events (pool ignition, field activation). This strengthens the no-video-first ruling.
- **Pixel:** "px" is always **native px** unless written "screen px". `native = screen / pixel_scale`.
- **Extent** = bbox of the coverage mask at **α ≥ 0.5**, stated per gate whether the comparand is arm-vs-arm (E1) or asset-vs-spec (E0-p, § 3).
- **Band provenance:** `strike_fast_v1` rests on **n ≈ 3** Hades exemplars with one unresolved ⚠ (Zeus bolt: 20 drawings / 0.33 s in data vs ≈ 4 visible frames in footage — ⊳G1 WARN-15c). It is a *reference*, not a law; Matt's eye rules over it (I-2).

### 2.1 Contract I — the skill spec (six hand-authored files)

One `VfxSkillSpec` per selected skill, written by the conductor from the source game and Matt's intent: `skill_id · source_game + skill · grammar · element treatment (visual_treatment_id — "ice"/"poison" are *treatment* names, not engine elements; the binding rule is parked in 04- and becomes load-bearing only if a generator-emitted skill ever enters, ⊳G1 WARN-1) · response_class (strike / field / aura_loop / material — four classes; channel dropped because no channel skill is among the six, ⊳G1 INFO-5) · mechanics {origin socket; aim rule; range / speed / radius / count / hop count; travel or ballistic params; active duration + tick schedule (**irregular** — FF-08); termination} · presentation {primitive bindings; body extents in BH; phase envelopes; enabled layers} · provenance (source-game observation or Matt ruling per field)`. Aim rules: release-locked · owner-tracking · ground-locked · target-tracking · event-linked. The renderer never selects targets or infers grammar from a name.

### 2.2 Contract II — four runtime grammars (the time owner)

Grammar = a Godot 2D template: a timeline of events moving painted primitives with engine transforms, driven by an **effect-age clock + event timestamps**. ⊳G1 WARN-6: per **FF-11** the phase names (anticipation / onset / peak / decay / residue; activation / ticks / deactivation) are **illustrative, not a required inventory** — a two-phase effect satisfies the grammar; per **FF-08** every tick/hop/pulse schedule carries **interval irregularity** (event-interval CV ≥ 0.25; a metronome trips); per **FF-12** the split *self-luminance vs cast contribution* is **executed here by construction** (body vs `PointLight2D`) and recorded to the registry. Direction is runtime rotation, never baked facings.

| Grammar | The one moving thing | Time band | Dial / implementation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1 Projectile (+ emitter)** | a head that translates + a trail that lags; optional scheduled child emissions | `strike_fast_v1` at confirmed contact | runtime — pooled `AnimatedSprite2D` head (optional 3–4-frame boil loop), `Line2D`/ribbon trail; children pooled; **burst interior = flipbook** |
| **G2 Thrown arc → field** | `screen = project(ground(t)) − up·height(t)`; landing accent; then a decal + a loop breathing for a fixed duration | ballistic + landing + `field_v1` (**UNMEASURED**) | runtime; flask = upright RGB material sprite, tilt ≤ 10°, no tumble unless commissioned |
| **G3 Instant bolt / chain** | a link primitive drawn N times between N event-supplied point pairs | event-driven; `strike_fast_v1` per contact, centrally budgeted | runtime, necessarily; endpoint strips restored by tooling |
| **G4 Self-aura loop** | owner-anchored ring/dome loop + floor light; pulse per (irregular) tick | `aura_loop_v1` (**UNMEASURED**) | runtime loop |

**The dial rule** (probe 2): *bake a flipbook where the shape changes internally and the duration is fixed; compose at runtime where extent, direction, count or duration vary.* Baking records existing motion; it cannot manufacture missing advection. Bake with an **effect-only visibility configuration** (`transparent_bg` clears the viewport, not the scene) at **native effect crop size (≤ 512²), not 1080p** (⊳G1 WARN-14a: ~10 MB per bake, not 1.4 GB; disk is at 20 GiB free); verify two repeated bakes match; reconstruct over four backgrounds.

**Time bands:** `strike_fast_v1` / `strike_splash_v1` exist (§ 2.0 caveats). **`field_v1` and `aura_loop_v1` are UNMEASURED.** ⊳G1 BLOCK-4: measuring them requires frames cut from source footage, which is the **open sourcing-class decision** `canonical/matt_decision_needed/2026-08-25-youtube-frame-extraction-sourcing-class.md` (a/b/c) — until Matt rules it, those bands stay **named Matt-approved fixture envelopes, labelled PROVISIONAL**, and the "own lap" has no authorisation. **V4 (hybrid oracle) is conditional, not dissolved** (⊳G1 BLOCK-6b): the class-scoped stack *is* option B of the hybrid; **R-C3-120's precondition stands** — Matt wants to SEE a VFX built to the spec before ruling — so V4 is ruled **after E0-p's burst is on the Desktop**, and `2026-09-15-hybrid-vfx-oracle.md` now points here as the single disposition.

### 2.2b Bursts — no-video-first (Matt ruled V15, 2026-09-15)

*"Try the path where Astra creates the peak image and then we work through bringing it to life without video… save the video gen usage for the known bottleneck in the character animation pipeline."* Video is the fallback only.

**The prior predicts the naive version fails** (⊳G1 WARN-9, stated honestly): probe 2 — *"a nova is not a ring scaling up"*; my own P4 — *"pure erosion of one silhouette may read as tech."* So E0-p step 3 (transforms + erosion over a static peak) is the **falsification arm**; step 5 (guided key states) is expected to carry the result and is budgeted as such.

1. **Peak still** (1 image): Astra paints one fire impact peak body on a plate against the scene/scale board.
2. **Decompose** (tooling, 0 images): isolate → segment the peak into painted pieces along plane boundaries; per piece: pivot, radial angle/distance from centre, band index, coverage mask.
3. **Animate the pieces** (runtime, 0 images): runtime onset flash → hold intact peak 1–2 frames → pieces fly outward on per-piece velocity/rotation curves (**scale quantised to integer native steps and within ±15 %** — ⊳G1 BLOCK-3) → **centre-out erosion** on each piece's distance field + **band-step dissolve** → residue **by erosion, never by scaling** to 15–25 % of peak coverage for 0.3–1.0 s. Measured by **VO1/VO3/VO8** (§ 4 namespaces) — **bake dependency:** `vfx_measure.py` takes `plate='alpha'`, so the burst is Movie-Maker-baked at native crop first (⊳G1 BLOCK-4).
4. **Matt's eye:** painted burst, or sticker explosion?
5. **Expected to be needed:** ≤ 2 guided key states (expanded, spent) painted over the tooling-transformed piece arrangement (S1 § 2b conditions), swapped discretely with holds — **with the red-team's instruments inherited** (⊳G1 WARN-9 / #75 cl. 6): a **correspondence test** (each piece in the key state maps to its source piece within 1 native px after the recorded transform) and a **boil test** (VO8 change rate between the two states within band; no shard count change; no contour thickening > 1 native px).

**Budget ≤ 4 images** (1 peak + 2 key states + **1 repair**); a failed RGBA extraction of the peak that the one key-plate fallback does not rescue **kills the probe** (not a fourth drive). **Gate:** Matt reads a painted burst at actual size in the E0 stack (one and four casts, dirt and foliage) and VO1/VO3/VO8 sit in band. **Kill → unlocks video fallback:** after step 5 it still reads as moving stickers or fails correspondence/boil.
