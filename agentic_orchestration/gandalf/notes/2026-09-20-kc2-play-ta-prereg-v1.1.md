## § C · THE DENOMINATOR LAW, STATED ONCE

### C.1 · The constructed denominator `D`

```
D_constructed = CHANNELLING + CHANNELLING_AND_MOVING + MOVING + IDLE      (PRE_FIGHT and DEAD excluded)
```

**Verified on the seal:** pooled `287 + 2211 + 166 + 119 = 2783 = D`. The per-salt sum equals the pooled sum, so the partition is exact at both grains. ⚑ **No sealed artifact carries `D`. It is CONSTRUCTED, and the seal says so in its own basis string.**

**Two identities, both 5/5 on the seal, and both graded as `TA-X-08`:**
```
n_player_ticks_observed  =  D + PRE_FIGHT
n_channelling + n_released  =  D
∴  uptime + (n_released / D)  =  1.000000     (exact, 5/5)
```

⚑ **The seal publishes `release_duty` under the key `⚑ release_duty_on_observed_ticks` — on `D + PRE_FIGHT`, not on `D`.** That is why the published per-salt duty max reads `0.214953` while the complement of the published uptime min is `0.216981`. **Two denominators, one salt, one pair of statistics.** **Every rate row in this prereg divides by `D` and by nothing else.**

**Requirement on the runtime (drax):** emit `D_constructed` **and** `PRE_FIGHT` **and** `n_ticks_released` **per salt**, so any rate can be reconstructed on either denominator and the grader cannot pick one by accident.

### C.2 · Pooled vs mean-of-salts — the rule

**Every band is a band on the MEAN-OF-SALTS. A port's pooled figure must never be compared against these bands.** Pooling weights each salt by its `D`, and `D` per salt is **[1084, 305, 106, 185, 1103]** — a **10.4× span**, so the two ~1,100-tick salts dominate and the 106-tick salt nearly vanishes. On the plant ratio the pooled/mean gap is **0.1057 against a half-width of 0.2797 — 38 % of the whole tolerance consumed by choosing the wrong one of two numbers that share a name.**

### C.3 · The graded object, and why the interval is a *prediction* interval

The graded object is **the mean of a NEW 5-salt run** compared against the mean of the sealed 5-salt run — not an individual salt, not the pooled figure. The interval is therefore a **prediction interval for a new 5-salt mean**, carrying **both** runs' sampling error. `ddof = 1`. **The arithmetic and every number live in gamora's file; none is transcribed here.**

### C.4 · T-B denominators are NOT these denominators

Carried from galadriel (KP-6), because the cap in § G hands statistics to a T-B grader: **HP occupancy is computed on `LIVE-MAX`** (`hp_max` as read at that frame) — this is what reproduces **42.84 %**; the NOMINAL 20,005 denominator gives **39.76 %**, a **3.08 pp gap with no fight in it**. And the **HP window is 181.0 s** while **energy/motion is 182.65 s** (0.90 % apart). *A grader that picks the wrong one manufactures a port defect.*

## § F · THE GRADED ROWS

### F.1 · Decisiveness classes (unchanged from v1.0, restated for a standalone read)

**EXACT** — identity, invariant, count, or declared-precision reproduction. A red says **the port is wrong**. May carry a **NUMERICAL** tolerance for floating-point representation; **never a STATISTICAL one.**
**BAND** — a distributional comparison at n = 5. **Individually AND collectively non-decisive**; no BAND row can produce a STRUCTURAL verdict. Graded in one of three **FORMS** — `INTERVAL` (mean-of-salts inside gamora's half-width), `RATIO` (dimensionless, where instruments disagree — D-MPOL2-2), `ORDERING` (a strict inequality between two of the port's own statistics; no width consumed).
⚑ **No width is ever defaulted.** An unfilled `WIDTH := gamora` slot at the first graded run makes the row **UNGRADEABLE**, not loose.
⚑ **Cross-implementation rows are NOT salt-paired** (bands-not-tape). **Inertness rows are salt-paired within the port** — which is why they can be exact when nothing else can.
