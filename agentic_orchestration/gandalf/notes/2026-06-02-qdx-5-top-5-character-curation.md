## Selection methodology

### Distinctness metrics scored

1. **Wave B name uniqueness** within QDX-5 roster (no duplicates allowed in top-5)
2. **Substrate combination** — note: substrate_trace cultural_tradition + period are NA across all 37 kits (governance gap; see companion note `2026-06-02-qdx-5-governance-lapse-skill-tree-minimums.md` § 3 sub-issue 3)
3. **Evocative/sui-generis name quality** — does the name produce a distinct mental image (not "Element Archetype" template)

### Cohesion metrics scored

1. **Name fits primary element** thematically (e.g., "Penumbra" / "Dusk" fits shadow primary)
2. **Skill count and chain composition** (despite governance lapse on minimums; see lapse note)
3. **Per-skill flavor decisions** drawn from primary's Q18 pool (substrate-led at per-skill layer)
4. **Internal coherence** — kit's parts hang together

### Diversity criteria for top-5

- 5 different primary elements (8 represented in roster; picked diverse subset)
- Mix of caster + martial archetypes
- Coverage of both modern-caster naming patterns (Cannonade Cleric / Stormcaller) AND classical mythological-register names (Penumbra Caster / Ember Caster)
