### (1) Received-side damage is bucketed PER-CROSSING, not per-hit

The engine emits **one `damage` event per nova crossing** with **n projectiles aggregated into a
single `delivered` value**. The worst crossing in the banked battery is **414.80 HP — and it is TWO
projectiles of 207.40, not one hit of 414.80.** At the endpoint leg the same object reads
**470.80 = 2 × 235.40**.

**The GD fixture showed the player MULTIPLE damage floaters where a naïve `replica-frame/v1`
consumer will render ONE flash.** That is a real fidelity gap on the rendering side, and it was
named rather than hidden: charter §8.17 **REJECTED** changing the kernel's event grain for this run
(Disposition 3 — it semantically shifts every received-side statistic and breaks before/after
comparability with the banked G-5 baseline, which buckets per-crossing) and **routed the
decomposition to the consumer side**, i.e. to you.

**Render flash and floater cadence by realized count — never one lump.** A 414.80 crossing is two
floaters, ~0.04 s apart in the ring's own geometry, not a single 414.80 slab.

**⚑ WHERE THE COUNT LIVES — and this is the discrepancy in §4.1, read it before you build the
decomposer.** `realized_count` is **NOT a field on the trace.** It exists in three places, none of
them per-event on the JSONL:

| location | what it is |
|---|---|
| `SpatialFightEngine.gd_nova_crossings` — a **7-tuple**, `(tick, t_star, attack_id, r_star, delivered, by_element, n)`; **field index 6 (0-based) is the realized projectile count** | the authoritative ledger, **engine runtime only** |
| `report["a_dmg_1_grain"].per_tier[<tier>].max_realized_count` | the per-fight **maximum**, aggregate only (reads **2** on boss, **0** on nova-free tiers) |
| `wr1_battery2_statistics.json → G_B_killability.by_cell[*].realized_count` | the probe distribution (`{min, max, mean, support_bounds, expected}`) |

**What you can do from the trace alone:** divide. `realized_count = round(delivered ÷ unit_payload)`

`pre_endpoint`. This is exactly how Gate-2 proved the decomposition, and it is exact — the

legs**, i.e. **every crossing delivers exactly 1× or 2× the unit payload, and never anything else.**

`element == "cold"` on a `damage` event targeting the player.
