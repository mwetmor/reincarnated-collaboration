## 2. The pivotal judgment — is the envelope's boss-fragility genre-correct fragility, or a composition DEFICIENCY?

This is the design call the dispatch correctly identifies as pivotal. I rule it a **composition DEFICIENCY worth a bounded rocket investigation — NOT acceptable genre-correct fragility, and NOT a b6-is-the-outlier story.** The reasoning is specific and it turns on a comparison the data hands us for free.

**The genre-correct-fragility argument, and why it does NOT hold here.** A glass single-target striker *should* find bosses harder than swarm — that is the archetype tension I have defended throughout (D3 Demon Hunter pre-itemization is a swarm-shredder that needs specific investment to survive rift bosses; PoE glass-cannon ST builds delete packs then die to a boss slam without flask/defensive-layer tech). If the envelope rogue cleared boss at, say, 0.35 (just over the 0.30 floor) while swarm sat at 1.0, I would call that genre-correct fragility and accept it. **But "genre-correct boss-fragile" means "needs investment / clears bosses with effort," NOT "lands literally zero boss kills at a modifier well above the killable calibration."** A glass ST rogue that cannot kill a boss AT ALL when handed generous headroom is not a fragile striker — it is a striker *with no boss-killer tool that functions.* That is past the fragility line. Genre-correct fragile rogues in every ARPG I have shaped still KILL the boss; they just do it on a knife's edge. Zero-at-M=1.0 is not a knife's edge. It is a missing tool.

**The decisive comparison — b6 is NOT the outlier; b6 is the PROOF the archetype CAN clear boss at this role shape.** The b6 rogue template kit, at the SAME archetype, cleared boss at **0.967** with mini_boss 0.667 and elite 0.75 (Prereq-B cell 4, final_modifier 0.6133). That is not an over-performance anomaly to be explained away — that is a glass-ST rogue clearing boss *at a healthy modifier the same way a well-composed D2 Assassin or PoE ST build clears a boss: decisively, because it has the burst and the survival tech to land the kill.* The b6 boss-clear is the genre-CORRECT shape. The envelope's zero is the DEVIATION. So the question "is b6 the outlier?" answers itself: **b6 is what a correctly-composed glass-ST rogue does to a boss; the envelope is the one that is wrong.** The envelope is *strictly worse than a proven-good baseline at the same role histogram* — that is the textbook signature of a composition deficiency, not an archetype truth.

**Player-consequence anchor (why this matters past the sim):** a player who rolls the envelope-composed glass-ST rogue would experience a build that shreds trash and then *cannot finish a boss at all* — not "dies on a knife's edge to the boss," but "chips it forever and times out." That is the single most demoralizing ARPG experience there is (the "my build hits a wall and there is no kill in it" feeling — the D3-vanilla Inferno-difficulty-cliff trauma). The b6-composed rogue gives the player the genre-correct version: a glass striker that *can* drop the boss if they play the burst window. The deficiency is felt, not cosmetic. **Fix the deficiency.** Verdict: **composition DEFICIENCY → bounded rocket investigation warranted (§5 Option a).**

## 3. The count-vs-efficacy locate — the role floor is CORRECTLY SCOPED; efficacy is a SEPARATE composer concern (the floor is not incomplete, it is one of two layers)

The finding is sharp: the envelope's role histogram MATCHED b6's forced floor (mobility 2 + defensive 1 + burst 1 + AoE-share) yet produced opposite boss outcomes. I locate this precisely, because the framing matters for what rocket does next.

**The role floor did exactly what it was specified to do, and no more.** The role-floor math-note is explicit (§1, §6): the floor guarantees role PRESENCE keyed on coordinates — `def_bin=glass → ≥1 defensive`, `*-fast → ≥2 mobility`, `single-target → ≥1 burst + AoE-share`. It is a *role-composition floor.* It reserves WHICH ROLES are present. It says nothing — by design — about the POWER, SCALING, or geometry-specific boss-efficacy of the skills that fill those roles. That was correct scope: the floor's job was to close the "zero defensive / zero sub-60 burst" gap (it did), not to guarantee that the burst skill it reserves is a boss-killer.

**So is the floor INCOMPLETE?** No — it is *correctly scoped but it is one layer of a two-layer problem we only just separated.* The floor guarantees the kit has A burst skill. It does not guarantee that burst skill carries the boss. The b6 template did MORE than match role counts: b6 forced `skill_power_tier=58` on its burst (role-floor note Rule B reconciliation), fielded defensive=5 (not 1) and mobility=3 (not 2) at kit_size 13 (not 10), and — critically — drew its boss-killer geometry from the b6 builder's specific selections rather than the envelope's weighted geometry draw. **The envelope filled the burst SLOT; b6 filled it with a burst that WORKS on boss.** That is the efficacy gap, and it lives in three candidate places (rocket to localize): (i) skill POWER_TIER — the envelope's burst draws at rogue's native 50–58 with no power floor on the *reserved* burst, while b6 forced 58; (ii) the specific GEOMETRIES the envelope's weighted draw lands the burst/damage roles on vs b6's selections; (iii) SCALING — whether the envelope's reserved-role skills scale into the boss HP/duration window the kills-only timeout enforces.

**The locate, stated cleanly:** the role floor is a PRESENCE guarantee and is correctly scoped as such — it is NOT incomplete in its own terms. **Boss efficacy is a SEPARATE, second composer layer** that the count-vs-efficacy finding has now isolated for the first time: *given role presence, does the skill that fills the boss-killer role have the power/geometry/scaling to actually kill a boss?* The floor answered "is there a burst?" (yes). The open question is "is the burst a boss-killer?" (no, on the envelope; yes, on b6). **Do NOT bolt efficacy onto the role floor** — that would overload a clean coordinate-derived presence floor with a power/scaling concern it should not carry, and risk re-introducing the kind of conjunctive complexity the smuggle-test was designed to forbid. Treat efficacy as its own composer investigation (§5 Option a), informed by exactly *what b6 does to make its burst land that the envelope does not.*
