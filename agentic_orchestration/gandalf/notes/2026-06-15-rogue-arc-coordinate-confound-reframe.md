## 0. One line

The control I demanded did its job — but it falsified my *named* confound (power_tier) and surfaced the *real* one (coordinate): **b6 was never a fair answer-key for the rogue cell.** At matched power AND matched coordinate, b6 lands **0/60** boss kills too — its celebrated 0.9667 was a *medium/small-AOE* build, while the whole arc compared it against the envelope at *close/single-target/glass*. **Both builders fail to clear the boss at the close/ST/glass coordinate** — that shared boss-failure is the load-bearing claim, and it is a property of the **coordinate**, not of either kit's composition. (Critically: the two arms reach 0 kills by DIFFERENT mechanisms — the envelope dies dealing only ~640 dmg, offense-starved; b6's close/ST kit deals ~26,053, **40× more**, drives the boss to ~88% HP, and STILL dies. They are NOT equivalent kits at that coordinate; b6's close/ST build is dramatically more boss-effective and merely falls short. The shared-failure claim does NOT assert composition-equivalence — it asserts that even a far stronger kit cannot bridge this coordinate's reach/durability ceiling, which is exactly why **the coordinate is the deficiency**.) The boss-failure is a property of the **close/ST/glass coordinate-shape** (fragile; lacks boss reach/durability), shared by every builder there — NOT a composition gap between envelope and b6, NOT power_tier, NOT a DoT channel the envelope uniquely lacks. The bleed "boss fix" loses its boss-efficacy justification; the boss-efficacy question relocates onto coordinate-shape tools — which is the **role-floor work already in flight**, not new work.

The per-fight damage instrumentation (jack-ryan's audit, §7) is what makes the shared-failure claim precise rather than over-reaching: **both close/ST arms are 0/60, but they are not the same failure.** The envelope is glass-cannon-without-the-cannon (~640 dmg). b6's close/ST kit deals 40× that, drives the boss to ~88% (best 71%), and still cannot finish — a coherent kit that fights (23–33 player actions/fight, ~22.6s mean, zero 0-action or 0-damage fights) and loses. That is the cleanest possible evidence that the ceiling is the **coordinate**, not the **composition**: a dramatically more boss-effective kit still walls at this coordinate.

Two findings, both load-bearing:

1. **The power_tier confound (my Q4 flag) was FALSE.** The Lever-C envelope was already at power_tier **58**, not 50. The `50` rocket/Q4 read is the structural `PlayerClass` shell value (HP/stat container set by `_build_structural_template`); the fighting skills carry the composer-stamped 58 from `ARCHETYPE_TEMPLATES.skill_power_tier`, uniform across all 13. Q4 read the shell, not the skill. The production-power follow-on is moot (production = the same 58).
2. **The coordinate confound (the real one) — b6 is not a fair comparator.** b6 fails the boss at close/ST/glass — but NOT "exactly as the envelope does." b6 deals 40× the envelope's damage there and drives the boss to ~88% (best 71%) and *still* fails; the envelope is offense-starved at ~640 dmg. The shared property is the **0-kill boss-failure**, not the composition: both fall on the wrong side of the coordinate's reach/durability ceiling, by very different margins. b6's 0.9667 was earned at a *different coordinate* (medium/AOE). The arc's foundational comparison — "envelope ZERO at close/ST vs b6 0.967 at the SAME histogram → composition deficiency" — was reading the same *role histogram* but **two different range/shape coordinates**, and attributing the gap to composition. That is a one-variable-at-a-time violation on the coordinate axis, and it survived all the way to a closed verdict. (The reframe asserts coordinate-shared boss-failure, NOT builder-equivalence — see §0, §7.)

## 2. What HOLDS, what's OVERTURNED

- **HOLDS — the architectural conclusion.** No single global modifier rescues the envelope: M=1.0 zero-kills, robust. The control *reinforces* it — both builders are 0/60 at close/ST/glass regardless of converged mod (envelope at 0.07, b6 at 0.88, both zero) AND regardless of raw offense (b6 lands 40× the envelope's damage, ~88% boss HP, and still zero). The boss failure is a **shape** property, not a **scaling** property and not a **composition** property. (This is the clean part: a glass close-range single-target shape cannot reach/survive the boss no matter how you scale its hits OR how much more boss-effective its kit is — b6's far stronger close/ST kit walls at the same coordinate.)
- **OVERTURNED — the terminal attribution.** "b6 is the answer key the envelope must match; the gap is kit composition" is FALSE. There is **no in-tree kit** that kills the boss at close/ST/glass. The deficiency is a coordinate-shape property shared by every builder at that coordinate.

## 4. The new branch — where the boss-efficacy question actually goes

The boss-efficacy question relocates from *"fix the envelope's composition / scale bleed"* to *"should the close/ST/glass coordinate kill bosses at all, and if so via what coordinate-shape tools?"*

- **The genre read.** A glass-cannon close-range single-target build with no durability/reach tools that clears trash fast but walls on the pinnacle boss is a **recognized, legitimate ARPG shape** — the clearspeed/speedfarm archetype (PoE clearspeed builds that buy a carry for Ubers; Diablo speed-farm builds that can't solo the hardest bosses; Last Epoch glass builds). The genre does NOT make every coordinate kill every boss. Boss-capability is delivered by **coordinate diversity** — some shapes kill bosses (reach + durability + burst), some farm trash (clearspeed glass). That is healthy build identity, not a defect.
- **If we DO want close/ST/glass boss-capable,** the lever is coordinate-shape tools — **reach and/or durability and/or a real burst shape** — NOT bleed, NOT a global modifier. And that lever is **already in flight**: the **role-floor work** adds the defensive (0→floored) and burst (0→floored) roles the envelope rogue was missing (rocket §4 audit: envelope defensive=0/burst=0 vs b6 5/1/3). The role-floor fix IS the coordinate-shape intervention. The real boss-efficacy test is therefore the **G7 HOLD-SIM re-pass on the *floored* rogue at close/ST/glass** — already queued. The reframe does not open a new workstream; it **removes a misattributed lever (bleed) and points the boss-efficacy question at work already in flight.**
- **The honest open question for the re-disposition (and for Matt):** if the *floored* envelope STILL fails the boss at close/ST/glass, the live design question becomes "is close/ST/glass a trash-clearer identity (legitimate; answer the boss via other coordinates) or do we give it dedicated reach/durability?" — a coordinate-identity call, not a bug hunt. We do not answer that until the floored-rogue re-pass returns.
