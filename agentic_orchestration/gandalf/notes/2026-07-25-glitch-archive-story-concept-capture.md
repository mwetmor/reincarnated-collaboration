## Matt's pitch, verbatim

> You are a human teenager in something like slightly post-modern day (maybe 2040), and you
> find a retro floppy disk of some old ARPG. You're crafty, so you make a floppy-disk-drive
> adapter for your favorite VR headset using your 3D printer and turn on the game. From there
> you find yourself in the ARPG and play for just a bit before realizing the disk is either
> glitched and the tape is disintegrating while the adapter is somehow melding with other
> similar games in the archive. You've got to make it out alive, and the only way is to hop
> from game to game by killing historical champions (and saving them along the way with your
> adapter) who are locked in an eternal build vs build faction-struggle. They see you as just
> another one of either their peers or enemy faction members. They also start battling with
> mutated/glitched enemies and mini-bosses which are not killing but actually deleting
> everything from the game. Over the course of the game, you begin to realize that the
> glitched monsters are actually the evil demons of the ARPGs melding with modern day AI
> Agents, like Mr. Anderson from the matrix meets the prime evils from Diablo. Within each
> level is the caravan led by your current faction, and you're able to sync your adapter to
> their caravan to sync gear and items to the cloud to save them as well as potentially
> upload champions that you've saved. Each build-faction seems to charge your adapter, adding
> something unique from their arsenal of powers into it. You would like to spend more time
> figuring out how to use the powers you're gifted, but you don't have long to wait around
> though, because the glitched demons multiply across the levels as time goes on, and only
> time will tell whether you will be able to find a way out before the glitch-demons erase
> you forever along with the old floppy disk.
>
> If you do make it to the end of the main acts of the game, and find yourself in the final
> act, you find that your adapter has been super-charged and you're able to connect it into
> something like a bio-mechanical cryogenic glass vault, which creates the ultimate ARPG
> test-build, generated from the faction-blessing of all of the build-factions that you
> defeated/joined along the way. You just know that these new powers will be what you need to
> face the glitch head-on and defeat it. Ultimately, though, you're only able to send it back
> to where it came from temporarily, but it's enough to get out of the game. Will you put the
> VR headset back on, and give it another spin?
