## 1. Block A — T4 + skill tree architecture lock (6 items)

### Verdict A.1 — Skill tree architecture (Q7 item 1; D69) — **REQUIRES-MATT-CREATIVE-RATIFICATION**

**Decision:** LE-style per-skill mini-trees vs chain-based investment vs hybrid.

**Gandalf-recommended answer:** **Hybrid — chain-based investment as the PRIMARY structure with LE-style per-skill micro-investment as the SECONDARY layer.**

**Specifically:**
- **Primary (chain-based):** Class has 3-4 chains; each chain is a linear-ish investment path of T1 → T2 → T3 → T4 (capstone where applicable per D83). Player spends skill points along the chain; chain investment unlocks chain nodes in sequence. This is the LOAD-BEARING progression structure per D63-D64.
- **Secondary (per-skill micro-investment):** ONCE a skill node is unlocked via chain investment, the player can optionally invest 1-3 ADDITIONAL points into THAT skill to enhance its parameters (damage %, AoE radius, cooldown reduction). Per-skill caps are tight (3 micro-investment slots max per node) to prevent the LE-style explosion-of-options cognitive load.

**Why hybrid:** Pure chain-based misses the LE-recognized strength that "skill-as-its-own-tree" creates ownership-of-the-skill. Pure LE-style mini-trees collapse the T4-as-chain-capstone architecture (D81 + D83) — there's no chain to capstone. Hybrid preserves chain-as-progression-structure AND skill-as-personally-tuned-instrument.

**Alternatives:**
- (A) **Pure chain-based (PoE keystone / Diablo skill tree lineage)** — simpler; cleaner T4-as-capstone semantics; risk = skills feel like "I picked it once and move on"; missing build-craft depth at the skill level
- (B) **Pure LE-style per-skill mini-trees** — high build-craft depth per skill; risk = T4-as-chain-capstone architecture (D81/D83) loses coherence; risk = decision-space explodes (8 actives × 5-10 mini-tree nodes each = 40-80 micro-decisions per build = cognitive overload at casual tier)
- (C) **Hybrid (recommended)** — chain-as-progression + bounded per-skill micro-investment
- (D) **PoE-style "passive forest" supplement** — chain-based PLUS a class-wide passive web (no chain affiliation); rejected per D82 (flat 8 active skills) — passive forest fights the bounded-skill-budget commitment

**Recommendation rationale:**
- Composes with D83 (T4 count = chain count - 1) cleanly — chains have first-class status
- Composes with D81 (T4 algorithm canonical form) — capstones live at chain top per Phase 1 implementation
- Composes with D82 (flat 8 active skills) — micro-investment doesn't proliferate active count
- Composes with D70/D71 (skill point economy) — point budget naturally divides between chain-unlock spending vs micro-investment spending; player choice
- Discipline #23 framing-audit: Q1 load-bearing assumption is "T4 lives at chain capstone position" — verified per D81; Q2 refutation evidence considered (LE pure-mini-tree pattern, GD pure-mastery-tree pattern) — both refute T4-as-chain-capstone if adopted purely; Q3 hybrid refines the framing rather than executes against either pure pattern

**Why REQUIRES-MATT-CREATIVE-RATIFICATION:** the hybrid choice IS architectural creative design — it commits to a specific cognitive-load profile + a specific player-feel for build-craft. Matt should ratify the cognitive-load commitment (3 micro-investment slots per skill).

### Verdict A.3 — Skill point economy (Q7 item 3; D71) — **RATIFIED**

**Decision:** Per-point vs per-chain unlock vs hybrid + total budget.

**Verdict:** **Per-point allocation with chain-prerequisite gates. Total budget = 60 points at L50 endgame baseline.**

**Specifically:**
- **Per-point allocation:** player earns 1 skill point per level (L1 → L50 = 50 base points) + 10 bonus points from progression-node completion events = 60 total at L50 endgame
- **Chain-prerequisite gates:** to unlock T2 in a chain, player must have invested ≥3 points in T1; T3 requires ≥3 in T2; T4 requires ≥3 in T3 (chain-investment threshold = 9 points minimum to reach T4)
- **No exclusivity locks** — player can invest across all chains (per D64: "all T4 capstones REACHABLE; no exclusivity-gate locks")
- **Investment math:** with 60 points and 9-point chain-prerequisite-to-reach-T4, player can fully unlock T4 in 2 chains (18 points minimum) + spread remaining 42 points across micro-investment (per Verdict A.1 hybrid) + other-chain partial investment
- **T4 selection separate from investment:** reaching T4 unlocks the T4 OPTION; player then SELECTS which T4 to activate (composes with D81 Phase 2 "multiple T4 options per chain with selection mechanic")

**Why 60 points at L50:** balances meaningful-choice (player cannot max everything) against build-completion (player can complete a full T4 chain + supplement). Math-grounded per chain count 3-4 × T4-investment-floor 9 = 27-36 minimum-to-reach-all-T4s = 24-33 points remaining for differentiation = right band for meaningful choice without feeling starved.

**Why chain-prerequisite gates (3-point thresholds):** prevents 1-point-skim-everything-to-reach-T4 anti-pattern. Forces chain commitment as decision. Composes with Verdict A.1 hybrid (chain-investment is the LOAD-BEARING progression structure).

**Composition with D65 respec-with-legendary-trigger:** respec returns all 60 points; player re-allocates fully; chain-prerequisite gates re-apply.

**Discipline #29 commitment-to-consequence:** 60-point budget + 9-point chain thresholds = ARCHITECTURAL COMMITMENT; reversibility cost = full-rewrite of skill-point economy if Cycle 13 sim findings show 60 is wrong number. Mitigation: Cycle 13 sim validates the 60-point budget against playability gate D61.

**Stands standalone — gandalf seam authority.** Numbers are calibration-iterable in Cycle 14+ if sim findings warrant.
