### L1. Mathematical model for build-defining-ness

Per Q-A verdict § 2.2:

```
kit_power = α·(class)
          + β·Σ(substrate_pairs)
          + γ·substrate_triple
          + δ·T4_node
          + η·(T4_node × substrate_triple)  ← BUILD-DEFINING-NESS LIVES HERE
          + ε
```

Where η is the interaction-term coefficient. Build-defining-ness IS η > 0 (plus mode-shift + differential properties).

### L2. Hypothesis test framework extension

Per Q-A verdict §§ 4-5:
- **H8** (build-defining-multiplicity): η coefficient significantly different from zero (p < 0.05) with meaningful effect size (Cohen's d > 0.5 OR partial η² > 0.06)

- **H9** (bimodal-enhancement): kit_power distribution exhibits non-unimodal structure (Hartigan's dip test rejects unimodality at p < 0.05 OR GMM model selection identifies ≥ 2 components)
