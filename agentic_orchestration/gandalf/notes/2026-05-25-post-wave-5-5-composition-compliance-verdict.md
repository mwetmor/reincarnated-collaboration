## 1. Pattern A-deep framing-audit (Discipline #23 three-question protocol)

### 1A. Fantasy +10.2pp OUT (33.8% → 45.2%; band 30-35%)

#### Q1 — What load-bearing framing assumptions does the original ±5pp ceiling depend on?

The composition policy v1 § 2.1 fantasy target band 30-35% derives from three composed assumptions:

1. **A1 — Sketch D HEFTY allocation as upper boundary.** Per `v1-bc-target-intent-2026-05-24.md` § 4.1 Sketch D, Cross-cultural / Pan-Fantasy / Hybrid is "HEFTY at ~20%" with substrate-coverage RICH (fantasy_generic 24.8%). The 30-35% band combines Pan-Fantasy ~20% + a portion of substrate-led fantasy_generic skew. The 5pp ±tolerance was a soft-band placeholder rather than a hard architectural commitment.

2. **A2 — Pre-Wave-5.5 substrate composition is the reference baseline.** The 30-35% target assumes the substrate composition at Phase 3 fire-time (when v1_scope = 3,042 was first materialized) represents the natural substrate-led-skew Sketch D accepts. Post-Wave-5.5, 791 rows were removed — primarily contamination, not balanced sampling — so the post-Wave-5.5 substrate is structurally different from the reference baseline.

3. **A3 — Composition-policy target weights apply absolute volumes, not relative shares.** Implied assumption that the per-axis ±5pp tolerance band measures relative composition share at a fixed total v1_scope size. If total v1_scope size changes substantially (3,042 → 2,251 = -26%), the relative share computation operates on different denominator without the policy adjusting target percentages.

4. **A4 — Substrate-led-skew acceptance is bounded by 5pp.** Implied assumption that "substrate-led skew" per Sketch D § 4.1 is loosely defined enough to allow ±5pp wobble but tight enough to prevent ≥10pp deviation.

#### Q2 — What evidence currently in hand refutes these assumptions?

- **Refutes A1 + A2:** Wave 5.5 eviction was contamination-removal (D1c subtype scope-creep + Mode-C-by-semantics tagging artifacts), NOT balanced down-sampling. Per elrond closeout § 3.3, fantasy count went from 1,028 → 1,017 (-11 rows; net unchanged in absolute terms); historical went from 1,749 → 1,181 (-568); military_modern went from 243 → 32 (-211). Fantasy didn't grow; historical and military_modern shrank. The composition consequence is a math-of-composition artifact: same absolute fantasy count over smaller total = larger fractional share. The substrate's underlying fantasy-skew (per composition policy § 2.2 "fantasy_generic HEFTY") is unchanged; only the relative share computation reflects the contamination removal.

- **Refutes A3:** the composition policy § 2.1 target bands were authored against a substrate at fixed total size. Wave 5.5 was an unforeseen mid-cycle remediation reducing v1_scope by ~26%. The policy didn't anticipate this volume change. The ±5pp band is now applied against a denominator different from the one the policy was calibrated for.

- **Refutes A4:** Sketch D § 4.1 explicitly calls out fantasy_generic at substrate-share 24.8% RICH; § 4.3 explicitly says "Cross-cultural / Pan-Fantasy: ~15-18% target (trim — over-represented)." So Sketch D anticipated fantasy_generic substrate over-representation. The 45.2% post-Wave-5.5 share is fantasy_generic-dominated (44.9% per closeout § 3.4) — i.e., the same RICH substrate signal Sketch D acknowledged, now amplified by contamination removal in the historical pool.

#### Q3 — Should the framing be refined rather than executed as-framed?

**YES — refine the framing.** The post-Wave-5.5 +10.2pp fantasy over-share is:

1. **Substrate-led-faithful** (per Sketch D § 4.1 RICH characterization of fantasy_generic substrate; per § 4.3 explicit trim-target acknowledgment of over-representation)
2. **Composition-consequence-of-contamination-removal**, not sampling bias (the absolute fantasy count barely moved; the denominator shrank)
3. **Aligned with composition policy § 2.2 "Pan-Fantasy / Hybrid HEFTY per Matt"** intent (the explicit 20% over-allocation per Matt directive for Pan-Fantasy)

The framing "fantasy is +10.2pp out of band, requires re-balancing" is the wrong framing. The right framing is "fantasy is now 45.2% because contamination removal reduced historical + military_modern denominators; the substrate-led-skew per Sketch D accepts fantasy-RICH composition; this is the truer signal of the v1_scope's design-intent fit."

### 1B. Military_modern -3.6pp OUT (8.0% → 1.4%; band 5-8%)

#### Q1 — What load-bearing framing assumptions does the original 5-8% band depend on?

The composition policy v1 § 2.1 military_modern target band 5-8% derives from:

1. **A1 — Trim from substrate 8.4% per Sketch D fantasy+historical-leaning.** Policy § 2.1 explicitly says "significant trim from substrate's 8.4%; defer modern firearms unless D10 Path C confirms." The 5-8% band is itself a TRIM from substrate.

2. **A2 — Path-C-deferred state.** D10 Path C (per composition policy § 2.1 contextual reference) addresses "modern firearms" inclusion. The Path C decision was DEFERRED per Sketch D. The 5-8% band reflects Path-C-deferred posture (keep some military_modern for genre-bridging; trim significantly per fantasy+historical lean).

3. **A3 — Substrate's military_modern pool is balanced between handheld weapons and heavy systems.** Implied assumption that the 8.4% substrate military_modern share contains a mix of genre-bridgeable handheld military weapons (assault rifles, sidearms, sniper rifles) + heavy systems (UAVs, missiles, naval vehicles, artillery). Trimming the heavy systems would yield ~5-8% handheld military weapons.

#### Q2 — What evidence currently in hand refutes these assumptions?

- **Refutes A3 explicitly.** Per elrond closeout § 1.3 per-source × subtype cross: odin-army-tradoc (2,258 rows; the dominant military_modern source) classified as 1,814 siege_vehicle + 408 ammo_consumable + only 36 handheld_weapon. The substrate's military_modern Tier-A pool is **OVERWHELMINGLY heavy systems** (UAVs, missiles, ATGMs, mortars) — not balanced. The pre-Wave-5.5 8.0% v1_scope military_modern share was almost ENTIRELY heavy systems contamination admitted via the Sub-phase A Tier-A preferred-include rule without subtype-check.

- **Refutes A2.** D10 Path C deferred = "trim modern firearms heavily; keep some for genre-bridging." The post-Wave-5.5 retention of 32 military_modern rows is almost-entirely-handheld (per elrond closeout § 5.1: "handheld_weapon-classified items (sniper rifles, machine pistols, etc.)" — genre-bridgeable). The post-Wave-5.5 1.4% share is MORE aligned with D10-Path-C-deferred intent than the pre-Wave-5.5 8.0% share was.

- **Pre-Wave-5.5 50-row spot-check § 2 A6 stratum:** 4/4 = 100% FAIL on the military_modern sample — UAVs, drones, landing craft. Zero plausibly-genre-bridgeable hardware. The pre-Wave-5.5 8.0% military_modern share was actually 100% genre-misfit per the empirical sample.

#### Q3 — Should the framing be refined rather than executed as-framed?

**YES — refine the framing.** The post-Wave-5.5 1.4% military_modern share is:

1. **More aligned with D10-Path-C-deferred design intent** than the pre-Wave-5.5 8.0% (which was almost-entirely heavy-systems contamination)
2. **Genuine handheld military hardware** per elrond's § 5.1 characterization (sniper rifles, machine pistols, etc.) — genre-bridgeable to fantasy-isekai modern-military fantasy archetypes
3. **Below the original band 5-8%** in absolute terms but ABOVE the implicit "what we actually wanted" target (~0-2% genuine genre-bridgeable military_modern after heavy-systems trim)

The framing "military_modern is -3.6pp out of band, requires re-fire to recover share" is the wrong framing. The right framing is "military_modern is now 1.4% because Wave 5.5 evicted the heavy-systems contamination that was inflating the share to 8.0%; the residual 1.4% is what genuine genre-bridgeable handheld military_modern looks like in the substrate; this is the truer signal of D10-Path-C-deferred design intent."
