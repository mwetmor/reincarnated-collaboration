## 0. TL;DR — verdict

**Stars are PRIMITIVES. Kits are CONSTELLATIONS — named patterns of primitives the engine has connected into Pareto-balanced compositions. The lasso selects primitives; the game looks up which named constellation best covers the player's primitive selection.**

This is **Option 4** in the four-option assessment below. It supersedes the kit-as-star proposal from the 2026-06-05 cosmograph-pivot record AND from my onboarding-resume response. Matt's pushback is correct and load-bearing — kit-as-star was substrate-blind at the *generative* layer in the same way that NA-on-cultural_tradition was substrate-blind at the *attribute* layer (per this morning's framing-audit recognition).

The cosmograph metaphor becomes LITERAL: primitive points are stars; engine-recognized patterns of primitives are constellations; faction overlays are mythological storying of multiple constellations into shared narrative. **Players do what astronomers do — draw imaginary lines between stars to find named patterns the engine has charted before them.**

This verdict is sequenced AT the cosmograph commission boundary, not after — meaning the elrond + drax dispatches authored in the same workstream-window should reflect this verdict, not the kit-as-star scoping.

## 1. The four options under assessment

### Option 1 — Star = Kit (the original proposal)
- Each star is a pre-generated kit at its 8-axis BC coordinate
- Constellations = factions (cluster_id groupings)
- Lasso → k=1 nearest kit
- Backdrop: empty BC space; possibly weapon-substrate field stars

### Option 2 — Star = Primitive (raw substitution)
- Each star is a single primitive (one skill, one weapon-form, one T4 strategy, one element-attribute pairing, one modifier type)
- Constellations = semantic clusters of related primitives (e.g., "the DEX-burst-melee region")
- Lasso → set of primitives → unstructured display
- No kit-level identity until player composes

### Option 3 — Layered (background + bright + overlay)
- Background field: primitives as faint dots
- Mid-layer: kits as bright stars
- Top-layer: faction overlays
- Lasso resolves at kit-layer (k=1)
- Primitives are visible-but-not-interactive

### Option 4 — Star = Primitive; Constellation = Kit (the verdict)
- Each star is a primitive point in the engine's generative substrate
- Each kit the engine has produced becomes a CONSTELLATION — a named pattern connecting the primitives the kit USES into a coherent shape
- The kit's name is the constellation's name (e.g., "Driftstone Warden of the Broken Reach")
- The brightest stars in the constellation = the load-bearing primitives (Pareto-defining per BDI ω+τ → β prediction)
- Lasso selects primitives; game scores constellation-overlap; returns the kit (constellation) whose primitive-pattern best covers the player's selection
- Faction overlays group multiple constellations into shared mythological context (Ursa Major + Ursa Minor sharing bear-mythology)

## 2. Per-option assessment

### Option 1 — Star = Kit

**Design-intent fidelity:**
- HONORS Architecture B substrate-BOUND boundary (canonical 39) at kit-level
- HONORS cohesion-judge LLM-naming work (kit names + narratives exist)
- HONORS faction-cluster pre-computation (factions are constellation groupings)

**Substrate-led discipline violations:**
- **MISALIGNED with substrate-as-cohesion-only architecture** (canonical/story/substrate-design-supplement-2026-05-21.md § 1.3 verbatim: *"You don't enter Diablo II and pick 'I want my Sorceress to be fire-themed.' You build a fire-themed Sorceress by picking fire skills. The CHOICE of skills creates the identity. The same is true in PoE. The QD-engine's substrate-as-cohesion-only architecture makes this explicit at the engine layer."*)
- **MISALIGNED with mechanical-generation-is-substrate-agnostic principle** (substrate-supplement § 1.1 Level 2 — the engine pulls from unified mechanic pool; substrate identity is POST-generation labeling). Kit-as-star treats the kit as the primary substance; the engine treats the kit as emergent composition.
- **MISALIGNED with BDI ω+τ → β intent** (canonical/story/bdi-omega-tau-tables-v1-2026-05-22.md § 1 — the engine PREDICTS which substrate-pairs (composed primitives) carry build-defining intensity. Kit-as-star hides the β-magnitude surface from the player.)

**Build-identity differentiator alignment:**
- **MISALIGNED with genre-canonical Family B (extractable/imbue-able power)** (arpg-build-defining-mechanisms-2026-05-29 findings — D2 Runewords, D3 Kanai's Cube, D4 Legendary Aspects all operate at PRIMITIVE acquisition, not kit selection). Kit-as-star doubles down on "you pick a complete kit" which is the WEAKEST genre pattern (closest analog: D4 Class Specialization, which is the least beloved D4 mechanic).
- **WEAKLY ALIGNED with Family D (passive-tree capstone)** at best — PoE Keystones are primitive-level commits, not kit-level

**Sparsity failure mode:**
- 68,040 BC cells; 1.5% occupancy at 1,000 seasons (canonical/story/qd-engine-bc-axes-lock-2026-05-20.md § 2 verbatim: *"This is sparse — characteristic of MAP-Elites at higher dimensionality — but functional"*). Sparsity is BY DESIGN.
- Kit-as-star makes sparsity a *visualization deficit* (empty regions); Option 4 makes sparsity a *feature* (the named constellations sit within a rich primitive sky)

**Player experience:**
- Solves blank-canvas at frame one (named-bearer stars + faction labels)
- But: modification is awkward — sliders move lasso-position abstractly; player doesn't see the PRIMITIVES they're shifting
- The kit-as-star surface hides the engine's most interesting work (composed-primitive identity prediction) from the player

**Verdict on Option 1:** REJECTED. Was the right first-instinct framing but is substrate-blind at the generative-substrate layer. Same failure-class as the NA-on-cultural_tradition framing caught earlier this session.

### Option 2 — Star = Primitive (raw)

**Design-intent fidelity:**
- HONORS substrate-as-cohesion-only architecture (primitives are first-class)
- HONORS substrate-led discipline at the right layer

**Failure modes:**
- **No kit-level identity surfaces.** Player sees primitives without seeing the engine's named compositions. The blank-canvas problem returns through the back door: "OK, I've selected katana + DEX + cleave + ELEMENT_CONVERSION T4 — *what character is this*?"
- **Cohesion-judge work invisible.** The LLM-named identities + faction narratives + mythic weight do not surface.
- **Discards the engine's curated-output value.** The engine produces ~150 named-bearer kits with prose identities; Option 2 makes these invisible.

**Verdict on Option 2:** REJECTED. Over-corrects on the substrate-blindness diagnosis; loses what kit-level work has produced.

### Option 3 — Layered (background + bright + overlay)

**Design-intent fidelity:**
- HONORS both layers (primitives + kits)
- HONORS faction overlays

**Failure modes:**
- **Three-layer cognitive load.** Players parse three semantic registers (faint primitive dots; bright kit stars; faction polygons). High onboarding cost.
- **Layer-disconnect.** Lasso resolves at one layer (kits); primitives are visible-but-not-interactive. This creates the question "if I can see the primitives, why can't I select them?"
- **Primitive layer is decorative.** Without functional interaction, primitive stars become field noise. The substrate-led claim is performative, not load-bearing.

**Verdict on Option 3:** REJECTED-WITH-PARTIAL-USE. Layering is a good *visualization* discipline but the wrong *interaction* model. The Option 4 architecture USES layering visually (faction overlays as constellation-grouping context) but locates interaction at the primitive-and-constellation level.

### Option 4 — Star = Primitive; Constellation = Kit

**Design-intent fidelity:**
- HONORS substrate-as-cohesion-only architecture EXACTLY (substrate-supplement § 1.3 in its verbatim claim: choice-of-primitives creates identity)
- HONORS Architecture B substrate-BOUND boundary (canonical 39 § 0.5 dependency chain — kits emerge from substrate + mechanic pool composition)
- HONORS BDI ω+τ → β prediction (kits ARE named because their composed-primitive β crossed legendary-identity threshold)
- HONORS canonical 39 § 0.5.1 6-step content lifecycle (kits = compositions of T4 nodes + skill chains + gear specs + legendary instances + sim validation; all of these are primitives connected into a kit-pattern)

**Build-identity differentiator alignment:**
- **STRONGLY ALIGNED with genre-canonical Family B (extractable/imbue-able power)** — primitives ARE the extractable/imbue-able powers. Player lassoing primitives is doing what D4 Aspect-assembly does mentally.
- **STRONGLY ALIGNED with Family D (passive-tree capstone)** — capstone primitives (T4 strategies, keystone-equivalent gear specs) are extra-bright stars
- **STRONGLY ALIGNED with Family A (intra-skill transformation)** — skill-internal transformations show up as primitive constellations within the larger kit pattern
- **STRONGLY ALIGNED with the speedfarm ↔ push binary** (37% empirical variant-splitting; arpg-community-axes finding) — player can re-lasso the same constellation with different primitive emphasis to get the speedfarm vs push variant

**Constellation metaphor LITERAL:**
- Sky = primitive substrate (full generative vocabulary)
- Pattern of stars = pattern of primitives a kit uses
- Constellation name = kit name (cohesion-judge-LLM-derived)
- Brightest stars in constellation = load-bearing primitives (β-driving)
- Faction = mythological grouping of constellations into shared narrative (e.g., Ashwind Vanguard contains multiple constellations united by faction-story)
- Bright vs faint constellations = engine's confidence in the named kit (Pareto rank + gauntlet pass rate)

**Sparsity becomes truthful:**
- Empty regions of primitive space = primitives the engine has not yet COMPOSED into a constellation
- This is honest signal: D10 evidence (genre-skew) AND engine maturity AND future generation territory all visible
- Future engine cycles ADD constellations without re-architecting the sky

**Player experience:**
- **Blank-canvas resolved at four layers:**
  1. Frame one: primitive sky is dense + colorful by element-attribute coupling
  2. First glance: constellation outlines + names are visible (already storied)
  3. First lasso: primitive selection + matched constellation appears instantly
  4. Modification: tilt primitives → constellation match flickers → constellation crossing felt as identity shift
- **Engine's most interesting work is visible** — composed-primitive identity prediction (BDI β) is exactly what the player is exploring

**Failure modes:**
- **Implementation complexity is real but tractable.** Primitive extraction per kit requires JOIN over (skill composition + T4 selection + gear specs + weapon-form tokens + element + faction). All of this exists in the engine's content lifecycle (canonical 39 § 0.5.1).
- **Resolution ambiguity** when lasso spans multiple constellations partially. Solvable with weighted-overlap scoring (per-primitive Pareto weight × overlap fraction); show top-3 candidates if no clear winner.
- **Visualization needs care** — drawing lines between every kit's primitive set creates clutter. Solution: render constellation lines only for the NEAREST constellations to the player's current lasso position; render faint constellation halos for unselected kits.
- **Engine substrate-vocabulary extraction needs elrond+rocket collaboration** to enumerate which primitives are first-class stars (vs derivatives or modifiers of primitives). This is design-spec-as-math handoff per Discipline #18.

**Verdict on Option 4:** ADOPT. This is the architectural pivot that honors substrate-led discipline at the generative-substrate layer.

**Total first-class primitive stars: ~270-330.** Rich enough sky for visual engagement; sparse enough for lasso resolution to be tractable.

### 4.2 The constellation (what makes a named pattern)

A **constellation** is the set of primitives a kit USES, connected by lines into a recognizable shape.

For each named-bearer kit in `cycle14-season-00{1,2,3}-wave-b-identities.json`:

1. **Primitive extraction:** the kit's composition surfaces primitive memberships:
   - element_primary → one element-star
   - attribute (STR/INT/WIS/DEX) → one attribute-star
   - bc_tuple_json (range / tempo / amplitude / proxy_density) → BC-axis stars
   - weapon-form tokens (from chain_composition + gear specs) → weapon-form stars
   - T4 strategy (from character_t4_candidate) → T4-strategy star (extra-bright)
   - resource_model → resource-economy star
   - defensive profile (derived from gear) → defensive-profile star
   - parent_cluster_id → faction halo membership
2. **Constellation-line drawing:** connect the primitive-star set with thin lines (constellation pattern)
3. **Brightness gradient:** load-bearing primitives (high BDI β contribution per ω+τ) render brightest; supporting primitives render fainter
4. **Constellation name:** the kit's `kit_name_canonical` (e.g., "Driftstone Warden of the Broken Reach")
5. **Constellation narrative:** the kit's `kit_identity_narrative` (already cohesion-judge-approved)

### 4.3 The lasso → constellation resolution algorithm

1. Player draws closed polygon over the cosmograph
2. Compute SET of primitive-stars within the lasso (2D projection)
3. For each known constellation (kit), compute:
   - `coverage_fraction` = (lassoed primitives in constellation) / (total primitives in constellation)
   - `density_score` = (lassoed primitives in constellation) / (total lassoed primitives)
   - `weight_score` = Σ (load-bearing primitive weight × coverage) per BDI β contribution
   - `composite_score` = 0.4 × coverage_fraction + 0.3 × density_score + 0.3 × weight_score
4. **Matched constellation = max(composite_score)**
5. If top-2 are within 5% of each other → show BOTH as ambiguous, let player narrow lasso
6. If no constellation scores ≥ 0.3 → side panel says: *"You've gazed into uncharted sky. The nearest charted form is X."* — show nearest single match + flag as "no clean constellation; possible future-engine territory"

### 4.4 Modification flow

Player has matched a constellation. Side panel shows:
- The kit's name + narrative (existing rich-character-view component repurposed from cycle-18 recovery-2)
- The constellation's primitive set, with load-bearing primitives highlighted
- **"Shift" controls:** click any primitive star to ADD it to the lasso (re-resolve) or REMOVE it (re-resolve)
- **Spirit guide voice (Phase D):** *"You've drifted from the Ashwind Vanguard's banner into the Iron Threshold's discipline — the strike now lands grounded instead of gust-borne."* (Constellation crossing detected as semantic event)

### 4.5 Visualization architecture

| Layer | Render | Opacity |
|---|---|---|
| Background field | Weapon-substrate library projection (89K rows) — faint cultural-tradition-tinted dots | 0.1-0.2 |
| Primitive stars | ~270 first-class stars per § 4.1 | 0.6-0.9 by primitive load-bearing in current lasso context |
| Constellation lines | Connecting lines for nearest 3-5 constellations to current lasso position | 0.4-0.8 |
| Constellation labels | Kit names for top constellations | Bright on nearest; faint elsewhere |
| Faction halos | Faction-grouping polygons (mythological context) | 0.15-0.25 |

### 4.6 What changes vs the kit-as-star proposal

| Element | Kit-as-star (rejected) | Primitive-star + kit-constellation (verdict) |
|---|---|---|
| What a star represents | One pre-generated kit | One primitive (skill / weapon-form / T4 strategy / element / etc.) |
| Constellation meaning | Faction (group of kits in BC neighborhood) | Kit (named pattern of primitives) |
| Lasso target | Nearest kit-star | Nearest matching constellation by overlap-score |
| Sparsity meaning | Empty BC cells = visualization deficit | Empty primitive regions = future-engine territory (truthful signal) |
| Substrate fidelity | Kit-level (post-cohesion) | Primitive-level (engine's actual generative substrate) |
| Player mental model | "Pick a character" | "Compose a pattern; recognize an identity the engine has named" |
| Modification | Tilt abstract sliders | Add/remove primitive stars; constellation match updates |
