## Flag #2 — What banks across the roguelite reset? (carried from index §5)

**The contradiction.** `gameplay-loop-design.md` §8 (Nemesis mechanic) keeps the **lieutenant-becoming** as a persistent gain — *"+3, you keep what you kill."* But §23.3 states the **champion-body is NOT kept** at run-end. These read in tension.

**The question.** When a roguelite run ends, what persists — the *becoming/identity*, or nothing of the embodied power?

**gandalf lean (not a ruling).** The **roster/grimoire entry** banks (the record of who you became — a Hall-of-Heroes-style permanent); the **run-specific embodied power resets** (you re-earn the body each descent). This is the Hades model (Mirror/Heat banks; the run resets) and it preserves the roguelite tension while honoring the reincarnation premise. **Why it matters / player consequence:** this gates the engine's per-kit-level model (tracker III.2) and the +3-becoming band — the engine cannot spec per-kit progression against the run until the persistence contract is ruled.

## Flag #3 — The temporal triad breaks under the patron reframe

**The contradiction.** `canonical/story/2026-06-13-companion-as-hall-of-heroes-ally-commitment.md` §6 (Matt-confirmed 2026-06-13) locks a **temporal triad**: past-self **companion** fights beside you · future-self **spirit guide** advises you · present-self **Earth Self** is you. The v2 set **RETIRES the warm future-self spirit guide** (Matt 2026-06-30) and splits its advisory function two ways: **(A)** the **demigod-jailer** — the jailer-mentor who *selects and steers* (keystone §16–17, §19.1) — as **tutorial instructor**; **(B)** the caged **death-god/patron** as a **seldom-heard guidance** voice. **Neither is the player's future self.** *(Two-entity distinction, Matt 2026-06-30: the **patron god** Daikoku/Mahakala is a SEPARATE presence — the hijacked, caged, **unreadable** deity the player communes with [god-speech §19.3] — it is NOT an advisor and NOT the demigod. The advisor/steerer is the demigod-jailer; the patron is the god.)* So the triad's future corner does not survive as written.

**The question.** Does the future-advisor corner survive in some form, or does the triad collapse to a dyad (past companion + present self) with the **demigod-jailer** occupying a different, non-triad role (the steering voice above/outside the player — and the **patron god** a deeper, separate presence again)?

**gandalf lean (not a ruling).** Collapse the *temporal* triad to a **past↔present dyad** (companion + Earth Self), and let the **demigod-jailer** be the **fourth, asymmetric voice** — not a corner of the player's own timeline but the power that *selects and steers* it (the **patron god** deeper still, caged behind the jailer). This is thematically *stronger*: the antagonistic-helpful steerer should NOT be cozily folded into the player's self-continuity; its wrongness depends on it being other. **Player consequence:** the "future self advises you" warmth is exactly the AI-companion tone v2 is moving away from; keeping it would blunt the demigod-mentor's menace. But this unwinds a Matt-confirmed structure — Matt's call.

**RESOLVED 2026-06-30 (consequence of the Flag #6 ruling; not a separate Matt ruling).** The future-self advisor corner does **not** survive as a timeline-corner. Its functions are redistributed per the #6 split: **steering** → the demigod (asymmetric, *other*, outside the player's self-continuity — the lean's "fourth voice," confirmed); **daily relationship/warmth** → the **hub ensemble** (the faith's people, not a future self); **rare metaphysical guidance** → the death-god (§19.3). The temporal triad collapses to a **past↔present dyad** (companion + Earth Self). *The past-self companion's survival + unlock trigger is a separate live question — see Flag #4 (Path-Pure molt→run-trigger), still queued.* The "future self advises you" warmth is gone by design — the hub relationship replaces it without the self-continuity coziness that would have blunted the demigod's menace.

## Flag #4 — Season cadence → run-model equivalent (the Path-Pure bootstrap)

**The contradiction.** The companion doc §7 (RULED **Path Pure**, Matt 2026-06-16) is built on a **per-season cadence**: *"season 1 you fight alone; your first molt returns as your first companion in season 2."* The seasonal-release model was **retired 2026-06-02** (`2026-06-02-season-archive-realm-expansion-pivot.md`) and v2's run model is **25-min / 20-floor roguelite descents** (`gameplay-loop-design.md` §23). "Molting is season-end" and "first molt returns season 2" have no season to live in.

**The question.** What is the run-model equivalent of molting + the Path-Pure absence→arrival beat? Does a "molt" happen per-descent? Per-meta-progression-tier? Does the companion unlock at a **descent-count or Hall-population threshold** instead of "season 2"?

**gandalf lean (not a ruling).** Re-anchor "molt" to a **meta-progression milestone** (e.g., first full Hall ascension after N descents), not a calendar season. The Path-Pure *beat* (you fight alone first; your own first ascended self returns as your first companion) is too good to lose — it just needs a run-model trigger instead of "season 2." **Player consequence:** the first-companion arrival is a tentpole emotional moment; if it silently never fires because "season 2" no longer exists, the whole companion layer is dead content. This needs a concrete unlock criterion.

The death-god's **Daikoku-as-luck masking Mahakala-as-death** duality stays as **unreadable-Rorschach texture** — the player may *project* a trickster reading onto its silence (that projection is the Rorschach working), but it does **not** deliver jabs, because a jabbing god is a *readable* god and Matt ruled it unreadable (§19.3). ## Flag #6 — The loop-doc's "patron companion" IS the keystone's demigod-mentor (cross-Matt-doc contradiction; re-labels a core subsystem)

**The contradiction (intra-v2-set — surfaced 2026-06-30 by Matt's god-vs-demigod correction).** Two Matt-authored v2 docs assign the **voice-in-your-head antagonistic-helpful companion** to DIFFERENT entities:
- `gameplay-loop-design.md` §§2c/14/15/16 (END-STATE AUTHORITY for the loop): the **patron deity** IS the voice — the build-helper/tutor (§2c.1), the opening-tutorial voice (§14 "patron-voice tutorial"), the Hades/GLaDOS/Wheatley **antagonistic-helpful banter companion** (§15 "The patron companion"), the **"you owe it"** Faustian-debt holder, and the target of the **defiance↔devotion retort axis** (§16). The word "demigod" **never appears** in the doc.
- `story-keystone.md` §§16-17/§19 (latest story canon; governs the story frame per index §3): the **demigod-jailer** is the guidance/steering/normalizing voice (§19.1 "the demigod-mentor speaks right after" — the **Mercer manipulation**); the **patron god** (Daikoku/Mahakala) is the CAGED, rare, **unreadable** deity the player rarely communes with (§19.3 — "interesting"; emphatically NOT chatty antagonistic-helpful banter).

Matt's 2026-06-30 correction ("the patron deity is most definitely not a demigod. It is a god. The demigod is the jailer") resolves the COSMOLOGY but not the loop-doc's labeling: the chatty antagonistic-helpful companion the loop doc calls "the patron" matches the keystone's **demigod-mentor**, not the caged unreadable patron god.

**The question.** Does the loop-doc's whole §§14-16 companion subsystem — the antagonistic-helpful banter, the "you owe it" debt, the defiance↔devotion retort axis, and the patron-banter **tech decision** (§435 LLM-vs-templated) — **re-label from "the patron" to "the demigod-mentor"** (the patron god then being the deeper caged deity, §19.3)? Or is the relationship genuinely split (demigod steers + banters; patron god is the rare unreadable communion)?

**RULED — Matt 2026-06-30.** The banter/relationship axis owner is **neither the demigod nor the death-god — it is the HUB NPC ENSEMBLE** (Rita and the cult's human faces). Matt **rejected the premise** that the retort axis needs a single chatty voice at the player's shoulder: *"the banter-relationship was never one voice; it's an ensemble in the hub — the Hades model."* The full reassignment:
- **(A) The demigod** — tutorial voice (§14) + **punctuated Mercer-mentor** at key story beats (keystone §19.1). **NOT daily banter.** *Optional enhancement, a deliberate phase: warmer/chattier early → recedes and cools as the reveal nears, so the withdrawal of the companion becomes a tragedy-beat. Never the steady-state loop voice.*
- **(B) The death-god / patron** — rare, **unreadable Rorschach** (§19.3); seldom-heard guidance. **Unchanged.**
- **(C) The hub ensemble** — **owns the daily antagonistic-helpful banter (§15) and the defiance↔devotion personality axis (§16), which IS the cult-standing economy (§23.5) given a voice.** Defiance↔devotion is expressed through **actions** (hand-in vs. hoard §23.4; how you die; how you treat the faithful) and judged in the hub by NPCs reacting to standing + heresy. "Who you are to the voice" → "who you are to the faith, witnessed by its people."

**Why this beats gandalf's prior lean (which was WRONG — it carried the same hidden premise Matt rejected).** gandalf had leaned demigod-owns-the-banter; that rested on assuming the axis needs *one* legible interlocutor. Matt's answer is better on four load-bearing counts: **(1) Mercer-reveal dilution** — a background-helpful betrayer *detonates*; a quip-companion who's been snarking at you for 80 hours merely *turns*. (2) **Tonal collision** — warm-seductive Mercer and snarky-antagonistic GLaDOS cannot be the same mouth. (3) **Double-coding doesn't scale** — innocent-first-pass/damning-on-replay is a scalpel for a *few* keystone beats, impossible across *hundreds* of banter lines. (4) **The Rorschach is better for defiance↔devotion** — defiance into a silent unreadable void (not knowing if it notices, or if your refusal means anything) is more existentially loaded than defiance against a god who argues back; the dread is the asset. **The dissolve:** the relationship layer was always the *hub*, and the §23.5 cult-standing economy already implements it mechanically — so §16 and §23.5 are the same axis, now unified. **Player consequence:** the player's primary relationship is now the *faith and its people* (a relationship that can be *lost* — the tragic-hub-face beat lands because the bond was real), not a disembodied quipping AI — which is exactly the AI-companion tone v2 was already moving away from. **PROPAGATED:** `gameplay-loop-design.md` §§2c/14/15/16/21/22 relabeled 2026-06-30 (§2c carries the consolidated ruling note). **Cross-ref:** Flags #3 and #5 resolve cleanly under this ruling (see each).
