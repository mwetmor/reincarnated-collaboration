## Flag #2 — What banks across the roguelite reset? (carried from index §5)

**The contradiction.** `gameplay-loop-design.md` §8 (Nemesis mechanic) keeps the **lieutenant-becoming** as a persistent gain — *"+3, you keep what you kill."* But §23.3 states the **champion-body is NOT kept** at run-end. These read in tension.

**The question.** When a roguelite run ends, what persists — the *becoming/identity*, or nothing of the embodied power?

**gandalf lean (not a ruling).** The **roster/grimoire entry** banks (the record of who you became — a Hall-of-Heroes-style permanent); the **run-specific embodied power resets** (you re-earn the body each descent). This is the Hades model (Mirror/Heat banks; the run resets) and it preserves the roguelite tension while honoring the reincarnation premise. **Why it matters / player consequence:** this gates the engine's per-kit-level model (tracker III.2) and the +3-becoming band — the engine cannot spec per-kit progression against the run until the persistence contract is ruled.

## Flag #3 — The temporal triad breaks under the patron reframe

**The contradiction.** `canonical/story/2026-06-13-companion-as-hall-of-heroes-ally-commitment.md` §6 (Matt-confirmed 2026-06-13) locks a **temporal triad**: past-self **companion** fights beside you · future-self **spirit guide** advises you · present-self **Earth Self** is you. The v2 set replaces the warm future-self **spirit guide** with the **patron deity** (Daikoku/Mahakala — an antagonistic-helpful jailer-steerer, keystone §16–17). **The patron is NOT the player's future self.** So the triad's future corner does not survive as written.

**The question.** Does the future-advisor corner survive in some form, or does the triad collapse to a dyad (past companion + present self) with the patron occupying a different, non-triad role (the jailer-voice above/outside the player)?

**gandalf lean (not a ruling).** Collapse the *temporal* triad to a **past↔present dyad** (companion + Earth Self), and let the patron be the **fourth, asymmetric voice** — not a corner of the player's own timeline but the power *over* it. This is thematically *stronger*: the antagonistic-helpful patron should NOT be cozily folded into the player's self-continuity; its wrongness (the slow horror of learning what Pax is) depends on it being other. **Player consequence:** the "future self advises you" warmth is exactly the AI-companion tone v2 is moving away from; keeping it would blunt the patron's menace. But this unwinds a Matt-confirmed structure — Matt's call.

## Flag #4 — Season cadence → run-model equivalent (the Path-Pure bootstrap)

**The contradiction.** The companion doc §7 (RULED **Path Pure**, Matt 2026-06-16) is built on a **per-season cadence**: *"season 1 you fight alone; your first molt returns as your first companion in season 2."* The seasonal-release model was **retired 2026-06-02** (`2026-06-02-season-archive-realm-expansion-pivot.md`) and v2's run model is **25-min / 20-floor roguelite descents** (`gameplay-loop-design.md` §23). "Molting is season-end" and "first molt returns season 2" have no season to live in.

**The question.** What is the run-model equivalent of molting + the Path-Pure absence→arrival beat? Does a "molt" happen per-descent? Per-meta-progression-tier? Does the companion unlock at a **descent-count or Hall-population threshold** instead of "season 2"?

**gandalf lean (not a ruling).** Re-anchor "molt" to a **meta-progression milestone** (e.g., first full Hall ascension after N descents), not a calendar season. The Path-Pure *beat* (you fight alone first; your own first ascended self returns as your first companion) is too good to lose — it just needs a run-model trigger instead of "season 2." **Player consequence:** the first-companion arrival is a tentpole emotional moment; if it silently never fires because "season 2" no longer exists, the whole companion layer is dead content. This needs a concrete unlock criterion.

## Flag #5 — The §1 patron-sketch device-orphans (re-home or retire)

**Context.** `design-decisions-session.md` §1 (the earliest patron sketch — sealed death-deity on Ereshkigal/Nergal/Morrígan) is **superseded** by Daikoku/Mahakala. The *origin* is dead. But §1 carried four **devices** that are not auto-dead — several fit Daikoku/Mahakala *better*:

| Device | Disposition question |
|---|---|
| **Trickster-jab** (the patron's needling voice) | Daikoku-as-luck-god masking Mahakala-as-death fits a trickster register *better* than a solemn sealed-god. Re-home to the patron voice? |
| **Layered crackable-alias + authored-slow true-name** (the player slowly learns the patron's real name) | This IS the Four-Veils knowledge-stratification engine (keystone). The "Daikoku → Mahakala" reveal is exactly a crackable-alias→true-name arc. Re-home? |
| **Seven-gated descent** as floor-structure | v2 is 20-floor / three-beat (§23). Does the seven-gate motif retire, or survive as a sub-structure (e.g., 7 named thresholds within 20 floors)? |
| **Descent ≠ underworld aesthetic** | v2 descent is a **soul-temple breach into a living realm** (keystone §14), not an underworld. The "not-underworld" instinct survives — confirm retire-the-label-keep-the-instinct. |

**gandalf lean (not a ruling).** Re-home trickster-jab + layered-name to the patron (they fit Daikoku/Mahakala better than they fit the dead sealed-god); retire seven-gated as a hard floor-count but keep it available as flavor; the descent≠underworld instinct already survives in keystone §14. **Player consequence:** these are texture, not structure — low-risk either way, but worth a quick ruling so they're not silently amputated.
