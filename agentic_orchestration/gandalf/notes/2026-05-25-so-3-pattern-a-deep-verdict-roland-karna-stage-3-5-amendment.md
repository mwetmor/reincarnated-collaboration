## 1. Framing-audit (Pattern A-deep three-question protocol per Discipline #23 + gandalf OP § 4.1)

### Q1: What load-bearing framing assumptions does the Sketch F anchor selection depend on?

The Sketch F anchor framework (composition policy § 5.2 + `v1-bc-target-intent-2026-05-24.md` § 5.2) depends on:

1. **A1 — Composite scoring auto-promotes substrate rows containing Sketch F anchor names.** Implied assumption that Tier-S/A composite scoring (quality_composite_score + provenance richness + cultural-lineage signal) reliably surfaces named-bearer anchors to Tier-S/A and through D1a auto-include.

2. **A2 — `extracted_named_bearer` field is a reliable anchor-matching signal.** Implied assumption that the Stage 1.5 named-bearer extraction populates this field accurately, including handling of compound-bearer cases.

3. **A3 — Substrate-residence of an anchor implies the substrate row is a valid substrate seed for that anchor.** Implied assumption that if Stage 1.5 tags a row with Karna-bearer, that row is a sensible substrate seed for a Karna-anchored form at Phase 5 cohesion-coalescence.

4. **A4 — The 9 substrate-resident anchors + 4 gap-fill split (per Stage 1.5 finding) is binding.** Implied assumption that Roland + Karna substrate-residence is sufficient for cell-coverage; gap-fill is not needed for them.

5. **A5 — Register-cap + tier-protection compose to preserve named-bearer anchor presence at v1_scope.** Implied assumption that the Phase 2 sampler will not lose anchor-bearing rows to register-cap competition.

### Q2: What evidence currently in hand (or surfaceable in current scope) could refute these assumptions?

Empirical refutation evidence — surfaced in <2 minutes via direct substrate query during this sub-agent invocation:

- **Refutes A1 + A5:** Roland's 3 substrate rows are Tier-B (NULL-subtype) historical NULL-typed. They don't compete via composite-scoring auto-promotion at Tier-S/A — they sit at Tier-B. Sub-phase B (Tier-B archetype-floor-fill) didn't surface them because the archetype-floor-fill prioritizes by archetype-cell, not by named-bearer presence. Sub-phase C (Tier-B register-target fill) didn't surface them because they're already at register-cap (historical at 57.5%). **Empirical:** Roland is not protected by the existing sampler structure at any phase.

- **Refutes A2 + A3:** Karna's 6 substrate-resident rows include id=177014 "Tank EX (Karna Tank)" — a 2002-era Indian DRDO main battle tank prototype. The `extracted_named_bearer = "Karna"` is a metadata-allusion (the tank was named after the mythological figure), NOT an indication the substrate row is a Vedic-mythology weapon seed. Per the 50-row spot-check § 2 + § 4 Diagnosis 3, this is the EXACT pattern Discipline #25 semantic-layer rep-audit was authored for (`canonical/story/marginal-lineage-tagging-pattern-2026-05-23.md` § 2.4). The substrate row is correctly D1c-excluded (subtype=siege_vehicle) but the bearer-field is misleading the anchor-coverage analysis.

- **Refutes A4:** if 2 of 6 Karna-bearer substrate rows are Mode-C-by-semantics artifacts (Tank EX + at least one of the compound-bearer rows with 10+ Indian mythology names), the "substrate-resident" count is overstated. Even with Path 1 named-bearer protection, the protected rows would include Tank EX and other contamination — strictly worsening the Karna anchor seed pool.

- **Compounds with 50-row spot-check finding:** the broader substrate-tagging-artifact pattern (Dark Elf Particle Rifle tagged as Thor; AIM-68 Big Q tagged as Quetzalcoatl; KelTec SUB-2000 tagged as Grendel; etc.) demonstrates that the named-bearer / named-mythological-match fields contain systemic Mode-C-by-semantics contamination across MULTIPLE anchors. The Roland + Karna cases are not isolated; they're symptoms of a broader pattern.

### Q3: Should the framing be refined rather than executed as-framed?

**YES — refine the framing.** The elrond Phase 3 framing presents 3 paths under the assumption that all 3 of Roland's substrate rows and all 6 of Karna's substrate rows are legitimate anchor seeds. The empirical refutation reveals this is partly false — substrate-tagging artifacts contaminate the Karna seed pool. The 3-path framing should be refined to:

- **Path 1-refined (re-run with named-bearer protection):** would protect 3 Roland rows (likely all legitimate) + 6 Karna rows (4 legitimate + 2 likely artifacts). Net: lifts 7 of 9 to v1_scope; 2 artifacts contaminate Karna anchor's substrate-seed pool. Cost: ~9 additional rows; v1_scope grows to ~3,051.

- **Path 2-refined (defensive Stage 3.5 amendment):** engine-author ~3-5 Roland entries + ~3-5 Karna entries. These bypass the substrate-tagging-artifact problem entirely. Roland's substrate residents (Durendal, Précieuse) become Sidecar B / v1.1+ enrichment seeds rather than v1_scope members — they remain in substrate, not lost. Karna's substrate residents include some artifacts that should NOT be amplified.

- **Path 3 (accept-and-defer):** accepts the south_asian under-representation (2.6% vs target 3-4%) AND accepts that the Vedic anchor has no engine-authored representation in v1. Design-coherence cost: high. South_asian is one of the cultural traditions Matt has explicitly named for substrate richness (per `canonical/story/gear-substrate-rule-table-v1-2026-05-22.md` historical context). Karna is the Tier-1 anchor for Vedic — losing it would be a genre signature loss.

**Path 2-refined wins on three criteria:**

1. **Anchor-seed cleanliness:** engine-authored entries are guaranteed-clean per D7 AI-tell discipline + gandalf curation. Substrate-protection inherits artifact contamination.
2. **Cultural-tradition lift:** Karna gap-fill compounds with the broader south_asian under-representation (2.6% → expected lift toward 3-4% target with Karna gap-fill + Sidecar B Vedic enrichment).
3. **Architectural defensiveness:** Path 2 covers BOTH the compound-bearer fragility AND the substrate-tagging-artifact contamination AND the south_asian under-representation in a single ~6-10 entry amendment. Path 1 covers only the first.
