Playtest 4 · design

barrow_v2: the King's Henge

What the arena actually requires

The simulation that drives every fight has no floor. It assumes an open plane with nothing blocking movement or sight. Its only geometry is six spawn points, an 8 m scatter circle around each, and your start position at the centre. The walls, the four islands and the six green pools you see in the arena are a drawing traced from your minimap footage, and the simulation never reads it.

That drawing doesn't fit the simulation. Measured against the spawn points, p01 (west) and p04 (east) are 8 to 9 m outside the walls with their scatter circles entirely off the floor. p02, p03 and p06 sit on the wall line with half or more of their circles outside. Only the centre ambush point fits.

The concept

You fight inside a great henge on a snowbound hill. The floor is flat, open snow and ice, ringed by standing stones on a low kerb that marks the edge you can't cross. Outside the stones, five barrow mounds face inward. Their carved doors are the spawn points, so monsters walk out of the dead's own houses. Snow slopes, birch groves and cairns run off beyond them, all outside the floor.

  • p02, north: the King's Barrow. The largest mound, with the carved lintel door already built for the first barrow. The Barrow King comes from here last.
  • p01 west and p03 south: two lesser barrows.
  • p04 and p06, east and south-east: one long twin barrow with two doors, since the two points are only 29° apart.
  • p05, the ambush 10 m from the start: a field of shallow graves inside the henge. Bodies burst up through the snow, which is exactly what the emerge animations already do.
  • Inside the henge, the variety comes from things you can walk over: frozen tarns, toppled stones lying flat, drifts, a worn stone path to the King's door. Nothing inside blocks movement, so what you see and what the simulation computes agree.

Each barrow will look different. Seven loose concept sketches are being drawn to choose from: the King's Barrow, a broken barrow, an ice-sealed barrow, a stone-ship barrow, a cairn barrow, a burnt barrow and a chained twin barrow.

Why it fits

  • Same camera. The barrow was already built at the arena's pitch (52.95°) and the arena's plate scale (100.62 px/m). The only change is dropping its 47° yaw to the arena's zero yaw.
  • Same characters. The heroes and enemies stay the arena's sprite packs. Nothing is re-rendered.
  • Same art kit. The 25 barrow models (stones, lintel, birches, cairns, the raven) carry over. About five new pieces: three mound faces with doors, kerb stones, flat fallen stones.
  • No simulation change. It's the first arena floor derived from the simulation's own geometry instead of a minimap trace.

Rule with one word each

barrow_v2 forks

Each fork has my recommendation first. "Accept all" takes every recommendation.

F1The floor's shape

Recommend A: the henge shown above. It's convex, contains all six spawn circles, and matches the simulation exactly.

B: a hub with five walled passages out to the doors, which is more enclosed and more barrow-like. But monsters chasing you from another passage would hit the walls where the simulation has none, which counts as a recorded mismatch. C: paint the barrow over the current traced floor, which leaves two spawn points outside the walls.

F2Obstacles inside the floor

Recommend A: none. The simulation has none.

B: two to four cairns that block only you, an arena-feel choice with a recorded mismatch. C: obstacles that block monsters too, which needs a new reference fight (REFERENT-v2).

F3The six green pools

Recommend A: drop them. Frozen tarns are walkable decoration, and the crater v5 ice reaction gets a stage.

B: keep six damaging pools re-themed as grave-blight. The footage never measured their damage, so a number would have to be decided.

F4The theme

Recommend A: the King's Henge, with barrow doors as spawn points and the King's Barrow north.

B: an open snowfield ringed only by standing stones, with no mounds. It's simpler and cheaper, but monsters have nowhere to come from.

F5How it renders

Recommend A: painted ground and depth-sorted pieces in the arena runtime first, with the live 3D ground layer added afterwards.

B: a full 3D version with the heroes and enemies as 3D models. It's the largest job and leaves the sprite packs behind. C: the painted plate only, never adding the 3D ground.

F6When to start

Recommend A: greybox now (no spend), painting after the enemy lanes finish waves 151–156.

B: paint now, in parallel, competing for the weekly image allowance. C: wait until the Barbarian is in the arena.

F7Which monsters fight there

Recommend A: the same roster as the arena reference, which is fixed by the simulation. The Glitch Archive frame lets realms bleed into each other.

B: a barrow-native roster of draugr and the Barrow King. That's a new reference fight (REFERENT-v2), designed and simulated from scratch.
