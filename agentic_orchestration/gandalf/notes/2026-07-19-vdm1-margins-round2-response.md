## 2. D-3 — "why would I avoid #6 + the sub-bar accruals?"

**Honest answer: under our actual frame, you wouldn't.**

**What the ≥3-kit gate is for.** R-7 runs two tracks. *Quantitative* mints (numeric range-extensions — #1/#4/#5) are free+ledgered regardless of kit-count: they widen an existing dial, they don't add a primitive. The ≥3-kit gate bites only on *qualitative* mints — genuinely new engine primitives — and it is a **build-authorization bar, not a record bar.** Two real reasons it exists:

1. **Queue-protection.** Every qualitative mint is a downstream obligation (spec + implement + balance + test). A primitive forced by one kit may serve exactly one kit. The gate keeps the builder queue free of idiosyncrasies. This is the D4-over-PoE discipline: D4 constrained primitive-count for maintainability; PoE went maximalist and pays for it forever in onboarding + balance surface.
2. **The mint-rate tripwire.** Prevents primitive-count explosion where every faintly-novel kit spawns a primitive.

**Why R-6 dissolves the fear.** Under R-6, **MAPPED = data; a mint is a docket entry — a recorded claim ("the genre wants primitive X, evidenced by kits Y") — NOT a build commitment.** Building happens later through normal seams + gates when a builder pulls from the docket. Recording #6 costs nothing *unless recording auto-authorizes a build* — and it doesn't. You only lose the signal by discarding it.

**Recommendation — (a) mint-all, with a three-tier evidence stamp on every qualitative mint.** The gate becomes a *sort-key on build-authorization*, not a *filter on the record*:

- **Tier A — attested (≥3 independent kits):** two-tier accumulator (~10 kits), six-kit qualifier (#3), graduated-3 (#2). **Build-authorized** — independently reinvented across games; the falsifiability the gate protects is satisfied.
- **Tier B — quantitative (free track):** #1/#4/#5 — sail regardless; numeric extensions, not primitives.
- **Tier C — provisional / watch (1–2 kits):** #6 vaal-blade-vortex, twister (~1), HoWA (~1–2), GD emitters (~1–2). **Recorded, evidence-stamped, NOT build-authorized.** They become VDM-2's pre-registered hypothesis set: does the next basin corroborate? Graduate on corroboration; drop on silence.

Captures the whole signal (Matt's completeness instinct), preserves R-7's discipline (builder pulls Tier A; tripwire holds), converts "discard #6" into "let VDM-2 falsify or graduate it." Genre precedent: GGG ships experimental uniques and watches whether ≥3 build archetypes crystallize before spending balance budget — watch-then-graduate, not discard.

## 5. The Orchestrator role question — decline the crown, take a scoped seat

Matt's ask: add "Orchestrator" or "Master Orchestrator (of Autonomous Runs)" to gandalf's role-swap profile, on the observation that gandalf's autonomous runs have outperformed knight-rider's. gandalf's counsel: **grant less than offered — on purpose.**

**Why VDM-1 actually worked.** It was a **design-stewardship-shaped run where orchestration WAS design-judgment** — ELICITOR (pre-run decision grill) → SPEC-AUTHOR (R-1…R-9 charter) → DRIFT-CRITIC (blind rider + review book). Every orchestration decision was a judgment call (attestation, parsimony, convention-holding). Native gandalf ground. Not generic task-sequencing.

**The run-type boundary.** Design/research/content runs deliver *judgment* — reward continuous judgment-adjustment, substrate-led adaptation, genre knowledge (gandalf). Engine-build runs deliver *working code behind gates* — reward seam discipline, gate enforcement, cross-specialist sequencing, and not-being-the-judge-of-your-own-output (knight-rider).

**Three objections to "Master Orchestrator":**
1. **Collapses the critique-pair.** gandalf is the generative-side half of a two-sided critique with jack-ryan; the pair is load-bearing because no single agent holds both design authority and its check. Master-of-Runs = orchestration + design + no independent design-critic seat. (Diablo analog: the lead designer cannot also greenlight the lead designer — the org shape that let D3's launch auction house ship.)
2. **Over-generalizes from one run-type.** "gandalf's design/research run beat KR's runs" licenses "route design-runs to the design steward," NOT "gandalf runs all runs." Accepting the stretch would be the exact scope-creep drift gandalf is chartered to catch.
3. **Conductor ≠ sole QA.** VDM-1 was trustworthy because of external checks gandalf did not control (legolas blind rider; Matt's review-book ratification). A master role with no mandated external gate removes the independent check on the conductor's judgment.

**Proposal — scoped role-tag `SHOWRUNNER` (journey-shaper lineage; neutral alt: `RUN-CONDUCTOR`):**
- **Thematic precision:** the project is a *serial content generation pipeline*; a showrunner holds a serial production's coherence (writers' room, season arc, continuity) without being head of engineering. Scope is in the name.
- **Scope boundary:** autonomous **DESIGN / RESEARCH / CONTENT** runs only (corpus crawls, mapping/adjudication, design-elicitation, content-generation-and-curation, style/register). **NOT engine-build orchestration** — stays knight-rider. Discriminator: *is the run's deliverable primarily judgment/content, or working code?* Judgment → SHOWRUNNER; code → KR; mixed → phases split by owner (gandalf conducts design phase, hands build phase to KR).
- **Non-negotiable condition:** every SHOWRUNNER run carries a **mandated external QA gate** the conductor does not control (blind rider / jack-ryan Gate-2 / Matt review book / independent agent pass). Conductor never sole judge of conductor's output.
- **Governance path:** Matt approves scope + name → gandalf drafts the role-tag into role definition + OP + reciprocal boundary note in KR's scope doc → **jack-ryan ratifies the framing** (independent check that gandalf hasn't over-scoped his own charter) → lands in canon.

**Diagnostic question back to Matt (sharpens the boundary, guards against misdiagnosis):** which KR runs underperformed — *design/research-shaped* or *engine-build-shaped*? If design-shaped: SHOWRUNNER is the clean fix. If build-shaped: moving runs to gandalf relocates the problem rather than fixing it, and KR's build-orchestration deserves its own diagnosis.
