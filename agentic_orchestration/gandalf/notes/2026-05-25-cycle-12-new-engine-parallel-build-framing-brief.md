### Q3 — Integration vs replacement of legacy ClassGenerator

**Background:** Legacy ClassGenerator.generate() exists and works (used in Cycle 11 § 8 testing). New BC-target subspace generator can either:
- **REPLACE** legacy — legacy ClassGenerator deprecated; new generator is the only path
- **AUGMENT** legacy — both exist; new generator is the production path; legacy preserved as fallback/test scaffold
- **COMPOSE** — new generator wraps legacy with substrate-binding layer added on top

**Decision options:**

| Option | Pattern | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — REPLACE** | Legacy generator deprecated; removed from active codepath | Cleanest architecture; no legacy maintenance | Loses fallback path; testing harness needs migration |
| **B — AUGMENT (PARALLEL)** | Both generators exist; new generator default; legacy kept as fallback | Safe migration; testing fallback preserved | Codebase has two paths; ongoing maintenance burden |
| **C — COMPOSE** | New generator calls legacy with substrate-binding layer added | Reuses legacy code; reduces rewrite scope | Tighter coupling; harder to evolve new architecture independently |

**Recommendation:** Option B (AUGMENT) for v1 safety. Legacy preserved as fallback during transition; new generator is production path. Post-v1.1 stability, consider deprecating legacy per Option A.
