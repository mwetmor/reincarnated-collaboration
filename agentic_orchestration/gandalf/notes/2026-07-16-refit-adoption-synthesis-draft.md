## 5. The entanglement (the load-bearing insight)

The adoption fork is two questions wearing one coat:

- **Q-grain (upstream):** are gear-grain mcd rows and class-grain LA rows legitimate atlas citizens at kit grain — admit, exclude, or re-grain (decompose LA classes to kits; roll mcd gear-skills up)?
