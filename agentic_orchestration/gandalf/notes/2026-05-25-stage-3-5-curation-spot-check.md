## 4. Single FLAG: `moctezuma_obsidian_blade_knife` cultural-sensitivity refinement

### 4.1 The flag

The tecpatl (sacrificial obsidian knife) is historically the **ritual implement used in Aztec human sacrifice**, specifically in heart-extraction ceremonies at the Templo Mayor. The current entry's player-facing framing is:

> Player-facing: "Tecpatl Ritual Blade of the Sun Rite"

And describes the weapon as:

> ritual sun-rite vocabulary + melee high-tempo single mechanics + Aztec ceremonial period

This framing — "Sun Rite" + "ritual" without further qualification — RISKS softening or aestheticizing the historical-religious gravitas of Aztec human-sacrifice practice. The tecpatl is not a generic "ritual blade"; it is the specific instrument of a specific religious-sacrificial ceremony with profound theological and cultural meaning in pre-Columbian Mexica thought (and continuing scholarly attention in Mesoamerican studies).

### 4.2 What this is NOT

- This is NOT a Tier 3 exclusion call. The tecpatl IS a canonical Aztec weapon-form historically attested in codices and archaeology, and pre-Columbian Mesoamerican religion is not an active living religious practice (it was substantially destroyed during the Spanish conquest; what survives is reconstructed historical-religious scholarship + indigenous-heritage cultural practices). Tier 3 ("living-religious / marginalized-culture") does NOT apply.
- This is NOT a request to remove the entry. The tecpatl's inclusion in v1 substrate is appropriate per the Moctezuma anchor's INT-melee-priest-king-archetype coverage.
- This is NOT a BLOCK on Wave 6 acceptance. The substrate-row is acceptable; the SUBSTRATE column values (canonical_name, cultural_lineage_canonical, register_canonical) are correct and Discipline #25 rep-audit PASSES.

### 4.3 What this IS — a Phase 5 cohesion-judge refinement note

When the cohesion-judge at Phase 5 generates the PLAYER-FACING form name + description for this substrate row, the LLM-templated player-facing copy needs gandalf-curation refinement to:

- Honor the tecpatl's religious-sacrificial gravitas (not soften it as generic "ritual blade")
- OR (alternatively) re-frame the form's mechanical-use as a non-sacrificial application of the tecpatl design (the obsidian-edged knife was also used for non-sacrificial cutting tasks; if the player-facing form is a "warrior's combat-blade derived from the tecpatl tradition," the framing can sidestep the sacrificial connotation honestly)
- Avoid the "Sun Rite" aestheticization that elides the religious complexity

### 4.4 Recommended path

**Defer to Phase 5 cohesion-judge calibration** with explicit gandalf-curation gate for this row. Specifically:

- Substrate row stays in v1_scope (`weapon_knowledge_entries.canonical_name = 'moctezuma_obsidian_blade_knife'`; PASS per Wave 6 acceptance criteria)
- When Phase 5 cohesion-judge generates player-facing form-name + description for any form drawing on this substrate row, gandalf-curates the player-facing copy specifically
- Alternative paths at Phase 5: (a) re-frame as combat-blade non-sacrificial application; (b) honor sacrificial gravitas with appropriate weight; (c) demote substrate row at Phase 5 if no clean player-facing framing emerges
- Per composition policy § 9.2 Stage 3.6 research-replacement: when v1.1+ Track M Aztec-codices crawl lands, the player-facing copy can be replaced with a scholarly-attested phrasing

### 4.5 Why not route as Wave 6 amendment

Routing this as a Wave 6 dispatch amendment request would be over-rotation:

- The substrate row IS acceptable; the issue is downstream at player-facing copy generation
- Phase 5 cohesion-judge calibration is gandalf's authoring queue territory (per ground-state § 5 active workstream); the calibration spec naturally incorporates the gate for this row
- Wave 6 acceptance criteria (per dispatch § 5.5) gate on substrate-row insertion + gandalf curation PASS + Discipline #25 PASS — all three SATISFIED for this entry at substrate-curation level
