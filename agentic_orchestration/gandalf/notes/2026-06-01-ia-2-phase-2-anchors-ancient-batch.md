**Design rationale (batch-level):** the ANCIENT cell gap analysis in IA-2.P1 audit § 2 surfaces three structural under-coverage patterns: (1) fire and water are mythologically thin at caster-class implements despite extensive STR/DEX-coded representation; (2) wind, lightning, shadow lack cross-cultural diversity beyond Vedic Vayavyastra / Norse Mjölnir / WoW-fantasy-shadow; (3) holy is well-backed by Vedic canon but underserved on Egyptian/Aztec/Buddhist staves. This batch fills those structural gaps with named-mythological-bearer anchors drawn from Vedic / Greek / Norse / Egyptian / Mesoamerican / Slavic / Japanese / Buddhist canon — explicitly diversifying cultural traditions and avoiding mono-source concentration.

**Novel-design flag application:** entries marked `novel_design_flag: true` are gandalf-authored compositional novelties grounded in cultural-canon substrate (deity attribution + implement-form precedent + register signal) but not single-source-named in extant mythological canon. Entries marked `novel_design_flag: false` are substrate-direct anchors where the name + bearer + form are mythologically attested (Mjölnir-pattern, Vajra-pattern, Trishula-pattern). Per LOCK D § 2.3: substrate-led novel is YOURS authority.
