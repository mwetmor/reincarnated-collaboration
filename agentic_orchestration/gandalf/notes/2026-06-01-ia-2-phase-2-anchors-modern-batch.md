**Design rationale (batch-level):** the WS2.P1 audit surfaces ONE dominant structural finding repeated across all 7 primaries: **modern-caster is a missing substrate axis, not differential coverage**. The 19 modern-scientific overlay entries locked at Q18 (`fusion`, `thermal`, `combustion`, `hydro`, `hydraulic`, `seismic`, `tectonic`, `sonic`, `shockwave`, `plasma`, `flash`, `ion`, `voltage`, `tesla`, `stellar`, `solar`, `photon`, `laser`, `prismatic`) plus modern-caster-adjacent vocabulary (`cryo`, `blackhole`, `singularity`, `darkmatter`) are EXPLICITLY authoring-from-blank-slate territory.

This batch operates on a fundamentally different authoring discipline than ANCIENT (mythological-canon-direct) and MEDIEVAL (historical-attestation + caster-rebalance + alchemy/witchcraft compositional). MODERN is **substrate-led NOVEL DESIGN throughout** — every entry is a fresh authoring grounded in:
1. **Modern-scientific overlay vocabulary** (Q18 lock; 19 entries; canonical primary-element assignment preserved)
2. **Structural-template inventory from `nick-aschenbach-dnd-data`** (~17 sci-fi-coded D&D entries: Antimatter Carbine, Plasma Foil, Fusion Blade, Laser Sword, Ion Cannon, EMP Grenade, Cryo Bomb, Singularity, Voidmaw, etc. — re-cast at the caster-attribute layer)
3. **Sci-fi RPG / cyberpunk / lab-tech naming registers** — drawing on the broader cultural-canonical sci-fi register without inventing entirely fictional bearers
4. **Form-as-caster-vessel discipline** — every entry is INT/WIS-coded by construction (staff / rod / focus / gauntlet / projector / channeler / lance — NOT carbine/rifle/grenade as standalone, even where the structural template is firearm-class). Where a firearm-form name is preserved (e.g., "Carbine-Caster"), the compound naming flags the form-shift from DEX-firearm to INT/WIS-caster

**Novel-design flag application:** per LOCK D § 2.3, ALL MODERN entries are flagged `novel_design_flag: true` because no canonical single-source-named bearer exists for modern-scientific caster implements in the way Mjölnir / Solomonic Athame / Roland's Durendal do for ANCIENT / MEDIEVAL. The substrate-led grounding is in **vocabulary** (Q18 lock + nick-aschenbach-templates) and **form** (caster-vessel adaptation of sci-fi-coded weapon templates), NOT in named-bearer attribution. This is the WS2.P1 § 4.2 confirmation made operational: "the 19 designer-curation-modern-scientific-overlay entries (per WS1A.Q18 § 7 lineage table) are the audit's primary MODERN target. These entries were added at the WS1A.Q18 designer-curation gate **precisely because substrate was absent**."

**Special calibration note — MODERN.lightning is 9 anchors:** per WS2.P1 INFO-1, MODERN.lightning gets the largest per-cell allocation because the Q18 lock places the densest modern-scientific overlay vocabulary at lightning (`plasma`, `flash`, `ion`, `voltage`, `tesla` = 5 entries — the highest single-primary count in the 19-entry overlay). Coverage of all 5 overlay tokens + 4 umbrella adjacencies (railgun-coilgun / EMP / static-discharge / arc) requires the 9-anchor allocation. This is the audit's evidence-grounded calibration, not arbitrary inflation.
