## DISCREPANCY DETAILS

### 1 — Attribute and derived-stat deltas: +0.3% to +3.9%, one axis −0.5%

**Not a MISMATCH.** Two sufficient causes, both structural:

**(a) MI affix magnitudes roll within ranges.** Four of thirteen slots are monster infrequents with prefix + suffix: `Devastating Solael-Sect Legguards of the Eagle`, `Imposing Kaisan's Eye of Alacrity`, `Aggressive Gargabol's Ring of Wrath`, `Imposing Mark of Harvoul of Ruin`. Name-identity does not imply value-identity. Matt's Legguards alone carry `+57 Physique / +55 Cunning / +95 Spirit` — a roll band wide enough to absorb the entire observed attribute delta by itself. **No construction path short of copying exact roll values can eliminate this**, and GD Stash construction from a name list will not.

**(b) `b28gD0KN` leaves 2 attribute points unspent; Matt's character has 0 available.** Accounts for part of the Physique/Cunning delta. Note this is *consistent with* § 1.7's "left 10 points in the tank" advice being a leveling-time heuristic that the final tested spec had largely drawn down.

**Direction of the deltas is worth recording explicitly.** Matt's fixture is fractionally *stronger* than the spec on Health, Energy, all three attributes, OA, Armor and Physical Resist, and fractionally weaker on DA alone (−14, −0.5%). For a difficulty-calibration fixture this is the benign direction: an observed wave-150 result is not inflated by an under-built character. Any calibration claim should carry the sentence *"the fixture measured 0.5–3.9% above spec on all axes but Defensive Ability."*

**Recommended language going forward.** Retire "matches the grimtools build 100%." Use **"name-identical across all 13 slots; derived stats within +3.9% / −0.5% of the published spec, explained by MI affix-roll variance."** That is both stronger and more defensible than the round number, because it is measured.

### 2 — grimtools raw-item values vs in-game character-scaled values

A reader comparing tooltips side by side will see numbers that look like mismatches and are not. Examples from the Gutsmasher tooltip:

| Line | grimtools `#353` | in-game `#495` |
|---|---|---|
| EoR modifier, bleeding | `990 Bleeding Damage over 3 Seconds` | `21117 Bleeding Damage over 3 Seconds` |
| Gutsplosion, physical | `255 Physical Damage` | `300 Physical Damage` |
| Gutsplosion, recharge | `0.5 Second Skill Recharge` | `0.6 Second Skill Recharge` |

grimtools' item view renders **base item values**; the in-game tooltip renders values already scaled by the character's `+396% Physical` / `+293% Bleeding` and cooldown modifiers. **These are the same item.** Flagging so this does not resurface later as a false discrepancy.

### 3 — Per-skill rank grid is recoverable without a new capture

Frames `523`, `524`, `526`, `531`, `535`, `538`, `544`, `550` have the smallest tooltip footprint, and tooltip placement shifts between frames such that **the union of two or three frames per mastery clears the whole icon field.** A future pass wanting the complete rank table can composite from those frames alone. **No re-capture is owed.** Recording this so nobody asks Matt to re-walk the trees.

### 4 — Client patch level not directly observed

grimtools reports `Game version: 1.3.0.0`. The engine tracker pins Matt's client at `1.3.0.5`. No screenshot in either set shows the client version string. If any part of the delta in § 1 is later attributed to a 1.3.0.0→1.3.0.5 item change, that attribution is **inference, not measurement** — the substrate does not carry the client version. A single main-menu screenshot would close this permanently and costs nothing on the next launch.
