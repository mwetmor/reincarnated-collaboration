## 3. Why the composite ROSE — the stronger-than-expected result

This is not "the recipe survived real content." It is "real content *improved* the score on exactly the axes it supplies." The reading splits cleanly along the premium-perception decomposition (~40% lighting + 30% VFX + 20% material + 10% geometry):

- **Lighting + VFX held at 5 — the controllable layer is content-agnostic.** The recipe owns these axes (the rig + the GPUParticles3D hero event), not the geometry. They scored 5 on graybox and 5 on the cathedral because the recipe is the same. This is the A-holds bet confirmed a second time: *the fidelity budget lives in lighting + VFX, and that investment is portable across content.*
- **Material + geometry rose 4→5 — real curated content beats a graybox on its own axes.** A graybox dungeon is geometrically and materially thin by construction; Synty's shipping cathedral (arches, altar, layered stone, ritual dais) is not. The two axes the graybox scored 4 on are precisely the two a real curated environment supplies — so the score rose exactly where you'd predict. The graybox was the *floor* of A-holds, not its ceiling.

The genre truth holds: Diablo II on sprites, Hades on stylized 2D, Torchlight Infinite (cheapest geometry, most premium read). Cheap geometry + a strong lift = premium — and *real* cheap-but-curated geometry is strictly better than a graybox, at no recipe cost.

## 5. The marketing-render caveat — SATISFIED (the discipline that protected the build worked)

The routing note § 3 made this load-bearing: the Synty reference frames are Synty's **own Unity marketing renders**, the calibration/mood anchor, NOT the pass bar. Both seams held the line:

- **drax did NOT pixel-match.** The marketing render's glowing pentagram is Unity post-processing on a flat-material ritual circle. drax drove his own red emissive sigil via the proven lift hero-glow lever — register-correct VFX from the validated GPUParticles3D path, not a fidelity-chase of an unattainable post.
- **galadriel scored vs the RUBRIC, lifecycle-sampled.** She used the Synty render for mood/composition calibration only — never as a pass bar or pixel-match target — and sampled across the 100-frame lifecycle (not still-vs-still), so VFX (the highest-leverage axis) was not under-represented. Our pentagram reading differently from Synty's glowing-sigil post is **by design** per the caveat; she scored VFX on its own register merits.

The trap the caveat guarded against (chase the marketing post → burn budget OR feel falsely short) was avoided. The score is a clean rubric-pass on our own build, exactly as A-holds demanded.
