## 1. What the artifact actually is (re-characterized)

`observed_kpm = kills / (duration_s / 60)`. A greedy-capstone caster one-shots a 3–4 mob blue/elite pack in 0.4 s (4 ticks). `4 / (0.4/60) = 600`. The 600 is not a throughput — it is `1 / (tick-floor)`. **KPM is undefined for a sub-second clear**: there is no "per minute" in a fight that lasts 0.4 s and kills 4 mobs; the sample is too small in BOTH dimensions (duration and kill-count) to express a rate. The metric has fallen off its domain. You cannot fit a band over a number that is measuring the discretization floor instead of the fight.

This is the same thing my three-fork §1 already said ("you cannot fit a band over saturated junk") — I simply assumed the junk could be *moved into* the measurable range by HP. gamora's sweep proves it cannot, because the cohort is bimodal: the `H` that moves the tail breaks the median.

**A geared caster vaporizing a blue trash pack in under a second is THE ARPG caster power-fantasy, not a defect.** Diablo III Tal Rasha / Archon wizard deleting a pack with one capstone; PoE clear-speed casters (Arc, Spark, Ball Lightning) one-shotting white/blue packs off-screen; D4 sorc clearing density on contact. Slow trash is not caster identity; it is the betrayal of it.

Therefore **candidate resolution #1 (per-shell HP inflation) is REJECTED — on genre grounds, independent of gamora's empirical proof that it also fails P1∧P2∧P3.** Inflating magic_pack / elite_pack HP to slow the caster clear turns trash into sponges. We do not do it. (Note the data already honors this: magic_pack is uniformly saturated [blue trash *should* evaporate], while elite_pack is mostly measurable [median 367 — elites are the real density beat, and most casters take a measurable moment on them]. The instrument is telling the genre-true story; only the sub-second tail reads as garbage.)

**Pacing-governance — where it actually lives.** My three-fork §3a said "let the clear-room ceiling do the pacing-governance," and that is right *for in-domain cells*. But the thing a ceiling should catch is "this kit trivializes the SEASON's density" — a season-level pacing concern — NOT "this kit deleted one blue pack fast." The per-pack KPM ceiling was never the right tool for caster-burst pacing. **Flag that as a separate, future pacing-governance question; do not jam it into the per-pack band, and do not build it in this run.**

It is NOT gated on a KPM number, and it is **EXCLUDED from the band FIT** so the band is never fit over 600s. (This is candidate #2 — the measurement floor — RESHAPED from "clamp to a manufactured number" to "guard as out-of-domain, gate on completion." 4. **Do NOT inflate clear-shell mob HP** (§2 — genre defect). The boss shells stay untouched (banked).

**Why a guard and not a clamp:** a clamp manufactures a fake throughput to feed the band; a guard admits the metric does not apply to this cell and routes it to the gate that does (completion).
