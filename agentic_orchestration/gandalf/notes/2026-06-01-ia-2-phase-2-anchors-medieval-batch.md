**Design rationale (batch-level):** the MEDIEVAL cell-gap analysis in IA-2.P1 audit § 3 surfaces four structural under-coverage patterns: (1) fire / water / wind are functionally absent of medieval-mage-class implements (witch / alchemist / Crusader-era enchantment registers are entirely unrepresented); (2) lightning is thin — Mjölnir/Gungnir are STR-coded melee, and the substrate has zero medieval-caster lightning anchors; (3) holy is tome-heavy (Sefer, Picatrix, Solomonic) but lacks reliquary-scepter-relic register; (4) **MEDIEVAL.shadow is the single worst cell in the entire 21-cell grid** with only 1 row (Charlemagne's relic-talisman, which is itself ambiguously shadow-coded).

This batch fills those gaps along three intentional axes:
- **Carolingian / Arthurian named-weapons reframed for caster** (Joyeuse, Durendal, Hauteclère, Curtana) — substrate already has these as STR-coded melee swords (per § 3.3 / § 3.5 / § 3.6); this batch authors their caster-vessel / reliquary-class variants per the Poseidon-Trident-Focus / Hades-Bident-Focus pattern from the ANCIENT batch
- **Witch / alchemist / grimoire tradition** as the medieval-caster register the substrate explicitly lacks (audit § 3.1 / § 3.2 / § 3.4 / § 3.5 / § 3.7 all flag this gap)
- **CRITICAL MEDIEVAL.shadow filled via 6 anchors across diverse traditions**: Solomonic grimoire (Athame), Hermetic-Arabic (Picatrix), Hebrew-magical (Sefer HaRazim necromancer-coding), Norse seiðr (Völva staff), plague-doctor Black-Death iconography, Inquisition-era paradox-shadow (Iron Maiden as reliquary). This is the audit's flagged largest-single-cell-gap; the 6-anchor allocation prioritizes cross-tradition breadth + dark-grimoire / death-trade / heretical-mystic vocabulary

**Novel-design flag application:** entries marked `novel_design_flag: true` are gandalf-authored compositional novelties grounded in cultural-canon substrate (named bearer + implement-form precedent + register signal) but not single-source-named in extant historical/legendary canon. Entries marked `novel_design_flag: false` are substrate-direct anchors where the name + bearer + form are historically/legendarily attested (Joyeuse / Durendal / Hauteclère / Curtana / Oriflamme / etc.). Per LOCK D § 2.3: substrate-led novel is YOURS authority.
