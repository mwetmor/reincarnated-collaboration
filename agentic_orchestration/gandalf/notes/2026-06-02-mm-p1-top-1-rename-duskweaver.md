## 1. Why rename was required

Wave B identity LLM produced "Penumbra Caster of Dusk Meridian." Strong as a draft but failed at the kit-identity layer for one specific reason:

- **Generic archetype templating.** "Caster" is a template archetype label — Caster / Mage / Cleric / Fighter — that flattens character identity into class taxonomy. Character names should read as characters, not as class+setting compositions.

Matt's selected rename retains the thematic register ("Dusk" + "Meridian") that worked in the draft while eliminating the template-archetype label and elevating the cosmic-event resonance ("Eclipsed" upgrades "Dusk Meridian" from a time-of-day reference to a celestial-event station).

## 2. Rename: "Duskweaver of the Eclipsed Meridian"

### 2.1 Archetype-as-identity — "Duskweaver"

- **Novel archetype construction.** "Duskweaver" is not a class label drawn from a template list — it's an archetype constructed from action (weaving) + medium (dusk). This is the canonical move for distinctive character naming in classical-fantasy and isekai traditions (compare: Stormcaller, Doomsayer, Painwright).
- **Flavor-vocab integration, not flavor-vocab labeling.** "Dusk" is Q18 shadow-flavor-pool vocabulary, but it appears here INSIDE a novel archetype construction rather than as a thematic adjective tacked onto a template label ("Dusk Caster"). The integration changes what the word does: it's a verb-object kernel, not an aesthetic descriptor.
- **Phonetic register.** Two-syllable trochee carries weight; the K-onset of "weaver" gives bite that anchors the soft-D of "Dusk." Reads as a name a character would carry.

### 2.2 Epithet — "of the Eclipsed Meridian"

- **"Eclipsed Meridian"** upgrades the original "Dusk Meridian" decisively. "Dusk" was a time-of-day reference; "Eclipsed" is a celestial-event station. The character is named from a moment of occlusion — composes directly with the chernoff-celestial-body emergence story (a form materializing from a constellation-pattern at a specific celestial moment).
- **"Meridian"** retains the cardinal-line + cosmic-axis resonance that made the original draft work. A meridian is a great circle through the celestial poles — exactly the substrate for the chernoff-emergence narrative.
- **"of the X"** suffix convention. Strong character-naming structure across Diablo (Mephisto, Lord of Hatred), Dark Souls (Gwyn, Lord of Sunlight), and modern isekai. Reads as named character with cosmic locus.

### 2.3 Composition with MM-P1 chernoff-celestial-body video

The 8-second video shows Stage A constellation emergence → Stage B materialization. The narrative beat:

> *Stars resolve into the outline of a humanoid form along the celestial axis. The constellation gains depth at the moment of eclipse. A figure materializes in tattered period clothing — the one who weaves dusk, whose station is the meridian of occluded light.*

"Duskweaver of the Eclipsed Meridian" carries that beat cleanly. The name DOES the storytelling — Matt's pick is doing more work than mine would have.
