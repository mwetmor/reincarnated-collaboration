## §0 — THE CROSS-SPEC RULING, FIRST, BECAUSE IT DECIDES ALL THREE

**R-X-1 (veto-open) — M-1 and M-2 are ONE mechanism for gate purposes, and neither passes G-A alone.**

The charter files mitigation and the nova as separate mechanisms with separate builds. Arithmetically they
are not separable, and a build that lands each one *correctly and independently* can still leave G-A reading
1.000. The proof is in §1.2 and §1.3 and it is short:

- Grim Dawn's armour law (EXT §2.1) is `taken = dmg − 0.70 × min(dmg, A)`. For every hit **at or below A**
  this is a flat −70 % — **scale-invariant, exactly like RDR's own `armor/(armor+3000)`.** Adding armour to
  a hit already below A changes *nothing*.
- The sim's entire pre-wave hazard is sub-armour chip (worst drop 45.9–59.2 HP absolute; verdict §A-4.3).
  Against A = 342, **every one of those hits is in the saturated regime.** M-1 alone therefore moves the
  absolute worst drop by **0.0 HP** and G-A stays at 1.000.
- The shape mechanism is the **STRADDLE** — hits that *exceed* armour, where the law stops being a
  percentage and becomes a constant subtraction whose fractional bite falls as the hit grows. **Only M-2
  produces hits in that regime.** M-2 alone (no mitigation itemization) changes the numerator and the
  denominator of the G-A statistic in lockstep and also leaves it near 1.000.

**Build consequence for gamora:** land M-1 and M-2 in either order, but **do not grade G-A on either
landing individually**, and do not read a 1.000 after the first landing as a build failure. G-A is graded
once, on the composed pair, on the post-wave battery.

**R-X-2 (veto-open, and the one I most want the conductor to see) — G-A has no numeric predicate yet.**
Charter §2 says G-A reads *"2.12-class (fixture), not 1.000 (pure scale)."* **"2.12-class" is a gesture, not
a threshold.** The kit-spec amendment that landed hours ago (`…g4-kit-spec-v2.md` §10.3) made it spec law
that *a threshold authored after the measurement is not a threshold*. G-A currently has no band, no tier
scope, and no cut rule. **It must be pinned before the post-wave battery executes, or G-A is un-gradeable
and the run's headline gate resolves by argument.** This spec supplies the statistic's exact identity
(§1.1) and the tier scoping it requires (§1.4) so the pin is cheap; **it does not author the band — that is
the conductor's pin, not this seam's, and authoring it here after seeing §1's arithmetic would be exactly
the failure §10.3 forbids.**

# M-1 — MITIGATION ITEMIZATION (`mitigation_delta`)

## §1 — MATH NOTE

### 1.1 — What G-A actually measures (an identity, not a model)

G-A's statistic is *normalized fall ÷ EHP ratio*. Write `W_pre`, `W_post` for the p99 worst incoming drop in
**absolute HP** and `P_pre`, `P_post` for the pool:

```
fall      = (W_pre / P_pre) ÷ (W_post / P_post)  =  (W_pre / W_post) × (P_post / P_pre)
EHP_ratio = P_post / P_pre

fall ÷ EHP_ratio  ≡  W_pre / W_post
```

**The G-A statistic is identically the ratio of ABSOLUTE worst drops across the gear step.** Nothing else.

Check against both sides of the measured record (verdict §A-4.3), no recomputation of either:
- Fixture: `0.3302 / (0.0738 × 2.11) = 2.1205` — reproduces the banked **2.12**.
- Sim: `0.07583 / (0.03581 × 2.1173) = 1.0002` — reproduces the banked **1.000**, and explains why the
  verdict also found `worst_drop_abs` **byte-identical on 143/150 matched fights.** Those are the same fact.

**⇒ G-A ⟺ "the R2→R3 gear step must cut the absolute worst incoming hit by ~2.12×."** That is the sentence
gamora should build against. It is a single measurable on a single hit, not a distribution-shape puzzle.

### 1.2 — The GD law, and why RDR's current law can never move this statistic

**Source (EXT §2.1, `records/game/combatformulas.dbr` + `records/game/gameengine.dbr`):**

```
physcialDamageDefenseEquationDLEP = physicalDamageDV * (1 - sumAbsorptionDV)
physicalDamageDefenseEquationDGP  = (sumProtectionDV * (1 - sumAbsorptionDV)) + (physicalDamageDV - sumProtectionDV)
armorDefensiveAbsorption          = 70.0
```
(the `physcial` typo is in the source). Both branches collapse to one closed form:

```
taken_physical(d, A) = d − 0.70 × min(d, A)          [deterministic; NO roll]
```

Regime behaviour, which is the whole design content of the operator:

| regime | closed form | fractional bite | scale-invariant? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `d ≤ A` (sub-armour) | `0.30 d` | **70 %, constant** | **YES** |
| `d > A` (straddle / super-armour) | `d − 0.70A` | `0.70A/d` → falls as `d` grows | **NO** |

RDR's own law is `foundation/math_model.py:131` `compute_physical_damage` →
`reduction = armor/(armor+3000)`, a magnitude-independent fraction. **A magnitude-independent fraction
applied to every hit cannot change the normalized shape of the drop distribution — it multiplies numerator
and denominator by the same constant.** The sim's 1.000 is therefore not a tuning miss and not a data gap;
it is a **structural impossibility of the operator in place.** No armour value, no `K`, no gear step
expressed through that function can ever move G-A off 1.000.

That single sentence is, in my reading, the most load-bearing thing this spec says, and it should travel
verbatim into the build's math note and into Gate-2's finding.

### 1.3 — The payload split, which outranks the armour question

**Armour in GD is physical-ONLY** (EXT §2.2/§2.4: `combatformulas.dbr` defines exactly two damage-defense
equations, both `physical*`; **no cold equation exists**). Every other type routes through a flat
`defensive<Type>` percentage, capped `playerDefenseCap = 80`.

The death-2 nova (`records/skills/nonplayerskills/bossskills/primordian_frigidring.dbr`, rank 4) is
**200 cold / 118 physical per projectile = 62.9 % cold** (EXT §1.1).

⇒ **An armour-only mitigation step can touch at most 37.1 % of the fixture's worst hit.** Even *zeroing the
entire physical leg* yields `W_pre/W_post = 1/(1−0.371) = 1.59` — **below 2.12.** This is not a tuning
distance; it is a ceiling.

> **R-M1-1 (veto-open) — `mitigation_delta` is a THREE-CHANNEL object or G-A is structurally unreachable.**
> The gear step must itemize (a) **armour** on the physical leg, (b) **per-element resistance** on every
> elemental leg, (c) **conversion / channel-shift** (the fixture's own itemization, kit spec v2 §1.7, is
> literally *≥125 Armor + the amatok aura's +16 + an 18 % Physical→Acid conversion* — two of its three
> members are not armour). A build that ships (a) alone satisfies the mechanism's name and fails its gate.

The sim already has the home for (b): the typed-resistance resolver spine
(`spatial_engine.py:3909-3918`, `elemental_resistances` on `CombatantState` via
`spatial_resolver_adapter.py:239-240`; math note `typed-resistance-resolver-spine-and-calibration-2026-06-21.md`).
M-1 does not need a new mitigation channel — it needs the **gear step to move the existing ones.**

### 5.7 — Spatial resolution: how projectile-count-vs-range resolves in the sim

The 140 %-band geometry is **spatial**, and the sim's tick is 0.1 s → the ring front advances **1.4 m/tick**
against a 1.5 m explosion radius. Tick-grain evaluation can skip a target.

> **R-M2-4 (veto-open) — ANALYTIC RING-CROSSING, deterministic-fractional, sub-tick.**
>
> 1. **Model:** do not simulate 16 projectile entities. At launch, mint a ring event at
>    `(origin_x, origin_y, t_launch)`. The ring's radius is `ρ(t) = 14.0 × (t − t_launch)`, alive until
>    `ρ = 12.0` (t = 0.857 s).
> 2. **Crossing:** the payload lands at the **sub-tick time where `ρ(t)` first equals the target's
>    distance**, solved by linear interpolation of the target's motion across the tick (the target moves;
>    this is why tick-grain is insufficient). The crossing radius `r*` sets both the band and `n(r*)`.
> 3. **Payload:** `phys = n(r*) × 118 × s(r*)`, `cold = n(r*) × 200 × s(r*)`, with `n` **fractional and
>    deterministic** — no rounding, no RNG.
> 4. **One target, one crossing.** Re-entry into the footprint does not re-trigger.
>
> **Why deterministic-fractional and not a stochastic integer count:** an integer draw is a new RNG
> consumption (R-M2-3's hazard, again) and the fractional value is already the honest *expectation* over an
> unmodelled azimuth. **Named bias, and it is gate-adverse:** the real ring has variance (a player can eat 1
> or 4 depending on where they stand between spokes); the sim's burst distribution will be **too narrow**
> and will **understate tail danger.** Recorded, not smoothed. If G-B fails narrowly, this bias is the first
> place to look — and the fix is a variance model, not a magnitude increase.
>
> **Verification oracle (required, math-before-code's falsifier):** the analytic `n(r)` is checked against a
> one-off **discrete 16-ray computation** (place 16 rays at 22.5°, blast radius 1.5, count coverage of a
> point target) at `r ∈ {1, 2, 2.5, 5, 9, 12}`. Agreement tolerance **≤ 5 %** or the operator is wrong and
> §5.2's whole table with it. This is the check that turns EXT §1.3's D-grade into something buildable.

**Fire condition, composed:** the cast fires when the player is within **10.0 m at cast time**; the band
applies at the player's distance **at crossing time**. Those are different instants separated by
`wind_up_s + r/14`. That separation *is* M-3's window and must not be collapsed.

## §9 — MATH NOTE / DESIGN SURFACE

### 9.1 — The respec, and its source warrant

**Grim Dawn has no scaling evasion stat** (EXT §3.1). An exhaustive field-name sweep of all four archives for
`dodge|evade|evasion|deflect|miss|fumble|block` returns exactly two player-facing avoidance stats —
`characterDodgePercent` (2–10 % on 105 item records) and `characterDeflectProjectile` (3–15 % on 184) — and
**no equipped item in the fixture's save carries either.** The fixture had **zero** statistical avoidance.

> **⇒ The Primordian WIN was POSITIONAL. Nothing in GD's stat model could have produced it.** Specifying
> M-3 as a dodge *chance* would model a mechanic the fixture did not use and the game barely has. M-3 is a
> **policy over space and time.**

Two source-anchored surfaces exist if a statistical layer is ever wanted, and both are named so nobody
reaches for a chance roll by default: OA-vs-DA PTH (`probabilityToHitEquation`, `pthMinimum = 55`, and note
it is *also* a damage multiplier to **×1.5** across six thresholds — a DA deficit gets hit more often **and
harder**), and the AI reposition block on `controller_boss_viloth.dbr` (`DodgeChance 50`, `DodgeDelay 3000 ms`,
`DodgeDistance 2.0`, `MinDodgeDistance 4.0`) — which is a **2 m lateral AI sidestep, not a damage-avoidance
roll**, present on 407 controller records corpus-wide. That last one is the *correct source-anchored shape*
for a telegraph-reactive movement policy, and M-3 should be recognizably its cousin.

### 9.2 — THE DESIGN CONTENT: evade toward minimum exposure, not maximum distance

§5.2's profile makes the naive policy actively harmful. From `r = 5 m` (payload 804), a "back away from the
telegraph" policy with a 1.0 s window and ~4 m/s movement reaches `r = 9 m` — payload **823**. **The flinch
costs 2 % more damage.** From `r = 3 m` the flinch is worse still.

> **R-M3-1 (veto-open) — the policy scores CANDIDATE POINTS BY PROJECTED PAYLOAD, using M-2's own payload
> function. It never scores by distance.** One source, two consumers — the §7.1 no-drift invariant applied
> to the policy layer, not just the render layer. The policy calls the same `nova_payload(r)` the resolver
> will call. **A second copy of the exposure math inside the policy is the defect this ruling exists to
> prevent**, and it would be invisible until the day the two disagreed.

The correct policy behaviour that falls out, unforced:
- **Escape** past 12.0 m when `movement_speed × t_remaining ≥ 12.0 − r` — the only unconditional safety.
- Otherwise **close to the 2.5⁻ m shelf** (payload 647) or **hold just inside 9.0⁻ m** (payload 588),
  whichever the movement budget reaches.
- **Never stop inside `[9.0, 12.0]`** and never inside `r < 2.0`.

That behaviour — *the danger is readable, the right answer is counter-intuitive, and mastery is the
difference* — is the telegraph contract of §14.24's design-DNA note (*"dangerous = visible = dodgeable…
winnable through skill, not stat-checking"*) arriving as arithmetic rather than aspiration.

### 9.4 — Policy specification

**Seam:** the existing policy layer — `spatial_gauntlet/policy/{seam,considerations,exposure_map}.py`
(BW-1, 2026-07-22). `MovementIntent` currently enumerates `{ADVANCE, HOLD}`; M-3 adds **`EVADE`**.

**Inputs (all already readable; no new engine state):**

| input | source |
|---|---|
| live `TelegraphSpec` | M-2's mint (origin, `fire_time_s`, `wind_up_s`, `radius_m = 12.0`) |
| `t_remaining` | `fire_time_s − t_now` |
| player position, `movement_speed` | `SpatialEntity` |
| `nova_payload(r)` | **M-2's function, called — not copied** (R-M3-1) |
| `reaction_latency_s` | declared parameter (§9.6) |

**Decision (pure, deterministic, no RNG — the seam's existing law: `NO RNG is consumed here`):**

```
t_eff   = t_remaining − reaction_latency_s
if t_eff <= 0:                      return HOLD          # too late to read it
budget  = movement_speed × t_eff
C       = candidate points on the ray through (origin → player) and its two lateral
          offsets, sampled at reachable radii within `budget`, arena-clamped
target  = argmin over C of  nova_payload(||c − origin||)
if payload(target) >= payload(current):   return HOLD     # never move to a worse cell
return EVADE(target)
```

**Constraints:**
- **Movement is capped at `movement_speed × dt` per tick and arena-clamped.** No teleport. The §5 ruling of
  the temporal-decoupling doc — *"Teleport: **the trap.** It pulls you to range, dissolving the very
  coordinate"* — applies verbatim to a policy that jumps.
- **Evade costs uptime.** A tick spent evading is a tick not attacking. The policy must not return `EVADE`
  and an attack in the same tick. Without this, evasion is free and R-M3-2's hazard arrives by the back door.
- **A frozen player cannot evade** (§5.4). Freeze is the counter to the policy, and that coupling is the
  fight's actual structure — the CC answers the dodge, exactly as it does in Monster Hunter and Souls.
- **`decision` events are emitted** per evade, so Godot draws the movement intent (R-KC1-19a: *sim thinks,
  Godot renders*; the `replica-frame/v1` schema already streams `(tick, chosen_target_id, intent)` plus
  per-frame `heading_rad` — the channel exists and needs no schema change).

## §S — THE OWED KIT-SPEC AMENDMENTS (status, not duplication)

The cell brief owed the **S-1 A-floor predicate defect** amendment plus stubs for the other two. **All three
landed while this cell was in flight** — sibling cell **WR1-SPEC-AMEND**, charter §8.5, commit `587a17a1`,

re-author (a second copy of a ruled predicate is a drift vector, not a deliverable).

**S-1 — the corrected predicate (§10.2), stated as owed.** The defect: S-1's control leg read *"Single-target
arm returns A ∈ [0.98, 1.05]"* — **a band that assumed A's floor is 1.0.** It is not: claws compile at
`cooldown_seconds = 0.0` and the sim has no player attack-speed model, so sequential single-target kills land
inside one 0.5 s `kill_event_bin_s` and **A's floor is 2.667 / 1.333 / 1.200 / 1.000 by tier** — a property
of the pinned kit and the binning grain, not of the geometry S-1 exists to test. The repaired predicate has
**three required legs plus an ordering check**:

1. **Leg 1 — geometry neutralization** (promoted diagnostic → co-equal predicate): control arm returns mean
   `len(targets_hit)` per player skill press **= 1.000, max = 1.0** over every fight. Sim-side instrument only.
2. **Leg 2 — the coincidence-floor clause:** the control leg is graded against **the control's own measured
   A**, not against 1.0, whenever `cooldown_seconds = 0.0` **or** no attack-speed model is compiled. The
   floor must be **verified degenerate** (mean = min = max across seeds) or S-1 is **NOT-EVALUABLE**.
3. **Leg 3 — the A-lift (the graded leg):** `A_lift = A_canonical ÷ A_control`, **per tier, at matched
   seeds**; passes iff **`A_lift > 1.25`** at the G-5a pack sizes. *The 1.25 is transplanted verbatim from
   the pre-registered predicate — moved to the right denominator, not re-picked.*
4. **Leg 3b — negative-control ordering:** at a spread tier where the arc has nothing to catch, `A_lift` must
   read **strictly below the smallest pack-tier lift**. One-sided, no magnitude band. Violation ⇒
   **NOT-REPRODUCED**, not a tuning note.
   *(Plus a confound clause: arms shown outcome-comparable at the lift tiers before any lift is quoted.)*
