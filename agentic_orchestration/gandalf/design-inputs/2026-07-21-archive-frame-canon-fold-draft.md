## §A — The one-line, and the load-bearing recognition

**The frame in one line (Matt's, preserved):** *RDR is a descent through the shelved, chronological history of ARPG build-craft — you are an archival process reading (inhabiting) one record at a time, proving your skill against each era's champions to earn passage deeper, until you reach the frontier at the bottom: builds that don't exist yet, derived from the atlas's empty cells.*

**The recognition that makes this worth a fold (Matt's §0, sharpened):** across this project's history the fiction kept *generating* design knots while the systems kept *being* the answer — the story fought the mechanics. This is **the first narrative frame derived FROM the mechanics rather than imposed ON them.** The atlas *becomes* the Archive; the chronological run-spine *becomes* the descent through history's shelves; the reincarnation loop *becomes* the reading-head; element-as-light *becomes* the reader's signature; the ghost field *becomes* the frontier finale. When the same sentence describes both the fiction and the mechanic, the design has cohered.

I concur with that recognition **as a design instinct** — it is the correct reason to take the frame seriously, and it is rare. But a fold-steward's job is to test whether "derived from the mechanics" survives contact with the mechanics *as they are already locked*, not as the spine imagines them. **The §C coherence audit is where that test lives, and it is the reason this is a DRAFT and not a ratification.** Three of the frame's cleanest claims sit in tension with Matt-ruled canon (the death-faith creed valence, the manufactured-rebellion eruption, the demigod-jailer selection arc). None is fatal; all need a ruling. I flag them plainly rather than smoothing them — smoothing a seam is how drift enters.

## §B — The frame, folded (what the Archive Frame *asserts*)

*This section restates the spine in canonical register. It asserts nothing Matt hasn't proposed; it is the object the audit operates on.*

### B.1 — The Archive as place [FRAME — proposed]
A place holds the soul of every build that ever mattered. **Time runs through it as depth** — history is shelved chronologically, so *descending = moving forward through the genre's years* (2000→2025). The player is an archival process moving *down* through the strata, proving skill against each era's champions to earn passage deeper.

The device is a **collector / archive / afterlife** — a frame whose whole premise is *gathering things that don't belong together.* This is the load-bearing structural property: **an archive doesn't cohere, it collects.** Fifteen unrelated game-worlds, each champion still living its own story, do not need to belong together — they are *shelved, not unified.* A preserved champion still living its old story is *what a stored record is*; you interrupt it by arriving. (This dissolves the oldest incoherence complaint — that the roster is a bag of unrelated worlds — by making non-cohesion the *premise* rather than a bug.)

### B.2 — The reframe + the inhabitation grammar [FRAME — proposed]
**"Keep what you kill" → "try your skill against the archive."** You still reincarnate into each champion you best — but it is now a **digital archival process, not a theft.** Your prior inhabited form **de-resolves into particulate** (a beam of light-data returning to the archive) as you re-resolve into the next historical champion. The visual grammar: *one record archived, next record loaded.*

The distinction the spine calls load-bearing: **you are not stealing a body — you are the reading head, and the build skeleton is the record the archive stores, while you (soul, element) are what animates it.**

> ⚠ **This is the largest single collision with locked canon.** The keystone's §19.1 first-reaping beat is *deliberately visceral, queasy, guilt-bearing* — "did I just kill myself?", own-corpse destruction that must read queasy, not luminous — and §23.3's escape is *"fleeing a world you just killed, pockets full of its souls,"* the accruing moral complicity that feeds the ending. The Archive reframe proposes the **opposite valence: reverent, luminous, preservation-not-death.** See §C.1 — this is the first and most important [OPEN-MATT], and it is *not* a wording choice; it is a choice about what the game is *about.*

### B.3 — What the frame resolves, in the spine's own accounting [ANALYSIS]
1. **Predation → trial.** "Keep what you kill" made every enemy prey; "try your skill against the archive" makes them worthy tests you honor. Same mechanic, inverted valence.
2. **The element crack → reader/record.** The long-open knot: "keep what you kill" implied taking the enemy's *form*, but the soul is a fixed element, so a fire kit re-expressed as your frost soul felt like a *lesser* version — you didn't keep what you killed. The archive frame dissolves it: you're not taking their body, you're loading their record, and the record is neutral — of course it carries your element, because you're the reader and the build is the text.
3. **Tone drift → luminous preservation.** *(The spine reads the Necromancer/death-cult origin as a tone that "never fit the montage's inherent celebration.")* See §C.1 — under v2 canon the death-cult origin is **locked** (A2), so "resolve tone drift by going luminous" is a proposed *pivot*, not a resolution of an open tension. Flagged, not accepted.
4. **[Bonus] Element-as-light is now coherent as FICTION.** Element stayed light because the 15-game lattice made rich elements unbalanceable — a mechanical necessity that previously needed diegetic hand-waving. Now natively justified: you are a reader with a fixed signature; every record carries your signature lightly over its own structure. **This one I endorse without reservation** — it aligns exactly with the *already-canon* soul-as-lens loot fiction (`agnostic-loot-story-spec.md`: item = fixed light, soul = lens). The Archive Frame and the loot spec say the same thing in the same words. That is a genuine convergence, not a pivot.

### B.4 — Run structure [FRAME — proposed]
- **Strata = era-bands, chronological (2000→2025) = acts.** The run marches forward through the genre's history; each era-band is an act with its own champions.
- **Difficulty curve inherited free.** Builds genuinely grew more mechanically elaborate over 25 years, so early→late escalates complexity *diegetically* — origin eras teach on clean archetypes, modern eras test on baroque interaction-density. A designed onboarding ramp the genre's own evolution supplies.
- **Roster-gating is diegetic.** In each era you may wield only what existed *yet* — your fixed soul incarnating through progressively more modern records.
- **Era-champions = boss-archetype encounters** (per the standing ruling: extract boss *archetypes* as folk-knowledge, build your own; never replicate a specific game's boss). Each era's gate is your own instantiation of that era's characteristic encounter pattern.
- **Art is decoupled** (standing ruling): the "play through the ages" *structure* stands even with uniform art. Era-art (StyleProfile presets over shared Synty characters → shader-degradation → native fidelity tiers) is upside, never a dependency.

### B.5 — The frontier as finale [FRAME — proposed — the keystone claim]
The bottom of the descent — **past the modern era — is the frontier: a zone populated by kits Matt built to test new families,** derived from the atlas's ghost field (the empty cells, the hybrid door, the union cells). You descend through 25 years of settled history to reach the shelf *that isn't full yet* — where history is still being written — and you fight **the genre's unbuilt future.**

The arc is **known → known → known → UNKNOWN.** Every era below the pinnacle is *recognition*; the pinnacle is *discovery.* The whole descent is a montage of the genre's memory terminating in its frontier — which is *also literally what the project is*: cataloguing the past to build what's next. Story and purpose become the same gesture.

It also resolves the predictive-devlog dilemma without the epistemic risk: you're not *predicting* these kits are great — **you ship them as content and let players find out.** The frontier-as-finale is the union-cell thesis *delivered as a boss zone instead of a claim.*

> ✓ **This is the frame's strongest, cleanest piece, and it lands on real infrastructure.** The atlas ghost field is **live engine canon** (`atlas-derivation-charter-2026-07-14.md`, Edition I frozen 2026-07-14 on gates A+C+D+F-1; axes **PERFORM↔DEPLOY / EMBODY↔LAUNCH**). "Unlit ghosts ARE the unexplored space" is a shipped rendering law. The frontier finale is buildable *from data that already exists.* One caveat rides it — Finding F-1 — see §C.5.

## §C — COHERENCE AUDIT (required)

*Every touchpoint where the Archive Frame meets locked canon, classified: **COMPATIBLE** (folds clean), **TENSION** (collides; needs a ruling), or **GATED-ON-FLAG** (cannot resolve until an open Q2/Q3/Q4 flag resolves). This is the spine of the whole draft — the fold cannot be ratified until the TENSION rows are ruled.*

### §C.1 — vs. the DEATH-FAITH frame + the creed valence (A2 · keystone §14/§19.1 · loop §23.3) — **TENSION (the load-bearing one)**

**The collision, stated plainly:** the Archive Frame's central emotional move is **luminous preservation** — enemies are "worthy tests you honor," reincarnation is "reverent, a record archived," the reaper origin is read as tone-drift to be resolved *away.* The v2 canon is the **opposite** and it is Matt-ruled:
- **A2 (locked):** the player is *a soul bound to a death-cult.* The death-faith frame is the retitle's whole identity.
- **Keystone §19.1 (LOCKED):** the first reaping is *"visceral, irreversible… the own-corpse destruction must read queasy, not as a triumphant fatality. Don't let it be purely cool."* The realization is *"holy crap, did I just kill myself?"* — power curdled into dread.
- **Keystone §19.2 (LOCKED):** the moral progression is *shock → curation → complicity* — "curating your own self-deaths for a god's amusement… a connoisseur of your own deaths. A more damning portrait than going numb."
- **Loop §23.3 (LOCKED):** *"every successful run ends with you fleeing a world you just killed, pockets full of its souls… the escapes ARE the player's repeated, embodied complicity."*

**These two registers are not compatible as-written.** The death-faith frame's entire dramatic engine is *complicity you cannot wash off*; the Archive Frame's is *reverent stewardship you can feel good about.* You can build a game on either. You cannot build it on both without one hollowing the other.

**BUT — the collision is narrower than it looks, and there are three genuine reconciliation paths (this is the §6-adjacent decision, and it routes into the motive fork).** The Archive-vs-death-faith tension **is the same fork as §6's motive fork** wearing different clothes: *reaper-motive* keeps the death-faith valence; *rescue-motive* pulls toward luminous preservation; *trial-motive* sits between. So this row does not need an independent ruling — **it is decided by the §6 ruling** (§D below). What I flag here is the *consequence table* so the §6 ruling is made with eyes open:

| If §6 resolves as… | The creed valence becomes… | What breaks / what must change |
|---|---|---|
| **Reaper** | death-faith intact; luminous-preservation DROPPED | the Archive is a *predatory* archive — you strip records, not honor them; keystone §19.1/§19.2/§23.3 stand untouched. The frame survives as *setting*, its reverent register does not. **Cleanest canon-compatibility; weakest on the spine's own thesis.** |
| **Trial** | hybrid: the *fights* are honored contests, the *reaping* is still a real death you author | keystone §19.1 "did I just kill myself?" survives (you still destroy your own body to load the next record); §19.2 complicity survives *if* the god still watches; §23.3 survives. The Archive adds *reverence for the opponent* without removing *guilt for the method.* **Most reconcilable — honor the champion, still pay the price.** This is the reading where the frame folds with the least canon damage. |
| **Rescue** | luminous preservation *foregrounded*; death-faith recedes to a lie-you're-inside | requires the largest keystone reconciliation — §19.1's queasy self-murder and §23.3's "world you just killed" must be *re-motivated* (you destroy to preserve; the eruption is corruption-you-fight, not rebellion-you-caused). **Richest thematically per the spine; largest collision with the manufactured-rebellion keystone — see §C.3.** |

**Verdict:** TENSION, resolved by the §6 motive ruling. Do **not** let the frame silently install luminous-preservation — that would overwrite A2 and three LOCKED keystone beats without a ruling. The reverent register is *available* under trial/rescue and *forbidden* under reaper; Matt's ruling picks.

**Genre/mythic note (register):** the "honor the champion you defeat, then carry them" instinct is ancient and load-bearing — it is the *psychomachia* (the soul's battle where each vice-figure bested is *incorporated*, not merely slain), and its ARPG-native form is Shin Megami Tensei / Persona's defeat-and-recruit (loop §1 already cites this pedigree). Diablo has *never* done this — Diablo's dead stay dead and the loot is the reward; the Archive Frame's "the defeated becomes your next self" is genre-novel *for an ARPG* and genre-ancient *for the JRPG lineage the loop doc already claims.* That lineage supports **trial** most naturally (honored contest → incorporation). Rescue reaches for a different myth (the archivist-against-oblivion; Borges' Library, the Alexandria burning) which is powerful but pulls *away* from the combat verb toward a curatorial one.

### §C.2 — vs. the KEYSTONE DEMIGOD ARC (A5/A6 · keystone §16/§17 · Q4/B4) — **GATED-ON-FLAG (Q4) + TENSION on the antagonist**

The keystone's demigod-jailer arc is fully authored and Matt-ruled: **the demigod built the cage, is barred from the god, scans the anonymous ranks, and selects a risen initiate who can commune with the god, then steers them** (§17); the four-tier Second-Foundation stratification (§16) — major cult deceived, minor cult manufacturing rebellions, inner core suspecting the deadening, demigod knowing. The player descends *through the tiers as the veils tear.*

**The Archive Frame proposes a different descent geometry: down through 25 years of genre history.** These are two different "down." The keystone's descent is *through knowledge-tiers of a conspiracy*; the Archive's is *through chronological strata of build-craft.* **Are they the same axis or two axes?** This is the fold's central structural question and it **cannot be resolved without Q4** (the keystone reveal-sequence flag is still OPEN).

Three ways they could relate — **all [OPEN-MATT], gated on Q4:**
- **(a) Congruent axes** — the eras *are* the tiers: the shallow archive (2000-era clean archetypes) is the major-cult surface (the deceived); the deep archive (modern baroque builds) is the inner core; the frontier is the demigod's own workshop. *Elegant if it holds; risky — it welds the genre-history axis to the conspiracy axis and if they don't naturally align, one is forced.*
- **(b) Orthogonal axes** — you descend the archive (era-strata) *while* the conspiracy reveals *laterally* (fragments, the god's phrases, the escaping kindlers). The Archive is the *spatial* spine; the manufactured-rebellion is the *epistemic* spine; they run in parallel. *Safest; preserves both structures intact; the frame is the "where," the keystone is the "what's really going on."*
- **(c) The Archive is the demigod's lie** — the "you are honoring history" framing *is* the cult's self-deceiving story (like §23.3's "the realm erupted, proof they needed our mercy"), and the reveal is that the Archive was never reverent — it was a harvest wearing a museum's face. *Most thematically integrated with the keystone; requires the reaper/rescue valence tension of §C.1 to resolve toward the reveal.*

**Antagonist TENSION:** the spine's rescue-motive names the antagonist as **"forgetting."** The keystone's antagonist is **"the deadening"** (§15) — the terminal flattening of will, then life, that the harvest *approaches.* These rhyme beautifully — *forgetting* and *deadening* are both entropy-of-meaning — but they are **not the same thing** and the keystone's is LOCKED. If rescue wins (§6), the fold must either (i) map "forgetting" onto "the deadening" (defensible — a forgotten history *is* a kind of deadening) or (ii) hold them distinct and risk two antagonists. **[OPEN-MATT]:** does the Archive's "forgetting" = the keystone's "deadening," or is it a distinct threat? Lean: *map them* — one antagonist, two faces (the archive corrupting = the will deadening), which also feeds §C.3.

**Also gated:** A5's split (demigod = punctuated Mercer-mentor; god = unreadable Rorschach; hub ensemble = daily banter) must survive the fold. The Archive Frame introduces a new candidate voice — *the archive itself* / *the reading-head's own narration* — which risks reinflating a fourth speaking entity. See §C.7 (personae) and the D7 note (§E). **The archive must NOT become a chatty narrator** — that would dilute the god's unreadability and the demigod's punctuated betrayal exactly as the keystone §19.1 note warns.

### §C.3 — vs. THE MANUFACTURED REBELLION + THE ESCAPE (keystone §14 · loop §23.3) — **TENSION (structural, mechanical-load-bearing)**

This is the row where the frame touches the game's *most mechanically-committed* narrative structure, so it needs the most care.

**The run model §23.3 is LOCKED and it is performed with the player's own hands every run:** you harvest two conduits → **combining them erupts the realm** → *this eruption IS the manufactured rebellion (§14)* → you flee as the champion. The buried truth is *you caused the eruption; the realm was at peace until you harvested it.* The god eating the soul-mass is *the harvest feeding, NOT a defusing/saving act* (§23.3 explicitly forbids framing extraction as heroic).

**The Archive Frame's per-era gate is a champion fight to "earn passage deeper."** On the surface this maps cleanly to the §23.3 Structure-2 champion. **But the frames disagree on what the fight MEANS:**
- **§23.3 (locked):** you reap the champion → reincarnate into its body → the realm *erupts because you combined the conduits* → you flee, complicit.
- **Archive Frame (proposed):** you best the champion → load its record → *earn passage* → descend. No eruption, no manufactured rebellion, no fleeing-a-world-you-killed — the champion is a *turnstile,* not a *harvest-with-a-cost.*

**These cannot both be the run's climax.** Either the run ends in *eruption + guilty flight* (locked) or in *earned passage + reverent descent* (proposed). This is not a valence quibble like §C.1 — it is **two incompatible run-shapes,** and the run-shape is the thing the engine builds toward.

**Reconciliation paths — [OPEN-MATT], and this one is genuinely hard:**
- **(a) The Archive Frame is the META-structure; §23.3 is the PER-RUN structure.** Each *run* is still harvest→eruption→guilty-escape (locked, untouched); the *Archive* is the *sequence of runs* — each realm you harvest is one era's record, and *descending the archive = the succession of realms across many runs.* Under this reading the frame **does not touch §23.3 at all** — it re-describes what the *between-runs progression* means. **This is the cleanest reconciliation and I lean toward it hard:** the manufactured-rebellion run-loop is preserved bit-for-bit, and the Archive becomes the *shape of the campaign* (realm 1 = 2000-era record, realm N = modern record, frontier = the finale). The eruption stays; the reverence, if any, is the *archive's framing of the campaign,* not the run.
  - *Consequence to check:* this makes "you honor the champion" and "you caused the eruption" **coexist** — you honor the record *and* you harvest it and it erupts. That is actually *richer* than either alone (the museum that strip-mines its own exhibits) and it re-routes §C.1 toward the reaper/trial reading, not rescue. The reverence becomes *hypocrisy the player enacts* — which is exactly the keystone's Rorschach discipline (§23.3: "misreadable as holy on the surface, damning once known").
- **(b) The Archive REPLACES the manufactured-rebellion run-loop.** The champion is a turnstile; no eruption; the descent is reverent. **This would supersede §23.3 and the §14 keystone spine — the single most mechanically-committed structure in the game.** I flag this as *possible but very expensive* — it discards the run model the engine is being built against and the moral-complicity engine that feeds the ending. Not recommended without Matt explicitly choosing to rebuild the run.
- **(c) The eruption IS the archive's corruption (rescue-path integration).** Combining the conduits doesn't erupt a *realm* — it *corrupts the record,* and the "rebellion" is the record fighting its own erasure. This folds the manufactured-rebellion INTO the rescue-motive: you descend to preserve, but the act of harvesting *is* what corrupts, so you are *both* the archivist and the vandal. **Thematically the most integrated; requires §6 = rescue; requires re-motivating §23.3's eruption from "manufactured rebellion" to "record corruption."** Rich, but it rewrites the buried truth of the keystone — a large ruling.

**Verdict:** TENSION, and the reconciliation path is coupled to the §6 ruling. **Reading (a) — Archive-as-meta, run-model-untouched — is the lean** because it preserves the most locked canon and is the only path that requires *zero* rewrite of §23.3. Rulings (b) and (c) are available if Matt wants deeper integration at the cost of reopening the run model.

### §C.4 — vs. THE RUN MODEL §19/§23 (the reap beat + run shape) — **COMPATIBLE (under §C.3 reading (a)), with two fold-notes**

If §C.3 resolves as (a) [Archive-as-meta], the §19/§23 mechanics fold **clean**:
- **§20 reap beat (LOCKED — control-transfer + visible soul-travel + first-person look-back at your own destroyed body):** the Archive Frame's "de-resolve into particulate → re-resolve into next record" is a **re-skin of the SAME beat,** not a new one. The soul-soar (§20.1) *is* the light-data beam; the look-back at the destroyed old body (§20.2 step 5) is the "record archived" moment. **The frame gives the already-locked beat a cleaner name and a visual justification for the light-data grammar — a genuine improvement, and a COMPATIBLE one.** One caution: §19.1's beat must stay *queasy* under reaper/trial (§C.1); only under rescue does it soften — so the reap-beat's compatibility is itself downstream of §6.
- **§23.1 three-beat descent (Structure→Biome→Structure→Escape) + ~25-min run:** untouched by the frame under reading (a). The era-strata are the *campaign* axis, not the *within-run* axis. **COMPATIBLE.**

**Fold-note 1 — "descend" is overloaded across three canon layers.** The word "descend" now means: (i) the *within-run* descent through floors (§23.1); (ii) the *conspiracy* descent through knowledge-tiers (keystone §16); (iii) the *Archive* descent through era-strata (this frame). A future writer must not conflate them. **[OPEN-MATT]:** the fold should name these three "descents" distinctly (proposed: *floor-descent* / *veil-descent* / *era-descent*) so the vocabulary stays clean — the same discipline the tracker applies to the three "spaces."

**Fold-note 2 — the era-gating claim needs an engine reality-check.** "In each era you may wield only what existed yet" (B.4) is a lovely diegetic gate, but the roster is derived from the atlas corpus, and the atlas is organized **by behavior, not by game-of-origin** (Gate C: franchise R² ≤ 0.15 — the map *deliberately* mixes franchises). **The kits do NOT carry a clean "year of origin" coordinate** — that was a *refused* organizing axis. So "wield only what existed in this era" is **not free from the atlas** the way the spine implies; it needs either (i) a separate era-tag on kits (an authoring cost) or (ii) a looser "era-flavored" gating that doesn't claim strict chronology. **[OPEN-MATT] + routes to elrond/rocket:** does era-gating need a real per-kit era coordinate, or is it presentation-only flavor? See §C.5.

### §C.5 — vs. THE ATLAS + GHOST FIELD (frontier finale; `atlas-derivation-charter`) — **COMPATIBLE, with the F-1 constraint**

The frontier-as-finale (B.5) lands on **real, frozen engine canon** and is the frame's strongest fold:
- The ghost field is *drawn, not implied* (charter §4): unlit ghosts = unexplored space, rendered figure-ground. The frontier finale is *literally the unlit ghosts made into a boss zone* — the fiction and the data structure are the same object. **COMPATIBLE and mutually reinforcing.**
- Edition I is frozen (2026-07-14) on a 14-dim basis, axes **PERFORM↔DEPLOY / EMBODY↔LAUNCH.** The "frontier at the bottom" is expressible *today* from `atlas.json`'s derived basis.

**The one constraint the frame must respect — Finding F-1:** the atlas's Gate B (negative geography) **failed reversed** — "kit death is not geography," published verbatim and non-downgradable: *the unexplored ghost field contains no forbidden zones derived from corpse-geography.* The Archive Frame's frontier must **not** be framed as "dangerous unexplored territory where builds go to die" or "the forbidden deep." F-1 says the unexplored space is *not* a graveyard-by-region. **[OPEN-MATT] + fold-constraint:** the frontier's fiction should read as *unwritten* (the shelf not yet filled), NOT as *forbidden* or *cursed* (which F-1 empirically refuted). The spine already leans this way ("history still being written") — this note just locks it against a tempting-but-false "here be dragons" register.

**Second fold-note (mechanical honesty):** the frontier kits are "kits Matt built to test new families" (B.5) — these are the **engine-native / experimental frontier** kits (loop §18: "a retention asset, not a launch feature… do not gate launch on the experimental pipeline"). So the frontier-finale, *as a shippable zone,* inherits loop §18's timing: **it is a post-launch / late-content beat, not a day-one one.** The frame's *fiction* (the descent terminates in the unbuilt future) is day-one-authorable as a *promise*; the *populated frontier zone* rides the experimental pipeline's timeline. **[OPEN-MATT]:** does the frontier ship as a real playable zone at launch (expensive; gated on the experimental pipeline) or as a *named, glimpsed* finale that fills in post-launch (cheaper; matches loop §18)? Lean: glimpsed-then-filled, per §18's existing ruling.

### §C.6 — vs. Q2 / B1 (run-persistence) — **GATED-ON-FLAG**

The Archive Frame implies a *cumulative* descent — you get *deeper* into the archive over many runs, era by era. **What "deeper" means for persistence is exactly the open Q2/B1 contract** (what survives a run vs. resets; Grimoire-register persistence; the "playthrough" boundary for the free re-summon token). **The fold must NOT silently answer this.** Specifically: "descending the archive across runs" *sounds like* it asserts a persistent depth-progression — but whether depth persists, resets, or banks is Q2's to rule. **[OPEN-MATT / GATED]:** the frame is *compatible with* any Q2 resolution but *presupposes none* — the era-descent's persistence model is Q2's output, and this draft explicitly defers to it. (Note the clean composition: §23.3's *body-persists* ruling — "you are your latest body… you walk into realm N+1 wearing realm N's champion" — already gives the era-descent a persistence spine: your *serial body-chain* IS your descent-depth marker. That composes beautifully and does NOT resolve Q2's open sub-questions; it just means the frame doesn't fight the one persistence ruling already made.)

### §C.7 — vs. Q3 / B3 (companion) — **GATED-ON-FLAG (light touch)**

The Archive Frame doesn't obviously need a companion, but it introduces a candidate: *do archive personae accompany you?* (a bested champion-record riding along). Under A11 + A9, a companion — *if B3 ships one* — is a **grimoire-summoned claimed soul, never a past self, Path-Pure (run 1 alone).** The Archive Frame is **compatible** with that (a "claimed record you can re-load as an ally" fits the reading-head grammar) but **must not resolve B3** — whether a companion ships at all is still open. **[OPEN-MATT / GATED]:** the frame neither requires nor forbids a companion; it defers to B3.

## §D — §6 THE MOTIVE FORK [OPEN — MATT RULING NEEDED]

*Why the player cuts through the archive sets the whole register. This is the spine's §6, preserved as OPEN. Matt leans rescue but did NOT rule. All three presented as structured options with dramatic consequence + genre/mythic precedent + the lean marked AS a lean. This draft resolves NOTHING here.*

**The three readings — each with what it does to the whole:**

### Option 1 — REAPER (claim souls / records to grow)
- **Register:** predatory; the dark death-faith origin intact. The archive is a hunting ground; you strip records.
- **Consequence:** **fully preserves A2 + keystone §19.1/§19.2/§23.3** (the queasy self-murder, the connoisseur-of-your-own-deaths complicity, the guilty flight). The Archive's *reverent* register is DROPPED — the museum framing survives, its luminous valence does not.
- **Genre/mythic precedent:** Diablo's death-economy (the dead stay dead, you take their drops) transposed onto records; the *pillager-of-tombs* archetype (Diablo I's Tristram catacombs; the grave-robber-as-hero the genre was built on). Mythically: the *tomb-raider*, the *soul-eater* (the Norse *draugr*-hoard, the wight's barrow).
- **Cost:** cuts *against* the spine's core recognition (the frame was *derived* to make the champions "worthy tests to honor, not prey to consume"). The spine explicitly moves *away* from this valence. **Not the spine's lean — kept for completeness because it is the maximally canon-preserving option.**

### Option 2 — TRIAL (best each legend to earn passage / prove worthy)
- **Register:** the Contest-of-Champions register; enemies are worthy opponents; clean competitive clarity. You honor the champion *and* still pay the reaping's price.
- **Consequence:** the **most reconcilable** with locked canon (per §C.1/§C.3). Keystone §19.1 survives (you still destroy your own body to load the next record — the price is real); §19.2 complicity survives *if* the god watches; §23.3 survives under C.3 reading (a). Adds *reverence for the opponent* without deleting *guilt for the method.* You honor the champion, then you harvest them anyway — the hypocrisy is the theme.
- **Genre/mythic precedent:** Shin Megami Tensei / Persona defeat-and-recruit (loop §1's own cited pedigree — the JRPG lineage where the bested foe is *incorporated*, not merely slain); the *psychomachia* (each vice-figure bested is taken into the soul). Mythically: the *contest of champions* (the Norse einherjar — the honored dead who fight eternally), the *worthy-opponent* code (bushido's respect for the defeated).
- **Cost:** the "reverence" and the "harvest" sit in productive tension rather than resolving — which is *good drama* but requires the writer to hold both (the museum that strip-mines its exhibits). Cooler emotionally than rescue (no one to save).

### Option 3 — RESCUE / COLLAPSE (the archive is failing — history is being corrupted/erased; you descend to preserve it)
- **Register:** the most emotionally resonant, and *thematically true to the real project*: you are literally building a preservation project that saves the genre's history from being forgotten. Enemies become *history worth saving*; the antagonist is *forgetting.*
- **Consequence:** requires the **largest keystone reconciliation** (per §C.1/§C.2/§C.3). §19.1's queasy self-murder and §23.3's "world you just killed" must be *re-motivated* (you destroy to preserve; the eruption becomes record-corruption you fight, not rebellion you caused — §C.3 reading (c)). The antagonist "forgetting" must map onto or displace the keystone's "deadening" (§C.2). This is the reading that *reopens the most locked canon* — but also the one where fiction and real-project-purpose become *the same gesture,* which the spine (rightly) calls rare and powerful.
- **Genre/mythic precedent:** the *archivist-against-oblivion* — Borges' Library of Babel, the burning of Alexandria, the monk-copyist preserving a dying civilization's memory through a dark age. Mythically: *Mnemosyne* (memory as the mother of meaning) against *Lethe* (the river of forgetting). In games: the "preserve the world's memory" finale-turn (NieR's archival melancholy; Outer Wilds' "the universe ends but the knowing mattered").
- **Cost:** pulls the combat verb toward a *curatorial* one (you fight to *save,* which sits oddly against a genre built on *taking*); reopens the manufactured-rebellion keystone; needs the "forgetting vs. deadening" antagonist reconciliation. **Highest reward, highest canon-cost.**

### The lean (Matt's, preserved AS a lean — NOT a ruling)
**[RECOMMENDED in the spine]:** trial-or-rescue, **leaning rescue** for emotional weight (*"a story whose fiction and whose real purpose are the same gesture is rare and powerful"*); trial if clean competitive clarity is preferred.

**gandalf's steward-note on the lean (offered, not deciding):** rescue is the *most beautiful* reading and the *most expensive* — it reopens the run model's buried truth (§C.3(c)) and the keystone's antagonist (§C.2). Trial is the *most reconcilable* — it folds with near-zero canon damage under §C.3(a), and its "honor the champion, still pay the price" hypocrisy is *itself* the death-faith Rorschach discipline the keystone already runs. **If Matt wants the frame to fold with the least disturbance to locked canon, trial is the path. ## §E — THE AI-TELL / D7 DISCIPLINE (required — where the archive conceit implies LLM content)

*The AI-tell line (D7): no raw-LLM narrative surfaces at major story moments. The Archive conceit is unusually LLM-adjacent — "an archive of infinite generated records," "the reading-head loads a record" — so this section names where the conceit implies LLM-generated content and how the templated-structure discipline holds.*

**The canon rule (D7, from `story-expansion.md` §12/§173 + `agnostic-loot-story-spec.md`):** weighted narrative surfaces are **lookup, not generation** — rendered from engine-emitted records, *no runtime LLM narration,* templated-blanks register only if any voice is present. *"Synthetic re-description would dissolve exactly the earned specificity that makes the listing land."* Loot: LLM names only; computed rules text.

**Where the Archive Frame *tempts* a D7 violation — and how the discipline holds:**

1. **"The archive of records" tempts an LLM-narrated record for each champion.** The frame's whole conceit ("you load a record") invites a generated *story* per record. **D7 forbids this at weighted moments.** The hold: the record's *weight* comes from it being *the kit the player fought and bested* — its name (LLM-named at emission, per the loot spec's naming surface), its **computed** stats, its **structural** archetype — NOT from a runtime-generated backstory. **The archive stores STRUCTURE (the build skeleton = the record), which is exactly the D7-safe object.** A record is a *build,* not a *paragraph.* This is a *feature* of the frame, not a risk: the spine's own grammar ("the build skeleton IS the record; you are what animates it") **is D7 compliance stated in fiction** — the record is structural, the animation is the player's fixed element.

2. **The frontier's "unbuilt future kits" tempt LLM-generated finale content.** The frontier kits are ghost-field-derived — but per the atlas charter they are **derived by a pre-registered statistical pipeline and hand-built by Matt** ("kits Matt built to test new families"), NOT runtime-LLM-generated. **D7-safe by construction:** the frontier is *authored/derived* content, not *narrated-on-the-fly* content. The reveal beat ("you fight the genre's unbuilt future") is a **templated frame** over pre-built kits, not a generated monologue.

3. **The archive-as-narrator temptation (the biggest one).** If the Archive *speaks* — narrating your descent, describing each era, eulogizing each champion — that is a weighted narrative surface and D7 governs it. **[OPEN-MATT]:** do archive personae / the archive-voice speak at all? (§C.2 warns against reinflating a fourth chatty entity beside the god/demigod/hub-ensemble.) **The D7-safe answer, if any voice is present:** templated-blanks register only — era-labels and champion-names slotted into authored frames (*"[ERA]. [CHAMPION_NAME], of [REALM]. Best them to pass."*), the **computed** fields filled from emitted records, **zero runtime free-text.** The keystone's god-speech §19.3 is the *model*: a small authored pool, curated hard, engine may *propose* but human *curates,* never free-generated at the weighted moment. **Lean: the archive is SILENT or near-silent** (matching the god's rarity-is-the-mechanism discipline) — the era-descent is *shown* (the strata, the champions, the frontier), not *narrated.* An over-talkative archive would both violate D7 and dilute the god's unreadability.

**Fold-constraint (D7, locked-forward):** any archive-facing text surface is **lookup + templated-blanks**, never runtime LLM narration; the record is *structure* (D7-safe); the frontier is *derived/authored* (D7-safe); the archive-voice, if it exists, follows the god-speech §19.3 authored-pool discipline. **The Archive Frame is, if anything, MORE D7-defensible than a conventional narrative frame** — because its core noun (the record = a build skeleton) is inherently structural rather than prose. That is worth stating in the ratified fold: *the conceit that looks most LLM-adjacent is actually the most templated-structure-native.*
