A.3 — Post-mortem

Successes:
  • Autonomous-operation protocol worked end-to-end. The "Matt out, SME agents decide, gandalf cross-cutting, knight-rider sequences" model produced 33 parallel specialist sessions with no escalation friction.
  • R8 generative-pipeline-flip delivered measured evidence for a question we had been holding intuitively for weeks. The data carries its own theme.
  • Knight-rider's tag-firing discipline + push-readiness summaries kept all 4 repos in sync; zero ADR-006 hard-constraint violations.
  • The disposition-arc pattern (category-of-completion when ex-ante metrics meet reality awkwardly) emerged as a reusable framing for hypothesis-vs-evidence misalignment.
Failures / friction:
  • 3 of 4 dispositions were category-of-completion reframes rather than direct PASS. This is honest given how hypothesis tests met reality, but the pattern frequency suggests initial hypothesis-test thresholds were too tight in places. R2 H1 variance ≥ 0.10 in particular was instrument-limited (the 43/3/4 sample imbalance was an authoring oversight in the gap-solutions doc).
  • R4 + R5 player-experience validation depends on Matt playtest, which means the engine-rebuild "completion" claim has a 30-min asterisk that's been sitting at the gate since 07:05Z.
  • R8 inverted_no_naming arm produced unshippable output (TSI 1.0). The cost-savings (99.7%) was real but disposable. The mode was committed too early in the design — should have started with cohesion-first, naming-retained, and treated naming-removal as a downstream experiment.

Recommendations for future hive-mind runs

  1. Pre-pre-test hypothesis-test thresholds with a smoke run. The R2 H1 0.10 threshold was authored without a baseline-distribution measurement; the 28pp empirical delta was correctly directional but the sample size was authoring-misaligned. Future hypothesis-test gates should run a 1-class smoke first.
  2. Bake "category-of-completion" framing into the protocol explicitly as a valid disposition shape — not as a "we lowered the threshold" act. The 4-sub-claim pattern that emerged 3× this session deserves protocol-level naming.
  3. Playtest-pending tags should fire at gate-cross with a clear waiting-period namespace. Currently v0.12-r5-hypothesis-test-passed and v0.16-r4-hypothesis-test-passed are "held" tags — they exist as notional pointers. This works but is implicit; a held/ namespace might be cleaner.
  4. R8-style A/B experiments should isolate variables. The inverted_no_naming arm conflated coalescence-first AND template-naming-only. The cleaner experiment would have been: arm 1 baseline; arm 2 inverted-with-LLM-naming; arm 3 inverted-with-template-naming (template-mode-only). The cohesion failure of the naming variant could have been measured independently of the inversion test.
