# Character Loadouts — Family Review

**For Matt + son.** Five seasons of generated content. Each season has 10–11 classes; 2 are act-bosses (encountered as bosses in demo1), the rest are playable.

## What you're looking at

For each class:
- **Name** — LLM-generated, evocative
- **Identity** — what they are mechanically (archetype, element, role)
- **Flavor text** — the LLM's interpretation of who they are
- **Abilities** — 5–6 named abilities
- **Carried gear** — the 4 items they have equipped (what drops on defeat if they're an act-boss)

## Why this review matters

Your reactions to these characters inform demo1 priorities:
- Which classes sound most fun to play first?
- Which seasons feel most appealing thematically?
- Which gear catches your eye?
- Which act-bosses sound intimidating?

Take notes. The demo agent will start with whichever classes you flag as exciting.

## Quick season summary

Five seasons, ~10-11 classes each, varied themes. Skim this table first; then deep-dive into whichever sound most interesting.

| Season | Anchor | Theme | Element flavors |
|---|---|---|---|
| 001001 | The Deep Trench | wind | pitch · brine · thrum · basalt |
| 001002 | The Crypt of the First Saint | earth | wax · marrow · exhalation · tear |
| 001003 | The Cathedral of Bone | water | torch · milk · breath · bone |
| 001004 | The Throne Room of the Mad King | earth | char · mercury · draft · throne |
| 001005 | The Ghost Town of the Gold Strike | water | coal · slick · dust · gold |

Each season has 2 act-bosses (encountered as bosses in demo1) and 8-9 regular playable classes. Each class carries 4 gear items — those drop when the act-boss is defeated.
